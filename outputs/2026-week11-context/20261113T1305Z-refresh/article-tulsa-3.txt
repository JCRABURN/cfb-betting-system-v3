Tulsa Rolls Past Tulane, 60-10
11/7/2026 2:44:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Photo Gallery
Share:
NEW ORLEANS, La. —
The Tulsa Golden Hurricane secured their fifth win of the season in convincing fashion with a 60-10 win over the Tulane Green Wave. The 60 points are the most in the Tre Lamb era and the most in program history over Tulane.
Tulsa's 50-point victory was its largest in program history over Tulane. Tulsa finished with 338 passing yards, 224 rushing yards and 31 first downs while holding Tulane to 204 total yards.
Baylor Hayes completed 26 of 33 passes for 338 yards and four touchdowns and added 16 rushing yards. Hayes set career highs in passing yards and passing touchdowns and was Tulsa's first 300-yard passer since Cooper Legas threw for 333 yards against UTSA on Oct. 26, 2024.
Oran Singleton caught 13 passes for 177 yards, a career-high, and three touchdowns. It was his second game this season with more than 100 receiving yards. His 13 receptions set the Yulman Stadium single-game record. Damari Alston rushed 14 times for 52 yards and a touchdown, caught two passes for 24 yards. Thomas Davis recorded five tackles, 1.5 sacks and a forced fumble.
Tulsa put together an 11-play, 75-yard opening drive, using three Singleton catches for first downs before Baylor Hayes found Singleton for a 3-yard touchdown making it 7-0. Tulane moved from its 23 to the Tulsa 24 behind five Trace Johnson completions, but Kyran Duhon's second-down sack helped set up fourth-and-5, and John Allen's rush lost 10 yards with a fumble recovered by Tulane for a turnover on downs.
The defense closed the first quarter by holding Tulane to minus-7 yards on its final series, with JD Drew dropping a completion for a 3-yard loss and Arias Nash sacking Johnson before Singleton returned the punt 13 yards and Tulsa finished the first quarter ahead 7-0.
The second quarter began with Baylor Hayes finding Singleton for 32 yards, and after Alston converted fourth-and-1 at the Tulane 16, Owen Carey made a 26-yard field goal at 10:48 to make it 10-0.
Tulsa's offense continued to roll in the second quarter as they had a 10-play, 68-yard drive that included a 22-yard Hayes-to-Singleton conversion and ended with Hayes' 7-yard touchdown pass to Calloway at 5:27 for a 17-0 lead.
On Tulane's next snap, Drew intercepted Trace Johnson at the Tulsa 31 and returned it 23 yards, setting up a short field that Tulsa turned into Javon Ross' 11-yard touchdown run at 4:14 and a 24-0 lead. Thomas Davis later sacked Johnson on a play that resulted in a fumble Tulane recovered before punting, and Tulsa drove 63 yards in eight plays for Hayes' 3-yard touchdown pass to Singleton. Tulsa went to halftime ahead 32-0.
Tulsa's defense opened the third quarter by forcing a three-and-out, with Chris Thompson Jr. stopping Maurice Turner for a loss and Thomas Davis and Jaylen George combining on a sack before Tulane punted.
Baylor Hayes moved Tulsa downfield on the next possession, completing passes to Calloway and David Wells Jr. before finding Singleton for a 27-yard touchdown making it 39-0.
After another Tulane punt, Tulsa went 84 yards in 11 plays. Hayes had completions of 32 yards to Javon Ross and 22 yards to Calloway, and Damari Alston's fourth-down conversion at the Tulane 10 before Alston scored from 6 yards out making it 46-0.
Tulane finished the opening possession of the fourth quarter with Cooper Helmke's 38-yard field goal, but Tulsa took over at its own 47 after the ensuing onside kickoff and moved 53 yards in three plays, with Seth Davis scoring on a 31-yard run to make it 53-3.
Tulsa's defense stopped Tulane on fourth-and-4 when Dedaunte Scott tackled Trace Johnson after a 1-yard gain, and later NuNu Campbell intercepted Dagan Bruno at the Tulsa 45 and returned it 16 yards to the Tulane 39.
Tulsa turned that takeaway into another touchdown, driving 39 yards on six rushing plays and scoring on Sevion Morrison's 3-yard run for a 60-3 lead. Tulane put together a late 73-yard drive capped by Bruno's 4-yard touchdown pass to Bredell Richardson. Tulsa came away with a 60-10 victory over Tulane
Tulsa football team hosts Florida Atlantic at 2:30 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 14, at H.A. Chapman Stadium in Tulsa, Okla. This will be homecoming for the Golden Hurricane.
