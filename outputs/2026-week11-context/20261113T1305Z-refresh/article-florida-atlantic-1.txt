Messer Named Semifinalist for Burlsworth Trophy
11/10/2026 6:26:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Ben Paul
BOCA RATON, Fla.
– With a remarkable 2026 season in hand, Florida Atlantic football's fifth-year WR
Easton Messer
was announced as one of 10 semifinalists for the Burlsworth Trophy, denoting the nation's best walk-on player, on Tuesday afternoon. Messer is the only player among the 10 who resides in a G6 program.
After beginning his collegiate career as a walk-on, Messer has ascended to be one of the top receivers in the game. His '26 campaign, his second year with the Owls, has seen him reach new heights, currently the nation's leader in receptions (81), tied for the lead in punt return scores (two), and No. 4 in both receiving touchdowns (12) as well as yards (983). In total, he has accounted for 17 scores in nine games and is the first Owl to have double-digit receiving touchdowns in a single season.
In the past three games, Messer has taken an even greater leap, scoring 12 total touchdowns in four different ways: passing (one), rushing (two), receiving (eight), and punt returning (one), making him the only FBS player to have recorded at least one of each in 2026. These performances have resulted in three weekly honors from the American Conference, two from the Paul Hornung Award, and two by the Burlsworth Trophy. During that span, the 'Owl Raid' offense is averaging 50.6 points per game and has clinched Bowl eligibility for the first time since 2020.
Among active FBS players, the Louisville, Kentucky native is No. 1 in career receptions (283) as well as all-purpose yards (4,770) as well as receiving yards (3,356). He has collected a reception in his last 39 games and has the third longest active streak.
The honor only adds to Messer's growing national recognition, also as a watch list member for the Biletnikoff Award and the Paul Hornung Award. Florida Atlantic is the second university to nominate Messer. He was recognized by the Burlsworth committee for his junior season at Western Kentucky.
ABOUT THE BURLSWORTH TROPHY
The selection committee will announce three finalists on Nov. 17. The finalists and their families will be honored at the annual Burlsworth Trophy Award Show on Monday, Dec. 7, at 7 p.m. CST at Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art in Bentonville, Arkansas, where the 2026 recipient will be announced.
The Burlsworth Trophy is named in honor of Brandon Burlsworth, who joined the University of Arkansas football team as a walk-on in 1994 without a single Division I scholarship offer. He earned a scholarship, became a three-year starter and was named an All-American in 1998. The Indianapolis Colts selected him with the 63rd overall pick in the 1999 NFL Draft. He was killed in an automobile accident 11 days later.
His life inspired the motion picture "Greater," released nationally in 2016. His legacy continues through the Burlsworth Trophy and the Brandon Burlsworth Foundation, which supports the physical and spiritual needs of children, particularly those with limited opportunities.
The Burlsworth Trophy is a member of the National College Football Awards Association (NCFAA) and is featured annually as part of ESPN's College Football Awards programming. For more information about the Burlsworth Trophy, visit BurlsworthTrophy.com.For more information about the Brandon Burlsworth Foundation, visit brandonburlsworth.org.
