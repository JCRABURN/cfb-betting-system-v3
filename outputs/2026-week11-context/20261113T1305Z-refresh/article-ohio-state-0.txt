Pro Bucks: Week 9
11/10/2026 4:54:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Blake Bennett
COLUMBUS, Ohio –
Ohio State rookie linebacker
Arvell Reese
delivered a historic defensive performance in Week 9, recording three sacks in the New York Giants' victory over the Philadelphia Eagles. Elsewhere, Houston Texans quarterback C.J. Stroud threw five touchdown passes, while Dallas Cowboys rookie safety
Caleb Downs
recorded his first career NFL interception to seal a victory over the Indianapolis Colts.
Reese Makes History in Dominant Defensive Performance
New York Giants rookie recorded 12 tackles, including 10 solo stops, three sacks, three tackles for loss and a forced fumble in a 19-11 victory over the Philadelphia Eagles on Sunday.
Reese's second quarter strip-sack against the Eagles gave New York possession of the ball near midfield. Reese then added another sack later in the second quarter before recording his third in the second half. Reese became the youngest player in NFL history to record three sacks in a game and the first rookie since individual tackles began being tracked in 1994 to record at least 10 tackles and three sacks in a single game.
Reese's strip-sack occurred in just 2.29 seconds, the fastest time to sack recorded in the NFL this season, according to Next Gen Stats. His performance helped the Giants limit Philadelphia to 220 total yards while recording seven total sacks defensively.
Stroud Shines in Five Touchdown Performance
Houston Texans quarterback C.J. Stroud completed 21-of-35 (60.0 pct.) passes for 384 yards and five touchdowns in a 44-22 victory over the Los Angeles Chargers on Sunday.
Stroud connected with wide receivers Kayshon Boutte and Tank Dell for 12-yard and 63-yard touchdown passes during the first half, helping Houston establish a 24-9 halftime lead. The Buckeye continues the impressive performance in the second half, extending their lead to 38-9 in the fourth quarter.
Stroud finished the game with a 125.5 passer rating, averaging 11.0 yards per pass and he added 20 rushing yards on two carries.
Styles Continues Impressive Rookie Campaign
Washington Commanders rookie linebacker
Sonny Styles
recorded 13 tackles, including six solo stops and a tackle for loss, in a 20-13 loss to the Los Angeles Rams on Sunday.
The Buckeye tied his career high in tackles for the third time this season, previously recording 13 against the New York Giants in Week 5 and San Francisco 49ers in Week 6.
Styles has continued to make an impact during his rookie campaign, accumulating 67 tackles, a sack, an interception and four pass deflections through eight games.
Smith-Njigba Surpasses 1,000 Receiving Yards
Seattle Seahawks wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba recorded seven receptions for 51 yards in a 17-3 victory over the Arizona Cardinals on Sunday.
Smith-Njigba surpassed 1,000 receiving yards on an 8-yard reception in the first quarter, becoming the first NFL player to reach the milestone this season.
The Buckeye became the third player in Seahawks history to record three consecutive 1,000-yard receiving seasons, joining Steve Largent and Tyler Lockett. Smith-Njigba leads the NFL with 71 receptions, 1,036 receiving yards and nine receiving touchdowns.
Werner Leads Saints Defense in Overtime Victory
New Orleans Saints linebacker Pete Werner recorded a team-high 11 tackles, including three solo stops, in a 23-22 overtime victory over the Cleveland Browns on Sunday.
Werner tied for the team lead in tackles, helping New Orleans hold Cleveland to just 95 rushing yards on 34 attempts. The Saints defense also prevented the Browns from scoring on a fourth-and-goal attempt from the 1-yard line in the second quarter.
This was Werner's fourth double-digit tackle performance of the season, bringing his total to 65 tackles through eight games. Werner has also notched two sacks and two pass breakups as he's continued his production for the New Orleans Saints.
Buckeyes Around the League
Cleveland Browns defensive back Ronnie Hickman notched nine tackles, including four solo stops and two pass deflections in a 23-22 overtime loss to the New Orleans Saints on Sunday. Hickman finished second on the team in tackles, bringing his season total to 57 through nine games.
Elsewhere, New Orleans Saints wide receiver Chris Olave recorded seven receptions for 98 yards in a 23-22 overtime win over the Cleveland Browns. Meanwhile, teammate Chase Young added four tackles, a sack and a pass deflection, bringing him to eight sacks on the season and ranking him second in the NFL this season.
