CHESTNUT HILL, Mass.
– The Boston College football team fell to Florida State, 31-24, on Saturday afternoon. The Eagles dropped to 4-5 and 2-4 in the ACC, while the Seminoles improved to 4-5 and 2-4 in the league.
​
Mason McKenzie
finished 15-for-27, throwing for 166 yards, two touchdowns, and rushing for 38 yards.
Bo MacCormack III
ran for 105 yards and a touchdown on 16 carries. MacCormack,
Kaelan Chudzinski
, and
Javarius Green
each had three catches, leading the pass-catchers.
Nedrick Boldin Jr.
led the team with 49 receiving yards on two catches and a touchdown.
Bodie Kahoun
led the defense with nine tackles.
Marcus Upton
recorded his first-career interception halfway through the third quarter while
Charlie Comella
recorded a career high eight tackles.
​The Eagles scored on the opening drive when MacCormack caught a 6-yard pass to cap off a 13-play, 75-yard drive. McKenzie was perfect on 3-for-3 passing on third down to keep Boston College's drive alive.
​After forcing Florida State into a three-and-out, the Eagles made it a two-possession game when
Luca Lombardo
knocked in a 48-yard field goal.
​The Seminoles cut it to 10-7 a minute into the second quarter when Ashton Daniels scored on a quarterback keeper from four yards out.
​Boston College reestablished the 10-point lead with 9:24 to go in the second quarter when Boldin Jr. caught a 29-yard screen pass and ran it in for his second career touchdown.
​Florida State responded with an eight-play, 75-yard drive capped by a 20-yard rushing touchdown to cut the deficit to 17-14 with six minutes to go in the first half.
​The Seminoles took their first lead of the game with 51 seconds to go in the half when they ran in a fake field goal attempt from a yard out.
​The Eagles forced their first turnover of the afternoon when
Max Tucker
tipped a pass from Daniels, and Upton intercepted it. BC then went 51 yards in six plays to take a 24-21 lead with four minutes left in the third quarter when McKenzie found
Javarius Green
from two yards out.
​Florida State evened the game at 24 with 13:22 to go in the fourth quarter with a 22-yard field goal following a 14-play, 71-yard drive. They then took the lead with 2:29 to go on an 11-yard touchdown run.
​The Eagles travel to Notre Dame next Saturday for their non-conference finale at Notre Dame Stadium. Kickoff is set for 3:30 p.m. on NBC.
