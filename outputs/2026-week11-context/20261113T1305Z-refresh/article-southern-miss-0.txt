Southern Miss Falls at Home Thursday Night to Sun Belt East Foe James Madison

HATTIESBURG, Miss. --
Southern Miss rallied from an early 10-point deficit and briefly took the lead before James Madison dominated the second half for a 54-23 victory Thursday night at Renasant Stadium in Sun Belt Conference action.
The Golden Eagles had a two-game winning streak snapped, falling to 3-6 overall and 2-3 in league play, while the Dukes improved to 8-2 and 5-2.
The Golden Eagles provided their strongest stretch late in the first half, scoring two touchdowns to move in front 14-13 with 37 seconds remaining. Quarterback
John White
, who was making his first career start at QB for the Golden Eagles, capped an 11-play, 53-yard drive with a 3-yard touchdown run early in the second quarter, and
Steven Robinson's
2-yard touchdown run completed a 10-play, 82-yard march just before halftime.
But Southern Miss could not protect the advantage. James Madison moved 53 yards in 29 seconds after Robinson's score, setting up Morgan Suarez's 40-yard field goal as time expired to give the Dukes a 16-14 halftime lead.
James Madison seized control after the break, scoring touchdowns on its first two possessions of the third quarter. JC Evans ran 16 yards for a score, then added a 2-yard touchdown run to extend the lead to 30-14. The Dukes added three more touchdowns in the fourth quarter, turning a competitive game into a lopsided final.
White completed 22 of 35 passes for 266 yards and a touchdown, connecting often with
Kaden Saunders
and
Mario Sanders
. Saunders caught six passes for 122 yards, highlighted by a 45-yard reception in the fourth quarter. Sanders had six catches for 98 yards and caught a 34-yard touchdown pass from White with 9:10 left, though the ensuing two-point attempt failed.
Robinson led the Golden Eagles on the ground with 54 yards on 15 carries and a touchdown.
Jamarice Wilder
added 42 rushing yards. Linebacker
Mathis Haygood
led Southern Miss with 13 tackles.
Evans accounted for four touchdowns for James Madison, running for three and passing for another. He completed 19 of 26 passes for 251 yards, while Corey Scott caught six passes for 138 yards. James Madison's 24-point fourth quarter sealed the outcome before 24,182 fans.
Southern Miss showed it could move the ball through the air and answer adversity before halftime, but the Dukes' second-half efficiency and pass rush proved decisive.
The Golden Eagles return to action next Saturday, Nov. 14, when they travel to Ruston to face league foe Louisiana Tech. Game time is set for 4 p.m.
