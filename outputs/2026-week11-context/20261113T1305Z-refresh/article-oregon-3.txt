NFL #ProDucks: Week 9
11/12/26
|
Football
Share:
By:
Drew Tinson
From DeForest Buckner's debut in new threads to Juwan Johnson's overtime touchdown, Week 9 delivered plenty of memorable moments for former Ducks. Bucky Irving kept rolling on the ground, while Evan Williams added another interception to his season total. Here's a look at how our #ProDucks performed around the NFL.
Chicago Bears 27, Tampa Bay Buccaneers 24
Dillon Thieneman
recorded five tackles in the Bears' win over Tampa Bay. He ranks seventh among all rookies and first among rookie defensive backs in tackles.
Irving continues to make his case as one of the top running backs in the league with another stellar performance. Irving was the engine for the Bucs offense, rushing for 97 yards and two touchdowns, while catching four passes for 28 yards. Irving has been on a tear this past month, rushing for 419 yards and five touchdowns, and catching seven passes for 35 yards. Since Week 5 of the NFL season,
Tez Johnson has proven to be a big-play threat for the Tampa Bay offense, hauling in two receptions for 76 yards. Johnson's 38 yards per reception led all NFL receivers in Week 9.

New Orleans Saints 23, Cleveland Browns 22
Johnson finished with two receptions for 34 yards, including a clutch go-ahead touchdown catch in overtime. The Saints turned to Johnson on third down, and he delivered, helping secure an overtime victory over the Browns.
Green Bay Packers 45, New England Patriots 27
Fresh off being named NFC Defensive Player of the Month for October, Williams recorded five tackles, one pass defended and an interception as part of a Green Bay defensive effort that forced four turnovers against the Patriots. Williams' latest interception moved him into a tie for third in the NFL in interceptions this season.
Christian Gonzalez finished with 10 tackles for the Patriots, marking the first double-digit tackle performance of his NFL career.
Detroit Lions 19, Miami Dolphins 9
Penei Sewell continues to prove he is one of the NFL's premier offensive linemen, delivering another elite performance in which he allowed no pressures, sacks or quarterback hits. Sewell earned an overall grade of 83.6 and a pass-blocking grade of 89.8, according to Pro Football Focus (PFF), which ranked him as the NFL's top offensive tackle for the seventh consecutive week. The Lions' four-game winning streak is tied for the second-longest active streak in the NFL.
Denver Broncos 26, Carolina Panthers 21
Bo Nix and the Broncos tacked on their 5th consecutive win, sitting atop the NFL's active win streak leaderboard. Nix completed 25-of-37 passes for 237 yards and two touchdowns. Troy Franklin and Alex Forsyth both played for the Broncos in the win, Franklin hauled in three receptions for 31 yards, and Forsyth saw action on special teams.
San Francisco 49ers 38, Las Vegas Raiders 17
After a trade sent him to San Francisco two weeks ago, Buckner suited up for the 49ers for the first time. He made one tackle and heavily affected the pocket, finishing with two quarterback hits. Deommodore Lenoir also saw action for the 49ers defense, finishing with three tackles, including one for loss before being sidelined with a foot injury in the second half. Jordan James rushed for 11 yards on 3 attempts and returned two kicks for 31 yards.
Jackson Powers-Johnson saw action on special teams for the Raiders.
New York Giants 19, Philadelphia Eagles 11
Jevon Holland, Kayvon Thibodeaux, and Nikko Reed all contributed for the Giants defensive effort in the win over their NFC East rival. Holland and Thibodeaux each made four tackles, while Reed had one.
Johnny Mundt saw action at tight end for the Eagles.
Jacksonville Jaguars 33, Baltimore Ravens 10
Arik Armstead and
Emmanuel Pregnon
both started for the Jaguars in the win. Armstead made two tackles and Pregnon contributed on the offensive line.
Los Angeles Rams 20, Washington Commanders 13
Josh Conerly Jr. contributed for the Commanders, starting on the offensive line.
Kansas City Chiefs 35, New York Jets 12
Jeffrey Bassa has become a consistent contributor for the Chiefs special teams unit, recovering a fumble on the opening kickoff, setting the tone for the matchup. Bassa added two tackles in the win.
Jadon Canady
also saw action for Kansas City, finishing with one tackle.
Kenyon Sadiq
has become a consistent target for the Jets, catching all four of his targets for 49 yards. Sadiq has also emerged as a blocker, recording a 71.0 grade in pass-blocking scenarios, according to PFF.
Houston Texans 44, Los Angeles Chargers 22
Justin Herbert completed 13-of-23 passes for 193 yards and a touchdown, adding seven yards on one carry.
Alex Harkey
saw action at right guard for the Bolts, allowing no sacks or quarterback hits.
Jamaree Caldwell and Troy Dye both contributed for the Chargers on the defensive side of the ball. Caldwell finished with one tackle.
Dallas Cowboys 27, Indianapolis Colts 23
T.J. Bass played on special teams for the Cowboys in the win.
Bryce Boettcher
recorded a career-high 12 tackles, reaching double-digit tackles for the first time in his career.
Cincinnati Bengals 34, Atlanta Falcons 17
Brandon Dorlus made two tackles for the Falcons.
Seattle Seahawks 17, Arizona Cardinals 3
Jordan Burch finished with two tackles for the Cardinals.
