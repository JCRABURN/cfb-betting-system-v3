Easton Messer Named Jet Award Finalist
11/11/2026 6:52:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
BOCA RATON, Fla
. – Florida Atlantic football's fifth-year WR/PR
Easton Messer
has been named a finalist for the 16th annual Jet Award, which recognizes the most outstanding return specialist in college football, announced the Jet Foundation on Wednesday.
The five finalists were chosen by a vote of the Jet Award media panel from a list of 10 semifinalists in an effort to honor the electrifying talents of players who excel on punt and kickoff returns.
Messer earns the honor in the midst of a season in which he is currently tied for the FBS lead with two punt returns taken back for a touchdown.
This year's finalists include four punt returners and one kickoff returner, representing the SEC, Big Ten, Pac-12, American Conference and the Sun Belt. The winner of the 2026 Jet Award will be selected by a distinguished panel of voters including media members from the Football Writers Association of America (FWAA), former winners of the Jet Award, and two of the esteemed founders Johnny Rodgers and William Reed.
Adding to the growing accolades for his 2026 campaign, Messer is also a Burlsworth Trophy semifinalist and on the watch lists for both Biletnikoff and Paul Hornung Awards.
2026 JET AWARD FINALIST
(listed alphabetically)
:
Jaylon Hawkins
, WR, R-Fr, San Diego State, KR (20 kickoff returns for 655 yards, 32.7 avg, 3 TDs; leads the FBS in kickoff return yards; Pac-12 Special Teams Player of the Week)
Easton Messer
, WR, Sr, Florida Atlantic, PR (11 punt returns for 247 yards, 22.4 avg, 2 TDs, including a school-record 82-yard return vs. Rice; two-time American Conference Special Teams Player of the Week)
Michael Scott,
WR, Soph, James Madison, PR (18 punt returns for 287 yards, 15.9 avg, 2 TDs; also 14 kickoff returns for 507 yards, 36.2 avg)
Vicari Swain
, CB, Jr, South Carolina, PR (18 punt returns for 231 yards, 12.8 avg, 2 TDs; South Carolina's career leader in punt return TDs; SEC Special Teams Player of the Week)
Javon Tracy
, WR, Sr, Minnesota, PR (21 punt returns for 347 yards, 16.5 avg, 2 TDs, both in one game vs. Eastern Illinois, a Minnesota first; Big Ten Special Teams Player of the Week)
The award, named in honor of Heisman Trophy winner and College Football Hall of Famer Johnny "The Jet" Rodgers, will culminate with the presentation at the Jet Award Gala on April 8, 2027. Last year's recipient, Kaden Wetjen from Iowa, a two-time winner, epitomized the skill and impact recognized by the Jet Award.
About the Jet Award Foundation:
The Jet Award Foundation raises money to provide scholarships to students majoring in the trades at Metro Community College and Iowa Western Community College. Since 2017 more than 500 students have been awarded aid.
