Cowboys in the NFL: Week Nine
November 11, 2026
|
Cowboy Football
Share:
STILLWATER -
Former Oklahoma State players had a busy Week 9, led by a 65-yard touchdown run from Justice Hill. Jaylen Warren and the Pittsburgh Steelers were on a bye.
Hill ran nine times for 85 yards and a touchdown and caught three passes for 7 yards in the Baltimore Ravens' 33-10 loss to the Jacksonville Jaguars on Thursday, Nov. 5. His 65-yard run came 11 seconds after a Jacksonville touchdown. It was Baltimore's first touchdown after halftime since Week 5, and it snapped the Jaguars' 24-game streak without allowing a 75-yard rusher. Hill also returned two kickoffs for 45 yards. Baltimore fell to 4-5 and hosts the Los Angeles Chargers on Monday, Nov. 16.
65-yard touchdown for Justice Hill! Tune in on Prime. https://t.co/kvYH0fyt7X
— Baltimore Ravens (@Ravens)
November 6, 2026
Chuba Hubbard rushed 16 times for 79 yards in the Carolina Panthers' 26-21 loss to the Denver Broncos on Sunday, Nov. 8. Carolina fell to 3-5 and visits the New Orleans Saints on Sunday, Nov. 15.
Malcolm Rodriguez led the Detroit Lions with eight tackles, two of them solo, in a 19-9 win over the Miami Dolphins. Tyler Lacy added two tackles, and Tay Martin caught an 11-yard pass from punter Jack Fox on a second-quarter fake punt that gave Detroit the ball at its own 41. Martin also added an assisted tackle. The Lions won their fourth straight game to improve to 6-2 and face the New England Patriots in Munich, Germany, on Nov. 15.
Ollie Gordon II carried 16 times for 55 yards and caught one pass for 9 yards in the Dolphins' loss. The Dolphins visit the Indianapolis Colts on Nov. 15.
Tylan Wallace caught one pass for 6 yards in the Cleveland Browns' 23-22 overtime loss to the Saints. The Browns host the Houston Texans on Nov. 15.
The San Francisco 49ers, who have linebacker Nick Martin, beat the Las Vegas Raiders 38-17 to improve to 7-1.
