Riley Named Finalist for Paul Hornung Award
11/12/2026 10:00:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
BOISE, Idaho –
Boise State running back
Dylan Riley
was picked a finalist for the Paul Hornung Award, the Louisville Sports Commission announced. The junior is one of seven finalists for the award, which is presented annually to the most versatile player in college football. Riley is the first player in program history to be a finalist for the award.
Through nine games, Riley leads the FBS with 1,241 rushing yards and 1,547 all-purpose yards. His averages of 137.9 rushing yards and 171.9 all-purpose yards per game are also the most in the country. He has 13 total touchdowns, running for 11 and catching two while tallying a nation-high four touchdowns of 70+ yards. The Rancho Verde HS product is a semifinalist for the Maxwell Award and was featured on the Associated Press and Sports Illustrated Midseason All-America second teams.
Riley has been featured on the Paul Hornung Award Honor Roll three times, first after his performance at Fresno State, where he totaled 227 all-purpose yards and two touchdowns. He was selected again after notching 158 all-purpose yards and two touchdowns at Washington State. His most recent selection came following the Colorado State win, where he compiled 300 all-purpose yards, one shy of the program record, and three touchdowns. For his efforts, he was named the Paul Hornung Award and Pac-12 Offensive Player of the Week. He also picked up Doak Walker National Running Back and Pac-12 Offensive Player of the Week after the home-opener win over Memphis, where he ran for a career-high 206 yards on 19 carries.
No. 17/18/24 Boise State (8-1, 5-0 Pac-12) takes its eight-game win streak into a conference showdown with Oregon State (5-4, 3-2 Pac-12) on Nov. 14. The Broncos and Beavers meet on The Blue at 4 p.m., with the game televised on USA Sports. That morning, Boise State will host ESPN's College GameDay on DeChevrieux Field. Entry for fans is free and parking begins at 2 a.m. Gates open at 4:30 a.m. and live television hits start at approximately 5:40 a.m. The famed pregame show airs live from 7-10 a.m. on ESPN, ESPNU, the ESPN App and Disney+.
