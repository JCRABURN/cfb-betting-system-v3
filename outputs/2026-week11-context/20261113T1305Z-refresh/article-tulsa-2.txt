TULSA HOSTS FLORIDA ATLANTIC FOR HOMECOMING
• Tulsa hosts Florida Atlantic for Homecoming on Saturday, Nov. 14, at H.A. Chapman Stadium. Kickoff is set for 2:30 p.m. CT on ESPN+. It will be the 101st Homecoming game in program history.
• The Golden Hurricane enters Saturday with a 55-42-3 all-time Homecoming record. Tulsa played its first Homecoming game in 1926, defeating Arkansas 14-7 at McNulty Park.
• Saturday marks the first time Florida Atlantic has been Tulsa's Homecoming opponent. The Owls' two previous visits to H.A. Chapman Stadium were Tulsa's 47-44 overtime win in the 2015 season opener and Florida Atlantic's win in the 2024 regular-season finale.
•Tulsa's most recent Homecoming victory came on Oct. 26, 2024, when the Golden Hurricane rallied from a 28-point deficit to defeat UTSA 46-45. Tulsa trailed by 18 entering the fourth quarter.
•The last three Homecoming games have all been decided by seven points or fewer. Tulsa fell to Charlotte 33-26 in overtime in 2023, edged UTSA by one point in 2024 and lost to Temple 38-37 in overtime on Oct. 25, 2025.
•Florida Atlantic leads the overall series 4-1, but the teams have split their two meetings in Tulsa. A Homecoming win would give the Golden Hurricane its sixth victory of the season.
THOMPSON'S COMEBACK EARNS NATIONAL RECOGNITION
• Graduate linebacker
Chris Thompson Jr.
was named to the 38-player Comeback Player of the Year Award Midseason Watch List on Nov. 2. He is one of three American Conference players on the list, joining North Texas running back Jahiem White and Tulane linebacker Dickson Agu.
• After missing the entire 2025 season because of injury, Thompson leads the Golden Hurricane with 84 tackles through nine games, averaging 9.3 tackles per contest.
• Thompson is tied for second in the American Conference in both total tackles and tackles per game. He is tied for 17th nationally in total tackles and tied for 16th in tackles per game.
• Thompson recorded three tackles, including 1.5 tackles for loss, in Tulsa's 60-10 win at Tulane. He enters the Florida Atlantic game with 5.0 tackles for loss, one sack, one fumble recovery and one pass breakup.
• He recorded at least seven tackles in each of Tulsa's first eight games and has four double-digit performances. His season high of 15 tackles came against Army, when he also recorded two tackles for loss and his first sack of the season. He posted 14 tackles against North Texas and 10 each against Oklahoma State and Rice.
• Thompson earned American Conference Defensive Player of the Week honors after recording 10 tackles and recovering a fumble in Tulsa's season-opening 24-10 win over Oklahoma State. He also helped the Golden Hurricane make two fourth-down stops.
• A team captain in both 2025 and 2026, Thompson has returned from injury to lead Tulsa in tackles as the Golden Hurricane enters its Homecoming game one win shy of bowl eligibility.
• College Sports Communicators presents the award in association with The Associated Press and Fiesta Sports Foundation. The award recognizes college football student-athletes who overcome injury, illness or other circumstances, and three winners will be honored following the season.
ROSS EXTENDS HIS STREAK TO 28
• Wide receiver
Javon Ross
has caught a pass in 28 consecutive games, a streak that spans his time at Bethune-Cookman and Tulsa.
• Ross extended the streak with a 32-yard reception in Tulsa's 60-10 win at Tulane. He also scored on an 11-yard run, giving him a rushing touchdown in back-to-back games.
• Ross has caught a pass in all nine games with the Golden Hurricane, totaling 29 receptions for 359 yards and three receiving touchdowns.
• Over his last four games, Ross has totaled 16 catches for 237 yards, highlighted by season highs of seven receptions and 90 yards at Navy.
• Ross has scored in three consecutive games. He caught an 11-yard touchdown pass at Rice, scored through the air and on the ground against Army and added a rushing touchdown at Tulane.
• Ross will look to extend the streak to 29 games when Tulsa hosts Florida Atlantic for Homecoming on Nov. 14.
• His streak is tied for 10th nationally with UTSA's David Amador II with 25 or more consecutive games with a reception.

TULSA'S OFFENSE PICKS UP THE PACE IN CONFERENCE PLAY
• Tulsa has scored 203 points in five American Conference games, averaging 40.6 points per game to rank fourth in league play through Nov. 7. That's up from 23.8 points per game during the Golden Hurricane's four-game nonconference schedule.
• The Golden Hurricane has scored at least 35 points in four of its five conference games: 44 against North Texas, 38 at Rice, 35 against Army and 60 at Tulane.
• Tulsa is averaging 420.2 yards of total offense against conference opponents, up from 354.8 in nonconference play. The Golden Hurricane gained 562 yards at Tulane, pairing 338 passing yards with 224 rushing yards and scoring four touchdowns each through the air and on the ground.
•
Baylor Hayes
has passed for at least 200 yards in all five league games, averaging 265.6 passing yards and throwing 13 touchdown passes. He set career highs with 338 passing yards and four touchdown passes at Tulane.
•
Oran Singleton Jr.
has averaged 8.2 receptions and 102.0 receiving yards in conference play, totaling 41 catches for 510 yards. His 13-catch, 177-yard performance at Tulane included three touchdown receptions.
• Tulsa leads the American Conference in red-zone touchdown rate in league games, reaching the end zone on 23 of 29 trips (79.3 percent). The Golden Hurricane has scored on 27 of those 29 trips, including seven of seven at Tulane.
• Tulsa's 60 points at Tulane were its most under head coach
Tre Lamb
. The Golden Hurricane returns home to face Florida Atlantic on Nov. 14, looking to carry that production into Homecoming.
SINGLETON Jr.  EARNS BILETNIKOFF RECOGNITION
• Wide receiver
Oran Singleton Jr.
was added to the 2026 Biletnikoff Award Watch List on Monday, Nov. 9, earning national recognition following his standout performance at Tulane.
• Presented by the Tallahassee Quarterback Club Foundation, the Biletnikoff Award recognizes college football's outstanding FBS receiver, regardless of position.
• Singleton caught 13 passes for 177 yards and three touchdowns in Tulsa's 60-10 victory at Tulane, establishing career highs in receiving yards and touchdown receptions.
• His 13 receptions set a Yulman Stadium single-game record. Singleton accounted for half of Tulsa's 26 completions and more than half of its 338 passing yards.
• Singleton caught three of
Baylor Hayes'
four touchdown passes as the Golden Hurricane recorded its highest scoring total under head coach
Tre Lamb
and its most points in an American Conference game.
• His performance helped Tulsa finish with 562 yards of total offense, including 338 passing and 224 rushing, in the Golden Hurricane's largest victory over Tulane.
• Singleton joins the award's watch list as a midseason addition, with receivers eligible to be added throughout the season based on their performances. The Biletnikoff Award semifinalists will be announced Nov. 16, followed by three finalists Nov. 24.
HAYES SETS CAREER HIGHS IN WIN AT TULANE
•
Baylor Hayes
completed 26-of-33 passes for career highs of 338 yards and four touchdowns without an interception in Tulsa's 60-10 victory at Tulane.
• Hayes completed 78.8 percent of his passes, his best mark this season, and averaged 10.2 yards per attempt. He added 16 rushing yards to finish with 354 yards of total offense.
• He became Tulsa's first 300-yard passer since Cooper Legas threw for 333 yards against UTSA on Oct. 26, 2024. This was his first game of his career throwing for over 300 yards.
• Hayes threw three of his four touchdown passes before halftime, helping Tulsa build a 32-0 lead. He found
Oran Singleton Jr.
twice and
Jimmy Calloway
once in the first half before connecting with Singleton again for a 27-yard touchdown in the third quarter.
• Hayes has passed for at least 200 yards in all five American Conference games: 211 against North Texas, 268 at Navy, 249 at Rice, 262 against Army and 338 at Tulane.
• In conference play, Hayes has completed 114-of-172 passes (66.3 percent) for 1,328 yards and 13 touchdowns. He is averaging 265.6 passing yards per league game.
• Hayes has thrown multiple touchdown passes in four of Tulsa's five conference games, including three each against North Texas and Rice and four at Tulane. He also ran for two scores against North Texas, giving him 15 touchdowns accounted for in league play.
• The Golden Hurricane has scored at least 35 points in four of its five conference games and is averaging 40.6 points per game in league play.
• Hayes enters the Florida Atlantic game with 1,681 passing yards, 14 touchdowns and two interceptions in seven appearances. He has completed 65.3 percent of his passes and topped 200 yards in six of those seven games.
SINGLETON SETS YULMAN STADIUM RECORD
•
Oran Singleton Jr.
set a Yulman Stadium single-game record with 13 receptions in Tulsa's 60-10 win at Tulane. He established career highs with 13 catches and 177 receiving yards and caught three touchdown passes.
• The 177 yards receiving are the most by a Tulsa reciever since Joseph Williams went for 158 yards on Nov. 14, 2024 against East Carolina.
• The receiving touchdowns are also the most by a TU player since Williams had three in the 2024 game against ECU. He is one of 12 Tulsa players since 1997 to record three receving touchdowns in a single game.
• Singleton Jr. moved the chains with three first-down catches on Tulsa's opening possession, then capped the 75-yard drive with a 3-yard touchdown reception.
• He added another 3-yard touchdown catch just before halftime and opened Tulsa's second-half scoring with a 27-yard touchdown reception.
• Singleton Jr. accounted for exactly half of Tulsa's 26 completions and more than half of its 338 passing yards against the Green Wave.
• The performance marked his second 100-yard receiving game of the season, following an 11-catch, 104-yard outing against North Texas.
• Singleton has caught at least five passes in six consecutive games. In five American Conference games, he has totaled 41 catches for 510 yards, averaging 8.2 receptions and 102.0 receiving yards per game.
•  Singleton enters the Florida Atlantic game leading the Golden Hurricane with 56 receptions, 712 receiving yards and seven touchdown catches through nine games.
SACKS AND TAKEAWAYS
• Tulsa recorded six sacks for 40 yards and intercepted two passes in its 60-10 victory at Tulane. The Golden Hurricane held the Green Wave scoreless through three quarters while building a 46-0 lead.
• Tulsa limited Tulane to 204 total yards, including 112 through the air and 92 on the ground. The Green Wave converted just two of 12 third-down attempts and one of three fourth-down attempts.
•
Thomas Davis
finished with five tackles, 1.5 sacks and a forced fumble. He shared a sack with
Jaylen George
on Tulane's opening possession of the second half, helping Tulsa force a three-and-out.
•
Kyran Duhon
recorded his team-leading seventh sack of the season, while Davis increased his total to 5.5. Duhon is tied for 14th nationally in total sacks through games of Nov. 7.
• The Golden Hurricane has 24 sacks through nine games, tied with East Carolina for the American Conference lead. Tulsa has already surpassed its 2025 total of 23 and is averaging 2.67 sacks per game, up from 1.92 last season.
•
JD Drew
and
NuNu Campbell
each intercepted a pass, and Tulsa turned both takeaways into touchdowns. Drew's second-quarter interception set up
Javon Ross'
11-yard touchdown run, while Campbell's fourth-quarter interception led to
Sevion Morrison's
3-yard score.
• Tulsa has generated 17 takeaways this season, with nine interceptions and eight fumble recoveries. Through nine games, the Golden Hurricane has already exceeded its entire 2025 takeaway total by six.
•The Golden Hurricane committed no turnovers at Tulane and has just five giveaways through nine games. Tulsa leads the American Conference with a plus-12 turnover margin and ranks fourth nationally at plus-1.33 per game.
TULSA RANKS IN THE NATIONAL TOP 10 IN RED-ZONE TOUCHDOWN RATE
• Tulsa scored on all seven red-zone trips in its 60-10 win at Tulane, finishing six with touchdowns and one with a field goal.
• The Golden Hurricane has scored touchdowns on 27 of 35 red-zone trips this season. Its 77.1 percent touchdown rate ranks ninth nationally.
• Including four field goals, Tulsa has scored on 31 of 35 red-zone trips. Its 88.6 percent overall scoring rate ranks fourth in the American Conference and is tied for 42nd nationally.
• Tulsa's red-zone touchdown rate has climbed from 54.8 percent in 2025 to 77.1 percent this season. Through nine games, the Golden Hurricane has already scored four more red-zone touchdowns than it did all of last season.
• Tulsa has scored 14 rushing touchdowns and 13 passing touchdowns in the red zone this season, with no interceptions or lost fumbles on those 35 trips.

RECRUIT LOCAL
• Tulsa's 2026 roster features 27 players from the Tulsa metropolitan area, demonstrating the Golden Hurricane's commitment to recruiting and retaining local talent.
• The group includes 17 players whose listed hometown is Tulsa, four from Owasso, three from Bixby and one each from Collinsville, Jenks and Mannford.
• Local players represent every phase of the roster, with Tulsa-area talent appearing on offense, defense and special teams.
• The group includes players from several of the area's most successful high school programs, including Bixby, Booker T. Washington, Cascia Hall, Collinsville, Holland Hall, Jenks, Metro Christian, Owasso, Rejoice Christian and Union.
• Tulsa's local contingent includes returning Golden Hurricane players as well as student-athletes who returned home after beginning their collegiate careers elsewhere.
• Head coach
Tre Lamb
and his staff continue to prioritize relationships with area high school coaches while providing local players an opportunity to compete at the FBS level in their hometown region.
WHAT YOU DIDN'T KNOW ABOUT TULSA'S FOOTBALL TRADITION
•
New Year's Day Bowl Streak:
Tulsa was the first school to play in five consecutive New Year's Day bowl games: Sun Bowl (1942), Sugar Bowl (1943, 1944), Orange Bowl (1945), and Oil Bowl (1946).
•
Ellis Jones:
An All-American guard in 1945, Jones played with one arm and was a standout on the College All-Star team vs. the Chicago Bears.
• Pro Football Hall of Fame:
Four former Tulsa players are enshrined: Bob St. Clair, Jim Finks, Steve Largent, and Drew Pearson.
•
Heisman Runners-Up:
Jerry Rhome (1964) and Howard Twilley (1965) finished second in Heisman Trophy voting.
•
Glenn Dobbs (1940–42):
Considered Tulsa's greatest player, Dobbs also coached the Hurricane in the 1960s. Eighth Street near H.A. Chapman Stadium is Glenn Dobbs Drive, dedicated in 2000. A statue of Dobbs was dedicated before the 2013 spring game.
•
Zaven Collins (2020):
Consensus All-America linebacker who won the Chuck Bednarik and Bronko Nagurski awards as college football's top defender.
•
NFL Head Coaches:
Four former Tulsa players have served as NFL head coaches: Tommy Hudspeth (Detroit Lions), Sam Rutigliano (Cleveland Browns), Ray Rhodes (Philadelphia Eagles/Green Bay Packers), and Lovie Smith (Chicago Bears/Tampa Bay Buccaneers/Houston Texans).
•
College Football Hall of Fame:
Tulsa has three former players, two former head coaches, and two former assistant coaches inducted:
• Players:
Glenn Dobbs (1940–42, 1961–68), Howard Twilley (1963–65), Jerry Rhome (1963–64)
•
Head Coaches:
Francis Schmidt (1919–21), John Cooper (1977–84)
•
Assistant Coaches:
Darrell Royal (1951), Sammy Baugh (1963)
