Smith Named Semifinalist for Walter Camp Award
11/11/2026 11:58:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
This marks the second consecutive year that junior wide receiver Jeremiah Smith has been named a semifinalist for the Walter Camp Award given annually to the collegiate American football player of the year.
By:
Andrew Kovar
COLUMBUS, Ohio –
Ohio State junior wide receiver
Jeremiah Smith
was named a 2026 Walter Camp Award Semifinalist, as announced Wednesday afternoon.
The Walter Camp Award is given annually to the collegiate American football player of the year. Given out since 1967, the Walter Camp Award is the fourth-oldest college football award in the nation.
This marks the second consecutive year that Smith has been named a semifinalist for the Walter Camp Award.
Additionally, this is the second national award that Smith has been
named a semifinalist this week as he was also a Maxwell Award Semifinalist
on Tuesday.
Ohio State and the Walter Camp Award
The Walter Camp Player of the Year will be voted on by the 138 Football Bowl Subdivision head coaches and sports information directors later this month.
Five finalists will be announced later this month and the 60th recipient of the Walter Camp Player of Year award along with the 137th edition of the Walter Camp All-America team – the nation's oldest – will be announced on the ESPN College Football Awards Show on Friday, December 11.
Ohio State is one of four schools nationally to win the Walter Camp Award four or more times:
1974 Archie Griffin – RB
1975 Archie Griffin – RB
1996 Eddie George – RB
2006 Troy Smith – QB
Griffin is one of just three players to win the award twice.
More on
Jeremiah Smith
Entering Week 11 of the 2026 season, Smith has 69 receptions for 1,200 yards and 14 touchdowns, averaging 17.4 yards per catch and 133.3 receiving yards per game.
Smith leads the Big Ten in all five categories. Nationally, he leads the nation in touchdown receptions, ranks second in receiving yards, and receiving yards per game, and he leads all Power 4 receivers in each of those three categories.
Smith's production this season includes the two highest receiving yardage totals of his career: 217 yards against Illinois on Sept. 26 and 206 yards at No. 14 Iowa on Oct. 3. He also recorded 151 receiving yards against both Ball State and No. 4 Texas.
Following the victory over Illinois, Smith was the consensus National Player of the Week, being named AP, Walter Camp and Maxwell Player of the Week. Additionally, Smith has won Big Ten Offensive Player of the Week twice this season after his performances against Illinois and Iowa.
His performance has earned midseason All-America recognition from The Associated Press, The Athletic, On3 and Sports Illustrated. He also was named to the preseason watch lists for the Maxwell, Walter Camp and Biletnikoff awards and received Big Ten preseason honors.
Smith goes into Saturday as the program's all-time leader in receptions, receiving yards, receiving touchdowns and the Big Ten career receiving touchdowns leader. Smith is also encroaching on the Big Ten career receiving yards.
Smith is the only Ohio State receiver and became the second receiver in Big Ten history to have three 1,000-yd receiving seasons, joining Braylon Edwards (Michigan).
