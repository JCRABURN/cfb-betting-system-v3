GAME NOTES
Saturday will be the fourth meeting with Washington State and the second time playing in Pullman.
The Rams have lost the last three games in the series, including the last time in Pullman in 2022.
Colorado State won the first meeting in 2013 in Albuquerque, N.M., in the New Mexico Bowl.
CSU will be looking for its first road win of the season and the first since 2024 at Nevada - nine consecutive games.
The game will be the latest the Rams' have played all season, kicking at 8:30 p.m., MT.
The Rams will bring the nations best red zone offense to the Palouse, scoring a touchdown on 82.9% of their chances -- 35 trips and 31 scores with 29 touchdowns.
