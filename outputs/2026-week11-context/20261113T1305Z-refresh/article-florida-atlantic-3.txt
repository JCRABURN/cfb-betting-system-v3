11/11/2026 10:15:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
BOCA RATON, Fla
. – Inside the Owls' Burrow, the official weekly television show for Florida Atlantic Athletics, will air episode 13 of season 11 this week on Thursday, Nov. 12. Every 30-minute show this season will air on The Spot stations in the South Florida market.
​
The Spot, South Florida 39 (WSFL-TV), will air the show weekly on Thursdays at 5:30 p.m. ET in the Miami-Ft. Lauderdale DMA with replays running to be determined. The Spot, South Florida 9 (WHDT-TV), will air the show on Thursday at 6 p.m. ET in the West Palm Beach-Fort Pierce DMA with replays to be determined.
Inside the Owls' Burrow is hosted and produced by
Frank Forte
and covers FAU sports, featuring player profiles, coach interviews and behind-the-scenes access to all varsity teams. Inside the Owls' Burrow is produced in collaboration with Florida Atlantic Sports Properties (Playfly Sports).
Forte will review last week's victory over RV UTSA with Hagerty Family Head Football Coach
Zach Kittley
. Kittley and company dealt the Roadrunners their first conference loss of 2026 and will look to deal another loss this week when the Owls travel to Tulsa, Oklahoma, to face the Golden Hurricane at 3:30 ET on Saturday. The contest is the sixth meeting between the American Conference schools, and the two are knotted at 1-1 in Tulsa. The game will air on ESPN+ as well as Fox Sports South Florida 640 AM.
Fifth-year WR
Dominique Henry
will be featured this week in "Pads Off." Plus, Forte will catch up with former Owl DL
Johnnie Sloan
, who is the head football coach at Coconut Creek High School.
Those outside of the Miami and West Palm TV markets can watch the show on FAUSports.com or the FAU Athletics YouTube channel.
