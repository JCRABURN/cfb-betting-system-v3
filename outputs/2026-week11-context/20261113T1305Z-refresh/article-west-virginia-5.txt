Kansas Game to be Televised on ESPNU at 5:30 p.m., ET
November 08, 2026 10:48 AM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Mike Montoro
MORGANTOWN, W.Va. –
The Big 12 Conference has announced that WVU's Nov. 14 Big 12 Conference football game against Kansas at Milan Puskar Stadium will be televised on ESPNU at 5:30 p.m., ET.
Tickets for WVU's final two home games of the season are available for purchase by
CLICKING HERE
.
Saturday, Nov. 14 vs. Kansas
Heroes Day
The 2026 Heroes Day game will be Saturday, Nov. 14, against Kansas. The Mountaineers will honor and pay tribute to former and active duty military members and organizations throughout the day. The game against the Jayhawks also will round out Mountaineer Week celebrations on campus.
Fans also can directly support our heroes as part of the West Virginia Heroes program, presented by the West Virginia Lottery. You can help provide local Veterans, Active Duty Military, First Responders and their families an opportunity to attend a Mountaineer home game! Fans can sponsor a HERO by donating just $20. The West Virginia Lottery will match the first 500 donations. To sponsor a HERO today,
CLICK HERE
.
Military veterans also can access potential tickets through Vet Tix. Vet Tix provides tickets to events for veterans which reduce stress, strengthen family bonds, build life-long memories and encourage service members and veterans to stay engaged with local communities and American life. For potential ticket opportunities,
CLICK HERE
.
