: Saturday, Nov. 14 -- at Oregon (Eugene, Ore.), 12:30 p.m. PT (TV: FS1)
ANN ARBOR, Mich.
-- The University of Michigan football team relinquished the Paul Bunyan Trophy with a 15-6 loss to Michigan State on Saturday (Nov. 7). Playing under the lights at Michigan Stadium, the Wolverines compiled only 192 yards of total offense and converted on just two of their 10 third-down attempts.
Ball control was key, as U-M was outpossessed 38:07 to 21:53, largely due to four offensive turnovers (two interceptions, two fumbles).
The Wolverines finally broke the shutout with 1:35 left in the fourth quarter as
Savion Hiter
punched in a one-yard score set up by a 31-yard catch by
JJ Buchanan
: the longest play from scrimmage by U-M.
Defensively, Michigan fought hard all game despite long stretches on the field, holding MSU to 12 points on four red-zone trips. Six players contributed tackles for loss, including three sacks -- two by
John Henry Daley
and
Smith Snowden
's second sack of the season.
After missing last week due to injury, Daley tied his career high with the two sacks and totaled eight tackles (2.5 for loss).
Troy Bowles
also put up a great performance in the front seven, recording a career-high 15 tackles (one for loss). He entered tonight's contest tied for 14th in the Big Ten in total tackles with 57.
The Maize and Blue defense performed admirably in the first 30 minutes, allowing just 137 total yards and entering halftime down one possession. Daley hounded the Spartans in the first half, recording both of his sacks, including a strip-sack in the second quarter, which forced an MSU punt.
U-M could only muster three drives of more than 25 yards, with only one ending in points, and the other two resulting in a fumble and a missed field goal.
Michigan continues its schedule next Saturday (Nov. 14) against Oregon at Autzen Stadium. The game is scheduled for a 12:30 p.m. PT kickoff on FS1.
