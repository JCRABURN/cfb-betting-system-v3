Ducks Move Up To No. 16 In CFP Rankings
11/10/26
|
Football
Share:
By:
Nate Krueger
EUGENE, Ore. -
The Oregon football team moved up seven spots to No. 16 in the latest edition of the College Football Playoff rankings released on Tuesday evening.
Oregon has now been ranked in each of the last 33 CFP top 25 releases, coming in at No. 23 in the initial 2026 rankings last week.
The Ducks (6-3, 4-2 Big Ten) have played one of the most challenging schedules in the country with five of their first nine opponents currently ranked in the CFP top 25 - No. 4 UCLA, No. 18 USC, No. 20 Ohio State, No. 24 Boise State and No. 25 Oklahoma State.
The Ducks are coming off back-to-back wins over ranked teams, defeating then-No. 20 Northwestern in Eugene before taking down then-No. 12 Ohio State last week in Columbus. Oregon also owns a road win over then-No. 12 USC and a season-opening win over Boise State, which is undefeated since that game and No. 24 in the CFP rankings.
Oregon is one of two teams in the nation, along with No. 3 Alabama, to have multiple double-digit road wins over ranked opponents. The Ducks defeated USC, 41-27, in Los Angeles on Sept. 26, and scored 38 unanswered points en route to a 41-28 win over the Buckeyes in Ohio Stadium. UO is also one of four teams nationally (Georgia, Texas, Ole Miss) with three or more wins over teams ranked at the time of the game.
The Ducks will host Michigan (5-4, 2-4 Big Ten) this Saturday, Nov. 14, in Autzen Stadium at 12:30 p.m. PT (FS1).
