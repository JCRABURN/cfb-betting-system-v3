Eagles travel to Georgia State on Saturday
STATESBORO -
In-state rivals meet Saturday in Atlanta as the Georgia Southern football squad takes hits the road to take on Georgia State in Sun Belt Conference action. Kickoff is set for 3:32 p.m. at Center Parc Stadium and it will be carried on ESPN+. The game will also be available via radio on the Georgia Southern Sports Network. Of Note:
Georgia Southern and Georgia State have met 12 times before with the series tied, 6-6.
Southern won the past three games. The Eagles are 2-2 at Center Parc Stadium against their in-state rival.
Georgia State head coach Dell McGee was an assistant coach at Georgia Southern in 2014 and 2015. He served as the interim head coach for the Eagles in the 2015 GoDaddy Bowl, leading the team to a 58-27 win over Bowling Green.
Georgia Southern has 66 of 118 players on its roster (55.1%) from the state of Georgia.
This will be the third Georgia Grown Bowl between Georgia Southern and Georgia State. The Georgia Department of Agriculture and Georgia Grown has partnered to support each institution to highlight the rivalry and showcase Georgia's No. 1 industry – agriculture. After the game, Georgia Agriculture Commissioner Tyler Harper will present the winner of the match up with the Commissioner's Cup – a symbol of Georgia's excellence in agriculture, education, and athletics.
