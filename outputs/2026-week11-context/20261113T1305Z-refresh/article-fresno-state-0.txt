2026 Bulldogs in the NFL: Week 9
11/10/2026 3:25:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
By:
N'Gai Washington | Strategic Communications Student Intern
FRESNO, Calif. -
Week nine of the NFL season is in the books. Let's take a look at how former Bulldogs did across the League.
DaRon Bland, Dallas Cowboys
Bland had four tackles and one pass breakup in the Cowboys' win over the Colts last weekend.
Week 9 Result:
Dallas Cowboys 27, Indianapolis Colts 23
Week 10 Game:
Sunday (11/15) vs San Francisco 49ers (7-1)
Jake Haener, New York Giants
Haener was added to their practice squad ahead of Week 9, meaning he was not active for the Giants' game against the Philadelphia Eagles
Week 9 Result:
New York Giants 19, Philadelphia Eagles
Week 10 Game:
Thursday (11/12) vs Washington Commanders (2-5-1)
Arron Mosby,  Green Bay Packers
Since Mosby was elevated from the practice squad for the maximum of three allowable consecutive weeks, Green Bay chose to hold him out of the game rather than signing him to the 53-man roster. He returns to Green Bay's practice squad.
Week 9 Result:
Green Bay Packers 45, New England Patriots 27
Week 10 Game:
Sunday (11/15) vs Minnesota Vikings (6-2)
Ronnie Rivers, Los Angeles Rams
Rivers was activated from injured reserve ahead of the game against the Commanders, but he didn't take a snap in his first game back.
Week 9 Result:
Los Angeles Rams 20, Washington Commanders 13
Week 10 Game:
Sunday (11/15) @ Arizona Cardinals (3-6)
Nikko Remigio, Kansas City Chiefs
Remigio, despite being listed as questionable with a hip injury leading up to the matchup, was active on special teams last week. He was active and handled return duties, recording two kickoff returns for 44 yards and two punt returns for eight yards
Week 9 Result
: Kansas City Chiefs 35, New York Jets 12
Week 10 Game
:  Sunday (11/15) @  Atlanta Falcons(3-6)
Jacob Spomer
, Los Angeles Chargers
Spomer was active but did not take any snaps.
Week 9 Result:
Houston Texans 44, Los Angeles Chargers 22
Week 10 Game:
Monday (11/16) @ Baltimore Ravens (4-5)
Evan Williams, Green Bay Packers
Williams had a shining performance he had five tackles, an interception, and one pass swatted away.
Week 9 Result:
Green Bay Packers 45, New England Patriots 27
Week 10 Game:
Sunday (11/15) vs Minnesota Vikings (6-2)
