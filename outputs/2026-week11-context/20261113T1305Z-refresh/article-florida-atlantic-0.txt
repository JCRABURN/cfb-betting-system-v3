Game Notes #10
Share:
By:
Ben Paul
BOCA RATON, Fla.
– Florida Atlantic football is back to its usual Saturday slate and will be on the road this week facing Tulsa on Nov. 14. Kickoff is at 3:30 p.m. ET on ESPN+ and on Fox Sports South Florida 640 AM.
The Owls (6-3, 3-2) and the Golden Hurricane (5-4, 2-3) are both coming off 60-point performances in the previous week. It marks the first time since 2004 that two teams ( Alabama/Arkansas) scored 60+ in the same week and then faced each other the following week. FAU's 60-29 win came against first-place and RV UTSA and clinched the team's first Bowl-eligible season since 2020.
Hagerty Family Head Football Coach
Zach Kittley
's offense, currently top 10 in scoring, is averaging 50.6 points in the last three weeks, thanks in large part to fifth-year WR
Easton Messer's
12 touchdowns. These have come in four different forms: by pass, rush, reception, and punt return, making him the only FBS player this season to have at least one of each.
Messer, a semifinalist for the Burlsworth Trophy and watch list member for the Biletnikoff and Paul Hornung Awards, is No. 1 nationally with 83 receptions and is No. 4 in both receiving yards (983) and touchdown catches (12). The latter is a single-season program record.
Distributing the ball to him is graduate QB
Caden Veltkamp
, currently the FBS leader in completions per game (27.11) and No. 3 in passing touchdowns (27). Messer, along with senior WR
Kelby Valsin
, are the only teammates in the top 10 nationally in receptions per game, with the latter ranking No. 7 (7.33).
The Owls are also coming off one of their best defensive outings of the season, logging six sacks, most in a game since 2023, and 11 tackles for loss, most since 2024. Fifth-year LB
Joseph Sipp Jr.
continues to lead the team with 64 total tackles.
Special teams is led by graduate K
Collin Rogers
, the active FBS leader in career points with 443. The unit's return game remains one of the best in the country, ranked No. 2 in punt returns (21.69) and No. 14 in kickoff returns (26.7), with four touchdowns between the two of them.
FOLLOW THE OWLS
Inside the Owls Burrow airs on The Spot, South Florida 39 (WSFL-TV) weekly on Thursdays at 5:30 p.m. in the Miami-Ft. Lauderdale DMA with replays running to be determined. The Spot, South Florida 9 (WHDT-TV), will air the show on Thursday at 6 p.m. in the West Palm Beach-Fort Pierce DMA with replays to be determined.
The Coaches Radio Show presented by Duffy's Sports Grill, takes place at the Duffy's on St. Andrew's Boulevard in Boca Raton. Fans can join in person or listen live on FOX Sports South Florida from 6 to 7 p.m. This week's guests include Coach Kittley, Offensive Line Coach
Stephen Hamby
, and Head Women's Basketball Coach LeAnn Freeland.
