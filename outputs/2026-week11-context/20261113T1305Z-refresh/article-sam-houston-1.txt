Jax State downs Kats
11/7/2026 10:48:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Share:
JACKSONVILLE, Ala. -
Landyn Locke
and
DJ Bailey
each threw a second-half touchdown pass as Sam Houston fell 47-14 to Jax State on Saturday night at AmFirst Stadium. It was the first game for the Bearkats under co-interim head coaches
Freddie Aughtry-Lindsay
and
Eric Raisbeck
.
Sam Houston (1-8, 0-5 Conference USA) stopped the Gamecocks on fourth down at the Bearkat 9-yard line and held them scoreless in the first quarter. Jax State (7-2, 5-0 CUSA) opened the scoring with 8:35 left in the second quarter and took a 17-0 lead into halftime.
The Gamecocks extended their lead on a 67-yard touchdown pass from Caden Creel to Deondre Johnson on their opening drive of the second half. Locke answered with a 64-yard touchdown pass to
Chris Reed
to cut the lead to 24-7 with 13:43 left in the third quarter. Locke finished 16-of-21 for 126 yards.
Bailey led a 10-play, 75-yard scoring drive in the fourth quarter. He connected with
Grady O'Neill
for 33 yards and found him again on fourth-and-5 for a 7-yard touchdown with 7:21 remaining. Bailey went 5-of-7 for 63 yards.
Sam Houston returns to Bowers Stadium to host Kennesaw State on Nov. 14 at 1 p.m. CT.
QUICK HITTERS
- Locke surpassed 2,500 career passing yards and moved into 20th in program history with 2,544.
- Bailey's touchdown pass in the fourth quarter was the first of his career.
- Reed caught six passes for 80 yards and O'Neill caught six for 61. It was the first game this season in which two Bearkats had at least six receptions.
- Coco Brown led the Kats with a season-high 50 rushing yards on 10 carries.
-
Jace Arnold
recorded six tackles and a sack.
