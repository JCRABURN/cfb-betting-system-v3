Troy Bowles (Michigan Photography)
Postgame Notes: Michigan State 15, Michigan 6
11/7/2026 11:52:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
• Today's game marked the 336th consecutive crowd of 100,000-plus fans at Michigan Stadium. Attendance for today's game was 111,172.
• Today's loss drops U-M to 5-4 on the season and 2-4 in Big Ten play. It also snapped a four-game series win streak for U-M against Michigan State and dropped U-M to 75-39-5 in 119 all-time meetings with the Spartans.
• U-M is now 52-24-3 in the series all-time when the game is played in Ann Arbor and 42-30-2 in 74 contests for the Paul Bunyan Trophy.
•
Savion Hiter
scored the Wolverines' lone points on a one-yard rush for his third touchdown of the season.
• The 13-play, 75-yard scoring drive was highlighted by a 31-yard reception by
JJ Buchanan
to put Michigan on the one-yard line. U-M has at least one scoring drive of 10-plus plays in eight of nine games this year.
•
Troy Bowles
led the defense with a career-high 15 tackles, including six solo stops and one tackle for loss.
• With his first-quarter PBU against the Spartans,
Zeke Berry
reached 27 career pass breakups, tying Jeremy LeSueur (2000-03, 27) for eighth-most all-time at Michigan. James Whitley (1997-2000, 28), Lavert Hill (2016-19, 29) and Charles Woodson (1995-97, 30) are next on the top-10 list.
•
Smith Snowden
had his most productive game as a Wolverine, recording six tackles, including one sack.
•
John Henry Daley
matched his career high with eight tackles and recorded two sacks, including a forced fumble that led to a fourth down.
•
Cam Brown
matched his career long with a 52-yard punt. Brown has punted 41 times this season with 19 punts inside the 20-yard line. He dropped a punt at the Michigan State 1-yard line in the third quarter.
•
Travis Johnson
made his first career start in today's game. Johnson became the 14th Wolverine to make his first collegiate start this season, joining defensive end
Tariq Boney
, tight end
Jalen Hoffman
, linebackers
Troy Bowles
and
Chase Taylor
and defensive end
Dom Nichols
against Western Michigan; tight end
Eli Owens
against Oklahoma; cornerback
Shamari Earls
and wide receiver
Salesi Moa
against UTEP; offensive lineman
Avery Gach
(rhino) against Iowa; offensive lineman
Manuel Beigel
(rhino) against Penn State; and quarterback
Tommy Carr
, defensive end
Benny Patterson III
and right tackle
Marky Walbridge
against Indiana.
