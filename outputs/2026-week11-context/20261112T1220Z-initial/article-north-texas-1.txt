Dominant Second Half Leads North Texas to Homecoming Win Over Rice
11/7/2026 6:39:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Share:
Share:
DENTON
—
Jahiem White
rushed for 139 yards and three touchdowns,
Tayven Jackson
threw a touchdown pass and scored twice on a pair of runs, and North Texas beat Rice 52-23 on Saturday for the Mean Green's sixth consecutive win.
North Texas (7-2, 5-0 American Conference) has a one-game lead over Memphis and UTSA atop the conference standings. It is the Mean Green's first 5-0 start in conference play since 2004.
Rice (1-8, 0-5) has lost eight in a row since a 31-3 win over Houston Christian, a member of the FCS, in the season opener. The Owls have lost 11 straight against Football Bowl Subdivision opponents, dating to last season.
After managing just 10 first-half points, North Texas erupted for 42 in the second half, scoring touchdowns on each of its final six possessions. The Mean Green finished with 495 yards of total offense and converted two Rice turnovers into 14 points.
White's second touchdown, a 1-yarder, gave the Mean Green a 24-14 lead with 4:22 left in the third quarter. On the next play from scrimmage,
Zakye Barker
forced a fumble that was recovered by
Bryce Garza
at the Rice 32, and five plays later Jackson hit
Dalton Carnes
with a 19-yard touchdown pass to extend the lead to 17 points.
Jackson completed 18 of 31 passes for 303 yards, and
Justin Stevenson
had five receptions for 117 yards. White added a 60-yard touchdown run early in the fourth quarter, and
Brendon Haygood
scored on a 4-yard run with 1:55 remaining.
Gael Ochoa threw a 36-yard touchdown pass to Will Swartz, while Tyvonn Byars and Patrick Crayton Jr. added rushing touchdowns for the Owls.
Up next, North Texas visits UTSA next Saturday in a pivotal American Conference matchup. Kickoff is set for 8 p.m. at the Alamodome on ESPNU.
GAME INFORMATION
Team captains were
Tayven Jackson
and
Zakye Barker
. Barker has served as a captain in every game this season.
North Texas won the coin toss and elected to defer.
NORTH TEXAS DEFENSE
Held Rice scoreless in the first quarter for the third time this season (UNLV, Tulsa and Rice).
Recorded stops on each of the opponent's first three drives for the first time since the UNLV game.
Recorded its seventh fumble recovery of the season after entering the game ranked 21st nationally with six.
Bryce Garza
recovered a forced fumble by
Zakye Barker
in the third quarter.
Recorded 2.0 sacks, giving the defense multiple sacks in consecutive games and multiple sacks in five games this season.
Forced two turnovers, both resulting in North Texas touchdowns.
NORTH TEXAS OFFENSE
Scored on its opening drive in six of nine games this season, including each of the last four games.
Scored 10 first-half points, tying its second-lowest halftime total of the season. North Texas scored six points against Indiana and 10 against Tulsa.
Recorded its sixth consecutive 40-point game and seventh of the season.
Scored 21 points in each of the final two quarters.
UNT was perfect in the redzone in seven trips, finishing with six touchdowns and one field goal.
JAHIEM WHITE
Rushed 21 times for 139 yards and three touchdowns.
Recorded his ninth consecutive game with a rushing touchdown, the longest active streak in the FBS.
Extended his scoring streak to 11 consecutive games dating back to his time at West Virginia.
Recorded his sixth multi-touchdown game of the season and ninth of his career.
Recorded his seventh 100-yard rushing game of the season, leading the FBS.
Now has 16 rushing touchdowns on the season and 30 for his career.
Recorded his fifth touchdown of at least 50 yards and sixth rush of 50 or more yards.
Scored at least three touchdowns for the second time in his career. His career high is four against Houston Christian earlier this season.
Reached 16 total touchdowns this season and 33 for his career.
TAYVEN JACKSON
Completed 18 of 31 passes for 303 yards, one touchdown and no interceptions.
Scored on two rushing touchdowns.
Now has four rushing touchdowns on the season and 12 for his career.
Threw his 23rd touchdown pass of the season and 39th of his career.
Extended his streak to eight consecutive games with a touchdown pass.
Had his first sack in six consecutive quarters. His previous sack came against Navy in the third quarter.
JUSTIN STEVENSON
Recorded five receptions for 117 yards.
Recorded his fourth consecutive 100-yard receiving game and fifth of the season.
AARON ALEXANDER
Recorded four total tackles (two solo), 0.5 sacks and 1.5 tackles for loss.
The half sack gives him 0.5 for the season and one for his career.
ZAKYE BARKER
Recorded his first forced fumble of the season and fourth of his career.
Finished with 10 tackles and a forced fumble.
DALTON CARNES
Recorded his first receiving touchdown of the season and fourth of his career.
It was his first receiving touchdown since Oct. 12, 2024, against Florida Atlantic.
DOMINIC DE FREITAS
Improved to 6-for-6 on field-goal attempts since the start of conference play with a 37-yard field goal at the 6:03 mark of the first quarter.
Has made 25 of his last 31 field-goal attempts dating back to Oct. 10, 2025.
BRYCE GARZA
Recorded his second pass breakup of the season and career in the third quarter.
Recovered the first fumble of his career.
BRAYDEN KNOX
Recorded 0.5, bringing his season total to 3.0 and career total to 4.0.
For the game, recorded three total tackles (one solo), 0.5 sacks, and 1.0 tackles for loss.
IAN RATLIFF
Recorded his eighth punt inside the 20-yard line of the season.
DAVION RHODES
Recorded a team-high fifth sack of the season and ninth of his career.
PEYTON TAYLOR
Recorded his second interception in as many games, returning it 41 yards.
