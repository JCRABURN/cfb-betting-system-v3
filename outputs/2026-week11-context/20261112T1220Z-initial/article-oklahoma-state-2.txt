The Basics
The Oklahoma State football team (6-3 overall; 4-2 Big 12) returns home this week to host No. 10 Texas Tech (8-1 overall; 5-1 Big 12). TTU is the second AP top-10 team to play in Stillwater this season and the highest-ranked conference opponent to visit Boone Pickens Stadium since OSU defeated No. 10 Oklahoma in 2023. Kickoff is set for 8:30 p.m. CT Saturday at Boone Pickens Stadium in Stillwater.
On the Air
The game will be televised live on ESPN with Roy Philpott, Brock Osweiler and Sherree Burruss on the call. It will also be carried live on the Cowboy Radio Network, with
Dave Hunziker
handling play-by-play,
John Holcomb
providing analysis and
Deion Imade
reporting from the sideline. Fans outside of the Cowboy Radio Network can listen to the OSU broadcast for free through The Varsity Network app. It will also be broadcast on national radio via Westwood One Sports with Danny Reed and Derek Rackley on the call.
Sold Out!
Saturday's game is sold out, just as the previous three home games were at Boone Pickens Stadium. The November 28 game against Kansas is the only home game left on this year's schedule that is not yet sold out, and a limited number of tickets remain for that contest. Tickets are available at www.okstate.com/tickets or by contacting the ticket office at 877-255-4678. Fans can still secure their tickets to sold out games through SeatGeek, the official secondary partner of OSU Athletics.
Blackout Boone Pickens Stadium and "Salute to Service"
This weekend's game marks the 2026 "Blackout" game, when all OSU fans in all sections are encouraged to wear black clothing. It is also OSU's annual "Salute to Service" game, presented in recognition of veterans and active-duty military members.
Another AP Top-10 Opponent in Stillwater
OSU has hosted 54 AP Top-10 opponents in its history, including 46 that were conference opponents. Saturday's matchup with Texas Tech marks the highest AP-ranked conference opponent to visit Stillwater since OSU defeated No. 10 Oklahoma on November 4, 2023. The Cowboys defeated No. 6 Oregon earlier this year in OSU's home opener, and this weekend's game against Texas Tech marks the 11th time Oklahoma State's hosted multiple AP-top 10 team in one season (1971, 1973, 1975, 1976, 1979, 1985, 1989, 1995, 2015, 2018, 2026). The last time OSU hosted multiple AP top-10 teams in the same season was 2018, when it hosted and defeated two, first knocking off No. 6 Texas, 38-35, then beating No. 7 West Virginia, 45-41.
A win Saturday would tie the OSU single-season record for home wins against AP top-10 opponents, matching the two each by the 2018 and 1976 teams. It would also make Oklahoma State one of just four teams in the nation to have won each of its past four home games against AP top-10 opponents. The others are Georgia, Ohio State and Texas.

OSU Against AP-Top 10 Opponents, Regardless of Location
This year is also the 41st that Oklahoma State has faced multiple AP-top 10 opponents in the same season, regardless of location. The last time it happened prior to this year was 2023. OSU has won multiple games against AP top-10 teams in five seasons, including a school-record three wins in 1976 and two wins each in 1972, 2011, 2018 and 2021. Oklahoma State has won three of its past five and six of its past 11 games against AP top-10 opponents, regardless of location.

Eight Straight Games Above 30 Points - One Away From Matching the OSU Single-Season Record
The Cowboys have scored more than 30 points in eight consecutive games for the first time since an eight-game streak during the 2021-22 seasons. If they reach nine, it would tie OSU's single-season streak record that was set in the final nine games of the 2011 season. It would also mark the longest stretch overall for OSU since the school-record 13-game run across the 2011 and 2012 seasons. The only other double-digit streak was a 10-game stretch from 1987-88.
The Series
Saturday's game marks the 53rd overall meeting between Oklahoma State and Texas Tech in a series that dates to 1935. The 2026 season also marks the 30th time the teams have faced each other in the 31 years of Big 12 Conference play.  During the Big 12 era, Oklahoma State holds a 16-13 edge in the series against Texas Tech, including wins in 12 of the past 16 matchups. In the all-time series, TTU holds a one-game advantage with a 25-24-3 edge. OSU leads the all-time series in Stillwater, 14-9, and has won nine of the past 11 played in Stillwater.
Eric Morris
, who played and coached at Texas Tech, is 0-1 in his head coaching career against the Red Raiders, and Texas Tech's Joey McGuire is 1-1 in his head coaching career against Oklahoma State.
In the Rankings
The Oklahoma State football team is not ranked this week, but it is receiving votes in both the AP Top 25 and the US LBM Coaches Poll, sitting at the equivalent of No. 28 in both polls. With the exception of last year, the Cowboys have been ranked at some point in every season since 2008. Including the 2026 season, Oklahoma State has been ranked in the top 15 of the Associated Press poll in 15 of the past 19 seasons and has reached the top 10 in 11 of those 19 years. The first 2026 College Football Playoff rankings were released last week. It's worth noting that OSU has been part of the CFP rankings in eight of the past 11 seasons entering 2026. The Cowboys have also been included in 43 of the all-time CFP Rankings, which ranks among the most appearances in the country. Texas Tech is No. 10 in both the AP and coaches polls.
Oklahoma State from a Distance
After not winning a conference game in 2024 or 2025, Oklahoma State essentially pushed the reset button for the 2026 season. OSU brought in head coach
Eric Morris
to take the reins of the Cowboy program, and he then brought in more than 40 new staff members and 91 new players to build a fresh foundation for the future. The 91 newcomers are strongly believed to be the most on one team in the history of major college football, surpassing Colorado's 87 in 2023. The biggest name he brought with him was quarterback
Drew Mestemaker
, who leads all FBS players in passing touchdowns and all Power 4 players in passing yards and is now widely discussed as the potential No. 1 pick in the next NFL Draft and as a contender for the Heisman Trophy. The group has made a big impact, and here are some key notes on what they've done so far:
• Oklahoma State has gone three-and-out on only 5.5% of its drives this season, lowest in the FBS.
• Oklahoma State is averaging 43.9 points per game this season after scoring 14.2 points per game in 2025. The +29.7 improvement is the largest in the FBS in 2026.
• Oklahoma State has scored 207 points in the second half this season, most in the FBS.
• Oklahoma State has averaged 7.89 yards per play on first down this season, most in the Big 12 and sixth most in the FBS.
• Oklahoma State is the only Power 4 team to have at least 30 points and 400 yards of offense in every single conference game this season.
•
Drew Mestemaker
has eight games this season with at least 300 passing yards and multiple passing touchdowns, most in the FBS.
• Mestemaker has the longest active FBS streak of 300-yard passing games (8) and 200-yard passing games (24).
• Mestemaker is one of three FBS players in the last five seasons to have at least 2,900 passing yards and 29 pass touchdowns through nine team games. The other two are Cam Ward (2024) and Drake Maye (2022).
• Oklahoma State's 31 passing touchdowns leads the FBS and are its most through nine games in school history.
91 Newcomers Mark the Most in College Football History
The 2026 Oklahoma State roster features 91 newcomers, which is strongly believed to be the most on one team in the history of major college football, breaking the mark of 87 set by Colorado in 2023. Newcomers make up 79% of the 115-man roster. The team includes 66 new transfers, 24 returning players, 13 returning letterwinners, five players who had at least four OSU starts last season and 18 total players who had played in a game for Oklahoma State prior to 2026. The transfers arrived with a combined total of 45,088 total snaps of Division I experience.
The
Eric Morris
Era is Underway
Eric Morris
is in his inaugural season as Oklahoma State's head coach in 2026. He was named the 25th head coach in program history on November 25, 2025. An elite offensive mind and talent developer, Morris had been the head coach at North Texas since 2023. His hire marked the first time since 1969 that Oklahoma State hired a sitting head coach and the first time in school history OSU hired a sitting head coach from a school that is in the FBS. In three seasons at UNT, Morris built the Mean Green from a 5-7 record in his first season to a team that won 12 games in year three and reached a conference championship game with a College Football Playoff berth on the line. Those three seasons produced three of the top four seasons for total offense per game in North Texas history.
A Top-10 Transfer Portal Class
Of those 91 newcomers, 66 are new transfers (57% of the team). That group makes up a transfer portal class that ranks No. 7 in the nation according to both The Athletic and 247Sports.
