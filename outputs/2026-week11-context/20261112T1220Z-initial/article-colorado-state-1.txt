(S10, E14) This week on the Colorado State Insider Podcast, we start on the hardwood as basketball season is underway at Colorado State for both the men's and women's teams. Rams women's basketball head coach Ryun Williams joins Brian to discuss the start of the season, recap the team's early performances, and look ahead to what's next for the Rams.
Then, we turn our attention to football as Rams Radio analyst Marty Lenz joins the show to recap Colorado State's game against Boise State and discuss what's ahead for the Rams over the final stretch of the season. Plus, Chris King, the radio voice of the Washington State Cougars, joins the podcast for an inside look at Washington State and a preview of Saturday night's match-up in Pullman, Washington.

(S10, E12) This week on the Colorado State Insider Podcast, we look back at Colorado State's win over San Diego State and turn our attention to Saturday's matchup with Utah State. Rams Radio analyst Marty Lenz joins Brian to break down the win over the Aztecs and preview the Aggies. Then, Scott Garrard, the radio voice of Utah State football, joins the show for an inside look at the Aggies and what CSU can expect on Saturday.
Plus, basketball season is right around the corner, and we get you ready for both the men's and women's seasons at Colorado State.
