Oklahoma State Debuts in CFP Rankings at No. 25
November 10, 2026
|
Cowboy Football
Share:
STILLWATER
– The Oklahoma State football team is in the College Football Playoff Rankings for the first time since December 3, 2023, coming in at No. 25 in Tuesday night's rankings.
It is OSU's 44th overall appearance in the CFP Rankings, which is the 11th-most in the nation.
The Cowboys are one of six Big 12 teams included this week, joined by No. 2 Utah, No. 10 BYU, No. 12 Texas Tech, No. 14 Houston and No. 23 Arizona.
No. 25 OSU hosts No. 12 TTU Saturday at 8:30 p.m. CT in Boone Pickens Stadium.
Season tickets are sold out to Oklahoma State football games this year, but there are still opportunities to get tickets to see the Cowboys in Stillwater. A small number of single-game tickets remain for the November 28 game against Kansas, but Saturday's game against Texas Tech is sold out.
Tickets are available at
www.okstate.com/tickets
or by contacting the ticket office at 877-ALL-4-OSU. Fans can still secure their tickets to sold out games through
SeatGeek
, the official secondary partner of OSU Athletics.
