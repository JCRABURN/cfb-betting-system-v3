Tulsa's Baylor Hayes Named Manning Award Star of the Week
11/9/2026 12:05:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
NEW ORLEANS, La. –
Tulsa quarterback
Baylor Hayes
has been named one of eight Manning Award Stars of the Week following his career performance in the Golden Hurricane's 60-10 road victory over Tulane on Saturday, Nov. 7.
Hayes completed 26-of-33 passes for a career-high 338 yards and four touchdowns without an interception, leading Tulsa to its highest-scoring performance of the season. He connected on 78.8 percent of his pass attempts while helping the Golden Hurricane roll up 562 yards of total offense against the Green Wave.
Three of Hayes' four touchdown passes went to wide receiver
Oran Singleton Jr.
, who finished with 13 receptions for 177 yards and three touchdowns. Tulsa scored eight touchdowns in the victory, including four through the air, as the Golden Hurricane earned its second American Conference win of the season.
Hayes is joined on this week's Manning Award Stars of the Week list by Notre Dame's CJ Carr, Texas Tech's Thomas Castellanos, Boise State's Max Cutforth, Middle Tennessee's Roman Gagliano, Texas' Arch Manning, Wake Forest's Steele Pizzella and Alabama's Keelon Russell.
The Manning Award, sponsored by the Allstate Sugar Bowl, was created in 2004 to honor the college football accomplishments of Archie, Peyton and Eli Manning. It is the only major college football quarterback award that considers postseason performances in selecting its winner.
The award has recognized outstanding weekly quarterback performances through its Stars of the Week program since 2011. Fans have the opportunity to vote for their favorite quarterback, with weekly winners recognized throughout the season.
The Manning Award finalists will be announced in early December, with the winner revealed following the College Football Playoff National Championship.
For more information on the Manning Award and to participate in fan voting, visit
AllstateSugarBowl.org
.
Tulsa returns to H.A. Chapman Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14, to host Florida Atlantic in American Conference action. Kickoff is scheduled for 2:30 p.m. CT on ESPN+.
