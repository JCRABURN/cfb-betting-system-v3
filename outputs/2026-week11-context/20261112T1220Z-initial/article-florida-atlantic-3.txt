Kittley Named Coach of the Week by CBS Sports
11/9/2026 7:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Ben Paul
BOCA RATON, Fla.
– After a 60-point performance against first-place RV UTSA to secure Bowl eligibility, Florida Atlantic's Hagerty Family Head Football Coach
Zach Kittley
was selected by CBS Sports as the American Conference Coach of the Week.
This marks Kittley's third recognition this season as the conference's coach of the week and fourth in his two-year career.
Coach Kittley's offense was on display in the 60-29 victory over the Roadrunners, with four touchdowns from fifth-year WR
Easton Messer
(three receiving, one passing) and three by graduate TE
AJ Johnson
(two rushing, one receiving). Graduate QB
Caden Veltkamp
tossed four scores of his own as the Owls delivered UTSA its first loss during league play.
Nationwide, FAU boasts a top 10 offense in scoring with 40.8 points per game while ranking 20th in total offense (453.7).
The Owls will face another team coming off a 60-point outing in Tulsa on Saturday, Nov. 14. Kickoff is at 3:30 p.m. ET on ESPN+.
FOLLOW THE OWLS
Inside the Owls Burrow airs on The Spot, South Florida 39 (WSFL-TV) weekly on Thursdays at 5:30 p.m. in the Miami-Ft. Lauderdale DMA with replays running to be determined. The Spot, South Florida 9 (WHDT-TV), will air the show on Thursday at 6 p.m. in the West Palm Beach-Fort Pierce DMA with replays to be determined.
The Coaches Radio Show presented by Duffy's Sports Grill takes place at the Duffy's on St. Andrew's Boulevard in Boca Raton. Fans can join in person or listen live on FOX Sports South Florida from 6 to 7 p.m. This week's guests include Hagerty Family Head Football Coach
Zach Kittley
, Offensive Line Coach
Stephen Hamby
, and Head Women's Basketball Coach LeAnn Freeland.
