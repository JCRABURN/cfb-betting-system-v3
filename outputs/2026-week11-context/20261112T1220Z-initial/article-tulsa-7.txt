TULSA, Okla.
– Tulsa Head Football Coach
Tre Lamb
met with the media Monday as the Golden Hurricane prepares to return home for Saturday's American Conference matchup against Florida Atlantic at H.A. Chapman Stadium.
Lamb reflected on Tulsa's 60-10 road victory over Tulane and discussed the team's preparations for the Owls as the Golden Hurricane looks to build on its momentum heading into the final stretch of the regular season.
