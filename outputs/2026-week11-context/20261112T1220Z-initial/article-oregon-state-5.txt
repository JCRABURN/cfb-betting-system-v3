Athletic Communications
CORVALLIS, Ore. —
Gyriece Goodman
intercepted a pass and recovered a fumble, and
TJ Crandall
blocked a field goal and made a career-high nine tackles, but Oregon State fell to Texas State, 42-21, on Saturday at Reser Stadium.
Goodman's interception, the first of his career, came late in the first quarter and ended a Texas State drive at the Oregon State 17. In the fourth quarter he recovered a fumble by Davian Jackson at the Texas State 50, and the Beavers turned it into
Kourdey Glass's
2-yard touchdown run.
Goodman also broke up a pass and finished with six tackles. He is the second Beaver this season with an interception and a fumble recovery in the same game, joining
Sailasa Vadrawale III
at Houston.
Crandall blocked Jacob Bonilla's 28-yard field goal attempt in the second quarter with Texas State ahead 14-7, the first blocked kick by a Beaver since
Mason White
against Air Force on Nov. 16, 2024. It was the first of Crandall's career. He made nine tackles, topping his previous best of five.
Braden Atkinson
completed 20 of 36 passes for 192 yards and an interception, ending his school-record streak of eight straight 300-yard games.
Jesse Legree
caught eight passes for 99 yards and has 1,209 this season.
AJ Newberry's
9-yard touchdown run with 59 seconds left in the first quarter cut the deficit to 14-7. Oregon State did not score again until
Cornell Hatcher Jr.'s
1-yard run with 8:52 remaining.
Oregon State plays at Boise State on Saturday, Nov. 14. Kickoff is set for 3 p.m. PT on USA Sports.
