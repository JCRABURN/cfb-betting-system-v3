Weekly Coaches Show Returns to Thursday
11/10/2026 4:07:00 PM
|
Football, Women's Basketball
Share:
Hagerty Family Head Football Coach Zach Kittley will be joined by OL Coach Stephen Hamby and Head Women’s Basketball Coach LeAnn Freeland
BOCA RATON, Fla.
- The Florida Atlantic Coaches Show, presented by Duffy's Sports Grill, will return to Thursday this week from 6 to 7 p.m. at the Duffy's Sports Grill on St. Andrew's Boulevard in Boca Raton.
Fans can join in person or listen live on FOX Sports South Florida 640 AM. This week, Hagerty Family Head Football Coach
Zach Kittley
will be joined by Offensive Line Coach
Stephen Hamby
and Head Women's Basketball Coach
LeAnn Freeland
.
The football team is coming off last Thursday's bowl-clinching win over RV UTSA. The Owls' upended a first-place Roadrunners team, 60-29, under the national spotlight. This week, FAU will face another road challenge when the Owls travel to Tulsa to face the Golden Hurricane, who scored 60 points last week as well, before a homecoming crowd.
The Owls' offense will continue to be led by the highly decorated duo of graduate QB
Caden Veltkamp
and fifth-year WR/PR
Easton Messer
. Between the two, five weekly awards and recognitions have been bestowed this week. Kittley has also gained attention, earning his third CBS Sports American Conference Coach of the Week honors.
Join the show in person each week for the chance to win trivia prizes and other giveaways and be on the lookout for special appearances from Owlsley.
The Florida Atlantic Coaches Show continues every Thursday from 6-7 p.m. There is no show Thanksgiving week (Nov. 26).
