GAME NOTES
Saturday will be the 15th meeting with Boise State and the sixth time facing the Broncos while they are ranked in the Top 25.
The last time BSU was in Fort Collins, the Rams won their first game against the Broncos after a Hail Mary pass play as time ran out to tight end Dallin Holker.
The game will be the second this season that CSU has hosted a ranked team (then-No. 11 BYU)
Last season the two played in Boise with the Broncos coming away with a 49-21 victory.
Saturday's game will also feature CSU's Snow Days. The four-day celebration returns, bringing winter recreation, research and outdoor industry events to campus. The marquee of the fun will be a Rail Jam and Vendor Village ahead of kickoff on Saturday.
