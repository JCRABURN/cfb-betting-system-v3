Everything you need to plan your Saturday gameday
By:
Kyle Kavanaugh
RUSTON –
A rivalry renewed. A stadium dressed in red. Another Saturday in Ruston.
Louisiana Tech returns to Origin Bank Field at Joe Aillet Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14, to host Southern Miss in a Sun Belt Conference showdown. Kickoff is set for 4 p.m. on ESPN+ as the Bulldogs welcome the Golden Eagles back to Ruston for a Red Out, presented by Experience Ruston.
With the return of a familiar rival and a full afternoon of festivities planned, Bulldog fans are encouraged to
wear RED, arrive early and review the Football Game Day Guide
before heading to campus.
VIEW THE COMPLETE FOOTBALL GAME DAY GUIDE
The comprehensive guide serves as the one-stop resource for everything fans need to know before they go, including tickets, parking and traffic routes, tailgating, concessions and pricing, gameday timelines, Bulldog Village, Walk of Pride, stadium maps and policies, and everything happening around Saturday's game.
SATURDAY'S GAME
Louisiana Tech vs. Southern Miss
Saturday, Nov. 14 | 4 p.m.
Origin Bank Field at Joe Aillet Stadium
TV: ESPN+
Theme: Red Out | College of Business
Game Sponsor: Experience Ruston
FIVE THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE YOU GO
Wear RED:
Bulldog fans are encouraged to fill The Joe with red for Saturday's Red Out. Special Red Out T-shirts, courtesy of Experience Ruston, and Sic' Em Dogs red rally towels will be available while supplies last.
Parking and Traffic:
Stadium parking lots and tailgating open at 8 a.m. Parking credentials are required to enter stadium lots after 8 a.m. Fans should review recommended arrival routes and updated postgame traffic patterns due to ongoing construction on Tech Drive.
Bulldog Village and Walk of Pride:
Bulldog Village opens at 1 p.m. with food, music, vendors and family-friendly activities. Welcome the Bulldogs during the Walk of Pride at 1:45 p.m., followed by the pregame pep rally.
Stadium Entry:
Gates, suites and the Davison Athletics Complex open at 2:30 p.m. Fans are encouraged to have mobile tickets ready and review the clear bag policy before arriving.
Gameday Entertainment:
Saturday's festivities include Frisbee Dogs at halftime, recognition of Louisiana Tech's College of Business and a variety of in-game promotions, entertainment and giveaways.
RED OUT GAMEDAY HIGHLIGHTS
Saturday's Red Out will feature several opportunities for fans to show their Bulldog pride, including promotional Red Out T-shirts courtesy of Experience Ruston, red rally towels and additional giveaways throughout the stadium, while supplies last.
Louisiana Tech will also recognize its College of Business throughout the afternoon, with student organizations participating in the Bulldogs' pregame run-out and College of Business representatives serving as guest coaches.
At halftime, fans can enjoy a special performance from the Frisbee Dogs, while the Band of Pride, Regal Blues, cheerleaders and Champ will help bring the energy throughout the game.
THIS WEEK'S CONCESSION SPECIALS
Fans can enjoy a featured
red beans and rice with sausage
special for $12, or $15 with a drink, along with
$2
popcorn
at concession stands.
The complete concession menu, pricing and locations are available in the Game Day Guide.
PLAN AHEAD. WEAR RED. BARK LOUDER.
Fans are encouraged to arrive early and take advantage of the full gameday experience, beginning with morning tailgating and continuing through Bulldog Village, Walk of Pride and kickoff.
With ongoing construction impacting traffic patterns around Joe Aillet Stadium, fans should review the recommended arrival and postgame exit routes before traveling to campus.
Whether you're coming for the rivalry, the Red Out or another Saturday with the Bulldog Family, everything you need to make the most of gameday is available in one place.
Everything you need to know before you go.
