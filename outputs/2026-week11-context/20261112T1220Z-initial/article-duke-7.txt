Sheppard and Pellicciotta Earn ACC Weekly Honors
11/9/2026 2:01:00 PM
|
Football
Read the NC State Game Recap
Share:
DURHAM, N.C. –
Duke running back
Nate Sheppard
and safety
Andrew Pellicciotta
received ACC weekly honors following their performances against NC State on Saturday. Sheppard was chosen as the ACC Running Back of the Week while Pellicciotta was named the ACC Specialist of the Week.
Sheppard now has six ACC weekly honors in his career (five running back and one rookie), tied with
Daniel Jones
(2016-18) for the fourth most by a Blue Devil in a career. In addition, his four ACC player of the week accolades are tied for the second most in a year by a Blue Devil while his four running back of the week picks are the most offensive back (prior to the league splitting running backs and quarterbacks into separate awards) selections in a campaign at Duke. Sheppard's five career running back of the week picks are tied with
Leo Hart
(1968-70) for the second most offensive back selections in a career by a Blue Devil.
Sheppard, a native of Mandeville, Louisiana, rushed 18 times for 217 yards (12.1 yards/carry) and scored three touchdowns. He finished with 221 all-purpose yards against the Wolfpack while NC State produced 217 yards of total offense during the game. Sheppard totaled 98 rushing yards in the first quarter, the third-most by a Blue Devil in an opening stanza. His 98 yards were the fifth-most in any quarter and most since
Mateo Durant
ran for 130 yards in the fourth quarter against Charlotte in 2021. Sheppard finished the first half with 168 ground yards, the second most in a half by a Blue Devil.
Sheppard's rushing performance was his fifth 100-yard game of the season, tied for the sixth most in a year by a Blue Devil. Sheppard recorded his second 200-yard game this season and the 11th in program history. He is up to 1,189 yards rushing yards this fall, the third most in a year in program history. Sheppard joined
Randy Cuthbert
in 1989 and 1992 as the only Blue Devils with two, 1,000-yard seasons. Sheppard is the first to do so in consecutive seasons.
In the first quarter, Sheppard set the Duke single-season record with his 15th rushing score of the campaign. The sophomore added two more touchdowns on the ground, finishing the contest with three rushing scores and 17 on the season. Sheppard now has 28 career rushing touchdowns, tied with
Tom Davis
(1941-44) for second-most by a Blue Devil. Sheppard's 21 total touchdowns scored this season are the most in a campaign for a Blue Devil.
Pellicciotta returned a missed field goal for a 100-yard touchdown as the first half expired. His return pushed Duke's lead from 24-3 to 31-3 at intermission as Duke scored the final 17 points of the second quarter in the last 3:30 of game clock. With N.C. State's Kanoah Vinesett coming up short on a 50-yard field goal into the wind, Pellicciotta caught it at the back of the end zone and brought it out and zig-zagged his way across the field for the TD.
The score was Duke's first return for a touchdown of a field goal attempt since
Ross Cockrell's
75-yard return versus FIU on Sept. 1, 2012. Pellicciotta, a native of Malvern, Pennsylvania, also has a fumble scoop-and-score this season (vs. North Carolina, Oct. 17) and is the first Blue Devil to have a special-teams touchdown and a defensive touchdown in the same season since
DeVon Edwards
during the 2013 campaign.

The ACC's other weekly honors included SMU's Kevin Jennings (quarterback), Yamir Knight (receiver) and Marques White (defensive line), Florida State's Xavier Chaplin (offensive line) and Amari Thomas (rookie), North Carolina's Timir Hickman-Collins (linebacker) and Clemson's Ashton Hampton (defensive back).
Duke returns to action Saturday with a 12 p.m. kick at No. 5 Miami. The game will be televised on ABC.
