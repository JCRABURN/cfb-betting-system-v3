Top tight end award committee sends Cougar to semifinals
HOUSTON –
For the second consecutive season, a Houston tight end has been named a John Mackey Award Semifinalist as
Patrick Overmyer
advanced to the semifinals of the top tight end award.
The Kingwood, Texas, native has hauled in seven receiving touchdowns, the second most by an FBS tight end, and is tied for the seventh most among all pass catchers in college football. His 497 receiving yards through nine games are the 6th most by an FBS tight end this year.
Overmyer tied the FBS tight end single-game record at Kansas State catching 4 touchdown passes in Houston's road win. His four receiving touchdowns were the third most in program history and the most by a Cougar tight end.
Overmyer follows
Tanner Koziol
as Houston's second consecutive tight end to advance to the semifinals of the Mackey Award.

ABOUT THE JOHN MACKEY AWARD
The John Mackey Award is presented annually to college football's most outstanding tight end. Established in 2000 by the Nassau County Sports Commission, the award is given annually to the tight end who best exemplifies the play, sportsmanship, academics, and community values of Pro Football Hall of Fame tight end John Mackey.
