Kansas at West Virginia Set for 4:30 p.m. Kickoff on ESPNU
11/8/2026 9:45:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
LAWRENCE, Kan. –
The Big 12 Conference announced its week 11 lineup on Sunday, which features the Kansas Jayhawks traveling to Morgantown, West Virginia to take on the West Virginia Mountaineers at 4:30 p.m. CT on ESPNU.
The week 11 matchup will be Kansas' fourth of five road games this season. Kansas and West Virginia have squared off 14 times in the series history, where the Mountaineers hold an 11-3 advantage, including a 7-1 lead in games played in Morgantown. Kansas last won in Morgantown in 2022, resulting in a 55-42 Jayhawk victory.
The 4:30 p.m. kickoff will be Kansas' first 4:30 p.m. kickoff of the season and its fourth after 4 p.m. Kansas has played on ESPNU twice this season with wins over Long Island and Middle Tennessee.
