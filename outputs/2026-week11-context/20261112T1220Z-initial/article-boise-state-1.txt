Boise State Offensive Line Selected as Semifinalist for Joe Moore Award
11/11/2026 2:30:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
BOISE, Idaho –
The Boise State offensive line was featured on the list of semifinalists for the Joe Moore Award, the organization announced. The award is presented annually to the top offensive line unit in college football and is the only major award given to a unit, not an individual. The Broncos are one of 10 teams featured on the list.
The offensive line paves the way for a Bronco unit that ranks in the nation's top 10 in several categories. Boise State is second in average time of possession (35:29), third in third down conversions (53.4%), fifth in turnovers lost (5), seventh in rushing yards per game (237.1), seventh in sacks allowed per game (0.78), 10th in points per game (39.0) and 10th in fourth down conversions (73.7%).
Right guard
Roger Carreon
has a Pro Football Focus grade of 81.1, the second-highest in the nation at the position. Center
Stewart Taufa
has a PFF run blocking grade of 79.2, sixth-highest in the FBS at the position. Tackles
Tyler Ethridge
and
Daylon Metoyer
have started all nine games, while left guard has been split between
Zander Esty
,
Carson Rasmussen
and
Jason Steele
.
The Broncos have won eight straight games, scoring over 30 points in all eight contests. They compiled 669 yards of offense in a 64-38 win at Colorado State, the eighth-highest single-game total and highest-scoring road performance since 1952. They totaled 352 rushing yards in the victory, the third 300-yard rushing effort of the season. Running back
Dylan Riley
leads the FBS in rushing yards (1,241) and all-purpose yards (1,547) while quarterbacks
Maddux Madsen
and
Max Cutforth
have combined to throw for 2,129 yards and 22 touchdowns with just one interception.
No. 17/18/24 Boise State (8-1, 5-0 Pac-12) plays Oregon State (5-4, 3-2 Pac-12) at 4 p.m. on Nov. 14. The Broncos will host ESPN's College GameDay on DeChevrieux Field before clashing with the Beavers on The Blue. The sold-out game will air nationally on USA Sports.
--BroncoSports.com--
