Concert and Tailgate Experience Set for Florida Atlantic
11/10/2026 11:51:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
TULSA, Okla. ––
Tulsa fans can begin Saturday's Homecoming football festivities at Chapman Commons, where the Golden Hurricane Tailgate will feature live music, food, drinks and activities for fans of all ages.
The tailgating will open at 10:30 a.m. with DJ Chuck G on the Stage until 12:15 p.m. when the team walk begins on 11
th
Street. Following the team Nashville-based country artist Grace Lee will take the stage on Chapman Commons at 12:30 p.m., bringing energy and excitement to Golden Hurricane fans ahead of the 2:30 p.m. kickoff.
Leer, a singer-songwriter known for her powerful vocals and energetic live performances, gained national recognition as a Top 10 finalist on Season 18 of
American Idol
. A former NCAA Division I soccer player at UC Berkeley, Leer has built a growing career in country music, combining her passion for music and sports through performances across the country.
Her viral wedding anthem, "Best Friend for Life," reached No. 1 on Vevo's Country Music Video Chart, while her latest single, "Small Town Stranger," continues to showcase her distinctive country sound. Leer has also performed the national anthem for professional sporting events, including the U.S. Women's National Soccer Team, and served as a lead vocalist for the Country Hits Symphony Tour.
With more than 450,000 social media followers and 15 million global streams, Leer brings a national following to Tulsa's game-day festivities. Fans can listen to Leer's music and learn more about her upcoming performances by visiting her official website at
https://www.graceleer.com/
.
Located on Chapman Commons, the TU Tailgate features live music, inflatables and a beer garden. Fans can also reserve a space on Chapman Commons and bring their own tailgating supplies. Those interested in reserving a tailgate space can
click here
.
In addition to the live music, fans can enjoy the Tulsa Tailgate Rib Cook-Off on Chapman Commons as part of the Homecoming festivities. The barbecue competition will bring an added element of excitement to the Golden Hurricane's pregame tailgate, giving fans another reason to arrive early and enjoy the atmosphere before kickoff. Starting at 12 p.m. fans will be able to sample free ribs from the Tulsa Tailgate Rib cook off.
The Golden Hurricane Champions Fund and Alumni Association tailgate tents will open approximately three hours before kickoff. The Tulsa men's and women's golf team will be signing autographs starting at 1 p.m.
Tailgating areas will open four hours before kickoff throughout the 2026 season and close 30 minutes before kickoff, allowing fans ample time to enter H.A. Chapman Stadium.
Fans are encouraged to arrive early, enjoy the music and experience the game-day atmosphere before the Golden Hurricane kicks off against Florida Atlantic at 2:30 p.m.
Visit
www.TulsaHurricane.com
for the latest news and information on the football program. Fans can also follow the program on social media by searching 'TulsaFootball' on
X
,
Facebook
and
Instagram
.
