Veltkamp Part of Davey O’Brien Class of ‘26
11/10/2026 5:52:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Ben Paul
BOCA RATON, Fla.
– Florida Atlantic football's graduate QB
Caden Veltkamp
has been selected to the Davey O'Brien QB Class of 2026, making him a candidate for the yearly award given to the nation's best signal caller, announced on Tuesday.
Veltkamp is one of 30 quarterbacks across the nation to make the Class of '26. He has previously been named to the weekly Davey O'Brien Great 8 on two occasions during the season, following wins over RV Navy and RV UTSA.
Through nine games, Veltkamp has commanded a top 10 scoring offense and has been the national leader in completions per game in back-to-back seasons, guiding the Owls to a Bowl-eligible record for the first time since the 2020 campaign. The longest active FBS streak in games of throwing at least three touchdown passes belongs to the Bowling Green, Kentucky native, currently at five straight.
Currently, he holds a completion percentage of 71.6% while throwing for 2,746 yards and 27 touchdowns. Veltkamp has also successfully kept the ball out of harm's way with only five interceptions.
ABOUT THE DAVEY O'BRIEN AWARD
The Davey O'Brien National Quarterback Award is presented annually to the nation's best college quarterback and is the oldest and most prestigious national quarterback award. The 50th Annual Davey O'Brien Awards Dinner honoring the winner will be held Monday, March 8, 2027, at The Fort Worth Club in Fort Worth, Texas. For more information, visit www.daveyobrienaward.com.
For the sixth straight year, fan voting on social media (Facebook, Instagram and Twitter) will earn players bonus selection committee votes during the semifinalist, finalist and winner voting rounds. First-round fan voting began immediately following the QB Class announcement and will run through Sunday, Nov. 15. To vote, fans must simply like the official graphic from the @daveyobrien social media account that highlights their favorite quarterback.
The list of semifinalists selected from the QB Class will be named on Tuesday, Nov. 17. The Foundation will announce the three finalists the following week (Tuesday, Nov. 24). The 2026 Davey O'Brien Award winner will be unveiled live on ESPN on Friday, Dec. 11, during The Home Depot College Football Awards.
