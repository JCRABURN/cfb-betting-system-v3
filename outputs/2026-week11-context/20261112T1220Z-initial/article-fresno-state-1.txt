Bulldogs drop road test to Aggies
11/7/2026 10:58:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Postgame Press Conference
Postgame Notes
Share:
By:
Fresno State Athletics Strategic Communications
LOGAN, Utah –
Fresno State mounted a fourth-quarter comeback behind a late touchdown from
Jayden Mandal
to
Ezekiel Avit
, but the Bulldogs fell to Utah State 17-10 on Saturday night at Merlin Olsen Field at Maverik Stadium.
Fresno State held Utah State to 269 yards of offense and outgained the Aggies 278-269, but was limited to 16 rushing yards and did not reach the end zone until the final two minutes.
How it Happened
After a scoreless opening quarter, Utah State broke through on the third play of the second period. Sesi Vailahi finished an 80-yard drive with a 2-yard touchdown run to give the Aggies a 7-0 lead early in the second.
Utah State doubled its advantage on its next possession. McCae Hillstead capped an eight-play, 46-yard drive with a 3-yard touchdown run, putting the Aggies ahead 14-0 with 9:47 remaining in the first half.
Fresno State's defense kept the Bulldogs within two scores before halftime, stopping Utah State on fourth down at the Fresno State 37-yard line with 1:04 left in the second quarter.
The Bulldogs opened the second half with their longest possession of the night, traveling 66 yards in 14 plays and taking 7:42 off the clock.
Manaki Watanabe
completed the drive with a 32-yard field goal, cutting the deficit to 14-3 with 7:14 left in the third quarter.
Fresno State continued to threaten in the fourth. Beginning at its own 49-yard line, the Bulldogs moved to the Utah State 18 before the Aggies stopped them on fourth down with 9:15 remaining.
Utah State followed with a 10-play, 57-yard drive that ended with Kian Afrookhteh's 43-yard field goal. The kick extended the Aggies' lead to 17-3 with 4:21 left.
The Bulldogs responded with a 75-yard touchdown march. Mandal completed seven passes during the 11-play drive, including a 12-yard scoring strike to Avit with 1:30 remaining. Watanabe's extra point pulled Fresno State within seven at 17-10.
Fresno State attempted an onside kick following the touchdown, but Utah State recovered and ran out the remaining time.
Up Next
Fresno State stays on the road to face Texas State next Saturday, November 14. Kickoff is set for 1 p.m. PT on The CW.'
