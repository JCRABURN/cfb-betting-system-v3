Sonny Styles to Serve as Bell Ringer Against Northwestern
11/10/2026 9:56:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
Sonny Styles, an All-American linebacker, 2024 National Champion and 2026 First Round Pick, is set to serve as the sixth Bell Ringer of the 2026 season.
By:
Andrew Kovar
COLUMBUS, Ohio –
Sonny Styles
,
an All-American linebacker, 2024 National Champion and 2026 First Round Pick, is set to serve as the sixth Bell Ringer of the 2026 season when the Buckeyes host Northwestern on Nov. 14. Kickoff is scheduled for Noon ET.
Styles is the 14th Bell Ringer since the tradition began in 2025 and the second player from the Coach
Ryan Day
era, joining Jaxon Smith-Njigba.
Styles, the All-American linebacker who starred at Ohio State from 2022-25, developed into one of the nation's most versatile defensive players while helping the Buckeyes capture a national championship. A Pickerington, Ohio, native, he
began his collegiate career at safety before moving to linebacker ahead of the 2024 season. He finished his Ohio State career with 244 tackles, 9.0 sacks and nine pass breakups before being selected by the Washington Commanders with the seventh overall pick in the 2026 NFL Draft.
Styles' transition to linebacker helped elevate Ohio State's defense during its 2024 national championship season. Starting all 16 games, he finished second on the team with 100 tackles, including 10.5 tackles for loss and
6.0 sacks. He added five pass breakups, a forced fumble and a fumble recovery while earning second-team All-Big Ten honors from the conference's coaches. His 906 defensive snaps ranked second on the team.
Styles delivered some of his strongest performances during the College Football Playoff, recording 24 tackles, four tackles for loss, two sacks and a forced fumble across Ohio State's four postseason victories. He matched his then-career high with nine tackles against Texas in the Cotton Bowl semifinal and helped escort Jack Sawyer into the end zone on his game-clinching 83-yard fumble return. The victory sent the Buckeyes to the national championship game, where they defeated Notre Dame to secure the program's ninth national title.
Returning for his senior season in 2025, Styles became a team co-captain and earned the Block "O" Award, receiving the No. 0 jersey in recognition of his toughness, accountability
and character. He led Ohio State with 82 tackles, including 46 solo stops, and added 6.5 tackles for loss, an interception and three pass breakups. He earned First Team All-Big Ten honors from both the coaches and media, First Team All-America recognition from The Sporting News and Second Team All-America honors from The Associated Press. His season included a career-high 12 tackles against Indiana in the Big Ten Championship Game.
Beyond his contributions on the field, Styles earned multiple Ohio State Scholar-Athlete and Academic All-Big Ten honors and graduated with a degree in sport industry. In spring 2025, he was Ohio State's male nominee for the Big Ten's Jackie Robinson Community & Impact Award, recognizing his efforts to make a positive impact through sports and community involvement. His selection
by Washington Commanders continued Ohio State's tradition of producing First Round linebackers; alongside
Arvell Reese
, he helped make the Buckeyes the first program to have two linebackers selected among the top 10 picks in the same NFL Draft.
Bell Ringer Information & Honorees
Alpha Phi Omega members have long rung the Victory Bell across campus following Ohio State victories. That postgame tradition continues, and in 2025, Ohio State introduced a pregame Bell Ringer tradition at Ohio Stadium.
Before kickoff, a featured guest rings the Victory Bell to energize the crowd as the Buckeyes prepare to take the field. Two-time Heisman Trophy winner Archie Griffin became the first Bell Ringer before Ohio State's Aug. 30, 2025, season opener against Texas.
The words "For Vigor" are inscribed in the concrete above the bell inside Ohio Stadium. The phrase reflects the effort, energy
and enthusiasm the Bell represents each time it rings — and the spirit Ohio State asks Buckeye Nation to bring on game day.

Experience the Bell Ringer Up Close
You can be a part of the Bill Ringer tradition through
Buckeye Experiences
.
You and a guest will get to go inside the bell tower to watch the day's honorary bell ringer ring the Victory Bell as the 'Shoe comes to life before kickoff! This experience meets 45 minutes prior to kickoff at the check-in booth located
inside Gate 30.
Game Themes for November 14 vs. Northwestern
This weekend's matchup will serve as
Ohio State's Military Appreciation Game
, presented by Anduril. Through the Our Honor Defend military appreciation program, Buckeye Nation will come together to recognize the veterans and service members who have served,
are serving or will serve our country.
Inspired by the values of duty, loyalty and courage, Our Honor Defend reflects Ohio State's pride in supporting military-connected students and honoring those who defend honor with character and integrity.
Proceeds from officially licensed Our Honor Defend products
support Ohio State Veterans, ROTC and other military-connected student scholarships.
