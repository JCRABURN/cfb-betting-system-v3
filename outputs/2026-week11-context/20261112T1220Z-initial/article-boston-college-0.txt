Head Coach Bill O'Brien Media Availability

Share:
Official game notes, depth chart, weekly news conference and more ahead of the Eagles' week-11 match-up against the Fighting Irish
By:
Athletic Communications
Match-Up:
Boston College (4-5, 2-4 ACC) at #3 Notre Dame (8-1)
Date and Time:
Saturday, Nov. 14 • 3:30 p.m. ET
Location (Stadium):
South Bend, Ind. (Notre Dame Stadium)
Watch:
NBC
Radio:
WEEI 93.7 FM
GAME NOTES
FIRST-AND-10
» Boston College Football travels to South Bend, Ind. to take on #3 Notre Dame on Saturday, November 14 at 3:30 p.m.
» The game will be televised on NBC.
Dan Hicks
(play-by-play) and
Jason Garrett
(analyst) are on the call with sideline reporter
Caroline Pineda
.
» Coverage on the Boston College Sports Network begins 30 minutes prior to kickoff on WEEI 93.7 FM.
Jon Meterparel
(play-by-play) is joined on the call by former BC linebacker
Pete Cronan
in the booth and former BC quarterback
Scott Mutryn
on the sideline.
EAGLES BY THE NUMBERS
» The Eagles defense has 15 takeaways (12 interceptions, three fumble recoveries), which is the most in the ACC.
» Boston College’s 12 interceptions is the most in the ACC and the seventh-most nationally.
»
Charlie Comella’s
four interceptions leads the Eagles in 2026, and is tied for seventh-most nationally.
»
Comella
finished with a career-best eight tackles vs. Florida State (11/7).
» Through nine games, Boston College has allowed 187.3 passing yards per game to opposing teams, which ranks second in the ACC.
»
KP Price
leads the ACC in solo tackles (41) with
Marcus Upton
ranking second (40).
»
Upton
recorded his first-career interception vs. Florida State (11/7).
»
Anthony Palano
ranks fourth in the ACC in total tackles (76) and fifth in solo tackles (36).
»
Mason McKenzie
ranks in the top-10 in two rushing categories in the ACC: Total rushing yards (676, 6th overall, best among ACC quarterbacks) and rush yards per game (75.1, 8th, best among ACC quarterbacks).
» At 254.7 yards per game,
McKenzie
ranks seventh in total offense in the ACC.
»
Nedrick Boldin Jr.
caught a 29-yard touchdown in the second quarter vs. Florida State (11/7), his second career touchdown reception and the longest reception he’s had in his career. He finished with a career-high 49 yards on two catches.
»
Javarius Green
caught his second career touchdown on a two-yard reception in the third quarter vs. Florida State, his first touchdown since the Pitt game (10/17).
»
Bo MacCormack III
averages 5.04 yards per carry, which ranks eighth in the ACC.
»
MacCormack
scored on a six-yard rushing touchdown in the first quarter vs. Florida State (11/7) on the team’s opening drive, his fifth rushing touchdown this season. He finished with 105 yards on 16 carries, the second time in his career he’s rushed for 100+ yards in a game.
» Averaging 165.9 rushing yards per game, The Eagles own a top-10 rushing attack in the ACC (7th).
