Houston Offensive Line Earns Joe Moore Award Semifinal Honors
11/11/2026 3:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
Cougars named to first Joe Moore Award Semifinals in program history
HOUSTON –
For the first time in program history the Houston Football offensive line was named a Joe Moore Award semifinalist on Wednesday.
Houston's offensive line, consisting of
McKenzie Agnello
,
Anthony Boswell
,
Alvin Ebosele
,
Shadre Hurst
and
Drew Terrill
, as well as
Ronnell McLain
who earned a start after Terrill was briefly injured, have allowed the third fewest tackles for loss in the country (23) while the group as a whole lead the Big 12 in offensive grade and are third nationally with an 84.0 offense grade as a unit.
Houston has three linemen in the national grade rankings, led by
Anthony Boswell
(No. 2) and
Shadre Hurst
(No. 9) in the top 10, with
Drew Terrill
(No. 39) also inside the top 40.
Measured against the rest of the Big 12, Boswell, Hurst and Terrill all grade in the top 10, and the Cougars' offensive line leads the conference by 2.9 grade points.
Nationally, the Cougars offensive line grades as the fourth best in the country and grades third in the Power 4 conferences.
When running the ball, the Cougars have averaged 207.7 yards per game, the 17
th
most in the country behind the fourth-best run block team in the country.
Houston's pass blocking grades out as the 14
th
best in the country and is one of five schools with two or fewer sacks allowed with an 80+ pass block grade. The Cougars have allowed the eighth fewest pressures in the country.
ABOUT THE JOE MOORE AWARD
The award recognizes college football's Most Outstanding Offensive Line Unit since its inception in 2015. The Joe Moore Award has recognized elite O-Line units for their unparalleled toughness, effort, teamwork, consistency, technique and ability to finish.
