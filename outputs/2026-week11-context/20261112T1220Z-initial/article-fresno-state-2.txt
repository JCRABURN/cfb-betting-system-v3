QUICK LOOK
Fresno State will hit the road again for its second road test in as many weeks, taking on Texas State for the first time in program history on Saturday.
Saturday's game marks the first and only time the Bulldogs are on the road for back-to-back weeks this season.
It is also the second time in as many weeks leaving California and the Pacific Time Zone. Fresno State will play in its third time zone in as many weeks (PT/MT/CT).
Fresno State will also be playing its first game before 6 p.m. PT of the season.
Saturday is Fresno State's first trip to the state of Texas since taking on then-No. 23 Texas A&M on the road on Sept. 8, 2007. Fresno State fell in triple overtime, 47-45.
Fresno State is 7-8-1 when playing in the state of Texas. Its most recent win was a 2004 win at Rice.
Fresno State has one player on its roster from the state of Texas - DB
Cam Cole
.
With a win on Saturday, Fresno State would become bowl eligible for the sixth-straight season.
After scoring only 10 points last Saturday, Fresno State's streak of seven consecutive games with at least 23 points came to an end. It was the longest active streak with that many points scored since 2022-23.
Defensively, the Bulldogs have held opponents to 24 points or fewer in six games this season, including three to 10 points or fewer.
Fresno State did not force or commit a turnover in the loss at Utah State last weekend. It was just the second time this season that neither team had a turnover.  The Bulldogs are 0-2 in those games.
The Bulldogs have had seven different starting offensive line combinations through nine games this season.
WR
Josiah Freeman
had a career-high eight catches last weekend, hauling in 114 yards for his second 100-yard receiving game of the season and third of his career.
QB
Jayden Mandal
had a career-high 39 pass attempts at Utah State. Mandal completed 22 passes for 262 yards and a touchdown, his most yards since his lone 300-yard performance of the season against Sacramento State (Sept. 12).
DL
Dylan Hampsten
has had at least 1.0 TFL in all but one game this season. He has a team-high 10.0 on the season.
