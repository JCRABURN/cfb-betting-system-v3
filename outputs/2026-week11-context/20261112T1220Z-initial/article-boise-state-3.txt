NFL Broncos: Week 10
11/11/2026 3:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
BOISE, Idaho –
Boise State will be represented by eight players and one head coach in Week 10 of the NFL season, with former Broncos in action across three days. John Bates and the Washington Commanders kick things off with a Thursday Night Football contest against the New York Giants. The NFC East foes will clash at 6:15 p.m. MT on Prime Video. Ahmed Hassanein and the Detroit Lions open Sunday's slate at 7:30 a.m. MT, playing the New England Patriots in Germany. Three Boise State products will share the field on Sunday afternoon, as the Las Vegas Raiders (Ashton Jeanty) host the Seattle Seahawks (George Holani, DeMarcus Lawrence) for a 2:05 p.m. MT showdown. Scott Matlock and the Los Angeles Chargers close the weekend with a Monday Night Football battle in Baltimore, meeting the Ravens on ESPN at 6:15 p.m. MT. Full Week 10 information can be found below. All times listed are Mountain.
THURSDAY
Washington Commanders (John Bates) at New York Giants – Prime Video, 6:15 p.m.
Bates started at tight end and played 24 total snaps in Washington's 20-13 loss to the Los Angeles Rams. He was the Commanders' highest-rated offensive player on Pro Football Focus, notching an overall grade of 86.0 and run blocking grade of 82.2. He has started all eight games this season.
SUNDAY
Detroit Lions (Ahmed Hassanein) vs. New England Patriots – FOX, 7:30 a.m.
Hassanein was inactive in the Lions' 19-9 win over the Miami Dolphins, which extended their win streak to four games. Detroit is tied for second in the NFC North at 6-2 and can move into a tie for first with a win this weekend.
Buffalo Bills (Khalil Shakir) at New York Jets – CBS, 11 a.m.
Shakir had a team-high 58 receiving yards on four receptions in Buffalo's 23-6 win over the Minnesota Vikings. He gained a first down on all four catches and notched 40 yards after catch, putting his season total at 313, fifth-most in the NFL. The Bills improved to a league-best 7-1 and currently have the NFL's top scoring (32.0 ppg) and total (386.4 ypg) offense.
Jacksonville Jaguars (Ezra Cleveland) at Tennessee Titans – FOX, 11 a.m.
Cleveland started at right guard and played all 56 offensive snaps in Jacksonville's 33-10 win over the Baltimore Ravens. The Jaguars churned out 319 yards of offense in the victory. Jacksonville boasts a 6-2 record and has a three-game lead in the AFC South. The Jaguars also have the league's top point differential at +108.
New Orleans Saints (Kellen Moore) vs. Carolina Panthers – FOX, 11 a.m.
Moore led the Saints to a 23-22 overtime win against the Cleveland Browns, stopping Cleveland's two-point conversion on the final play of the game. New Orleans averaged 5.0 yards per carry and finished with 396 total yards. The Saints are now 3-5 and tied for first in the NFC South.
Las Vegas Raiders (Ashton Jeanty) vs. Seattle Seahawks (George Holani, DeMarcus Lawrence) – CBS, 2:05 p.m.
Jeanty ran for 25 yards and had five receptions in the Raiders' 38-17 loss to the San Francisco 49ers. He has 42 receptions for 371 yards on the season, most among NFL running backs. He ranks second in the position group and seventh overall with 318 yards after catch. Holani played 18 total snaps in Seattle's 17-3 win over the Arizona Cardinals, making a solo tackle on kickoff coverage. Lawrence was dominant defensively, totaling three tackles, 2.0 TFLs, a solo sack and a forced fumble. Through 166 career games, he has 68.5 sacks and 113 TFLs. The Seahawks allow 244.2 yards per game, the lowest of any defense in the league.
MONDAY
Los Angeles Chargers (Scott Matlock) at Baltimore Ravens – ESPN, 6:15 p.m.
Matlock made his first start of the year, playing a season-high 42 snaps in the Chargers' 44-22 loss to the Houston Texans. Los Angeles totaled 315 yards of offense, including 138 on the ground, averaging 4.6 yards per carry.
--BroncoSports.com--
