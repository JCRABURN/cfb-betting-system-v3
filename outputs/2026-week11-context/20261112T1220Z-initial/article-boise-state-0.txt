ESPN College GameDay Built by Home Depot
Boise State Announces Preliminary Details for ESPN’s College GameDay
11/11/2026 4:00:00 PM
|
Football, General
Share:
Calling Bronco Nation to fill DeChevrieux Field, located just steps from Albertsons Stadium
BOISE, Idaho
– ESPN's
College GameDay
Built by The Home Depot is back for its 40th season and will be live from Boise State for the first time since 2010 before the No. 24 Broncos face the Oregon State Beavers.
ESPN's
College GameDay
will host its three-hour live pregame show from DeChevrieux Field from 7-10 a.m. MT on Saturday, Nov. 14. DeChevrieux Field is a large grass practice field just west of Albertsons Stadium that sits along Bronco Circle and the west parking lot of Albertsons Stadium.
"Bronco Nation, this is our opportunity to show the country what makes Boise State and our community ELITE," said Boise State Athletic Director
Jeramiah Dickey
. "We need everyone to show up early, wear their blue and orange, bring the energy, and help us set a new College GameDay attendance record. Let's pack DeChevrieux Field, make some noise and show ESPN what it means to be part of Bronco Nation. It's time to raise the bar once again."
Date
Saturday, November 14
Gates Open
4:30 a.m. MT
Show Time
7-10 a.m. MT, with live TV hits starting around 5:40 a.m.
Location
On DeChevrieux Field, located on the west side of Albertsons Stadium.
Fans will be able to enter DeChevrieux Field from the north, west and east gates.
More information will be shared soon on which gate is used for entry for the premium seating area in front of the stage.
Tickets
College GameDay is free to attend and no tickets are required.
Parking
Fans Attending College GameDay
Parking on the Boise State campus will be limited due to the Bronco volleyball and football games later in the day.
The South Commuter Lot off Grant Street will be available for $20. This parking location will open at 2 a.m. on Saturday, November 14.
Parking
Football Season Parking Pass
Bronco fans with a football season parking pass in the east and west parking lots will be able to park beginning at 2 a.m. on Saturday, November 14.
Broadcast Channels
ESPN, ESPNU, ESPN App, Disney+
ESPN College GameDay Scavenger Hunt Comes to Boise State
The ESPN College GameDay Scavenger Hunt continues at Boise State University, giving students and fans an interactive way to engage with the show throughout the week leading up to Saturday's live broadcast. While registration is now open, the contest starts on Wednesday, November 11 and runs through Friday, November 13 at 3:30pm MST, via a
dedicated digital microsite
, with three challenges that include snapping photos of campus landmarks and answering trivia about ESPN College GameDay for a chance to win.
Fifteen winners will be selected at random from participants who complete all three tasks. Each winner, plus one guest, receives access to a premium seating area near the front of the ESPN College GameDay broadcast within camera view and exclusive ESPN College GameDay merchandise. Additional participants will also win official ESPN College GameDay merchandise.
ESPN collaborated with experiential agency Assembli on the interactive digital and on-site fan experience, and with Flowcode connecting the initiative inside the new College Football ESPN Fan House.
First 50 in Line for ESPN College GameDay to Score Prize Packs
Fans who show up early for ESPN College GameDay at Boise State University could score more than bragging rights. ESPN will reward the first 50 fans in line for Saturday's live broadcast with an exclusive ESPN College GameDay prize pack.
Prize packs will include a windbreaker, disposable camera, Jimmy John's chips and gift card, sunglasses, and more, and will be distributed ahead of Saturday morning's live ESPN College GameDay broadcast which begins at 7 a.m. MST.
Additional information is available at
College GameDay Hub
.  Follow @BroncoSportsFB, @BroncoSports, @Albertsons_Stad, @BoiseStateBAA  and @CollegeGameDay for updates.
About College GameDay Built by The Home Depot
ESPN's College GameDay Built by The Home Depot enters its 40th season in 2026 – and 33rd originating from game sites. College GameDay has won the Sports Emmy in 2026, 2025, 2024, 2018, 2017, 2016, 2015, 2014, 2011, 2010 and 2008 for best weekly studio show; in 2021, 2019 and 2018 for best technical studio team; in 2018 and 2023 for outstanding sports feature: short form; and in 2026 for outstanding edited sports special for "Not So Fast, My Friend: A Lee Corso Special," totaling 17 Sports Emmys for the premier college football pregame show. College GameDay airs every Saturday at 9 a.m. ET on ESPN, ESPNU, the ESPN App and Disney+ throughout the college football season.
