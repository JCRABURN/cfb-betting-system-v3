Riley Selected Semifinalist for Doak Walker Award
11/17/2026 10:00:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
BOISE, Idaho –
Boise State junior
Dylan Riley
was selected one of 10 semifinalists for the Doak Walker Award, the PwC SMU Athletic Forum announced. The award is presented annually to the nation's top running back.
Riley ranks third in the nation in rushing yards (1,291), rushing yards per game (129.1), all-purpose yards (1,623) and all-purpose yards per game (162.3). He averages 6.52 yards per carry, 11th-most in the country, and is tied for 14th in the FBS in rushing touchdowns (11) and total touchdowns (13). He leads the FBS with five 70+ yard scrimmage plays, scoring on four of them.
Riley has reached 100 rushing yards in a single half in six of Boise State's 10 games this season. He ran for a career-high 206 yards against Memphis on Sept. 12, then followed with a 198-yard effort against South Dakota on Sept. 19. The Moreno Valley, Calif. Native added 227 all-purpose yards and two touchdowns at Fresno State and totaled 300 all-purpose yards at Colorado State, one shy of the program record.
A product of Rancho Verde HS, Riley is a finalist for the Paul Hornung Award (nation's most versatile player) and a semifinalist for the Maxwell Award (nation's best player). He was tabbed Midseason All-America Second Team by the Associated Press and Sports Illustrated and is featured on the Pony Express Award Midseason Watch List with quarterback
Maddux Madsen
.
Riley earned Doak Walker National Running Back of the Week following his 206-yard rushing performance against Memphis and won Paul Hornung Player of the Week after his 300-yard all-purpose game at Colorado State. He is a three-time Paul Hornung Award Honor Roll selection and two-time Pac-12 Offensive Player of the Week.
Three finalists for the award will be selected on Nov. 24 and the winner will be announced during The Home Depot College Football Awards, which airs live on ESPN on Dec. 11. Riley is Boise State's second semifinalist in the last three years, joining Ashton Jeanty, who won the award in 2024.
No. 15/15/24 Boise State (9-1, 6-0 Pac-12) meets San Diego State (5-5, 4-2 Pac-12) for the final regular season game on The Blue, Nov. 21. The Broncos and Aztecs start at 7:30 p.m. on USA Sports.
--BroncoSports.com--
