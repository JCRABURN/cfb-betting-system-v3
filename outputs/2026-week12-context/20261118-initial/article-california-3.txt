Activities Will Take Place Across The Bay Area Prior To Saturday’s Rivalry Game
By:
Cal Athletics
A myriad of traditions and events associated with the Big Game will take place during the week leading up to the annual football contest between Bay Area rivals California and Stanford. Below are the highlights of known activities and public events in the Cal community the week leading up to the 129th Big Game at Databricks Field at California Memorial Stadium on Saturday, November 21. Kickoff is scheduled for 4:30 p.m. PT and the game will be televised by the ACC Network.
In addition, Wheeler Hall, Doe Library, the Campanile and Sather Gate on the Cal campus will be lit in Blue and Gold each night from sunset until 2 a.m. PT during the week leading up to Saturday's Big Game.
Monday, November 16
8:30 am - 2 pm – Cable Car Rally (Ghirardelli Square and Union Square Plaza, San Francisco, CA)
Wednesday, November 18
11:30 am – The Big Sail at St Francis Yacht Club, San Francisco, CA (99 Yacht Road, San Francisco, CA)
1 pm – Tree Chopping Rally (Savio Steps, Berkeley, CA)
Thursday, November 19
7 pm – Cal Men's Basketball vs. East Texas A&M (Haas Pavilion, Berkeley, CA)
8 pm – Night Rally (Berkeley, CA, starts at Bowles Hall, runs through campus and City of Berkeley, ending at Unit 3 Courtyard)
Friday, November 20
Noon – Noon Rally (Sproul Hall, Berkeley, CA)
6 pm (Doors)/Approximately 7 pm (Rally) – Bonfire Rally (Greek Theatre, Berkeley, CA)
7 pm – Cal Volleyball vs. Boston College (Haas Pavilion, Berkeley, CA)
Saturday, November 21
4:30 pm – The 129th Big Game vs. Stanford (Databricks Field at California Memorial Stadium, Berkeley, CA)
Post-game – Cal Football Postgame Live Show (Gate 1 California Memorial Stadium, Berkeley, CA)
Sunday, November 22
1 pm – Cal Volleyball vs. Syracuse (Haas Pavilion, Berkeley, CA)
*Check CalBears.com And Cal Athletics Social Media Channels For Updates On Event Schedule; Schedule Subject To Change; All Times Pacific
