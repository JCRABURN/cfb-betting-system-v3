Bulldogs In The NFL: Week 10 Recap
November 18, 2026
|
Football
Share:
By:
Kyle Tatelbaum, UGAAA Sports Communications
ATHENS-----As Week 10 of the 2026 NFL season ended, former Georgia Bulldogs continued to make their presence felt across the league.
James Cook delivered a season-high 157 rushing yards and extended his touchdown streak to eight games for Buffalo. Matthew Stafford threw for three touchdowns for Los Angeles, and George Pickens tied his career high with 11 receptions while recording the first three-touchdown game of his career for Dallas.
Here is a look at how former Georgia players performed in Week 10.
Washington Commanders 24, New York Giants 21
Andrew Thomas, OT: Played all 65 offensive snaps for the Giants, allowing just one pressure on 40 pass-blocking plays.
Buffalo Bills 31, New York Jets 12
James Cook, RB: Led the Bills on the ground, finishing with a season-high 157 yards on 18 carries while extending his touchdown streak to eight games following a 74-yard run for a score.
Carolina Panthers 20, New Orleans Saints 17
Trevor Etienne, RB: Tallied three carries for a yard plus caught two passes for nine yards on 11 snaps and had three punt returns for 43 yards including a long of 20 yards.
Monroe Freeling
, OT: Played all 70 offensive snaps for New Orleans, delivering 35 pass blocks.
Oscar Delp
, TE: Did not record any offensive statistics through seven snaps for the Saints.
Zamir White, RB: Did not record any statistics for New Orleans.
Cleveland Browns 27, Houston Texans 24
Tyson Campbell, CB: Appeared on 64 of 67 defensive snaps for Cleveland, deflecting three passes while recording five tackles, one for loss.
Kamari Lassiter, CB: Played all 68 defensive snaps for Houston, deflecting a pass while making five tackles, including a loss.
Jacksonville Jaguars 40, Tennessee Titans 23
Jordan Davis, DT: Ruled inactive by the Jaguars after being traded from the Philadelphia Eagles the week prior.
Travon Walker, DE: Appeared on 53 defensive snaps for Jacksonville, recording one tackle and a pass deflection.
Nazir Stackhouse, DT: Took advantage of limited playing time for the Titans, making four tackles on 23 snaps.
Kansas City Chiefs 35, Atlanta Falcons 13
Zachariah Branch
, WR: Ruled out by the Falcons with a hamstring injury, marking his second consecutive missed game.
Charlie Woerner, TE: Appeared on 32 offensive snaps for Atlanta, making five pass blocks without recording any other statistics.
Los Angeles Rams 34, Arizona Cardinals 6
Stetson Bennett IV, QB: Served as the backup quarterback for Los Angeles.
Warren McClendon Jr., OL: Played all 76 offensive snaps for the Rams, making 28 pass blocks without allowing a pressure.
Matthew Stafford, QB: Completed 18 of 26 passes for 249 yards and three touchdowns, now ranking fourth in the NFL in passing yards despite no passing attempts in the fourth quarter.
Indianapolis Colts 24, Miami Dolphins 22
CJ Allen
, LB: Earned a Pepsi Rookie of the Week nomination for recording a team-high nine tackles and one sack, playing all 69 defensive snaps for Indianapolis. He has made 29 tackles in three games this month.
Sedrick Van Pran-Granger, OL: Appeared as the Colts' backup center, recording no statistics.
Malik Herring, DE: Played 18 defensive snaps for the Dolphins without recording any statistics.
Minnesota Vikings 30, Green Bay Packers 10
Chaz Chambliss, OLB: Saw limited action for the Vikings, making two tackles.
Tyrion Ingram-Dawkins, DE: Ruled out and listed on the inactive list by Minnesota due to an ankle injury sustained in Week 9.
Brett Thorson
, P: Booted three punts for 177 yards for an average of 59.0, pinning two inside the 20-yard line for the Vikings.
Ben Yurosek, TE: Tallied 40 offensive snaps for Minnesota, catching his only target for seven yards.
Javon Bullard, S: Played 36 defensive snaps for the Packers, making five tackles, two of which for loss.
Devonte Wyatt, DT: Appeared on 42 defensive snaps for Green Bay, tallying one tackle.
Detroit Lions 35, New England Patriots 10
Tate Ratledge, OL: Played 61 of 71 offensive snaps for Detroit, making 33 pass blocks without surrendering a pressure.
Jared Wilson, OL: Appeared on 53 of 59 snaps for the Patriots at center, recording 33 pass blocks.
Cincinnati Bengals 38, Pittsburgh Steelers 18
Dylan Fairchild, OL: Tallied 63 of 66 offensive snaps for Cincinnati, recording 46 pass blocks while allowing just one pressure.
Amarius Mims, OL: Played flawlessly on the Bengals' offensive line, making 46 pass blocks on 63 snaps.
Colbie Young
, WR: Appeared on 29 offensive snaps for Cincinnati and recorded no statistics.
Daylen Everette
, CB: Logged 28 defensive snaps for the Steelers and recorded three tackles.
Darnell Washington, TE: Notched 30 offensive snaps for Pittsburgh, catching one of three targets for five yards.
San Francisco 49ers 46, Dallas Cowboys 40
Mykel Williams, DL: Tallied 31 defensive snaps for the 49ers, making two tackles, including a half sack.
Broderick Jones, OT: Saw limited action for the Cowboys, appearing on six snaps without recording any statistics.
George Pickens, WR: Led Dallas offensively, tying his career high in receptions with 11 for 145 yards and three touchdowns in the first three-touchdown game of his career.
Seattle Seahawks 21, Las Vegas Raiders 13
Brock Bowers, TE: Caught each of his two targets for 18 yards for Las Vegas before exiting the game due to injury in the first half.
Nakobe Dean, LB: Played all 57 defensive snaps for the Raiders, making eight tackles, including a tackle for loss while recovering a fumble.
Eric Stokes, CB: Tallied 52 defensive snaps for Las Vegas, deflecting a pass while recording three tackles.
Quay Walker, LB: Appeared on 57 defensive snaps for the Raiders, collecting eight tackles, including a tackle for loss and recovering a fumble.
Los Angeles Chargers 23, Baltimore Ravens 20
Ladd McConkey, WR: Ruled out by the Chargers due to back spasms and did not travel with the team.
John Jenkins, DT: Saw limited action for Baltimore, making a tackle for loss on 10 snaps.
Nick Moore, LS: Served as the long snapper for the Ravens.
Roquan Smith, LB: Recorded eight tackles for Baltimore on all 57 defensive snaps.
Malaki Starks, S: Appeared on 54 of 57 defensive snaps for the Ravens, deflecting a pass while making four tackles.
