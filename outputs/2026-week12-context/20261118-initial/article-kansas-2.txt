Jayhawks Welcome No. 12 BYU to Lawrence for Senior Day
11/16/2026 10:45:00 AM

Share:
LAWRENCE, Kan. –
Following a convincing 37-14 road win over West Virginia, the Kansas Jayhawks are set to return to David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium on Saturday to host No. 12 BYU in a Senior Day clash set for 11 a.m.
The game will be televised by ABC with Bob Wischusen, Louis Riddick and Stormy Buonantony on the call.
Kansas (4-6, 2-5 Big 12) dominated last time out against in Morgantown, rushing out to a 30-7 halftime lead over the Mountaineers. Quarterback
Isaiah Marshall
had a strong performance, rushing for a career-high 164 yards and a touchdown, while completing 15-of-18 passes for 152 yards through the air. The Jayhawks also got two rushing touchdowns each from
Chase Belcher
and Khijohn Cummings-Coleman.
Defensively, Kansas held West Virginia to just 262 yards of total offense and had nine tackles-for-loss and four sacks in the win.
Leroy Harris III
collected two more tackles for loss and one more sack in the win, bringing his season totals to 16 tackles-for-loss and 7.5 sacks. The 16 TFLs are tied for first in the Big 12 and fourth in the country and the 7.5 sacks are second in the conference and 16
th
nationally.
Harris, linebacker
Trey Lathan
, defensive end
David Santiago
and others have helped lead a defense that ranks third in total defense in the league and second in passing defense. Kansas is giving up 335.6 yards per game and just 190.1 passing yards a game. West Virginia managed just 35 rushing yards against Kansas last game.
The game will feature two of the best defenses in the conference. BYU ranks first in the league in total defense, second in rushing defense, third in scoring defense and fourth in passing defense. The Cougars are led by a trio of defenders that were all named to the Preseason All-Big 12 Team in linebacker Cade Uluave, safety Faletau Satuala and defensive lineman Keanu Tanuvasa. Uluave leads the team with 61 tackles, 5.5 tackles-for-loss and 2.5 sacks. Satuala has 54 tackles, four tackles-for-loss and an interception, While Tanuvasa has 7.5 tackles-for-loss from the line.
Offensively for BYU, quarterback Bear Bachmeier leads the charge. He's thrown for 2,126 yards and 14 touchdowns on the season, while rushing for 449 yards and 10 touchdowns. He leads the team in rushing, however, star running back LJ Martin has returned from injury and is averaging 7.2 yards per carry in six games this season. Legend Glasker leads the receiving room with 42 catches for 706 yards and five touchdowns.
The Jayhawks and Cougars have met just three times prior all-time with Kansas winning all three matchups. Kansas first beat BYU in 1992, before topping the Cougars in conference play in 2023 and 2024, first in Lawrence and then in Provo.
At No. 12 in the latest AP Top 25, BYU will be the highest-ranked team to play at KU since the Jayhawks beat No. 6 Oklahoma in 2023. Kansas has at least one ranked win in three of the past four seasons under coach
Lance Leipold
.
