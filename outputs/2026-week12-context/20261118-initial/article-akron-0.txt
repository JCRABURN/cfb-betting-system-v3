Game Notes
MACTION HEADS TO UMASS:
The University of Akron football team closes out the road portion of its 2026 schedule on Wednesday, heading to UMass to face the Minutemen in McGuirk Alumni Stadium. Kickoff is set for  7 p.m. ET with coverage on CBS Sports Network and the Akron Sports Radio Network via flagship 640 WHLO-AM.
THE SERIES:
Wednesday marks the seventh game in an all-time series led 4-2 by Akron. This is only the second matchup in Amherst, where UMass holds a 1-0 lead with a 37-29 win in 2019. Two other road tests took place in Foxborough.
Zips Need to Know
1.
Akron closes out the road schedule on Wednesday, ahead of a Black Friday, Senior Day matchup with Buffalo on Nov. 27 at noon inside InfoCision Stadium..
2.
Akron brings back 59 players from the 2025 squad — 30 offense, 26 defense and 3 specialists.
3.
Akron's final four games of the regular season will be played on weeknights or weekdays with two Tuesdays, one Wednesday and a Friday. All told, UA had five midweek games in 2026.
4.
Akron football is playing its 127th season in 2026 and is celebrating its 40th season in the NCAA FBS. The program dates to 1891 and was the first to make the jump from Division I-AA or FCS in 1987.
5.
The Zips' Offensive Coordinator of the Game presented by Arby's Steak Nuggets promotion was the buzz of the college football world leading up to the season. Season ticket holder Marty Whims II assisted football Coach Moorhead in designing the script for Akron's opening drive against Robert Morris on Sept. 12.

Streaks, Storylines & Sidebars
Akron football is playing its 127th season in 2026 and is celebrating its 40th season in the NCAA FBS.
The program dates to 1891 and was the first to make the jump from Division I-AA or FCS in 1987.
Former All-American and NFL HOF defensive end, Jason Taylor, was inducted to the Mid-American Conference Hall of Fame in May.
Head Coach
Joe Moorhead
is in his fifth season at the helm of the Akron program.
Akron returns 59 players from the 2025 squad — 30 offense, 26 defense and 3 specialists.
The Zips added 48 newcomers — 21 offense, 23 defense and 4 specials.
Of the 48 newcomers, 32 are transfers and 16 are freshmen.
The Zips return 16 starters — 9 offensive starters and 7 defensive starters.
UA ended 2025 on a 3-1 stretch, defeating BGSU, UMass and Buffalo while falling to Kent State
WILLIAMS FINDS THE END ZONE TWICE:
Marcel Williams
caught five passes for 85 yards and two touchdowns against Western Michigan, scoring on receptions of 35 and 39 yards. He leads Akron with 42 receptions for 502 yards and five receiving touchdowns this season.
BROUGHTON'S EFFICIENT RETURN:
Since returning to the starting lineup against Ohio,
Cibastian Broughton
has completed 34 of 52 passes for 405 yards and two touchdowns. He completed 66.7% of his passes against Western Michigan and has thrown just one interception in 118 attempts this season.
GANT NEARING 800 YARDS:
Jordan Gant
has rushed for 791 yards and seven touchdowns on 152 carries, averaging 5.2 yards per attempt. He needs nine rushing yards to reach 800 this season and has accumulated 1,823 yards in two seasons at Akron.
DAVIS' BREAKOUT SEASON:
Tairan Davis recorded a career-high 15 tackles against Western Michigan, including 13 solo stops. He ranks third on the team with 58 tackles, 41 solo, after recording just two tackles in nine appearances during the 2025 season.
JOHNSON SETTING THE PACE:
Donelius Johnson
leads Akron with 89 tackles, including 46 solo stops and seven tackles for loss. He has registered nine tackles in each of the Zips' last two games and averages 8.9 stops per contest.
FOURTH-QUARTER PRODUCTION:
Akron has scored a season-high 68 points in the fourth quarter, accounting for 36.6% of its 186 points. The Zips have scored 38 in the first quarter, 44 in the second and 36 in the third.
QUICK-STRIKE SCORING:
Akron's two touchdowns against Western Michigan came on drives of 74 and 75 yards, requiring just seven combined plays and 2:58 of possession time. Broughton connected with Williams on both scoring plays.
TURNOVER-FREE FOOTBALL:
Akron did not commit a turnover against Western Michigan, its third turnover-free game of the season. The Zips also played without a giveaway against Wake Forest and Eastern Michigan.
THE TURNOVER TIRE RETURNS!
Defensive Turnovers mean the return of the Turnover Tire for the team from the Rubber City. A Zips player who corrals a turnover has the honor of wearing a branded tire around his neck on the sidelines.
