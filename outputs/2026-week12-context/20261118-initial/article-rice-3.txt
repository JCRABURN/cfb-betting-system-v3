Owls fall 20-17 on Homecoming
By:
Chuck Pool (cpool@rice.edu)
Houston-
Tulane stormed back from a 11-point deficit to hold Rice scoreless in the second half and spoil the Owls' Homecoming with a 20-17 win on Saturday at First Community Stadium.
How it Happened
—The game was pushed back two hours to account for the Green Wave's late arrival in Houston after bussing down I-10 after their flight was grounded during Friday's disruption of flights across the country. Both teams hit the field with their offenses clicking, scoring on their first two possessions after long drives.   Rice took the opening kickoff and drove to the Tulane nine before settling for
Tommy Bauchiero's
fifth field goal of the season. Tulane responded with a 15-play, 58-yard drive for a tying field goal.
Koa Akui
forced the Green Wave to settle for the three, dropping quarterback Dagan Bruno for a four-yard loss on third and three. The Owls ate up the rest of the opening quarter with a march from their own 25, finding the end zone four minutes into the second quarter when
Gael Ochoa
combined with
Max Mosey
on a four-yard score.  Tulane moved to the Rice 24 on its next possession, but settled for its second field goal to cut the Rice lead to 10-6. Tulane forced the first punt of the game on Rice's next opportunity and looked to be in position to take the lead, but
Jamir Benjamin
jumped a throw from Tulane's Trace Johnson at the Rice 38 and raced down the east sideline for the Owls' first pick-six in two seasons to build the lead to 17-6.   The Green Wave threatened on their first effort of the third quarter as a 45-yard burst by Maurice Turner set them up at the Rice 23. But the Owls' defense rose to the occasion on fourth and two as
Luca Akirtava
forced Bruno into an errant throw on fourth down to turn Tulane over on downs at the Owls' 15. Rice could only move to the 29 before being forced to punt  , and Tulane launched another lengthy drive, this time to a touchdown with 3:08 left in the third to close to 17-13.   After trading punts, Tulane recovered a Rice fumble at the Owls' 34-yard line and needed just three plays to move in front, 20-17. Rice mounted one last charge, covering 55 yards in 11 plays, but Ochoa was ruled to have been stopped short at the Tulane 20 on fourth down with 2:32 left.   The Owls had one last shot, taking over at their 28 with 1:03 remaining, and converted one fourth down,  but saw the game end on  their 36 as time ran out.
Up Next:
Rice has a quick turnaround with a Thursday night road game at Temple looming ahead.   The Owls will wrap up the 2026 season at First Community Stadium on November 28.
Notes:
Series vs. Tulane
This was the 39th meeting between Rice and Tulane.
The Owls are now 20-18-1 against the Green Wave and 13-8 at home.
Team Notes

,

18 Owls have made their first career start this season.
Alberding's
first start came on the day the Owls brought back their wing decals that were first introduced in 1990. Alberding's father, Shawn, was a three-year starter on the defensive line from 1990-92, including the debut of the wings on 9/1/90 when Rice defeated Wake Forest 33-17.
Rice scored a field goal on the team's opening possession of the first half, the sixth time in the last nine halves the Owls scored on their opening possession.
#12
Jamir Benjamin
had the team's first pick-six since Tyson Flowers (34 yards) against Texas Southern (9/7/24).
Individual Notes
#3 S
Koa Akui
Had his first interception of the season, and sixth of his career, to close the first half.
Finished with eight tackles and 1.5 tackles for loss and leads the Owls with 10.5 TFL's, all of which have come in the last eight games.
#4 QB
Gael Ochoa
Ran for 82 yards and now has 405 this season.
His 405 yards rank 26
th
at Rice among quarterbacks, and he and
Jacurri Brown
have combined to rush for 1,074 this year.
#12 CB
Jamir Benjamin
Had his first career interception, a 62-yard pick-six with just 31 seconds left in the first half.
It was the longest return of an interception by an Owl since Gabe Taylor had a 72-yard return vs. Tulane in 2023 and the longest pick six since Taylor scored on a 91-yard return vs. McNeese in 2022.
#54 LB
Austin Harnetiaux
Led the team with nine tackles to increase his career-best total to 65.
#49 K
Tommy Bauchiero
Bauchiero connected on a 27-yard field goal on the opening drive of the game, his fifth in the last five games since taking over as the Owls' placekicker.
