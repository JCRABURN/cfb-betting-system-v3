Game Time Set for Illinois' Nov. 28 Game at Northwestern
November 16, 2026
|
Football
Share:
News
CHAMPAIGN, Ill. —
Illinois' regular-season finale at Northwestern will kick off at 11:30 a.m. CT on Saturday, Nov. 28 on Big Ten Network, the conference office and television partners announced Monday.
The Fighting Illini have won two straight in the series and look to retain the Land of Lincoln Trophy for the third consecutive year in their first visit to Northwestern's new Ryan Field.
Friday, Nov. 27
11 a.m. CT - Nebraska at Iowa (CBS)
6:30 p.m. CT - Minnesota at Wisconsin (NBC)
Saturday, Nov. 28
11 a.m. CT - Michigan at Ohio State (FOX)
11:30 a.m. CT - Illinois at Northwestern (BTN)
2:30 p.m. CT - USC at UCLA (CBS)
2:30 p.m. CT - Penn State at Maryland (FS1)
3:15 p.m. CT - Michigan State at Rutgers (BTN)
Games being held for six-day selection
Washington at Oregon
Purdue at Indiana
