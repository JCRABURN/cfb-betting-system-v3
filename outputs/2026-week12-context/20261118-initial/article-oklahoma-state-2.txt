Bowick a Biletnikoff Award semifinalist
November 16, 2026
|
Cowboy Football
Share:
STILLWATER
– Oklahoma State wide receiver
Justin Bowick
is one of 13 semifinalists for the 2026 Biletnikoff Award, announced Monday by the Tallahassee Quarterback Club (TQC) Foundation, Inc.
He is one of two Big 12 semifinalists for the honor, which annually recognizes the college football season's outstanding FBS pass catcher, regardless of position.
Bowick has 49 catches for 868 yards and 10 touchdowns this season. His 10 receiving touchdowns rank as the most in the Big 12, the fourth-most in the Power 4 and the sixth-most in the FBS. He also has 17 catches of least 20 yards, ranking as the sixth-most in the nation.
The Biletnikoff Award's semifinalists, three finalists, and award recipient are selected by the Biletnikoff Award National Selection Committee, a group of 650 prominent college football journalists, commentators, announcers, Biletnikoff Award winners, and other former receivers.
A small number of single-game tickets remain for Oklahoma State's final home game, scheduled for November 28 against Kansas. Tickets are available at
www.okstate.com/tickets
or by contacting the ticket office at 877-ALL-4-OSU.
