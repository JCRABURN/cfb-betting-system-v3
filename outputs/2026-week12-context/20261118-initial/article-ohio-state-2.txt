Fahey Earns Big Ten Player of the Week Honors
11/16/2026 2:57:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
In Saturday’s win vs. Northwestern, Fahey completed 21-of-32 passes for 201 yards and three touchdown passes.
By:
Andrew Kovar
ROSEMONT, Ill. –
Ohio State freshman quarterback
Luke Fahey
was named the Big Ten Freshman of the Week, as announced by the conference on Monday afternoon.
Fahey helped lead the Buckeyes to their 12-straight win over Northwestern and 17th consecutive against the Wildcats at Ohio Stadium. With the 28-19 victory against Northwestern, Ohio State grabbed its 49th win at home since the start of the 2019 season, which is the highest total in the country.
Fahey, making his third career start, completed 21-of-32 passes for 201 yards and three touchdowns, connecting with junior wide receiver
Jeremiah Smith
, sophomore running back
Isaiah West
and senior wide receiver
Devin McCuin
.
For the season he is 87-of-133 for 1,024 yards and eight touchdowns. The Ohio State true freshman record for yards is 1,311 by Terrelle Pryor in 2008 and touchdowns passes is 13 by Braxton Miller in 2011. Additionally, he is just the fourth Ohio State true freshman to throw for over 1,000 yards joining Art Schlichter in 1978, Pryor in 2008 and Miller in 2011.
