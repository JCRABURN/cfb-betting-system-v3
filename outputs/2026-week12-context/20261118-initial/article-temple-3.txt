PHILADELPHIA –
Temple football closes its 2026 home schedule on Thursday, November 19, when Rice visits Lincoln Financial Field for the first meeting between the two programs. Kickoff is set for 7:30 p.m. ET, with the game televised on ESPN and available on OwlSports.com/listen.

Things to Look for in Week 12...
1
First Time for Everything
Thursday marks the first meeting between Temple and Rice. Temple is 5-7 in its first games against the other 12 current American Conference members and 3-3 in such debuts since joining the league in 2013. The Owls most recently won a first meeting against Florida Atlantic, 18-15 in overtime, on November 16, 2024.
2
Battle of the Owls
Temple and Rice are two of four current FBS programs named the Owls, along with Florida Atlantic and Kennesaw State. Rice will be the second different Owl-nicknamed FBS opponent Temple has faced; the first was Florida Atlantic, which Temple defeated in overtime in 2024.
3
Thursday Night Lights
Temple plays its first Thursday game since hosting Army on September 26, 2024. This is the Owls' 34th regular-season Thursday contest (13-17-3 all-time). Their last Thursday victory came at South Florida in 2019, while their last Thursday home win was a 31-17 decision over Tulsa on September 20, 2018.
4
A Familiar Foe?
Although Temple and Rice have never played, head coach
K.C. Keeler
faced Rice while leading Sam Houston in 2024. His Bearkats defeated Rice, 34-14, in Houston on August 31, jumping ahead 17-0 in the first quarter and 24-7 at halftime.
5
Ground Control
Temple has rushed for 1,742 yards through 10 games and reached at least 140 rushing yards eight times, including 159 against UAB. Rice has surrendered 1,759 rushing yards in 10 games (175.9 per game), setting up a ground-game matchup for the Owls.
6
Curly's Command
Curly Ordonez
set a career high with 16 total tackles against UAB, his fifth double-digit tackle game of 2026. He also had two tackles for loss, a sack and a pass breakup. Ordonez enters the Rice game with 100 total tackles and 61 solo tackles; the notes list him as the FBS leader in solo tackles.
7
JoJo on the Go Go
JoJo Bermudez
has 295 punt-return yards this season, just 10 short of the school record set by Anthony Young in 1982. Bermudez had a school-record 159 punt-return yards at Navy, and Temple enters the week leading the FBS at 22.38 punt-return yards per game.

Close Calls
Losses by 2 to UTSA, 2 to FAU and 3 to Tulane
Rice enters the first meeting with Temple at 1-9 overall and 0-6 in American Conference play under second-year head coach Scott Abell. Rice opened the year with a 31-3 victory over Houston Christian before dropping nine straight games. Despite its record, Rice has played several close contests, including a 16-14 loss to UTSA, a 51-49 setback to Florida Atlantic and a 20-17 loss to Tulane. Against the Green Wave, Rice led 17-6 at halftime before Tulane scored 14 unanswered second-half points. Rice features a run-heavy offense, averaging 265.7 rushing yards per game according to the game notes' statistical comparison, while allowing 175.9 rushing yards per contest. The Owls finish their regular season against Army on November 28.
