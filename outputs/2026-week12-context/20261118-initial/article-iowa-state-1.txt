Flora a Doak Walker Award Semifinalist
11.17.2026
|
Football
Share:
AMES, Iowa –
Aiden Flora
is one of 10 semifinalists for the 2026 Doak Walker Award, which is presented annually to the nation's top running back, the SMU Athletic Forum announced Tuesday.
Flora, one of three Big 12 running backs among the semifinalists, is the first Cyclone to be named a semifinalist for the award since Breece Hall was in back-to-back seasons in 2020 and 2021.
With two weeks remaining in the regular season, Flora leads the nation in rushing (1,335) and is fourth nationally averaging 7.2 yards per carry. His 1,335 rushing yards rank eighth on ISU's all-time single-season record list. He has 12 rushing touchdowns on the season, the 12
th
-most nationally.

The Adel, Iowa native has a knack for big games as the only player in the country with three games of 200-plus rushing yards and multiple rushing touchdowns this season. He has rushed for 175-plus yards in three-consecutive games, the first Cyclone in history to do so and the first FBS player to do so since Boise State's Ashton Jeanty in 2024. He is the sixth Big 12 player in history to accomplish the feat and the first since 2004 (Cedric Benson, Texas).
Flora is responsible for the three best, and five of the top eight, single-game rushing performances in the Big 12 this season (221, 216, 212, 194, 182). He joins Heisman Trophy finalist Troy Davis as the only Cyclones with three 200-yard rushing games in a single season (1995 & 1996).
A walk-on turned scholarship player, Flora is a threat to score from anywhere on the field with four rushing touchdowns of 60-plus yards, tied for the most nationally. His six rushing touchdowns of 40-plus yards are also tied for the most in FBS this season.
Against five of ISU's opponents this season, Flora has rushed for the most yards that defense has allowed in a game all season.
Flora leads the country with 41 rushes of 10-plus yards this season.
Three finalists will be announced Tuesday, Nov. 24. The winner will be unveiled live on ESPN during The Home Depot College Football Awards on Friday, Dec. 11.
Flora and the Cyclones are back in action at UCF Friday at 5 p.m. (CT) on FS1.
