Check out this season's fifth edition of the Olive & Blue Revieux following Tulane football's 20-17 road win over Rice on Saturday.
Listen back to all the best moments from the victory over the Owls! Plus, postgame analysis with Steve Barrios and Maddy Hudak, and
Corey Gloor
's locker room interviews with Coach
Will Hall
and LB
Makai Williams
and WR
Bredell Richardson
!
