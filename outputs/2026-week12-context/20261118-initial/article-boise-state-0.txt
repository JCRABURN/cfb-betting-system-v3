Broncos Secure Three More Pac-12 Player of the Week Awards
11/16/2026 1:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
BOISE, Idaho –
For the second straight week, Boise State secured three Pac-12 weekly honors, the conference announced. Wide receiver
Rasean Jones
was named Freshman of the Week for the fourth consecutive week and added his second Offensive Player of the Week honor of the year. Defensive back
Travis Anderson
earned Defensive Player of the Week for the first time this season. The duo helped the Broncos to a 46-41 win over Oregon State, clinching hosting duties in the Pac-12 Championship.
Jones was dominant against the Beavers, catching 11 passes for 230 yards and three touchdowns. His 230 yards are the fifth-most in program history and he is one of three FBS players to notch a 10-catch, 200-yard, 3-touchdown performance this season, joining Jeremiah Smith (Ohio State) and Malachi Toney (Miami). The freshman scored on receptions of 19, 16 and 26 yards, with the final score coming on a fourth down conversion in the fourth quarter.
Anderson notched his fourth interception of the season, returning it 58 yards for his first career pick six. He also had a team-high eight tackles, including six solo. The junior added a solo TFL and one pass breakup in the win. His four interceptions rank eighth in the FBS this season.
This is the second straight week Boise State has collected three conference awards.
Dylan Riley
secured Offensive Player of the Week, Jones was tabbed Freshman of the Week and
Terrious Favors
was named Special Teams Player of the Week after the Broncos' 64-38 win at Colorado State. The Broncos are up to 11 weekly honors from the Pac-12 this season. Jones has won Freshman of the Week four straight times and added Offensive honors twice to put his total at six. He is the first Bronco to secure four consecutive weekly awards. Anderson is being recognized for the first time in his career.
No. 15/15/25 Boise State (9-1, 6-0 Pac-12) hosts San Diego State (5-5, 4-2 Pac-12) in the regular season home finale on Nov. 21. The Broncos and Aztecs clash at 7:30 p.m. in Albertsons Stadium, with the game airing on USA Sports.
--BroncoSports.com--
