Zachman Honored by Big Ten
11/16/2026 1:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
ROSEMONT, Ill. –
Seventh-year defensive back
Preston Zachman
was tabbed Big Ten Co-Defensive Player of the Week on Monday (Nov. 16) after his double-digit tackle effort against No. 14/15/18 USC. The Big Ten Conference office announced Zachman and UCLA cornerback Scooter Jackson as co-players of the week on Monday (Nov. 2).
Zachman posted his second career double-digit tackle game and first since 2024 as a member of the Wisconsin Badgers. His 10 stops ranked second on the team – and second in the game – and he added his first career forced fumble and first interception as a Hoosier.
His interception of Jayden Maiava came in the second quarter and led to Indiana's nine-play, 29-yard drive that ended in a field goal. He also forced a first-quarter fumble that was recovered by USC.
His defensive effort was a large part of a defense that limited USC to just 13 points to mark just the fifth time in 63 games that USC was held under 20 points. Of Lincoln Riley's 128 games as a head coach, he has only been held under 14 points twice (at Wisconsin, 2026; at Indiana, 2026).
Zachman is the first Indiana defender to earn Big Ten Defensive Player of the Week since
Aiden Fisher
in 2025. He is the first safety to garner a Big Ten weekly award since
Jamier Johnson
against Penn State in 2020.
