James Named MW Defensive Player of the Week
11/16/2026 11:15:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
LAS VEGAS - North Dakota State's
Ray James Jr.
was named Old Trapper Mountain West Defensive Player of the Week by the conference office Monday, Nov. 16. It is the first player of the week award of his career.
James, a third-year linebacker from Minneapolis, Minn., made a game-high 10 tackles and scored on a 49-yard interception return in the fourth quarter to help seal NDSU's 21-13 road victory over Hawaii. He is second on the team this year with 49 total tackles and three interceptions.
The NDSU defense limited Hawaii to a season-low 262 yards of total offense and had two takeaways including defensive tackle
Logan Larson's
first-quarter interception that set up a Bison touchdown two plays later.
James is the 10th NDSU player to earn an MW player of the week honor this year, and the Bison have claimed 15 awards altogether.
North Dakota State (8-2, 5-2 MW) closes out conference play against Northern Illinois at 3 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 21, in the annual Harvest Bowl game at Gate City Bank Field at the Fargodome.
