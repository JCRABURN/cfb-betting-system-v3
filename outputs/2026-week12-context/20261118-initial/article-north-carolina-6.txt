Three Tar Heels Set To Compete In 113th Grey Cup
November 15, 2026
|
Football
Share:
By:
Noah Hughes
CHAPEL HILL, N.C. –
Three former North Carolina football standouts, defensive linemen Malik Carney, Tomari Fox and Noah Taylor, are headed to Canadian football's biggest stage. The trio and the Edmonton Elks' defensive unit will face the Montreal Alouettes in the Canadian Football League's (CFL) 113th Grey Cup on Sunday, Nov. 15.
The trio helped lead the Elks to a 13-5 regular-season record and first place in the West Division, earning a first-round bye and a direct path to the West Final. Edmonton went on to beat the BC Lions in that game to book its spot in the Grey Cup.
Here is a look at each Tar Heel's path to the championship game.
Carney, an Alexandria, Va., native, played in Chapel Hill from 2014 to 2018, transitioning from linebacker to a dominant defensive end. He made 10 starts in 2016 and became a full-time starter in 2017, finishing his UNC career with 90 solo tackles, 86 assisted tackles, 33 tackles for loss and 17.5 sacks.
Carney is in his fifth CFL season and first with Edmonton, earning his first CFL All-Star selection this year after tallying 33 tackles, 11 sacks, four forced fumbles and three tackles for loss in 17 games. He is making his second consecutive Grey Cup appearance, having won the 2025 title with the Saskatchewan Roughriders. In the West Final win over the BC Lions, Carney recorded two tackles and a sack to help clinch Edmonton's spot in the title game.
Fox, a Lawrenceville, Ga., native, played for the Tar Heels from 2019 to 2023 and made an immediate impact, appearing in 12 games as a true freshman and starting against App State and Clemson. He went on to start 15 more games over the rest of his UNC career and was named to the All-ACC Academic Team in 2020. He finished with 44 solo tackles, 56 assisted tackles, 10.5 tackles for loss and 6.5 sacks.
Fox signed with Edmonton in April 2025. He played in the Elks' first three games and recorded one tackle before an ankle injury on July 1, 2025, sent him to the injured list. He was activated on June 23, 2026, and has played 10 games this season, recording 10 tackles and one sack.
Taylor, a Silver Spring, Md., native, transferred to UNC for his 2022 graduate season after four seasons at Virginia. He started all eight games he played at outside linebacker, posting 28 tackles, six tackles for loss, two quarterback pressures and two pass breakups. His 3.5 sacks tied for the team lead, and he registered a season-high seven tackles and a pass breakup that led to an interception in the final seconds of the win at Duke.
Taylor tore his ACL in October 2022 and did not return to the field until signing with the Elks in July 2024. In 42 games since, he has totaled 67 tackles, three tackles for loss, 16 sacks and one forced fumble. In the West Final win over BC, he recorded three solo tackles and a sack.
The 2026 Grey Cup kicks off at 6 p.m. ET on Sunday, Nov. 15, at McMahon Stadium in Calgary, Alberta.
