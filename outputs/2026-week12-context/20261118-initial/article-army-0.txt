College GameDay to be at MetLife Stadium for 127th Army-Navy Game Presented by USAA
November 14, 2026
|
Football
Share:
EAST RUTHERFORD, N.J.
(November 14) – College GameDay Built by The Home Depot, ESPN's premier college football pregame show, will make its second visit to MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, N.J., for the 127th playing of the Army-Navy Game Presented by USAA on Saturday, Dec. 12,  — the show's 10th all-time appearance at 'America's Game.' The two-hour live program will air from 10 a.m. to noon ET on ESPN and ESPNU, originating from outside MetLife Stadium in Lot J.
For Army, the ESPN program represents another moment in the national spotlight. Fox's
Big Noon Kickoff
and
Fox NFL Sunday
each visited the U.S. Military Academy campus on November 7 and November 8, respectively.
Army will be the first non-power conference team to be featured in
Big Noon Kickoff
and
College Gameday
in the same season.
College GameDay returns to the Army-Navy game for the first time since 2023 in Foxborough, Mass. The show's previous Army-Navy stops include East Rutherford, N.J. (2021); West Point, N.Y. (2020); Philadelphia (2015, 2017-19); and Baltimore (2014, 2016).
Rece Davis returns as host, joined at the desk by analysts Kirk Herbstreit, Desmond Howard, Pat McAfee and Nick Saban. Rounding out the cast are college football insider Pete Thamel, college football analyst "Stanford Steve" Coughlin and reporters Jen Lada and Jess Sims.
Army West Point Athletic Association, Inc. (AWPAA)
AWPAA is an IRS code 501(c)(3) non-profit corporation established to execute the United States Military Academy's intercollegiate athletics program.  AWPAA is led by a board of directors that provides guidance to the director of athletics, who serves as the primary advisor to the USMA Superintendent and the USMA leadership on all NCAA operations. The AWPAA mission is to recruit, educate, train and inspire leaders of character who are committed to the Army values through an extraordinary Division I athletic experience.
