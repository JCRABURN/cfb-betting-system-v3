Cougars remain ranked in College Football Playoff Top 20
HOUSTON –
The Houston Football team remained in the Top 25 of the College Football Playoff's third rankings of the season announced Tuesday night.
The #20 ranking reflects the same ranking the Cougars (7-3, 4-3 Big 12) have in the AP Poll from Monday afternoon.
Houston heads on the road its final road game of the regular season at West Virginia on Nov. 21.
The Cougars are seeking a 10-win season for the second consecutive year – what would be a program first – with two regular season games to play in addition to Houston's bowl game.
