Baugh Named Doak Walker Award Semifinalist
Tuesday, November 17, 2026
|
Football
Share:
Jadan Baugh has rushed for 905 yards and leads the SEC in touchdowns (15) and points scored (90).
By:
Sullivan Bortner
GAINESVILLE, Fla. – Florida Gators running back
Jadan Baugh
has been tabbed as one of 10 semifinalists for the 2026 Doak Walker Award, as announced by the PwC SMU Athletic Forum on Tuesday.
Presented annually to the top running back in college football, the Doak Walker Award will be cut down to three finalists on Tuesday, Nov. 24. The winner will be unveiled live on ESPN during the College Football Awards show on Friday, Dec. 11. Baugh is one of three SEC running backs to be named a semifinalist with six different conferences represented.
As the SEC leader in rushing touchdowns (15, sixth in FBS), total touchdowns (15, eighth in FBS), points scored (90, 13th in FBS) and scoring average (9.0, 15th in FBS), Baugh is 95 yards away from becoming the first player in program history with back-to-back, 1,000-yard rushing seasons. Through 10 games, the Atlanta, Ga. native has rushed for 905 yards on 178 carries (5.1 YPC, 90.5 YPG) on top of 11 catches for 73 yards.
Boasting more rushing touchdowns through 10 games than two of the last three Heisman Trophy-winning running backs (Mark Ingram and Reggie Bush), Baugh also leads the SEC with a 90.8 HandsFumble grade (third in FBS) and 40.7% breakaway percentage (per Pro Football Focus). The junior bellcow grades second in the SEC with an 87.1 rushing grade (10th in FBS) and 84.6 offensive grade among running backs (14th in FBS).
Entering Week 11, Baugh's 15 rushing touchdowns rank second in team history behind Tim Tebow's 2007 school record (23). He has recorded 590-consecutive touches and 527-straight rushes without a fumble.
Looking at the national picture, Baugh is one of three qualified FBS running backs (Jahiem White, Nate Sheppard) and one of four FBS players (only in SEC) with at least 15 rushing touchdowns while averaging 5.0-plus yards per carry, and one of three FBS running backs (only in SEC) with 15 rushing touchdowns and 975 scrimmage yards (White, Sheppard).
In the Week 11 win at No. 11 Kentucky, Baugh became the 10th Gator all-time and first since La'Mical Perine (2016-19) to eclipse 3,000 career scrimmage yards (rushing and receiving) with a 12-yard rush in the second quarter.
Baugh and the Gators (6-4, 4-4 SEC) look to secure a second-straight victory this Saturday in The Swamp against Vanderbilt (4-6, 1-6 SEC). Kickoff is set for 12 p.m. with television coverage on SEC Network.
About the Doak Walker Award
Named after Doak Walker, a legendary figure in college football and the NFL, the Doak Walker Award presented by the PwC SMU Athletic Forum has honored the nation's top running back since 1990. Since 2022, the award has also recognized a national running back of the week. A select 20-person committee from the award's full voting body of media members and past winners chooses each weekly recipient.
The Doak Walker Award is a member of the National College Football Awards Association, which encompasses college football's most prestigious awards. The NCFAA's 25 awards have honored nearly 1,000 recipients since 1935.
