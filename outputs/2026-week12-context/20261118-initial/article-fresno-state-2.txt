Explosive return earns Luke Pac-12 Special Teams Player of the Week nod
11/16/2026 12:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Savannah Stoeckle / Associate Athletics Director for Strategic Communications
FRESNO, Calif. -
Fresno State senior
Rayshon Luke
has been named the Pac-12 Special Teams Player of the Week, announced by the conference on Monday.
Luke totaled a team-high 180 all-purpose yards in Fresno State's loss at Texas State last Saturday, highlighted by 135 kickoff return yards. He kickstarted the Bulldogs' scoring efforts with a 100-yard kickoff return for a touchdown on Fresno State's first possession of the game. It was the Bulldogs' first non-offensive touchdown of the season.
The senior returner had Fresno State's first kickoff return for a touchdown since 2023, and the 12th in program history. It was the longest kickoff return for a touchdown since 1934 when Walter Byrd had a 102-yard return prior to kickoffs being officially listed at a maximum of 100 yards.
Luke is the fourth different Pac-12 player to return a kickoff for a touchdown, and the third to do so from 100-yards out this season.
Monday's announcement marks Luke's second weekly honor from the conference, earning the offensive player of the week recognition on Sept. 28. He is the second Bulldog to be named special teams player of the week, joining
Manaki Watanabe
on Sept. 21.
Luke and the Bulldogs are back at home this week, hosting Colorado State on Saturday. Kickoff is set for 4:30 p.m. on The CW. Tickets are available
here
.
