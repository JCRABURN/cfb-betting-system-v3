Minicucci Sets Completion-Percentage Record, Defense Stands Tall in Football’s 24-16 Win Over FIU
11/14/2026 5:55:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Delaware Press Conference
Photo Gallery
Share:
NEWARK, Del. –
QB
Nick Minicucci
completed 27-of-30 passes (90-percent), setting the Delaware single-game completion percentage record with at least 25 pass attempts as the University of Delaware football team took down FIU, 24-16, on Saturday evening at Delaware Stadium. The Fightin' Blue Hens rise to 5-5, 3-3 CUSA, while the Panthers rise to 4-6, 2-4 CUSA.
HEN HIGHLIGHTS
Minicucci set Delaware single-game completion percentage record with at least 25 pass attempts, completing 27-of-30 passes (90-percent) for 230 passing yards and two passing touchdowns. He added 69 yards rushing and a score on the ground, including a career-long 62-yard run. His 62-yard scamper was the longest run by a Delaware QB since Andy Hall ran for an 85-yard TD on September 27, 2003 at Hofstra.
WR
Donovan Lewis
hauled in all eight of his targets for a career high in catches with 63 receiving yards.
LB
Gavin Moul
had 13 tackles, his 11th career game with double-digit tackles.
DT
Trace Scott III
blocked FIU's fourth-quarter game-tying PAT attempt along with five tackles to tie a career best.
LB
Jawarren Corbin
had a career-high 1.5 TFLs to go with his second-career interception and a career-best seven tackles.
S
Kevin Moore
tallied his second-career interception.
HOW IT HAPPENED
On FIU's first possession of the game, Corbin snagged an interception off a deflected pass. UD converted the turnover into points as Minicucci found TE
Elijah Sessoms
in the flat for an 11-yard touchdown to go up, 7-0. The Panthers evened the game at seven on the ensuing offensive drive.
The Blue & Gold defense came up big with two goalline stands late in the first half with the latter featuring a Moore interception in the endzone, leaving a 7-7 tie at the break.
K
Tucker Smack
opened the scoring in the second half, drilling a 22-yard field goal with 8:24 left in the third quarter to put the Hens ahead, 10-7.
FIU tied the game at 10 with a 30-yard field goal early in the fourth frame. Minicucci connected with RB
Viron Ellison Jr.
towards the sideline who scampered into the endzone for six. Smack tacked on the PAT to put Delaware up, 17-10, with 8:46 left in regulation. With 5:42 remaining in the fourth, the Panthers found paydirt to cut the UD lead to one. Scott III blocked the game-tying extra point attempt to keep the Hens in front, 17-16. Minicucci broke free for a 62-yard run down to the one-yard line to set up a short touchdown run by the QB. UD elected to kick the extra point, putting the Hens in front, 24-16, with 3:49 left on the clock. On fourth down and eight, the Delaware defense came up with a stop to give the offense the ball back near midfield with under two minutes to play.
GAME NOTES
Since
Ryan Carty
took the helm in 2022, UD is 23-0 when allowing less than 20 points, with 16 of those wins coming at home. As the defensive coordinator,
Manny Rojas
is 32-0 when his defense allows less than 20 points since 2020-21.
Delaware is 28-2 when winning the turnover margin under Carty.
The Blue & Gold now holds a 27-5 record at home under Carty, including winning 17 of the last 19 in The Tub.
In the Carty era, UD is 13-2 in the traditional blue on gold uniform.
OL
Patrick Methlie
made his first start in a Blue & Gold uniform.
Minicucci's 230 passing yards in the game moves him to 7,097 for his career, passing Super Bowl MVP Joe Flacco for second on the Delaware all-time career passing yards list and 1,117 behind Matt Nagy for most all-time.
Minicucci's two passing touchdowns in the game moves him to 56 for his career, matching Nolan Henderson for second on the Delaware all-time career passing touchdowns list. Matt Nagy holds the record with 58 touchdowns through the air.
UP NEXT
The Fightin' Blue Hens will head to New Mexico State on Saturday, November 21 at 3 p.m. The game will air on ESPN+.
