Beavers Hang Tight, Come Up Just Short At No. 17 Boise State
November 14, 2026
|
Football
Box Score
Oregon State Post-Game Notes
Photo Gallery
Share:
By:
Athletic Communications
BOISE, Idaho —
Maalik Murphy
threw for a career-high 396 yards and three touchdowns, and
Jesse Legree
caught 12 passes for 140 yards and a score, but Oregon State fell to No. 17 Boise State, 46-41, on Saturday at Albertsons Stadium.
Murphy completed 31 of 39 passes, topping the 371 yards he threw for against Fresno State on Sept. 6, 2025. His touchdown passes went to Legree (15 yards),
Cynai Thomas
(12) and
Eric Olsen
(20). He also threw an interception that Travis Anderson returned 58 yards for a touchdown late in the first quarter, and Boise State's two-point conversion made it 15-7.
Oregon State scored on its opening possession, a seven-play, 75-yard drive capped by Legree's touchdown. It was Legree's eighth 100-yard game of the season, tying Brandin Cooks (2013) for second in school history. He has 1,349 yards and 15 touchdown catches this season, fourth and second on the single-season lists.
Boise State led 32-20 at halftime and 39-27 early in the fourth quarter. Thomas' touchdown made it 39-34, and after Rasean Jones caught a 26-yard touchdown pass from Maddux Madsen, Olsen's 20-yard score with 4:25 left cut the deficit to 46-41. Boise State then used 11 plays to run out the clock.
Jake Reichle
ran for two touchdowns (1 and 5 yards) and
Cornell Hatcher Jr.
scored from 2 yards, his sixth straight game with a rushing touchdown. Oregon State finished with 506 yards, 396 through the air, and scored on all six of its red zone trips.
Aiden Sullivan
had 12 tackles, a sack, a forced fumble and a fumble recovery. His recovery at the Oregon State 24 early in the third quarter set up Reichle's 5-yard touchdown run, a 10-play, 76-yard drive.
Oregon State returns to Reser Stadium to host Utah State on Saturday, Nov. 21. Kickoff is set for 7:30 p.m. PT on CBS Sports Network.
