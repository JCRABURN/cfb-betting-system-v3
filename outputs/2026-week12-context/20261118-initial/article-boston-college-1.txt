Eagles Drop Non-Conference Finale at Notre Dame
November 14, 2026
|
Football
Box Score
Football: BC ND Game Book
Share:
Mekhi Dodd rushes for career high 45 yards
SOUTH BEND, Ind. —
The Boston College football team fell to No. 3 Notre Dame, 41-7, on Saturday afternoon. The Eagles dropped to 4-6 and 2-4 in the ACC, while the Fighting Irish improved to 9-1.
Mason McKenzie
finished 8-for-21 for 51 yards.
Mekhi Dodd
ran for a career-high 45 yards, and
Bo MacCormack III
led Boston College with three catches for 20 yards. MacCormack also added his sixth rushing touchdown of the season in the third quarter.
Anthony Palano
led the Eagles defense with 11 tackles.
Marcus Upton
and
Kemori Dixon
both had eight tackles. Dixon's eight tackles were a career high.
Ashton Cunningham
forced a fumble late in the second quarter, and
Nedrick Boldin Jr.
recovered it for his first career fumble recovery. Price recorded his fourth interception of the season early on in the third quarter. He also added his second career sack in the fourth quarter.
Notre Dame opened the scoring with a 26-yard touchdown pass with just over six minutes to go in the first quarter.
The Fighting Irish made it a two-possession game six minutes into the second quarter when they scored from 10 yards. Notre Dame scored their third touchdown of the afternoon with two minutes to go in the first half. The Fighting Irish defense got a touchdown just after the two-minute warning when they recovered a fumble in the end zone. Notre Dame finished the first half with a 40-yard field goal.
Price intercepted the Fighting Irish on the opening drive of the third quarter, setting up a three-yard rushing touchdown by MacCormack.
Notre Dame responded with a seven-play, 73-yard drive capped by a 13-yard touchdown pass. They added their second field goal with 12 minutes to go.
The Eagles return home next Saturday when they host the Syracuse Orange. Kickoff is set for 12:00 p.m. on ACC Network.
