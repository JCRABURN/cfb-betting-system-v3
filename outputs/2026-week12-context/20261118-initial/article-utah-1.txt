Utes in the Pros: Week 10
11/12/2026 10:52 AM
|
Football
Share:
SALT LAKE CITY
– With several teams on byes in Week 10, it'll be a quieter schedule for Utah Football alumni in the NFL—but 17 Pro Utes are still expected to suit up.
All across the league, 30 alums are on team rosters. Ten teams with a Utah Football connection on the roster are in either first or second place in their respective divisions. It includes a packed NFC North, where just half a game separates the division-leading Chicago Bears, Detroit Lions and Minnesota Vikings.

In addition to on the field, former Utah Utes are making their mark on NFL sidelines as well. Former quarterback Brian Johnson (2004-08) is now on the Los Angeles Rams' staff as a senior offensive assistant, while Utah signal-caller Mike McCoy (1992-94) has joined the Las Vegas Raiders as assistant head coach.
