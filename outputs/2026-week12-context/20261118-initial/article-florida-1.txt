Broadcast Information: Florida vs. Vanderbilt
Tuesday, November 17, 2026
|
Football
Share:
The matchup will be available on streaming platforms, with audio broadcast available in multiple formats.
GAINESVILLE, Fla. -- The Florida Gators football team welcomes Vanderbilt to the Swamp for its final SEC matchup of the season for Senior Day hosted by Regions Bank on Saturday, Nov. 21. Following the ceremonies, the Commodores and Gators will be set to kickoff at 12 p.m. ET, airing on SEC Network.
Single-game tickets are on sale for the 2026 Florida Football season under first-year head coach Jon Sumrall. Fans can purchase single-game tickets for the final home game of the year at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium against Vanderbilt on Nov. 21.
In addition to all the broadcast information, fans are encouraged to follow along during the game on social media.
