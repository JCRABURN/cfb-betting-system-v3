Postgame Notes: Texas Tech at Oklahoma State
November 14, 2026
|
Football
Share:
Texas Tech Football Postgame Notes
The Red Raiders defeated the Cowboys, 52-27, Saturday evening.
Date:
Nov. 14, 2026
Kickoff:
8:40 p.m. Central
Location:
Boone Pickens Stadium; Stillwater, Okla.
Weather:
41°, rain
Attendance:
52,168
Final Score:
No. 12 Texas Tech 52, No. 25 Oklahoma State 27
TEAM
Texas Tech improved to 9-1 for the second straight season. It is the ninth time in school history the Red Raiders have opened a season with a record of 9-1 or better and first time doing so in back-to-back seasons: 1932 (9-1), 1938 (10-0), 1941 (9-1), 1953 (9-1), 1973 (9-1), 1976 (9-1), 2008 (10-0), 2025 (9-1) and 2026 (9-1).
The Red Raiders' 6-1 start to Big 12 play marks the third time in program history they have reached that mark and the second time in as many seasons. The other occurrence came in 2008 when Texas Tech started conference play 6-1.
Saturday's win gave
Joey McGuire
six Big 12 victories for the third straight season. It is the fifth time in program history the Red Raiders have secured at least six Big 12 wins in a single season: 2005 (6-2), 2008 (7-1), 2024 (6-3), 2025 (6-1) and 2026 (6-1).
Saturday marked the Red Raiders' third straight win against Oklahoma State, their longest winning streak in the series since the 2000-02 seasons. The win evened the series at 25-25-3 through 53 meetings.
The win improved the Red Raiders to 3-0 this season against top-25 opponents. Texas Tech is currently one of seven FBS teams with three ranked wins this season, joining Texas (5), Georgia (4), Ole Miss (3), Oregon (3), Florida (3) and Utah (3).
Texas Tech jumped out to a 21-0 lead after the first quarter. It matched the team's season high for points in an opening quarter, also reached against Arizona State on Oct. 17. The 21-point deficit was the largest Oklahoma State has faced this season.
Texas Tech entered Saturday ranked second in the FBS in fourth-quarter point differential. The Red Raiders moved into first nationally after outscoring Oklahoma State, 17-7, in the final quarter to improve to plus-77 for the season. Texas Tech passed UCLA, which now stands at plus-74.
The win improved the Red Raiders to 15-3 under
Joey McGuire
in November and extended their winning streak in the month to eight games. That is the highest winning percentage in the Big 12 during that span.
Texas Tech entered Saturday as one of six FBS teams with at least 24 points in every game, one of three with at least 150 rushing yards in every game and the only team to accomplish both feats in every game this season. It is the longest streak to begin a season in the Big 12 since 2019 Oklahoma State (10 games). Texas Tech matched that mark against Oklahoma State by totaling 442 yards of offense, including 174 rushing yards.
THE OFFENSE
Thomas Castellanos
finished with 263 passing yards and four touchdown passes. Saturday marked the second time in his career he has recorded at least three touchdown passes in consecutive games. It was also the first time a Red Raider had thrown at least four touchdown passes in consecutive games since Nik Shimonek did so against Oklahoma and Kansas State in 2017.
J'Koby Williams
became the 10th Red Raider in program history to record a 1,000-yard rushing season, eclipsing the mark Saturday night with 124 rushing yards. He has 1,078 yards through 10 games.
Williams recorded his third straight 100-yard rushing game and his fifth of the 2026 season.
Williams took a pitch from
Thomas Castellanos
34 yards for a touchdown on the second play from scrimmage for the Texas Tech offense. It was Williams' team-leading 15th explosive play and his ninth play of at least 30 yards.
Terrance Carter Jr.
found the end zone for the third straight game, matching Castellanos' streak of three consecutive starts. Carter has scored four of his five touchdowns this season over the past three games.
Castellanos lost his first fumble of the season, which was recovered by the Cowboys in the red zone. Oklahoma State needed only one play to convert the turnover into a touchdown, as Drew Mestemaker scored on a 13-yard run to cut the Cowboys' deficit to 21-10 with 7:33 remaining in the first half.
Micah Hudson
caught an 80-yard touchdown pass in the second quarter. It was the longest reception by a Red Raider since Dalton Rigdon caught an 81-yard touchdown pass at West Virginia in 2019.
Malcolm Simmons
caught a 45-yard touchdown pass in the third quarter. It was his third touchdown reception of the season and his second score of the game, following his opening kickoff return for a touchdown.
Texas Tech finished with nine explosive plays, four of which resulted in touchdowns.
Texas Tech scored a touchdown on its opening drive for the third straight game, with
Thomas Castellanos
starting at quarterback for the third consecutive week.
THE DEFENSE
John Curry
intercepted his team-leading fourth pass of the season. It was the first turnover forced by the Texas Tech defense since recording two takeaways at Cincinnati on Oct. 24. Curry's interception led to seven points, as his 13-yard return set up the Red Raiders at the Oklahoma State 16-yard line for a five-play touchdown drive.
Ben Roberts
recorded his 15th career game with at least 10 tackles, logging 11 in the first half. He finished with a team-high 13 tackles. His 361 career tackles rank 10th in program history, and he needs two more to surpass Cody Davis (362).
Trey White
recorded at least 1.0 sack for the third straight game, finishing with one sack Saturday.
Saturday marked the fifth straight game in which the Red Raiders recorded at least 3.0 team sacks. Texas Tech has 32 sacks through 10 games this season, surpassing its total of 29 through the first 10 games of the 2025 season.
Ben Roberts
recorded his second interception of the season and the team's second takeaway in the fourth quarter.
Texas Tech continued its strong defense on fourth downs Saturday, allowing Oklahoma State to convert only one of four attempts. The Red Raiders entered the game ranked third in the FBS in fourth-down defense. Opponents have now converted just 7 of 26 attempts this season (26.9%).
SPECIAL TEAMS
Malcolm Simmons
returned the opening kickoff 97 yards for a touchdown. It marked the second straight season the Red Raiders returned the opening kickoff against Oklahoma State. It was also Texas Tech's first kickoff return for a touchdown since
J'Koby Williams
returned the opening kickoff 99 yards to start last season's contest in Lubbock. Before the past two seasons, the last time Texas Tech returned the game's opening kickoff for a touchdown was Nov. 11, 2017, when Keke Coutee returned a kick 92 yards for a touchdown against Baylor.
Will Karoll
had four punts inside the 20-yard line, including two inside the 10-yard line.
Stone Harrington
connected on a 54-yard field goal in the fourth quarter. It tied for the 10th-longest field goal made in program history. It was the fifth field goal of at least 50 yards in his career, the most by a Texas Tech kicker in the Big 12 era. He owns three of the 10 longest field goals in program history.
