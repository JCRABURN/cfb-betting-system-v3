Dalton Riggs Named Semifinalist for Mannelly Award
11/16/2026 7:54:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
Fifth year long snapper Dalton Riggs has had clean snaps all season and had a key fumble recovery in the victory over Northwestern.
By:
Andrew Kovar
LAKE BLUFF, Ill. –
Ohio State fifth year long snapper
Dalton Riggs
was named a 2026 Patrick Mannelly Award Semifinalist, as announced Monday night.
Riggs has had clean snaps in all 85 snaps he has totaled. Additionally, Riggs had a key fumble recovery in the victory over Northwestern. The recovery set up the Buckeyes final touchdown in the fourth quarter to seal the win over the Wildcats.
The Mannelly Award recognizes college football's best long snapper each year. This marks the eighth season the award has been handed out.
Prior to his time at Ohio State, Riggs has snapped in 24 games including in all 12 games for UCF a season ago. Riggs was also the starting long snapper at BYU in 2024 where he also saw action during the 2022 and 2023 seasons.
Mannelly Award Process
The Award, created in 2019, benefits Bernie's Book Bank, a non-profit organization located in Lake Bluff, Illinois.
The list of semifinalists will be narrowed to three finalists announced on Monday, November 30, 2026 and the winner selected at a live ceremony in downtown Chicago on Saturday, December 12, 2026.
