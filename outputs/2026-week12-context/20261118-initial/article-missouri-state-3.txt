First and Ten Notes
Missouri State takes to the road Saturday for a 1 p.m. kickoff at Jacksonville State. The Bears (3-3) have won 2 of their last 3 in Conference USA and are in a five-way tie for third place with two games to play. Mo State trails only Jax State (5-1) and first-place Liberty (6-0) in the league standings.
All-America candidate
Jared Lloyd
(LB, Daytona Beach, Fla.) remains No. 1 nationally in tackles per game (11.70) and total tackles (117). His season total is the most by a Bear since 2016 and third-most ever by a Bear since defensive stats were standardized (2004). Lloyd has recorded double-figure tackles in 9 of the last 13 games. He also leads all CUSA players in solo tackles per game (5.60) which is 5th in FBS.
Lloyd has logged 6 games this season with at least 13 total tackles, the most in the FBS. The last CUSA player with more in a single campaign was FAU's Azeez Al-Shaair (7) in 2017.
Mo State is tied for the national lead in defensive touchdowns (5), sharing the top spot with Alabama, Texas Tech and North Dakota State. After not posting any defensive scores in their first 16 FBS games, the Bears have logged 5 D-scores in their last 7 contests.
WR
Jmariyae Robinson
(5th yr., Cocoa, Fla.) now owns at least a share of all major MSU career receiving records with 182 receptions, 2,228 yards and 18 touchdowns in 47 games. He also has a pass reception in 44 consecutive games, which is also a club record.
The Bears are 2-2 in one-score games this season, including last week's 27-21 OT win over visiting New Mexico State for Missouri State's second "walk-off" victory of the year (Kennesaw State).
WR
Isaiah Myers
(5th yr., Cincinnati, Ohio) is just 80 receiving yards away from catching Ty Scott (2021) for the program single-season record (1,110). Myers is the second Bear to eclipse 1,000 receiving yards in a season and has already matched the team record for season TD receptions (8), shared with four others.
The Bears have had a second-half lead in each of their last 6 Conference USA games.
On a national scale,
Isaiah Myers
leads all FBS receivers in 30+ yard receptions (14), is 2nd in 20+ yard receptions (21), 6th in total receiving yards (1,030), 9th in yards per reception (20.20) and 16th in receiving touchdowns (8) -- all of which lead Conference USA.
Mo State has forced its foes to punt on their opening drive of the game in 5 of the last 6 contests.
CB
Joel Boamah
(Sr., Newark, N.J.) ranks 8th nationally in fumbles recovered (2), 13th in passes defended per game (1.10), 31st in interceptions (3) and 32nd in interceptions per game (0.30). He is this week's CUSA Defensive Player of the Week after forcing two turnovers vs. NM State, including a game-winning 88-yard interception return in overtime.
K
Yousef Obeid
(Sr., Dearborn, Mich.) has converted 43 straight extra points and 5 straight field goals.
The Bears defense has secured 13 takeaways this season, including a league-best 7 fumble recoveries. Likewise, Mo State has lost just 3 fumbles all season which is tops in CUSA and 16th nationally. The Bears have a +1.5 turnover margin in November.
Ramone Green Jr.
(Sr., Nixa, Mo.) has played in a team-high 35 consecutive games, dating to 8/31/24.
