Terps Earn 24-21 Last-Minute Win Over Wisconsin for Second Straight Victory
11/14/2026 12:00:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Buy Tickets To PSU Game
Photo Gallery
Share:
Quarterback Cardell Williams set a program record with 19 consecutive completions in his first start as a Terp.
COLLEGE PARK, MD —
Maryland and Wisconsin battled in a defensive duel on Saturday afternoon at SECU Stadium, where the Terps outlasted the Badgers to improve to 4-6 (2-5 Big Ten) on the season and 2-1 under interim head coach
Aazaar Abdul-Rahim
.
With
Malik Washington
sidelined with an injury, quarterback
Cardell Williams
made his first start as a Terp. He had an incredibly efficient day, throwing for 183 yards on 21-of-23 passing. He completed a program-record 19 consecutive passes and his 91.3 completion percentage also set a program record for a single game.
Trailing 21-14 in the final minutes,
Cardell Williams
connected with
Preston Howard
for a 39-yard touchdown pass to tie the game with his first touchdown pass as a Terp and his 19th consecutive completion.

Trey Reddick
then delivered a crushing strip-sack to give the Terps the ball back in Wisconsin territory.
Sean O'Haire
hit a 30-yard field goal with 29 seconds left, giving Maryland its first last-minute win since 2024.
TREY REDDICK BRINGS THE BOOM AND THE TERPS TAKE OVER 💥 📺: @FS1 https://t.co/xE2qIyjBKh

November 14, 2026
Maryland's defense was stout on Saturday, forcing
three turnovers and limiting the Badgers to 201 total yards, including just 77 through the air by Wisconsin (6-4, 4-3 Big Ten) quarterback Ryan Hopkins. The Terps are the only team in the Big Ten that has forced a turnover in every conference game this season.
The Maryland offense found its footing late in the first half. A nine-play, 75-yard touchdown drive featured three plays of 10+ yards and was capped by
Cardell Williams'
first rushing touchdown as a Terp.
Cardell Williams
was 14-16 (87.5%) for 106 yards in the first 30 minutes of play, including a streak of 12 consecutive completions to end the half. The Badgers led 10-7 at the break of the sixth all-time meeting between the two programs.
Cardell takes it in for 6️⃣ 📺: @FS1 https://t.co/qoAAPhEuK5
— Maryland Football (@TerpsFootball)
November 14, 2026
Chris Durr Jr.
hauled in eight receptions for 39 yards to move into fifth in Maryland single-season receptions (70) and 13th in Maryland single-season yards (804). His five 100-yard games in 2026 are tied for the most in school history.
The Terps improved to 2-1 since their bye week and take considerable momentum into next Saturday's road game against No. 18 USC.
How it Happened:
1st Quarter:
Wisconsin won the toss and deferred, sending Maryland on offense first.
Durr Jr. recorded his first two receptions of the day on the Terps' opening drive.
Wisconsin took an early lead on a five-play, 57-yard scoring drive.
Lavon Johnson
recovered his first career fumble on a Badgers' 4th-and-1 to give Maryland the ball in Wisconsin territory.
Bryce McFerson
pinned the Badgers at the 1-yard line with a perfect punt.
Maryland trailed 7-0 after 15 minutes of action.
2nd Quarter:
Johnson recorded his second career sack early in the frame.
Wisconsin added to its lead with a 37-yard field goal midway through the quarter.
Cardell Williams
found Durr Jr. for a 10-yard completion.
Cardell Williams
hit Howard for a 30-yard gain to move the ball into Wisconsin territory.
Na'eem Abdul-Rahim Gladding
put the Terps in the red zone with an 18-yard reception.
Cardell Williams
put Maryland on the board with a one-yard rushing touchdown with 3:16 left in the half.
Maryland fumbled in the red zone in the final minute, allowing Wisconsin to take its 10-7 lead into the break.
3rd Quarter:
Wingate's forced fumble — eventually recovered by
Ricardo Cooper Jr.
— set the Terps up in the red zone.
Cardell Williams
gave the Terps their first lead with his second rushing touchdown of the game with 8:24 to play in the quarter.
Wisconsin connected on a 42-yard field goal to cut Maryland's lead to 14-13 with 4:23 left in the frame.
Maryland took a one-point lead into the final 15 minutes.
4th Quarter:
Wisconsin took a 21-14 lead with a touchdown run and 2-point conversion in the opening minute.
DeJuan Williams
extended the Terps' drive with a 13-yard rush on 4th-and-1.
Maryland's defense forced a 3-and-out to get the Terps the ball back near midfield.
The Terps' defensive unit came up again to get Maryland the ball back in Wisconsin territory with 2:19 to play.
Cardell Williams'
19th consecutive completion hit Howard for a 39-yard touchdown to tie the game with 2:11 to play.
Reddick forced a fumble to give Maryland the ball in Wisconsin territory with less than two minutes left.
O'Haire's 30-yard field goal gave the Terps a 24-21 lead with 29 seconds left.
Maryland closed out its 24-21 win.
Williams' Wonderful Debut:
Cardell Williams
made his first start as a Terp on Saturday against the Badgers.
He threw for 183 yards on 21-23 passing to set a program record with a 91.3 completion percentage.
Cardell Williams
hit Howard for a 39-yard touchdown in the final minutes to tie the game with his first touchdown pass as a Terp.
He completed his final 19 passing attempts, setting a new program record for consecutive completions.
Cardell Williams
also ran for 17 yards and two scores on Saturday.

Beating the Badgers:
Saturday marked the sixth all-time meeting between Maryland and Wisconsin in a series that dates back to 2014.
Maryland earned its second-ever win over the Badgers and its first in College Park.
The Terps have now won each of the last two meetings between Maryland and the Badgers.
Maryland's biggest win in series history was a 27-10 victory in Madison, Wisconsin, last season.
Terps force another fumble 💪 📺: @FS1 https://t.co/JyVHwq6WDw
— Maryland Football (@TerpsFootball)
November 14, 2026
Dominant Durr Jr.
Durr Jr. hauled in eight receptions for 39 yards against the Badgers on Saturday.
His 70 receptions are the fifth-most in Maryland single-season history.
His 804 yards rank 13th in Maryland single-season history.
Durr Jr. has five games this season with 100+ receiving yards, which is tied for the most in a single season in Maryland history.
Celebrating Champions:
Maryland welcomed back the 2001 ACC Champion Maryland Terrapins back to SECU Stadium on Saturday.
The team was honored on-field during the first quarter alongside former head coach Ralph Friedgen.
Champions forever 🏆 Honoring our 2001 ACC champions back on their home field, 25 years after a season we'll never forget. https://t.co/RNRASnZ1VM

November 14, 2026
Coach's Notes:
"The biggest thing we talked about this week was going from family to brotherhood. When you talk about brotherhood, it's about someone bringing someone else up to their standards. What you saw on the field was big brothers taking care of little brothers."
"We expect to win every week. That's how we prepare."
"Malik got banged up last week, so we were really cautious with him this week. It's not a long-term injury. One thing I will never waver from is taking care of my kids."
"I'm happy for Cardell in that he got the opportunity to play in a Power 4 football game. It's also a testament to how we prepare as a staff and infuse confidence into the next man up. I'm really happy that he was able to excel."
On
Cardell Williams'
records: "It comes from protection and wide receivers making plays. He's very accurate."
"Sometimes the unknown is a secret weapon for you."
"They believe that as long as there are seconds on the clock, somebody can make a play. We're a good team. I keep saying it."
"Teachable moments are always better when you have a win behind them. We'll get (penalties) fixed."
"We'll put the best product on the field that we can. It's all about winning. We'll do whatever we need to do to win the football game."
Williams' Words:
"I didn't think about the completion rate or anything like that. I was just playing football."
"After the first half, everything just slowed down for me. I had a better feel of the game and everything was comfortable."
"We shot ourselves in the foot with penalties, but fought through it and came out with the win."
"Howard helped make sure I was good and comfortable as the game went on."
"Malik was pretty much a coach on the sideline today."
