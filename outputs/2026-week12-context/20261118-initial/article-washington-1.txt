Football At MSU Moved To 2:30 p.m. PT Kickoff On Saturday
November 13, 2026
|
Football
Gameday Central
Share:
Due to travel delays from the FAA ground stoppage, Washington football's game at Michigan State has been moved to Saturday, Nov. 14 at 2:30 p.m. PT on Big Ten Network.
For news, scores, highlights and more,
download the Go Huskies app
on your mobile device. Follow @UW_Football on Instagram, X, Threads, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube for the latest on the Dawgs.
