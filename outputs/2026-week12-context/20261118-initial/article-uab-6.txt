Jonah Delange Named Lou Groza Award Semifinalist
11/17/2026 11:00:00 AM
|
Football
2026 Groza Semifinalist Release
Share:
BIRMINGHAM, Ala. –
UAB senior kicker
Jonah Delange
has been named one of 20 semifinalists for the 2026 Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Award presented by the Orange Bowl, the Palm Beach County Sports Commission announced Tuesday.
Delange, who was also a semifinalist in 2024, is one of just five players on this year's list to have previously earned the distinction. He joins Vanderbilt's Brock Taylor, Texas Tech's Stone Harrington, Northwestern's Jackson Kleather and Texas' Gianni Spetic as returning semifinalists.
The Mobile, Alabama, native has put together a record-breaking season for the Blazers, connecting on 23-of-28 field-goal attempts (82.1 percent) through 10 games. His 23 made field goals rank second nationally and lead the American Conference, while his average of 2.30 field goals per game ranks second in the FBS.
Delange established a new UAB single-season record with his 23rd made field goal in the Blazers' 36-10 victory at Temple on Nov. 14, surpassing the previous mark of 22 set by Swayze Waters in 2007. He connected on field goals from 50 and 30 yards against the Owls, marking his third make from 50 yards or longer this season.
Earlier this season, Delange established a UAB record with a 57-yard field goal at Memphis, surpassing the previous school mark of 54 yards set by Ty Long in 2012. He has converted 13 field goals from 40 yards or longer this season, including three from 50-plus yards.
Delange has scored 90 points this season and remains perfect on extra-point attempts, converting all 21 PATs in 2026.
For his career, Delange ranks third in UAB history with 54 made field goals and is the program's all-time leader in field-goal percentage at 81.8 percent (54-of-66). He has converted all 81 of his career PAT attempts and ranks fifth in program history with 243 career kicking points.
Delange is one of just five kickers nationally with at least 20 made field goals this season and one of seven FBS kickers with at least three field goals from 50 yards or longer while converting better than 80 percent of their overall attempts.
The 20 semifinalists will be evaluated by a voting panel of more than 100 FBS head coaches, sports information directors, media members, former Groza Award finalists and current NFL kickers. The three finalists will be announced Tuesday, Nov. 24, and honored at the 35th annual Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Awards Banquet on Dec. 7 in Palm Beach County, Florida.
The winner will be announced live on ESPN during The Home Depot College Football Awards on Friday, Dec. 11.
Delange and the Blazers return to Protective Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 21, to host UTSA in UAB's regular-season home finale.
About the Lou Groza Award
The Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Award, presented by the Orange Bowl, is awarded annually to the nation's top collegiate placekicker. Established in 1992 and administered by the Palm Beach County Sports Commission, the award is named in honor of Pro Football Hall of Famer Lou "The Toe" Groza, who played 21 seasons with the Cleveland Browns and helped revolutionize the role of the placekicker in professional football. Since the award's inception, 34 finalists, including 18 winners, have gone on to play in the NFL. For more information, visit
LouGrozaAward.com
.
