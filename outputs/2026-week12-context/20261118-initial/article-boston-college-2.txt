Official game notes, depth chart, weekly news conference and more ahead of the Eagles' week-12 match-up against the Orange
By:
Athletic Communications
Match-Up:
Boston College (4-6, 2-4 ACC) vs. Syracuse (5-5, 3-5 ACC)
Date and Time:
Saturday, Nov. 21 • 12:00 p.m. ET
Location (Stadium):
Chestnut Hill, Mass. (Alumni Stadium)
Watch:
ACC Network
Radio:
WEEI 93.7 FM
GAME NOTES
FIRST-AND-10
» Boston College Football returns to Alumni Stadium for the Jay McGillis Memorial Senior Day game, taking on Syracuse on Saturday, November 21 at 12:00 p.m.
» The game will be televised on ACC Network.
Roxy Bernstein
(play-by-play) and
Dave Clawson
(analyst) are on the call with sideline reporter
Madison Hock
.
» Coverage on the Boston College Sports Network begins 30 minutes prior to kickoff on WEEI 93.7 FM.
Jon Meterparel
(play-by-play) is joined on the call by former BC linebacker
Pete Cronan
in the booth and former BC quarterback
Scott Mutryn
on the sideline.
EAGLES BY THE NUMBERS
» The Eagles defense has 17 takeaways (13 interceptions, four fumble recoveries), which is the most in the ACC.
» Boston College’s 13 interceptions is the most in the ACC and the seventh-most nationally.
»
Charlie Comella
and
KP Price
each have four interceptions to lead the Eagles in 2026, which is tied for eighth-most nationally.
»
Price
recorded his eighth career interception and his second career sack at Notre Dame (11/14).
» Through 10 games, Boston College has allowed 191 passing yards per game to opposing teams, which ranks third in the ACC.
»
Anthony Palano
ranks second in the ACC in total tackles (87).
»
Palano
totaled 11 tackles at Notre Dame (11/14), his third game this season with 10 or more tackles.
»
Marcus Upton
ranks second in the ACC in solo tackles (44).
»
Ashton Cunningham
forced a fumble on a Notre Dame (11/14) punt return, his first career forced fumble.
»
Mason McKenzie
ranks in the top-10 in two rushing categories in the ACC: Total rushing yards (686, 8th, best among ACC quarterbacks) and rush yards per game (68.6, 8th, best among ACC quarterbacks).
» At 235.3 yards per game,
McKenzie
ranks ninth in total offense in the ACC.
»
Bo MacCormack III
averages 4.9 yards per carry, which ranks eighth in the ACC.
»
MacCormack
scored on a three-yard rushing touchdown set up by a KP Price interception in the third quarter at Notre Dame (11/14), his sixth rushing touchdown this season.
» The touchdown moved
MacCormack
into the team lead for rushing touchdowns this season.
»
Mekhi Dodd
rushed for a career-high 45 yards on six carries at Notre Dame (11/14).
» Averaging 157.8 rushing yards per game, The Eagles own a top-10 rushing attack in the ACC (7th).
