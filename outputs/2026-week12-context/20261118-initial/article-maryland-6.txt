Terps in the NFL: Week 10
11/17/2026 12:10:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
That's a wrap on Week 10! Catch up with the Terps around the NFL.
Here's how these our former Terps performed on the field during this weekend:
stills calls game
Week 10 of the NFL season has arrived, and several former Terps delivered standout performances for their respective teams.
Tareeb Stills
recorded four tackles and forced a late fumble to help seal the Chargers' 23-20 win over Baltimore on Monday night.

Tai Felton
caught three passes for 33 yards in Minnesota's 30-10 win over Green Bay.
Deonte Banks
recorded three tackles, including one for loss, and a pass defended against the Commanders.
Chad Ryland
made both field-goal attempts, each from 49 yards, accounting for all of Arizona's points against the Rams.
Josh Woods
recorded two tackles against Kansas City.
DJ Moore
caught four passes for 90 yards, including a 45-yard touchdown, and added four rushing yards in Buffalo's 31-12 win over the Jets.
