Ducks Keep Climbing Up To No. 13 In CFP Rankings
11/17/26
|
Football
Share:
By:
Nate Krueger
EUGENE, Ore. -
The Oregon football team continued its rise in the College Football Playoff rankings on Tuesday evening, moving up three spots to No. 13.
Oregon has now been ranked in each of the last 34 CFP top 25 releases, coming in at No. 16 in last week's rankings after opening at No. 23 the week before.
The Ducks (7-3, 5-2 Big Ten) have played one of the nation's toughest schedules and boast three wins over teams currently in the top 18 of the CFP committee's rankings: No. 14 Ohio State, No. 17 Boise State and No. 18 USC. Oregon also defeated then-No. 20 Northwestern on Oct. 31 to begin its current three-game winning streak.
UO has also faced No. 4 UCLA - the only remaining Big Ten team - as well as Oklahoma State, which was No. 25 in the CFP rankings last week.
Oregon is one of only two teams in the nation, along with No. 3 Alabama, with multiple double-digit road wins over ranked opponents. The Ducks defeated USC, 41-27, in Los Angeles and scored 38 unanswered points en route to a 41-28 win over the Buckeyes in Ohio Stadium.
UO is also one of six teams nationally (Florida, Georgia, Ole Miss, Texas, Utah) with three or more wins over teams ranked at the time of the game.
The Ducks have been one of the nation's best teams during its current three-game winning streak, fueled by a resurgent offense. Oregon leads the Big Ten with 35.3 points per game over the last three weeks while averaging 434.3 total yards per game in that stretch. UO's defense limited Michigan to just seven points and 286 yards last week after a dominant performance at Ohio State the week before in which the Buckeyes managed zero points and just 39 total yards between the second and third quarters combined.
The Ducks will play their final road game of the regular season this Friday, Nov. 20, at Michigan State (5:00 p.m. PT, FOX).
