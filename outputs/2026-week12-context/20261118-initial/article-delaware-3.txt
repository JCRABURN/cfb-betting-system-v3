Football's Minicucci Earns CUSA Offensive Player of the Week for Third Time
11/16/2026 1:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
DALLAS –
University of Delaware football QB
Nick Minicucci
has been named Conference USA's Offensive Player of the Week for his performance against FIU, the league announced Monday. It is the third time this season that Minicucci has earned the league's weekly honor.
Minicucci completed 27-of-30 passes (90-percent), setting the Delaware single-game completion percentage record with at least 25 pass attempts in the 24-16 win over FIU. It's the best percentage for any UD quarterback who has thrown at least 15 passes in a game. He threw for 230 passing yards and two passing touchdowns, while adding 69 yards rushing and a score on the ground, including a career-long 62-yard run. His 62-yard scamper was the longest run by a Delaware QB since Andy Hall ran for an 85-yard TD on September 27, 2003 at Hofstra. He became only the fourth player in the nation this season to complete 90-percent of his passes with at least 30 attempts and first in CUSA since Caden Veltkamp of WKU at Middle Tennessee on September 14, 2024.
Minicucci's 230 passing yards in the game moves him to 7,097 for his career, passing Super Bowl MVP Joe Flacco for second on the Delaware all-time career passing yards list and 1,117 behind Matt Nagy for most all-time. Minicucci's two passing touchdowns in the game moves him to 56 for his career, matching Nolan Henderson for second on the Delaware all-time career passing touchdowns list. Matt Nagy holds the record with 58 touchdowns through the air.
Minicucci and the Hens return to action Saturday at New Mexico State for a 3 p.m. ET kickoff on ESPN+.
