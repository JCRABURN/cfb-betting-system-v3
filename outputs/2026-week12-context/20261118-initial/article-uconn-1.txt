Huskies Rout James Madison
11/14/2026 4:43:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Share:
EAST HARTFORD, CT
– UConn quarterback
Kalieb Osborne
threw for 274 yards and three touchdowns and had 88 yards rushing to lead the Huskies to a 46-22 win over James Madison in front of 25,530 at Pratt & Whitney Stadium at Rentschler Field Saturday afternoon.
Osborne completed 16-of-20 passes and threw touchdowns to
Shamar Porter
,
JD Willoughby
and
Cyncir Bowers
in the win as the Huskies even their record at 5-5 on the season. The UConn defense held JMU, now 8-3 on the year, to just 95 yards rushing and 331 yards of offense. Linebacker
Hayden Pegg
finished with 11 tackles and added the pick and a sack in the win.
Bowers had his third 100-yard effort of the season, running for 123 yards on 25 carries and two scores to go with his touchdown grab. Porter finished with four catches for a career-high 135 yards, marking the first 100-yard game by a UConn receiver this season.
The Huskies got the opening kick-off and put together a 12-play drive that stalled out on the JMU three-yard line. Facing a third and goal, Osborne was sacked and the Huskies settled for a 28-yard field goal from freshman
Jack O'Connor
for the early 3-0 lead. JMU got points on their opening drive as well, answering with a 14-play drive where Evans converted on a fourth-and-one to keep it alive, then finished it off with a one-yard plunge for six. The Dukes had a fake set up for the extra point try, but came up short putting them up 6-3 with 2:24 in the first.
UConn's Ky Wilson put the Huskies right back in front on the ensuing kick-off, taking it at the goal line and bringing all the way back for a 100-yard touchdown return and a 10-6 lead. It was the first UConn kick return touchdown since 2024 when
Mel Brown
brought one back at UMass and the first 100-yard return since the 2010 season when Nick Williams returned one 100-yards against Rutgers. It is the fifth 100-yard kick return touchdown in program history.
The Huskies missed out on a chance to increase their lead when a 40-yard field goal missed its mark in the second quarter. The Dukes took advantage and stuck it in the endzone to retake the lead. A roughing the passer penalty negated a UConn interception on the drive and Evans made it count, hitting wide out Braeden Wisloski for a 16-yard score to make it 13-10 with 2:02 left in the half.
UConn continued to make it a back-and-forth first half and answered the JMU scoring drive. On the third play of their drive with their two-minute offense, Osborne hit a streaking
Shamar Porter
up the left sideline for six, retaking the lead 17-13 with 1:30 left in the half. The Huskies halted a late second drive by JMU as linebacker
Hayden Pegg
came up with his second interception of the season. UConn quickly flipped the field thanks to a 36-yard scramble by Osborne and tacked on a 21-yard field goal from
Mike Baker
as time expired to make it 20-13 at the break. It was Baker's first field goal for the Huskies.
The Huskies pushed their lead to 26-13 with an 11-play drive that closed out the third quarter.  UConn was helped by an unsportsmanlike call on JMU and Osborne found
Cam Abshire
deep into Duke territory to set up a first and goal. Bowers capped the drive with a nine-yard TD, his ninth of the season, with 55 seconds left in the quarter.
JMU answer with a scoring drive but had to settle for a 36-yard field goal from Morgan Suarez to cut into the lead, 26-16, at 13:06 of the fourth. Osborne answered with his second touchdown toss of the day, hitting
JD Willoughby
on a 35-yard scoring strike with 10:42 left to push the lead to 33-16.
The Huskies scored twice more in the fourth quarter to put the game out of reach. Osborne threw his third TD of the game, hitting Bowers from two yards out at 5:44 to lead 39-16. Bowers finished out the scoring with a 17-yard score with 2:39 left, his team leading 11
th
touchdown this season.
Up Next: The Huskies close out the home portion of their schedule next Saturday, November 21 when they host Old Dominion at Pratt & Whitney Stadium at Rentschler Field. Kick-off is at 3:30 pm
