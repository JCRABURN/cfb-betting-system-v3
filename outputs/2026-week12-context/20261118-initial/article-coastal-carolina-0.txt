Know Before You Go: Coastal Carolina vs. Arkansas State
11/11/2026 1:35:00 PM
|
Football
Arkansas State Tickets
Arkansas State Parking
Share:
CONWAY, S.C. –
Coastal Carolina football closes its home schedule on Saturday, Nov. 14, when the Chanticleers host Arkansas State at Brooks Stadium for Senior Day and Salute to Service. Kickoff is set for 1 p.m. ET on ESPN+.
Fans are encouraged to arrive early, enjoy the pregame activities and be in their seats to help celebrate Coastal's seniors before kickoff.
Here is everything fans need to "Know Before You Go."
GAME-DAY TIMELINE
8 a.m. – Parking lots open
10 a.m. – Teal Town opens
10 a.m. – CCU Alumni TEALgate opens
10:45 a.m. – Chant Walk
11:30 a.m. – Gates open
12 p.m. – CCU Alumni TEALgate closes
12:30 p.m. – Teal Town closes
12:30 p.m. – Senior Day recognition
1 p.m. – Kickoff
THANK YOU, SENIORS
Ahead of Saturday's game, 23 Chanticleers are expected to be honored during a pregame Senior Day ceremony at Brooks Stadium. Fans are encouraged to be in their seats for the recognition.

TICKETS AVAILABLE
Fans can purchase
single-game tickets for Saturday's home finale
or call the Chanticleer Athletics Ticket Office at 843-347-TIXX (8499).
CHANT WALK
Fans can welcome the Chanticleers to Brooks Stadium during the Chant Walk at 10:45 a.m. The team will begin near Teal Town, continue through the premium tailgate area and enter the stadium through Gate 1.
Fans are encouraged to line the route and be loud as the Chanticleers make their way to the stadium.
TEAL TOWN
Teal Town, located on the southeast side of Brooks Stadium, will be open from 10 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. and serve as the central pregame gathering spot for students, alumni and fans.
Teal Town will feature free inflatables for children, Coastal Carolina merchandise from Teal Tide Trading Company and Gemini Boutique, the Coastal Pre-Game Radio Show with Joe Catenacci and beverages available for purchase.
Food will be available for purchase from Bojangles, Philly Pretzel and Stella's Ice Cream.
View the game-day map
CCU ALUMNI TEALGATE
The Alumni TEALgate will take place from 10 a.m. to noon in Blanton Park before the Chanticleers take on Arkansas State at Brooks Stadium.
Alumni and their guests can enjoy light snacks, refreshments and a complimentary CCU-branded air freshener while supplies last. Sawgrass will provide live entertainment throughout the event, and the Chanticleer Regiment will perform at 11:40 a.m.
Stella's Homemade Ice Cream, an alumni-owned business, and Knowledge Perk Coffee will have trucks on-site. The Alumni TEALgate will feature lawn games, inflatables, face painting and other family-friendly activities. Alumni and their guests are welcome to bring their own drinks, snacks, coolers and lawn chairs.
PREPAY FOR PARKING AND SAVE
Fans are encouraged to
purchase parking in advance
to save time and money on game day.
FREE CONCESSIONS
The CCU Kickoff Meal Deal returns, offering free hot dogs, nachos, popcorn and fountain drinks to every ticketed fan at designated concession stands.
Fans must present their game ticket through the Coastal Carolina Athletics App at a designated free concession location. Free concessions and alcohol sales inside Brooks Stadium will end at the conclusion of the third quarter.
Specialty items and food from outside vendors will remain available for purchase.
IN-GAME PROMOTIONS
The City of Conway is Saturday's presenting game sponsor. In-game promotions include the following.
Sparks Toyota RAV4th Quarter
Teal Tide Trading Co. T-Shirt Cannon
Bojangles Big Glove Boxing
Celsius Human Hamster Ball Bowling
Bubba's Fish Shack Gone Fishing
Royal Farms Cluckin' Bucket Chicken Chuck
Team Dodge Team Dodge Ball
Muscle Milk Flex Cam
DIGITAL TICKETING
All tickets for Coastal Carolina Athletics events are digital and accessible through the Coastal Carolina Athletics App. Fans should download their tickets and add them to their mobile wallets before arriving at Brooks Stadium.
Fans should fully charge their phones and have their tickets ready before approaching the gates.
RIDE-SHARE DROP-OFF
Fans using a ride-share service are encouraged to use the Wall Building as their drop-off location. The Wall Building is located at 119 Chanticleer Drive, Conway, SC 29526, near Brittain Hall and the HTC Pond.
ARRIVE EARLY
Parking lots open at 8 a.m., followed by Teal Town at 10 a.m. and the Chant Walk at 10:45 a.m. Gates open at 11:30 a.m. Fans should allow extra time for parking and stadium entry so they can be in their seats for the pregame Senior Day ceremony.
Coastal Carolina and Arkansas State kick off at 1 p.m. ET on ESPN+.
