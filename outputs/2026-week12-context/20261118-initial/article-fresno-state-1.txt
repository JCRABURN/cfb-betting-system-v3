QUICK LOOK
Fresno State is in the hunt for its sixth win of the season on Saturday. With a win on Saturday, Fresno State would become bowl eligible for the sixth-straight season.
The Bulldogs return home, and to California, for the first time in two weeks. Fresno State will play its last two regular season games at home. The Bulldogs had nine of their 12 regular season games scheduled in their home state of California.
Fresno State's 4:30 p.m. PT kickoff is its earliest home kickoff of the season.
Fresno State has scored at least 23 points in eight of its 10 games played this season.
Fresno State has scored at least 31 points in each of its four home games this season.
Fresno State looks for its fourth home win of the season this Saturday, tying the number of wins at home in 2024 and 2025.
The Bulldogs' 35 points on the road at Texas State last weekend were the most in a road game this season.
35 points scored in a loss were the most for Fresno State since a Dec. 12, 2020 neutral site loss to New Mexico. The Bulldogs fell 49-39 to the Lobos in the season finale.
Each of Fresno State's five victories has come by at least 14 points, with an average winning margin of 22.4 points.
On the opposite end of that, all four of the Bulldogs' losses in conference play have been by one possession.
Rayshon "Speedy" Luke returned a kickoff 100 yards for a touchdown against Texas State. It was Fresno State's first kickoff return for a touchdown since 2023, and just the second one of 100 or more yards in program history.
Luke notched the program's 12th kickoff return for a touchdown all-time. It was the longest kickoff return for a touchdown by a Bulldog since a 102-yard return by Walt Byrd against Northern Arizona on October 13, 1934.
Luke was named the Pac-12 Special Teams Player of the Week for his explosive return last weekend.
The Bulldogs have had seven different starting offensive line combinations through 10 games this season.
DB
Gerayas Grimes
forced and recovered a fumble on the same play against Texas State, being solely responsible for the Bulldogs only turnover gained in the game. He became the first Bulldog to force and recover a fumble on the same play since Elijah Gates at UConn in 2022.
ABOUT THE MATCHUP
Fresno State and Colorado State will meet for the 19th time. The Rams lead the series 11-7.
The series began on Sept. 19, 1992, when Fresno State earned a 52-21 victory in Fresno.
Fresno State is 5-5 against Colorado State at home, 2-5 in Fort Collins and 0-1 at a neutral site.
The teams have met each year since 2024
Colorado State won the most recent meeting, defeating Fresno State 49-21 in Fort Collins on Oct. 10, 2025.
The Bulldogs have won the last two meetings played in Fresno, defeating the Rams 38-17 in 2020 and 28-22 in 2024.
With a win, Fresno State would defeat Colorado State in three consecutive home meetings for the first time in series history.
Fresno State's 52-21 victory in 1992 represents its largest win and highest-scoring performance in the series. The 52 points scored by the Bulldgs in that game were the most for any team in a single game in series history.
The Bulldogs' narrowest win over the Rams was a 32-30 victory in Fresno on Oct. 4, 2002.
The programs' only postseason meeting came in the 2008 New Mexico Bowl, with Colorado State winning 40-35 in Albuquerque.
Five meetings have been decided by three points or fewer. Colorado State won 34-32 in 1993, 44-42 in 1994 and 34-31 in 2015, while Fresno State won 25-22 in 2001 and 32-30 in 2002.
The 1994 matchup remains the highest-scoring game in the series. The teams combined for 86 points in Colorado State's 44-42 victory.
Saturday's matchup will be the first between Fresno State and Colorado State as members of the Pac-12. The teams enter their third conference together, being previous conference opponents in the WAC and Mountain West.
Colorado State has the edge in both prior conference over Fresno State, owning a 4-1 record over the Bulldogs in the WAC and a 4-3 record as Mountain West foes.
