Pat Fitzgerald Weekly Press Conference Coverage
11/16/2026 2:45:00 PM
|
Football
MSU Game Notes
Share:
EAST LANSING, Mich. –
Michigan State head football coach
Pat Fitzgerald
held his weekly press conference Monday inside the Spartan Stadium North End Zone Media Center to preview this Friday's game against Oregon.
The Spartans (3-7, 1-6 Big Ten) will host the No. 14/14 Ducks (7-3, 5-2 Big Ten) on Friday, Nov. 20 at 8 p.m. on Senior Night in Spartan Stadium. The game will be televised on FOX.
SCHEDULE UPDATE:
The Big Ten Conference announced on Monday that Michigan State's game at Rutgers on Saturday, Nov. 28 will kick off at 4:15 p.m. The game will be televised on the Big Ten Network.
