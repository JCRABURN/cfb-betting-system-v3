Davis Picks Up Second Earl Campbell Tyler Rose Award Honorable Mention
Nov 17, 2026
|
Football
Share:
TYLER, Texas –
University of Memphis wide receiver
Jemyri Davis
was named to the honorable mentions list for the Earl Campbell Tyler Rose Award Player of the Week for a second time this season, the organization announced Tuesday.
Davis, a graduate of Everman High School in Fort Worth, Texas, had a career night in Memphis' 52-41 win over South Florida last Thursday, catching five passes for 161 yards and a touchdown, with the yardage total setting a new career high.
On the season, the redshirt junior leads Memphis in receiving yards and touchdowns with 561 and seven, respectively. His seven receiving touchdowns are good for 28
th
in the country, while he also ranks 11
th
in the FBS with an average of 20.04 yards per reception.
Davis' recognition marks the fourth time this season that a Tiger has been recognized for weekly honors for the Earl Campbell Tyler Rose Award, with Davis being an honorable mention twice and
Hunter Tipton
being selected as the Player of the Week once, along with also being an honorable mention.
The Earl Campbell Tyler Rose Award recognizes the top offensive player in Division I football who also exhibits the enduring characteristics that define Earl Campbell: integrity, performance, teamwork, sportsmanship, drive, community and tenacity: specifically, tenacity to persist and determination to overcome adversity and injury in pursuit of reaching goals.
In addition, the nominee must meet one of more of the following criteria: played football at and graduated from a Texas High School and/or currently playing at a Texas Division I four-year institution.
