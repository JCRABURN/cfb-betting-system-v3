Kansas at Oklahoma State Set for 5:15 p.m. Kickoff in Stillwater
11/16/2026 12:30:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
LAWRENCE, Kan. –
Kansas football will close out its regular season schedule against the Oklahoma State Cowboys on Saturday, Nov. 28 with kickoff slated for 5:15 p.m. CT on ESPN2, the Big 12 Conference announced on Monday.
The game will serve as Kansas' regular season finale and the 76
th
meeting all-time between Kansas and Oklahoma State. Oklahoma State owns the series lead at 41-31-3, including a 19-14-2 mark in games played in Stillwater. Kansas has won two of the last three meetings, including a 38-21 victory in 2025, while the Jayhawks search for their first win in Stillwater since 2007.
Prior to the matchup between Kansas and Oklahoma State, the Jayhawks will celebrate Senior Day on Saturday, Nov. 21 at 11 a.m. against No. 12 BYU at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium.
