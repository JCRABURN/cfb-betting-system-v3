Quoted: Lee Beebe Jr., Khobie Martin & Jamari Sharpe

BLOOMINGTON, Ind. ––––
As Indiana Football's week 12 matchup at Washington on Saturday (Nov. 21) approaches, running backs
Lee Beebe Jr.
and
Khobie Martin
and defensive back
Jamari Sharpe
addressed the media inside the Don Croftcheck Football Team Room on Tuesday (November 17).
Below is a partial transcript of the press conference, while video of the media sessions can be found on the right sidebar at IUHoosiers.com.
Lee Beebe Jr.
| RB | 5
th
Year
On when everyone in the running back room is healthy…
LB: If we can do anything, we can do the impossible. Just with the three of us — I feel like we can just be that running back group.
On if the team is better than the outside perception…
LB: I definitely feel like it's way, way better than what the media makes it out to be. And I feel like there's a lot behind closed doors that a lot of people don't see.
On how beneficial the bye week was…
LB: Super beneficial. I love the bye week. I had time to get myself back right and I felt great. Felt like it was back week two — that's how my body felt.
Khobie Martin
| RB | 3
rd
Year
On what it was like being back out there…
KM: It was real good to be back on the field. Being out, sitting on the sideline that long, watching my teammates put in that hard work, was a hard feeling. I felt like a little bit of a couch potato — but I wasn't, I was still rehabbing, in the weight room doing what I needed to do to get back on the field.
On what flipped in the second half…
KM: I don't really know about the schematic difference. I just know we went out there with a little more of a chip on our shoulder and started playing harder. Not saying we weren't playing hard before, but it was like, okay, let's wrap this up.
On the weekly CFP rankings and upcoming opponents…
KM: Honestly, no. I don't try to pay attention to that. And when it does come, that's when I'll pay attention to it. But as of right now, I'm just more focused on the next opponent, which is Washington. And then next week I'll be focused on Purdue.
Jamari Sharpe
| DB | 5
th
Year
On his interception to close the game…
JS: I knew it was their last play, so I knew they had to go take a shot. So as I was in my stance, I had dropped back and saw them throw the ball, and I made a good play on the ball.
On
Jaylen Bell
…
JS:
Jaylen Bell
, he's a great player. You know, he's smart. He's a very smart corner. Even though he's young, he's been in the system for a minute. You know, he's been working. He's a smart football player.
On the increase in the number of forced turnovers in recent games…
JS: We're constantly putting ourselves in positions for those. You know, just trusting everybody to do their job, trusting the D-line to get home. Like I said, the ball has to come out fast half of the time because you know we have a like a nice D-line. So you know, as the ball, as the quarterback drops back, we just play off the quarterback's vision.
