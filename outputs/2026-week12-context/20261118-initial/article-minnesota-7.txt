FIVE THINGS TO KNOW
1.
Minnesota (5-5, 3-4 B1G) plays its final home game of the 2026 season Saturday when it hosts NR/RV Northwestern (6-4, 3-4). Kickoff is set for 11 a.m. on FS1 and the Gopher Radio Network.
2.
The Golden Gophers enter the contest with a 5-5 record overall. Minnesota has had that record or better at the 10-game mark in every season under
P.J. Fleck
(excluding the 2020 COVID season where only seven games were played). Minnesota was also 5-5 in 2017-18 and 2023, 6-4 in 2021 and 2024-25, 7-3 in 2022 and 9-1 in 2019. The team is looking to tally its eighth six-win season under
P.J. Fleck
, which would rank third in program history. Only Henry L. Williams (12) and Bernie Bierman (10) have had more six-win seasons in Gophers annals.
3.
The Gophers have enjoyed remarkable success at home since the start of the 2025 season, going 11-2 overall at Huntington Bank Stadium. Within the Big Ten, that's tied for the fourth best win percentage over that span along with Oregon. Only Indiana (13-1), Ohio State (12-1) and USC (12-1) have better overall home records within the conference. The Gophers are 7-1 at home against Big Ten teams since the start of 2025. That ties Minnesota with those aforementioned schools for the best home conference record since the start of last season. Nationally, the only schools in the country with more home wins against their league opponents in that span are BYU, San Diego State, SMU and Texas Tech, who all have eight such wins. More on Minnesota's home success on Page 5 of the Notes.
4.
Minnesota has gone up against a defensive gauntlet since the beginning of October, including facing Michigan, Iowa, Indiana and UCLA, though the Gophers did go 2-2 against those teams. All four of those defenses entered their respective contests ranked 22nd or better in total defense in the FBS, including three ranked eighth or better. All four ranked 23rd or better in scoring defense, including three in the top-8 with Indiana at No. 2 and UCLA at No. 5 at the time of the game. All four were top-15 in yards per play allowed, including Indiana at No. 2 and UCLA at No. 3. All four were top-18 in rush defense and top-14 in rush yards allowed per attempt, including Indiana at No. 1. Last week, Penn State entered ranked 19th nationally in total defense, third in passing yards allowed per game and No. 1 in pass yards allowed per attempt.
5.
The 2026 campaign is the 10th for
P.J. Fleck
at Minnesota. He is tied for the fourth-longest tenured coach in program history, the second-longest active tenured coach in the Big Ten and the 10th-longest tenured head coach overall in the FBS. Fleck's Minnesota record stands at 71-49, putting him fourth in program history in overall wins. He's also fourth in Big Ten wins (42) and fifth in games coached (120). Fleck's overall win percentage of .592 is third best among Minnesota coaches with more than 40 games under their helm behind only Henry L. Williams (.786, 1900-21) and Bernie Bierman (.716, 1932-41, '45-50). In his 14th season overall as a college head coach, Fleck is 101-71 (.587), making him one of 21 active full-time head coaches to reach 100 wins at the FBS level.
