Maryland-Penn State to Kick Off at 3:30 PM on Nov. 28
11/16/2026 1:50:00 PM
|
Football
Buy Tickets to PSU Game
Share:
COLLEGE PARK, MD --
Maryland football's 2026 home finale against Penn State on Saturday, Nov. 28 will kick off at 3:30 p.m. and air on FS1, it was announced Monday.
The game will mark the 49th all-time meeting between the programs, which met annually for 11 consecutive seasons before last year. Maryland will also celebrate Senior Day, honoring several Terps in a pregame ceremony for their contributions to the program.
Maryland will look to earn its third consecutive victory when it travels to No. 15 USC this week, while Penn State will put its three-game winning streak on the line when it hosts Rutgers on Saturday.
