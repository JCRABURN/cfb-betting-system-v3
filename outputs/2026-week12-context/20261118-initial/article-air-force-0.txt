THE BREAKDOWN
• Air Force and UTEP will be playing for the first time since 1995 when both schools were members of the Western Athletic Conference.
• Air Force leads the overall series, 14-1, including a 7-0 mark at home.
• Air Force is bowl eligible for the first time since 2023. The Falcons have been bowl eligible 14 times in
Troy Calhoun
's 20 seasons as head coach. He has led the Falcons to 13 bowl games overall and has an 8-5 record, including a current five-game winning streak.
• Air Force's eight wins are the most since 2023 when the Falcons finished 9-4. The eight regular season wins are the most since 2022 when the team had nine and finished 10-3. Air Force's four MW wins are the most since 2023 when the team finished 5-3.
THE SERIES
• The teams are playing for the first time since 1995.
• Air Force and UTEP are playing for the 16th time overall and 15th time as conference foes.
• The schools played 14 times as members of the WAC from 1982-95.
• The first meeting came on Sept. 9, 1978, in El Paso which resulting in a 34-25 Air Force win when the Falcons were an independent.
• Air Force leads the overall series, 14-1, including a 13-1 mark as conference foes.
• The Falcons are 7-0 at home, 6-1 at UTEP and 1-0 in neutral site games.
• Air Force has won seven straight dating back to 1988.
• Air Force defeated UTEP, 56-43, in 1995 at the Academy in the last meeting.
• The teams played at the Alamo Dome in San Antonio, TX, in 1994, resulting in a 47-7 AF win.
LAST MEETING VS. UTEP
(OCT. 7, 1995)
Air Force won a shootout, 56-46, in Falcon Stadium. The teams matched touchdowns in the first quarter, the last coming on a touchdown by Beau Morgan to take it 14-14. UTEP took over the second quarter and closed the half with a 31-21 lead. The Falcons took control in the third quarter with 21 points, keyed by a Morgan touchdown run and pass and took a 42-28 lead into the fourth quarter. After exchanging touchdowns, the Falcons put the game away on another Morgan touchdown run to make up the final margin. Air Force finished with 550 total yards, including 448 rushing. Morgan rushed 15 times for 148 yards and four touchdowns. He hit 7-13 passes for 130 yards and a score. Current Air Force slotbacks coach
Jake Campbell
added 76 yards on eight carries. UTEP had 595 total yards, including 327 rushing and 288 passing.
Post-Game Notes
• Air Force overcame a 17-point deficit to win the game.
• The 52 combined points in the first half were the most in a game since 1989.
• The 102 combined points were the second most in program history.
• The teams combined for 1,145 yards, which ranked as the fifth-most at the time.
• The 727 combined rushing yards were the sixth most and most since 1989.

LOOKING AT UTEP
• UTEP is in its first year in the Mountain West. The Miners were members of Conference USA from 2005-25 prior to this season. UTEP was a member of the WAC from 1968-2004 where they played Air Force 14 times.
• UTEP plays its last two games on the road, starting with Air Force this week. They close the season at NIU. UTEP celebrated Senior Day during last week's game vs. Wyoming.
• The "Orange Swarm" defense allowed a season-low 17 points to an FBS school in a 17-14 loss vs. Hawaii. It ties the lowest allowed versus an FBS program in the Scotty Walden era – 15 allowed vs. at Sam Houston (2025) & at LA Tech (2024). It's the least allowed in the Sun Bowl since yielding six against FIU on Nov. 19, 2022.
• The UTEP football team continues to pay tribute to the late Luke Laufenberg throughout the 2025 season. Laufenberg passed away at the age of 21 on Thursday, Aug. 22, 2019 after battling cancer for two years.
• The "Trench Warrior" is a single digit that is assigned to the most deserving D-Lineman. They are a leader, and they embody UTEP's culture on and off the field. It is something head coach Scotty Walden created to signify that wearing a single digit on the DL means you're a "bad dude." On Aug. 25, Walden announced that defensive linemen #1 Elijah Fields and #9 Oryend Fisher will don single digits in 2026.
• Quarterback Raymond Moore III leads the Miners in rushing and passing. He has rushed for 686 yards and four touchdowns. Moore III has hit 68-115 passes for 633 yards and three touchdowns.

NATIONAL RANKINGS
Air Force is receiving votes in the AFCA Coaches poll for the second straight week and third time this season. The Falcons had 14 points to rank 34th this week. The team received one point last week. Air Force had three points in the week eight poll earlier this season.
AN AIR FORCE WIN WOULD...
• Air Force would improve to 9-2 overall and 5-2 in Mountain West play. The nine wins would be the most in the regular season since 2022 when the team was 9-3 before finishing 10-3. The five MW wins would be the most since finishing 5-3 in 2022.
• Air Force would finish the season 6-1 at home, matching the program record for wins in one season. The Falcons would record six homes wins for the sixth time under head coach
Troy Calhoun
.
• Air Force would improve to 15-1 all-time vs. UTEP, including a 14-1 mark as conference foes. The Falcons would improve to 8-0 at home vs. UTEP and win for the eighth straight time in the series.
LAST WEEK
Air Force defeated San Jose State, 24-3, to win its third straight game. The Falcons rushed for a season-high 452 yards and won for the third time this season without completing a pass.
Carter Cremascoli
opened the scoring with a 38-yard field goal.
Kemper Hodges
scored the first touchdown on a four-yard run to give the Falcons a 10-0 lead.
Evan Medders
scored on a career-long 81-yard touchdown to give the Falcons a 17-0 halftime lead. Following a field goal from San Jose State in the third quarter, Hodges added his second touchdown run to make up the final margin. Medders led three Falcons with 100-plus yards with 146 yards and a touchdown on 13 carries. Hodges added 117 yards and two scores and
Owen Allen
103 yards on 12 carries. The defense held San Jose State to 10 first downs, 2-12 on third-down conversions and just 224 total yards.
Blake Fletcher
led the defense with eight tackles, while
Roger Jones, Jr.
added six.
LAST WEEK'S POST-GAME NOTES
Team
• Air Force won its third straight game, which is the second-longest streak of the season as the team won four straight earlier this season.
• Air Force improved to 7-3 overall vs. San Jose State, including a 4-1 mark at home. The Falcons have won three of the last four in the series.
• Air Force forced a punt on San Jose State's opening drive of the game. The Falcons have forced a punt on the opening drive in three straight games and six times overall this season.
• Air Force held San Jose State scoreless in the first quarter. The Falcons have held three straight opponents without points in the first quarter. Air Force has done it seven times in 10 games this season.
• Air Force scored 17 points in the second quarter for the second straight game (Army). The 17 points in one quarter is the second most this season, trailing only the 21 points in the fourth quarter vs. UNLV.
• Air Force held San Jose State to just 62 yards on 18 plays and 8:18 time of possession in the first half.
• Air Force shutout San Jose State in the first half, marking the second time the Falcons have shutout an opponent in the first half and first time since holding Duquesne scoreless in a 34-0 win in the second opener.
• Air Force rushed for a season-high 452 yards, the fourth 400-plus yard game of the year.
• Air Force had multiple 100-yard rushers with
Evan Medders
gaining 146,
Kemper Hodges
117 and
Owen Allen
103. The Falcons have had multiple 100-yard rushers three times this season (NIU, UNLV).
• Air Force had three players with more than 100 yards in the same game (Medders 146, Hodges 117 and Allen 103). It is the first time the Falcons have had three players with 100 or more rushing yards in the same game since Utah State in 2021 when Haaziq Daniels had 102, Brad Roberts 113 and Emmanual Michel 133.
• The three points allowed to San Jose State are the fewest allowed vs. conference opponent since holding SDSU to 3 in a 13-3 win in San Diego, Nov. 26, 2022.
• Air Force did not have any passing yards for the third time this season (NIU, UConn).
• Air Force captains for the game were LB
Blake Fletcher
, OLB
Isaac Hubert
, QB
Liam Szarka
and OL
Luke Vonderhaar
. Fletcher has been a captain for every game.
Individual Notes
• Senior QB
Kemper Hodges
started at quarterback for the second straight game. He rushed for 117 yards on 26 carries, his third straight game with 100 or more rushing yards.
• Sophomore QB
Evan Medders
recorded a career-long 81-yard touchdown run in the second quarter. The play is the longest by a Falcon this season. The 81-yard run ties as the fourth-longest in program history and is the longest since Haaziq Daniels set the school record with a 94-yard run vs. Florida Atlantic in 2021. Medders' previous long run was 41 yards earlier this season vs. NIU. Medders finished 146 yards rushing and a touchdown on 13 carries. The 100-yard game is his fourth this season (NIU, UNLV, Wyoming, SJSU), the most by any Falcon.
• Junior P

• Senior FB
