Bulldogs’ comeback slips away in 37-35 loss at Texas State
11/14/2026 5:56:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Postgame Press Conference
Postgame notes
Share:
By:
Fresno State Athletics Strategic Communications
SAN MARCOS, Texas -
Fresno State erased a 14-point deficit and took its first lead in the fourth quarter, but Texas State scored with one minute remaining to edge the Bulldogs 37-35 on Saturday at UFCU Stadium.
Fresno State trailed 21-7 after the opening quarter and 28-14 midway through the second, but scored touchdowns on four extended drives to move in front 35-31 with 10:33 remaining in the game.
How it Happened
Texas State opened the scoring with a 12-yard touchdown pass from Brad Jackson to Zechariah Sample, but Fresno State answered immediately.
Rayshon Luke
returned the ensuing kickoff 100 yards for a touchdown, tying the game at seven just 15 seconds later.
The Bobcats responded with two more first-quarter touchdowns. Davian Jackson scored on a 4-yard run before Brad Jackson connected with Sample for a 30-yard touchdown on the final play of the period, giving Texas State a 21-7 advantage.
Fresno State opened the second quarter with a 10-play, 75-yard drive. Mandal completed the possession with a 2-yard touchdown pass to
Brock Lium
, pulling the Bulldogs within seven.
Texas State answered with a 59-yard touchdown pass from Brad Jackson to Chris Dawn Jr., but the Bulldogs again responded with a lengthy march. Fresno State traveled 75 yards in 13 plays and took 6:43 off the clock before Donelson scored on a 1-yard run, cutting the deficit to 28-21 at halftime.
After Texas State extended its lead to 31-21 on a 35-yard field goal, Fresno State reached into its special teams playbook for its third touchdown. Facing fourth down at the Texas State 19-yard line, holder
Nick Verdugo
pitched it up to kicker
Ryan Kobzeff
who took the snap and raced into the end zone for a 19-yard touchdown. The score capped an 82-yard drive and brought the Bulldogs within 31-28 with 16 seconds left in the third quarter.
Fresno State's defense forced a three-and-out on the Bobcats' next possession, giving the Bulldogs an opportunity to take their first lead. Mandal directed a seven-play, 71-yard drive, which Donelson finished with a 2-yard touchdown run to put Fresno State ahead 35-31 with 10:33 remaining.
Texas State threatened to retake the lead on its ensuing possession, reaching the Fresno State 1-yard line.
Gerayas Grimes
forced a Torrance Burgess Jr. fumble as he approached the goal line, and would later recover the ball in the back of the end zone for a touchback with 5:55 remaining.
The Bulldogs were unable to capitalize on the turnover, however, as Texas State intercepted Mandal at the Bobcats' 48-yard line with 3:39 left. The Bobcats followed with a nine-play, 53-yard drive, taking a 37-35 lead on Davian Jackson's 2-yard touchdown run with one minute remaining. The Bulldogs blocked the ensuing extra-point attempt to keep Fresno State within two. The Bulldogs took possession at their own 25-yard line with a chance to set up a winning field goal, but a fumble on the second play of the drive allowed Texas State to secure the victory.
