CHAPEL HILL, N.C. –
University of Louisville defensive back
Jabari Mack
grabbed an interception in the final minute to secure a 16-14 win at North Carolina on Saturday evening in Chapel Hill.
Louisville (7-3, 6-2 ACC) caused a pair of turnovers and recorded six sacks as the Cardinals earned their fifth straight win.
Lincoln Kienholz
went 23-of-31 for 154 yards including a three-yard touchdown connection to
Jaleel Skinner
early in the second quarter.
Lawayne McCoy
led the Cards through the air in his return to the field, hauling in six catches for 64 yards.
Keyjuan Brown
powered UofL on the ground with 78 yards on 11 carries.
Mack totaled seven tackles and grabbed an interception to help solidify the win.
Stanquan Clark
topped the Cards' defense with 10 total tackles while adding a pair of tackles-for-loss including a sack.
Koen Entringer
recorded seven total tackles with a pair of sacks and a forced fumble early in the second quarter that set up a Louisville touchdown.
Louisville Head Coach
Jeff Brohm
said:
"We really stepped up and made a lot of big plays in the second half. I thought they played really good the whole game. We were able to pressure at times and get some key stops, then the four plays and out and the interception at the end was huge. So, great job by them. Offensively, moved in the first half, then we started to have some penalties. Knocked us back, got us off rhythm. Second half didn't have the ball much in the third quarter, and still we had some costly things that hurt us. So, we got to get better. But it was good to come out with a win on the road against North Carolina, who's played good football all year. So, really proud of our team."
