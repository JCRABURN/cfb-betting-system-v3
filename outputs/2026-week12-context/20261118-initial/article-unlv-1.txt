Football Rebels Take Three Of Four MW Weekly Honors
11/15/2026 5:48:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
MWN's @JKurtz_MWN reveals the @OldTrapper MW Football 𝗪𝗲𝗲𝗸 𝟭𝟭 Players of the Week @unlvfootball @NDSUfootball #BuiltBold https://t.co/zune9m2gdJ
— Mountain West (@MountainWest)
November 16, 2026
LAS VEGAS (UNLVRebels.com) –
UNLV senior quarterback
Jackson Arnold
has been named Old Trapper Mountain West Offensive Player of the Week while redshirt freshman punter
Matthew Choules
("Chewls") is both the Special Teams Player of the Week and Freshman of the Week, the league announced Monday.
Arnold (Denton, TX) earned his second league nod this fall by having a hand in all three of his team's touchdowns in the 21-17 upset victory at then-No. 23 New Mexico on Saturday night. He led the team in both passing yards (201) and rushing yards (98) and leads the MW in total touchdowns responsible for with 24 (17 passing and seven rushing). With UNLV trailing 17-7 in the fourth quarter, he found
Kayden "Big Play" McGee
for a 43-yard touchdown and then on the next drive ran it in himself from 44 yards out to give the Rebels a lead they would not relinquish en route to snapping the Lobos' 11-game home win streak.
Choules (Canberra, Australia) is the first UNLV punter to earn the MW special teams award since
Logan Yunker
in 2015 and is also the first specialist from the program to grab the freshman honor. Choules punted six times for a 49-yard average with UNM gaining a total of zero yards on returns. His night included a career-long 70-yarder and, after his team rallied to take the lead in the fourth quarter, he nailed a 50-yarder that pinned the Lobos on their own three-yard line as the Rebels notched their first win over a ranked opponent since 2008.
UNLV, which became bowl-eligible for the fourth consecutive season for the first time ever, makes its final regular-season road trip with a visit to San Jose State on Saturday at 4 p.m. The Rebels will then host UNR in the annual Battle for the Fremont Cannon on Nov. 28 at 6 p.m. Tickets for the Senior Night game at Allegiant Stadium are available now by visiting unlvtickets.com or calling (702) 739-FANS (3267).
