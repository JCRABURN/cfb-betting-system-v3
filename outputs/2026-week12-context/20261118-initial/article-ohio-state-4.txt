Pro Bucks: Week 10
11/17/2026 9:50:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Blake Bennett
COLUMBUS, Ohio –
Many Ohio State standouts notched impressive performances across the NFL in Week 10, highlighted by Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 171 receiving yards, Quinshon Judkins' game-winning touchdown and another 100-yard receiving performance from Garrett Wilson. On the defensive side of the ball, rookies
Arvell Reese
,
Sonny Styles
and
Caleb Downs
each led their respective teams in tackles, continuing their impressive debut seasons in the NFL.
Smith-Njigba Makes History in Seahawks Victory
Seattle Seahawks wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba recorded eight receptions for 171 yards and a touchdown in a 21-13 victory over the Las Vegas Raiders on Sunday.
After getting a slow start in the first half with one reception for 11 yards, Smith-Njigba erupted for 144 receiving yards in the third quarter, which set the franchise record for most receiving yards in a quarter.
Smith-Njigba became the first player in the Super Bowl era to record at least 1,200 receiving yards and 10 touchdowns through his first 10 games of the season.
2026 NFL Receiving Rankings
Receiving yards: 1,207 (1st)
Receptions: 79 (1st)
TD's: 10 (1st)
PFF Overall Grade: 92.8 (1st)
Styles and Reese Shine in Thursday Night Matchup
Ohio State teammates
Sonny Styles
and
Arvell Reese
recorded impressive performances against one another on Thursday night, each recording 13 tackles in the Washington Commanders' 24-21 victory over the New York Giants.
Styles recorded 13 tackles, including five solo stops and a tackle for loss. His fourth quarter tackles for loss forces New York to punt, giving Washington an opportunity to regain possession and they eventually scored the game-winning touchdown.
On the opposite sideline, Reese continued his impressive campaign, recording 13 tackles, including four solo stops, following his 3.0 sacks last week against the Philadelphia Eagles.
The Buckeyes have emerged as two of the NFL's most productive rookies. Reese has accumulated 81 tackles and 4.5 sacks, while Styles has recorded 80 tackles, 1.0 sacks and one interception.
Downs Records Double-Digit Tackles Against the 49ers
Dallas Cowboys rookie safety
Caleb Downs
recorded 11 tackles, including three solo stops, in a 46-40 loss to San Francisco.
Downs recorded his second double-digit tackle performance of the season. The Buckeye's performance followed his lockdown performance against the Indianapolis Colts in week nine.
Through the first 10 games of the season, Downs has accumulated 66 tackles, 2.0 sacks, an interception, three pass deflections and two forced fumbles as he continues to establish his role and continue his production.
Buckeyes Around the League
Buffalo Bills rookie cornerback
Davison Igbinosun
recorded four tackles, two pass breakups and the second interception of his career in a 31-12 win over the New York Jets on Sunday.
Elsewhere, Arizona Cardinals linebacker Baron Browning recorded six tackles, including three solo stops and a tackle for loss, in a 34-6 loss to the Los Angeles Rams on Sunday. While fellow Arizona linebacker Cody Simon collected his first career interception, six tackles and a pass deflection.
Houston Texans quarterback C.J. Stroud completed 20-of-38 (52.6 pct.) passes for 211 yards and three touchdowns in a 27-24 loss to the Cleveland Browns.
