Raiola Named Big Ten Offensive Player of the Week
11/16/26
|
Football
Share:
By:
Nate Krueger
EUGENE, Ore. -
Oregon quarterback Dylan Raiola was announced on Monday as the Big Ten Offensive Player of the Week for his performance in the Ducks' 31-7 win over Michigan.
It is the first career Big Ten honor for Raiola, who is the first Duck this season to earn Big Ten Offensive Player of the Week. He is the second Duck to earn a conference honor in 2026, along with kicker Gage Hurych (Special Teams, Week 7).
Raiola was terrific in his fifth start of the season, completing 20-of-28 passes for a season-high 341 yards and a career-high-tying four touchdown passes with no interceptions. Three of his touchdown passes went for 49 yards or more, including a 60-yard TD to Jeremiah McClellan on the fourth play of the game.
It was the third time Raiola has thrown four touchdown passes in his career, and first as a Duck. He recorded his seventh career game with three-plus passing touchdowns and fourth with 300-plus passing yards. He has thrown multiple touchdown passes with no interceptions in each of the last three games.
Raiola has found his groove in recent weeks while leading the Ducks to a three-game winning streak. Since Week 9, Raiola leads the Big Ten and ranks third nationally in passing yards (934) and passing yards per game (311.3) while ranking fourth in passing touchdowns. He is one of only three quarterbacks to have at least seven touchdown passes and zero interceptions over the last three games, and his 176.9 passer rating during that stretch is best in the conference and No. 6 in college football.
Raiola and the Ducks (7-3, 5-2 Big Ten) will play their final road game of the regular season this Friday, Nov. 20, at Michigan State (5:00 p.m. PT, FOX). Oregon is up to No. 14 in the latest Associated Press and AFCA Coaches polls and will learn its updated College Football Ranking on Tuesday evening.
