Flora the Paul Hornung Award Player of the Week
11.17.2026
|
Football
Share:
AMES, Iowa – Iowa State running back
Aiden Flora
is the Paul Hornung Award Player of the Week, the Louisville Sports Commission announced Tuesday. The award is presented annually by Texas Roadhouse to the most versatile player in major college football.
It is the second time this season Flora has received a weekly recognition from the Hornung Award. Fans can vote for Aiden online at
https://poll.fm/17450495
.
Flora stuffed the stat sheet in Iowa State's 41-13 win over Cincinnati on Saturday. He rushed for 216 yards and three touchdowns, had four receptions for 38 yards and completed one pass for 37 yards against the Bearcats.
Flora averaged 7.7 yards per carry against Cincinnati and scored on touchdown runs of 77, 11 and five yards, all of which came in the first quarter. He now has four rushing touchdowns of 60-plus yards this season, tied for the most nationally.
One of the most versatile players in the country, Flora also connected with his high school teammate
Vince Benetti
on a pop pass for a 37-yard gain on a fourth-down conversion in the first quarter. He accounted for 254 all-purpose yards in the game (216 rush, 38 rec), his fifth game this season with 200-plus all-purpose yards.
It was Flora's fourth game of the season with at least 200 scrimmage yards and multiple touchdowns from scrimmage. The only running backs in Big 12 history with more such games in a single season are 1998 Ricky Williams (six), 1997 Ricky Williams (six), 1996 Troy Davis (five), 1996 Byron Hanspard (five) and 1996 June Henley (five).
Dating back to 1956, Flora is the only FBS player with a punt return for a touchdown, two passing touchdowns and 12 rushing touchdowns in the same season. He is 4-for-4 passing for 85 yards and two touchdowns this season.
With two weeks remaining in the regular season, Flora ranks fourth nationally averaging 161.9 all-purpose yards and 14
th
in the country with 13 total touchdowns. He leads the nation in rushing (1,335) and is fourth nationally averaging 7.2 yards per carry.
Iowa State is back in action at UCF Friday at 5 p.m. (CT) on FS1.
