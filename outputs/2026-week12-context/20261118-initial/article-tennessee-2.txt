Bishop Named Burlsworth Trophy Finalist
November 17, 2026
|
Football
Share:
KNOXVILLE, Tenn. –
Tennessee running back
DeSean Bishop
was named a finalist for the 2026 Burlsworth Trophy on Tuesday, which is awarded annually to the most outstanding college football player who began his career as a walk-on.
Bishop was a semifinalist for the award last year after a breakout junior season that saw him rush for 1,076 yards and 16 touchdowns and becomes just the second player in program history to be tabbed as a finalist for the award, joining Nick Reveiz (2010).
The Knoxville native has built on that success this year with an SEC-leading 1,163 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns through 10 games. Bishop's impressive senior season has him garnering Heisman Trophy buzz and has earned him a spot as a semifinalist for the Walter Camp Player of the Year Award and the Maxwell Award.
Entering this weekend's regular season home finale against LSU, Bishop is one of just four players in the FBS with at least 1,100 rushing yards and 13 rushing touchdowns. He has found the end zone in 12 of his last 14 games dating back to last season and his 13 rushing scores rank second in the SEC and tied for ninth nationally.
Bishop has six 100-plus yard rushing performances this year and ranks seventh nationally in rushing yards on the year, including a Power 4-leading 715 rushing yards in the second half of games. The bruising senior back also ranks sixth nationally with 833 rushing yards after contact, according to Pro Football Focus.
The finalists and their families will be honored at the annual Burlsworth Trophy Award Show on Monday, Dec. 7, at Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art in Bentonville, Arkansas, where the 2026 recipient will be announced.
The full list of finalists for the 2026 Burlsworth Trophy can be found below.
