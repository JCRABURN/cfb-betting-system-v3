Wake Forest’s Gio Lopez Named Davey O’Brien Award Semifinalist
11/17/2026 3:59:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
This is the latest honor for Lopez, as he is also a semifinalist for both the Maxwell and Walter Camp Awards.
By:
Stephen Czarda
Share:
FORT WORTH, Texas –
Wake Forest redshirt junior quarterback
Gio Lopez
has been named a semifinalist for the Davey O'Brien Award, the Davey O'Brien Foundation announced on Tuesday. With his selection, Lopez is in contention for the 2026 Davey O'Brien National Quarterback Award, which is presented annually to the nation's best quarterback and is the oldest and most prestigious national quarterback award.
Through 10 games, Lopez ranks top-25 nationally in passing yards per game (241.1) and passing touchdowns (19), as the Demon Deacons are currently on pace to set a new program record for points per game. In terms of ESPN's advanced analytics, the Madison, Ala., native ranks No. 1 nationally in PAA (74.2) and second in EPA (73.9) and RAW (90.1).
He also has the highest offensive rating by all quarterbacks in Pro Football Focus' metrics and ranks No. 3 amongst all quarterbacks in rush rating and No. 5 in pass rating.
Additionally, Lopez is the only quarterback in the country with 25 or more total touchdowns to just one turnover.
Lopez has totaled 27 touchdowns along with 2,377 passing yards and 351 rushing yards.
Earlier this month, Lopez was named a semifinalist for both the Maxwell and Walter Camp Awards.
In October, he was also named a midseason addition to the Manning Award watchlist.
Lopez has
earned three Davey O'Brien Great 8 selections, is a three-time ACC Quarterback of the Week and has twice been named a Manning Award Star of the Week.
The Davey O'Brien Foundation will announce the three finalists next Tuesday. The 2026 Davey O'Brien Award winner will be unveiled live on ESPN on Friday, Dec. 11, during
The Home Depot College Football Awards.
