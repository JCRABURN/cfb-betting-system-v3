Messer Named Biletnikoff Award Semifinalist
11/16/2026 12:10:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
BOCA RATON, Fla.
–
Easton Messer
, Florida Atlantic football's dynamic fifth-year wide receiver, is one of 13 semifinalists for the Biletnikoff Award, as announced by the Tallahassee Quarterback Club (TQC) Foundation, Inc. on Monday afternoon.
The coveted award is given annually to the most outstanding collegiate receiver. The Louisville, Kentucky native is making his case in a season that has seen him score more points than any wide receiver in the nation, currently with 108 on 18 total touchdowns.
Messer currently leads the FBS in receptions (86), is tied for No. 3 in receiving yards (1,033) and ranks No. 5 in touchdown catches (13). His remarkable numbers have helped lead an explosive Owls offense that is No. 6 nationwide in passing yards per game (320.8) on the way to securing the program's first Bowl-eligible season since 2020.
Among the 12 semifinalists, Messer is tied for the single-game high in receptions with 16 on 16 targets. He is on a six-game streak with at least one touchdown catch, matched by only three other candidates for the award, and is one of just three semifinalists to have multiple games of at least three scoring receptions. He is also riding a 40-game streak with a reception which is third among active receivers.
This marks yet another honor racked up by the wideout. In the past week alone, Messer has also been named a finalist for the Paul Hornung and Jet Awards as well as a semifinalist for the Burlsworth Trophy.
In the upcoming week, three finalists will be voted on by a committee of 650 prominent college football journalists, commentators, announcers, Biletnikoff Award winners, and other former receivers. The finalists will then be officially unveiled on Nov. 24, with the winner announced on Dec. 11 on The Home Depot College Football Awards.
