Angeli Earns Manning Award Star of the Week
11/16/2026 4:25:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
After leading the Syracuse football team back from a 15-point deficit in the final minutes at NC State, quarterback
Steve Angeli
has been named one of the Manning Award Stars of the Week, the Allstate Sugar Bowl announced Monday.
Angeli completed 27-of-42 passes for 272 yards and two touchdowns as the Orange rallied for a 34-31 victory at Carter-Finley Stadium on Saturday. Syracuse improved to 5-5 and is one win away from bowl eligibility.
Over the past four games, Angeli has thrown for 250 yards per game and thrown six touchdowns with just one interception.
It was one of a number of awards Orange players earned on Monday from the performance, which included:
Calvin Russell III's
selection as ACC Offensive Receiver and Rookie of the Week and
Tunmise Adeleye's
selection as ACC Defensive Lineman of the Week.
