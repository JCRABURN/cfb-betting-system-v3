QB Kalieb Osborne Named to Davey O'Brien Great 8
11/16/2026 12:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
STORRS, CT
- UConn quarterback
Kalieb Osborne
was named to the Davey O'Brien Award Great 8 for week 11 thanks to his performance in leading the Huskies to a 46-22 win over James Madison over the weekend. The Davey O'Brien National Quarterback of the Week, selected from the Great 8 list, will be revealed Tuesday at 11 a.m. (CT).
Osborne led the Huskies to their fifth win of the season, completing 16-of-20 passes for 274 yards and three touchdowns. He ran for 88 yards on 16 carries to finish with 362 yards of total offense. His three touchdown throws, two in the fourth quarter, tied his career high. On the season, he is completing 60 percent of his passes (162-270) for 2,376 yards and 19 touchdowns with four interceptions. He has a 154.18 passer rating.
The Davey O'Brien Foundation was created in 1977 and is celebrating its 50th anniversary season this year. The National Quarterback Award, the oldest and most prestigious college quarterback award, was first issued in 1981.
