Football Game Notes
Press Conference: Coach Chadwell
Press Conference: Players
Share:
As part of the team's preparations for their Conference USA game against WKU, Liberty Head Football Coach
Jamey Chadwell
met with the media on Tuesday.
During the media session, Chadwell shared his final thoughts on the Military Appreciation Day game win over MTSU, plus looked ahead to this week's Senior Day game against WKU.
After Chadwell's weekly presser, offensive lineman
Joep Engbers
and linebacker
Micah Pollard
addressed the media from the Media Room inside the Liberty Football Center.
The Flames clinched a spot in the Dec. 4 CUSA Football Championship Game with their 28-23 win over Middle Tennessee last Saturday. One more victory would wrap up at least a share of their second CUSA regular-season crown (also 2023). A Liberty win combined with a Jax State loss would guarantee the CUSA title game will be played at Williams Stadium.
Liberty is riding a number of streaks, including nine straight wins overall, five consecutive home wins and five wins over WKU in a row. As a result, the Flames are ranked No. 22 in this week's AP Top 25 for their first national ranking since 2023.
Jamey Chadwell
is 34-14 in his fourth season at Liberty and owns a 65-20 coaching record dating back to the start of 2020. Chadwell is 23-8 against CUSA opponents with the Flames, including 13-3 at home.
Liberty rushed for a season-best 370 yards against Middle Tennessee and now ranks No. 11 nationally with 221.5 rushing yards per contest.
Kam Davis
(career-high 20 carries, 126 yards) posted his third 100-yard outing this season and scored a TD for the sixth game in a row.
Liberty's defense is only allowing 11.7 ppg in its six CUSA victories and became the first team in conference history to limit five consecutive CUSA foes to 14 points or fewer. The Flames have held four straight opponents below 100 rushing yards for the first time in their FBS era.
