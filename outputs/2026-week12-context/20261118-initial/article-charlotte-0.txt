Sluggish Second Half Hurts Niners Against ECU
11/14/2026 7:29:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
PDF Box Score
Postgame Notes
Share:
CHARLOTTE, N.C. --
Charlotte Football suffered a 17-16 loss to East Carolina on Saturday afternoon (Nov. 14) at Jerry Richardson Stadium.
The one-point loss was Charlotte's (1-9, 1-5 American) narrowest margin of defeat since 2017 and the fourth loss by a single point in program history. With a pair of overtime losses and a five-point defeat at App State in Week 3, this is just the second season the 49ers have had four one-score losses.
"That was down to the wire," head coach
Tim Albin
said. "We knew going in it was going to be a low-scoring game, and it was. It was the second week in a row we couldn't get the ball in the end zone. An unbelievable effort from the defense. There was a time in the fourth quarter when we had five starters out. I'm really liking some of the things I'm seeing on the back end for two weeks in a row. It's been a focus point since week five, and I like where it's headed. I'm really proud of the defensive piece today. Offensively, when we can't run the football, it's tough to play quarterback."
East Carolina (6-4, 4-2 American) outgained the Niners 307-200 and held Charlotte to only 22 yards in the second half. A stand by Charlotte's defense that led to a missed field goal gave the Niners a chance to march down the field with just over two minutes remaining, but an interception dashed the Green and White's hopes of a game-winning drive.
Building on one of its best games of the last two seasons a week ago at UAB, Charlotte's defense allowed the fewest points in a game since holding East Carolina to seven points in the first meeting between the two sides in 2023.
The Niners had multiple takeaways for the third game in a row, getting two interceptions and a pair of fumble recoveries.
Dy'Lon Womack
was responsible for half of the takeaways, notching his first interception and scooping up a fumble right before the end of the first half.
Jonathan Patterson
had his second interception in as many games with the first takeaway of the day and long snapper
Henry Searcy
jumped on a muffed punt to set up the first of three field goals from
Aiden Bui
.
Along with the two interceptions, Charlotte's defense broke up eight passes to match the program record for pass break-ups in a single game. It's the sixth time the 49ers have broken up eight passes in one contest and the first time doing so since the 2018 season opener against Fordham. The Niners also had 10 tackles for loss in the game, matching their season high set against Louisiana. The 10 against the Pirates give the Niners 54 TFLs on the year, surpassing last season's total with two games still to play.
Gavin Willis
led the team with 2.5 of those tackles for loss as part of a season-high 13 tackles for the senior captain. It's his fourth double-digit tackle game of the season and gives him 88 on the year to move into seventh place on Charlotte's single-season tackle list. Fellow linebacker
Kadin Schmitz
joined him in double digits with 11 against ECU for his third game this year with 11 stops.
Bui scored the majority of Charlotte's points with three made field goals, matching a Jerry Richardson Stadium record for field goals in a single game. It was the 10th time a Charlotte kicker has connected three times in a game, with Bui being the seventh different 49er kicker to accomplish the feat. His first field goal of the game was a 50-yarder, a career-long and Charlotte's first 50-yard FG since the 2024 season.
The Niners' only touchdown of the game came on an 11-yard pass from
Conner Harrell
to
Jaden Barnes
in the second quarter, giving Barnes a touchdown in three consecutive games after not finding the end zone in the first seven weeks of the season.
Derrick Eley
had the majority of Charlotte's receiving yards, matching his career high with 96 yards on only four catches.
Harrell threw for 161 yards to outgain the Pirates through the air, but ECU's defense held the 49ers to just 39 rushing yards compared to 164 for the visitors. Charlotte's rushing yards were deflated from 47 yards in sacks, with
Henry Rutledge
picking up 66 yards on 15 carries.
HOW IT HAPPENED
Charlotte scored the first points of the game after turning an ECU muffed punt into a field goal. The Pirates held Charlotte to a three-and-out, but the East Carolina returner couldn't field the ball cleanly and the Niners' long snapper was there to pounce on the loose ball. Charlotte only managed three yards after the turnover, but Bui connected on a career-long 50-yarder to open the scoring.
Patterson snagged his second interception in as many games to end ECU's next possession, but the Green and White tossed one of their one play later, setting up an East Carolina touchdown to start the second quarter.
Charlotte countered with a touchdown of its own, marching 75 yards down the field in seven plays. A 16-yard pass to Eley with a personal foul tacked on put the Niners in the red zone, and two plays later Harrell found Barnes in the corner of the end zone for the score.
The Niners' defense came up big again after taking the lead, holding ECU to a three-and-out, an interception, and a fumble on the Pirates' final three drives of the first half. Charlotte used the two turnovers to tack on a pair of field goals, with Womack central to both takeaways.
He picked off a tipped pass to set up the second field goal of the day from Bui and then scooped up a fumble forced by Willis to allow Bui a third field goal of the half. Womack initially took the fumble to the house, but a penalty brought the play back to midfield. Harrell again found Eley to set up the scoring drive, this time for 30 yards, and Bui connected from 37 yards out to end the half.
Charlotte's offense was stymied in the second half, managing only 22 yards in the final 30 minutes. The Niners crossed into ECU territory just once after the break, on a drive that ended in a turnover on downs. Charlotte's five other drives in the second half were four three-and-outs and a game-ending interception.
East Carolina scored on its first drive coming out of halftime to pull within two after forcing a punt on Charlotte's first possession of the second half. The Niners held ECU to a turnover on downs and a punt, but then the Pirates got in position to kick a go-ahead field goal halfway through the fourth quarter.
The Pirates missed a field goal that would have extended their lead to four points with just over two minutes to go, giving the Niners a chance to mount a comeback. A six-yard run by Rutledge brought the game to the two-minute timeout, and the very next play was an interception to secure the win for ECU.
