Weigman Named Semifinalist for Davey O’Brien Award
11/17/2026 3:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
Quarterback advances to semifinals of QB of the Year Award
HOUSTON –
A week after he was named to the Davey O'Brien Quarterback Class of 2026, Houston Football quarterback
Conner Weigman
earned a spot in the semifinals of the award given to the top quarterback in college football.
The fifth-year signal caller from Cypress, Texas, was one of 13 quarterbacks named a semifinalist, along with one of five from the Big 12 Conference and became Houston's first Davey O'Brien Award Semifinalist since Greg Ward Jr. in 2015, and aims to be the first Cougar to win the award since Case Keenum in 2011.
In his final collegiate season, Weigman has tied his single-season high with 25 passing touchdowns through 10 games and has tallied 32 total touchdowns this season – the third most in the country.
Weigman is the only Power 4 quarterback with 25+ passing touchdowns and 5+ rushing touchdowns (25 and 7, respectively) and is one of two Power 4 conference gun slingers with 25+ passing touchdowns and three-or-fewer interceptions (Darian Mensah, Miami).
Since 2025, Weigman has tallied 68 total touchdowns (50 passing, 18 rushing), the most by a player who has only played Power 4 football and the second most among all active quarterbacks.
This season, Weigman has accounted for 3+ touchdowns in eight different games, tied for the most in the country.
The Cypress, Texas, native has been one of the best players on third down, guiding the Cougars to what would be the best third down conversion percentage in the last 30 years – 58.9 percent through 10 games.
Houston has converted at least half of its third-down attempts in all 10 games this season under Weigman's guidance at quarterback; a record that is tied for the longest streak within a season by an FBS team in the last 30 years (Georgia Tech – 10 straight in 2014).
ABOUT THE DAVEY O'BRIEN AWARD
The Davey O'Brien National Quarterback Award is presented annually to the nation's best college quarterback and is the oldest and most prestigious national quarterback award. The 50th Annual Davey O'Brien Awards Dinner honoring the winner will be held Monday, March 8, 2027, at The Fort Worth Club in Fort Worth, Texas.
