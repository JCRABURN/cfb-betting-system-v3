Jones Named Shaun Alexander Award Freshman of the Week
11/17/2026 8:00:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
BOISE, Idaho –
Boise State wide receiver
Rasean Jones
was named the Shaun Alexander Award Freshman of the Week, the Maxwell Football Club announced. He earned the recognition for the first time in his career after a historic performance against Oregon State, where he totaled 11 receptions for 230 yards and three touchdowns in a 46-41 victory.
Jones recorded the fifth-highest single-game receiving total in program history and became the first freshman in school history to record multiple three-touchdown games. It was his second 100-yard, three-touchdown effort of the season, making him one of two FBS players to achieve that feat (Malachi Toney, Miami). His scores came on catches of 19, 16 and 26 yards, helping Boise State win its ninth straight game and clinch home field advantage in the Pac-12 Championship.
In the last three games, Jones has 26 receptions for 463 yards and seven touchdowns. He has 900 yards on the season, ranking eighth in the nation with 100.0 yards per game. His eight receiving touchdowns are the 16th-most in the country, despite missing a game. He ranks second among FBS freshmen in all three categories. The Baker City, Ore. native is a four-time Pac-12 Freshman of the Week and two-time Pac-12 Offensive Player of the Week.
No. 15/15/24 Boise State (9-1, 6-0 Pac-12) meets San Diego State (5-5, 4-2 Pac-12) for the final regular season game on The Blue, Nov. 21. The Broncos and Aztecs start at 7:30 p.m. on USA Sports.
--BroncoSports.com--
