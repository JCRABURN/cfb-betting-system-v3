Mahomes II throws for 348 yards and 5 touchdowns in big Sunday win
By:
Jay Strader
Red Raiders in the NFL | 2026 Regular Season Week 10, Nov. 11-Nov. 15
LUBBOCK, Texas –
Patrick Mahomes II and Chiefs lit up the scoreboard in Atlanta, putting up 35 points thanks to five passing touchdowns by the former Red Raider. Jordyn Brooks again led the Dolphins on the defensive end, recording nine tackles, a forced fumble and a pass breakup.
A complete list of statistics by former Red Raiders in the NFL is below.
ARIZONA CARDINALS
vs. Los Angeles Rams (L, 34-6)
S Dadrion Taylor-Demerson (2019-23):
Taylor-Demerson played 19 snaps against the Rams but did not record a stat
• Taylor-Demerson this week: No stats
• Taylor-Demerson this season: 7 GP / 2 GS | 15 tackles (10 solo)
WR
Reggie Virgil
(2025):
Virgil played in 16 snaps on Sunday but did not record a stat
• Virgil this week: No stats
• Virgil this season: 6 GP / 1 GS | 3 receptions for 54 yards, LNG 25
BALTIMORE RAVENS
vs. Los Angeles Chargers (L, 23-20)
DL Broderick Washington Jr. (2016-19):
Washington recorded his fifth start of the season and recorded a tackle in the Ravens Monday night loss to the Chargers.
• Washington Jr. this week: 1 tackle
• Washington Jr. this season: 8 GP / 5 GS | 9 tackles (3 solo), 3 PD, 1 INT
CAROLINA PANTHERS
at New Orleans (W, 20-17)
DL
Lee Hunter
(2025):
Hunter finished the game against the Saints with 3 tackles, including a tackle for loss, and a hit on the QB in just 16 snaps played.
• Hunter this week: 3 tackles, 1 TFL, 1 QB hit
• Hunter this season: 9 GP / 5 GS | 18 tackles (8 solo), 1 QBH
CINCINNATI BENGALS
vs. Pittsburgh Steelers (W, 38-18)
RB Tahj Brooks (2020-24):
Brooks registered 3 snaps on offense for the Bengals and 17 on special teams.
• Brooks this week: No stats
• Brooks this season: 9 GP / 0 GS | 3 returns (76 yards), 3 carries for 6 yards | 2 special teams tackles
DALLAS COWBOYS
vs. San Fransico 49ers (L, 46-40)
OL Terence Steele (2016-19):
Played all 83 offensive snaps for the Cowboys in his 10
th
consecutive start.
• Steele this week: No stats
• Steele this season: 10 GP / 10 GS
DETROIT LIONS
vs. New England Patriots (W, 35-10)
DL
Skyler Gill-Howard
(2026):
Gill-Howard played 31 of the 59 snaps on defense for the Lions in their win over the Patriots but did not record a stat.
• Gill-Howard this week: No stats
• Gill-Howard this season: 10 GP / 0 GS | 11 tackles (2 solo), 5 QBHs, 2.0 sack
KANSAS CITY CHIEFS
at Atlanta Falcons (W, 35-13)
QB Patrick Mahomes II (2014-16):
Mahomes II and Chiefs lit up the Falcons defense. He finished with 348 yards on 22 completions and five touchdowns to finish with a 135.5 QBR.
• Mahomes this week: 30-for-41, 321 yards, 3 TD | 2 rushes for 6 yards (long of 4)
• Mahomes this season: 9 GP / 9 GS | 199-for-298, 2,385 yards, 19 TDs, 4 INTs, 1 rushing TD
LAS VEGAS RAIDERS
at Seattle Seahawks (L, 21-13)
OL Caleb Rogers (2020-24):
Rogers recorded three snaps on special teams.
• Rogers this week: No stats
• Rogers this season: 9 GP / 3 GS
MIAMI DOLPHINS
at Indianapolis Colts (L, 24-22)
WR
Caleb Douglas
(2024-25):
Douglas caught three passes for 71 yards on eight targets in Sunday's game against the Colts as he finished as the team's leading receiver.
• Douglas this week: 3 catches for 71 yards on 8 targets.
• Douglas this season: 6 GP / 6 GS | 22 receptions for 367 yards, 2 TDs
LB Jordyn Brooks (2016-19):
Brooks recorded a team-high nine tackles including a tackle for loss and also forced a fumble and broke up a pass.
• Brooks this week: 9 tackles (6 solo), 1 TFL, 1 PD, 1 FF
• Brooks this season: 9 GP / 9 GS | 84 tackles (38 solo), 4 TFLs, 2 PD, 2 QBH, 1 FF
LB
Jacob Rodriguez
(2023-25):
Rodriguez recorded his ninth straight start for the Dolphins and recorded a solo tackle on Sunday.
• Rodriguez this week: 1 tackle (1 solo)
• Rodriguez this season: 9 GP / 9 GS | 80 tackles (36 solo), 4 TFLs, 1 INT, 2 QBH, 1 FF, 1 PD
NEW ENGLAND PATRIOTS
vs. Detroit Lions (L, 35-10)
QB
Behren Morton
(2021-25):
Morton was listed on the inactive list prior to the game as the emergency No. 3 quarterback.
• Morton this week: No stats
• Morton this season: 0 GP / 0 GS
NEW ORLEANS SAINTS
vs. Carolina Panthers (L, 20-17)
DE Tyree Wilson (2020-22):
Wilson recorded one tackle in the Saints loss to the Panthers on Sunday.
• Wilson this week: 1 tackle (1 solo)
• Wilson this season: 7 GP / 0 GS | 19 tackles (10 solo), 1 sack
NEW YORK JETS
vs. Buffalo Bills (L, 12-31)
DE
David Bailey
(2025):

• Bailey this week: Recorded a tackle and two pass deflections against the Bills.
• Bailey this season: 9 GP / 9 GS | 45 tackles (26 solo), 12 QBHs, 10.0 TFLs, 7.5 sacks, 3 FF, 3 PD
P Austin McNamara (2020-23):

• McNamara: 4 punts for 184 yards, 1 i20
• McNamara this season: 10 GP / 0 GS | 38 punts for 1,808 (47.6) yards, long 62, 12 i20, 2 TB
WASHINGTON COMMANDERS
vs. New York Giants (W, 24-21)
DB Tyler Owens (2022-23):

• Owens this week: 2 tackles (1 solo)
• Owens this season: 8 GP/ 0 GS | 8 special teams tackles (5 solo)
