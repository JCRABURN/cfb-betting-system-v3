Cards Aim to Extend Win Streak in Chapel Hill
November 13, 2026
|
Football
Share:
By:
Stephen Williams
LOUISVILLE, Ky. –
The University of Louisville football team will play its second consecutive road game as it takes on North Carolina in Chapel Hill at 7 p.m. ET on Saturday. The game will be televised on ESPN2 with radio coverage available on 93.9 The Ville and 970 WGTK.
HOW TO FOLLOW
TV –
ESPN2 (Mike Couzens, Tom Luginbill, Lauren Sisler)
Radio –
93.9 The Ville / 970 WGTK (Paul Rogers, Craig Swabek, Jody Demling)
Live Stats –
GoCards.com
Social –
@LouisvilleFB
TEAM NOTES
The Cardinals jumped in front early and never looked back in a 44-17 victory at Georgia Tech last Saturday.
The 27-point margin in Saturday's win was Louisville's largest road victory since defeating Kentucky 41-14 in the 2024 regular season finale, and the largest at a conference foe since a 62-22 win at Duke in 2021.
Jeff Brohm
secured career victory number 100 with the win at Georgia Tech. Only eight current FBS coaches reached 100 wins at the FBS level in fewer games than Brohm.
NOTABLE NUMBERS
44 –
Louisville's 44 points at GT were its most on the road under
Jeff Brohm
and the most since the 2021 game at Duke.
27 –
Louisville has scored at least 27 points in all nine games this season, one of two FBS teams to do so (Boise State).
251 –
The Cardinals rushed for 251 yards in the win at Georgia Tech, the fifth time this season they've had at least 250.
52 –
Tre Richardson
scored on a 52-yard rush at GT, keeping the Cards' streak of a 50-yard TD in every game this season alive.
11 –
Louisville has won 11 conference road games under
Jeff Brohm
, tied for the most in the ACC during that time.
INSIDE THE SERIES
Louisville and North Carolina have met eight times previously with the Cardinals holding a 5-3 edge in the series.
Despite being in the ACC since 2014, Louisville and North Carolina have only met one time as conference foes.
The only ACC meeting between the two teams came in 2017, as the Cardinals picked up a 47-35 victory in Chapel Hill.
Louisville is 3-2 all-time against the Tar Heels in Chapel Hill.
LOUISVILLE-NORTH CAROLINA CONNECTIONS
Louisville defensive end
Tyler Thompson
played the first three years of his collegiate career for the Tar Heels. Thompson played in 25 games for UNC and had a breakout 2025 season in which he ranked eighth in the ACC with 7.0 sacks.
Louisville defensive back
Tayon Holloway
started his collegiate career at North Carolina, playing two seasons in Chapel Hill. Holloway made six starts in 2023, finishing with 19 tackles and 1.5 TFLs.
North Carolina offensive coordinator Bobby Petrino did three stints on the Louisville coaching staff, including two as head coach. Petrino coached the Cards from 2003-06 and 2014-18, going 77-35 in nine seasons.
Petrino is also the brother of Louisville tight ends coach
Paul Petrino
.
UNC quarterbacks coach Matt Lombardi began his coaching career at Louisville working as a special teams quality control coach in 2014 before working with tight ends as a graduate assistant in 2015-16.
North Carolina wide receivers coach Garrick McGee has twice previously worked at Louisville. McGee was the offensive coordinator and quarterbacks coach in 2014-15 and then the wide receivers coach under
Jeff Brohm
from 2023-24.
Louisville wide receivers coach
Deion Branch
played seven of his 11 NFL seasons with the New England Patriots for current UNC head coach Bill Belichick. Branch was part of two Super Bowl winning teams in New England, including earning Super Bowl XXXIX MVP honors.
BROHM REACHES THE CENTURY MARK IN CAREER WINS
Jeff Brohm
earned the 100th victory of his coaching career with the 44-17 victory at Georgia Tech on Nov. 7. His 100 career wins have been distributed nearly evenly across his three head coaching stops – 30 at Western Kentucky, 36 at Purdue, 34 at Louisville.
Brohm became the 23rd active FBS coach to reach 100 wins at the FBS level and the ninth-fastest among the group to reach the century mark, doing so in 159 games.
Brohm's 34 wins at Louisville have him sixth all-time in program history, just three shy of Charlie Strong for fifth.
TAKING CARE OF BUSINESS ON THE ROAD
Louisville notched a 44-17 road victory at Georgia Tech on Nov. 7 to improve to 2-1 on the road this season. Under
Jeff Brohm
, the Cardinals are 12-5 in true road games, including an impressive 11-4 mark in road ACC contests.
The 11 road wins in ACC play over the last four seasons are tied with Clemson for the most during that span. Miami (10) is the only other ACC program with more than eight.
Louisville and Miami are tied for the most wins in ACC play overall, 21, since the start of the 2023 season.
CARDINALS FLEXING MUSCLES ON THE GROUND
The Louisville offense has been one of the best in the country this season, ranking sixth nationally in scoring offense and eighth in total offense. One of the ways the Cards are doing it is by gashing the opposition on the ground.
Louisville leads the country averaging 6.33 yards per carry and is second to only Army with 32 rushing touchdowns. Louisville's current 228.0 yards per game average would be its highest total on the ground since 2017, Lamar Jackson's final season.
The Cardinals have 26 carries this season that have gone for at least 20 yards and are tied for the national lead in rushes of 60, 70, 80 and 90 yards.
