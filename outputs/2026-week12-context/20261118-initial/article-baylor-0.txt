Kickoff Time at No. 10 BYU Moved to 6 p.m. CT
11/13/2026 10:15:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
WACO, Texas –
Due to flight challenges as a result of the FAA's national ground stop, the Baylor kickoff at No. 10 BYU has been moved to 6 p.m. CT with the game set for a telecast on FOX and a broadcast on the Baylor Sports Media Network, it was announced by the Big 12 Conference on Friday night.
Baylor (6-3, 4-2 Big 12) will face BYU (7-2, 4-2 Big 12) at 6 p.m. CT on FOX as a result of the travel impact. Baylor plans on departing Waco on Saturday morning.
- BaylorBears.com -
