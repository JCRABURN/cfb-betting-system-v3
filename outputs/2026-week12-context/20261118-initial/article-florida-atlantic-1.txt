Florida Atlantic Stalls in Tulsa
11/14/2026 11:55:00 PM

By:
Ben Paul
TULSA, Okla.
– Florida Atlantic football fell to Tulsa on the road, 38-21, on Saturday evening.
After nationwide travel issues forced a rare day-of-game flight, the Owls (6-4, 3-3) put up a fight with an active passing game but were ultimately outmatched by the Golden Hurricane (6-4, 3-3).
Graduate QB
Caden Veltkamp
broke his own single-game program record with 58 pass attempts, completing 32 of them for 340 yards and recording his seventh consecutive game with multiple touchdown throws.
VIDEO RECAP
THE BEGINNING
Both defenses held firm for much of the opening quarter before Tulsa struck first with two touchdowns.
Veltkamp soon found his favorite target, fifth-year WR
Easton Messer
, who hauled in a contested catch in the end zone for his 18th touchdown of the season, to close the deficit to 14-7.
THE TURNING POINT
The Golden Hurricane answered with another score to go back up by double-digits.
A strip sack and subsequent fumble recovery by senior DB
Derrick Rogers
gave the Owls the ball in opposing territory, and senior RB
Brady Tillman III
capped off the ensuing drive with an eight-yard rush into the end zone.
THE FINISH
Entering the second half trailing 21-14, Florida Atlantic fell behind further after conceding 10 unanswered points.
The fourth quarter opened with a quick touchdown pass to senior WR
Kelby Valsin
, with the Golden Hurricane yet again responding with a score of their own. An interception by graduate DB
JK Johnson
set up a lengthy drive for the offense that came up short on a fourth-and-goal, preventing a final chance at a comeback.
VIDEO RECAP
OFFENSE
In the first quarter, Veltkamp reached 10,000 collegiate passing yards, joining only six other active FBS quarterbacks. He finished the night with his 11th 300-yard passing game as an Owl.
Messer is now the first Owl to record back-to-back 1,000 receiving yard seasons and is currently on a six-game streak with at least one touchdown catch, a program record. He is on a 40-game streak with at least one reception, actively the third longest in FBS.
Valsin led the receivers with 10 catches for 94 yards, but it was freshman TE
Owen Pollock
who had the team's longest play of the night with a grab for 29 yards.
DEFENSE
As a unit, the defense totaled 12 tackles for loss. It marked the first time the Owls have logged double digits in back-to-back games since 2023.
Senior EDGE
Jonathan Bax
turned in another monster performance with 3.5 tackles for a loss and a sack in the first half alone.
Graduate DBs
Tyson Rooks
and
JK Johnson
each recorded their first interception as an Owl. The defense won the turnover battle, 3-1.
SPECIAL TEAMS
Fifth-year RB
Leonard Farrow
returned a kickoff for 54 yards.

Hagerty Family Head Football Coach
