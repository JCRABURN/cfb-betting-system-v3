Sategna Earns Second SEC Special Teams Player of the Week Honor
November  16, 2026
|
Football
OU vs. Ole Miss Recap
SEC Release
Share:
NORMAN —
University of Oklahoma wide receiver
Isaiah Sategna III
was named the SEC Special Teams Player of the Week for the second time this season after he returned a punt 75 yards for a touchdown in OU's 24-17 win over No. 19 Ole Miss on Sunday, the conference office announced Monday.
Sategna's punt return, which was his second of the season, gave the Sooners a 14-0 lead in the first quarter. He also had an 88-yard return in the season opener against UTEP, and is the first Sooner with multiple punt returns for a touchdown in a season since Jalen Saunders in 2013.
A fifth-year senior from Austin, Texas, Sategna is tied for the national lead with his two punt return touchdowns this season and ranks fourth nationally and leads the SEC with his average of 16.5 yards per punt return. He also leads the Sooners in receiving with his 50 catches for 628 yards and ranks fifth in the SEC with his 101.2 all-purpose yards per game.
Sategna was also named SEC Special Teams Player of the Week on Sept. 7. This is OU's seventh SEC weekly award this season. Senior defensive back
Peyton Bowen
was named SEC Defensive Player of the Week on Oct. 12, fifth-year kicker Tate Sandel was named SEC Special Teams Player of the Week on Oct. 12, freshman
Jake Kreul
was named SEC Freshman of the Week on Oct. 19, sixth-year linebacker
Owen Heinecke
was named SEC Defensive Player of the Week on Oct. 26 and sophomore offensive lineman
Michael Fasusi
was named SEC Offensive Lineman of the Week on Nov. 9.
Oklahoma (6-4, 4-3 SEC) hosts Texas A&M (4-6, 1-6 SEC) on Saturday at Gaylord Family – Oklahoma Memorial Stadium. Kickoff is set for 2:45 p.m. CT on ESPN2.
