Sheppard a Doak Walker Award Semifinalist
11/17/2026 12:01:00 PM
|
Football
All Aboard the Nate Train
Share:
DALLAS (SMU) –
Sophomore running back
Nate Sheppard
was announced Tuesday as one of 10 semifinalists for the Doak Walker Award by the PwC SMU Athletic Forum. The award is presented annually to the nation's top running back.
The 10 semifinalists include the nation's top three players in rushing yards and rushing yards per game, two returning semifinalists and six players who have earned the Doak Walker National Running Back of the Week honor this season.
Last week, Sheppard was tabbed a semifinalist for the
Walter Camp Player of Year
and the
Maxwell Award
. Sheppard is a five-time ACC Running Back of the Week and two-time Doak Walker National Running Back of the Week selection this fall.
Sheppard has tallied 207 carries for 1,350 rushing yards (6.38) and 19 touchdowns with 44 receptions for 399 yards (9.07) and four scores. He has completed his three pass attempts for 44 yards and a TD while collecting 1,719 all-purpose yards. Sheppard enters the weekend leading all FBS student-athletes in points per game (14.00), total points scored (140), total TDs scored (23), all-purpose yards per game (171.90) and plays from scrimmage of 10+ yards (54). He ranks second in rushing touchdowns (19), rushing yards (1,320) and rushing yards per game (132.00).
Sheppard has joined an elite list of student-athletes in the last 30 years to accumulate at least 1,300 rushing yards, 300 receiving yards, 40 receptions and 19 rushing touchdowns in a season with Larry Johnson (2002), Steven Jackson (2003), Brian Calhoun (2005), Jacquizz Rodgers (2009), Najee Harris (2020) and Cam Skattebo (2024),
Three finalists will be announced Tuesday, Nov. 24. The winner will be unveiled live on ESPN during
The
Home Depot College Football Awards
on Friday, Dec. 11. The formal presentation of the award will take place at the Doak Walker Award Presentation Banquet on Friday, Jan. 29, 2027, in Dallas. FOX Sports' Joel Klatt and Jenny Taft will host the event, which also will honor the recipient of the 2026 PwC Doak Walker Legends Award.
