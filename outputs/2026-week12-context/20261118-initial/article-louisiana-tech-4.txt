Mayfield, Matthews Tabbed With Sun Belt Weekly Honors
11/16/2026 1:12:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
Third-year linebacker Jadon Mayfield and fourth-year punter Lexington Matthews were named Sun Belt Defensive and Special Teams Players of the Week.
By:
Drew Parsley
RUSTON –
Louisiana Tech linebacker
Jadon Mayfield
and punter
Lexington Matthews
were named Sun Belt Conference Defensive and Special Teams Players of the Week, respectively, the league announced Monday following the Bulldogs' 63-31 victory over Southern Miss on Saturday.
LA Tech now has consecutive weeks with a Defensive Player of the Week after
Alonzo Jackson Jr.
received the weekly honor on November 9. It is the first time since 2025 the Bulldogs have multiple conference weekly honors in the same week as well as having at least one in consecutive weeks.
The pair of weekly accolades comes after Louisiana Tech (8-2, 6-0 SBC) extended its winning streak to seven games and clinched the Sun Belt West Division championship, securing a berth in the conference championship game in its inaugural season as a league member.
Mayfield delivered a standout defensive performance against the Golden Eagles, recording a game-high nine tackles, including four solo stops, while adding his first career interception and defensive touchdown.
With Louisiana Tech holding a 28-10 advantage midway through the second quarter, Mayfield intercepted Southern Miss quarterback John White and returned the ball 36 yards for a touchdown, extending the Bulldogs' lead to 35-10 with 9:14 remaining before halftime.
Mayfield's interception return sparked a historic defensive sequence as
Jordan McRae
followed with an 89-yard interception return for a touchdown on Southern Miss' ensuing possession. It marked the first time in at least the last 25 seasons that Louisiana Tech returned interceptions for touchdowns on consecutive opponent possessions.
The Bulldogs' defense forced five turnovers for the second consecutive game, including four interceptions and a fumble recovery, while recording six tackles for loss and three sacks. Louisiana Tech scored 28 points off turnovers and limited Southern Miss to 4-of-16 on third-down conversions.
Matthews played a pivotal role in Louisiana Tech's special teams' success, averaging 46.8 yards on five punts for 234 total yards while placing three punts inside Southern Miss' 20-yard line.
The Bulldogs' punter recorded a game-long 66-yard punt and consistently helped Louisiana Tech control field position throughout the contest. One of his most impactful punts came during the first quarter when he pinned Southern Miss at its own 1-yard line, with
Kam Franklin
preventing the ball from entering the end zone before
Alonzo Jackson Jr.
downed the punt.
Matthews also delivered a 47-yard punt in the second quarter that pinned the Golden Eagles at their own 10-yard line.
Mayfield, Matthews and the Bulldogs are back in action for their final regular-season home game hosting Arkansas State on November 21. Kickoff at Joe Aillet Stadium on Saturday is set for 6 p.m. CT on ESPN+.
