Game Time and TV Coverage Announced for 100th Territorial Cup
11/16/2026
|
Football
Purchase Tickets
2026 Football Schedule
Share:
By:
Arizona Athletics
TUCSON, Ariz. —
The 100th Territorial Cup on Saturday, Nov. 28 featuring a duel in the desert between Arizona and Arizona State at Casino Del Sol Stadium in Tucson will kick off at 7:00 p.m. MST with a national television broadcast on FOX, the Big 12 Conference announced this morning.
The Wildcats and Sun Devils will close out their regular seasons with their 100th all-time meeting. Arizona holds the lead in the all-time Territorial Cup series, 52-46-1.
Arizona, currently sitting at 7-3 with a 4-3 conference record, will head to Manhattan, Kansas for their final road game of the season this week before returning home for the Territorial Cup.
The Wildcats matchup with the the Sun Devils on FOX will be Arizona's 11th nationally televised game of the season.
