Noah Fifita Earns Davey O'Brien Award Semifinalist Selection
11/17/2026
|
Football
Noah Fifita Bio
Share:
By:
Arizona Athletics
TUCSON, Ariz. —
Arizona Football quarterback
Noah Fifita
, one of the top signal callers in the nation, has been named a semifinalist for the Davey O'Brien National Quarterback Award, the Davey O'Brien Foundation announced on Tuesday afternoon.
The Davey O'Brien National Quarterback Award is presented annually to the nation's best college quarterback and is the oldest and most prestigious national quarterback award. The Davey O'Brien Foundation is celebrating its 50th anniversary season this year.
Fifita, the nation's active career leader in passing yards and passing touchdowns, currently ranks top 10 in the nation in nearly every passing statistical category. Through 10 games he has thrown for 3,150 yards and 24 touchdowns, both marks that rank eighth in program history. Fifita has strung together five consecutive 300-yard passing performances and has led the Wildcats to seven wins for the third time in his career.
The Davey O'Brien Foundation will announce the three finalists Tuesday, Nov. 24. The 2026 Davey O'Brien Award winner will be unveiled live on ESPN on Friday, Dec. 11, during The Home Depot College Football Awards.
Fifita and the Wildcats are back in action, this Saturday, Nov. 21 in a road test against the Kansas State Wildcats. Kickoff is slated for 5:00 p.m. MST with a national television broadcast available on FS1.
