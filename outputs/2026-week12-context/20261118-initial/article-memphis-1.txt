Montgomery, Stokes Collect American Weekly Honorable Mentions
Nov 16, 2026
|
Football
Share:
IRVING, Texas –
University of Memphis linebacker
Mike Montgomery
and quarterback
Marcus Stokes
were named to the American's list of weekly award honorable mentions, the conference announced Monday morning.
Montgomery, a senior transfer from Southern Miss, set a new career high in tackles in Memphis' 52-41 win over South Florida with 19. His 19 tackles are the third-most by a player in an FBS game this season and mark the 11
th
time that a player in American Conference history logged 19-plus tackles.
On the year, Montgomery has four games with at least 10 tackles, leads the Tigers in total tackles (86) by 32 and is tied for 24
th
in the FBS in solo tackles (44).
Stokes was named an honorable mention for the third time this season after recording his fourth-straight game with at least 300 passing yards and third-straight with at least three passing touchdowns and five total touchdowns. Against the Bulls last Thursday, Stokes also logged his second-multi rushing touchdown game of the season, including a 47-yard score on Memphis' opening drive.
Later in the game, one of Stokes' three passing touchdowns went for 76 yards to
Jemyri Davis
. By the end of the game, Stokes logged his 11
th
completion of at least 45 yards this season after finding
Aidan O'Callaghan
for 53 yards.
UP NEXT
Memphis (8-2, 5-1 American) heads to Annapolis on Saturday, Nov. 21 for its 12
th
meeting in a row with Navy (3-6, 2-4 American) at 11 a.m. CT, with the game airing on ESPN2.
