Post-Game Notes: Washington
11/14/2026 11:59:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
GAME/SERIES NOTES
• Saturday's game time was pushed back to 5:30 p.m. from the originally scheduled 4:15 p.m. kick due to a delay in Washington's travel, caused by a nationwide ground stop on Friday.
• Saturday was Michigan State's Military Appreciation Day, and the Spartans wore a patriotic Sparty sticker on their helmets, with the Sparty mascot and an American flag decal . . . the end zones in Spartan Stadium were painted with stars & stripes in the "Spartans" . . . redshirt senior cornerback
NiJhay Burt
, the reigning Big Ten Defensive Player of the Week, led the team out carrying the American flag . . . a military flyover was planned for the originally scheduled start time, but with the altered time change, there was no flyover because of darkness.
• Michigan State played its first home game in nearly a month, with MSU playing at Spartan Stadium for the first time since Oct. 17 against Northwestern . . . the Spartans are now 2-4 at home this season.
• Saturday's game marked the sixth meeting between Michigan State and Washington . . . the Huskies now own a 5-1 series advantage . . . this marked the first-ever matchup between the two schools as Big Ten members.
• MSU is now 1-1 in the month of November this season after opening the third month of the schedule and the 11th month of the calendar with a 15-6 win at Michigan in the battle for the Paul Bunyan Trophy.
OFFENSE
• Michigan State scored a TD on its opening possession on Saturday with graduate tight end
Carson Gulker's
1-yard TD plunge from the Wildcat formation . . . it was the second time that MSU has scored on its opening drive, also doing so in the season opener vs. Toledo.
• Gulker's TD run snapped a streak of graduate placekicker
Liam Boyd
scoring MSU's last six scores, making a field goal in the fourth quarter of the game at UCLA on Oct. 24, then doing all of the Spartans' scoring in the win at Michigan with five field goals in the 15-6 Victory for MSU.
• Redshirt sophomore quarterback
Alessio Milivojevic
was 7-of-12 for 108 yards passing, while rushing for 5 yards on two attempts, before leaving the game with an injury in the second quarter with an injury and did not return.
• Graduate quarterback
Cam Fancher
came on in place of Milivojevic and was 14-of-22 for 134 yards, all season highs.
• Fancher also threw his first TD pass as a Spartan and his first since 2024 when he was at FAU . . . Fancher hit redshirt freshman wide receiver
Bryson Williams
with a 2-yard TD toss . . . Fancher's last TD came on Nov. 1, 2024, vs. USF.
• Fancher is now the third different MSU QB with a TD pass this season, joining Milivojevic (7, last vs. Northwestern, 10/17), and freshman QB Cayd Coffman (1, vs. UCLA, 10/24).
• Overall, it was Fancher's 28th career TD pass . . . Fancher is now tied for No. 28 in TD passes among active FBS quarterbacks . . . he is also tied for No. 45 among active FBS QBs in passing (5,820 yards).
• Graduate tight end
Carson Gulker
also scored his first as a Spartan . . . Gulker finished with three carries for 7 yards, adding three catches for 33 yards.
• Gulker now has 51 career rushing TDs, ranking tied for No. 5 among all active players in the NCAA, and Gulker is currently tied with Hunter Patterson of West Liberty, and Gulker is the only player in the Top 10 that is at a Power 4 team.
• Redshirt freshman wide receiver
Bryson Williams
snared his second TD catch of the season, with his first career TD reception coming vs. Northwestern (10/17)  . . . he is now tied with redshirt junior wide receiver Fred Moore for the team lead with two TDs each . . . Williams also had MSU's longest offensive play of the day with a 30-yard catch from Milivojevic on the Spartans' opening possession.
• Junior running back
Brandon Tullis
registered 20+ carries for 80+ yards for the second-straight game with game highs of 21 rushing attempts for 82 yards . . . he had a career-high 25 carries for 86 yards last week at Michigan . . . it is not just the first time in his MSU career that Tullis has had back-to-back games with 20+ carries, but it is also the first time he's had double-digit carries in consecutive games . . . Tullis added three carries for 23 yards Saturday, giving him receptions in back-to-back games after three catches last week.
DEFENSE
• MSU's defense/special teams registered at least one takeaway for the third game in a row, with freshman linebacker
Adam Shaw
recovering a muffed punt return early in the fourth quarter on Saturday . . . MSU has now recovered at least one takeaway in six of the 10 games.
• Senior linebacker
Dion Crawford
led MSU with six stops, marking the sixth time this season he's posted five or more tackles . . . Crawford also had at least 0.5 tackle for loss for the fourth-straight game, marking his longest streak as a Spartan and tying the longest streak of his career, with four games at Buffalo in 2024.
• Graduate safety
Nikai Martinez
registered five tackles, marking the eighth time out of the 10 games that he has registered four or more stops, as part of having five or more tackles for the fifth-consecutive game, posting five or more tackles six times total.
• Graduate edge player
Kenny Soares Jr.
logged his second sack of the season . . . he now has at least 0.5 TFL in four games with 4.0 total TFLs and 2.0 sacks this year . . . Soares now had 13.5 career TFLs with 5.0 career sacks.
• Redshirt junior defensive lineman
Eli Coenen
had a career-high two pass break-ups, deflecting a pair of passes at the line of scrimmage . . . the two PBUs on Saturday doubled Coenen's season total for pass break-ups, as he entered with two on the season, with one in his Spartan debut in the season-opener vs. Toledo (9/4) and one at Wisconsin (10/3).
• Coenen now has four pass break-ups on the season, tying
Cam Stodghill
for the team lead . . . additionally, Coenen now has five PBUs for his career, entering the season with just one in his previous three seasons, spanning 28 games, and now has four in 10 games this season.
SPECIAL TEAMS
• One game after a career-high tying five field goals and accounting for all of the Spartans' scoring with five field goals in the 15-6 win over Michigan, graduate placekicker
Liam Boyd
was 2-of-2 on PATs and missed a 50-yard attempt in the fourth quarter . . . it was Boyd's first miss that cleared the line of scrimmage as his previous three misses only missed by being blocked, including three of 50+ yards.
• Boyd is now 16-of-20 on field goals this season, with at least one field goal in seven of the 10 games . . . he is also 18-of-18 on PATs.
• Junior punter
Rhys Dakin
had five punts for a 39.6 ypp average, pinning three inside the 20, with a long of 49 yards, and a net return of 0 yards.
• Dakin started his night with a 36-yarder that was fair caught at the WASH 14, before a 28-yarder that went out-of-bounds in the second quarter, then a 42-yarder that was downed at the WASH 10 in the third quarter . . . in the fourth quarter, Dakin had a 43-yarder that was muffed by the UW returner at the WASH 34 and recovered by MSU at the 33 . . . he wrapped up his night with a 49-yarder that was fair caught at the WASH 22.
• With a long of 49 yards, Dakin only had his second outing this season without at least one 50-yard punt, with only one punt for 44 yards in the season-opener vs. Toledo (9/4) in his Spartan debut.
• Saturday's three punts inside the 20 give Dakin 15 this season and 62 for his career.
