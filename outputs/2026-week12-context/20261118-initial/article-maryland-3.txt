Game Notes
Share:
Maryland (4-6, 2-5 B1G)
vs. #18
USC (7-3, 4-3 B1G)
November 21, 2026
LA Memorial Coliseum
6:15 PM
BTN
COLLEGE PARK, MD --
M
aryland will visit No. 18 USC for the first time in program history on Saturday. In the teams' only previous meeting in 2024, the Terps rallied from a 14-point fourth-quarter deficit to defeat the Trojans, 29-28, in College Park.
the opening kick
Maryland is the only Big Ten school that has forced a takeaway in each of its conference games this season. The Terrapins have forced a takeaway in 15 of their 16 conference games since the beginning of last season.
Maryland has won each of its last two games by five or fewer points. It is the Terps' first time winning consecutive games by such a margin since a three-game run in 2006.
The Terps have three comeback wins of at least seven points this season, including each of their last two games. That is Maryland's most in a season since 2014 (also three).
Cardell Williams
is one of just five FBS players since 2011 to complete at least 90% of his passes and rush for two touchdowns in a single game. He is the only Big Ten player to accomplish the feat in the last 30 seasons.
Preston Howard
caught his first touchdown of the season with a 39-yard grab on Saturday to tie the game. That was the third-longest receiving touchdown by a Maryland TE over the last 15 seasons, behind Corey Dyches (68-yarder in 2022 and 44-yarder in 2023).
Zion Elee
leads all freshmen nationally and ranks fourth overall in the Big Ten with six sacks. He also is the only freshman in the country to have forced three fumbles this season.
Daniel Wingate
is one of only three active Big Ten players with multiple career pick-sixes, joining Michigan State's Charles Brantley and Indiana's Rolijah Hardy.
Sean O'Haire
delivered his first career game-winning kick with a 30-yard field goal with 29 seconds remaining, lifting Maryland to a 24-21 win over Wisconsin. His performance earned him Big Ten Special Team Player of the Week honors.
O'Haire is one of only three active Division I players with a perfect career extra-point percentage and a career field goal percentage of at least 87% at the FBS/FCS level.
Jamare Glasker
ranks second among Power Four players with 11 forced incompletions this season.
durr-ability
Wide receiver
Chris Durr Jr.
ranks third in the Big Ten and ninth nationally in receptions per game (7.00). His 70 receptions are third in the conference and rank ninth nationally.
Durr Jr.'s 70 receptions through 10 games are the second-most by a Maryland player since 1996, trailing only Tai Felton's 86 catches through 10 games in 2024. His 70 catches also rank fifth in Maryland single-season history.
Durr Jr.'s five 100-yard receiving games this season are tied for second in the Big Ten and third in the FBS, matching the school record shared by Tai Felton (2024) and Marcus Badgett (1992).
Durr Jr. recorded six catches for 104 yards against Rutgers and is one of six FBS players with at least four games of six or more receptions and 100 or more receiving yards this season.
Chris Durr Jr.
leads the Big Ten and ranks third nationally among FBS players with 565 yards after the catch.
dejuan delivers
Despite missing three games this season,
DeJuan Williams
leads the team with 509 rushing yards and five rushing touchdowns.
Williams rushed for 72 yards on a career-high 22 carries, surpassing 1,000 career rushing yards in a 24-21 win over Wisconsin.
Williams is the first Maryland player with multiple rushing touchdowns of 75 or more yards in the same season since Anthony McFarland Jr. in 2018 (two). The last Terps player with more than two in a single season was Brandon Ross in 2015 (three).
His 57 career receptions are the second-most by a Maryland running back since 2000, trailing only Roman Hemby (112).
Williams is one of only eight FBS RBs to have at least 55 catches since the start of last season.
Against Hampton, he became the first Big Ten player to have a 100-yard rushing game on three or fewer rushing attempts since Penn State's Chafie Fields vs. Arizona (8/28/1999).
DeJuan Williams
is one of five FBS players with multiple 75+ yard rushing touchdowns this season.
cardell steps in
Cardell Williams
led Maryland to a 24-21 comeback win over Wisconsin in his first start as a Terp, completing 21-of-23 passes for 183 yards and accounting for all three touchdowns (one passing, two rushing) and earned Manning Award Stars of the Week honors for his performance.
Williams set a school record with a 91.3% completion percentage, the second highest by a Big Ten quarterback this season and fourth highest among all FBS quarterbacks
His 91.3% completion rate ranks second among players making their first Big Ten start since 1996 (minimum 20 attempts), trailing only Wisconsin's Graham Mertz (95.2% in 2020)
Williams completed his final 19 passes, setting a school record for consecutive completions—the most by a Big Ten quarterback this season and tied for third most by any FBS quarterback in a game this season.
He capped his record-setting completion streak with a game-tying, 39-yard touchdown pass to
Preston Howard
with 2:11 remaining, helping Maryland secure its second consecutive victory.

wingate watch
Daniel Wingate
leads the team in tackles with 63.
Wingate tied for the team lead with seven tackles, forced a fumble and returned a fumble recovery 16 yards against Wisconsin.
Wingate recorded 10 tackles, including one tackle for loss, and returned an interception 52 yards for a touchdown against No. 5 Ohio State (10/10).
Wingate is one of only three active Big Ten players with multiple career pick-sixes, joining Michigan State's Charles Brantley and Indiana's Rolijah Hardy.
His two career interception returns for touchdowns tie Maryland's program record.
Wingate was named to the 2026 Butkus Award, Bronko Nagurski Trophy and Chuck Bednarik Award watch lists before the season.

defense gets home
Maryland's defense limited Wisconsin to just 201 total yards, including 99 passing yards, in its 24-21 victory. The 201 yards were the third-fewest allowed by the Terps in a conference game since 2014.
Maryland forced three turnovers and had four sacks against Wisconsin. That was the first time the Terps did that against an FBS opponent since 2022 versus Purdue.
Jamare Glasker
recorded four tackles, including three for loss and a sack, and added two pass breakups.
Lavon Johnson
recorded four tackles, a sack, a fumble recovery and a quarterback hurry.
Maryland is the only Big Ten school that has forced a takeaway in each of its conference games this season.
