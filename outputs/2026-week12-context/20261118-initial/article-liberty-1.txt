Engbers Named CUSA Offensive Lineman of the Week
11/16/2026 12:55:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
Following the team's best rushing performance of the season, Liberty's
Joep Engbers
has been named the CUSA Offensive Lineman of the Week.
The award marks the fourth time this season a Liberty player has been named the CUSA Offensive Lineman of the Week.
Left tackle
D.J. Geth
took home the honors on Sept. 28 (after the Coastal Carolina game) and Oct. 12 (after the Sam Houston game), while right guard
Alex Birchmeier
won the award on Oct. 5 (after the Delaware game).
Engbers, the Flames' starting left guard, blocked for Liberty's rushing attack that finished the MTSU game with a season-high 370 rushing yards, last Saturday.
Engbers played 71 of Liberty's 72 offensive snaps against the Blue Raiders and finished the game with an overall 83.7 PFF.com Grade for Offense. This marked Engbers' top PFF.com grade for the 2026 season.
The native of Losser, Netherlands, was the top ranked CUSA offensive lineman by PFF.com during Week 11.
Engbers had the fourth best PFF.com grade by all FBS offensive linemen in the country during Week 11. He also had the second-best Group of Six PFF.com player grade by an offensive lineman in the country during Week 11 (top grade was Central Michigan's Martin Koivisto)
With the Flames' victory over MTSU, Liberty moved to 9-1 on the season and gained sole possession of the top spot in the CUSA standings with a 6-0 record.
The victory over MTSU also allowed the Flames to secure a spot in the 2026 CUSA Football Championship game. The game will be contested at the site of the CUSA regular season champion on Friday, December 4. Kickoff will be at 6 p.m. ET and the game will be broadcast on the CBS Sports Network.
What's Next:
The Flames will close out the regular season portion of their 2026 home schedule when they welcome WKU to Williams Stadium on Saturday, November 21.
Prior to kickoff, Liberty will honor its 2026 senior class, as they potentially play the final home game of their career on the Mountain.
Liberty holds a perfect 5-0 record against WKU, including wins in 2023 (at WKU/42-29) and in 2024 (at Liberty/38-21) since the Flames have joined Conference USA.
