Gophers Fall at Penn State; Host Northwestern for Senior Day
11/14/2026 9:40:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Postgame Notes
Photo Gallery
Share:
STATE COLLEGE, Pa. (AP) — Rocco Becht threw three touchdown passes and ran for another score and Penn State pulled away from Minnesota 42-14 on Saturday.
Carson Hansen ran for 126 yards and a touchdown, Ben Brahmer caught two touchdown passes and Penn State's defense suffocated Minnesota early under a swirling mix of mist and flurries to become bowl eligible at Beaver Stadium.
The Nittany Lions (6-4, 3-4 Big Ten) got their third-straight win in league play thanks to a bruising ground attack that set the tone early and churned 173 yards of its 229 rushing yards in the first half.
Penn State led 28-0 at halftime after the Golden Gophers (5-5, 3-4 Big Ten) gave the Nittany Lions short fields to mount easy scoring drives.
Becht opened the scoring with a 1-yard plunge into the end zone five plays after Minnesota quarterback
Drake Lindsey
fumbled a snap at midfield on Minnesota's first possession.
Penn State only had 33 yards to go after Tyrell Chatman blocked the Gophers' first punt. The Nittany Lions went up 14-0 moments later when Brahmer pulled down a 19-yard touchdown pass between defenders.
A 19-yard punt return from Zay Robinson early in the second quarter set Becht and the offense up at midfield again. Hansen burst through Minnesota's defense for a 46-yard touchdown and the rout was on.
The Gophers failed to cross midfield again in the half and were flagged for fair catch interference on their final punt before halftime. The 15-yard penalty gave Penn State the ball at Minnesota's 36 and Taylor Mouser's offense needed just six plays to go up 28-0 on a rollout pass to Cam Wallace.
Brahmer's second touchdown catch opened the second half before
Javon Tracy
caught a pair of third-quarter touchdown passes for the Gophers who lost their third straight.
They couldn't pull any closer, and Penn State backup quarterback Alex Manske, who had open-heart surgery in March, tossed his first career touchdown pass to James Peoples to cap the scoring.
Up Next
Minnesota: Hosts Northwestern on Saturday.
Penn State: Hosts Rutgers on Saturday.
