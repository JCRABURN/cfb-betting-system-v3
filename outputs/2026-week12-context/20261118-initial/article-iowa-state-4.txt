Cyclones in the NFL: Week 10 Recap
11.18.2026
|
Football
Share:
Share:
As the NFL season hits the backstretch, three Cyclones (Brock Purdy,
Domonique Orange
and Enyi Uwazurike) all seem primed for a postseason run. Meanwhile, Charlie Kolar and the Chargers got on the board as other Cyclones impressed.
Week 11 of the season will see four games that feature Cyclones on both sides of the field. The Vikings take on San Francisco, Houston will host Indianapolis, the Jets travel to face the Chargers and the Broncos host the Raiders.
Brock Purdy – QB, #13, San Francisco 49ers (8-1)
Purdy and the 49ers exploded on offense Sunday afternoon, scoring a season-high 46 points. His 389 passing yards were a career high and marked the first time he eclipsed 300 on the season. It was the third time this season the Purdy slung at least three touchdowns.
Last Time Out
: W at DAL, 46-40 // 22-28 for 389 yards and 3 TD, 2 rushes for 21 yards
Up Next
: Nov. 22, vs. Minnesota (7:20 pm on NBC – game played in Mexico City)
Xavier Hutchinson – WR, #19, Houston Texans (3-6)
Hutchinson has now caught a receiving touchdown in three consecutive outings, as he continues to be one of C.J. Stroud's most trusted wideouts. His 58 receiving yards were his second-most in a game this season, as he averaged 14.5 yards per reception. It was the first game since week five that he did not have a kickoff return.
Last Time Out
: L at CLE, 24-27 // 4 catches for 58 yards and 1 TD
Up Next
: Nov. 19, vs. Indianapolis (7:15 pm on Prime Video)
Breece Hall – RB, #20, New York Jets (2-8)
Hall totaled over 100 yards from scrimmage on Sunday for the second time this season and first time since returning from injury. He was explosive in the passing game, averaging 16.8 yards per reception in the loss to AFC East-leading the Bills.
Last Time Out
: L vs. BUF, 12-31 // 11 rushes for 41 yards, 4 catches for 67 yards
Up Next
: Nov. 22, at LAC (3:05 pm on FOX)
Charlie Kolar – TE, #88, Los Angeles Chargers (1-8)
In his return to Baltimore, Kolar helped ice the game away with a late first-down conversion to give the Chargers their first win of the season. Kolar graded out as the highest offensive Charger (75.1) according to PFF.
Last Time Out
: W at BAL, 23-20 // 2 catches for 16 yards
Up Next
: Nov. 22, vs. NYJ (3:05 pm on FOX)
T.J. Tampa – CB, #27, Baltimore Ravens (4-6)
Tampa primarily played on special teams for the Ravens on Monday night, playing 26 of his 29 snaps there. He finished the night with one tackle and has made a tackle in each game he's appeared in this season.
Last Time Out
: L vs. LAC, 20-23 // 1 solo tackle, 29 total snaps
Up Next
: Nov. 22, at Carolina (12 pm on FOX)
Will McDonald IV – EDGE, #9, New York Jets (2-8)
McDonald recorded a sack for the second time in his last three outings, running his season total to four. He played over half the team's defensive snaps, earning a pair of QB Hits to go along with his tackle for loss.
Last Time Out
: L vs. BUF, 12-31 // 1 sack, 2 QB Hits, 34 total snaps
Up Next
: Nov. 22, at LAC (3:05 pm on FOX)
Darien Porter – DB, #26, Las Vegas Raiders (4-6)
Porter spent the second half as the team's No. 2 cornerback after sitting out the first half. During his time on the field, Porter allowed just one reception for 16 yards.
Last Time Out
: L vs. SEA, 13-21 // 30 defensive snaps
Up Next
: Nov. 22, at Denver (3:25 pm on CBS)
Domonique Orange
– DT, #97, Minnesota Vikings (7-2)
Orange saw his second-lowest snap share of the season in Sunday's win over Green Bay, only playing ten total snaps. Of his seven defensive snaps, he was in on two stops. Orange and Viking defense recorded eight sacks of Jordan Love.
Last Time Out
: W at GB, 30-10 // 2 tackles (1 solo), 10 total snaps
Up Next
: Nov. 22, vs. San Francisco (7:20 pm on NBC – game played in Mexico City)
Kene Nwangwu – RB, #3, New York Jets (2-8)
Nwangwu only saw two opportunities to return kicks on Sunday. He averaged 27 yards per return.
Last Time Out
: L vs. BUF, 12-31 // 2 kickoff returns for 54 yards, 6 special teams snaps
Up Next
: Nov. 22, at LAC (3:05 pm on FOX)
Jake Hummel – LB, #33, Houston Texans (3-6)
For the first time since week one, Hummel took the field with the Texans. The fifth-year pro had missed the last seven contests with a groin injury before returning against the Colts. Hummel returned to his valued special teams role, making a pair of tackles.
Last Time Out
: L at CLE, 24-27 // 2 tackles (1 solo), 21 special teams snaps
Up Next
: Nov. 19, vs. Indianapolis (7:15 pm on Prime Video)
David Montgomery – RB, #32, Houston Texans (3-6)
Montgomery missed his fourth straight game with an injury.
Last Time Out
: L at CLE, 24-27 // OUT (groin)
Up Next
: Nov. 19, vs. Indianapolis (7:15 pm on Prime Video)
Jalen Travis – OL, #75, Indianapolis Colts (3-7)
Travis missed last week's game with a knee injury.
Last Time Out
: W vs. MIA, 24-22 // OUT (knee)
Up Next
: Nov. 19, at Houston (7:15 pm on Prime Video)
Enyi Uwazurike – DL, #96, Denver Broncos (7-2)
Last Time Out
: BYE
Up Next
: Nov. 22, vs. Las Vegas (3:25 pm on CBS)
