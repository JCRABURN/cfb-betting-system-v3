Travel issues for Tulane delays kickoff
By:
Chuck Pool (cpool@rice.edu)
Houston-
-As a result of travel issues due to the national ground stop of air travel, Rice's Homecoming matchup with Tulane has been pushed back to a 3:30 kickoff at First Community Stadium.
The game had been slated for a 1 p.m. start, but the Green Wave were forced to charter buses to travel to Houston after their charter flight was grounded as part of the national shutdown of air travel and were not scheduled to arrive in Houston until after midnight.
This is the 39
th
meeting between the schools on the gridiron.  Rice leads the overall series 20-17-1, but the Green Wave has won the last two meetings (30-28 in 2023 and 24-10 in 2024).  Rice's last win in the series was a 17-13 verdict in 2013 that wrapped up the C-USA Western Division title and set up a meeting with Marshall in Houston for the conference championship game.
This will mark the third time the Green Wave has provided opposition for the Owls on Homecoming, with Rice winning both previous games.   Rice's 42-34 win over Tulane in 2005 provided the Owls their first Conference USA win and snapped a 14-game losing streak for Rice.    In 2009, the winless Owls outscored Tulane 21-6 in the second half to post a 28-20 win.
