Rice hosts Army for 2026 finale
By:
Chuck Pool (cpool@rice.edu)
Houston-
Rice will close out the 2026 football season with a 1 p.m. kickoff against Army on Saturday, November 28, with coverage on ESPN+.  The start time and broadcast assignment were jointly announced by the American Conference and the network on Monday.
This will be the 11
th
meeting in the series and the first in Houston since 2017, when Army defeated Rice 49-12.  The Black Knights have won the last four meetings to take a 5-4-1 lead in the series that began in 1958 when eventual Heisman Trophy winner Pete Dawkins stunned the home crowd with a 64-yard touchdown reception with less than a minute remaining to give third-ranked Army a 14-7 victory.
Rice will honor its senior class prior to the start of the home finale. Rice will also honor members of the military as part of Salute to Service Day.
Single-game and group tickets for the final game of the 2026 season are currently on sale through the
Rice Athletics Ticket Office.
