Chanticleers Host Arkansas State In Home Finale
11/13/2026 12:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
CONWAY, S.C. -
Coastal Carolina closes its home schedule Saturday afternoon when the Chanticleers host Arkansas State for Senior Day and Salute to Service at Brooks Stadium. Kickoff is scheduled for 1 p.m. ET, with the Sun Belt Conference matchup streaming on ESPN+.
Coastal enters the game at 3-6 overall and 2-3 in conference play following a 21-8 loss to Old Dominion. Arkansas State stands at 4-5 overall and 2-3 in the Sun Belt after a 19-17 loss to ULM. The Chanticleers will look to earn their fourth consecutive victory over the Red Wolves and close their home slate with a win.

Streaks, Storylines, Sidebars
HONORING THE SENIORS:
Saturday marks Coastal's final home game and annual Senior Day and Salute to Service contest. Twenty-three Chanticleers are expected to be recognized before kickoff.
THE SERIES:
Coastal Carolina and Arkansas State will meet for the seventh time, with the series tied 3-3. The teams have split their two previous meetings in Conway.
THREE STRAIGHT:
The Chanticleers have won three consecutive meetings against the Red Wolves, including a 52-23 victory in the most recent matchup at Brooks Stadium in 2020 and a 27-17 road win in 2023.
BOWL PUSH:
Coastal needs to win its final three regular-season games to reach six victories and bowl eligibility. The Chanticleers are seeking a seventh consecutive bowl appearance.
PULLIAM'S RECORD DAY:
Marcellius Pulliam
set Coastal's single-game record with 22 tackles against Old Dominion, the most by an FBS player this season. He surpassed the previous program record of 20 shared by Dominique Davenport and Quinn Backus.
RAISING THE BAR:
Pulliam leads the Sun Belt and ranks fourth nationally with 97 tackles. He needs three more to reach 100 and 13 to match Silas Kelly's Coastal FBS-era single-season record of 110.
BAILEY CLIMBING:
Deuce Bailey
ranks 10th on Coastal's single-season lists with 2,178 passing yards and 19 touchdown passes. His next touchdown pass would tie Alex Ross and Zach MacDowall for eighth with 20.
WAYNE'S WORLD:
Ja'Marion Wayne
leads Coastal with three interceptions and eight pass breakups. He intercepted a pass against Old Dominion and returned it 35 yards, Coastal's longest interception return of the season.
RED-ZONE RESISTANCE:
Coastal leads the Sun Belt in red-zone defense, allowing opponents to score on 24 of 34 possessions. The Chanticleers have kept opponents off the scoreboard on 10 trips inside the 20-yard line.
A FAMILIAR OPPONENT:
Arkansas State closed last season against the Missouri State program Beard guided to its first bowl appearance. The Red Wolves defeated the Bears 34-28 in the Xbox Bowl after Beard accepted the Coastal position.
SCOUTING THE RED WOLVES:
Quarterback Trey Owens leads the Sun Belt with 267.8 yards of total offense per game and has thrown for 2,133 yards and 12 touchdowns. Jaylen Bonelli leads Arkansas State with 518 receiving yards, while Joe Drew has a team-high 83 tackles.
Up Next
Coastal Carolina begins a two-game road stretch to close the regular season at Louisiana on Saturday, Nov. 21. Kickoff is scheduled for 3 p.m. ET at Cajun Field, with the game streaming on ESPN+.
