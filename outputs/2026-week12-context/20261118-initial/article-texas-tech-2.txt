Baylor Game Notes
Texas Tech Media Center
LUBBOCK, Texas —
No. 12 Texas Tech (9-1, 6-1 Big 12) can secure its second straight 10-win season and keep its Big 12 Championship Game hopes alive Saturday when it travels to Baylor (6-4, 4-3 Big 12) for its final regular-season road game, a 2:30 p.m. CT kickoff. Coming off its third top-25 win of the season last weekend at No. 25 Oklahoma State, the Red Raiders would record the eighth 10-win season in school history with a victory. Texas Tech can clinch a spot in the championship game in Arlington with a win at Baylor if BYU falls at Kansas, or by winning its final two games, first against the Bears and then on Thanksgiving against TCU.
The television broadcast will feature Jeff Kizer on play-by-play and analyst Mark Helfrich in the booth. Fans can also stream the game on their mobile devices or televisions through the FOX Sports app.
Texas Tech Sports Network will broadcast the game across more than 20 affiliates throughout Texas and New Mexico, with Brian Jensen providing the call alongside analyst John Harris and sideline reporter Chris Level. The radio broadcast can also be heard on SiriusXM channel 83 or through the Varsity app by searching "Texas Tech."
ONE REGULAR-SEASON HOME GAME REMAINS, GET TICKETS WHILE YOU CAN
Texas Tech hosts TCU at 7 p.m. CT on Thanksgiving Day, closing out its seven-game regular-season home schedule. A limited number of single-game tickets remain. Single-game tickets can be purchased online at
TexasTech.com
or by contacting a ticket sales representative at 806-742-TECH.
All six of the Red Raiders' previous home games this season were advance sellouts. In total, Texas Tech has had 13 consecutive home games sell out dating back to the start of the 2025 season.
Fans with tickets who are unable to attend the season finale should utilize the
Every Seat Counts
program, which is designed to ensure Galaxy Stadium is at full capacity each game day.
On May 11, Texas Tech Athletics announced it had sold out its season ticket allotment for the 2026 football season, marking the fourth consecutive year Red Raider football season tickets have sold out. Due to significant demand following the program's continued rise, Texas Tech increased its season ticket allotment for 2026. Red Raider fans purchased more than 33,400 season tickets, while the season-ticket renewal rate reached 95 percent, leaving only limited availability for the wait list.
THIS WEEK IN THE POLLS
College Football Playoff (No. 12):
Texas Tech was revealed as the No. 12 team in the third CFP rankings, released on Nov. 17. Its second straight week at No. 12.
Associated Press (No. 11):
Despite defeating CFP No. 25 Oklahoma State on the road, Texas Tech dropped one spot to No. 11 in the AP poll. Wake Forest moved from No. 11 to No. 10 after defeating SMU on the road.
AFCA Coaches Poll (No. 11):
The Red Raiders also dropped one spot to No. 11 in the U.S. LBM Coaches Poll.
This marks the 29th consecutive week the Red Raiders have appeared in the AP poll, the longest streak in program history.
Baylor enters the weekend unranked in all three polls.
RED RAIDERS DOWN ANOTHER RANKED FOE
Texas Tech delivered its third win over a ranked opponent last weekend after downing No. 25 Oklahoma State, 52-27, in Stillwater. The Red Raiders previously knocked off Houston, 28-26, to open Big 12 play, with the Cougars ranked No. 14 in the previous week's CFP rankings. Arizona, which was No. 23 in the CFP rankings last week, represented Tech's second top-25 win this season only a few weeks ago in a 34-26 victory.
Texas Tech and Utah are the Big 12's top teams when it comes to facing ranked opponents. In fact, the Red Raiders and Utes are two of seven FBS teams with three ranked wins this season. Texas Tech joins only Texas, Utah and Georgia as teams with three or more top-25 wins and no losses to ranked opponents.
Texas Tech's win over Arizona marked the 10th victory over a top-25 team already for the Red Raiders under
Joey McGuire
, who is 10-8 in those games overall. It is a significant improvement for a program that combined for only five such wins in the 10 seasons prior to his arrival. Texas Tech is tied with Utah with 10 wins over top-25 teams since the start of the 2022 season, which also ranks tied for 12th nationally. McGuire is the lone active Big 12 head coach (in at least his second year) to boast a winning career record against ranked opponents.
Texas Tech is now 6-1 against ranked opponents since the start of the 2025 season. That ranks as the second-highest winning percentage during that span, trailing only Indiana (7-1). The Red Raiders lead all Big 12 programs with six top-25 wins the past two seasons.
CASTELLANOS SPARKS RED RAIDER OFFENSE
The Red Raiders have ranked among the nation's top offenses since
Thomas Castellanos
took over at quarterback three weeks ago. Since then, the Red Raiders are 3-0 and averaging 46.3 points and 517.3 yards of total offense per game behind a balanced attack that has Texas Tech averaging 258.0 passing yards and 259.3 rushing yards over that three-game span.
The Red Raiders are coming off a 52-27 rout over No. 25 Oklahoma State, marking their third game with at least 50 points against a conference opponent, the most in the FBS
RED RAIDERS AMONG NATION'S SACKS LEADERS
Texas Tech is once again among the nation's leaders in sacks as the Red Raiders are tied for fifth in the FBS with 32.0 sacks, three more than their total at this point a year ago behind a defense that featured multiple NFL selections.
Texas Tech has combined for 21.0 sacks the past five weeks, which matches its highest total over a five-game stretch since 1996.
The Red Raiders, who share the FBS lead with 21.0 sacks over that five-game span, are nine sacks shy of last season's total that was the second highest in program history.
INSIDE THE BAYLOR SERIES
After a one-year absence, Texas Tech and Baylor will meet for the 84th time in history Saturday in a back-and-forth series that the Bears narrowly lead 42-40-1 overall. The two former Southwest Conference rivals had met annually since 1956 until a one-year break last season as part of the Big 12's four-year scheduling matrix. Baylor represents Texas Tech's most-played opponent in its 100-plus year history of football.
Baylor controlled a 19-17 advantage over the Red Raiders during their SWC tenure but immediately lost its next 15 games in the series upon entering the Big 12 in 1996. The Red Raiders are 19-10 against the Bears since the two schools joined the Big 12 as charter members.
The two schools returned the series back to campus sites in 2019 after playing the previous 10 meetings in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex (2009-18). The Red Raiders were only 4-6 against the Bears in DFW during that span with all but one game taking place at AT&T Stadium in Arlington. The two schools hosted the 2010 edition of the game at Cotton Bowl Stadium in Dallas.
Few teams have been able to come into Galaxy Stadium and defeat the Red Raiders under
Joey McGuire
, who has led Texas Tech to a 28-5 record at home since 2022. Baylor has delivered two of those losses, shocking the Red Raiders with a 45-17 loss in 2022 and then again in a 59-35 shootout in 2024. The Red Raiders had won 10-consecutive games against the Bears in Lubbock until Baylor spoiled the Ring of Honor ceremony for Patrick Mahomes II in its 2024 victory. The loss was also the first to Baylor at home since Sept. 29, 1990, when the Bears escaped Lubbock with a 21-15 victory early in that season.
Texas Tech pounded the Bears on the ground in their last visit to Waco as Tahj Brooks rushed for 170 yards and a touchdown for a 39-14 victory over the Bears in 2023. The Red Raiders took advantage of six sacks defensively in the win, while limiting Baylor to only 17 rushing yards and 1-of-6 on fourth downs.
Texas Tech has thrown for at least 300 yards in 16 of the 25 meetings since the Red Raiders moved to a spread offense first under Mike Leach in 2000, which includes a pair of 500-yard outings from Patrick Mahomes II during his career.
A CLOSER LOOK AT THE LAST MATCHUP IN 2024
Baylor, which entered the game with a 2-4 overall record and a 0-3 mark in Big 12 play, stunned Texas Tech, 59-35, on Oct. 19, 2024, at Jones AT&T Stadium. The Red Raiders entered at 5-1 overall and 3-0 in conference play, riding a four-game winning streak and sitting atop the Big 12 standings.
Texas Tech trailed 24-14 at halftime but opened the second half with a touchdown to cut Baylor's lead to 24-21. The Bears responded with 28 unanswered points to build a 52-21 lead early in the fourth quarter. Texas Tech scored twice in the final seven minutes, with Baylor adding a touchdown between the two scores to finish off the 59-35 victory.
Sawyer Robertson threw for 274 yards and a career-high five touchdowns, completing 21 of 32 passes without an interception in his return to his hometown of Lubbock.
Josh Cameron caught six passes for 75 yards and three touchdowns, while also returning a punt 73 yards to set up another Baylor score.
Bryson Washington rushed for 116 yards and two touchdowns on 10 carries, while
Behren Morton
threw for 286 yards and three touchdowns.
Caleb Douglas
caught nine passes for 99 yards and three touchdowns.
