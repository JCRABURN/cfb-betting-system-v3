Game Week Feature: The Next Generation
Share:
Everything you need plan your Saturday gameday
By:
Kyle Kavanaugh
RUSTON |
One more Saturday at The Joe. One more opportunity to celebrate the people who make Louisiana Tech special.
The Bulldogs return to Origin Bank Field at Joe Aillet Stadium for their final home game of the 2026 season on Saturday, Nov. 21, hosting Arkansas State in a Sun Belt Conference matchup. Kickoff is set for 6 p.m. on ESPN+.
Saturday's game, presented by FMOL Health | St. Francis Medical Center, will feature Senior Day, Education Game, Extra Yard for Teachers and Faculty & Staff Appreciation, recognizing the student-athletes, educators and individuals who invest in the next generation of Bulldogs.
IMPORTANT PARKING NOTICE:
Due to Louisiana Tech's fall commencement ceremonies, stadium parking lots will be closed from
8 a.m. until noon Saturday
. Tailgaters wishing to set up early must arrive before 8 a.m. Parking lots will reopen at noon.
Before heading to campus, fans are encouraged to review the
Louisiana Tech Football Game Day Guide
, the one-stop resource for everything they need to know before they go.
SATURDAY'S GAME
Louisiana Tech vs. Arkansas State
Saturday, Nov. 21 | 6 p.m.
Origin Bank Field at Joe Aillet Stadium
TV: ESPN+
Themes: Senior Day | Education Game | Extra Yard for Teachers | Faculty & Staff Appreciation
College of the Game: College of Education and Human Sciences
Game Sponsor: FMOL Health | St. Francis Medical Center
FIVE THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE YOU GO
Important Parking and Tailgating Update:
Stadium parking lots will open at 7 a.m. for early tailgate setup.
All tailgaters wishing to set up before graduation must arrive prior to 8 a.m.
Lots will be closed from 8 a.m. until noon for commencement activities and will reopen at noon. Fans should also review updated postgame traffic routes.
Senior Day:
Arrive early as Louisiana Tech recognizes its senior football student-athletes during a special pregame ceremony alongside their families and coaches.
Education Game and Extra Yard for Teachers:
Saturday celebrates educators who make a lasting impact on students and their communities, including recognition of Ruston Junior High educators and the development of a dedicated teacher wellness room.
Faculty & Staff Appreciation:
Louisiana Tech faculty and staff can claim two complimentary tickets, with additional guest tickets available for $15 each.
Gameday Experience:
Bulldog Village opens at 3 p.m., followed by the Walk of Pride at 3:45 p.m. Stadium gates open at 4:30 p.m. Fans can enjoy a featured brisket sandwich with baked beans and $2 popcorn at concessions.
CELEBRATING THE NEXT GENERATION
Saturday's game is about more than the final home kickoff of the season.
The day begins with Louisiana Tech's fall commencement at 9 a.m. inside the Thomas Assembly Center, welcoming the university's newest graduates into the Bulldog alumni family.
Later that evening, Louisiana Tech will recognize its football seniors before kickoff, celebrating their commitment, contributions and the people who helped shape their journeys.
As part of the Education Game and Extra Yard for Teachers celebration, Louisiana Tech will also honor educators from Ruston Junior High School and recognize the ongoing development of a dedicated mental health and wellness room for teachers, made possible through a $7,500 grant from the College Football Playoff Foundation in partnership with the Sun Belt Conference.
The College of Education and Human Sciences will be recognized as Saturday's College of the Game.
PLAN AHEAD. ARRIVE EARLY. BARK LOUDER.
With commencement activities impacting stadium parking access Saturday morning, fans are strongly encouraged to plan their arrival accordingly. Tailgaters must arrive before 8 a.m. to set up early or wait until parking lots reopen at noon.
Fans should also review recommended arrival routes, stadium entry procedures and updated postgame traffic patterns before traveling to campus.
With Saturday marking the final home game of the season, Bulldog fans have one more opportunity to fill The Joe, celebrate the seniors and help send the team off with a memorable home-field atmosphere.
One More Saturday. One More Chance to Bark Louder.
