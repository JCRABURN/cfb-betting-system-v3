Pro Bears Week 10: Nohl Williams Intercepts Michael Penix Jr.
11/18/2026 11:00 AM
|

Share:
Jared Goff Leads Lions To Win In Munich
By:
Cal Athletics
Former California football cornerback Nohl Williams and the Kansas City Chiefs secured another win this season as Golden Bears featured across the league during Week 10 of the NFL season. Williams picked off Atlanta Falcons quarterback Michael Penix Jr. and returned it 20 yards in addition to recording five tackles and a pair of pass breakups to help the Chiefs earn a 35-13 victory. Williams is tied for third in the NFL this season with 11 passes defended and his interception against Atlanta was his second of the season.
A couple of former Bears took the field in the NFL's Munich game this past weekend. Detroit Lions quarterback Jared Goff led his team to a victory over the New England Patriots, throwing for 303 yards and three touchdowns. Patriots cornerback Craig Woodson recorded four tackles in the game.
Keenan Allen made his 49ers debut this past weekend in Dallas against the Cowboys. Allen totaled 61 yards on five receptions as San Francisco beat the Cowboys, 46-40.
