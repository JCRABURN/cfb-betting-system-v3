Gilliam Adds to List of Accolades, Named Semifinalist for Outland Trophy
November 18, 2026
|
Football
Share:
KNOXVILLE, Tenn. –
Tennessee standout defensive lineman
Xavier Gilliam
added to his impressive list of accolades on Wednesday when he was named one of seven semifinalists for the 2026 Outland Trophy.
Created in 1946, the Outland Trophy is the third-oldest major college football award and has been presented annually to the best interior lineman in college football.
In his first season at UT, Gilliam has had a breakout campaign and emerged as one of college football's elite defensive players. The junior defensive tackle has been a game wrecker for opposing offenses, leading all SEC interior defensive linemen with 33 pressures, a mark that also ranks tied for sixth in the FBS.
Through 10 games, Gilliam leads the Vols and is fifth in the SEC with 11.5 tackles for loss to go along with six sacks, which also ranks fifth in the conference. His 88.1 PFF grade leads the SEC and ranks third nationally among interior defensive linemen.
The Montgomery Village, Maryland, native, has now been named a semifinalist for four different major awards: the Bednarik Award, the Lombardi Award, the Lott IMPACT Trophy and the Outland Trophy.
The official presentation to the winner will be made at the Outland Trophy Awards Dinner Sponsored by Werner Enterprises produced by the Greater Omaha Sports Committee on Jan. 27, 2027, at the Hilton Omaha Hotel in Omaha.
Tennessee has had two former players win the Outland Trophy in Steve DeLong (1964) and John Henderson (2000).
