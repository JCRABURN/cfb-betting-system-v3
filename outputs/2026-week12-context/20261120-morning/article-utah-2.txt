Dampier Earns Second Straight Big 12 Offensive Player of the Week Honor
11/16/2026 10:59 AM
|
Football
Share:
Senior quarterback tallied four touchdowns in road win at then-No. 23 Arizona
IRVING, Texas
— Utah quarterback
Devon Dampier
has been named Big 12 Offensive Player of the Week for the second consecutive week, the conference announced on Monday.
Dampier ran for two scores and threw for two more, including a game-winning, 70-yard scoring strike with 2:35 to play as No. 2 Utah pulled out a 40-39 victory over No. 23 Arizona in front of 48,858 at Casino Del Sol Stadium last Saturday.
The unbeaten Utes are one of just two remaining undefeated teams in the FBS, sitting at 10-0 for just the third time in school history and first time since the 2008 season.
Through Week 11, Dampier ranks fifth among all active Big 12 quarterbacks with 8,040 career passing yards. Dampier, however, has made his mark as a true dual-threat with 2,911 career rushing yards—leading all active FBS quarterbacks.
Dampier has been responsible for multiple touchdowns (passing + rushing) in all 10 games this season, the only FBS player to accomplish that feat.
He has contributed to 200-plus yards (passing + rushing) in all three of Utah's games this season against AP-ranked opponents (Houston, BYU, at Arizona). Including Dampier, nine FBS quarterbacks this season have three or more games against ranked foes with 200-plus total yards, but only Dampier, Trinidad Chambliss (Ole Miss) and Dylan Raiola (Oregon) have at least three wins in those games.
In four combined games against AP-ranked opponents and the SEC, Dampier has had a pass efficiency of 144.4, 62.3% completion rate and has averaged 211.5 passing yards per game.  He rushed for two touchdowns in all four games (for a total of eight) and had 85-plus rushing yards in three of the four, including 100 rushing yards in the road win over Arizona and 150 at home vs. Houston.
UP NEXT
Utah wraps up its regular season road slate on Saturday, Nov. 21 at TCU. The game will kick off at 7 p.m. MT and air on FOX.
