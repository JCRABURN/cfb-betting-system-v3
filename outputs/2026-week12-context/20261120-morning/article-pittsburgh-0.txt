PITTSBURGH –
The Pitt Panthers (6-4, 3-4 ACC) travel to Louisville, Ky. to visit the No. 23 ranked Louisville Cardinals (7-3, 6-2 ACC) in primetime on Saturday night at 7:30 PM on ESPN2.
Pitt and Louisville meet for the 23rd time in a series that dates to 1976. The all-time series is tied, 11-11, as is the ACC series, 3-3. Pitt has won six of the past 10 meetings.
Two of Pitt's three victories over Louisville under
Pat Narduzzi
came against ranked teams. The Panthers defeated No. 24 Louisville, 23-20, in 2020 and No. 14 Louisville, 38-21, in 2023. A win Saturday would be Pitt's first in Louisville since a 21-14 victory in 2011.
Pitt has won five of its past seven ACC road games, including a 35-33 victory at Virginia Tech this season. The Panthers scored at least 30 points in all five wins, averaging 35.2 points.

GAME STORYLINES
• Pitt continues its 137th season of football Saturday when it visits No. 23 ranked Louisville. The Panthers are 6-4 overall and 3-4 in the ACC. Louisville enters the game at 7-3 overall and 6-2 in conference play.
• Pitt secured bowl eligibility with a 34-11 victory over Georgia Tech on Oct. 31. A victory at Louisville would give the Panthers their seventh win and bring them to 4-4 in ACC play.
• Pitt ranks fifth in the ACC in total offense at 432.1 yards per game and third in rushing offense in conference games at 171.0 yards per game. Twelve Panthers have combined to catch 22 touchdown passes this season.
• True freshman
Corey Dailey
accounted for a career-high 215 yards of total offense against Florida State in his second collegiate start. He set career highs with 16 completions and 34 attempts, passing for 157 yards and a touchdown while leading Pitt with 58 rushing yards.
•
Ja'Kyrian Turner
caught a career-high seven passes for 41 yards and a touchdown against Florida State. He added 42 rushing yards to finish with a game-high 83 yards from scrimmage.
• True freshman
Damon Ferguson Jr.
leads Pitt with 694 rushing yards and seven total touchdowns. He has three 100-yard rushing games this season, highlighted by a career-high 185 yards and two touchdowns against Georgia Tech.
•
Cataurus Hicks
leads Pitt with 37 receptions, 730 receiving yards, six touchdown catches and 885 all-purpose yards. He averages 19.7 yards per catch and had four receptions for 113 yards and a touchdown against Louisville last season.
• Pitt ranks second in the ACC in rushing defense, allowing 71.2 yards per game. The Panthers have held seven of 10 opponents below 100 rushing yards and all seven ACC opponents below 150. In last season's meeting, Pitt held Louisville to 53 rushing yards on 34 attempts.
• Pitt has held its past two opponents to an average of 249.0 total yards and 96.0 rushing yards. Georgia Tech and Florida State combined to convert just six of 31 third-down attempts (19.4%), and neither threw a touchdown pass.
• The Panthers have recorded 24 sacks in six meetings with Louisville under Narduzzi, with at least two in every game. Pitt combined for 18 sacks and six interceptions in its three wins over the Cardinals and has returned three interceptions for touchdowns in the series during his tenure.
•
Cruce Brookins
led all players with 10 tackles against Florida State, including nine solo stops. He leads Pitt with 71 tackles and has 160 for his career.
Raion Strader
has recorded a sack in consecutive games and leads the Panthers with three interceptions and eight pass breakups.
•
Antonio Chadha
has made seven consecutive field goals and is 13-for-14 on field goals and 34-for-34 on extra points this season. Pitt has converted all eight field-goal attempts and all 18 extra-point attempts in six meetings with Louisville under Narduzzi.
•
Gabe Russo
averaged 44.8 yards on five punts against Florida State, with a long of 56 yards. He has placed 14 punts inside the opponent's 20-yard line and has 16 punts of at least 50 yards this season.
