Toledo Welcomes Bowling Green to the Glass Bowl for Battle of I-75
11/18/2026 2:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
Rivals will meet for the 91st time
TOLEDO, Ohio – The Toledo football team saves the biggest for last in its 2026 home schedule, taking on arch-rival Bowling Green in the Battle of I-75 on Friday at the Glass Bowl. Kickoff is at 7:30 p.m. on ESPN2.
The Rockets (8-2, 5-1 MAC) moved into a tie for first place after a Week 11 shutout of UMass, 24-0. Toledo did all of its damage in the first half, with
Connor Walendzak
breaking free for the game's opening touchdown in the first quarter, before a
Nicholas Thompson
pick-six and a Walendzak receiving touchdown capped the scoring in the second quarter. Meanwhile, the defense put in another dominant effort, with 10 tackles for loss and five sacks, while not allowing UMass to reach the red zone.
Toledo enters Friday's game with a 5-1 record at the Glass Bowl this season. The Rockets can win six home games for the third time in four seasons. Toledo is outscoring opponents 230-135 (+15.8 points per game) at home this season. The Falcons are coming off a 33-28 home win over Kent State last week.

The Launch Pad
• Toledo is tied for first place in the MAC standings with Buffalo, Western Michigan and Ohio with two games remaining. UT plays at Ohio in the regular-season finale.
• The Rockets lead the league in nine statistical categories - scoring offense (34.3 points), rushing offense (197.2 yards), total offense (453.4 yards), passing offense (256.2 yards), fewest sacks allowed (11), total sacks (31), first downs per game (21.4), opponent first downs (17.1) and opponent third down percentage (32.7%).
• Despite several injuries and zero returning starters, Toledo has established itself as one of the top offensive lines in the country, allowing just 11 sacks all season.
•
Andrew Zock
is putting up All-American numbers this season, especially in conference play. In six MAC games, Zock has 13.5 tackles for loss, 9.0 sacks, 16 QB hurries and four forced fumbles. He's recorded at least 2.0 tackles for loss in every MAC game.
•
Connor Walendzak
returned to the lineup in a big way against UMass, with 117 yards on 14 carries, including a 69-yard touchdown run. He added 41 receiving yards and a touchdown catch.
• Walendzak has worked in tandem with
CJ Miller
, who had 83 yards on 15 carries against UMass. Miller has reached 100 yards in three of Toledo's six MAC games.
• Toledo is 5-1 at home this season and has one of the best home records in the FBS over the last four years, with a 21-3 home mark since 2023.
