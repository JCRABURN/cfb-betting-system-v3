Stokes Named to Davey O’Brien Award Great 8 for Fourth Time in 2026
Nov 16, 2026
|
Football
Share:
FORT WORTH, Texas –
For the fourth time this season and third game in a row, University of Memphis quarterback
Marcus Stokes
were named to the Davey O'Brien Award Great 8, the Davey O'Brien Foundation announced on Monday.
Stokes did something not seen in multiple seasons against South Florida. After throwing for 313 yards and three touchdowns while rushing for 73 yards and a pair of scores, Stokes became the first FBS player to account for five or more touchdowns in back-to-back-to-back games since Bo Nix in 2023.
The West Florida transfer has become one of the nation's top passers with the Tigers, ranking in the top 10 nationally in passing efficiency (177.0), passing touchdowns (28), passing yards (2,845) and total offense (317.9), while guiding the Memphis offense to top 10 rankings in total offense (520.1), scoring offense (39.0) and first downs (252).
In Stokes' first season at the FBS level, he has been the American Conference Offensive Player of the Week twice, American Weekly honorable mention three times, Manning Award Stars of the Week three times and now has made the Davey O'Brien Great 8 for the fourth time. Along with those weekly honors, Stokes was also named to the Manning Award Midseason Watch List.
Earlier this season, Stokes became the first Memphis quarterback since 2020 to be named the Davey O'Brien National Quarterback of the Week, along with being named to the Davey O'Brien QB Class of 2026.
The Davey O'Brien Award is presented annually to the nation's best college quarterback and is the oldest and most prestigious national quarterback award.
