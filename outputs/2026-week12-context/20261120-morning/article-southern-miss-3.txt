Steven Robinson Named 2026 C Spire Conerly Trophy Nominee
11/17/2026 9:50:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
HATTIESBURG, Miss. –
Southern Miss redshirt junior running back
Steven Robinson
(Baltimore, Md.) earned selection as one of nine nominees for the 2026 C Spire Conerly Trophy released Tuesday morning by the Mississippi Sports Hall of Fame & Museum, which recognizes the most outstanding college football player in the state.
This season, Robinson leads Southern Miss in rushing yards with 876 on 174 carries for 5.0 yards per carry. His rushing total also ranks first in the Sun Belt Conference. That offensive production includes 10 rushing scores and one receiving touchdown, and his 11 total touchdowns are tops as well in the league.
Other highlights this season show Robinson registering a pair of three rushing touchdown performances, coming against Alcorn State and Louisiana, as well as three 100-yard rushing contests. Those top rushing performances include a season-best 151 yards at ULM. He also had a 145-yard contest versus Louisiana and a 123-yard game at home against Alcorn State.
His 87.6 rushing yards per game also leads the league.
Joining Robinson as nominees are:
* Alcorn State University:
Jaylon Tolbert
(Quarterback, Fort Lauderdale, FL, Senior)
* Belhaven University:
Jimmy Byrd
(Defensive Lineman, Mount Vernon, AL, Senior)
* Delta State University:
Price Nixon
(Kicker/Punter, Clinton, MS, Graduate Student)
* Jackson State University:
Nate Rembert
(Wide Receiver, Eustis, FL, Fifth-Year Student)
* Millsaps College:
Tameron Patterson
(Running Back, Corinth, MS, Senior)
* Mississippi State University:
Kelley Jones
(Cornerback, Clarksdale, MS, Senior)
* Mississippi Valley State University:
Javieane Stevens
(Outside Linebacker, Fort Myers, FL, Junior)
* University of Mississippi:
Trinidad Chambliss
(Quarterback, Grand Rapids, MI, Fifth-Year-Plus Student)
The C Spire Conerly Trophy recipient is determined through a ranked-choice voting process involving sports media representatives from across Mississippi who cover the participating programs, along with five selected scouts.
The 31st Annual C Spire Conerly Trophy Presentation will take place on Tuesday, December 1, at the Mississippi Sports Hall of Fame & Museum in Jackson. The evening will begin with a reception at 5:00 p.m. featuring refreshments and hors d'oeuvres, followed by the awards presentation at 6:00 p.m. honoring this year's nominees and celebrating Mississippi's rich tradition of college football excellence.
Tickets are available now. For event details or to purchase seats, visit msfame.com or call the Mississippi Sports Hall of Fame & Museum at 601-982-8264.
