Wake Forest’s Langston Hardy Named Finalist for Bronko Nagurski Trophy
11/19/2026 9:59:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
The award is given annually to the top defensive player in the entire country by the Football Writers Association of America.

Stephen Czarda
Share:
CHARLOTTE, N.C. –
Wake Forest redshirt senior defensive lineman
Langston Hardy
has been named one of four finalists for the Bronko Nagurski Trophy, presented annually to the top defensive player in the country by the Football Writers Association of America (FWAA) and the Charlotte Touchdown Club. Created in 1993, the award was established to complement the Heisman Trophy, which at the time had never been presented to a defensive player.
In his second season with the Demon Deacons, Hardy has once again proven to be one of the most productive defensive linemen in the country with an ACC-high 13.5 tackles for loss.

Through 10 games, the Jacksonville, Fla., native has totaled 45 tackles (21 solo) along with 5.5 sacks to captain a Wake Forest defensive unit that ranks top-25 in both total defense and scoring defense.
Additionally, Hardy has guided the Deacs to a top-five national ranking in rushing defense, holding opponents to 83.1 yards per game on the ground.
Over the last two years, Hardy has totaled 30.5 tackles for loss – third-most in the country amongst active players during that span.

A tackle, fullback, defensive end, offensive lineman, linebacker and passer, Bronislau "Bronko" Nagurski's talents were legendary. He was a consensus All-American defensive tackle in 1929, and also made All-America teams that year as a fullback, the only man ever picked at two positions. Ballots were found with the 11 (one-platoon) positions listed and only 10 names, Nagurski in two of the spots.
Nagurski was a charter member of the National Football Foundation Hall of Fame and the Pro Football Hall of Fame, and in college football's centennial year, 1969, he was elected to the Football Writers Association of America's all-time team.
In 1995, the FWAA named their College Football Defensive Player of the Year award in his honor.
About The Bronko Nagurski Trophy
Created in 1993 by the Football Writers Association of America, the Bronko Nagurski Trophy is awarded to the best defensive player in college football and is presented by the Charlotte Touchdown Club. The award was established to complement the Heisman Trophy Award, which at the time had never been presented to a defensive player.
The Bronko Nagurski Trophy Awards Banquet not only recognizes the best college defensive player, but also helps to raise money to recognize the recipients of our regional high school and college scholarships. All proceeds benefit the Charlotte Touchdown Club Scholarship Fund.
About The Charlotte Touchdown Club
The Charlotte Touchdown Club is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 1990 to promote high school, college and professional football in the Charlotte, N.C., area. Supported by more than 80 corporate sponsors, the club recognizes citizenship, scholarship, sportsmanship and leadership among area athletes and coaches. Individual and corporate supporters have helped raise nearly $3 million for the club's scholarship efforts.
