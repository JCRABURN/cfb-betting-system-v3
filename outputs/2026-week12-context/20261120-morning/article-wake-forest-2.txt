Wake Forest's Carlos Hernandez Named Semifinalist for Biletnikoff Award
11/16/2026 12:09:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
The senior wide receiver ranks top-10 in the country in both receptions and receiving yards.
By:
Stephen Czarda
Share:
TALLAHASSEE, Fla. –
Wake Forest senior wide receiver
Carlos Hernandez
has been named a semifinalist for the 2026 Biletnikoff Award, the Tallahassee Quarterback Club (TQC) Foundation, Inc. announced on Monday.
The Biletnikoff Award annually recognizes college football's most outstanding receiver.
Through the first 10 games of the season, Hernandez has set career-highs across the board with 67 receptions for 1,039 yards and eight touchdowns. His 1,039 receiving yards are fourth-most in the country and his 67 receptions are tied for the 10th-most in the country.
Eight times this season the senior has recorded 80 or more receiving yards, including a six-catch, 94-yard performance against SMU on Saturday where he became the first Demon Deacon wide receiver to reach the 1,000-yard mark since A.T. Perry in 2022.

The California native opened the season with five catches for 165 yards and a touchdown in just the first half against Akron while recording a nine-catch, 194-yard performance in a victory at No. 16 Louisville.
Including his 197-yard outing against Delaware last season, Hernandez is the first player in program history with a pair of 190-plus-yard receiving games.
