No. 5 Miami Holds off Duke, 36-25

MIAMI
- No. 5 Miami held off a persistent Duke, 36-25, Saturday afternoon at Hard Rock Stadium. The Blue Devils were led by sophomore
Nate Sheppard
, who scored on 47-yard and five-yard touchdown runs and broke the Duke single-season rushing record in the process.
Sheppard finished with 131 ground yards to move his single-season total to 1,320 to set the new Duke record. The sophomore also posted a team-high 93 receiving yards, good for 224 total scrimmage yards.
Sheppard posted the highest rushing total by a player facing Miami since Virginia Tech's Bhayshul Tuten tallied 141 yards on the ground against the Hurricanes on Sept. 27, 2024. Sheppard's 224 scrimmage yards are the most by a Miami opponent since Javonte Williams and Michael Carter racked up 236 and 308 scrimmage yards, respectively, for North Carolina on Dec. 12, 2020.
The Blue Devils were even at 10-10 with less than a minute to play in the opening half and trailed by just five, 22-17, with less than nine minutes to play before the Hurricanes scored two touchdowns in less than four minutes late in the final frame.
Jeremiah Hasley
capped Duke's scoring with a touchdown reception in the final minute, giving him 10 career TD grabs, the third most by a Duke tight end in program history.
HOW IT HAPPENED
After a scoreless opening quarter, a track meet broke out in the second quarter with the teams combining for 23 points. Miami struck first with a Jake Weinberg 39-yard field goal to open the second frame and took a 3-0 lead at the 14:43 mark. The two teams combined for 118 yards of total offense in the initial 15 minutes of action.
Duke answered the Hurricanes' score with a field goal of its own as
Gavin Strange
snuck in a 37-yard attempt to knot the game at 3-3 with 9:27 to go in the first half. The Blue Devils turned in their longest drive of the game to that point, covering 55 yards on 13 plays.
Jeremiah Hasley
caught an 18-yard pass on fourth-and-one from the Miami 41-yard line to help set up the field goal.
It was the Hurricanes' turn to answer on the ensuing drive with the game's first touchdown, a Mark Fletcher Jr. 4-yard run, to put Miami up 10-3 with 5:47 left in the first half. The touchdown was the first allowed by Duke in 12 quarters. The last TD against the Blue Devils was in the opening quarter by Virginia on Oct. 23.
Duke answered less than three minutes later with a
Nate Sheppard
47-yard touchdown run to even the game at 10-10 with 3:17 left before halftime.
Miami had a chance to make it a 17-10 lead with a first-and-goal on its final drive of the opening half, but was held to a 27-yard Weinberg field goal with 41 seconds remaining to send it to the half with a 13-10 Hurricane lead.
Miami had another first-and-goal on its opening drive of the second half, but was again limited to a Weinberg 24-yard field goal to extend its lead to 16-10 with 9:48 left in the third.
After stopping Duke near midfield, the Hurricanes took a 22-10 lead with 2:43 left in the third on a 63-yard pass to Javian Mallory.
The Blue Devils answered immediately with another Sheppard 47-yard run setting the stage for his five-yard TD rush to cut the deficit to 22-17 with 41 ticks left in the third.
Miami took the ensuing possession and covered 79 yards with Mallory scoring on a two-yard rush to make it 29-17 with 8:06 left. The Hurricanes added another touchdown with a five-play, 71-yard, drive capped by a 39-yard rush by Mallory with 4:21 remaining.
Duke punched it into the endzone one final time, with Hasley on the receiving end of a one-yard toss.
UP NEXT
Duke plays its final home game of the season Friday with a 7:30 p.m. kick on ESPN against Clemson.
