TCU Athletics and REVELXP Renew Partnership to Continue Elevating Horned Frogs Game Day Experiences
11/18/2026 2:44:00 PM
|
Football, General
Share:
FORT WORTH
— TCU Athletics and REVEL
XP
have agreed to a long-term partnership extension, reinforcing a successful collaboration focused on delivering premium tailgating and hospitality experiences for Horned Frogs fans.
Since partnering with TCU, REVEL
XP
has helped enhance the game day atmosphere by providing full-service tailgating services that allow fans to spend less time planning and more time enjoying the traditions and excitement of TCU Football. The extension reflects both organizations' shared commitment to delivering an elevated fan experience while continuing to build on the strong momentum of TCU game days. As part of the partnership extension, REVEL
XP
is also expanding its game day footprint with a new tailgating location on Clark Lawn, providing fans with another convenient option to gather and celebrate before kickoff.
"We are thrilled to continue this partnership with REVEL
XP
and strengthen our game day experience," said TCU Senior Deputy Athletic Director
Ryan Peck
. "REVEL
XP
has been a tremendous partner in bringing Horned Frogs fans together while supporting our efforts in the Name, Image, and Likeness space. It has been a true win-win for all sides"
Through the renewed agreement, REVEL
XP
will continue serving as the Official Tailgating and Hospitality Provider for TCU Athletics, offering a range of customizable game day experiences for fans, alumni, corporate groups, and special events throughout the football season. Beginning January 1, 2027, REVEL
XP
will also become the Preferred Equipment Rental Provider for TCU Athletics, further expanding the partnership by supporting university events with equipment rental services throughout the year.
"Our partnership with TCU has been built around creating memorable experiences that bring the Horned Frogs community together," said Wes Day, CEO of REVEL
XP
. "We're excited to continue growing alongside TCU Athletics and finding new ways to enhance game days for fans through exceptional hospitality and service."
REVEL
XP
's full-service tailgating packages provide fans with a hassle-free tailgate experience that includes complete management of game day logistics such as equipment, setup, cleanup, and on-site customer service. Each package comes with the essentials – tent, tables, and chairs – along with premium add-ons like catering, media packages, and more. Fans can also use REVEL
XP
's service that transports personal tailgating items to and from vehicles. In addition, REVEL
XP
offers pregame events for large groups with customizable hospitality options.
Fans can learn more about REVEL
XP
's TCU tailgating experiences by visiting
revelxp.com/teams/texas-christian-university.
About REVEL
XP
REVEL
XP
is a premier sports hospitality company. REVEL
XP
works with hundreds of college and professional teams and major sporting events to deliver exceptional fan experiences, including full-service tailgates, premium hospitality events and exclusive ticket packages. REVEL
XP
also provides comprehensive hospitality solutions to team and sports partners with ticket sales, event staffing, event management, premium hospitality management and venue installations. For more information, please visit
REVELxp.com
.
