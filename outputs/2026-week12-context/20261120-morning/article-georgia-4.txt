Cole Named A Lombardi Award Finalist
November 19, 2026
|
Football
Share:
ATHENS, Ga. --- Bulldog junior LB
Chris Cole
has been included as a Lombardi Award finalist given to college football's best linebacker or lineman, according to an announcement from the Lombardi Award Committee.
Cole, a native of Salem, Va., joins UCLA's Sammy Omosigho, Indiana's Tobi Osunsanmi and Texas' Colin Simmons as the four finalists.  The Lombardi Award winner will be announced at a ceremony in Houston on Dec. 9.
Cole is already a semifinalist for the Bednarik and Butkus awards during his stellar third season in red and black.  He leads the Bulldogs with 64 tackles, including 12 tackles for loss and five sacks (both team highs) and has collected three fumble recoveries, which ranks third nationally.  Cole returned one of those fumbles 85 yards for a touchdown versus Oklahoma and another 66 yards for a score against Florida.  He has helped anchor a defense that ranks ninth in the country in Scoring Defense (14.7 points/game).
The No. 1 Bulldogs travel to Columbia, S.C., to take on No. 21 South Carolina on Saturday.  ABC will televise the matchup at 7:30 p.m.
