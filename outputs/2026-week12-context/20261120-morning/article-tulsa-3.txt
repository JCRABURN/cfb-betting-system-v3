Tailgate Experience Set for Charlotte
11/19/2026 1:41:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
TULSA, Okla. ––
Tulsa fans can begin Saturday's football festivities at Chapman Commons, where the Golden Hurricane Tailgate will feature live music, food, drinks and family-friendly activities ahead of the Golden Hurricane's final home game of the 2026 season against Charlotte.
Game-day festivities begin at 9 a.m. with the opening of parking lots and the Great American Entertainment Petting Zoo. The petting zoo will be available from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., providing a fun and interactive experience for fans of all ages.
DJ Chuck G will take the stage at Chapman Commons beginning at 10 a.m., bringing music and entertainment to the Golden Hurricane Tailgate. Fans can then line 11th Street at 10:45 a.m. to welcome the Tulsa football team during the traditional team walk.
Gates at H.A. Chapman Stadium will open at 11:30 a.m., giving fans time to enter the stadium before the Sound of the Golden Hurricane takes the field for its pregame show at 12:30 p.m.
Located on Chapman Commons, the Golden Hurricane Tailgate features live music, inflatables and a beer garden. Fans can also reserve a space on Chapman Commons and bring their own tailgating supplies.
The Golden Hurricane Champions Fund and Alumni Association tailgate tents will also be open for fans to enjoy before kickoff.
Fans are encouraged to arrive early, take advantage of the family-friendly activities and enjoy the game-day atmosphere before Tulsa takes on Charlotte at 1 p.m. Saturday at H.A. Chapman Stadium.
