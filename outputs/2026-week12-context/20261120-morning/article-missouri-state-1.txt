Boamah Named CUSA Defensive Player of the Week
November 16, 2026
|
Football
CUSA Release
Share:
By:
MSU Athletics Communications
DALLAS -- Missouri State cornerback
Joel Boamah
(Sr., Newark, N.J.) has been named as Conference USA Defensive Player of the Week, the league announced Monday afternoon.
Boamah's 88-yard "walk-off" interception return for a touchdown in overtime against New Mexico State on Saturday marked his third interception of the year and his second pick six. He also snagged another takeaway in the second half with a forced fumble and recovery to halt an Aggies drive in Bears territory. Likewise, he finished with five total tackles and three passes defended in the Mo State homecoming victory.
He is the second Bear to earn CUSA Defensive Player of the Week honors this season, joining linebacker
Jared Lloyd
who earned the accolade on Oct. 20.
Joining Boamah on this week's conference awards list were: Nick Minicucci, Delaware (Offense), Brody Richter, NM State (Special Teams), Westen Ard, WKU (Freshman) and Joep Engbers, Liberty (Offensive Lineman).
With Boamah's help, the Bears jumped to the top of the FBS ranks in defensive touchdowns scored this season with five. The win marked Missouri State's sixth consecutive homecoming victory.
After a road game at Jacksonville State next Saturday (Nov. 21) at 1 p.m., the Bears return to Robert W. Plaster Stadium for the regular-season finale on Nov. 28 against Liberty.
A "pick six" ticket deal is already available for the Liberty game with bleacher tickets available for $6 plus fees. All remaining tickets for the Liberty game are available now at MissouriStateBears.com or by visiting the Great Southern Bank Arena Box Office, (417) 836-7678, during regular box office hours. Please visit MissouriState.edu/Homecoming for more Homecoming events and activities.
