Stockton Named Davey O’Brien Award Semifinalist
November 18, 2026
|
Football
Share:
ATHENS, Ga. --- Bulldog junior QB
Gunner Stockton
has been named a semifinalist for the Davey O'Brien Award presented to the nation's best quarterback, according to an announcement from the Davey O'Brien Foundation.
Stockton, a native of Tiger, Ga., is one of 13 semifinalists for the accolade and one of four from the Southeastern Conference.  Finalists will be named on Nov. 24.
A semifinalist for the Maxwell Award and Walter Camp Player of the Year, Stockton ranks second in the NCAA in Passing Efficiency (179.8) and fifth in Completion Percentage (71.4) during the Bulldogs' 9-1 start.  The National Football Foundation Scholar-Athlete Award winner has completed 157 of 220 passes for 2,109 yards, 21 touchdowns and only four interceptions.  Stockton has also rushed for 239 yards and three more scores while only getting sacked 11 times through 10 games.
The No. 1 Bulldogs travel to Columbia, S.C., to take on No. 21 South Carolina on Saturday.  ABC will televise the matchup at 7:30 p.m.
