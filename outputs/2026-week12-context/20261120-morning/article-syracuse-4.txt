Hatcher Named Biletnikoff Award Semifinalist
11/16/2026 12:10:00 PM
|
Football
Get Tickets
Share:
Syracuse wide receiver
Umari Hatcher
has been named one of 13 semifinalists for the Biletnikoff Award, presented annually to the nation's most outstanding receiver.
Amid a season in which he has totaled 1,045 receiving yards, the sixth-most by a Syracuse player in a single season in program history, Hatcher ranks third among all FBS players in receiving yards. He also sits 11th nationally in receptions per game (6.6) and is tied for 16th in receiving touchdowns (8).
Hatcher became the first Orange player to record four straight 100-yard receiving games since Steve Ishmael accomplished the feat in 2017. The streak began with 152 yards at UConn, followed by 126 yards at Virginia, 224 yards against Louisville and 103 yards at North Carolina.
His 224-yard performance against Louisville stands out as the eighth 200-yard receiving game in SU history. Hatcher hauled in a career-high 13 catches in the contest, the eighth-most receptions and sixth-most receiving yards by an FBS player in a single game this season.
Hatcher is one of three ACC receivers selected to the semifinalist list, joining Wake Forest's Carlos Hernandez and Miami's Malachi Toney.
Hatcher and the Orange will be on the road for the final time of the regular season this week when they take on Boston College before returning home to face Notre Dame. Tickets are on sale at
Cuse.com/FootballTickets
