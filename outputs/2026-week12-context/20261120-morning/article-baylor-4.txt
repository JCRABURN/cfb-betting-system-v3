Baylor at Houston Set for 8:30 p.m. on ESPN2
11/16/2026 12:54:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
WACO, Texas –
Baylor will travel to face No. 21 Houston on Saturday, Nov. 28, with the game set for an 8:30 p.m. kickoff with a telecast on ESPN2, it was announced by the Big 12 Conference office on Monday afternoon.
Baylor (6-4, 4-3 Big 12) faces Houston (7-3, 4-3 Big 12) in the regular-season finale in Houston. The Bears wrap up their home schedule on Saturday, hosting No. 11 Texas Tech (9-1, 6-1 Big 12) at McLane Stadium.
- BaylorBears.com -
