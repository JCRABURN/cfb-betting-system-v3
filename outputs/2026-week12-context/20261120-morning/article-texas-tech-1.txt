Harrington back on Lou Groza Award semifinalists list
November 17, 2026
|
Football
About the Lou Groza Award
Stone Harrington Bio
Red Raider Honors Hub
Share:
Harrington is among the biggest legs in college football with nine made field goals from 40-plus yards
LUBBOCK, Texas
– Texas Tech fifth-year senior kicker
Stone Harrington
was back on the semifinalist list Tuesday for the Lou Groza Award, recognizing the nation's top kicker. This is the second-consecutive season Harrington has been named among the 20 semifinalists.
Harrington ranks among the biggest legs in college football as head coach
Joey McGuire
and special teams coordinator
Kenny Perry
haven't been afraid to send him out from distance. He is 19 of 23 on field goal attempts overall on the season, including 9 of 11 from 40-plus yards. His only two misses from that distance have both been from 63 and 51 yards.
Harrington is tied for third currently in the FBS with nine made field goals from at least 40 yards, which includes two from 50-plus yards. He found the uprights from 52 yards earlier this season against Colorado and then again from 54 yards this past weekend in Texas Tech's win over No. 25 Oklahoma State. For his career, Harrington has 18 made field goals from 40-plus yards, the most by a Red Raider dating back to 1996 and the fifth-highest total among FBS kickers since the start of the 2025 season.
Harrington currently ranks tied for fifth in Texas Tech single-season history with his 19 made field goals this season, four shy of Ryan Bustin's record from 2013. He has 41 made field goals for his career, which is in a three-way tie for sixth all-time in program history alongside Ricky Gann (1981-84) and Scott Segrist (1985-88). Bustin shares the Texas Tech career mark with Clayton Hatfield (2015-18) as the two each made 50 field goals over their respective careers.
In addition to finding the uprights, Harrington remains one of the nation's top kickoff specialists, booting 45 of his 59 attempts for touchbacks so far this season. He is also 43-of-44 on extra points this season.
Semifinalists will be voted on by a panel of more than 100 FBS head coaches, communications professionals, media members, former Groza finalists and current NFL kickers to select the three finalists. These finalists will be announced on Nov. 24 and honored at the 35th-annual Lou Groza Awards Banquet on Dec. 7 in Palm Beach County. The same panel then selects the winner, who will be announced live on ESPN at the Home Depot College Football Awards on Dec. 11.
Harrington was one of 20 kickers selected as a semifinalist and one of three from the Big 12 Conference, joining Oklahoma State's Sam Keltner and Arizona State's Carson Smith.
