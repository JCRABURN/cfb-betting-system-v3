Willie Phaler, MTSU Athletic Communications
LYNCHBURG, Va.-
Middle Tennessee football (5-5, 3-3 CUSA) dropped its penultimate road contest of 2026 to the Liberty Flames (9-1, 6-0 CUSA), 28-23.
Trailing 14-10 at the break, the Blue Raiders found their footing in the aerial attack in the second half as redshirt sophomore quarterback
Roman Gagliano
tossed two passing touchdowns.
After punting the first drive of the third quarter, Gagliano found freshman wideout
Ziyon Butler
for a 23-yard score. The touchdown was the second of Butler's career, and follows up a career performance last week that earned him CUSA Freshman of the Week honors.
Trailing 28-17 with 8:44 to play, Gagliano threw his second touchdown of the afternoon, hooking up with redshirt freshman
Neo Clifton
for a 14-yard score. This finished off a six-play, 62-yard drive and brought the Blue Raiders within five points. This was the sixth scoring connection of the season between Gagliano and Clifton.
Overall, Gagliano went 24-34 for 202 yards to pair with his two scores– good for a 139.9 passer rating in the loss.
Middle Tennessee's offense found a groove in the second quarter, scoring on back-to-back drives. Kicker
Dominic Bourgeois
was good from 45-yards out to finish a drive kickstarted by a
Reggie Johnson
forced fumble, which was recovered by linebacker
Ashton Taylor
.
The Blue Raiders found paydirt on the next drive for the first time in the ballgame, as
Antonio Martin Jr.
punched in his fifth rushing score of the season. Martin's score pushed the Blue Raiders ahead of 10-7, marking their lone lead of the afternoon against Liberty.
Linebacker
Korey Smith
continued his excellent 2026 campaign for the Blue Raiders, coming home with a career-high 16 tackles. Smith entered the game with the third-most tackles in CUSA, and looks to maintain his pace at the top of the league with 96 to his name on the season.
By the Numbers:
9-2:
With two passing touchdowns today, quarterback
Roman Gagliano's
touchdown-to-interception ratio now sits at nine to two on the season. Gagliano has 18 passing touchdowns and four interceptions.
3:
Reggie Johnson's
forced fumble in the backend of the first quarter was his third forced fumble as a Blue Raider. The sack he was credited with on the play brought his career total to 5.5, and two on the season.
18:
Cam'ron Lacy
has now hauled in a reception in 18 straight games, hauling in 10 in Saturday's contest. The Toccoa, Georgia native is two catches back of 10th all-time in program history with 122 career receptions.
Postgame Interviews
Up Next:
Middle Tennessee will continue its final road trip of the regular season next Saturday, Nov. 21 as they travel to the lone star state to take on Sam Houston (1-9, 0-6 CUSA). Kickoff from Huntsville, Texas is slated for a 1:00 p.m. kickoff.
