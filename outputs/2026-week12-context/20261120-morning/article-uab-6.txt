Game Day Guide: UTSA, November 21
Senior Night: UAB vs. UTSA
Saturday, Nov. 21 | 7 p.m. CT | ESPNU | Protective Stadium
Theme:
Senior Night |
Entitlement Sponsor:
Publix
One Last Night at Protective Stadium
BIRMINGHAM, Ala.
– The UAB football team returns to Protective Stadium for its regular-season home finale Saturday, Nov. 21, hosting UTSA in a pivotal American Conference matchup presented by Publix. Kickoff is scheduled for 7 p.m. CT on ESPNU.
Saturday is Senior Night, as UAB Athletics recognizes
23 football student-athletes and one staff member
during a special pregame ceremony. The evening will also feature recognition of the 2026 UAB Athletics Hall of Fame class, special ticket promotions and activities throughout Blazer Village.
The Blazers (6-4, 4-2 American) enter the contest riding a two-game winning streak after securing bowl eligibility for the first time since 2022 with a 36-10 victory at Temple. UAB is also a perfect 5-0 at Protective Stadium this season and is seeking its first undefeated home campaign since 2019.
Fans are encouraged to arrive early, celebrate those being recognized on Senior Night and help send the Blazers off in style in their final home game of the 2026 season.

Special Events
Senior Night Ceremony, UAB Athletics Hall of Fame Recognition
Honoring Our Seniors
Saturday's regular-season home finale will feature a special pregame ceremony recognizing
23 UAB football student-athletes and one staff member
for their contributions to the Blazers.
The ceremony will take place before kickoff, giving fans an opportunity to celebrate those who have helped lead UAB to bowl eligibility and an undefeated home record entering the final game at Protective Stadium.
The Blazers have won all five of their home games this season, including American Conference victories over Navy, East Carolina and Charlotte. A victory Saturday would give UAB its first 6-0 home season since 2019 and its first undefeated campaign at Protective Stadium since the facility opened in 2021.
Fans are encouraged to be in their seats early for the recognition ceremony.
Celebrating the 2026 UAB Athletics Hall of Fame Class
UAB Athletics will recognize the
2026 UAB Athletics Hall of Fame class
during a first-half quarter break Saturday night.
The five-member class, which will be formally inducted Friday, Nov. 20, at The Florentine in downtown Birmingham, includes:
Aaron Johnson – Men's Basketball (2007-11)
Ivana Bozic – Volleyball (2005-08)
Rodregis Brooks – Football (1996-99)
Carmen Guzman – Women's Basketball (2003-07)
Paul Rosner – Men's Tennis (1992-95)
Fans will also have an opportunity for a
meet-and-greet with 2026 UAB Athletics Hall of Fame inductees following Blazer Walk
in Blazer Village.
The class includes former UAB football standout Rodregis Brooks, who earned second-team Associated Press All-America recognition in 1999 after recording nine interceptions, tying for the national lead.
Join UAB Athletics in celebrating the individuals whose accomplishments have helped shape the history and tradition of Blazer Athletics.

Make
BlazerGameDay.com
your first stop before heading to Protective Stadium. The site features game day updates, kickoff timelines, parking information, stadium policies and more.
Blazer Village – Opens at 3 p.m.
Blazer Village, UAB Athletics' official pregame fan fest, opens outside Protective Stadium at 3 p.m. and will feature activities, sponsor activations and opportunities to get ready for Saturday's game.
Activities include:
Inflatables for young Blazer fans

"Left in the Dust"

Blaze's Home Field Collection: "Left in the Dust"
Fans can stop by the
UAB Bookstore tent
to check out "Left in the Dust," the final addition to Blaze's Home Field Collection for the 2026 season.
If you missed out on any of the shirts throughout the season, don't miss your last chance to visit the UAB Bookstore tent and complete your collection of all six designs! Availability of previous designs may be limited, so be sure to stop by early.
Blazer Express Shuttle Service
Complimentary Blazer Express shuttles are available to UAB students and the general public.
Fans may park free of charge in the 12th Street Parking Deck and board the shuttle at the Hill Student Center beginning at 3 p.m. Service runs continuously to Protective Stadium and concludes one hour following the game.
Visit
BlazerGameDay.com
for complete shuttle information and schedules.
Blazer Walk – Approximately 4:35 p.m.
Get ready to welcome the Blazers to Protective Stadium for the final time this regular season!
Fans are encouraged to line Uptown alongside the UAB Marching Blazers, spirit teams and fellow supporters as the football team makes its way into the stadium.
Following Blazer Walk, fans can visit Blazer Village for a special
meet-and-greet with 2026 UAB Athletics Hall of Fame inductees
.
Special Ticket Offers
Fan Appreciation – Tickets Starting at $10
As a thank-you to Blazer Nation for its support throughout the 2026 season, UAB Athletics is offering a special
Fan Appreciation ticket promotion

$10
for Saturday's regular-season home finale against UTSA.
The promotion offers an affordable opportunity for fans of all ages to enjoy the final home game of the season and help the Blazers pursue an undefeated home record.
Click here to purchase Fan Appreciation tickets starting at $10.
Fans can also
purchase tickets online
or contact the UAB Athletics Ticket Office at 205-975-UAB1 for assistance.
UAB Students Get In Free
UAB students receive admission to all home football games at no cost through their mobile student ticket.
Students are encouraged to download and save their mobile tickets before heading to Protective Stadium.
In-Game Features
Senior Night Recognition
Before kickoff, UAB will recognize 23 football student-athletes and one staff member during a special Senior Night ceremony.
Fans are encouraged to be in their seats early to celebrate their contributions to the program.
2026 UAB Athletics Hall of Fame Recognition
Members of the 2026 UAB Athletics Hall of Fame class will be recognized on the field during a first-half quarter break.
The recognition will celebrate the accomplishments of Aaron Johnson, Ivana Bozic, Rodregis Brooks, Carmen Guzman and Paul Rosner.
Family Fun Zone
The Family Fun Zone, located on the north end of the stadium concourse opposite the student section, will feature inflatables and activities for young Blazer fans.
The area is free to enjoy with a completed waiver.

See You At Protective
Senior Night brings together the entire Blazer family for one final regular-season celebration at Protective Stadium as UAB hosts UTSA on Saturday, Nov. 21.
From recognizing 23 football student-athletes and one staff member and celebrating the 2026 UAB Athletics Hall of Fame class to Fan Appreciation ticket offers and activities in Blazer Village, Saturday promises to be a memorable evening for Blazer Nation.
With UAB undefeated at home and still in contention for a spot in the American Conference Championship Game, the Blazers will look to complete a perfect home season in front of their fans.
Arrive early, celebrate Senior Night and help make the final home game of 2026 a special night at Protective Stadium.
