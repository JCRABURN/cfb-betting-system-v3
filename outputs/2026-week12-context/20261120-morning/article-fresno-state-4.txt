Watanabe named a semifinalist for Lou Groza Award
11/17/2026 12:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Savannah Stoeckle / Associate Athletics Director for Strategic Communications
WEST PALM BEACH, Fla. -
Fresno State sophomore kicker
Manaki Watanabe
has been named a semifinalist for the Lou Groza Award, announced by the Palm Beach County Sports Commission on Tuesday. The Lou Groza Award is awarded annually to the nation's top place kicker.
Watanabe is one of 20 seminalists for the award, and one of two from the Pac-12.
This season, Watanabe is 15-of-19 in his field goal attempts. He has had at least one made field goal in all but one game played this season. Most notably, Watanabe is 4-of-5 in his attempts from 50 or more yards. His first miss at distance was last week at Texas State.
He has the most 50-yard field goals by a Bulldog in a season, and is just one 50-yarder shy of tying the program's record for career 50-yard field goals, set by Kevin Goessling (2009-10). At San Jose State, Watanabe became the first kicker in Fresno State history to make two field goals of 50 or more yards in a single game.
The transfer from Ashland University made four field goals in the games at San José State and Washington State, becoming one of the few kickers in program history to record multiple four-field-goal games in the same season.
He leads the conference and ranks 14th nationally with 1.7 made field goals per game. In his collegiate career, Watanabe has made 35 field goals in 21 career games. His average of 1.64 field goals made per game is third-best among all active NCAA players.
Watanabe adds 29 PATs to his total, leading the team with 74 points scored this season.
Watanabe is the first semifinalist for the award since
Dylan Lynch
in 2023. Semifinalists will be voted on by a panel of more than 100 FBS head coaches, SIDs, media members, former Groza finalists, and current NFL kickers to select the three finalists. These finalists will be announced on November 24th and honored at the 35th annual Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Awards Banquet on December 7th in Palm Beach County. The same panel then selects the winner, who will be announced live on ESPN at the Home Depot College Football Awards Show on Friday, December 11th.
The Bulldogs are back in action at home this Saturday, hosting Colorado State. Kickoff on The CW is set for 4:30 p.m. PT. Tickets are still available
here
.
