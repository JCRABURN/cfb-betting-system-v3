NFL #ProDucks: Week 10
11/19/26
|
Football
Share:
Justin Herbert led the Chargers to a primetime win, Penei Sewell delivered another dominant performance and Jevon Holland continued his impressive season with another interception, placing him among the NFL's leaders. Here's a look at how the #ProDucks made an impact in all three phases of the game this week.
Los Angeles Chargers 23, Baltimore Ravens 20
Herbert led the Chargers to a win on Monday Night Football, completing 12-of-18 passes. He also rushed for 51 yards on seven carries and opened the scoring for Los Angeles with a 22-yard touchdown run.
Jamaree Caldwell and Troy Dye both played for the Bolts' defense. Caldwell finishing one tackle and a quarterback pressure in the win.

November 17, 2026
Detroit Lions 35, New England Patriots 10
The Lions extended their winning streak to five games in Germany this week behind another impressive performance from Sewell. The Lions offensive tackle allowed no pressures and earned an overall grade of 90.2 according to Pro Football Focus (PFF). His grade ranked first among offensive tackles and second among all offensive linemen across the NFL. With the win, the Lions sit atop the NFC North standings.
Christian Gonzalez battled for the Patriots' defense, finishing with three tackles and his first interception of the season.
Washington Commanders 24, New York Giants 21
Josh Conerly Jr. started for the Commanders and helped anchor an offensive line that allowed no sacks on the day. Conerly earned an overall grade of 68.2 according to PFF.
Holland recorded his fifth takeaway of the season with a crucial fourth-quarter interception in the Giants' end zone. Holland also added three tackles. His four interceptions rank third in the NFL.
Kayvon Thibedeaux made a season high-eight tackles for the Giants, while also adding a pressure.

November 13, 2026
San Francisco 49ers 46, Dallas Cowboys 40
Deommodore Lenoir and DeForest Buckner contributed key plays for the San Francisco defense. Lenoir made five tackles and defended a team-high four passes. Buckner added three tackles and two quarterback pressures.
Jordan James returned five kicks for 101 yards, including a long of 26 yards for the 49ers.
T.J. Bass came in as a reserve at right guard for the Cowboys and allowed no sacks or quarterback hits. He earned an overall grade of 74.0 and a run-blocking grade of 84.8 according to PFF.
Jacksonville Jaguars 40, Tennessee Titans 23
Emmanuel Pregnon
was stellar in pass protection for the Jaguars, allowing no pressures and earning a pass-blocking grade of 85.0. His grade ranked seventh among eligible guards this week.
Arik Armstead had two quarterback pressures in the win over the Titans.
Minnesota Vikings 30, Green Bay Packers 10
Evan Williams racked up eight tackles and defended one pass for the Packers. Jordon Riley also contributed to the Green Bay defense, with one tackle.
Indianapolis Colts 24, Miami Dolphins 22
Bryce Boettcher
helped lead the Colts to a win over the Dolphins, finishing with seven tackles for Indianapolis, the second most on the team.
Pharaoh Brown played for the Colts, earning an overall grade of 74.6 according to PFF.
Kansas City Chiefs 35, Atlanta Falcons 13
Jadon Canady
and Jeffrey Bassa both played on defense and special teams for the Chiefs. Canady made three tackles and had the first pass defended of his career. Bassa added two tackles.
Brandon Dorlus finished with two tackles and half a sack for the Falcons.
Carolina Panthers 20, New Orleans Saints 17
Juwan Johnson caught two passes for 28 yards for the Saints.
Buffalo Bills 31, New York Jets 12
Kenyon Sadiq
had one reception for three yards for the Jets.
Los Angeles Rams 34, Arizona Cardinals 6
Jordan Burch played on special teams before suffering a season-ending knee injury early in the first quarter.
Seattle Seahawks 21, Las Vegas Raiders 13
Jackson Powers-Johnson saw action on special teams for the Raiders.
