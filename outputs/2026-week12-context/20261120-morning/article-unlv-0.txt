UNLV Roars Back To Stun No. 23 New Mexico
11/14/2026 9:25:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Share:
🏈 #MWFB Highlights from @unlvfootball's 21-17 comeback win at #25/23 New Mexico https://t.co/kpGxIgX3cQ
— Mountain West (@MountainWest)
November 15, 2026
ALBUQUERQUE, N.M. (UNLVRebels.com) –
Down two touchdowns in the second quarter,
UNLV (6-4/4-2)
outscored
No. 23/25 New Mexico (8-2/5-1)
21-3 the rest of the way to secure its first ranked win in 18 years, 21-17, before the ninth-largest crowd (37,440) in University Stadium history Saturday night.
The Rebels, who became bowl-eligible for a school-record fourth consecutive season, won in Albuquerque for the fifth straight time by holding the Lobos to just a field goal after halftime. UNM went ahead 14-0 before UNLV QB
Jackson Arnold
threw the first of his two touchdown passes – a 16-yarder to TE
Keyan Burnett
-- to draw within seven at the break.
UNM, which leads the nation in turnovers forced, continued the trend with a strip-sack fumble recovery and tipped pass interception – both thwarting UNLV drives deep into Lobo territory -- but the visitors were undeterred.
Arnold would eventually find Kayden "Big Play" McGee for a 43-yard touchdown early in the fourth quarter to bring the Rebels to within three points and then run in his own 44-yard score to complete the comeback. The Rebels finally got a turnover of their own with a
Tony-Louis Nkuba
interception to seal it.
STAT OF THE GAME:
This marked UNLV's sixth win over a ranked opponent in history and first since upsetting No. 13 Arizona State in Tempe in overtime on Sept. 6, 2008.
REBEL OF THE GAME:
QB
Jackson Arnold
accounted for all three of his team's touchdowns -- two in the air and one on the ground. He finished with 201 yards passing and 98 net yards rushing.
QUOTABLE:
"I'm glad that we got off the ground (after Friday's FAA ground stop) and were able to play the game. It's the first win over a ranked team since 2008, so I think that's pretty special. It shows our guys' toughness. We've faced a lot of different adversities this year. All week we were talking about how it's a couple plays that are going to make a difference in every game. A couple of times last year, we seemed to make a lot of those plays. A couple of times this year, we haven't made those plays. Tonight, I'm like, 'you gotta be focused, we gotta be tough, we gotta be disciplined, and we gotta be ready to make that play when our number's called.' And you know what? We can get better. You watch that game and there's going to be a lot of little mistakes. But against a really good team, we were able to overcome that and find a way to win the game and make the plays we needed to. I'm really proud of our guys and how tough they were in the second half."
--UNLV Head Coach Dan Mullen
NOTABLE:
-QB
Jackson Arnold
tossed his 16th and 17th touchdowns of the season
-Arnold also rushed for his team-leading seventh score
-
Kayden McGee
continued to live up to his nickname with his seventh TD catch of the season
-True freshman
Vincent Carner
had his best game as a Rebel, catching three passes for 58 yards
-LB
Cam Santee
led all tacklers with 12, including 2.5 TFL and a sack
-
Matt Choules
averaged 49 yards on six punts, including a career-long 70-yarder
UP NEXT:
UNLV closes out its road schedule with a visit to San Jose State next Saturday. Kickoff is set for 4 p.m. PT and the game will be shown nationally by CBS Sports Network.
