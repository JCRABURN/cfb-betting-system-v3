BOWLING GREEN, Ky. –
WKU Football will travel to Lynchburg, Virginia, to face No. 22 Liberty on Saturday, Nov. 21. Kickoff at Williams Stadium is scheduled for 12 p.m. CT and the game will be streamed on ESPN+.

ABOUT THE TOPS
LAST TIME OUT:
WKU scored twice in the final 2 seconds to defeat Jax State 34-24 in its annual Homecoming game on Saturday, Nov. 14, at Houchens-Smith Stadium.
Brock Glenn
connected with
Jyzaiah Rockwell
on a 10-yard touchdown pass with two seconds remaining to give the Hilltoppers a 28-24 lead. Safety
Dave Herard
then returned a fumble recovery 2 yards for a touchdown as time expired to seal the victory. WKU improved to 4-6 overall and 3-3 in Conference USA play while handing the Gamecocks (7-3, 5-1 CUSA) their first conference loss of the season.
TRUE FRESHMAN STANDOUTS:
WKU has seen several true freshmen standouts in the starting lineup this season – receiver
Jonathan Stafford Jr.
and linebacker
Westen Ard
for much of the season, and recently safety
Jaylen Pollard
. Stafford has been the winner of three CUSA Freshman of the Week awards. He has a team-leading 56 receptions for 537 yards this season. Stafford ranks second in program history in receptions for a freshman. Stafford trails only Malachi Corley on the list. Corley caught 73 passes during the 2021 season. Stafford is third among freshmen in program history for receiving yards in a season, trailing only Corley (691 yards; 2021) and Jay Davis (612 yards; 1968). Stafford is 29th nationally in receptions, and he is third among FBS freshmen in the category. Stafford was the first WKU player to record at least three catches in his first nine games with WKU since Jerreth Sterns in 2021. True freshman linebacker
Westen Ard
has started the last eight games for the Hilltoppers, ranks second on the team in total tackles with 63 and is coming off a CUSA Freshman of the Week performance against Jax State. He is second on the team in tackles for loss with 6.0, and his 3.0 sacks are a team-best. He's added three pass breakups and three quarterback hurries. He ranks 10th in CUSA in tackles per game with 7.0 – best among league freshmen and second nationally among true freshmen. Pollard has started back-to-back games at safety and is now up to 19 tackles, with 0.5 tackles for loss, over 10 games this season.
CUSA COMPETITION:
WKU Football has gone 43-18 in regular-season CUSA games since
Tyson Helton
took over the program in 2019. The Hilltoppers have gone 6-2 in CUSA play the last two seasons leading into 2026 and have finished with a winning record in conference play in each of the seven seasons under Helton. WKU is 247-154-9 all-time against conference opponents in its history.
WHAT YOU DO IN NOVEMBER, THEY REMEMBER:
November has been the most successful month for the Hilltoppers in seven seasons under Helton. WKU owns a 20-9 record in November under Helton, including a 19-7 record against CUSA competition. WKU has lost multiple conference games in November under Helton only once – in 2024, but the Hilltoppers won the finale against Jax State that year to earn a berth in the CUSA Championship Game. WKU has gone 1-1 this November, with a loss at Middle Tennessee and a win over Jax State.
TESTED ON THE ROAD:
The Hilltoppers will be playing in their seventh road game of the season when they face No. 22 Liberty on Saturday. WKU started off the 2026 campaign with three straight road games – at Nevada on Sept. 5, at No. 2 Georgia on Sept. 12 and at No. 4 Indiana on Sept. 19 – and also played at New Mexico State on Oct. 1, at Sam Houston on Oct. 14 and at Middle Tennessee on Nov. 7. The Hilltoppers played in four different time zones through the first five weeks of the season. The Hilltoppers were one of only five programs nationally with three straight road games this season, and they were the only school that had three straight road games without a bye week during that stretch. The Hilltoppers were also the only FBS program scheduled to play multiple nonconference games against teams that were ranked in the Top 10 of the AP Preseason Poll. The rest of CUSA had one such game combined. With the 2026 games at No. 2 Georgia, at No. 4 Indiana and now at No. 22 Liberty, WKU has faced multiple ranked FBS teams in the AP poll during the same season for the second time in program history – the Hilltoppers faced three teams in the AP Top 25 during the 2008 season at the time of the meeting.
QUOTABLE
"Yeah, excited about this week. Get to play a great Liberty team, and guys will be very excited. This is a big game for us and an opportunity to keep ourselves alive and in things. And so, there's a lot to play for this week. Whenever you play a really good opponent like a Liberty team, it should elevate your standard of play. And so, we're just anxious to get started for the week and be ready to try to get a big win." – WKU Football Head Coach
Tyson Helton
.
SERIES INFORMATION
Saturday's meeting will be the sixth all-time between WKU and Liberty and the third in Lynchburg, Virginia. The Flames lead the series 5-0, including 2-0 since both teams have been in Conference USA. Liberty claimed the last meeting 38-21 on Nov. 23, 2024, at Williams Stadium.
SCOUTING THE FLAMES
Liberty enters the game with a 9-1 overall record and a 6-0 record in CUSA play. The Flames have won nine consecutive games after dropping the opener at James Madison 20-13. Liberty's wins have come against Gardner-Webb, Ball State, Coastal Carolina, Delaware, Sam Houston, Kennesaw State, FIU, New Mexico State and Middle Tennessee.
Liberty was ranked No. 22 in the latest Associated Press poll.
The Flames are in the fourth season under Head Coach Jamey Chadwell, who holds a 34-14 record leading the program and a 132-71 record in 17 seasons as a head coach.
Liberty ranks first in CUSA in both scoring offense and scoring defense. The Flames have scored 32.8 points per game and have allowed only 14.5 points per game this season.
