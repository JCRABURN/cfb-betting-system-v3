Washington Defeats Michigan State, 21-14

Post-Game Notes
Share:
EAST LANSING, Mich. –
Michigan State fell to Washington on Saturday night at Spartan Stadium, 21-14. Tied at 14-14 late in the fourth quarter, Washington rushed for the game-winning 1-yard touchdown with 2:24 to play.
Graduate quarterback
Cam Fancher
was 14-for-22 passing for a team-best 134 yards and a touchdown. Redshirt sophomore quarterback
Alessio Milivojevic
made the start finishing 7-for-12 with 108 yards passing before leaving the game in the second quarter with an injury.
Junior running back
Brandon Tullis
led the team in rushing for the second straight game, carrying the ball 21 times for 82 yards. Redshirt junior wide receiver
Fredrick Moore
finished with four receptions for 66 yards, and redshirt freshman wide receiver
Bryson Williams
had four catches for 63 yards and a touchdown.
Senior linebacker
Dion Crawford
recorded six tackles to lead the Spartans, while fifth-year safety
Nikai Martinez
posted five stops. Graduate edge
Kenny Soares Jr.
had a sack and redshirt sophomore edge
Trey Lisle
tallied a tackle for loss. Redshirt junior defensive lineman
Eli Coenen
had two pass breakups at the line of scrimmage.
"First and foremost, I want to give Coach (Jedd) Fisch and the Huskies great credit," said MSU head coach
Pat Fitzgerald
. "They came in here and beat us. It's a great credit to them, especially with the tough trip they had—being delayed yesterday and hitting town in the wee hours of the morning. We knew they were a very talented, well-coached team, and they lived up to the billing today.
"We cannot expect to win football games and try to beat two teams. You can't try to beat your opponent when you beat yourself, and that's exactly what happened today. That's my responsibility. I thought we had this group ready to compete to win, and the self-inflicted wounds were too many to even list—from the penalties to the mistakes on offense, mistakes on defense, and penalties and mistakes in the kicking game. Inexcusable on my part.
"I'm bitterly disappointed for our seniors. But I want to say thank you, first and foremost, to our student body. I want to say thank you to our fans. Your support has been incredible. We haven't been in Spartan Stadium in a month, and for you to show up and help us out like you did, we greatly appreciate it. I can't even put into words how thankful I am for that, though bitterly disappointed in the outcome of the game."
SCORING SUMMARY
Michigan State marched right down the field on its first possession for an opening touchdown, going 75 yards on seven plays, capped by a 1-yard rushing TD by
Carson Gulker
, his first as a Spartan.
Washington (6-4, 3-4 Big Ten) tied the game on its second series, as Demond Williams Jr. connected on a 58-yard touchdown pass to Chris Lawson with 28 seconds left in the first quarter. The Huskies took a 14-7 lead midway through the second quarter on Williams' second TD throw to Lawson, this time from 17 yards out to finish a seven-play, 87-yard drive.
The Spartans tied it back up at 14 with a late scoring drive before halftime as
Cam Fancher
found
Bryson Williams
in the back of the end zone on fourth-and-goal from the 2. Fancher replaced starting quarterback
Alessio Milivojevic
on the scoring drive after Milivojevic left the game with an injury.
On its second series of the third quarter, MSU drove the ball to the UW 17-yard line, but Fancher threw an interception while trying to elude a sack, ending the Spartan scoring threat.
Early in the fourth quarter, the Spartans recovered a fumbled punt on the UW 33, however, they couldn't convert the turnover into points as
Liam Boyd
missed a 50-yard field goal wide right.
Late in the fourth, Washington took a 21-14 lead with an eight-play, 78-yard drive capped off by a 1-yard touchdown rush by Jayden Limar with 2:24 to play.
Running the two-minute drill, Michigan State drove down the field in the game's final moments, converting on a pair of fourth down opportunities to extend the drive with under a minute to play. Tight end
Carson Gulker
caught a short pass in Washington territory with 46 seconds remaining, but a forced fumble by the Huskies ended Michigan State's drive and sealed the victory for UW.
UP NEXT
Michigan State (3-7, 1-6) hosts Oregon on Senior Day Friday, Nov. 20 at 8 p.m. in Spartan Stadium. The game will be televised on FOX.
