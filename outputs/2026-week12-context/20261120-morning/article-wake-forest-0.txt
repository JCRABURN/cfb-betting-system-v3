Kickoff Time for Senior Day Against Duke Announced for 3 or 3:30 p.m.
11/16/2026 1:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
The No. 13 Demon Deacons will face the Blue Devils in their regular season finale at Allegacy Financial Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 28, at either 3 or 3:30 p.m. on either ESPN, ESPN2 or ACC Network.
By:
Stephen Czarda, Andrew Stern and KJ Smith
Share:
CHARLOTTE, N.C. –
The Atlantic Coast Conference has announced kickoff time and broadcast information for Week 13 of the 2026 season. No. 13 Wake Forest will face Big Four rival Duke on Senior Day on Saturday, Nov. 28, with kickoff time at Allegacy Financial Stadium set for either 3 or 3:30 p.m. on either ESPN, ESPN2 or ACC Network.
Time and network designations will be confirmed following the conclusion of the games on Saturday, Nov. 21.
This marks the 106th all-time meeting between the Demon Deacons and the Blue Devils, with the series dating back to 1889.
The Deacs will be looking for their first victory over the Blue Devils since they earned a 45-7 win at Allegacy Stadium during the 2021 season.
