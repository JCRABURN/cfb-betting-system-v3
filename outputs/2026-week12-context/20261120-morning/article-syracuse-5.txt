Orange Set to Battle Boston College Saturday
11/16/2026 3:24:00 PM
|
Football
Get Tickets
Coach Fran Press Conference
Share:
Syracuse is set to play its final road game of the regular season on Saturday afternoon when it travels to Chestnut Hill, Mass., to face Boston College. The game is slated for a noon kickoff and will air on ACC Network.
The Orange are coming off a 34-31 win over NC State where they scored 21 points in the fourth quarter to storm back for a victory. Head coach
Fran Brown
is ready to turn the page to this weekend's test, saying his team will face a very talented Boston College roster.
"We have a big week ahead of us, playing a really good football team," Brown said. "Going against this team, you throw records out of the window. It's going to be good football, backyard football. It's going to be a lot of fun going up there to compete against them in their home. It's a great atmosphere there and I'm excited to go and play against coach Bill O'Brien."
Boston College's offense is led by quarterback Mason McKenzie, who has racked up 1,667 yards passing in addition to his team-leading 686 rushing yards. Brown said he's a dynamic player and will test his defense this weekend.
"This kid is dynamic," Brown said. "He's all over the football field. He's a highlight tape just running around and making plays. He's intelligent and you can see that he leads. You can see that the offensive line protects him and means a lot to them. You can tell they have a leader on their football team."
Since the start of Brown's tenure, the Orange are 12-6 in games decided by one score. Syracuse's 12 wins over that stretch are tied for the second-most of any FBS team. The 2026 campaign has seen the Orange play in a national-high eight such games, including seven in a row. Syracuse added another to that list last weekend with the three-point triumph over NC State. Brown said his team's ability to win close games comes down to living in the moment and taking everything one day at a time.
"We just want to win the day, and they are consistently winning the day," Brown said. "You just fall back on your training from practice and all of the things that we work on, it helps us in the games a lot. We're staying as focused as much as possible and living in the moment and not to worry about the ending outcome during that moment. They better live in the moment today because we have a big opponent that wants to play us this weekend."
Syracuse saw freshman wide receiver
Calvin Russell III
tie the ACC and Syracuse single-game receptions record with 17 in the win. He finished with 137 yards and a touchdown. It wasn't just Russell who delivered in the receiver room, though, with
Umari Hatcher
finishing with 81 receiving yards and
Justus Ross-Simmons
catching the go-ahead touchdown and two-point conversion in the final minute.
Brown said chemistry, competitiveness and facing adversity head on has helped the entire receiver room flourish this season.
"They do a great job and they've developed a really good friendship in there," Brown said. "They really like it here and they compete. They love the way that we practice. When adversity hit them and things weren't going well for them, they continued to work. I hope they do the same thing this weekend."
The matchup with Boston College will serve as the 60th all time meeting between the two schools. The Eagles are the Orange's fifth-most frequent opponent all-time, trailing just West Virginia (61 games), Colgate (69 games), Penn State (71 games) and Pittsburgh (82 games).
After the Orange's game with Boston College, they'll return home for the regular-season finale, where they'll clash with Notre Dame. For tickets, visit
Cuse.com/FootballTickets
