"4 Downs" with WKU
#1
WKU handed Jax State its first CUSA loss last Saturday by scoring 13 points in the game's final two seconds. This week, the Hilltoppers will attempt to send Liberty to its first CUSA setback and record their first-ever win over the Flames. A win would also keep WKU's bowl hopes alive. The Hilltoppers have appeared in a bowl for seven straight seasons under Tyson Helton.
#2
Liberty is facing Western Kentucky for the first time in two years (since Nov. 23, 2024). The Flames have played 15 CUSA games against 10 different opponents since then, going 9-6 in those contests. Saturday's game will be the Flames' 218th contest at Williams Stadium. If Liberty wins, the Flames would be exactly 100 games over .500 (159-59) all-time at the facility, which opened on Oct. 21, 1989.
#3
Liberty has only allowed one opposing QB to throw for 300 yards since the start of 2024 (Delaware's Nick Minicucci in 2025).
Chase Reeves
is 14-for-14 on field goals during his first season as a Flame, setting a program record for field goals made without a miss. The NCAA and CUSA records for most FGs made without a miss in a season are 21, set by Cairo Santos of Tulane in 2012.
#4
The Flames are 9-0 this season and 33-4 under
Jamey Chadwell
when outrushing their opponents. Liberty is coming off a season-high 370-yard rushing effort against Middle Tennessee. That marked the team's most rushing yards since the last time WKU visited Lynchburg (Nov. 23, 2024). That day, the Flames churned out 419 yards on the ground in a 38-21 Senior Day win.
A LIBERTY WIN WOULD...
Enable the Flames to clinch at least a share of their second CUSA regular-season title (also 2023). If Jax State also loses to Missouri State, Liberty would claim the outright title and the right to host the Dec. 4 championship game.
Give the Flames their first 10-game winning streak since Liberty opened the 2023 season with 13 consecutive victories (Sept. 2-Dec. 1, 2023). It would be the fifth winning streak of 10+ contests in program history.
Mark the fourth time in 54 seasons as a football program the Flames have recorded 10 or more victories, including the third 10+ win campaign in the last seven years.
Improve the Flames' record to 6-0 all-time against WKU, tying St. Paul's as Liberty's most frequent opponent which it has never lost to on the gridiron.
Make the Flames the first CUSA team since WKU (2015-16) with two 10-win seasons within their first four seasons as a CUSA member.
Give the Flames their eighth regular season unbeaten at Williams Stadium and second under
Jamey Chadwell
.
Home Sweet Home
Liberty has posted a 21-4 record inside Williams Stadium under fourth-year Head Coach
Jamey Chadwell
.
The Flames are 5-0 at home this season, outscoring opponents by a combined 187-74.
Liberty is looking for its eighth season going unbeaten at home in the regular season, as the Flames went 7-0 in regular-season home games in 2023, 6-0 in 1979, 2007 and 2020 and 5-0 in 1992, 2010 and 2025.
VS. Bowl Teams
Western Kentucky beat Southern Miss in the 2025 New Orleans Bowl. WKU is one out of seven teams on Liberty's schedule who competed in bowl games last year.
James Madison - College Football Playoff (L to Oregon)
Coastal Carolina - Independence Bowl (L to LA Tech)
Delaware - 68 Ventures Bowl (Beat Louisiana)
Kennesaw State - Myrtle Beach Bowl (L to WMU)
FIU - First Responder Bowl (L to UTSA)
WKU - New Orleans Bowl (Beat Southern Miss)
Missouri State - Xbox Bowl (L to Arkansas State)
On Winning Streaks
The Flames have posted their first nine-game winning streak since Liberty won each of its first 13 games under current Head Coach
Jamey Chadwell
in 2023.
It is the fifth winning streak of nine games or more in program history and second under Chadwell.
Liberty's nine-game winning streak is tied with Boise State for third longest active winning streak among FBS members, trailing only Utah (16) and UCLA (10).

5-0 vs. WKU
Liberty has posted a perfect 5-0 record all-time against WKU in football. WKU is the only Division I opponent Liberty has faced 5+ times without a loss.
St. Paul's (6-0) and Concord (5-0) are the only other opponents which Liberty has faced five times or more while winning every meeting.
Coaching Matchup
Eighth-year WKU Head Coach Tyson Helton was named CUSA Coach of the Year in his first season at the helm, 2019. Meanwhile, fourth-year Liberty Head Coach
Jamey Chadwell
was named CUSA Coach of the Year in 2023, his first year coaching the Flames.
Chadwell's Flames have beaten Helton's Hilltoppers by double-digits in both meetings (42-29 at WKU/2023; 38-21 vs. WKU/2024).
