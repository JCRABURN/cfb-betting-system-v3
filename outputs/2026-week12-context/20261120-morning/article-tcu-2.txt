NOTING THE GAME
TCU closes out the regular season home slate with a visit from No. 2 Utah. The Utes are the highest-ranked opponent to visit Amon G. Carter Stadium since No. 2 Texas in 1970.
A win at home on Saturday would give the Frogs their fifth home win of the season and consecutive seasons with five home victories for the first time since 2014-15 (7-0, 6-0). The Frogs were 5-1 at home last season.
The game also marks the first home kickoff with true night conditions at the start of the game since hosting Oklahoma State on Nov. 9, 2024. An 8 p.m. kick is the latest home kick off since hosting Northwestern at Thursday, Sept. 2, 2004 at 8:30 p.m.
TCU has hosted just two undefeated teams in November since joining the Big 12 - falling both times to No. 11 Baylor (2019) and No. 3 Kansas State (2012).

NOTING THE HORNED FROGS
TCU is in its fifth season under the direction of head coach Sonny Dykes. Dykes is
one of eight active head coaches
to have appeared in the CFP National Championship game, along with Kirby Smart, Dabo Swinney, Ryan Day, Marcus Freeman, Kalen DeBoer, Mario Cristobal, and Curt Cignetti. Dykes and Cignetti are the only two coaches in CFP history to take over a losing program and direct them to the CFP in their first season.
In year three under defensive coordinator Andy Avalos, the Horned Frog defense has been elite. The unit currently leads the Big 12 in
Third Down Defense (32.4%)
,
First Down Defense (16.8) and Scoring Defense (15.2)
. Through 10 games, TCU's defense has allowed
just 152 points
, the fewest by a TCU defense through 10 games in the Big 12 era and fewest since the 2010 Rose Bowl team's defense allowed just 85 through 10 games.
TCU's tight end room has been one of the best in school history this season. In the past 20 years, no tight end group has recorded as many receptions (73) or yards (862) as this year's group while their eight combined touchdowns are the second most.
TCU is riding the
second-longest scoring streak in NCAA history at 431 games
. The Frogs have not been shutout since facing Texas on Nov. 16, 1991. Only Florida (483 games) has a longer streak.
Since 2022, TCU has posted 41 total victories, the fourth-most in the Big 12 over that span. Only Texas Tech, Utah, and BYU (all at 44) have more.
When TCU doesn't turn the ball over, the Frogs win. Generally. Under Sonny Dykes,
TCU is 33-5 when winning or even in turnover margin
and 8-17 when losing the turnover battle.
