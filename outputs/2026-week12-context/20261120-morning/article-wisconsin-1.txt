Lahm named a Lou Groza Semifinalist
November 17, 2026
|
Football
Share:
Badger kicker has converted 15-of-17 FGs this season
MADISON, Wis. –
Wisconsin 5th-year kicker
Gavin Lahm
has been named a Lou Groza Award Semifinalist, as announced by the Palm Beach County Sports Commission on Tuesday.
And the kick is good 🙌 @gavin_lahm has been named a Lou Groza Award Top 20 semifinalist! https://t.co/pIUTNEs0B3
— Wisconsin Football (@BadgerFootball)
November 17, 2026
Lahm has made 15 of his 17 field goal attempts this fall for the Badgers, converting on 88.2% of his attempts. That's the best clip for a Badger kicker since Rafael Gaglianone posted a 88.9% mark in 2017.
Lahm has converted twice from 51 yards this season and has not missed a kick from inside 40 yards (10-for-10). He is one of 20 collegiate place kickers to land on the Groza Award Semifinalist list. Lahm is the first Badger to be named a semifinalist since Philip Welch in 2008. Badger kicker Taylor Mehlhaff won the award in 2006.
Groza Award Semifinalists will be voted on by a panel of more than 100 FBS head coaches, SIDs, media members, former Groza finalists, and current NFL kickers to select the three finalists. These finalists will be announced on November 24th and honored at the 35th annual Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Awards Banquet on December 7th in Palm Beach County. The same panel then selects the winner, who will be announced live on ESPN at the Home Depot College Football Awards on Friday, December 11th.
The Award is named for National Football League Hall of Fame kicker Lou "The Toe" Groza, who played 21 seasons with the Cleveland Browns. Groza won four NFL championships with Cleveland and was named NFL Player of the Year in 1954. Although an All-Pro offensive lineman as well, Groza ushered in the notion that there should be a place on an NFL roster for a kicker.
Since the first Lou Groza Award was handed out in 1992, 34 finalists, including 18 winners, have gone on to appear in the NFL, earning 13 trips to the Pro Bowl and taking home 8 Super Bowls. That list includes 2026 NFL kickers Daniel Carlson Jake Elliott, Ka'imi Fairbairn, Ryan Fitzgerald, Matt Gay, Zane Gonzalez, Joshua Karty, Jake Moody, Will Reichard, Cairo Santos, and Andre Szmyt.
