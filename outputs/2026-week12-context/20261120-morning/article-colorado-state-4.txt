GAME NOTES
Saturday will be the 20th meeting with Fresno State and the fourth time playing in Fresno in the last five games of the series.
Saturday will be the first time all season playing back-to-back road games.
The Rams are 7-3 in the last 10 games against the Bulldogs which includes a three-year break where the two didn't meet.
CSU has lost the last two games in Fresno and hold a 5-5 mark overall while in the Valley.
The two played in the 2008 New Mexico Bowl with the Rams winning a dramatic 40-35 victory.
This will be the fifth game the Rams play an opponent coming off a loss the game before.
