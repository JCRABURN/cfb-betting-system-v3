BOWL ELIGIBILITY SECURED
• Tulsa earned bowl eligibility for the first time since 2021 with a 38-21 Homecoming victory over Florida Atlantic at H.A. Chapman Stadium.
• The Golden Hurricane improved to 6-4 overall and 3-3 in American Conference play, winning its second consecutive game and third in its last four outings.
• Tulsa has outscored its last two opponents 98-31, following a 60-10 road victory at Tulane with the Homecoming win.
• The Golden Hurricane generated 501 yards of offense against Florida Atlantic, including 309 passing and 192 rushing. Tulsa has surpassed 500 yards in consecutive games after producing 562 at Tulane.
• Tulsa scored the game's first 14 points and never trailed. After taking a 21-14 halftime lead, the Golden Hurricane scored 10 unanswered points in the third quarter to extend its advantage to 31-14.
• When Florida Atlantic cut the deficit to 31-21 early in the fourth quarter,
Damari Alston
answered with a 75-yard touchdown run on Tulsa's next offensive play.
• Tulsa finished all three red-zone possessions with touchdowns.
Baylor Hayes
threw two touchdown passes, while
Trequan Jones
scored twice on the ground and Alston added another rushing score.
• The victory was Tulsa's 56th on Homecoming, its first since 2024 and its largest Homecoming victory margin since 2018.
•  With bowl eligibility secured and two regular-season games remaining, Tulsa returns to H.A. Chapman Stadium to host Charlotte on Nov. 21, seeking its seventh victory and third consecutive win.
ONE MORE AT H.A. CHAPMAN STADIUM
• Tulsa hosts Charlotte on Saturday, Nov. 21, in its final home game of the regular season. Kickoff is scheduled for 1 p.m. CT on ESPN+.
• The Golden Hurricane enters the matchup at 6-4 overall and 3-3 in American Conference play after securing bowl eligibility with a 38-21 Homecoming victory over Florida Atlantic.
• Tulsa seeks its third consecutive victory and fourth win in its last five games. The Golden Hurricane has outscored its last two opponents 98-31.
• Saturday marks just the second meeting between Tulsa and Charlotte, with both contests taking place at H.A. Chapman Stadium.
• Charlotte won the inaugural matchup, 33-26 in overtime, on Nov. 4, 2023. Tulsa will look to even the series and earn its first victory over the 49ers.
• The Golden Hurricane has generated more than 500 yards of offense in consecutive games, producing 562 yards at Tulane and 501 against Florida Atlantic.
• Tulsa has scored at least 35 points in five of its six American Conference games this season, averaging 40.2 points against league opponents.
• A victory would give Tulsa seven wins with one regular-season game remaining and move the Golden Hurricane above .500 in American Conference play.
THOMPSON'S COMEBACK EARNS NATIONAL RECOGNITION
• Graduate linebacker
Chris Thompson Jr.
continues to lead Tulsa's defense after missing the entire 2025 season because of injury, totaling a team-high 93 tackles through 10 games.
• Thompson recorded nine tackles, including one tackle for loss, in Tulsa's 38-21 victory over Florida Atlantic, helping the Golden Hurricane secure bowl eligibility for the first time since 2021.
•  He enters the Charlotte game just seven tackles shy of 100 for the season. His total includes 33 solo stops and 60 assists.
•  Thompson ranks third in the American Conference in both total tackles and tackles per game, averaging 9.3 stops.
• His production has been consistent throughout the season, with at least seven tackles in nine of Tulsa's 10 games and four double-digit performances.
• Thompson recorded a season-high 15 tackles against Army, adding two tackles for loss and his first sack of the season. He also registered 14 tackles against North Texas and 10 apiece against Oklahoma State and Rice.
• Through 10 games, Thompson has contributed six tackles for loss, one sack, one fumble recovery and one pass breakup. tulsahurricane.com
•  Thompson was named to the 38-player Comeback Player of the Year Award Midseason Watch List on Nov. 2. He is one of three American Conference players recognized, joining North Texas running back Jahiem White and Tulane linebacker Dickson Agu.
•  He made an immediate impact in his return, earning American Conference Defensive Player of the Week honors after recording 10 tackles, recovering a fumble and contributing to two fourth-down stops in Tulsa's season-opening 24-10 victory over Oklahoma State.
• A team captain in both 2025 and 2026, Thompson has paired his return to competition with a continued leadership role for a Golden Hurricane team that has won six games and three of its last four.
• Presented by College Sports Communicators in association with The Associated Press and Fiesta Sports Foundation, the Comeback Player of the Year Award recognizes student-athletes who overcome injury, illness or other circumstances. Three players will receive the award following the season.
ROSS EXTENDS HIS STREAK TO 29
• Wide receiver
Javon Ross
recorded his first 100-yard receiving game with the Golden Hurricane, catching six passes for a season-high 163 yards in Tulsa's 38-21 victory over Florida Atlantic.
• His 163 yards surpassed his previous Tulsa best of 90 at Navy and accounted for 52.8 percent of the Golden Hurricane's 309 passing yards.
• Ross averaged 27.2 yards per reception, providing several key gains that helped Tulsa produce its second consecutive 500-yard offensive performance.
• His 38-yard reception helped set up Tulsa's opening touchdown, a five-yard pass from
Baylor Hayes
to
Jimmy Calloway
.
• Ross added a 41-yard catch during Tulsa's opening scoring possession of the third quarter.
Trequan Jones
capped that drive with a two-yard touchdown run to extend the lead to 28-14.
• He later contributed a 26-yard reception during the drive that ended with
Owen Carey's
41-yard field goal, giving Tulsa a 31-14 advantage.
• Ross extended his consecutive-games reception streak to 29, spanning his careers at Bethune-Cookman and Tulsa. He has caught at least one pass in all 10 appearances with the Golden Hurricane.
• He enters the Charlotte game with 35 receptions for 522 yards and three receiving touchdowns, averaging 14.9 yards per catch.
• Ross has totaled 12 receptions for 279 yards over his last three games, averaging 23.3 yards per catch against Army, Tulane and Florida Atlantic.
• His contributions extend beyond the passing game. Ross scored rushing touchdowns against both Army and Tulane, including an 11-yard score in the Golden Hurricane's 60-10 road victory.
• Ross will look to extend his reception streak to 30 consecutive games when Tulsa hosts Charlotte in its home finale.
• His streak is tied for 10th nationally with UTSA's David Amador II with 25 or more consecutive games with a reception.

OFFENSE DELIVERING IN CONFERENCE PLAY
• Tulsa has scored 241 points in six American Conference games, averaging 40.2 points per contest.
• The Golden Hurricane has scored at least 35 points in five of its six conference games, including 38 against North Texas, 38 at Rice, 35 against Army, 60 at Tulane and 38 against Florida Atlantic.
• Tulsa has outscored Tulane and Florida Atlantic 98-31 during its two-game winning streak, averaging 49.0 points and 531.5 yards of total offense.
• The Golden Hurricane has surpassed 500 yards in consecutive games, producing 562 yards at Tulane and 501 against Florida Atlantic. Tulsa combined for 647 passing yards and 416 rushing yards in those victories.
• Quarterback
Baylor Hayes
has passed for at least 200 yards in all six American Conference games, totaling 1,637 yards and 15 touchdown passes—an average of 272.8 passing yards per contest.
• Hayes has thrown multiple touchdown passes in five of those six conference appearances, including a career-high four at Tulane. He followed that performance with 309 yards and two touchdowns against Florida Atlantic.
• Tulsa's receiving depth has produced consecutive standout performances.
Oran Singleton Jr.
caught 13 passes for 177 yards and three touchdowns at Tulane before
Javon Ross
delivered six receptions for 163 yards against Florida Atlantic.
• The running game supplied two explosive touchdowns against Florida Atlantic, with
Trequan Jones
scoring from 78 yards and
Damari Alston
adding a 75-yard score. The pair combined for 180 rushing yards and three touchdowns.
• Tulsa has converted all 10 red-zone possessions into points over its last two games, finishing nine with touchdowns and one with a field goal.
• The Golden Hurricane enters the Charlotte matchup seeking its third consecutive conference victory after winning three of its last four American Conference games.
SINGLETON ADDS TO AWARD-WATCH SEASON
• Wide receiver
Oran Singleton Jr.
continues to lead Tulsa's passing attack, entering the Charlotte game with team highs of 63 receptions, 775 receiving yards and seven touchdown catches.
• Singleton followed his selection to the Biletnikoff Award Watch List with seven receptions for 63 yards in Tulsa's 38-21 victory over Florida Atlantic, helping the Golden Hurricane secure bowl eligibility.
• He has recorded at least five receptions in seven consecutive games, providing a consistent target throughout Tulsa's American Conference schedule.
• Singleton ranks third in the American Conference in receptions per game (6.3) and fourth in receiving yards per game (77.5) through games of Nov. 14.
• Over his last six games, Singleton has collected 48 receptions for 573 yards, averaging eight catches and 95.5 receiving yards per contest.
• His season includes two 100-yard receiving performances: 11 catches for 104 yards against North Texas and 13 receptions for 177 yards at Tulane. He also finished one yard shy of the milestone with 99 yards in his Tulsa debut against Oklahoma State.
• Singleton delivered his most productive outing at Tulane, establishing career highs in receptions (13), receiving yards (177) and touchdown catches (3). His 13 receptions also established a Yulman Stadium single-game record.
• That performance earned Singleton a place on the Biletnikoff Award Watch List on Nov. 9. The award recognizes college football's outstanding FBS receiver, regardless of position.
• Singleton has contributed beyond catching passes, throwing a 10-yard touchdown pass to
David Wells Jr.
at Navy. He also added 22 rushing yards against North Texas, finishing that game with 126 yards from scrimmage.
• He needs 25 receiving yards to reach 800 for the season and enters the Charlotte game averaging 12.3 yards per reception
BACKFIELD PROVIDES QUICK STRIKES
• Running backs
Damari Alston
and
Trequan Jones
combined for 180 rushing yards and three touchdowns on 20 carries in Tulsa's 38-21 victory over Florida Atlantic, averaging 9.0 yards per attempt.
• The duo accounted for 180 of Tulsa's 192 rushing yards and all three of the Golden Hurricane's rushing touchdowns.
• Jones finished with 88 yards and two touchdowns on nine carries, averaging 9.8 yards per attempt. His 78-yard touchdown in the second quarter extended Tulsa's lead to 21-7.
• The 78-yard score was Jones' second-longest run this season, trailing his 81-yard touchdown at Sam Houston. He has also scored on runs of 59 and 45 yards this year.
• Jones added a two-yard touchdown during the third quarter, finishing a three-play, 69-yard drive that pushed Tulsa's advantage to 28-14.
• Alston led Tulsa with 92 rushing yards on 11 carries, averaging 8.4 yards per attempt. He also caught three passes for 28 yards, finishing with 120 yards from scrimmage.
• After Florida Atlantic pulled within 31-21 early in the fourth quarter, Alston answered with a 75-yard touchdown on Tulsa's next offensive play. The one-play scoring drive took just 12 seconds.
• Alston's 75-yard run was his longest of the season. Together, Alston and Jones produced 153 yards on their two long touchdown runs against the Owls.
• Alston leads Tulsa with 573 rushing yards and eight rushing touchdowns, while Jones has contributed 520 yards and six scores. Jones is averaging 6.8 yards per carry.
• The pair enters the Charlotte game with a combined 1,093 rushing yards and 14 rushing touchdowns, giving the Golden Hurricane two backs with more than 500 rushing yards this season.
PRESSURE COMES FROM ACROSS THE FRONT
• Tulsa recorded three sacks against Florida Atlantic, increasing its season total to 27 through 10 games—four more than the Golden Hurricane recorded during the entire 2025 season.
• The Golden Hurricane ranks third in the American Conference in total sacks, trailing East Carolina's 29 and Memphis' 28. Tulsa's average of 2.70 sacks per game ranks third in the conference and tied for 27th nationally.
• Tulsa has increased its sacks-per-game average from 1.92 in 2025 to 2.70 this season, an improvement of approximately 41 percent.
• The Golden Hurricane has recorded nine sacks during its two-game winning streak, following a six-sack performance at Tulane with three against Florida Atlantic.
• Tulsa's 27 sacks have resulted in 197 yards lost by opposing offenses, the second-highest sack-yardage total in the American Conference behind East Carolina's 213.
•
Thomas Davis
led the pass rush against Florida Atlantic with 1.5 sacks, while
Kyran Duhon
,
Jaylen George
and
Arias Nash
each contributed a half-sack.
• Duhon leads Tulsa with 7.5 sacks, tying him for second in the American Conference. Davis ranks fourth with seven, giving the Golden Hurricane two of the conference's top four players in total sacks.
• Duhon and Davis have combined for 14.5 sacks, accounting for more than half of Tulsa's season total.
Dax Collins
has added 2.5 sacks, giving the Golden Hurricane three defenders with multiple sacks.
• Davis has recorded three sacks over the last two games, contributing 1.5 against both Tulane and Florida Atlantic. His seven sacks this season have more than doubled the three he recorded at Appalachian State in 2025.
FINISHING DRIVES IN THE RED ZONE
• Tulsa has scored on 34 of its 38 red-zone possessions this season, an 89.5-percent success rate, with 30 touchdowns and four field goals.
• The Golden Hurricane has finished 78.9 percent of its red-zone trips with touchdowns, an improvement of 24.1 percentage points from its 54.8-percent touchdown rate in 2025.
• Tulsa has scored on all 10 red-zone possessions during its two-game winning streak, producing nine touchdowns and one field goal against Tulane and Florida Atlantic.
• The Golden Hurricane went 7-for-7 in the red zone at Tulane, finishing six possessions with touchdowns in the 60-10 victory.
• Tulsa followed that performance by scoring touchdowns on all three red-zone trips in its 38-21 win over Florida Atlantic.
• Those three touchdowns against the Owls came on
Baylor Hayes'
five- and nine-yard passes to
Jimmy Calloway
and
Trequan Jones'
two-yard run.
• Tulsa has converted its last two games' red-zone opportunities into 66 points, including extra points, helping the Golden Hurricane outscore its opponents 98-31 during that stretch.
• The ability to finish drives has helped Tulsa score at least 35 points in five of its six American Conference games. The Golden Hurricane is averaging 40.2 points per game in conference play.
TAKEAWAYS KEEP TULSA AMONG THE NATIONAL LEADERS
• Tulsa has forced 18 turnovers through 10 games, ranking second in the American Conference and tied for 12th nationally in takeaways.
• The Golden Hurricane has collected 10 interceptions and eight fumble recoveries, trailing only Memphis' 19 total takeaways among conference teams.
• Tulsa shares the American Conference lead in fumble recoveries with Memphis and South Florida and is tied for second in interceptions with East Carolina.
• The Golden Hurricane has already surpassed its 2025 total of 11 takeaways by seven and doubled last season's four fumble recoveries.
• Tulsa has paired its takeaway production with eight giveaways, producing an overall turnover margin of plus-10. Its average margin of plus-1.00 per game leads the American Conference and ranks seventh nationally. d1sportsnet.com
•
Zach Williams
supplied Tulsa's latest takeaway with a fourth-quarter interception against Florida Atlantic, ending a drive that had reached the Golden Hurricane's 11-yard line.
• Tulsa has intercepted three passes during its two-game winning streak.
JD Drew
and
NuNu Campbell
recorded interceptions at Tulane before Williams added his against Florida Atlantic.
DUHON ENTERS TULSA'S TOP 10; DAVIS CLOSE BEHIND
• Defensive lineman
Kyran Duhon
enters the Charlotte game with a team-leading eight sacks, tying him for 10th on Tulsa's single-season list alongside Sedric Clark (1994), Tyrunn Walker (2011) and Trevis Gipson (2019).
• Duhon needs just a half-sack to reach 8.5 and join James Lockett (2008), Shawn Jackson (2010) and Ben Kopenski (2023) in a tie for seventh.
• One additional sack would give Duhon nine this season, matching the fifth-place total shared by Alain Karapetyan (2007) and DeAundre Brown (2012).
• Duhon has established a collegiate single-season career high, surpassing the seven sacks he recorded as a freshman at UTEP. He now has 15 career sacks across his time at UTEP, Oklahoma State and Tulsa.
•
Thomas Davis
is close behind with seven sacks, leaving him one shy of joining Duhon in Tulsa's single-season top 10.
• Davis has recorded 1.5 sacks in each of Tulsa's last two games, contributing three during victories over Tulane and Florida Atlantic.
• Duhon and Davis have combined for 15 sacks this season, giving the Golden Hurricane two productive pass rushers with opportunities to climb the program's record list.
• Davis' seven sacks have more than doubled the three he recorded at Appalachian State in 2025, while Duhon's season includes two-sack performances against Sam Houston and Rice.
• With two regular-season games remaining and bowl eligibility secured, both players have additional opportunities to build on their production and place their names among Tulsa's most accomplished single-season pass rushers.

RECRUIT LOCAL
• Tulsa's 2026 roster features 27 players from the Tulsa metropolitan area, demonstrating the Golden Hurricane's commitment to recruiting and retaining local talent.
• The group includes 17 players whose listed hometown is Tulsa, four from Owasso, three from Bixby and one each from Collinsville, Jenks and Mannford.
• Local players represent every phase of the roster, with Tulsa-area talent appearing on offense, defense and special teams.
• The group includes players from several of the area's most successful high school programs, including Bixby, Booker T. Washington, Cascia Hall, Collinsville, Holland Hall, Jenks, Metro Christian, Owasso, Rejoice Christian and Union.
• Tulsa's local contingent includes returning Golden Hurricane players as well as student-athletes who returned home after beginning their collegiate careers elsewhere.
• Head coach
Tre Lamb
and his staff continue to prioritize relationships with area high school coaches while providing local players an opportunity to compete at the FBS level in their hometown region.
WHAT YOU DIDN'T KNOW ABOUT TULSA'S FOOTBALL TRADITION
•
New Year's Day Bowl Streak:
Tulsa was the first school to play in five consecutive New Year's Day bowl games: Sun Bowl (1942), Sugar Bowl (1943, 1944), Orange Bowl (1945), and Oil Bowl (1946).
•
Ellis Jones:
An All-American guard in 1945, Jones played with one arm and was a standout on the College All-Star team vs. the Chicago Bears.
• Pro Football Hall of Fame:
Four former Tulsa players are enshrined: Bob St. Clair, Jim Finks, Steve Largent, and Drew Pearson.
•
Heisman Runners-Up:
Jerry Rhome (1964) and Howard Twilley (1965) finished second in Heisman Trophy voting.
•
Glenn Dobbs (1940–42):
Considered Tulsa's greatest player, Dobbs also coached the Hurricane in the 1960s. Eighth Street near H.A. Chapman Stadium is Glenn Dobbs Drive, dedicated in 2000. A statue of Dobbs was dedicated before the 2013 spring game.
•
Zaven Collins (2020):
Consensus All-America linebacker who won the Chuck Bednarik and Bronko Nagurski awards as college football's top defender.
•
NFL Head Coaches:
Four former Tulsa players have served as NFL head coaches: Tommy Hudspeth (Detroit Lions), Sam Rutigliano (Cleveland Browns), Ray Rhodes (Philadelphia Eagles/Green Bay Packers), and Lovie Smith (Chicago Bears/Tampa Bay Buccaneers/Houston Texans).
•
College Football Hall of Fame:
Tulsa has three former players, two former head coaches, and two former assistant coaches inducted:
• Players:
Glenn Dobbs (1940–42, 1961–68), Howard Twilley (1963–65), Jerry Rhome (1963–64)
•
Head Coaches:
Francis Schmidt (1919–21), John Cooper (1977–84)
•
Assistant Coaches:
Darrell Royal (1951), Sammy Baugh (1963)
