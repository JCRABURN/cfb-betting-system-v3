Preview: LA Tech vs. Arkansas State
11/19/2026 3:42:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
The Bulldogs host the Red Wolves at Joe Aillet Stadium for Senior Day on Saturday, Nov. 21.
By:
Drew Parsley
RUSTON --
Louisiana Tech returns home on Saturday as the Bulldogs welcome Arkansas State for their final home game of the regular season, celebrating Senior Day at Origin Bank Field at Joe Aillet Stadium.
GAMEDAY INFORMATION
Date:
Saturday, Nov. 21
Time:
6 p.m. CT
Location:
Ruston, La. (Origin Bank Field at Joe Aillet Stadium)
TV:
ESPN+
Live Stats:
LATechSports.com/Stats
Radio:
Z107.5 | LA Tech Sports Network | LA Tech Athletics App
SERIES HISTORY
Series:
LA Tech leads 26-12
In Ruston:
LA Tech leads 13-4
Streak:
W3
The Bulldogs (8-2, 6-0 SBC) enter Saturday riding a seven-game winning streak after clinching the Sun Belt Conference West Division championship with a 63-31 victory over Southern Miss last weekend. Louisiana Tech secured its first conference championship game appearance since 2016 and became the first program to earn a berth in the Sun Belt Championship Game during its inaugural season as a league member since the game's inception in 2018.
The Red Wolves (4-6, 2-4 SBC) have dropped their previous two contests at Coastal Carolina, and at home against ULM. They had won three of their five prior games after starting the season with a 1-2 mark through the first three games of the season.
Saturday will be the 29th all-time meeting between LA Tech and Arkansas State, and the first matchup in Ruston since 1997, when the 'Dogs took over the Red Wolves in Ruston with a final of 42-14. LA Tech enters Saturday with a three-game winning streak over Arkansas State and have taken 10 of the last 11 matchups.
This will be the first meeting since LA Tech defeated ASU in the 2015 New Orleans Bowl 47-28. The last time the Red Wolves beat the Bulldogs in Ruston dates back to 1988 with a final of 31-22.
WHAT'S AT STAKE / A LA TECH WIN…
Would be
Sonny Cumbie
's 28th coaching win at LA Tech and 30th of his coaching career
Would give LA Tech its first 7-0 start in conference play as a Division I FBS member
Would extend LA Tech's winning streak to seven games—the first such streak since 2019
Would give LA Tech its first 9-win season since 2019
Would make LA Tech 12-2 in their last 14 games—the first time recording 12 wins in a 14-game span since 2019-2020
Would give LA Tech its first 6-0 record at home in a single season since 2019 and third since 1973
CHAMPIONSHIP BOUND!
With their 63-31 win over Southern Miss, the Bulldogs clinched a berth in the Sun Belt Championship Game on December 4 in their first season as an SBC member. The Bulldogs are the first SBC team to reach the conference championship game in their first year of membership since the SBC Championship Game's inception in 2018. It is also the first time the Bulldogs will play in a conference championship game since 2016, when they were Conference USA West Division champions. LA Tech has not won a conference championship since 2011 as members of the Western Athletic Conference (WAC) as well as never having won a conference championship game they played in.
BULLDOG OFFENSE STAYS HOT
Louisiana Tech's offense has been hot all season long, ranking among the top five in the Sun Belt in passing efficiency (1st - 166.90), completion percentage (1st - 70.90%), passing yards/attempt (1st - 8.46), offensive touchdowns (2nd - 45), total touchdowns (2nd - 50), scoring offense (2nd - 36.5 points/game), passing offense (253 yards/game) and total offense (T-5th - 395.6 yards/game).
After
Eli Finley
posted 104 receiving yards vs. Southern Miss on Nov. 14, the Bulldogs improved to nine total 100-yard receiving performances by four separate players--both of which lead all Sun Belt programs. Finley and
Jalen Mickens
each have three such performances (both tied for the most in the SBC) followed by
Marcus Calwise Jr.
with two.
Talyn Shettron
also contributed with 102 receiving yards vs. UL-Lafayette on Oct. 10.
Quarterback
Blake Baker
enters Week 12 with the fourth-best passing efficiency rating in FBS and the second-best among Group of 6 QBs with a rating of 175.1. His current rating would be the fourth-best in a single season in Sun Belt history with a minimum of 100 pass attempts. Baker also holds the second-best completion percentage among qualifying FBS quarterbacks with a rate of 73%, trailing just Miami's Darian Mensah (76.3%).
'DOGS: A TOP GROUP OF SIX PROGRAM
Louisiana Tech is one of two G6 programs (Boise State) in the country who are currently undefeated when facing other G6 programs.
LA TECH RECEIVING VOTES IN AP POLL
For the first time since 2019, Louisiana Tech has received votes in the AP's College Football Top 25 Poll. The Bulldogs received 14 votes in the Week 11 poll, trailing Oklahoma (95), LSU (91), Duke (62), UTSA (36), New Mexico (32) and South Carolina (31) among teams sitting outside the Top 25.
TECH #TAKERS ON A ROLL
The Bulldog defense has kept their previous two opposing offenses in check, posting back-to-back games with five turnovers forced and scoring a defensive touchdown. In LA Tech's 41-17 victory at Troy, they had one interception and four fumble recoveries--one of which was returned 66 yards for a touchdown by linebacker
Alonzo Jackson Jr.
In the following game against Southern Miss at home, the Bulldogs picked off Golden Eagles QB John White four times--two of which were returned for touchdowns by
Jadon Mayfield
(36 yards) and
Jordan McRae
(89 yards) on back-to-back drives. It is the first time in at least the last 25 seasons the Bulldogs have back-to-back drives with a pick six.
The Bulldogs have 13 consecutive games forcing a turnover dating back to the 2025 season, with 23 interceptions and 10 fumble recoveries in that span. LA Tech also has a turnover forced in 21 of their last 23 games since the start of last year. Among those contests, the Bulldogs have forced multiple turnovers in 13 of them. Since the start of 2025, the Bulldogs have picked off opponents on 38 separate occasions along with 13 fumble recoveries. Since the start of 2025, the Bulldogs' 51 turnovers forced and 10 multi-interception games are the most among all FBS programs.
LA Tech is one of two FBS programs alongside Texas Tech as the only two FBS programs with interceptions returned for touchdowns on consecutive drives. On record, this is the first such occurrence in program history.
ALONZO JACKSON JR., 'DOGS DEFENSE CONTINUES TO DOMINATE
Defensively, the Bulldogs are top five in the SBC in interceptions (1st - 16), TFLs (1st - 81), turnover margin (1st - +14), interceptions returned for touchdowns (1st - 3), turnovers forced (1st - 26), scoring defense (2nd - 21.5 points/game), sacks (T-2nd - 28) and total defense (3rd - 332.9 yards/game).
Alonzo Jackson Jr.
is the only player in FBS this season with 17+ TFLs, 10+ sacks and 3 fumble recoveries. His 17.5 TFLs lead all qualifying defenders in the country, and he is also one of seven players in FBS with at least 10 sacks. With
Jordan McRae's
pick six vs. Southern Miss, he became the only SBC player with multiple interceptions returned for touchdowns this season.
