Bui Named Special Teams Player of the Week
11/16/2026 10:59:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
IRVING, Texas --
Aiden Bui
was named the American Conference Special Teams Player of the Week, the league announced Monday morning (Nov. 16).
Bui scored the majority of Charlotte's points with three field goals and one extra point in the 17-16 loss to East Carolina on Saturday. His three makes tied a Jerry Richardson Stadium record and made him the seventh different kicker in program history to make three field goals in a game, with the 10th total three-FG performance from a 49er.
Converting all three of his attempts in the game, Bui opened with a 50-yarder for a career high and Charlotte's longest field goal since the 2024 season. His 10 total points in the game are the most a 49er kicker has had since 2024 as well.
Bui's award is Charlotte's 28th all-time weekly conference honor and the Niners' seventh as members of the American Conference. It is the Green and White's first weekly award since the final game of the 2024 season, when Michael O'Shaughnessy was named the American Special Teams Player of the Week.
Special teams has been Charlotte's most decorated unit in the weekly awards, with Bui's win representing the 12th total Special Teams Player of the Week award in program history and making him the eighth different player in program history to be recognized as the league's Special Teams Player of the Week.
Charlotte is on the road this Saturday (Nov. 21) at Tulsa for a 2 p.m. ET contest before finishing the season out at home on Nov. 28 against Navy. Kickoff time for the Navy game is still to be announced.
