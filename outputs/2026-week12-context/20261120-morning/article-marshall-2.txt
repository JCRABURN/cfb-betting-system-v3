Everything you need to know before the Herd’s Senior Day contest on Saturday
2026 Football Game Tickets & Parking
HUNTINGTON, W.Va. –
Marshall Football returns to Joan C. Edwards Stadium on Saturday, November 21, for its final home game of the 2026 season as the Thundering Herd takes on Georgia State. Kickoff is scheduled for 4:02 p.m.
Saturday's game will feature a
Fan Appreciation Celebration
,
Senior Day
, and
Salute to Service
, with special ticket promotions, free popcorn for all fans, and opportunities for the Huntington community to come together for one final home gameday this season.
Fans are encouraged to
WEAR WHITE
as the Thundering Herd looks to close out its home schedule with a victory.
FAN APPRECIATION CELEBRATION
Marshall Athletics is saying thank you to Herd fans with a special Fan Appreciation Celebration featuring several promotions and opportunities to enjoy Saturday's game.
FREE POPCORN FOR ALL FANS
As a thank you for their continued support throughout the season, all fans attending Saturday's game will receive free popcorn.
SPECIAL SQUISHY TICKET PROMOTION
One of Marshall's most popular gameday collectibles is back! Fans can take advantage of a special ticket promotion featuring the Marshall Squish Cube.
A limited number of these popular collectibles remain available, and fans are encouraged to purchase early before supplies run out.
Football season ticket holders can take advantage of a special $15 discounted package that includes a game ticket and a Squish Cube. Season ticket holders who previously purchased the promotion at full price can contact the Marshall Ticket Office to discuss available options.
Fans who purchase the promotion can pick up their Squish Cube at the Fan Assistance Tent on gameday. Purchasers' names will be on the pickup list.
Purchase your Squish Cube package:
https://herdtickets.evenue.net/promotions/SQUISH
YOUTH TEAM TICKET PROMOTION
Marshall Athletics is welcoming youth sports teams from across the region to Saturday's game with a special ticket opportunity.
Participating youth teams can receive complimentary tickets for their players, while discounted tickets are available for parents, family members, and friends.
Teams that have already requested tickets can pick them up at the Marshall Ticket Office Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., or at the Fan Assistance Tent beginning at 1 p.m. on gameday.
FREE ADMISSION FOR HIGH SCHOOL STUDENTS
Marshall Athletics is also inviting high school students to pack The Joan for Saturday's home finale with free admission!
Students must complete the required registration form to be eligible. Each registered student will receive a nontransferable digital ticket that must be used by the student to whom it was issued.
SENIOR DAY
Saturday will mark the final home game for members of the Marshall Football senior class.
Prior to kickoff, Marshall Athletics will recognize and celebrate the student-athletes who have dedicated their time, effort, and commitment to the Thundering Herd football program.
Senior Day festivities are scheduled to begin at approximately
3:30 p.m.
Fans are encouraged to enter the stadium early and help honor these student-athletes for their contributions to Marshall University.
SALUTE TO SERVICE
Saturday's game will also feature Marshall's Salute to Service theme, recognizing the brave men and women who have served and continue to serve our country.
Marshall Athletics is proud to honor military members, veterans, and their families for their sacrifice, dedication, and service.

THUNDER WALK AND FAN FEST
The gameday excitement begins outside Joan C. Edwards Stadium with Thunder Walk and Fan Fest at 1:40 p.m.
Fans are encouraged to arrive early, welcome the Thundering Herd to the stadium, and enjoy the pregame atmosphere before heading inside for kickoff.
IMPORTANT GAMEDAY REMINDERS
WEAR WHITE:
Fans are encouraged to wear white to Saturday's game and help create a unified atmosphere inside Joan C. Edwards Stadium.
ARRIVE EARLY:
With Senior Day festivities beginning at 3:30 p.m., fans should plan to enter the stadium well before kickoff to avoid missing the pregame recognitions.
MOBILE TICKETING:
Marshall Athletics utilizes digital ticketing. Fans are encouraged to download their tickets to their mobile devices before arriving at the stadium for a smooth entry experience.
CLEAR BAG POLICY:
Joan C. Edwards Stadium follows a clear bag policy. Fans should review the stadium's bag guidelines before arriving.
CASHLESS VENUE:
Joan C. Edwards Stadium operates as a cashless venue. Fans should plan to use a credit or debit card for purchases.
TICKET INFORMATION
Tickets for Saturday's home finale are available through the Marshall Athletics Ticket Office.
Fans can purchase tickets online at
HerdTickets.com
or contact the Marshall Ticket Office for additional information about available promotions.
With special ticket opportunities, free popcorn, Senior Day recognitions, and Salute to Service festivities, Saturday provides one final opportunity this season for Herd fans to come together at Joan C. Edwards Stadium.
