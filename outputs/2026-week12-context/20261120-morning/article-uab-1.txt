UAB Announces 7 p.m. Kickoff Against UTSA on ESPNU
11/15/2026 9:00:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
BIRMINGHAM, Ala.
– The American Conference announced Sunday that UAB's regular-season home finale against UTSA on Saturday, Nov. 21, will kick off at 7 p.m. CT (8 p.m. ET) at Protective Stadium. The game will be televised nationally on ESPNU.
The Blazers (6-4, 4-2 American) return to Birmingham riding a two-game winning streak after defeating Temple 36-10 on Saturday to become bowl eligible for the first time since 2022. UAB enters its home finale with a perfect 5-0 record at Protective Stadium and an opportunity to finish undefeated at home for the first time since 2019 and the first time since moving into Protective Stadium in 2021.
Saturday's contest will feature several special recognitions, highlighted by Senior Day, as UAB honors its graduating student-athletes during a pregame ceremony.
The Blazers will also recognize the 2026 UAB Athletics Hall of Fame class during the game. The class, which includes former UAB football standout Rodregis Brooks, will be officially inducted Friday, Nov. 20, at The Florentine in Birmingham.
UAB has won six of its first 10 games under head coach
Alex Mortensen
, securing its most victories in a season since 2022. The Blazers will look to close out their home schedule with a sixth consecutive victory at Protective Stadium before traveling to North Texas for the regular-season finale on Nov. 28.
Tickets for Saturday's game are available at
UABSports.com/tickets
.
