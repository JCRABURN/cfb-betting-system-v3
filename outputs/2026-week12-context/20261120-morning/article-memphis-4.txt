Stokes Named Semifinalist for Davey O’Brien National Quarterback Award
Nov 17, 2026
|
Football
Share:
FORT WORTH, Texas –
University of Memphis quarterback
Marcus
Stokes
was announced as one of 13 semifinalists for the 2026 Davey O'Brien National Quarterback Award, the Davey O'Brien Foundation announced Tuesday afternoon.
Of the 13 semifinalists, Stokes is the only quarterback playing for a team outside of the Power 4 and Notre Dame.
Stokes has shined in his first action at the FBS level, leading the third-ranked total offense in the nation, including the 12
th
-ranked passing attack and eighth-best scoring offense. Stokes himself also ranks third in passing efficiency, fourth in passing touchdowns and eighth in passing yards.
Going into this weekend's matchup at Navy, Stokes has thrown for at least 300 yards in each of the last four games, throwing for at least three or more in the three most recent games. He also has at least one rushing touchdown in each of Memphis' last three games. With those totals, Stokes has accounted for five touchdowns in back-to-back-to-back games, the first time an FBS player has achieved this feat since Bo Nix in 2023.
For the sixth year, fan voting on social media (Facebook, Instagram and X) will earn players bonus selection committee votes. Second-round fan voting begins immediately following this announcement. To vote, fans must like the official graphic from the @daveyobrien social media account that highlights their favorite quarterback.
The Davey O'Brien Foundation will announce the three finalists Tuesday, Nov. 24. The 2026 Davey O'Brien Award winner will be unveiled live on ESPN on Friday, Dec. 11, during
The Home Depot College Football Awards
.
The Davey O'Brien National Quarterback Award is presented annually to the nation's best college quarterback and is the oldest and most prestigious national quarterback award.
UP NEXT
Memphis (8-2, 5-1 American) heads to Annapolis on Saturday, Nov. 21 for a meeting with Navy (3-6, 2-4 American) at 11 a.m. CT, with the game airing on ESPN2.
