Drzewiecki Named Semifinalist for Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Award
11/17/2026 11:00:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
Fifth-year player has made 17-of-19 field goals, including three makes from 50 yards or more
University of Minnesota kicker
Luke Drzewiecki
was named a semifinalist for the 2026 Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Award presented by the Orange Bowl today. The announcement was made by the Palm Beach County Sports Commission.
Drzewiecki is one of 20 semifinalists for the award. He was named First Team Midseason  All-American by the Sporting News and was a Lou Groza Award "Star of the Week" for Week 9. The fifth-year player has played in nine games this season and has been named Big Ten Special Teams Player of the Week three times. Drzewiecki leads Minnesota in scoring with 69 points (17 field goals, 18 point-after attempts)
Drzewiecki spent the last four seasons at New Mexico before joining Minnesota before the season. He is 17-of-19 on field goals, including eight makes from 40+ yards, highlighted by a career-long 60-yarder at Indiana. Drzewiecki's two misses this year both came from 56 yards against UCLA on Nov. 7.
Prior to his first miss against UCLA, he had made 26 straight field goals dating to his time at New Mexico, which was the longest active streak in the nation at the time. His 15 straight makes with the Gophers rank second in program history, one off the school record of 16 set by Dan Nystrom in 2002.
Drzewiecki is the first player in team history to be Big Ten Special Teams Player of the Week three times in a season, and he matched
Darius Taylor
(2023) and Shannon Brooks (2015) for most weekly honors overall in a season.
At Indiana, Drzewiecki went 3-for-3 on field goals, making his first from 37 yards, adding a 51-yarder in the fourth quarter and then a 60-yarder with 19 seconds remaining in regulation.
His 60-yard make was the second longest in school history and the longest ever made outdoors by a Gopher. Drzewiecki also became the third Gopher in program history to make three 50-yarders in one season (51 yards at Washington and 51 and 60 yards at Indiana) along with Adam Bailey (1997) and Dragan Kesich (2023), and he's the sixth to have three or more 50-yarders in a career.
Drzewiecki, with his two 50+ makes at Indiana, also joined Bailey (Penn State in 1997) and Emmit Carpenter (Purdue in 2016 and Fresno State in 2018) as the only Gophers to make multiple 50-yarders in the same game.
He went 2-for-2 on field goals in a 13-10 win against Iowa, making his first from 31 yards and then a 46-yarder as time expired to help Minnesota win Floyd of Rosedale. It was the first walk-off field goal of his collegiate career and the first for a Gopher since the 2023 season opener against Nebraska when Kesich did it.
Semifinalists will be voted on by a panel of more than 100 FBS head coaches, SIDs, media members, former Groza finalists, and current NFL kickers to select the three finalists. These finalists will be announced on November 24 and honored at the 35th annual Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Awards Banquet on December 7 in Palm Beach County. The same panel then selects the winner, who will be announced live on ESPN at the Home Depot College Football Awards on Friday, December 11.
The Award is named for National Football League Hall of Fame kicker Lou "The Toe" Groza, who played 21 seasons with the Cleveland Browns. Groza won four NFL championships with Cleveland and was named NFL Player of the Year in 1954. Although an All-Pro offensive lineman as well, Groza ushered in the notion that there should be a place on an NFL roster for a kicker.
Since the first Lou Groza Award was handed out in 1992, 34 finalists, including 18 winners, have gone on to appear in the NFL, earning 13 trips to the Pro Bowl and taking home 8 Super Bowls. That list includes 2026 NFL kickers Daniel Carlson Jake Elliott, Ka'imi Fairbairn, Ryan Fitzgerald, Matt Gay, Zane Gonzalez, Joshua Karty, Jake Moody, Will Reichard, Cairo Santos and Andre Szmyt.
For more information regarding the Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Award, please visit LouGrozaAward.com, or follow @LouGrozaAward on X/Twitter for updates as they happen.  Additional information can be obtained by contacting Jared Fleet, Director of Event Operations at the Palm Beach County Sports Commission at jfleet@palmbeachsports.com.
