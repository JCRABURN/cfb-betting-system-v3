Football Looks to Become Bowl-Eligible for Second-Straight Season at New Mexico State
11/19/2026 11:00:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
NEWARK, Del. –
The University of Delaware football team will travel to Las Cruces, N.M. to take on the New Mexico State Aggies on Saturday, November 21 at 3 p.m. ET on ESPN+. This will be the first-ever meeting between the two programs. Delaware is on the verge of six wins, which would make it bowl-eligible for the second-consecutive season.
Last season, UD, along with FBS newcomer and CUSA mate Missouri State, became the second and third first-year FBS programs from 2023-25 to make a bowl game, joining CUSA's Jacksonville State in 2023. Overall, Delaware is one of two programs (Jax State) in all of FBS football since 2012 to win a bowl game in its first season of FBS competition.

ABOUT THE FIGHTIN' BLUE HENS
The Fightin' Blue Hens enter the contest 5-5, 3-3 CUSA, coming off a 24-16 home win over FIU.
QB
Nick Minicucci
, the reigning CUSA Offensive Player of the Week, leads the team with 2,115 passing yards and 18 passing TDs. His 264.4 passing yards per game lead Conference USA, while his 18 TDs are tied for the best in the league. Minicucci completed 27-of-30 passes (90-percent), setting the Delaware single-game completion percentage record with at least 25 pass attempts in the 24-16 win over FIU. It's the best percentage for any UD quarterback who has thrown at least 15 passes in a game. He threw for 230 passing yards and two passing touchdowns, while adding 69 yards rushing and a score on the ground, including a career-long 62-yard run. His 62-yard scamper was the longest run by a Delaware QB since Andy Hall ran for an 85-yard TD on September 27, 2003 at Hofstra. He became only the fourth player in the nation this season to complete 90-percent of his passes with at least 30 attempts and first in CUSA since Caden Veltkamp of WKU at Middle Tennessee on September 14, 2024. Minicucci's 230 passing yards in the game moves him to 7,097 for his career, passing Super Bowl MVP Joe Flacco for second on the Delaware all-time career passing yards list and 1,117 behind Matt Nagy for most all-time. Minicucci's two passing touchdowns in the game moves him to 56 for his career, matching Nolan Henderson for second on the Delaware all-time career passing touchdowns list. Matt Nagy holds the record with 58 touchdowns through the air.
RB
Jo Silver
tops the team with 438 rushing yards on the campaign to go with two rushing touchdowns. RB
Viron Ellison Jr.
leads the team in rushing TDs (5).
WRs
Sean Wilson
, the team's leading receiver, is fourth in CUSA with 717 receiving yards. Wilson also tops the squad with 54 catches and five touchdown receptions, both top five in CUSA.
LB
Gavin Moul
leads the Hens with 73 tackles, while his 8.1 tackles per game rank third in Conference USA.
WR
Bryson Graves
has been lethal on special teams this season. He blocked a punt for a touchdown at Vanderbilt and ranks eighth nationally in combined kick return yards with 472. In kick return average, his 22.5 average ranks 18th in the country. Against Missouri State, he tallied 160 kick return yards, which are the most by a Hen since 2012 when Michael Johnson had 223 against Villanova.
The Hens are 49th in the nation with 2.30 sacks/game. UD's 23 sacks this season are tied for 49th most in the FBS and tied with Liberty for the most in CUSA.
The Blue & Gold boast the No. 1 passing offense in Conference USA with 292.8 passing yards per contest, all while using five different quarterbacks and having two other players throw a pass.
Delaware has forced multiple turnovers in five-straight games, the longest streak by any CUSA team this season and the longest active streak in the FBS. The last time Delaware had six-straight games with multiple takeaways was an eight-game run between 2011 and 2012.
Delaware is averaging 29.7 points per game (2nd in CUSA), while allowing 27.8 points per game (7th).
UD has recorded 399.4 yards of total offense per game (2nd in CUSA), while allowing 411.2 yards per game (9th).
ABOUT THE AGGIES
The Aggies are 3-7, 2-4 CUSA, coming off a 27-21 road overtime loss to Missouri State.
New Mexico State is led by third-year head coach Tony Sanchez, who owns a 10-24 record with the Aggies. Sancez also spent five seasons at the helm of UNLV as head coach, holding a career record of 30-64.
Former Furman QB Trey Hedden runs the offense for the Aggies, throwing for 1,272 yards, nine touchdowns, and 10 interceptions on the season.
On the ground, NM State is led by former Delaware State and ULM RB James Jones with 1,090 yards and five touchdowns. Jones leads CUSA in rushing yards and rushing yards per game (109.0).
WR AJ Williams III is the team's leading receiver with 447 receiving yards to go with five TDs.
On defense, LB Tory Gethers leads the way with 37 tackles.
NM State is averaging 20.7 points per game (8th in CUSA), while allowing 25.9 points per game (5th).
The Aggies have recorded 348.3 yards of total offense per game (9th in CUSA), while allowing 345.2.4 yards per game (4th).
