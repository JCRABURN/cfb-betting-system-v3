MADISON, Wis. -
Wisconsin Football (6-3, 4-2 Big Ten) will wrap up its 2026 road slate with a game against Purdue (3-7, 2-5 Big Ten) on Saturday, November 21, at Ross-Ade Stadium. Kickoff is scheduled for 11:00 a.m. CT on NBC.
The Badgers are coming off a road loss against Maryland and are looking to get back on track against the Boilermakers. LB Mason Posa posted a game-high 13 tackles, including 2.5 TFLs and one sack.
Purdue enters the game after falling 28-7 to Iowa on the road. QB Ryan Browne leads the team with 2,418 passing yards and 12 touchdowns through 10 games played.
ON THE ROAD AGAIN
Saturday’s matchup in West Lafayette will be the Badgers’ final road game of the regular season. If they win, it will be their first road win since a 17-point comeback at No. 13 Penn State on Sept. 26. Wisconsin’s 18 consecutive wins against the Boilermakers, dating back to 2003, mark the longest-active win streak by a Big Ten team against a conference opponent.
TRIPLE DIGITS
After logging 10 stops against Maryland, LB Cooper Catalano has now made double-digit tackles in four straight games. The last time a Badger did that was LB Chris Borland in 2013. Catalano’s 100 tackles on the season also make him the first Wisconsin player to reach that mark since S Hunter Wohler did so in 2023 (120).
DEFENSIVE DOMINATION
Wisconsin’s defense ranks 14th nationally and 10th in the nation during conference action. UW is allowing only 4.34 yards per play this season. That not only leads the Big Ten, but could mark UW’s lowest clip since leading the nation with 4.10 yards per play in 2021. During Big Ten play, the Badgers also lead the conference in passing TDs allowed, passing yards/attempt allowed and opponent plays of 20+ yards.
SERIES HISTORY
Wisconsin leads the all-time series against Purdue 54-29-8. The Badgers have won their last 18 contests against the Boilermakers, and haven’t lost in West Lafayette since 1997. In the teams’ last five meetings, Wisconsin has secured victory margins of 10+ points. Their most recent matchup was on Oct. 5, 2024 at Camp Randall, where UW plowed through to a 52-6 win.
