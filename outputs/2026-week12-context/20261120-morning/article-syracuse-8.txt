OPENING DRIVE
• The Orange will play their final road game of the regular season on Saturday, taking on Boston College for the 60th time in program history.
• Roxy Bernstein (play-by-play), Dave Clawson (analyst) and Madison Hock (reporter) will be on the call for the conference matchup, set for a noon kick airing on ACC Network.
CAN'T STOP CALVIN
• Freshman WR
Calvin Russell III's
17-catch, 137-yard performance was among one of the top performances by a true freshman in college football history.
• His 17 catches were the most in a game by a true freshman in the last 35 years.
• The 17 receptions also tied the SU and ACC all-time record for receptions in a single game and were the most by any player at the FBS level since December of 2024. Ervin Philips is the only other player in SU and ACC history to catch 17 passes in a game, also doing so against NC State in 2017.
• Russell now has 41 receptions on the season, giving him the Syracuse freshman record for receptions in a season, passing Taj Harris who had 40 in 2018.
• The rookie is also closing in on the SU record for receiving yards by a freshman, currently sitting fifth with 411. Taj Harris holds the record with 565 in 2018.
• Russell has surged up the Syracuse freshman receiving lists despite not making his collegiate debut until October due to his recovery from injury. At his current pace, he would be on track for over 700 yards receiving and 70 catches in a full 12-game regular season.
CARDIAC 'CUSE
• The Orange have played in eight games decided by one-score this season, more than any team in the nation.
• Syracuse has played in seven consecutive games decided by eight points or less. The streak is the longest active in the FBS and in SU history. It is just the third such streak among FBS teams since 2020, joining Old Dominion (2023) and Northern Illinois (2021) to have played seven straight one possesion games.
• Since the start of
Fran Brown
's tenure, Syracuse is 12-6 in games decided by one score. Syracuse's 12 wins in one-score games are tied for second-most over this stretch, trailing only Arkansas State's 13 wins.
• Syracuse has won seven of its last eight games that went to overtime, including five in a row under Brown. The five-game streak is tied with Illinois for the longest active streak among FBS teams.
• Since the start of Brown's tenure in 2024, the Orange's five overtime games are tied with Liberty for the most among FBS teams. Syracuse has the most overtime victories in that span and is one of five teams to be unbeaten with at least three overtime games: Buffalo (4), Illinois (3), Georgia (3), and Troy (3).
NICE CATCH, HATCH
• WR
Umari Hatcher
is among the nation's leading receivers, sitting third among all FBS players in receiving yards with 1,045 this season. He also ranks 11th in receptions per game (6.6) and is tied for 16th in receiving TDs (8).
• Hatcher's 1,045 yards is the sixth-most by a Syracuse player in a single season in program history.
• He was the first SU player to log four straight 100-yard receiving games since Steve Ishmael did so in 2017. His streak included 152 yards (at UConn), 126 yards (at Virginia), 224 yards (vs. Louisville) and 103 yards (at North Carolina).
• He logged the eighth 200-yard receiving game in SU history against Louisville, tallying 224 yards on a career-high 13 catches.
• His performance against Louisville yielded the eighth-most receptions (13) and sixth-most receiving yards (224) by an FBS player in a single game this season.
• He logged 100-yard receiving games with three different starting QBs.
ANGELI IN CRUNCH TIME
• After being inactive due to injury for a stretch of three games, QB
Steve Angeli
has returned to start the last four contests. He has orchestrated a game-winning drive in the final two minutes or overtime in three of those four starts.
• Angeli has saved his best for late, going 38-of-52 for 516 yards and five touchdowns in the fourth quarter and overtime over the last four games.
• Most recently, Angeli went 8-of-12 for 111 yards and two scores in the fourth quarter at NC State, including the game-winning 11-yard strike to
Justus Ross-Simmons
with 48 seconds left. He finished 27-of-42 with 272 passing yards and two touchdowns in the win.
• In the win over #19 SMU, Angeli finished 21-of-34 with 200 passing yards and a touchdown.
• Against North Carolina, Angeli was 11-of-14 for 173 yards and two touchdowns in the fourth quarter and overtime, including the game-winning score in the extra period.
DOUBLE TROUBLE OFF THE EDGE
•
Tunmise Adeleye
and
Keyshawn Johnson
have combined for 14.5 sacks, making Syracuse one of 12 teams in the nation to have a pair of teammates to combine for at least 14.5 sacks.
• Their 14.5 sacks is the most by a pair of SU teammates since Cody Roscoe and Stefon Thompson teamed up for 14.5 in 2021.
KEYSHAWN IS KEY
•
Keyshawn Johnson
has racked up 8.0 sacks this season, the most by an SU player in a single season since Cody Roscoe's 8.5 in 2021.
• He is one of 16 players nationally to have 8.0 sacks or more.
• Johnson forced the game-sealing fumble against NC State, a play where he was credited with a sack after forcing the ball loose before the quarterback started his throwing motion.
• He has logged 4.0 sacks over the last three weeks.
FAMILIAR FOE
• The Eagles are the Orange's fifth-most frequent opponent, trailing just West Virginia (61 games), Colgate (69 games), Penn State (71 games) and Pittsburgh (82 games).
SERIES HISTORY
• Syracuse and Boston College meet for the 60th time on Saturday.
• The Orange lead the all-time series, 34-25.
• Boston College has won the last three meetings between the two programs.
A WIN WOULD …
• Make the Orange bowl eligible for the second time in three seasons under head coach
Fran Brown
and fourth time in the last five years.
• Make Syracuse 4-1 in its last five games.
• Improve Syracuse's ACC record to 4-5.
• Give Syracuse its 762nd program win.
