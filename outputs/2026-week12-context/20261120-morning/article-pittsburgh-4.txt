Talkin’ Pitt: Meet the Challenge
11/17/2026 1:59:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
Straight from the podium: What Pitt’s coaches and players are saying as the Panthers prepare for this week’s matchup at Louisville.
PITTSBURGH—
Offensive line coach
Jeremy Darveau
, linebacker
Braylan Lovelace
and defensive lineman
Zach Crothers
discussed preparation up front, defensive chemistry and the opportunity ahead as Pitt prepares to visit Louisville.
Darveau: "We welcome big challenges."
Darveau credited Pitt's improved pass protection to the offensive line's development, veteran leadership and contributions from the quarterbacks and running backs. He emphasized that building the group has required patience and consistent work.
"Offensive line development is more of a marathon and not a sprint," Darveau said.
This week brings a test against Louisville's defensive front, including
Clev Lubin
, whose combination of power, speed and ability against the run stood out on film.
"We welcome big challenges," Darveau said. "That's why you play Power Four football, so you can go against big-time players like that and show what you've got."
Darveau also wants cleaner execution in the running game after Florida State challenged Pitt with movement and twists up front. Preparing for similar looks has been an emphasis this week.
"We've just got to put the hours in to make sure we get the result we want," he said.
Lovelace: "Keep fixing those little details."
Lovelace expects Louisville's running game to challenge Pitt with two talented, fast backs. The Panthers' response starts with the aggressive approach that has defined their defense throughout the season.
"We just know we've got to come down, stop the run, do what we do on defense and just make it happen," Lovelace said.
He believes the linebackers' chemistry continues to strengthen with experience. The communication built since spring has allowed the group to play more freely and build off one another.
That trust extends throughout the program.
"We believe in every single kid that sits in these chairs, and we believe in every single coach that comes in this room," he said.
While several close games have left Pitt looking back at missed opportunities, Lovelace said the focus must remain on correcting mistakes and moving forward.
"We just keep taking those on the shoulder, keep fixing those little details and keep moving on to be able to go get those wins," he said.
Crothers: "A great chance to show who we are."
Crothers moved inside to defensive tackle against Florida State, taking on a different role to help the defensive front. Previous experience at the position helped, as did communication with teammates when he needed clarification.
"Those guys definitely helped me out," Crothers said.
He sees that versatility as a product of Pitt's aggressive scheme and the staff's ability to identify where players can be most effective.
"We want to go make plays," he said. "We want to be aggressive, and at every position you do that a little bit differently."
Against Louisville, Crothers emphasized stopping the run to create opportunities to pressure the quarterback in passing situations. He believes the matchup offers a chance to demonstrate the defense's progress.
"We have gotten better since that Miami game," he said. "I know that all across the board. So it's a great chance to show who we are."
