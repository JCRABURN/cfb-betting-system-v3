Castellanos, Williams named to Pony Express Award Elite 11
November 18, 2026
|
Football
Pony Express Award Press Release
Red Raider Honors Hub
Share:
LUBBOCK, Texas —
The Pony Express Award, which celebrates the best tandem in college football, announced Wednesday that Texas Tech quarterback
Thomas Castellanos
and running back
J'Koby Williams
were among 11 duos across college football tabbed for its watch list.
The Pony Express Award is a regular-season honor recognizing the best tandem in college football, regardless of position. It is named after former SMU teammates Craig James and Eric Dickerson and celebrates selflessness, collaboration and true team excellence.
The Red Raiders have ranked among the nation's top offenses since Castellanos took over as Texas Tech's starting quarterback three weeks ago. Since then, Texas Tech is 3-0 and averaging 46.3 points and 517.3 yards of total offense per game behind a balanced attack that has produced averages of 258.0 passing yards and 259.3 rushing yards per game.
Over the last three weeks, Castellanos has accounted for 769 passing yards, 257 rushing yards and 12 touchdowns while leading Texas Tech to a 3-0 record that includes two ranked wins. Meanwhile, Williams eclipsed 1,000 rushing yards in his last outing, becoming the 10th Red Raider in program history to record a 1,000-yard season. He has 1,078 rushing yards through 10 games and has posted three straight 100-yard rushing games and five overall this season. A dynamic playmaker, he ranks sixth in the FBS in all-purpose yards per game with 151.7 and was named a Paul Hornung Award finalist last Thursday.
The final award winners are not necessarily limited to this watch list, as new candidates could emerge over the final few weeks of the season.
Texas Tech is the reigning Pony Express Award recipient, having won the award last season with its defensive duo of
David Bailey
and
Jacob Rodriguez
. The two were selected in the first and second rounds of the NFL Draft, respectively, last year.
The 2026 Pony Express Award winners will be announced in December.
2026 Pony Express Award Watch List
Arizona: Noah Fifita/Isaiah Mizell
Boise State: Maddux Madsen/Rasean Jones
Cincinnati: Evan Tengesdahl/Taren Tyo
Georgia: Chris Cole/Raylen Wilson
Houston: Conner Weigman/Amare Thomas
Miami: Darian Mensah/Malachi Toney
Notre Dame: CJ Carr/Jordan Faison
Oregon State: Braden Atkinson/Jesse Legree
Texas Tech:
Thomas Castellanos
/
J'Koby Williams
Utah: Devon Dampier/Braden Pegan
Wake Forest: Gio Lopez/Carlos Hernandez
