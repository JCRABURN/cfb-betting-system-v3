Wake Forest's Will Way Named ACC Offensive Lineman of the Week
11/16/2026 1:59:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
This marks the second time this season that Way has been recognized with ACC Offensive Lineman of the Week honors.
By:
Stephen Czarda
Share:
CHARLOTTE, N.C. –
Redshirt junior offensive lineman
Will Way
has been named ACC Offensive Lineman of the Week for Week 11, the conference office announced on Monday. This is the second time this season that Way has earned ACC Offensive Lineman of the Week honors.
Way captained Wake Forest to a dominating 49-7 victory at SMU on Saturday, handing the Mustangs their first home ACC loss after previously being 12-0 in conference play at Gerald J. Ford Stadium.
The Demon Deacons finished the day with seven touchdowns (six rushing, one passing) along with 500-plus total yards of offense.
Way opened running lanes for the Deacs to score five third down rushing touchdowns including a 27 yarder by Ty Clark on 3rd-and-18 during Wake's first drive of the game. Additionally, he helped lead
Gio Lopez
into the end zone for two rushing scores on 3rd-and-goal along with creating time for the quarterback to hit
Carlos Hernandez
through the air for a 44-yard score.
The Arizona native also oversaw an offensive line that limited SMU's potent pass rush to just one sack on the day.
