Football vs. Michigan State: 4:15 p.m. Kickoff
Nov 16
|
Football
Tickets
Share:
PISCATAWAY, N.J.
– Rutgers football's Nov. 28 matchup versus Michigan State at SHI Stadium will kick off at 4:15 p.m. on Big Ten Network, the Big Ten announced Monday. The game is presented by Provident Bank.
Tickets are on sale at
ScarletKnights.com
.
The Rutgers Boardwalk
, presented by Visit New Jersey, opens at 12:15 p.m. and will be part of Holiday Fest. Stop by for food trucks, giveaways and an appearance by the Coca-Cola Holiday Caravan, an iconic traveling celebration of the holiday season. The whole family will enjoy free rides, games, activities and live entertainment leading up to the pregame parade and team arrival. The program will honor the senior class during the Scarlet Walk.
The R Fund, Rutgers Athletics' development team, will partner with REPLENISH and the Rutgers Food Pantry this holiday season as part of the Third Annual R Fund Food Drive. Fans and supporters are encouraged to donate non-perishable food and hygiene items at bins located throughout The Rutgers Boardwalk and near entrance gates leading into SHI Stadium. Click
here
for more information.
