Adeleye, Russell III Earn ACC Player of the Week Nods
11/16/2026 2:13:00 PM
|
Football
Get Tickets
Share:
Two Syracuse standouts earned conference recognition this week. Wide receiver
Calvin Russell III
was named both ACC Offensive Receiver of the Week and ACC Rookie of the Week, while defensive lineman
Tunmise Adeleye
was tabbed ACC Defensive Lineman of the Week.
Russell hauled in 17 receptions for 137 yards and a touchdown in Syracuse's 34-31 victory over NC State on Saturday, tying the ACC and Syracuse single-game record set by Ervin Philips in 2017, also in a road contest against NC State.
Russell's 17 catches are the most by a freshman in the past 35 seasons and the most by any player in a game since 2024. Despite not making his collegiate debut until October, he now owns the Syracuse freshman single-season receptions record with 41, surpassing Taj Harris' 40 in 2018. His 411 receiving yards are the fifth-most by a freshman in program history, with two games still to play.
Adeleye was critical on the defensive line, finishing with 1.5 tackles for loss, including a half sack. With just over five minutes remaining and Syracuse trailing by 11, he recorded his first career interception and returned it 18 yards for a touchdown, pulling the Orange back within striking distance.
Adeleye is averaging 1.0 tackles for loss per game and has recorded at least 0.5 in all 10 games this season. He also has 6.5 sacks on the year and has posted at least a half sack in four straight games.
Syracuse now heads to Boston College for its final road game of the regular season, then returns home to face Notre Dame in the regular-season finale next week. Tickets are on sale now at
Cuse.com/FootballTickets
!
