Cowboys in the NFL: Week Ten
November 18, 2026
|
Cowboy Football
Share:
STILLWATER -
Former Oklahoma State players had a busy Week 10, led by Chuba Hubbard's two touchdowns and 172 yards from scrimmage in the Carolina Panthers' win over the New Orleans Saints. Hubbard will see Justice Hill on Sunday, Nov. 22, when the Panthers host the Baltimore Ravens.
Hubbard ran 27 times for 92 yards and two touchdowns and caught five passes for 80 yards in the Panthers' 20-17 win on Sunday, Nov. 15. The win improved Carolina to 4-5.

November 15, 2026
Ollie Gordon II carried 20 times for 76 yards and caught four passes for 39 yards in the Miami Dolphins' 24-22 loss to the Indianapolis Colts. Gordon had 115 yards from scrimmage, but the Dolphins missed three kicks, including a 51-yard field goal try in the fourth quarter that would have given them the lead. Miami (1-8) has lost three straight.
Malcolm Rodriguez had six tackles, one solo, in the Detroit Lions' 35-10 rout of the New England Patriots in Munich, Germany. Tyler Lacy added four tackles, one solo. Tay Martin helped bring down the returner on the opening kickoff. Detroit (7-2) hosts the Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Sunday, Nov. 22.
Jaylen Warren carried seven times for 15 yards and caught five passes for 26 yards in the Pittsburgh Steelers' 38-18 loss to the Cincinnati Bengals. Pittsburgh dropped to 6-3 after the Bengals scored 21 points in the second quarter. Mason Rudolph played late, going 1 of 3 for 2 yards and running once for 9 yards.
Hill carried three times for 4 yards and caught five passes for 34 yards in the Ravens' 23-20 loss to the Los Angeles Chargers on Monday, Nov. 16. A 17-yard catch from Jacoby Brissett late in the third quarter set up a field goal. The Ravens fell to 4-6 with their fifth straight loss.
Tylan Wallace caught two passes for 9 yards in the Cleveland Browns' 27-24 win over the Houston Texans. A 15-yard roughing penalty for a hit on a defenseless Wallace helped set up Quinshon Judkins' 5-yard touchdown run with 53 seconds left. Cleveland (6-4) rallied from a 17-point deficit, is off next week and hosts the Las Vegas Raiders on Nov. 29.
The San Francisco 49ers, who have linebacker Nick Martin, beat the Dallas Cowboys 46-40 to improve to 8-1. They face the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City on Sunday, Nov. 22.
