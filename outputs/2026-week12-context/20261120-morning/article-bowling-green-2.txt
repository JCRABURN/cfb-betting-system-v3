Bradley Tallied Five Sacks, Kingston Averaged 44.6 Yards Per Punt Against Kent State
By:
by Kyle Edmond, BGSU Athletics
CLEVELAND, Ohio -
The Mid American Conference announced the football players of the week Monday morning (Nov. 16). Of those named, Bowling Green's
Myles Bradley
earned the MAC Defensive Player of the Week honor and
Ryan Kingston
was named the MAC Special Teams Player of the Week. The awards mark the third and fourth weekly awards for the Falcons with Bradley being named Defensive Player of the Week on Sept. 28 and
Eriq George
earning the Defensive Player of the Week on Oct. 5.
Myles Bradley
, MAC Defensive Player of the Week
Bradley was a human wrecking ball for the Falcons defense against Kent State, amassing a program record five sacks in the game, tied for the third-most in MAC history. Bradley now has 14 sacks on the season, just five shy of BGSU's single-season record with two games remaining. Bradley added a career-high nine tackles, finishing second on the team, as well as two pass breakups, a forced fumble and a QB hurry. This marks the second award of the season for Bradley and the third defensive award for BGSU.
Ryan Kingston
, MAC Special Teams Player of the Week
Kingston played a key role in Bowling Green's win over Kent State, flipping the field multiple times in favor of the Falcons. Being called on seven times, Kingston averaged 44.6 yards per punt, including a game-long of 53 yards. Kingston managed to pin Kent State inside the 20 yard line once as well. The award marks the first of Kingston's career and the first special teams recognition for BGSU this season.
UP NEXT
Bowling Green will hit the road for the final time during the regular season next week, heading north for the Battle of I-75 presented by Fremont Federal Credit Union. The Falcons and rival Rockets are set for a 7:30 p.m. kick on ESPN2 inside the Glass Bowl on Nov. 20.
