Syracuse vs. Notre Dame is Sold Out
11/19/2026 5:01:00 PM
|
Football
Verified Resale Tickets
Share:
Syracuse's home finale vs. No. 3 Notre Dame is sold out!
Fans looking for tickets to the Nov. 28 contest are encouraged to use
Ticketmaster Verified Resale
for secondary market options.
It's the first sellout in the JMA Wireless Dome since the 2024 season. Syracuse football, winners of three of their last four, host the Irish on in a Noon kickoff on ABC.
