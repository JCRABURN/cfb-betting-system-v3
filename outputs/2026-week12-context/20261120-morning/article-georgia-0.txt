Bolden Named Lott IMPACT Trophy Semifinalist
November 18, 2026
|
Football
Share:
ATHENS, Ga. --- Bulldog junior S
KJ Bolden
has garnered Lott IMPACT Trophy semifinalist honors given to the nation's defensive player of the year, according to a recent announcement.
Bolden, a native of Buford, Ga., is one of nine semifinalists for the award and one of only three from the Southeastern Conference.  A national voter poll will select the winner and the trophy will be presented in Newport Beach, Calif., on Dec. 9.
Bolden has 33 stops, including two tackles for loss, and shares the team lead with two interceptions this season.  The Bednarik and Thorpe award semifinalist has helped put the Bulldogs in the top 10 in Scoring Defense with teams averaging just 14.7 points/game.
The No. 1 Bulldogs travel to Columbia, S.C., to take on No. 21 South Carolina on Saturday.  ABC will televise the matchup at 7:30 p.m.
