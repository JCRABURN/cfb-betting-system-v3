Louisiana, Georgia State Scheduled For Afternoon Kick
11/16/2026 4:14:00 PM
|
Football
2026 Football Schedule
Share:
Eighth meeting all-time between UL, GSU; first meeting in Atlanta since 2020
By:
Matt Sullivan | Director of Athletics Communications
NEW ORLEANS –
The Sun Belt Conference and ESPN announced on Monday that Louisiana's regular-season finale at SBC East Division member Georgia State, scheduled for Saturday, November 28, will officially kick off at 2 p.m. ET.
The game at GSU's Center Parc Credit Union Stadium (formerly Turner Field) in Atlanta will be broadcast on ESPN+.
The game between Louisiana (7-3, 4-2 SBC) and Georgia State (5-5, 2-4 SBC) will be the eighth all-time meeting between the schools with the Ragin' Cajuns holding a 6-1 lead. The teams last met in Atlanta in 2020 with Louisiana claiming a 34-31 overtime victory.
Louisiana will wrap up its home schedule on Saturday when it plays host to Coastal Carolina (4-6, 3-3 SBC) at Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium. Kickoff is scheduled for 2 p.m. and will be available on ESPN+.
