K-State’s Final Home Game Slated for Night Kick
Nov 15, 2026
|
Football
Share:
Share:
MANHATTAN, Kan. –
The Big 12 Conference and FOX Sports announced Sunday that Kansas State's final home game of the 2026 season this Saturday against Arizona will kick at 6 p.m. and be televised by FS1.
K-State and Arizona are meeting for a third-straight season, but this will be the first as a Big 12 Conference game. A pre-existing non-conference home-and-home series was scheduled for 2024 – Arizona's first year in the Big 12 – and 2025, and those games remained as non-conference meetings. K-State picked up a 31-7 win over Arizona two years ago in Manhattan, but Arizona got revenge last year with a 23-17 victory in Tucson.
Fans looking to purchase tickets to the Arizona game can order by visiting
www.kstatesports.com/tickets
or by calling 1-800-221-CATS. To experience a gameday like never before, fans can also register children 8th grade and younger in the first-ever Youth Combine Day to participate in pregame skills challenges in the Shamrock Indoor Practice Facility and receive a game ticket for just $20. Family and friends of registered participants can purchase discounted game tickets.
