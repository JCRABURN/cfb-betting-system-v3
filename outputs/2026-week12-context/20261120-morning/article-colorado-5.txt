Former Buffs Continue To Make Noise In The Pros
November 17, 2026
|
Football
Donate To Football
Buy Football Tickets
Share:
By:
Marcelo Brant, Athletic Communications Student Assistant
BOULDER – With 10 weeks of the 2026 NFL season in the books, former Colorado Buffaloes players are making an impact at the next level. Cornerback/receiver Travis Hunter, quarterback Shedeur Sanders, linebacker Nate Landman and many others continue to find success in the league week after week.
Hunter Keeps Defying the Odds
Hunter has consistently excelled on both sides of the ball this season. The former Buff recorded his third interception of the year in the Jacksonville Jaguars' Week 10 win over the Tennessee Titans.
"It was an unbelievable catch, an unbelievable play," Jaguars Head Coach Liam Coen said. "It was such a special play by obviously a special player."
His strongest performance of the season so far came in Week 6 during a narrow 34–31 loss to the Houston Texans at Wembley Stadium in London. The receiver and cornerback caught four passes for 59 yards and a touchdown while also recording an interception.
After the game, he reflected on his role as a two-way player with Jacksonville.
"Just being able to help the team on both sides of the ball when it matters most," he said. "It's really my job to do here, and I'm excited to do it."
The 2024 Heisman Trophy winner was the first NFL player since Brian Dawkins in 2002 to get a pick and post a touchdown reception in a game. He's also just the fourth player since the 1970 AFL/NFL merger to do it.
"I've been doing this since I was a baby, doing those types of things," he said. "We've just got to couple out with the win is really my main focus."
Sanders' First Start of the Year
Sanders made the second start of his professional career in Week 9, and the first in 2026. The Cleveland Browns QB made the most of the opportunity, putting up a clean 297-yard, two-touchdown, and no-interception performance against the New Orleans Saints. Despite his efforts, Cleveland came up just short in a tightly contested matchup, falling 23-22.
"I am really proud of him," Browns head coach Todd Monken said. "I am really proud of him and how hard he has worked since I've been here."
"Sitting on the bench a little bit gave me a different point of view on how to give everybody else confidence through the little things," Sanders said of his first starting opportunity this season. "The small things that matter."
"He has really matured," Monken added. "In terms of his work ethic [and] in terms of his diligence, and in terms of what he will say to the coaching staff and ask the questions, his feedback, those are all the right steps moving forward as any young player, especially a quarterback."
Landman Stands Out on Rams' Defense
In Inglewood, Landman continues to impress. The linebacker ranks 22nd in the NFL in total tackles, with 66, and has delivered several standout performances for the Los Angeles Rams this year.
His Week 5 showing against the Buffalo Bills on Monday Night Football was particularly impressive. Landman led the Rams with 13 total tackles, including six solo stops, and forced two fumbles. Despite his efforts, Los Angeles suffered a heartbreaking loss after missing a field goal in overtime.
The former Buff also ranks 13th in the NFL in solo tackles with 29 and leads the Rams with three forced fumbles this season. Before Week 1, head coach Sean McVay named Landman a team captain for the first time in his NFL career, reflecting the linebacker's growing leadership role within the defensive unit.
"I think he has really high emotional intelligence," McVay told TheRams.com in August. "I'm really pleased with him, and he's been a great addition for us."
Other Buffs in the NFL
Former Buff cornerback Chidobe Awuzie continues to see action after nine years in the league, recording 10 tackles and two passes defended for the Baltimore Ravens this year.
Rookie offensive tackle Kahlil Benson was signed by the Kansas City Chiefs as an undrafted free agent this offseason. After a great training camp, Benson made several starts for the Chiefs and was named the NFL's "Offensive Rookie of the Month" in September.
Second-year defensive end BJ Green II injured his elbow in the Jaguars' season opener against the Browns and went on injured reserve. Jacksonville opened his return-to-practice window on Nov. 11.
Second-year wide receiver Jimmy Horn Jr. saw action in Week 1 for Carolina and has since joined the Cleveland Browns practice squad.
Rookie defensive tackle
Amari McNeill
was signed by the Kansas City Chiefs as an undrafted free agent and played all three preseason games. The New York Jets signed him to their practice squad on Oct. 27.
Safety Isaiah Oliver entered his ninth season in the pros, recording 7 tackles and one pass defended for the Tennessee Titans this year.
Fourth-year fullback Brady Russell has caught three passes for 40 yards for the Seattle Seahawks this season, including a 20-yard reception in a 33–31 loss to the Washington Commanders on Sept. 27.
Second-year wide receiver Will Sheppard signed with the Miami Dolphins' practice squad on Sept. 1.
Guard Will Sherman, now in his sixth year in pro football, is on the New Orleans Saints' active roster this season.
Second-year safety Cam'Ron Silmon-Craig is on the Jacksonville Jaguars' practice squad after recording seven tackles and one pass defended in preseason.
Defensive tackle Josh Tupou, now in his ninth NFL season, is on the New York Giants' active roster after signing with the team in May.
Second-year wide receiver LaJohntay Wester has caught five passes for 58 yards for the Baltimore Ravens this season while serving as the team's primary punt returner, averaging 8.6 yards on 12 returns.
Several other former Buffs who finished elsewhere are making waves in the NFL, as well.  Those players include Pro-Bowl cornerback Christian Gonzales (New England), Mekhi Blackmon (Cleveland), Alex Harkey (LA Chargers), Colton Hood (NY Giants), Gerad Lichtenhan (Baltimore), Nikko Reed (LA Chargers), Jahquez Robinson (Baltimore), Valentin Senn (Arizona), Seydou Traore (Miami) and Jordyn Tyson (Arizona State).
