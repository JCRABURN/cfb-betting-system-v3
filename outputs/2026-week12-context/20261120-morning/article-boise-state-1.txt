Super Bowl Champion George Holani to Raise Blue Chaos Flag
11/18/2026 4:30:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
BOISE, Idaho –
Super Bowl champion and former Boise State running back George Holani will return to Albertsons Stadium to raise the Blue Chaos Flag before Boise State's contest against San Diego State on Nov. 21. Now playing for the Seattle Seahawks, Holani helped the franchise capture the Super Bowl LX title in his second year with the team. He will raise the flag prior to the Broncos' regular season home finale, which begins at 7:30 p.m.
"Coming back to Boise means everything to me," said Holani. "It's great to come back and see the standard and mission haven't changed."
Holani played for Boise State from 2019-23, running for 3,596 yards on 685 attempts, ranking sixth in school history in both categories. He is one of 12 Broncos to record multiple 1,000-yard seasons, doing so in 2019 (1,014) and 2022 (1,157). He was named to the All-MW Second Team both seasons and earned Mountain West Freshman of the Year in 2019. The St. John Bosco HS product was named to the Paul Hornung Award Honor Roll twice during the 2022 campaign. A native of Bellflower, Calif., Holani scored 34 total touchdowns in the Blue and Orange. In his five seasons, Boise State amassed 42 wins, a pair of MW titles and a Frisco Bowl win.
"When I think of Boise State, I think of smarts, toughness and humility," he said. "I think of the best fan base and city, and of, course, smashmouth football."
Holani joined the Seahawks in 2024 as an undrafted free agent and appeared in five games as a rookie. Last season, he played in 11 regular season games and two playoff games, totaling 490 all-purpose yards and two touchdowns. He notched 13 yards from scrimmage and recovered an onside kick in Super Bowl LX, helping Seattle to a 29-13 win over the New England Patriots. He has played in all 10 games this season, rushing for 117 yards and a touchdown while adding 88 receiving yards and 144 kick return yards. The Seahawks are 6-4 and tied for second in the NFC West.
No. 15/15/17 Boise State (9-1, 6-0 Pac-12) will celebrate Senior Night before clashing with San Diego State (5-5, 4-2 Pac-12). The game will be televised on USA Sports.
--BroncoSports.com--
