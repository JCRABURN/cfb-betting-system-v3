Chadha Named Lou Groza Award Semifinalist
11/17/2026 12:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
PITTSBURGH
–Kicker
Antonio Chadha
has been named one of 20 semifinalists for the
2026 Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Award
presented by the Orange Bowl, the Palm Beach County Sports Commission announced Tuesday. The award is presented annually to the nation's top collegiate place-kicker.
Chadha has converted 13 of 14 field goal attempts (92.9%), tied for second in the ACC in field goal percentage. The fifth-year kicker from Bryn Mawr, Pa., has also made all 34 of his extra-point attempts and leads the Panthers with 73 points.
Chadha opened the season with a 58-yard field goal against Miami (Ohio), the second-longest make in the ACC this year. He is 12-for-12 from inside 50 yards, including 4-for-4 from 40 to 49 yards. In ACC play, Chadha has made 10 of 11 attempts, highlighted by a four-field goal performance at Boston College.
Three finalists will be announced Nov. 24 and honored at the 35th annual Lou Groza Award banquet Dec. 7 in Palm Beach County, Fla. The winner will be announced live on ESPN during The Home Depot College Football Awards on Friday, Dec. 11.
