Cougars play first road game in the all-time series against West Virginia
HOUSTON –
#20 Houston Football heads to Morgantown, W. Va., for the first time in program history as the Cougars clash with the Mountaineers at noon CT, Saturday, on TNT.
GAME 11 – AT WEST VIRGINIA
#20 Houston (7-3, 4-3 Big 12) at West Virginia (5-5, 2-5 Big 12)
Saturday, Nov. 21 | Noon CT
Milan Puskar Stadium | Morgantown, W. Va.
Broadcast: TNT
Radio: KPRC 950 AM
WHAT TO WATCH FOR
Quarterback
Conner Weigman
has been one of the best, if not the best quarterback, over the last two seasons with 68 total touchdowns since 2025 all at a P4 school, which leads all P4 quarterbacks during that time. Weigman's 47 passing touchdowns and 18 rushing touchdowns are 5th and 3rd most, respectively, during that time. Weigman is 17-6 at Houston, the 5th most wins nationally, and the 3rd most in the Big 12, since 2025.
Houston's defense has been stout against the run. Half of Houston's opponents have failed to rush for 100 yards and Houston has held eight of 10 opponents below their rushing average this season.
Houston has forced a turnover in nine consecutive games after
Javion White's
interception on the first play of the second half at Colorado, the longest streak in the
Willie Fritz
era.
The Cougars put a focus on the offensive line last offseason, recruiting three All-Conference honorees to the group in
Anthony Boswell
,
Shadre Hurst
and
Drew Terrill
. The unit has been rewarded as a Joe Moore Award Semifinalist – the first in program history.
FIVE THINGS TO KNOW
Houston travels to Morgantown, W.Va., for the first time in program history following two Big 12 games against West Virginia at TDECU Stadium. The Cougars won the first meeting on a Hail Mary for their first Big 12 Conference win in program history in 2023.
Re'Shaun Sanford II
, a Comeback Player of the Year Watch List honoree, has torn ligaments in both of his knees over his four year career, both times before a season started. He missed his freshman season due to a knee injury in 2023 and then missed all of 2025 with a season-ending injury to his other knee. In 2026, Sanford has rushed for 100+ yards twice and rattled off two 50+ yard rushing touchdowns.
Houston is one of two schools in the country with three receivers with 5+ receiving touchdowns through Week 7. Receiver
Amare Thomas
(9) has hauled in 20 touchdowns in a Cougar uniform while
Patrick Overmyer
(8) and
Koby Young
(5) have each hauled in 5+ receiving touchdowns. Overmyer's eight receiving touchdowns are tied for the FBS lead.
Houston is one of 11 schools with AP Poll Top 25 Football and Men's Basketball teams and one of just six schools with Top 25 Football, Women's Soccer and Men's Basketball teams.
The Cougars' 20 wins away from TDECU Stadium in the last five years rank sixth in the nation and tie for the Big 12 lead.
SCOUTING WEST VIRGINIA
West Virginia enters the week with a 5-5 record, including a 2-5 mark in Big 12 play with wins over Coastal Carolina, UT Martin, Virginia, Cincinnati and TCU.
The Mountaineers rank 13
th
in the FBS in total offense, averaging 458.9 yards per game, thanks to a rushing offense that sits in sixth nationally with 240.7 yards per contest. Cam Cook and Michael Hawkins Jr. have posted 1,084 and 960 rushing yards, respectively, while combining for 28 touchdowns on the ground.
Isaiah Patterson leads the defense with 65 stops while six Mountaineers all tie for the team lead with one interception each.
LAST TIME AGAINST WEST VIRGINIA
West Virginia won the last meeting, 45-35, to even the series at 1-1 last year at TDECU Stadium.
Conner Weigman
threw for 309 yards and four touchdowns while
Dean Connors
added 71 yards on the ground.
Amare Thomas
caught three of Weigman's touchdown passes, nearly hitting the century mark with 99 yards in the game.
Carlos Allen Jr.
and
Eddie Walls III
each posted double-digit tackles with 11 and 10, respectively.
A HOUSTON WIN WOULD ...
Secure Houston's second straight 8+ win season.
