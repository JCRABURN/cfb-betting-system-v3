Messer Magic Shirt Available at On-Campus Store
11/17/2026 1:40:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Ben Paul
BOCA RATON, Fla.
– For fans of Florida Atlantic football, 'Messer Magic' T-shirts highlighting the historic season by fifth-year WR
Easton Messer
are available to purchase in-person at the Follett Campus Store and online
HERE
.
Messer continues to be recognized nationally as one of the best wideouts in the nation, currently a semifinalist for the Biletnikoff Award as well as the Burlsworth Trophy and a finalist for the Paul Hornung and Jet Awards.
Through 11 weeks, Messer is the national leader in receptions (86) and tied for No. 1 with two punt return touchdowns. He is the only player in the FBS this season to have scored touchdowns via passing (one), rushing (two), receiving (13), and punt returns (two), and has scored 108 points, most among any wide receiver.
Fans can rock their 'Messer Magic' shirts this Saturday at Flagler Credit Union Stadium, where the Bowl-bound Owls will host their home finale against South Florida, kicking off at 2:30 p.m. Also, earlier this season, and still carried in the Campus Store, is a T-shirt marking the historic kick returns of graduate RB
Leonard Farrow
.
Also on sale now for the Nov. 21 FAU football game is a 'Messer Magic' flash sale where end zone tickets cost just $10.33 in honor of Messer's 1,033 receiving yards, ranking fifth in the nation. To purchase, click
HERE
.

The Coaches Radio Show presented by Duffy's Sports Grill takes place at the Duffy's on St. Andrew's Boulevard in Boca Raton. Fans can join in person or listen live on FOX Sports South Florida from 6 to 7 p.m. This week's guests include Hagerty Family Head Football Coach
Zach Kittley
, Wide Receivers Coach
Trent Vasey
, and Vice President and Director of Athletics
Josh McCowan
