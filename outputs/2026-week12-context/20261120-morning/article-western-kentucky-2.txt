Westen Ard Named CUSA Freshman of the Week
11/16/2026 12:00:00 PM
|
Football
Support WKU Football
Purchase WKU Football Tickets
2026 WKU Football Schedule
Share:
BOWLING GREEN, Ky. –
WKU Football linebacker
Westen Ard
has been named the Conference USA Freshman of the Week, CUSA announced Monday.
In WKU's 34-24 win over Jax State on Saturday at Houchens-Smith Stadium, Ard led the Hilltoppers with eight total tackles, including five solo stops. One of Ard's biggest plays of the night was joining teammate
LaTrevion Thompson
on a sack midway through the fourth quarter to help keep the Hilltoppers in the game, which they won on a late touchdown. Ard also tallied one quarterback hurry.
Ard and the Hilltopper defense allowed Jax State just 210 yards in the game, including just 48 yards rushing, to help knock off a Gamecocks team that was previously unbeaten in CUSA play.
The Warner Robins, Georgia, native is currently second on the team in total tackles with 63, despite missing one game earlier in the season. He has a team-best three sacks, as well as six tackles for loss, which is second-most on the team. Ard has also tallied three pass breakups and three quarterback hurries. Ard's 7.0 tackles per game ranks second in the country by a true freshman this season.
It is the fourth CUSA Freshman of the Week award WKU has received this season. True freshman wide receiver
Jonathan Stafford Jr.
received the award in three consecutive weeks on Sept. 28, Oct. 5, and Oct. 12.
WKU improved to 4-6 overall, 3-3 in CUSA and 3-1 at home with the win over the Gamecocks. The Hilltoppers are scheduled to travel to Liberty for a noon CT game on Saturday, Nov. 21, at Williams Stadium in Lynchburg, Virginia. The game will be streamed on ESPN+.
