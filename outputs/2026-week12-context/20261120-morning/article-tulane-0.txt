Football Earns 20-17 Road Comeback Victory Over Rice
Nov 14, 2026
|
Football
Box Score
Share:
Photo Gallery
HOUSTON
-
The Tulane football team (5-5, 3-3 American) came back in the fourth quarter to defeat Rice (1-9, 0-6 American) on the road 20-17 on Saturday, Nov. 14, at First Community Stadium.
In the fourth quarter after trailing the entire game the Tulane defense forced a turnover to give the offense a short field. The team quickly scored as
Bredell Richardson
hauled in the 24-yard touchdown pass from
Trace Johnson
with 9:50 remaining to take a 20-17 advantage. It was Tulane's only lead of the game.
Rice scored first on the made 27-yard field goal Tommy Bauchiero on a 10 play, 66-yard drive with 9:22 remaining in the opening quarter.
Tulane tied the game on the ensuing possession on the 35-yard field goal from
Cooper Helmke
to finish off a 14 play, 58-yard drive with 2:51 remaining in the first quarter.
Rice took the lead on the team's next possession as Gael Ochoa connected with Max Mosey on the four-yard pitch-and-catch on fourth down to finish off the 15 play, 75-yard drive to give the Owls a 10-3 lead with 11:19 remaining before the half.
Tulane made it a 10-6 game on the made 41-yard field goal from Helmke with 6:27 remaining in the second quarter. The drive lasted 12 play, 51 yards.
Rice made it a two-score game as Jamir Benjamin took his interception back 62 yards for a touchdown to put the score at 17-6 with 31 seconds remaining in the half in what would be the score heading into the break.
Tulane again made it a one-score game, 17-13, as
DJ Dugar
ran it in from the goal line with 3:08 remaining in the third quarter to completed the 13 play, 61 yard drive and set up the late game heroics.
Offense:
Trace Johnson
went 19-for-38 for 190 with a touchdown and an interception. He also ran for 32 yards.
Maurice Turner
had 15 attempts for 90 yards.
DJ Dugar
totaled eight carries for 37 yards.
Destyn Hill
recorded five catches for 44 yards.
Zycarl Lewis Jr.
picked up four catches for 34 yards. Bredel Richardson's had a touchdown catch for 24 yards.
Defense:
Makai Williams
recorded 12 tackles.
Dickson Agu
tallied 10 tackles.
Anthony Rogers
picked up eight tackles including a pair of stops for loss.
Jah'rie Garner
had five tackles and a sack.
Ed Smith IV
also totaled a sack.
Special Teams:
Cooper Helmke
connected on a pair of field goals.
Brooks Moore
punted twice for an average of 46.5 yards a kick.
Team:
Tulane outgained Rice 351 to 256 including a 190 to 32 advantage through the air.
Next, Tulane returns home to take on North Texas on Saturday, Nov. 21, at a yet to be determined time.
