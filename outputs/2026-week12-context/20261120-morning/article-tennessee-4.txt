EXTENDED PERIMETER SECURITY CHECKPOINTS
The security perimeter will open at 11:30 a.m., and fans may move in and out of the secured perimeter throughout the day. Following fan feedback from the season opener, Tennessee Athletics has made several adjustments to improve entry flow, including:
Increasing the number of walk-through metal detectors around Neyland Stadium from 35 to 65
Adding new screening locations near G5/30 and the south side of Lot 9
Opening two additional entry points
Nearly doubling the number of metal detectors at Gate 21
Please note, coolers are not permitted through the extended perimeter. Fans wishing to move around campus are encouraged to use the stairs at the corner of G5/30 to access areas near G10 and Circle Park. Tickets will NOT be scanned at the security perimeter. Ticket scanning will occur only at stadium entry gates, which will open at 12:30 p.m.
For all remaining home games this season
concessions will be half-price for the first hour
after gates opening. Games during the noon slate also will be shown on the videoboard in the first hour of gates opening. Gates for Saturday's contest against Alabama will open at 12:30 p.m.

GAMEDAY EVENTS & ACTIVITIES
Senior Day:
There will be a senior/fifth-year player pregame ceremony. Fans are encouraged to be in their seats by 3:15 p.m.
Vol Village Presented by Toyota:
Vol Village presented by Toyota, serves as the ideal spot to view the Vol Walk and the Pride of Southland Band march. Admission is free to all fans with or without a game ticket. Located across from Circle Park, Vol Village features live music, food trucks and beverage stations, interactive displays and fun activities for all ages.
Vol Village will highlight a new artist or band each home game with a pregame concert series, providing Vol fans with the ultimate pregame atmosphere. This week's featured artist is
Matt Stillwell
. Vol Village opens at 12 p.m. for Saturday's game.
Truly's Tailgate:
Located outside the newly renovated Gate 9, fans are encouraged to visit the new and improved Truly's area for food, drinks and more! Truly's Tailgate has expanded with Texas Roadhouse and a number of other new food and beverage options for fans to enjoy before and during the game.
For any game starting later than noon ET, Truly's will open four hours prior to kickoff. For Saturday's game, the tailgate will open at 11:30 a.m. Truly's will stay open throughout the game, giving fans in the south concourse a variety of food, drinks and additional restroom options. Truly's will close at the end of the third quarter.

NEED TO KNOW
CFP Chase Continues
Despite a 22-7 road victory at Texas A&M last Saturday, the Vols fell one spot to No. 9 in this week's College Football Playoff rankings but still find themselves in good position for a spot in this year's playoff field with two regular season games remaining.
Bishop Chasing UT Career Records
Senior running back
DeSean Bishop
continues his chase to catch Travis Henry as Tennessee's career rushing leader, entering this Saturday's battle against LSU ranked fifth in program history with 2,694 career rushing yards and needing 385 to surpass Henry's mark of 3,078 from 1997-2000.
Bishop needs 159 yards to eclipse Johnnie Jones (1981-84) for fourth on UT's all-time rushing list. He already joined Jones as the only players in program history with back-to-back 1,000-yard seasons after a 109-yard performance in the Vols' Week 10 win over Kentucky.
The Knoxville native is also within reach of UT's career rushing touchdowns record of 37 held by Gene McEver (1928-31). Bishop enters Saturday's game ranked fourth in program history with 32 rushing scores to his name, just three shy of Dylan Sampson (2022-24) and James Stewart (1991-94) for second place.
No Fly Zone
Tennessee's pass defense turnaround from 2025 to 2026 has been exceptional under new secondary coaches
Anthony Poindexter
and
Derek Jones
. The Vols boast the SEC's second-best pass defense, allowing only 164.2 yards per game, a mark that ranks eighth in the nation.
UT has surrendered only four passing touchdowns this season, which is tied for the fewest in the FBS with North Dakota State. That's three fewer than the next closest Power Four teams in Indiana, Ohio State and TCU.
The Big Orange has tallied 11 interceptions, which is third in the SEC and tied for 12th in the nation, and has recorded multiple picks in four games while producing at least one in four consecutive contests entering this weekend's game. Junior safety
Dejuan Lane
is having a breakout year with five interceptions, which leads the SEC and is tied for second nationally.
More Vols in the Mix for Postseason Awards
Earlier this week, Tennessee had multiple players named finalists and semifinalists for some major postseason awards.
Bishop was tabbed as one of three finalists for the Burlsworth Trophy, which is awarded annually to the nation's top player who began his career as a walk-on. The Knoxville native was also named a semifinalist for the Doak Walker Award, which is presented to the nation's best running back.
Junior defensive lineman Xavier Gillam added to his impressive list of accolades, as well, after being named a semifinalist for the Outland Trophy (nation's best interior lineman) and the Lott IMPACT Trophy.
Rounding out the group was junior kicker
Cooper Ranvier
, who was chosen as a semifinalist for the Lou Groza Award on Tuesday and enters Saturday's contest having made 13 consecutive field goal attempts, including a career-best 5-for-5 performance in last weekend's road win at Texas A&M.
SERIES HISTORY
Tennessee leads series, 21-10-3
Saturday's meeting between the Vols and Bayou Bengals will mark the first between the two programs at Neyland Stadium since a 30-10 LSU win on Nov. 18, 2017. The last time the two teams met, Tennessee snapped a five-game losing streak to the Tigers with a dominant 40-13 victory in Baton Rouge on Oct. 8, 2022.
ABOUT LSU
First-year LSU head coach Lane Kiffin leads an extremely talented Tigers squad with a deceiving 6-4 record on the year. Three of the team's four losses have come by a combined six points, including a pair of one-point defeats at Kentucky in overtime on Oct. 10 and to No. 6 Texas last Saturday when the Longhorns scored on their final offensive play of the game. LSU enters this weekend's game leading the SEC and ranked in the top 10 nationally in both total offense (490.3 ypg) and total defense (262.3 ypg).
Quarterback Sam Leavitt leads the SEC in passing at 279.7 yards per game and ranks tied for fourth in the conference with 19 touchdown passes but also has thrown a league-leading 12 interceptions on the year. The Arizona State transfer is a threat with his legs, as well, with a team-leading eight rushing touchdowns.
Redshirt junior wide receiver Jackson Harris has emerged as the Tigers' top target in the passing game with team highs in receptions (55), yards (864) and touchdowns (9), all marks that rank among the top four in the SEC. Dilin Jones has been LSU's most productive running back with 185 carries for 838 yards (4.5 avg) and eight touchdowns.
Defensively, fifth year linebacker TJ Dottery leads the Tigers with 76 total tackles and two forced fumbles to go along with 2.5 tackles for loss, five quarterback hurries and three pass breakups. Senior edge rusher Princewill Umanmielen is one of seven players with multiple sacks on a unit that leads the nation with 40.0 this season. Umanmielen's 9.5 sacks rank second in the SEC and tied for eighth in the FBS. Senior defensive back Ty Benefield leads the LSU secondary with 49 tackles, 4.5 tackles for loss, four sacks and a team-best three interceptions.
