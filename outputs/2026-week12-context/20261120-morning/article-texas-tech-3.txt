Red Raiders stay put at No. 12 in CFP rankings
November 17, 2026
|
Football
Share:
LUBBOCK, Texas
– Following its dismantling of then-No. 25 Oklahoma State, Texas Tech (9-1, 6-1 Big 12) remained at No. 12 in the College Football Playoff rankings for the second straight week, as announced by the committee Tuesday evening.
The Red Raiders debuted at No. 15 in the initial CFP rankings before climbing to No. 12 in the second installment. Texas Tech stayed put following its 52-27 road win this past weekend.
The team has been sparked by
Thomas Castellanos
, who has led Texas Tech to its last three wins since taking over the starting quarterback role. He was named the Walter Camp National Offensive Player of the Week after accounting for 263 passing yards and four passing touchdowns, along with 38 rushing yards, in Texas Tech's win over the Cowboys.
Castellanos has totaled 257 rushing yards and 11 passing touchdowns over his last three games without throwing an interception. The only other FBS players this century to record at least 250 rushing yards, 11 passing touchdowns and zero interceptions in a three-game span are LSU's Jayden Daniels (2023), Liberty's Malik Willis (2020) and Ohio State's J.T. Barrett (2014). Texas Tech is averaging 46.3 points and 517.3 yards of total offense per game over its last three contests, with a balanced attack producing averages of 258.0 passing yards and 259.3 rushing yards per game.
The Red Raiders were one of five Big 12 teams ranked in Tuesday's CFP Top 25, down one from last week after Oklahoma State, which was ranked No. 25, fell out following its loss to Texas Tech. Utah (10-0) remained at No. 2 following a dramatic win at Arizona, while the Cougars (8-2) also stayed at No. 10 after their win over Baylor. Texas Tech sits at No. 12, followed by Arizona (7-3) at No. 19 and Houston (7-3) at No. 20. The Red Raiders defeated both the Cougars and Wildcats earlier this season, earning a 28-26 win over Houston on Sept. 18 and a 34-26 victory over Arizona on Oct. 31.
With two regular-season games remaining, the Red Raiders travel to Baylor this Saturday before closing the season at home on Thanksgiving against TCU. Texas Tech controls its own destiny in the Big 12 title race and can clinch a spot in the Big 12 Championship Game by winning its final two games. The Red Raiders could also clinch as early as this Saturday with a win at Baylor combined with a BYU loss at Kansas.
The CFP selection committee meets in person each Monday and Tuesday before revealing its rankings live on ESPN. The next CFP rankings will be released Nov. 24 at 6:30 p.m. CT, followed by the penultimate rankings on Dec. 1. The committee will release its final rankings and the 12-team playoff bracket Sunday, Dec. 6, following conference championship weekend.
