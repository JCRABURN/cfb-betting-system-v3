Check out 2026-27's 12th edition of THE CURRENT - the official podcast of Tulane University Athletics. THE CURRENT is presented by Bryan Subaru.
On the 12th episode of THE CURRENT for the 2026-27 school year, Maddie Hudak talked with Head Football Coach
Will Hall
, football student-athletes
Bredell Richardson
and
Josh Lewis
plus Head Beach Volleyball Coach
Eyal Zimet
and beach volleyball student-athlete
Skylar Ensign
.
