MICHIGAN STATE HOSTS NO. 13 OREGON ON SENIOR NIGHT FRIDAY IN SPARTAN STADIUM
• Michigan State hosts No. 13/14/14 Oregon on Senior Night Friday, Nov. 20 at 8 p.m. in Spartan Stadium. The game will be televised on FOX and streamed on FOX One with Tim Brando (play-by-play), Devin Gardner (analyst) and Josh Sims (sidelines) on the call. MSU will honor its 2026 senior class in a pregame ceremony.
• The Spartans (3-7, 1-6 B1G) dropped a hard-fought battle to Washington, 21-14, at home last Saturday. Tied 14-14 at the half, UW scored the game-winning touchdown with just 2:24 remaining in the game.
• Oregon heads to East Lansing on a three-game winning streak after beating Michigan in Eugene last Saturday, 31-7. The Ducks, ranked No. 13 in this week's College Football Playoff rankings and No. 14 in the AP Poll and AFCA Coaches Poll, stand at 7-3 overall and 5-2 in league play.
• Friday's game marks the ninth meeting between Michigan State and Oregon. The Ducks own a 5-3 series advantage, including a two-game winning streak. In the first matchup between the two schools as Big Ten members, Oregon beat MSU, 31-10, in 2024 at Autzen Stadium.
• MSU is 3-0 against Oregon in Spartan Stadium, including a top-10 matchup in 2015, as the No. 5 Spartans toppled the seventh-ranked Ducks, 31-28, on Sept. 12 en route to winning the Big Ten title and earning a bid to the College Football Playoff. That game in 2015 marked the first regular-season matchup of AP Top 10 teams in Spartan Stadium since No. 2 MSU tied No. 1 Notre Dame 10-10 in the famous "Game of the Century" in 1966. MSU also beat Oregon in East Lansing in the first-ever matchup between the two schools in 1979 and in the home opener in 1999.
• Michigan State is 13-0 in home Friday night games since 2011 and 15-2 overall in Friday night games during that same time span. Twelve of those home Friday wins came in season openers; MSU also defeated Purdue in a Big Ten Friday matchup on Nov. 22, 2024. MSU and Oregon also met on a Friday night in 2024 in Eugene.
• MSU's 10 opponents to date have a combined 64-34 record, which ranks as the seventh-toughest schedule in the FBS by the NCAA and the 24th-toughest schedule in the FBS by ESPN. Sitting at No. 14 in both the AP Poll and AFCA Coaches Poll, Oregon is the fourth ranked opponent of the season for Michigan State. The Spartans previously faced No. 3 Notre Dame (Sept. 19), No. 25 Northwestern (Oct. 17) and No. 9 UCLA (Oct. 24).

►TRENT FRALEY NAMED TO RIMINGTON AWARD MIDSEASON WATCH LIST
• Fifth-year center
Trent Fraley
is MSU's highest-graded offensive player (82.2) according to Pro Football Focus, which ranks No. 1 among centers in the Big Ten and No. 3 in the FBS.
• Fraley, who won the Rimington Award for the FCS while earning first-team All-America honors at North Dakota State last season, was added to the Rimington Award Midseason Watch List last month.
• Fraley has played a team-high 655 snaps on offense and has started 39 consecutive games at center in his career (29 at NDSU from 2024-25; 10 at MSU in 2026).
►PLACEKICKER LIAM BOYD RANKS AMONG BIG TEN LEADERS IN FIELD GOALS
• Fifth-year graduate kicker
Liam Boyd
ranks fourth in the Big Ten and tied for 15th in the FBS with 16 field goals; his previous career high was nine last season at Charlotte. Boyd is 6-for-7 from 30-39 yards and 7-of-7 from 40-49 yards and also connected on a career-long 51-yarder in the season opener vs. Toledo.
• Boyd was named the Big Ten Special Teams Player of the Week after tying his career high with five field goals in the 15-6 win at Michigan on Nov. 7; he also had five field goals against Toledo. Boyd is just the second MSU kicker to kick five field goals in two separate games in the same season (Paul Edinger, 1998).
►PUNTER RHYS DAKIN RANKS THIRD IN BIG TEN IN PUNTING AVERAGE
• Third-year punter
Rhys Dakin

He would rank No. 6 in the FBS as well, but doesn't have the minimum amount of punts to qualify for the national leaders (3.6 per game). Dakin was named to the midseason watch list for the Ray Guy Award earlier in November. A native of Melbourne, Australia, Dakin also ranks second in the conference with 15 punts of 50-plus yards and has placed 15 punts inside the 20.

BIG SPARTAN MOMENTS VS. OREGON IN SPARTAN STADIUM
• Connor Cook, the winningest quarterback in school history, completed 20-of-32 passes for 192 yards and two touchdowns to lead No. 5 Michigan State past No. 7 Oregon, 31-28, on Sept. 12, 2015, in the first matchup of AP Top 10 teams in Spartan Stadium since the 1966 "Game of the Century" between No. 2 MSU and No. 1 Notre Dame. ESPN College Game Day broadcasted from East Lansing on the day of the game, which was a primetime matchup on ABC.
• With MSU on top 24-21 in the fourth quarter, freshman LJ Scott sprinted down the sideline for a 38-yard touchdown to give the Spartans a 31-21 lead with 10:51 left in the game. Michigan State stopped Oregon on a fourth-and-16 play with 1:06 remaining from the Spartan 43-yard line as Riley Bullough rushed Duck QB Vernon Adams Jr. and forced an incompletion to seal the victory.
• During the 1999 season opener, defensive back Amp Campbell – who broke his neck in a game at Oregon during the 1998 season – returned a fumble 85 yards in the fourth quarter to help the Spartans defeat the Ducks, 27-20. It marked Campbell's first game back on the field since the injury.
• In the first-ever matchup between the two schools, Michigan State beat Oregon, 41-17, on Sept. 15, 1979, in Spartan Stadium. Derek Hughes had a 100-yard kickoff return for a touchdown, Morten Andersen kicked two field goals, including a 51-yarder, and Steve Smith rushed 23 times for 156 yards and one TD for the Spartans.
A GLANCE AT NO. 13/14/14 OREGON (7-3, 5-2 B1G)
• The Ducks fly into East Lansing with a 7-3 overall and 5-2 B1G ledger in tow, also riding a three-game win streak, bouncing back from a 21-10 loss at Illinois on Oct. 24, then beating No. 20 Northwestern at home in Eugene, 34-26, on Oct. 31. Oregon opened November with a 41-28 win at No. 12 Ohio State, then routed Michigan, 31-7, last Saturday, Nov. 14, in Eugene. Following Friday's game at MSU, the Ducks wrap up regular-season action hosting border-rival Washington on Nov. 28. Oregon is ranked No. 13 in this week's College Football Playoff rankings and No. 14 in both the AP Poll and AFCA Coaches Poll.
• In their last game, the Ducks defeated Michigan, 31-7. Junior quarterback Dylan Raiola was 20-of-28 passing for 341 and four TDs, earning Big Ten Offensive Player of the Week accolades.
• Raiola hit a pair of receivers for over 100 receiving yards each to sophomore Dakorien Moore and junior Jeremiah McClellan. Moore finished the game with seven catches for 101 yards and a TD, coming on a 59-yard pass from Raiola right before halftime, while McClellan had four catches for 105 yards and one score, a 60-yarder from Raiola on UO's opening possession to get the Ducks' scoring and they didn't look back.
• Sophomore running back Dierre Hill Jr. added one catch on a 49-yard catch and run for a TD early in the fourth quarter, adding 98 yards rushing on 21 carries. Junior running back Da'Juan Riggs also had a 2-yard scoring catch from Raiola.
• Junior placekicker Gage Hurych was 1-of-2 on field goals, missing from 46-yards out midway through the third quarter, while splitting the uprights from 21-yards out with 40 seconds left in the game to cap the scoring on the day and secure the win over the Wolverines.
• Junior linebacker Dylan Williams led a Ducks defense with 11 tackles, as Oregon held Michigan to only 71 yards passing, finishing with 286 yards of total offense, boosted by 215 yards rushing, including UM's only score on a 70-yard run in the second quarter.
• For the season, Oregon is second in the B1G and 26th in the FBS in scoring offense (33.8 ppg), while ranking 10th in the league and 33rd in the nation in scoring defense (20.6 ppg).
• UO is also second in the conference and 30th in the country in total offense (426.2 ypg), including third in the B1G and 14th in the FBS in passing offense (290.8 ypg), along with ranks of 11th and 98th in the league and nation, respectively with 135.7 ypg.
• The Ducks' defense is 11th in the B1G and 50th in the FBS in total defense (343.3 ypg), including 11th in the league and 65th in the nation in passing yards allowed (209.5 ypg), in addition to 12th in the conference and 52nd in the country in rushing defense (133.8 ypg).
• Raiola is fourth in the B1G and 35th in the FBS in completion percentage (.662) on 141-of-213 passing for 1,695 yards with 15 TDs against just two INTs. He ranks seventh in the league and 45th in the nation in passing TDs, while ranking ninth in the conference and 53rd in the country in passing yards/game (211.9).
• Moore leads UO's receiving corps, ranking fifth in the B1G and 16th in the FBS in receiving TDs (8), while ranking sixth in the league and 34th in the country in receptions/game (5.5), with 55 catches for 702 yards (12.8 ypc/70.2 ypg). He is also 11th in the B1G and 49th in the FBS in receiving yards/game.
• Hill leads the Ducks' ground attack with 117 carries for 572 yards (4.9 ypc/57.2 ypg), with five rushing TDs, while sophomore running back Jordan Davison is close behind with 91 rushes for 439 yards (4.8 ypc/54.9 ypg), with seven scores. Hill is 12th in the B1G in rushing ypg, while Davison is 13th, with Davison ranking fourth in the league in rushing TDs, while Hill is 13th.
• Junior defensive back Koi Perich and fifth-year linebacker Devon Jackson spearhead the Ducks' defense with 63 tackles apiece, ranking tied for 21st in the B1G.
• Senior linebaker Matayo Uiagalelei has 10.0 tackles for loss and 9.5 sacks, along with a team-leading nine QB hurries. Uiagalelei ranks sixth in the B1G in TFLs, while ranking second in TFL yards (69), and tied for sixth in TFL/game (1.0), along with ranking second in the conference in sacks, sack yards (69) and sacks/game (0.95).
• Hurych has made 33-of-33 PATS, along with 15-of-20 field goals, with a long of 44 yards. He is perfect from inside 40 yards (11-of-11), while going 4-of-6 from 40-49 yards and 0-3 from 50+.
• Sophomore punter Bailey Ettridge has 42 punts for a 45.6 ypp average, with a long of 67 yards, one of nine punts of 50+ yards, with 18 inside the 20 and inducing 19 fair catches. Ettridge is third in the B1G in punting (45.6).
• Ducks head coach Dan Lanning is in his fifth season with a record of 55-11.
