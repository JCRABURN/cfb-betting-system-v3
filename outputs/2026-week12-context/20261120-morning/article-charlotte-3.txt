CHARLOTTE, N.C. –
Charlotte Football has its final road game of the season this weekend with a trip out to Oklahoma to face Tulsa.
Kickoff is scheduled for 2:00 p.m. ET and the game will air on ESPN+. Charlotte's internet radio crew of Matt Swierad, Al Wallace, and Bobby Rosinski will have the call on
Charlotte49ers.com
and the Varsity Network App.
GAME DAY DETAILS
Charlotte (1-9, 1-5 American) at Tulsa (6-4, 3-3 American)
Saturday, Nov. 21 | 2:00 p.m. ET
H.A. Chapman Stadium
GAME COVERAGE
Television
»
ESPN+
(Will Conerly, LaDarrin McLane, Smacker Danielson)
Internet Radio

Charlotte49ers.com
GAME NOTES
LAST TIME
OUT
Charlotte was back at home last weekend against in-state rival East Carolina, falling to the Pirates 17-16. The Niners led for most of the game and were up by nine at halftime, 16-7, thanks to a trio of first-half field goals from
Aiden Bui
. Charlotte's offense was kept in check in the second half, failing to score a point and managing just 22 yards after the break.
SPECIAL TEAMS PLAYER OF THE WEEK
Bui's three field goals and one extra point made him responsible for 62.5% of Charlotte's points in the game and earned him Special Teams Player of the Week honors in the American Conference. It is Charlotte's 28th all-time weekly conference honor and the Niners' seventh as members of the American, but the first since the final week of the 2024 season. Special teams has been the Niners' most-awarded unit in the weekly awards, with Bui's win representing the 12th total Special Teams Player of the Week award in program history and making him the eighth different 49er to take home that honor.
BABA BUI
Bui's first field goal of the day was a career-long 50-yarder to open the scoring. It was the 12th field goal of at least 50 yards in program history and the first for the 49ers since 2024. Bui's three makes matched a Jerry Richardson Stadium record and marked just the 11th time a 49er kicker has made at least three field goals in a game, making Bui just the seventh different Green and White kicker to connect three times in one game.
TAKING IT AWAY
Charlotte had four takeaways against ECU, marking the second game in a row and the seventh overall the 49ers have tallied four takeaways in a single game. It is the first time Charlotte has recorded 4+ takeaways in consecutive games in program history. The Niners had caused just four turnovers through the first seven weeks and have forced 10 in the last three for 14 on the season, ranking 41st in the country in takeaways.
Dy'Lon Womack
and
Jonathan Patterson
both had interceptions, the second straight game with an interception for Patterson, while Womack and long snapper
Henry Searcy
pounced on fumbles. Womack's two takeaways mark the 10th performance in program history with multiple takeaways in a single game and the first since Al-Ma'hi Ali had two interceptions against Gardner-Webb in 2024.
PICK PARTY
With the interceptions from Womack and Patterson, Charlotte has recorded multiple interceptions in consecutive games after matching the program record with three picks at UAB. The Niners now have six interceptions across the last three games for a total of eight on the season. That leaves Charlotte tied for fourth in the conference, just three behind Memphis for the most in the American, and 45th nationally.
NO FLY ZONE
The Niners broke up eight passes on top of the two interceptions for a total of 10 passes defended. The eight pass breakups tie the single-game program record with the sixth game featuring eight PBUs, and the first game doing so since the 2018 season opener against Fordham.
Jameel Croft, Jr.
and
D'Nas White
each had two breakups while Patterson,
Gavin Willis
,
Jaylon Johnson
, and
Kaden Catoe
all had one.
UNDER THE CAMPUS SPEED LIMIT
Charlotte's defense held East Carolina to only 17 points, just the 22nd game in program history holding an opponent under 20 points. Coming in a loss, it's the sixth time Charlotte has held an opponent under 20 points and still lost the game. ECU was also the last team the 49ers held under 20, when Charlotte beat the Pirates 10-7 in the first meeting between the two sides in 2023.
IN THE BACKFIELD
The defense got into ECU's backfield early and often, registering a pair of sacks as part of 10 total tackles for loss. That ties with the Louisiana game for the most tackles for loss in a game this season and marks the first season with multiple double-digit TFL games since recording three in 2019. Charlotte has 54 total tackles for loss this season to already surpass last year's total and rank 66th in the country in TFLs per game.
THE THINNEST OF MARGINS
Saturday's loss was the first one-point loss for the 49ers since 2017 and the fourth loss by a single point in program history. It marked the fourth one-possession loss for the Niners this season, the most since having a program-record five one-score losses in 2014. With Charlotte's win coming by seven points, the team's five one-possession games are tied for the second-most in program history and the most since the 2018 season.
MAKING PLAYS
Jaden Barnes
scored Charlotte's only touchdown against the Pirates with his third TD reception in as many games. He has been Charlotte's most-targeted receiver this season, with 59 catches on 84 targets for 586 yards to lead the team in all three categories. His two games with nine receptions this season each tie for the seventh-most catches in a single game in program history. Barnes averages 5.9 receptions per game, ranking 24th in the country and fourth in the conference. His 59 total receptions are also fourth in the league and tied for 23rd in the nation.
ENDING PLAYS
Gavin Willis
has been all over the field defensively for the 49ers, leading the team with 88 tackles and 8.5 tackles for loss. He has the fourth-most total tackles in the American Conference and the 30th-most in the country, while his rate of 8.8 tackles per game is fourth in the conference and 31st nationally. After 54 tackles a year ago and 198 over two seasons at Bucknell, Willis is 14th among active NCAA players across all divisions with 340 tackles to his name. His 13-tackle performance against ECU put him seventh on Charlotte's single-season tackles list, just 20 away from matching the program record set by Nick Cook in 2016. With 142 stops as a 49er, Willis needs 26 tackles over the final two games to crack Charlotte's career top-10 list in only two seasons in the Green and White.
DEEP THREAT UNLOCKED
Derrick Eley
has just over half of Barnes' receptions this year (31 catches), but trails him by only 31 total yards (555). Eley is averaging 17.9 yards per reception this year to sit third in the American Conference and 28th nationally. He leads the 49ers in explosive plays through the air, with nine receptions of over 20 yards, including two catches for 30+ against ECU. Eley matched his career-high with 96 yards against the Pirates, coming on only four receptions for a career-best 24.0 yards per reception average.
