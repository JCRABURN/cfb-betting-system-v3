Osborne Named to Week 11 Manning Award Stars of the Week
11/16/2026 1:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
STORRS, CT
- Quarterback
Kalieb Osborne
was named to the Week 11 Manning Award Stars of the Week after leading the Huskies past James Madison on Saturday.
Osborne
completed an outstanding dual-threat performance, accounting for three passing touchdowns and 362 yards of total offense to lead the Huskies to a 46-22 victory over James Madison. Osborne completed 16-of-20 passes for 274 yards and three touchdowns, finishing with an impressive 80-percent completion rate. He also rushed for 88 yards on 16 carries.
The Manning Award was created by the Allstate Sugar Bowl in 2004 to honor the college football accomplishments of Archie, Peyton and Eli Manning.
The Manning Award selected 40 quarterbacks for its Watch List in the preseason and added an additional 16 quarterbacks on October 23. Finalists will be selected in early December, and the winner is scheduled to be announced following the College Football Playoff National Championship. In its first 22 years, the Manning Award has recognized the top names in college football. It has honored quarterbacks from 16 different schools and five different conferences.
