Bears Walk Off Aggies in Thrilling Homecoming Win
November 14, 2026
|
Football
Box Score
Final Stats (PDF)
Postgame Press Conference

Share:
By:
MSU Athletics Communications
SPRINGFIELD – An 88-yard interception return by
Joel Boamah
in overtime secured a remarkable comeback win for Missouri State here Saturday as the Bears rallied for a 27-21 homecoming victory over visiting New Mexico State.
The Bears (4-6, 3-3 CUSA) capped an 80-yard scoring drive in the final minute of regulation with
Connor Lair
tying the game, 21-21, with a 5-yard touchdown rush to force overtime.
NM State (3-7, 2-4 CUSA) had the ball first in the extra period and was facing a second-and-11 at the Missouri State 15 when Boamah wrestled the ball away from Aggies receiver AJ Williams III at the 12 and took the ball to the house. It marked the second pick-six of the game for the Bears, the fourth-longest interception return in program history and the second overtime win over New Mexico State is as many years.
"That was a heck of a homecoming game," said Bears head coach
Casey Woods
. "I'm really proud of our fans and our students for showing up today in great numbers and supporting us. I'm also proud of our team for staying determined throughout the second half and fighting until the very end."
Lair's touchdown was his fourth rushing score in five games. He had 28 ground yards on Mo State's final offensive drive of the game, including the dramatic game-tying score. Receiver
Isaiah Myers
also shared the spotlight down the stretch with acrobatic catches for 23 and 29 yards to set up a first-and-goal at the five.
New Mexico State had 51 seconds left on the clock on its final possession of regulation but was unable to get much traction with
Christian Ford
and
Josh Lukusa
coming up big on defense for the Bears.
With Missouri State collecting just two first downs in the second half prior to its final offensive series, the Bears relied heavily on their defense to keep them in the game.
Jared Lloyd
led the way with 15 total stops, while Ford tallied 8 tackles.
J.J. O'Neal
,
Jashawn Cooper
and Tim Brantley also collected 7 stops apiece with Boamah bringing down 5 ball carriers with a forced fumble, fumble recovery, two pass breakups and walk-off pick six to end the game.
Aggies running back James Jones carried 25 times for 150 yards and a touchdown to lead the visitors with TJ Washington Jr. collecting 107 yards on 15 attempts. Quarterback Trey Hedden finished 11-of-28 for 110 yards, two touchdowns and two interceptions.
Missouri State took a 14-7 lead into the half but was kept in check early in the second half with five second-half punts and two drives that ended with fourth-down stops. Meanwhile, the Aggies tied the game with a 14-yard pass from Hedden to Washington with 4:47 to go in the third and took the lead with a one-yard carry by Jones at the 9:09 mark of the fourth.
With 5:04 remaining in regulation, the Bears turned the ball over on downs at their own 27 but dodged a bullet with a missed 22-yard field goal by NM State's Kyah Francone a couple minutes later.
With the game in the balance, Missouri State took over at its own 20 and 2:46 on the clock while
Henry Belin IV
went to work. The Missouri State quarterback led the Bears on an 8-play, 80-yard scoring drive in crunch time to tie things up, 21-21, on Lair's dramatic score and spark the homecoming victory.
Belin finished 20-of-37 for 187 yards with an interception and a touchdown. Lair carried 11 times for a team-high 46 yards, and
Mekhi Miller
led MSU's receiving corps with a career-high 6 catches for 54 yards. Myers finished with 5 grabs for 89 yards, and
Jmariyae Robinson
had 4 catches for 10 yards and a TD to eclipse a pair of school records.
In the first half, the Bears took a 14-7 lead at the break, thanks in part to a 15-yard pick six by
Dylan Brooks
early in the second quarter. The sixth-year defensive end swiped a Hedden pass out of the air on third-and-19 and found paydirt.
The Aggies bounced back with a long kickoff return that set up a Hedden pass to Brodie Malone-Bradford six plays later which knotted the score at 7 apiece with 10:01 to go in the half.
Missouri State then embarked on its longest scoring drive of the campaign with Belin marching the Bears to a go-ahead score with 68 seconds to go in the second quarter. The 17-play, 90-yard march took 8 minutes, 47 seconds before Belin connected with Robinson for a 9-yard TD strike.
Robinson's catch eclipsed
Jermaine Saffold
's career yardage record of 2,221 and matched the career standard for TD receptions (18) as well while giving the Bears a 7-point advantage at the intermission.
For his career, Robinson now stands at 182 receptions for 2,228 yards and 18 touchdowns – all of which are Mo State career records. He shares the TD receptions record with Steve Newbold (1977-80) and Jeff Hewitt (1998-2000).
The win marked Missouri State's sixth consecutive homecoming victory. The two defensive scores vs. New Mexico State also pushed the Bears to five defensive touchdowns over their last seven ball games.
After a road game at Jacksonville State next Saturday (Nov. 21) at 1 p.m., the Bears return to Robert W. Plaster Stadium for the regular-season finale on Nov. 28 against Liberty.
A "pick six" ticket deal is already available for the Liberty game with bleacher tickets available for $6 plus fees. All remaining tickets for the Liberty game are available now at
MissouriStateBears.com
or by visiting the Great Southern Bank Arena Box Office, (417) 836-7678, during regular box office hours.
