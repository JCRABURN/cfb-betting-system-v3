Bourgeois named a semifinalist for Lou Groza Award
11/17/2026 11:00:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
By:
@MTAthletics
West Palm Beach, Fla.
-- The Palm Beach County Sports Commission has released the 20 semifinalists for the 2026 Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Award presented by the Orange Bowl and Middle Tennessee's
Dominic Bourgeois
is on the prestigious list.
Bourgeois, a senior out of Baltimore, Maryland, enters play this weekend 14-for-17 on field goals while standing an impressive 39-for-42 for his career. Bourgeois, who is 3-for-5 this year on field goals of 50 or more yards, ranks second in CUSA in scoring with 69 points.
Bourgeois is chasing the single-season record of 18 made field goals set by Crews Holt (2018) and Alan Gendreau (2009). He connected on a 52-yard field goal earlier this year at Marshall which went down as the longest by a Blue Raider since 2010 and matched the fifth longest in school history.
Semifinalists will be voted on by a panel of more than 100 FBS head coaches, SIDs, media members, former Groza finalists, and current NFL kickers to select the three finalists. These finalists will be announced on November 24
th
and honored at the 35
th
annual Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Awards Banquet on December 7
th
in Palm Beach County. The same panel then selects the winner, who will be announced live on ESPN at the Home Depot College Football Awards on Friday, December 11
th
.
The Award is named for National Football League Hall of Fame kicker Lou "The Toe" Groza, who played 21 seasons with the Cleveland Browns. Groza won four NFL championships with Cleveland and was named NFL Player of the Year in 1954. Although an All-Pro offensive lineman as well, Groza ushered in the notion that there should be a place on an NFL roster for a kicker.
Since the first Lou Groza Award was handed out in 1992, 34 finalists, including 18 winners, have gone on to appear in the NFL, earning 13 trips to the Pro Bowl and taking home 8 Super Bowls. That list includes 2026 NFL kickers Daniel Carlson Jake Elliott, Ka'imi Fairbairn, Ryan Fitzgerald, Matt Gay, Zane Gonzalez, Joshua Karty, Jake Moody, Will Reichard, Cairo Santos and Andre Szmyt.
