SERIES HISTORY
» The Blue Raiders and Flames have met four times on the football field and the series is led by Liberty 3-1. The teams first met in 1982 when they were called Liberty Baptist and MTSU won that game, 27-7. Both teams were I-AA members in the 1982 contest. The two teams met in 2021 at Liberty in a non-conference game with the Flames winning, 41-13. The meeting in 2023 was the first as conference foes and the Blue Raiders fell just short, 42-35 before losing to the Flames in 2024, 37-17.

OFFENSIVE EXPLOSION
» MTSU ended a seven-year drought against WKU in large part due to an impressive offensive output. The Blue Raiders put up 51 points, 513 yards and no turnovers in the win. It was the most points scored in the
Derek Mason
era and the most by the Blue Raiders since scoring 51 points in a win over Old Dominion in 2018. The 513 yards marked the first time the unit has topped the 500 mark since gaining 541 yards at WKU in 2025. It was also the fifth time this season MTSU has not committed a turnover.
MARTIN COMING UP BIG IN RUN GAME
» After a career-best day against WKU, running back
Antonio Martin Jr.
now has five 100-yard rushing games on the year. He is the first Blue Raider to have five 100-yard games in a season since I'Tavius Mathers in 2016 (Mathers had 8). Martin, who had 125 yards and a personal-best two TDs in the win over WKU, has had 100 yard games against Murray State, Nevada, Jax State, and KSU. Entering the Liberty game, Martin  has 711 rushing yards to rank third in CUSA (35th nationally).
BOURGEOIS CHASING RECORD
»
Dominic Bourgeois
has connected on 13 of 16 three-pointers this season. The all-time single-season record is 18 set by Crews Holt (2018) and Alan Gendreau (2009). The 13 made field goals are the most by an MTSU kicker since
Zeke Rankin
hit 16 in 2022. He needs one more to equal the eighth most in a single-season.
CHASING 1,000
» Antonio Martin is on pace to become the 12th different player in MTSU history to reach the 1,000-yard mark and the first since 2019 when quarterback Asher O'Hara had 1,058 yards. The last MTSU running back to record 1,000 yards in a season was I'Tavius Mathers in 2016 when he had 1,561 yards. Martin enters play against Liberty with 711 yards.
300-100-100
» The MTSU offense was in rare air in the win over WKU. The Blue Raiders had a 300-yard passer, a 100-yard receiver and a 100-yard rusher with
Roman Gagliano
passing for 312 yards,
Ziyon Butler
recording 129 receiving yards and Antonio Martin adding 125 yards on the ground. It marked the first time that has been accomplished at MTSU since Oct. 15, 2016 (vs WKU) when Brent Stockstill threw for 434 yards, Richie James had 223 receiving yards and I'Tavius Mathers added 143 yards on the ground.
FORCING TURNOVERS
» After nine games, the Blue Raiders have done a good job in forcing and coming away with turnovers. MTSU enters the Liberty game ranked T2nd in CUSA with 12 turnovers gained. The defense has registered six fumble recoveries and six interceptions. The team had 15 gained turnovers last year and are nearly to that mark in nine games. For the season, MTSU is +6 in turnover margin to rank 18th nationally and second in CUSA.
FRESHMAN BREAKOUT
» True freshman
Ziyon Butler
turned in a breakout game in the win over WKU. The Jacksonville, Fla., native hauled in six passes for 129 yards and a touchdown and it was the most receiving yards by a Blue Raider this season and the most overall since
Omari Kelly
had 239 yards against WKU in 2024. The 129 yards receiving also went down as the most by a freshman at MTSU since Richie James had 198 yards against FAU in 2015.
