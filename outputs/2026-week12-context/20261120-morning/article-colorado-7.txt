How to watch, listen and follow along — plus five things to know — for CU's game against the Bearcats
By:
CUBuffs.com
CINCINNATI– The Colorado Buffaloes embark on their final road trip of the 2026 season with a trip to Cincinnati on Nov. 21 to take on the Bearcats at
Nippert Stadium
. The game is scheduled to kick-off at 10:02 a.m. MT and will be streamed on ESPN+
The Buffs (4-6, 2-5 Big 12) are coming off a sensational
42-32 upset victory
over the 14th-ranked Houston Cougars at Folsom Field on Friday, Nov. 13. The win was CU's first over an opponent ranked in the Top-15 since 2007 and is one of the more dramatic victories in program history, with the Buffaloes erasing a 20-7 first-half deficit by scoring the next 28 points to seize control of the game. Cincinnati (5-5, 2-5 Big 12) has lost three straight games after falling 41-13 at Iowa State on Nov. 14. The Bearcats have been tough to beat at home, racking up a 5-1 record this year, which includes an upset win over then-ranked No. 8 Texas Tech on Oct. 24.
Saturday's game will be just the third meeting all-time between the Buffaloes and Bearcats, with CU holding a 2-0 advantage. Those two meetings came 52 years apart, with Colorado beating Cincinnati at home 56-14 on Sept. 16, 1972 and then triumphing again at Folsom 34-23 on Oct. 26, 2024, a win that made CU bowl-eligible in a full season for the first time since 2016.  This will be the first time in program history the Buffs will travel to Nippert Stadium.

Five Things To Know
1
-
Toppling No. 14:
Colorado beat No. 14 Houston 42-32 Friday night at Folsom Field, its first win over an AP top-15 team since a 27-24 win over No. 3 Oklahoma in 2007 and its highest-ranked win in the history of the CFP rankings. The Buffs trailed 20-7 with 2:36 left in the first half and scored the next 28 points, tied for the 22nd-largest comeback in school history. CU converted 10-of-14 third downs (71.4 percent), its best rate since matching it against Hawai'i in 2010, and posted 466 yards, its most since 2024.
2 -  O-Line of the Week:
Colorado ran 39 times for 249 yards and 5 touchdowns without allowing a sack, and the line of
Xavier Payne
,
Chauncey Gooden
,
Sean Kinney
,
Leon Bell
and
Larry Johnson III
was named the Big 12 Offensive Line of the Week. The 249 yards were CU's most since 2021 and most against a ranked team since 2016; the 5 rushing touchdowns were the most since 2020.
3 -  Three In One Quarter:
Richard Young
(38 yards),
DeKalon Taylor
(67) and
JaQuail Smith
(32) all scored on long runs in the second quarter. It was the first time three different Buffs scored on runs of 30 or more yards in the same game since 1958, and only the second time it has happened in the same quarter; the other was also the second quarter, against Kansas State in 1954 (runs of 95, 50 and 46). Taylor's 67-yarder on his only carry was CU's longest run since 2020. "Trackhawk" hit 21.1 mph on the play, the fastest player in the FBS for the week. Smith's 32-yarder with 15 seconds left in the half gave CU its first lead, 21-20.
4 - Rando's Night:
Randon Fontenette
was named the Big 12 Defensive Player of the Week after a career-high 14 tackles, including 12 solo stops. On Houston's first snap after CU's only turnover, which opened the second half, he strip-sacked Conner Weigman and
Santana Hopper
recovered. Fontenette is the third straight Buff to win the award, following
Gideon ESPN Lampron
the previous two weeks. It's the first time that has happened in CU history and CU's fourth this season. Fontenette has 90 tackles
5 -  JoJo Tops 2K:
Joseph Williams'
53-yard touchdown catch pushed him past 2,000 college receiving yards (2,010) and was his 100th reception as a Buff. He caught five passes for 80 yards, his sixth game this season with at least 80, and has 63 catches for 933 yards and 7 touchdowns, moving into 12th on CU's single-season lists for both receptions and receiving yards.
