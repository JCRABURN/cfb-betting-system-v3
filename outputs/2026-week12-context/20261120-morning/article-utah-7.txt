Utes in the Pros: Week 11
11/19/2026 7:17 PM
|
Football
Share:
SALT LAKE CITY
– The second half of the NFL season is here as 19 former Utah Utes are expected to suit up in Week 11. All throughout the league, 30 Utah Football alumni are on team rosters.
Six teams with Pro Utes on the roster either lead their division or are in a tie for first place, highlighted by a three-way tie in the NFC North between Detroit, Minnesota and Chicago.
In addition to on the field, former Utah Utes are making their mark on NFL sidelines as well. Former quarterback Brian Johnson (2004-08) is now on the Los Angeles Rams' staff as a senior offensive assistant, while Utah signal-caller Mike McCoy (1992-94) has joined the Las Vegas Raiders as assistant head coach.
