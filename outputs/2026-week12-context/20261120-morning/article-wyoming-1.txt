Wyoming hosts New Mexico in high-stakes game Friday at War Memorial Stadium
By:
Ryan Thorburn
EL PASO, Texas – Wyoming took care of business on the final road trip of the regular season by grinding out a 27-15 victory over UTEP late Saturday night at the Sun Bowl.
The Cowboys (7-3 overall, 6-1 Mountain West), who have won five consecutive games, host New Mexico on Friday at War Memorial Stadium (7 p.m., FS1). The Lobos (8-2, 5-1) dropped into a tie in the loss column with UW in the conference standings after losing 21-17 to UNLV earlier Saturday.
"We have a big one next Friday, and we're going to enjoy this one until we land in Laramie tonight," UW head coach
Jay Sawvel
said during his postgame radio interview. "After that it is on to the next one, and that next one is a big one."
Landon Sims
, making his first career start in place of an injured
Tyler Hughes
, finished 19-for-25 passing for 289 yards and a touchdown.
Tight end Kyle Frend had three receptions for 110 yards in the first half and finished with 113 yards on four targets. Samuel "Tote" Harris led UW with 66 yards rushing and also had 82 yards receiving.
"I thought Landon played really well. It's not a surprise that he played really well," Sawvel said. "We'll be fine with
Landon Sims
at quarterback. I don't question that."
The Miners (1-9, 0-6) scored the first nine points of the second half to cut their 18-point halftime deficit to 24-15.
UTEP placekicker Carolos Arreola made a 55-yard field goal for the only points in the third quarter. Elijah McCoy added a 1-yard touchdown run with 12:00 remaining but the 2-point conversion attempt failed.
After UW's third lost fumble, Arreola missed a 37-yard field goal with a chance to make it a one-score game.
Sims hit Harris for a 49-yard completion that set up a 41-yard field goal by
Erik Sandvik
to give UW a 27-15 cushion with 6:40 remaining.
Jack Dunkley
came up with a fourth-down sack to end UTEP's next drive and upset hopes.
"You never minimize winning. We're happy to go 1 and 0 this week," Sawvel said. "We also know that the second half of that game we could have played a lot better. We played a good first half."
The Pokes led 24-6 at the intermission after racking up 293 yards and 11 first downs. UW finished with a 443-305 advantage in total yards.
After giving up an 80-yard touchdown run by quarterback Raymond Moore III that gave the Miners an early 6-0 lead, the Cowboys reeled off 24 unanswered points to seize control before halftime.
Frendt had catches of 45 and 22 yards to get the visitors in the red zone in the first quarter. Running back
Markell Holman
flipped a 3-yard touchdown pass to tight end
Landon Pace
on a beautifully designed play by offensive coordinator
Christian Taylor
.
The extra point gave UW a 7-6 lead.
Jones Thomas
created pressure to force a muffed punt by UTEP. On the next snap Sims threw a 21-yard touchdown pass to Harris.
Jaden DaCosta
forced a fumble that was recovered by Thomas to thwart a scoring opportunity for the Miners at the UW 25-yard line.
On fourth-and-2, Harris took a pitch on the right side of the formation and scampered for a 33-yard touchdown to make the score 21-6 with 8:26 remaining in the second quarter.
Sandvik made a 30-yard field goal with 54 seconds left in the half.
The Cowboys lead the all-time series with UTEP 27-6-1. The previous meeting between the former WAC rivals was a 42-19 UW win on Nov. 25, 1995 in El Paso.
