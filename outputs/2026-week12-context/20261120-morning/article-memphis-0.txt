Memphis-Temple Set for 2:30 p.m. Kickoff on Black Friday
Nov 16, 2026
|
Football
Share:
IRVING, Texas –
The American Conference has announced its television designations for Nov. 27, with Memphis set to kick off its regular season finale against Temple at 2:30 p.m. in Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium.
UP NEXT
Memphis (8-2, 5-1 American) heads to Annapolis on Saturday, Nov. 21 for a meeting with Navy (3-6, 2-4 American) at 11 a.m. CT, with the game airing on ESPN2.
BUY TICKETS NOW
Single-game tickets can be purchased online at
THIS LINK
or by calling the Memphis Ticket Office at (901) 678-2331. For group tickets and experiences, please contact the Memphis Ticket Office at (901) 678-2331.
