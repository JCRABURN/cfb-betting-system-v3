Sheppard Named ACC Co-Running Back of the Week
11/16/2026 2:07:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
DURHAM, N.C. –
Duke running back
Nate Sheppard
received ACC Co-Running Back of the Week accolades for his performance at No. 5 Miami on Saturday.
Sheppard now has seven ACC weekly honors in his career (six running back and one rookie), tied with
Ben Bennett
(1980-83) and
Jamison Crowder
(2011-14) for the second most by a Blue Devil in a career. In addition, his five ACC player of the week accolades are tied with
Clarkston Hines
(1989) for most in a year by a Blue Devil. Hines, a College Football Hall of Fame member, was the league's wide receiver of the week five times in 1989. Sheppard's six career running back of the week picks are tied with Bennett for the most offensive back selections in a career by a Blue Devil.
Within the league, Sheppard is first individual to receive five ACC Running Back of the Week accolades in a season since Boston College's AJ Dillon in 2019. Sheppard is also the first to receive ACC Running Back of the Week accolades in three consecutive weeks since Trey Benson of Florida State in 2022.
At Miami on Saturday, Sheppard registered 16 carries for 131 yards and two touchdowns and 12 receptions for 93 yards while completing both pass attempts for 16 yards. He posted 224 yards from scrimmage, the most by a Miami opponent since Javonte Williams and Michael Carter racked up 236 and 308 scrimmage yards, respectively, for North Carolina on Dec. 12, 2020. Sheppard's 131 ground yards were the most by a Miami opponent since Virginia Tech's Bhayshul Tuten tallied 141 yards on Sept. 27, 2024.
With his 131 yards on Saturday, Sheppard set the Duke single-season record with 1,320 yards. Sheppard's 100-yard performance was his sixth of the season, tied for the most in a year by a Blue Devil. His nine career 100-yard rushing games are the sixth most by a Blue Devil.
Sheppard tallied two rushing touchdowns to increase his total to 19 ground scores in 2026, already a Duke single-season record. His 23 total touchdowns scored this season are the most in a campaign for a Blue Devil while his 30 career rushing touchdowns and just one shy of tying
Brandon Connette
(2010-13/31) for the program record. Sheppard is up to 35 career touchdowns scored, the second most in a career by a Blue Devil, behind only
Hines (1986-89/38).
Sheppard caught a career-high 12 passes during the contest, marking his fourth game of the season with five or more receptions and his second with 10 or more.
The ACC's other weekly honors included Miami's Darian Mensah (quarterback), Stanford's Micah Ford (co-running back), Charlie Eckhardt (defensive back) and Emmet Kenney (specialist), Syracuse's Calvin Russell III (receiver and rookie) and Tunmise Adeleye (defensive lineman), Wake Forest's Will Way (offensive lineman) and Virginia's Kam Robinson (linebacker).
