OREGON STATE RETURNS HOME TO FACE UTAH STATE
Oregon State returns to Reser Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 21, to host Utah State. Kickoff is set for 7:30 p.m. PT.
The game will air on CBS Sports Network and on the Beaver Sports Radio Network, which can also be heard on the Varsity Network for mobile apps and smartphones in addition to a direct link found on osubeavers.com.
Jordan Kent will call play-by-play with Robert Turbin serving as the analyst and Brandon Baylor as the sideline reporter.
Oregon State leads the all-time series with Utah State, 3-1, and is 2-0 in Corvallis. The Aggies won the last meeting, 24–13, in the LA Bowl on Dec. 18, 2021, the first meeting since 1998.
The matchup is the first between the teams in Corvallis since 1997, a 24-16 win by the Beavers.
The Beavers will be honoring their senior class prior to Saturday's game.
The matchup marks the last in conference play for Oregon State and the rest of the Pac-12 as the "flex" contests on Nov. 28 are considered non-conference.
Oregon State enters Saturday's game tied for fourth in the Pac-12 standings but the Beavers are still alive for a berth in the conference title game. OSU can still play in the game with a win and losses by San Diego State, Texas State and Fresno State.
Saturday's game marks the last of the season at Reser Stadium, where Oregon State is averaging 35,580 fans over the first five home dates of 2026.
Jesse Legree
is fourth in the Oregon State record books with 1,349 receiving yards, just 30 shy of Mike Hass' total in 2004. He is 183 shy of Hass' total in 2005, which is second in OSU's history. Legree is a Biletnikoff Award semifinalist, announced Monday morning.

ON THIS DATE
Oregon State has played 18 games on Nov. 21 and is 11-6-1 on the date.
OSU last played on the date in 2020, a 31-27 win over California at Reser Stadium. Jermar Jefferson ran 18 times for 196 yards and a touchdown.
Thirteen of those 18 games were against Oregon, and the Beavers are 7-5-1 in them. The most recent was the 44-41 double-overtime win over the Ducks in 1998.
Jacquizz Rodgers ran 24 times for 165 yards and two touchdowns in a 42-10 win at Washington State on Nov. 21, 2009.
FORTY-POINT GAMES
Oregon State scored 41 points at Boise State, its fifth 40-point game of the season (52-17 over Montana, 56-26 at Colorado State, 66-14 over San Diego State, 48-30 against Washington State and a loss to Boise State, 46-41). That ties the 2003 and 2013 teams for the most in a season in school history, with two games remaining. No Beaver team had more than three before 2002.
Oregon State is averaging 38.3 points per game, the highest in school history for a season of nine or more games, ahead of 34.8 for the 2013 team. Its 383 points through 10 games are the most in school history, ahead of 379 for the 2023 team.
The 41 points are the sixth-most the Beavers have scored in a loss, behind 53 against Washington State in 2019, 46 against Eastern Washington in 2013 and 42 in 1979, 1996 and 2015, and the most since 2019. Three of the five 40-point games this season reached 50 or more (52, 56 and 66).

JAMARCUS SHEPHARD'S FIRST SEASON
JaMarcus Shephard
was named Oregon State's head coach this past November.
He came to Corvallis after spending two seasons at Alabama, most recently as the Crimson Tide's assistant head coach, co-offensive coordinator and wide receivers coach.
He arrived at Oregon State with a reputation as one of the nation's premier developers of wide receivers, having coached a consensus All-American, a first-team All-American, multiple NFL draft picks and a string of nationally ranked passing offenses across stops at Alabama, Washington, Purdue, Washington State and Western Kentucky.
Shephard coached in the 2023 national title game while with the Huskies. Washington's passing game ranked second nationally at 343.7 yards per game while the Huskies scored 36.0 points per game. The unit featured one of the nation's top wide receiver corps with a pair of 1,000-yard seasons from first team All-American Rome Odunze, who recorded 92 receptions for 1,640 yards and 13 touchdowns, and Ja'Lynn Polk, who caught 69 passes for 1,159 yards and nine scores.
The duo went on to be selected in the first and second rounds, respectively, of the 2024 NFL Draft.

BOISE STATE NOTES
Maalik Murphy
completed 31 of 39 passes for 396 yards and three touchdowns, his career high in yards. His previous best was 371 against Fresno State on Sept. 6, 2025. His touchdown passes went to
Jesse Legree
(15 yards),

Murphy's 396 yards give him 552 this season and 2,357 in two seasons at Oregon State. He has 5,767 career passing yards and 42 touchdown passes. His interception was returned 58 yards for a touchdown by Travis Anderson with 1:47 left in the first quarter, and Boise State converted a two-point try for a 15-7 lead.
Jesse Legree
caught 12 passes for 140 yards and a touchdown, his eighth 100-yard game of the season, tying Brandin Cooks (2013) for second in school history behind Mike Hass' nine in 2005. He has 1,349 receiving yards and 15 touchdown catches this season, fourth and second on Oregon State's single-season lists.
Eric Olsen
caught four passes for 67 yards and a 20-yard touchdown, and has now caught at least one pass in 25 consecutive games. He has 43 catches for 502 yards and five touchdowns this season, second and third among Beaver tight ends since 2001 in catches and yards.
Cornell Hatcher Jr.
scored on a 2-yard run in the second quarter, his sixth straight game with a rushing touchdown.
Jake Reichle
ran for two touchdowns (1 and 5 yards).
Oregon State's 41 points are the most in a loss since it scored 53 against Washington State on Nov. 23, 2019, and fourth-most in a loss since 2001. Boise State's 46 are the most Oregon State has allowed since Oregon scored 49 on Sept. 14, 2024.
Oregon State scored on its first possession, a seven-play, 75-yard drive capped by Legree's touchdown.
Oregon State did not intercept a pass, ending a streak of seven straight games with an interception, the longest since eight straight in 2012. Boise State's first drive ended in a punt, and opponents have scored on their first drive in one of the last nine games (Texas State).
