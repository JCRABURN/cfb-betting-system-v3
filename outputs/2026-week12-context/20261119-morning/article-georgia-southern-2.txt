Team looking to go 1-0 on the week for fifth straight week
STATESBORO -
The Georgia Southern football squad hits the road for the second straight week as it travels to Alabama to take on Troy in Sun Belt Conference action. Kickoff is set for 4:02 p.m. (ET)/3:02 p.m. (local) at Veterans Memorial Stadium and it will be carried on ESPN+. The game will also be available via radio on the Georgia Southern Sports Network. Of Note:
Georgia Southern and Troy have met 22 times with the Trojans holding a 15-7 lead in the series that dates back to 1934. Since GS restarted football in 1984 as an NCAA program, the series is tied at 7-7 after Troy's win in 2024 in Statesboro. The Eagles have lost three straight games in Veterans Memorial Stadium after winning their first three in the venue.
The Eagles are 26-18 all-time in the modern era against teams from the Yellowhammer State and 13-13 in games played in the state of Alabama.
Georgia Southern has six players from Alabama on its roster, including starting tight end
River Helms
, who is from Lester.
Quarterback
Max Johnson
has thrown three interceptions in 326 passes this year (109:1 ratio). He has streaks of 127 and 118 passes without an interception, the fourth and fifth longest, respectively, in program history.
Seventh-year offensive lineman
Caelan Williams
is the one player on Georgia Southern's roster who owns a win over Troy as an Eagle. That came in 2000 when Williams was redshirting.
