Bishop & Ranvier Named Semifinalists for Doak Walker and Lou Groza Awards
November 17, 2026
|
Football
Share:
KNOXVILLE, Tenn. –
Tennessee running back
DeSean Bishop
and placekicker
Cooper Ranvier
were named semifinalists for the Doak Walker and Lou Groza Awards, respectively, on Tuesday.
Bishop is just the third Tennessee running back – and the second in the
Josh Heupel
era – to be named a semifinalist for the Doak Walker Award, joining Dylan Sampson (2024) and Travis Stephens (2001), who is the only Vol tailback to be named a finalist for the award.
A former walk-on, Bishop has become just the second player in Tennessee history to produce back-to-back 1,000-yard rushing seasons. Johnnie Jones previously accomplished the feat in 1983 (1,116) and 1984 (1,290). Bishop reached the milestone in his ninth game of the season with a 109-yard performance against No. 6 Kentucky.
Bishop's 1,163 rushing yards this season rank 13th in Tennessee single-season history, putting him 73 yards away from cracking the top 10. His total also leads the SEC, ranks fifth in the Power Four and seventh in the FBS through 10 games.
Averaging 116.3 rushing yards per game, Bishop leads the SEC and ranks eighth nationally. His 13 rushing touchdowns rank second in the SEC and are tied for ninth in the FBS. That total is also tied for eighth in Tennessee single-season history.
Ranvier is the seventh Vol placekicker to be named a semifinalist for the Lou Groza Award and is the second in the Heupel era. He joins Charles Campbell (2023), Brent Cimaglia (2019), Daniel Lincoln (2007), James Wilhoit (2006), Alex Walls (2000) – the only finalist – and Jeff Hall (1995).
The Lexington, Kentucky, native has been spectacular in his first season as a Vol, leading the SEC in field goal percentage at 94.7 percent after converting 18-of-19 attempts. That mark ranks fourth in the SEC and is currently the best in Tennessee history, surpassing the previous school record of 92.3 percent set by John Becksvoort (12-of-13 in 1993).
Dating back to last season at Louisville, Ranvier has made 22 of his last 24 field goal attempts. He has converted 13 consecutive field goal attempts and is a perfect 35-of-35 on PATs this season. Ranvier owns a season-long of 49 yards and is 9-of-9 on attempts between 40 and 49 yards.
At Texas A&M on Nov. 14, he tied the Tennessee school record for made field goals in a game, delivering a perfect 5-of-5 performance. His five made field goals established a career high and were the most by a Vol in 26 years as No. 8 Tennessee defeated Texas A&M, 22-7, on the road.
Ranvier matched four other Vols who share the school record: Alex Walls vs. Florida in 2000, Fuad Reveiz vs. Kentucky in 1982 and vs. Memphis State in 1982, and Alan Duncan in 1978 against Kentucky. Ranvier more than doubled the Aggies' point total (16) with field goals from 23, 30, 32, 44 and 27 yards.
