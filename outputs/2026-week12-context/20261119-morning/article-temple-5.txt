Owls in the NFL - Week 10
11.17.26
|
Football
Share:
By:
Adam Borenstein
Sacks keep coming for Titans' linebacker Jacob Martin (2014-17). He recorded yet another sack on Sunday, bringing his season total to 7.5. Martin has now recorded at least one sack in six of his nine games this season. The Titans fell to the Jaguars, 40-23, while tight end David Martin-Robinson (2018-23) did not record any statistics.
Kicker Brandon McManus (2009-12) remained perfect on field goals since signing with the Rams during their 34-6 win over the Cardinals. He converted both of his attempts from 24 and 43 yards while also adding four PATs. With his two field goals, McManus surpassed 300 made field goals for his career.
The Bills continued their winning ways, defeating the Jets, 31-12, to improve to 8-1 on the season. Buffalo totaled 389 yards of offense behind the play of left tackle Dion Dawkins (2013-16), while safety Sam Franklin (2016-19) recorded one tackle.
Cornerback Rock Ya-Sin (2018) and the Lions traveled to Munich, Germany, to take on the Patriots. Ya-Sin recorded two tackles, including a tackle for loss, and a pass breakup as Detroit cruised to a 35-10 victory.
Linebacker Jordan Magee (2019-23) did not play in the Commanders' 24-21 win over the Giants on Thursday Night Football.
Head Coach Todd Bowles (1982-85) and the Buccaneers were on a bye this week. Tampa Bay will return to action next Sunday against Ya-Sin and the Lions.
Linebacker Arnold Ebiketie (2017-20) and the Eagles were also on a bye this week. Philadelphia will return to action Sunday when they take on the Steelers.
The following players remain on inactive rosters:
Dallas – OL Matt Hennessy (2016–19) –Injured
Philadelphia – CB Christian Braswell (2017-20) – Practice Squad
