Houston Game to be Televised on TNT/TruTV/HBO MAX at 1 p.m., ET
November 15, 2026 11:12 AM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Mike Montoro
MORGANTOWN, W.Va. –
The Big 12 Conference has announced that WVU's Nov. 21 football game against No. 14/16 Houston at Milan Puskar Stadium will be televised on TNT and will be simulcast on TruTV and HBO MAX at 1 p.m., ET.
Tickets for WVU's senior day matchup against Houston are available for purchase by
CLICKING HERE
.
Saturday, Nov. 21 vs. Houston
|
GET TICKETS
Senior Day
The matchup with Houston on Nov. 21 will be Senior Day.
Gold and Blue Gratitude
The game against the Cougars will serve as an opportunity to thank Mountaineer football fans for their support all season long as part of Gold and Blue Gratitude. WVU will aim to impact more than 1,000 ticket holders via fan experience opportunities and giveaways at the game to show appreciation!
Group Ticket Packages
The Mountaineer Ticket Sales and Service team is offering special group ticket packages to the Houston game for WVU Parent's Club. For more information on group ticket sales,
CLICK HERE
.
