'Cuse and Notre Dame Set for Noon Kick on ABC
11/16/2026 4:31:00 PM
|
Football
Get Tickets
Share:
Syracuse's regular season finale has been set for a Noon start on ABC, the ACC announced on Monday.
Syracuse hosts No. 3 Notre Dame for Senior Day, and the final game in the Loud House this season. Tickets are available at
Cuse.com/FootballTickets
.
Winners of three of their last four, Syracuse first hits the road to play at Boston College on Saturday, for a Noon start on ACC Network.
Don't miss your last chance to see the Orange in the Loud House this season and secure your seats today!
