Football’s Start Time and Network Announced for North Texas
Nov 15, 2026
|
Football
Share:
IRVING, TX –
The kickoff time and the network for Tulane football's Nov. 21 home game against North Texas was recently announced by the American Conference.
The Green Wave and the Mean Green square off against each other at 11 a.m. CT on Saturday, Nov. 21, in a game that will also be broadcasted on ESPNU.
Tulane leads the all-time series with North Texas 4-0 since the start of play between the schools in 2013. Tulane has won each of the three games since the Mean Green rejoined the conference.
The Tulane program leads the conference in blocks kicks (3) and punt return defense (2.69). The program is second in defensive touchdowns (2) and net punting (43.38). The Green Wave are third in fourth down conversion defense (42.9 %), kick returns (23.80), and redzone defense (73.9%).
TJ Smith
is second in combined kick return yardage (430).
Cooper Helmke
leads the conference in field goal percentage (86.7%).
Kevin Adams III
and
Anthony Rogers
are tied for second in the conference in interceptions (2).
William Hudlow
leads the conference and ranks seventh nationally in punting (47.0).
Johnnie Daniels
is eighth in rushing touchdowns (6).
Dickson Agu
leads the conference in total tackles per game (9.6) and fumble recoveries (2). Adams is second in the conference and in the nation in solo tackles a game (6.00) and is ninth in the league in total tackles a contest (8.2).
Next, Tulane (5-5, 3-3 American) is at home at Yulman Stadium against North Texas (7-3, 5-1 American) on Nov. 21 at 11 a.m.
TICKETS
Tickets for the football, volleyball and the upcoming men's and women's basketball seasons can be purchased by calling 504-861-WAVE (9283), logging on to TulaneTix.com or visiting the ticket office at the James W. Wilson Jr. Center.
