MOBILE, Ala. –
South Alabama built a 14-point first-quarter lead that proved too much for Troy to overcome, and the Jaguars defeated the Trojans, 27-14, Saturday at Hancock Whitney Stadium in the annual "Battle for the Belt".
The win was just South Alabama's (5-5, 3-3 SBC) second in the last nine meetings between the two schools.
Trae'von Dunbar broke touchdown runs of 46 and 44 yards on South Alabama's first and third drives of the game to build a 14-0 lead.
Making his first start in three weeks,
Goose Crowder
pulled Troy (4-6, 2-4) within a touchdown on a 45-yard run on fourth-and-1 in the opening minutes of the second quarter. It was the longest of Crowder's six career touchdown runs and the second-longest by a Trojan this season.
The Jags added a 29-yard field goal with just over four minutes left in the half, taking a 17-7 lead into the break.
Crowder completed 24-of-41 passes for 231 yards and a touchdown and rushed for 58 yards on 11 carries, despite losing 24 yards on three sacks. South Alabama's Bishop Davenport was far less efficient, completing just 9-of-21 passes for 122 yards and rushing for 39 yards on six carries.
Troy's secondary broke up a season-high eight passes, led by
Jordan Turner's
three and
Justin Powe's
two. It was Turner's first start of the season and first action on defense; he added four tackles while Powe had a team-high nine stops.
Despite the deficiencies through the air, South Alabama found holes in Troy's rushing defense. Led by Dunbar's 153 yards on just 14 carries, the Jaguars rushed for 246 yards, averaging 6.3 yards per carry.
The South Alabama lead swelled to 27-7 midway through the third quarter on a second short field goal and a 28-yard touchdown run by Davenport.
Crowder led the Trojans on a 13-play drive following the South touchdown, capped by a fourth-and-goal touchdown pass to
Nick Hampton
, pulling Troy within 13 with 14:05 to play.
Troy's defense held the Jags on three straight plays, giving the ball back to the offense at the Troy 44-yard line. The Trojans marched down to the Jags' 33-yard line, but an offensive face mask penalty pushed Troy behind the sticks, stalling the drive.
After the defense held again, Troy had a first down at the South Alabama 14-yard line, but a holding penalty again pushed the Trojans behind the sticks, killing the drive – the Trojans had three drives end on downs in the game.
Up Next
Troy closes out its home schedule on Saturday, Nov. 21, when it hosts Sun Belt East Division leader Georgia Southern at The Vet; kickoff is set for 3 p.m.
Quotable – Troy Head Coach
Gerad Parker
"They outplayed us tonight, especially up front early. You can't get down 14 points early to start a game and expect to get back. They won the rushing battle, nobody turned the football over, surprisingly, and if you don't win the turnover margin and the rushing battle, you lose rivalry games."
Notable
• Troy fell to 10-5 all-time against South Alabama.
• Troy fell to 5-3 all-time against South Alabama in Mobile and 2-2 against the Jags at Hancock Whitney Stadium.
• Despite the loss, Troy has won seven of the last nine games in the series.
• Cornerback
Jordan Turner
made the first start of his career; he is the 24th player this season to make his first Troy start.
• Troy won the coin toss (8-2) and elected to defer to the second half. The Trojans are now 28-36 in the last five seasons on the coin toss.
• Troy kicked off to open a game four times this season; the Trojans are 0-4 in those games.
• Troy's opponent has scored on its opening drive of the game in three straight contests after the Trojans had gone five consecutive games without allowing an opening-drive score and 6-of-7 to open the season.
•

45-yard touchdown run on 4th-and-inches in the second quarter was his third rushing touchdown of the season and sixth of his career. It's his first rushing TD since having two in the victory over Southern Miss.
•

six career rushing touchdowns are tied with
