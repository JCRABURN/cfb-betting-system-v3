Except Simon McClannan, donned in his purple hoodie, stands above the rest, this 22-year-old native of Omaha, Nebraska, who serves as that proverbial light among the gray clouds outside, his smile radiating proof that even when times are tough, goodness still exists, and stories of triumph rise from gridiron defeat.
