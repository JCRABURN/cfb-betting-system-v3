Postgame Notes: #18 Oregon 31, Michigan 7
11/14/2026 7:22:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
• Today's loss brings the Wolverines to 5-5 on the season.
• In the all-time series with Oregon, U-M fell to 3-4. The Wolverines are now 0-2 at Autzen Stadium.
• Quarterback
Tommy Carr
started the game and
Bryce Underwood
entered after two drives. Underwood played the remainder of the first half. Carr re-entered at the start of the third quarter, and Underwood saw more time in the fourth.
• Running back
Savion
Hiter
's 70-yard touchdown run put Michigan's only points on the board on the first play of the second quarter. It was the longest touchdown run by a freshman back since Tyrone Wheatley's 74-yard touchdown against Northwestern in 1991.
• Hiter's touchdown was U-M's longest offensive play this year and a career-long rush for the first-year back, who scored the fourth touchdown of his freshman season (all rushing).
• Hiter finished the day with 13 carries, a career-high 107 rushing yards and the score.
• As a whole, the rushing offense produced 5.4 yards per carry on the day: 40 rushes for 215 yards. It marked U-M's second game this season with 200-plus rushing yards (211, Penn State).
• On Oregon's next possession following the U-M touchdown, the Wolverine defense shut down the Ducks from a first-and-goal setup at the four-yard line. Oregon elected to go for the end zone on fourth down, and U-M turned them over on downs.
• Entering the game, Oregon hadn't allowed a sack in its past two contests (Northwestern, Ohio State). That streak ended when
Dom Nichols
sacked Dylan Raiola on third down midway through the third quarter, improving his season total to 3.5 sacks. Oregon missed the ensuing field goal (46 yards).
• Oregon's 21-point first half marked the first time this year any team has eclipsed 14 points in a single half against U-M.
•
Manuel Beigel
, who has made three starts at the rhino position this season, made his first career start at guard on the left side in today's game.
• Beigel's start at guard gave U-M its sixth different starting offensive line combination across 10 games.
• First-year wide receiver
Jaylen Pile
made his collegiate debut in the game, playing one snap out wide.
