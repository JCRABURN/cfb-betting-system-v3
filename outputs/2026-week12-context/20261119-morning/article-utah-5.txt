SALT LAKE CITY
— The No. 2-ranked Utah football team wraps up its regular season road slate on Saturday, Nov. 21 at TCU from Amon G. Carter Stadium. The Utes (10-0, 7-0 Big 12) and Horned Frogs (5-5, 3-4 Big 12) will meet for the 10th time overall and second time as members of the Big 12 Conference. TCU leads the all-time series, 5-4.
SATURDAY LINEUP
Venue:
Amon G. Carter Stadium (Fort Worth, Texas)
Time:
7 p.m. MT
Watch (FOX):
Connor Onion (play-by-play), Robert Griffin III (analyst), Alexa Landestoy (sideline)
ESPN 700 AM & 92.1 FM:
Bill Riley
(play-by-play), Steven Sylvester (analyst), Zack Moss (sideline)
UTAH FOOTBALL: NEED TO KNOWS
•  The Utes are 10-0 to start a season for the third time in program history (2004, '08)
•  Utah has won 16 straight games dating back to the 2025 season, the longest active winning streak in the FBS and tied for the second-longest in recorded program history. Utah also won 16 straight games from 2007-09 and 18 straight games from 2003-05.
•  Utah has won 12 straight conference games, also the longest active streak in the FBS.
•  Since the College Football Playoff was established in 2014, Utah is 24-13 when playing as a CFP-ranked team and 5-3 when ranked in the CFP Top 10.
•
Morgan Scalley
is the third coach in the last 10 seasons to start 10-0 (or better) in his first full season as a FBS head coach, along with Ohio State's Ryan Day in 2019 (13-0) and UCF's Josh Heupel in 2018 (12-0).
•  Utah is the second Big 12 team in the last decade to start 10-0, along with TCU in 2022. Among the five power conference teams to start 10-0 in 2024 (Indiana, Oregon) or 2025 (Indiana, Ohio State, Texas A&M), all five made the CFP.
THE NUMBERS
•  QB
Devon Dampier
has been responsible for multiple touchdowns (pass + rush) in all 10 games this season, the most in the FBS. He has rushed for and thrown for multiple scores in the same game in back-to-back contests and has recorded multiple TD passes in nine of Utah's 10 games.
•  Dampier is the third power conference QB since 2000 with 20+ passing TDs and 8+ rushing TDs in a 10-0 start to the season, joining Justin Fields (Ohio State) in 2019 and Vince Young (Texas) in 2005.
•  In four combined games against the SEC and AP-ranked opponents (Arkansas, No. 14 Houston, No. 7 BYU, No. 22 Arizona), Dampier has had a pass efficiency of 144.4, 62.3% completion rate and has averaged 211.5 passing yards per game.  He rushed for two touchdowns in all four games (for a total of eight) and had 85-plus rushing yards in three of the four, including 100 rushing yards in the road win over Arizona and 150 at home vs. Houston.
•  Utah has led in 74.4 percent of regulation time this season, tied for third highest in the FBS behind Notre Dame (80.3%), Georgia (80.0%) and Ohio State (74.4%).
•  Utah has outscored opponents by 87 points (103-16) in the first quarter this season. That is the second-highest point differential in the first quarter in the FBS (Georgia, +115) and tied for the second-fewest points allowed (Georgia, 14).
