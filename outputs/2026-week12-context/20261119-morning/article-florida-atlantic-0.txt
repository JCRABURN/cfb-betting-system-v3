Where Are They Now: Johnnie Sloan
11/13/2026 11:00:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Ben Paul
BOCA RATON, Fla. –
Johnnie Sloan, a member of the inaugural Florida Atlantic football team, carries the lessons he learned from FAU's inaugural Head Coach Howard Schnellenberger with him to this day as the head coach at Coconut Creek High School.
In four seasons with the Owls, Sloan made an impact on the defensive line and is still in the record books to this day, where he sits at No. 2 all-time in program tackles for loss. Sloan resides at the helm of the Coconut Creek football program and has coached eventual FAU players including Chris Jones and
Nimari Brantley
.
Sloan sat down with
Frank Forte
to talk about his journey from player to coach in "Where Are They Now?".
This segment airs on "Inside the Owls' Burrow," the team's weekly show airing each Thursday throughout the season on the Spot. The Spot, South Florida 39 (WSFL-TV), will air the show weekly on Thursdays at 5:30 p.m. ET in the Miami-Ft. Lauderdale DMA with replays running to be determined. The Spot, South Florida 9 (WHDT-TV), will air the show on Thursday at 6 p.m. ET in the West Palm Beach-Fort Pierce DMA with replays to be determined.
