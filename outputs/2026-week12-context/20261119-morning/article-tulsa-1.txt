TULSA, Okla. —
Tulsa Football is bowl eligible for the first time since 2021 after a 38-21 Homecoming win over Florida Atlantic on Saturday night. Tulsa finished with 309 passing yards and 192 rushing yards and scored touchdowns on all three red-zone trips. This was Tulsa's 56th win on Homecoming and the first since the 2024 season.
Baylor Hayes
completed 21-of-32 passes for 309 yards and two touchdowns.
Javon Ross
finished with six receptions for 163 yards. Alston rushed for 92 yards and a touchdown on 11 carries along with three catches for 28 yards.
Trequan Jones
scored two rushing touchdowns and finished with 88 yards on nine carries.
On Florida Atlantic's opening drive, Tulsa forced a turnover on downs when
Jaylen George
and
Devin Robinson
stopped Leonard Farrow on fourth-and-2, but Tulsa's ensuing possession stalled after an illegal-formation penalty negated a fourth-down completion. Florida Atlantic moved from its 10 to the Tulsa 48 with a 19-yard completion to Kelby Valsin and a fourth-down 15-yard pass to Dom Henry before punting, and the teams traded punts through the middle of the quarter.
Max Tulen
later backed Florida Atlantic to its 2 with a 61-yard punt, and although the visitors neared midfield,
Thomas Davis
had a 9-yard sack during the series before
Dax Collins
ended it with a fourth-down stop of Antonio Johnson for a 6-yard loss.
Baylor Hayes
then completed a 38-yard pass to Ross and a 5-yard touchdown to
Jimmy Calloway
, putting Tulsa ahead 7-0.
Hayes finished Tulsa's opening possession of the second quarter with a 9-yard touchdown pass to Calloway, putting Tulsa ahead 14-0. Florida Atlantic answered with a 68-yard drive that included a fourth-down conversion and ended with Caden Veltkamp's 7-yard touchdown pass to Easton Messer.
Tulsa came back in 41 seconds when Jones broke a 78-yard touchdown run for a 21-7 lead. This was Jones' second longest rush of the season. Florida Atlantic later turned a Tulsa fumble near midfield into points, moving 46 yards and scoring on Brady Tillman's 6-yard run to make it 21-14. Tulsa's final drive reached the Florida Atlantic 37 after Oran Singleton's fourth-down catch, but the possession ended with Tyson Rooks' interception at the goal line as Tulsa carried a 21-14 lead into halftime.
Tulsa's defense produced the first meaningful swing of the third quarter when Florida Atlantic started at the Tulsa 40 but was stopped on downs at the 31 after a fourth-down review overturned the original spot. Hayes immediately hit Ross for 41 yards, added a 10-yard completion to Singleton, and Jones finished the three-play drive with a 2-yard touchdown making it 28-14. Florida Atlantic later turned it over on downs again near midfield, and Tulsa moved back into scoring range behind a 26-yard Hayes completion to Ross before Carey made a 41-yard field goal for a 31-14 lead. The teams traded empty possessions from there before Florida Atlantic used a 28-yard fourth-down completion from Veltkamp to Antonio Johnson to reach the Tulsa 2 in the closing seconds, but the quarter ended with Tulsa still ahead 31-14.
Florida Atlantic opened the fourth quarter with a score on Veltkamp's 2-yard pass to Kelby Valsin, making it 31-21. Tulsa answered one play after the touchback when Alston ran 75 yards for a touchdown, extending Tulsa's lead to 38-21. Florida Atlantic drove to the Tulsa 11 on the next possession, but
Zach Williams
intercepted Veltkamp ending the drive. Tulsa later moved across midfield behind Hayes' 36-yard completion to Singleton before Florida Atlantic intercepted Hayes. Florida Atlantic used the takeaway to move inside the five yardline, but
Tyson Williams
hurried a fourth-down incompletion.
Tulsa would earn its sixth win of the season with a 38-21 victory over FAU. This was the largest homecoming win for TU since the 2018 season.
Tulsa hosts Charlotte at 1 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 21, at H.A. Chapman Stadium in its final home game of the regular season.
