HATTIESBURG, Miss. –
The Southern Miss football team returns to the state of Louisiana for the third time this fall when they travel to Louisiana Tech for a Sun Belt Conference West Division showdown Saturday at Joe Aillet Stadium in Ruston. Game time is set for 4 p.m.
The game can be seen via ESPN+ as well as heard on an
affiliate
of the Southern Miss Sports Network and through SouthernMiss.com.
Southern Miss holds a 1-1 record in the state of Louisiana this season, dropping a 24-21 contest at Tulane in September, while also defeating ULM 35-21 in Monroe last month.
This contest marks the second-straight season the Golden Eagles (3-6 overall, 2-3 Sun Belt) will play in Ruston, as the Bulldogs (7-2, 5-0) return to the Sun Belt Conference in 2026.
Quarterback
John White
is expected to make his second consecutive start for the Golden Eagles after throwing for 266 yards and a touchdown pass last week against James Madison.
Steven Robinson
continues to lead to Southern Miss in rushing with 782 yards on 157 carries for a 5.0 yards per attempt. He currently is tied for the league lead in rushing touchdowns with 10 and his 11 total scores ranks first in the league.
Jamarice Wilder
ranks second with 518 yards on 81 carries.
For the receiving corps,
Mario Sanders
continues to lead the Golden Eagles with 50 catches for 698 yards and six touchdown receptions.
Kaden Saunders
is next with 22 grabs for 333 yards, while
AJ Little
has 18 catches for 275 yards and a touchdown.
On defense, the Golden Eagles are led by
Mathis Haygood
, who has 83 tackles, while
Michael Robinson III
ranks second at 77 and
Landon Sylvie
at 62.
Jameer Lewis
holds the Southern Miss defense lead with four sacks.
This game marks the 55th meeting between the schools, renewing the third longest series in Southern Miss' history.
The Golden Eagles hold a 36-18 record over LA Tech in a series that dates back to 1935. This will be the second consecutive year that the two schools have met in Ruston with the Bulldogs collecting a 30-20 decision a year ago.
Overall, Southern Miss is 17-10 in Ruston as this will be their seventh game there since the 2013 campaign, holding a 3-3 mark over that time.
Following the LA Tech game, the Golden Eagle return home for its final contest at Renasant Stadium this fall against South Alabama, Saturday, Nov. 21. Game time is set for 3 p.m.
