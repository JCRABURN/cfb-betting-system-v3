Wyoming’s Game Time Against UCONN Set for 10 a.m.
11/16/2026 11:53:00 AM
|
Football
Buy Tickets
Share:
Contest on FS1
By:
Nick Seeman
LARAMIE, Wyo. (Nov. 16, 2026) –
The Wyoming Cowboys will face the UCONN Huskies in the regular season finale at 10 a.m. on Saturday, November 28. The contest will be televised on FS1.
Ticket Information
Fans may order tickets online, via email or by phone at:
•Go to GoWyo.com/tickets
•Email tickets@uwyo.edu
•Call (307) 766-7220
•Stop by the UW Athletics Ticket Office on the West Side of the Arena-Auditorium on the corner of Willett Drive and 19th Street
