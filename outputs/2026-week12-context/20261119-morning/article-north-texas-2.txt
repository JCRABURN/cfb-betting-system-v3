White Named Doak Walker Award Semifinalist
11/17/2026 11:00:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
Share:
DALLAS — North Texas senior running back
Jahiem White
was named one of 10 semifinalists for the 2026 Doak Walker Award on Tuesday by the PwC SMU Athletic Forum Board of Directors.
White leads the American Conference, and is fifth nationally, with 1,218 rushing yards and is sixth nationally with 121.8 yards per game. His 16 rushing touchdowns rank fifth nationally, and he averages 6.92 yards per carry. He's also third nationally in total touchdowns with 18.
The Doak Walker award is given annually to the best running back in college football based on performance on the field, athletic ability and character. White is one of 10 semifinalists, and three finalists, as voted on by the Doak Walker Award National Selection Committee, will be announced on November 24. The committee will cast a second vote in December to determine the recipient. The National Selection Committee consists of past recipients, former NFL All-Pro and college All-America running backs, media members and selected special representatives.
The recipient of the 2026 Doak Walker Award will be announced live on
The Home Depot College Football Awards
on December 10. The award is named for SMU's three-time All-America running back Doak Walker.
