NEBRASKA 31 (6-4, 3-4), RUTGERS 0 (2-8, 1-6)
SHI STADIUM • PISCATAWAY, N.J.
NOVEMBER 14, 2026 • ATTENDANCE: 36,425
PISCATAWAY, N.J.
– Rutgers football (2-8, 1-6) lost to Nebraska (6-4, 3-4), 31-0, on Saturday afternoon at SHI Stadium.
RB
Clay Thevenin
carried 10 times for 43 yards and TE
Logan Blake
brought in a career-high three receptions.
DB
JJ Conyers
paced the defense with 10 tackles and a pass breakup, while DB
Devonte Anderson
added eight tackles with one for loss and broke up a pass.
