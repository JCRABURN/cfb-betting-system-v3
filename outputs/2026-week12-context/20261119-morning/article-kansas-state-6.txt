Wildcats in the NFL – Week 10
Nov 17, 2026
|
Football
Share:
Share:
MANHATTAN, Kan.
– Each week throughout the 2026 season, k-statesports.com recaps the action for the 18 former Kansas State football players currently in the NFL.
Felix Anudike-Uzomah, DE – Kansas City Chiefs
Anudike-Uzomah, a first-round pick in 2023, made his ninth start of the season and 12
th
of his career, collecting a tackle for loss and quarterback hit among his 35 defensive snaps in Kansas City's 35-13 victory at Atlanta. In nine games this season, Anudike-Uzomah has registered 15 tackles – including three for a loss – and six quarterback hits among his 312 defensive snaps. The Chiefs are 7-2 prior to hosting Arizona on Sunday.
Cooper Beebe, C – Dallas Cowboys
Beebe, who is in his third season, made the 37
th
start of his career and was in for all 83 offensive snaps during the Cowboys' 46-40 home loss to San Francisco. In 10 games this season, Beebe has played on all 707 offensive snaps for Dallas, which is 4-6 prior to hosting Tennessee on Sunday.
JuJu Brents, CB – Miami Dolphins
In his fourth overall season and second in Miami, Brents started his sixth-straight game as he recorded four tackles among his 42 defensive snaps during the Dolphins' 24-22 loss at Indianapolis on Sunday. Brents has totaled 33 tackles, one tackle for loss, a pass breakup and a fumble recovery over his 386 defensive snaps in nine games this season. Miami, which fell to 1-8, plays at Buffalo on Sunday.
DJ Giddens, RB – Indianapolis Colts
Giddens, who is in his second season, was in for two offensive snaps during Indianapolis' 24-22 home win over Miami. In two games this season, Giddens has rushed six times for 43 yards for the 3-7 Colts, who play at Houston on Thursday night.
Josh Hayes, DB – Tampa Bay Buccaneers
In his fourth season, Hayes is on injured reserve. The Buccaneers, who are 3-6 , had their bye last week and play at Detroit on Sunday.
Sam Hecht, C – Carolina Panthers
Hecht, a fifth-round selection by Carolina in this year's draft, was inactive for the Panthers' 20-17 win at New Orleans. In seven games this season, Hecht has seen time on 35 special teams plays and 11 offensive snaps for Carolina, which is now 4-5 prior to hosting Baltimore on Sunday.
Timmy Horne, DT – Tennessee Titans
In his fourth season, Horne recorded one tackle among his 39 defensive snaps and eight special teams plays during the Titans' 40-23 home loss to Jacksonville. In four games this season, Horne has nine tackles over 109 defensive snaps and 23 special teams plays for the 1-8 Titans, who play at Dallas on Sunday.
Easton Kilty, OL – New Orleans Saints
Kilty, a rookie free agent signee prior to the 2025 season, was in for a pair of special teams plays as New Orleans dropped a 20-17 decision at home to Carolina. The Saints, who are 3-6, play at Chicago on Sunday.
KT Leveston, OL – Cleveland Browns
Leveston, who is in his third season, is on Cleveland's practice squad. The Browns earned a 27-24 home win over Houston to improve to 6-4 as they enter their bye week.
Adrian Martinez, QB – Seattle Seahawks
A second-year player who has seen time with the 49ers and Jets during his career, Martinez was signed to the Seahawks' practice squad two weeks ago. Seattle picked up a 21-13 win at Las Vegas to move to 6-4 as the Seahawks enter their bye week.
Jacob Parrish, CB – Tampa Bay Buccaneers
Parrish, a third-round draft pick in 2025, and the Buccaneers were off last week. Parrish has started 13
th
games in his career and eight this season, recording 39 tackles, four tackles for loss, a league-leading 14 passes defended and two interceptions over 442 defensive snaps. Tampa Bay, which is 3-6, comes out of its bye to play at Detroit on Sunday.
VJ Payne, S – New York Jets
A seventh-round selection in this year's draft, Payne came off injured reserve to make his NFL debut, seeing time on 14 special teams plays during New York's 31-12 home loss to Buffalo. The Jets are 2-8 this year before a game at the Los Angeles Chargers on Sunday.
D.J. Reed, DB – Detroit Lions
In his ninth season overall and second in Detroit, Reed was inactive for the Lions' 35-10 win over New England in Munich, Germany. In seven games this season, Reed has collected 42 tackles, three tackles for loss, an interception and six pass breakups. The Lions improved to 7-2 as they host Tampa Bay on Sunday.
Dalton Risner, OL – Cincinnati Bengals
Risner, who is in his eighth season overall and second in Cincinnati, made the 101
st
regular-season start of his career but only played on 32 snaps as he came out of the game against Pittsburgh, one that the Bengals won 38-18 on Sunday night. In nine games this season, Risner has seen time on 535 offensive snaps for the 7-2 Bengals, who play at Washington on Sunday night.
Marques Sigle, S – San Francisco 49ers
In his second season, Sigle registered five tackles, a pass breakup, forced fumble and fumble recovery among his 45 defensive snaps and 22 special teams plays in the 49ers' 46-40 win at Dallas. In six games this season, Sigle has registered 34 tackles, one tackle for loss, a pass breakup, forced fumble and fumble recovery among his 311 defensive snaps and 76 special teams plays. San Francisco improved to 8-1 before a Sunday-night game against Minnesota in Mexico City.
Ben Sinnott, TE – Washington Commanders
In his third season, Sinnott made his fifth start of the season and 10
th
of his career, recording four catches for 42 yards and a touchdown over 24 offensive snaps and 12 special teams plays to help the Commanders earn a 24-21 win at the New York Giants on Thursday night. Over nine games this season, Sinnott has five catches for 48 yards and a touchdown among his 231 offensive snaps while also seeing time on 102 special teams plays. Washington is now 3-5-1 prior to hosting Cincinnati on Monday night.
Skylar Thompson, QB – Baltimore Ravens
Thompson, who is in his fifth season overall and first with the Ravens, is on injured reserve. Baltimore suffered a 23-20 Monday-night home loss to the Los Angeles Chargers to fall to 4-6 before playing at Carolina on Sunday.
Russ Yeast, S – Cincinnati Bengals
Yeast, who is in his third season overall and first as part of Cincinnati's active roster, recorded a tackle on kickoff coverage among his 12 special teams plays during the Bengals' 38-18 home win over Pittsburgh on Sunday night. Yeast has recorded seven tackles over his 21 defensive snaps and 62 special teams plays in five games this season for the 7-2 Bengals, who play at Washington on Monday night.
