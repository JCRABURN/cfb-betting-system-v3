Reeves Named Semifinalist for 2026 Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Award
11/17/2026 11:55:00 AM
|
Football
Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Award
Share:
The nation's most consistent kicker is drawing national attention as Liberty's
Chase Reeves
has been named a semifinalist for the 2026 Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Award.
Reeves was one of 20 players who were named to the semifinalist listing earlier today. The award is presented annually to the top placekicker at the NCAA FBS level.
Through the first 10 games of the 2026 season, Reeves is a perfect 14-for-14 in field goal attempts.
Reeves is one of two FBS kickers in the country who have yet to miss a field goal attempt this season and meet the minimum requirement to be nationally ranked in the statistical category (play in 75 percent of the team's games).
Reeves is playing in his fifth year of collegiate football and transferred to Liberty during the offseason from McKendree University. He started his playing career at Southern Illinois (2022-24).
The native of Granite City, Ill., currently leads Conference USA and ranks No. 43 nationally, averaging 7.8 points per game.
Reeves has knocked down multiple field goals in a game five times in 2026, including three field goals in helping Liberty to road wins at Delaware and New Mexico State.
Eleven of his 14 field goals have been 30 yards or longer. Reeves is 5-of-5 on field goal attempts from 30-39 yards and 6-of-6 on field goal attempts 40-49 yards.
Semifinalists will be voted on by a panel of more than 100 FBS head coaches, SIDs, media members, former Groza finalists and current NFL kickers to select the three finalists.
These finalists will be announced on November 24th and honored at the 35th annual Lou Groza Collegiate Place-Kicker Awards Banquet on December 7th in Palm Beach County.
The same panel then selects the winner, who will be announced live on ESPN at the Home Depot College Football Awards on Friday, December 11th.
The award is named for National Football League Hall of Fame kicker Lou "The Toe" Groza, who played 21 seasons with the Cleveland Browns. Groza won four NFL championships with Cleveland and was named NFL Player of the Year in 1954.
Although an All-Pro offensive lineman as well, Groza ushered in the notion that there should be a place on an NFL roster for a kicker.
Since the first Lou Groza Award was handed out in 1992, 34 finalists, including 18 winners, have gone on to appear in the NFL, earning 13 trips to the Pro Bowl and taking home 8 Super Bowls.
That list includes 2026 NFL kickers Daniel Carlson Jake Elliott, Ka'imi Fairbairn, Ryan Fitzgerald, Matt Gay, Zane Gonzalez, Joshua Karty, Jake Moody, Will Reichard, Cairo Santos and Andre Szmyt.
