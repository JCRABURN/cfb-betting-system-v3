Need to Know; Gameday at The Vet - Georgia Southern Game
11/18/2026 1:20:00 PM
|

Gameday at The Vet

Share:
By:
Troy Athletics Communications
Football is back at The Vet on Saturday, when Troy hosts Georgia Southern to close out the 2026 home schedule. Kickoff is set for 3 p.m. between the Trojans and Eagles.
Troy Athletics is excited to announce new items taking place around gameday this season at The Vet, along with reminders of returning items that will help make your gameday experience the best it can be.
Visit TroyTrojans.com/Gameday before each visit to campus to see the latest information on parking, traffic flow and tickets for each of Troy's home games.
Ticket Sales / Will Call / Gate Information
Tickets will be sold on gameday at the following locations:
Main Ticket Office (Tine Davis Fieldhouse) - 8 a.m. to 9:30 a.m.
Gate One - 10 a.m. until halftime
Will call is located at Gate One of The Vet and will be open from 10 a.m. until halftime.
All gates open 90 minutes prior to kickoff, while the Stadium Club and Suites open two hours prior to kickoff.
Clear Bag Policy
Troy Athletics will implement a clear bag policy beginning with the 2026-27 athletic season for all ticketed athletic events on campus to enhance safety and security. Fans can visit TroyTrojans.com/clearbag for full details.
Season ticket holders can pick up their clear bag season ticket holder gift at the Ticket Office during normal business hours throughout the season or on gameday at the Gate 1 ticket office until halftime.
Student Ticket Specials / Promotions
Troy students can purchase student guest tickets for $1 by visiting TroyTrojans.com/StudentTickets, stopping by the Troy Ticket Office, or calling 334-670-3681. Sales run until 5 p.m. on Friday.
Students who purchase a student guest ticket will be entered into a drawing for the Vance Law Firm $333 Field Goal Contest.
Free energy drinks from Coca-Cola at the start of the fourth quarter.
2006 Sun Belt Championship Reunion
The 2006 Trojans began one of the greatest runs in program history, as Troy would go on to win five straight league titles under legendary head coach Larry Blakeney. After opening the season with one of the most difficult schedules in the country – Florida State, Georgia Tech and Nebraska – Troy won seven of its last eight games, including a 41-17 blowout of Rice in the New Orleans Bowl, Troy's first-ever bowl title.
Omar Haugabook,
Gary Banks
and Zach Yenser led the Troy offense while Brannon Condren earned all-league first team honors on defense and Leodis McKelvin set the stage for his All-America season the following year.
All returning members of the 2006 squad will receive up to four complimentary tickets to the game and will help lead the team into The Vet during Trojan Walk. Additionally, Troy Athletics will host an exclusive tailgate party for the team and their families in Tailgate Terrace, and the player will be recognized on the field during the game.
Members of the 2006 team are asked to RSVP by visiting TroyTrojans.com/06FB.
Band Day
Troy's annual High School Band Day is set for Saturday, and this year's event will once again provide high school band students from throughout the Southeast with a chance to experience gameday alongside the Sound of the South.
Attending high school bands will have the opportunity to participate in a pregame practice with the Sound of the South on the football practice fields. During this time, they will receive instruction from the Sound of the South staff and participate alongside current members of the famed Sound of the South.
As part of the game, students will be seated in the South End Zone of The Vet directly next to the Sound of the South. Halftime will feature one of the highlights of the Fall as the Sound of the South will be joined by all the High School Band Day participants for a stirring halftime performance.
Family and friends of participants in Band Day can purchase game tickets by visiting TroyTrojans.com/Promo and using the code BANDDAY.
Mobile Ticketing
Mobile ticketing and parking remain an option for fans to use on gameday.
Ticket purchasers can manage their tickets on their smartphones by visiting TroyTrojans.com/tickets and logging into their account. We strongly encourage all fans to download their tickets before game day. Just before reaching the gates, fans will open their digital wallet on their smartphone, select their game tickets or parking passes, and hold their smartphone near the scanner.
Parking
All on-campus parking is reserved for members of the Trojan Warrior Club. Lots are assigned based on annual philanthropic donations to the Trojan Warrior Club. To learn more about parking, contact Gary Wilbert (gwilbert@troy.edu).
Single-game parking is available for purchase in Lot 5 for $25 per game by visiting TroyTrojans.com/FBTickets.
Handicap parking is located in the Troy University Rec Center Parking Lot; fans needing access to this lot must have a valid state-issued handicap tag and access the lot via John M. Long Drive.
Traffic Information
George Wallace Drive will CLOSE two (2) hours prior to kickoff until one (1) hour after the completion of the game from International Blvd. (traffic light at Charles Henderson High School) to Johnny Long Drive.
Fans with access to the National Guard Armory Lot and North End Zone Lot will be granted access to those lots within the two-hour closure with the proper parking pass. ALL traffic to those lots MUST enter from the north end of George Wallace Drive – from Downtown/Elm Street, not Highway 231.

Senior Day Festivities
Before the game, 21 football seniors will be honored on the field before their final regular-season home football game. During the game, Troy Cheer seniors will also be recognized.
Trojan Walk
Troy's traditional pregame march into Veterans Memorial Stadium, Trojan Walk, will begin at the intersection of University Avenue and George Wallace Drive. The Trojans will walk through the brick portal into Tailgate Terrace before entering the stadium at Gate 1. Trojan Walk will begin at 12:45 p.m.
Troy Athletics Kids Zone
The Troy Kids Zone opens at 11 a.m. in the Riddle-Pace Field outfield. Children of all ages are invited to take part in the fun with free inflatables until 2 p.m.
Horn Beverage Tailgate Terrace Concert Series
Troy's popular Horn Beverage Tailgate Concert Series returns for the 2026 season with an exciting lineup for each of the Trojans' six home games at The Vet. The free concert will take place each week in Tailgate Terrace three hours before kickoff.
Band: Blackberry Breeze
Performance Time: 12-2 p.m.
About:
One of the most dedicated party bands in the Southeast, Blackberry Breeze boasts a diverse song list people love and a show that will leave party-goers wanting more! With the sole purpose of entertaining their audience to the max, Blackberry Breeze makes any celebration a huge success.
Labeled as one of the best cover bands in the state of Alabama, Blackberry Breeze was born out of a garage in Dadeville, Ala., in 2012. The high-energy cover band plays the best tunes from the 80s, 90s, 2000s, as well as rock/classic rock, top 40, hip-hop, reggae and Motown.
Stadium Store
The Barnes & Noble Troy University Bookstore will have two Stadium Store locations around The Vet on gameday. Before the game, a store will be set up in Tailgate Terrace for fans to purchase the latest Troy gear and once the gates to the stadium open, fans will be able to purchase gear at the Stadium Store located on the West Concourse (Press Box side). Barnes & Noble will also have a stadium store setup within the Stadium Club for patrons of this area as well.
Troy Athletics Health Stations
Troy Athletics will have free sunscreen stations located throughout the concourse of The Vet in addition to a hand sanitizer in the Stadium Club.
Troy Regional First Aid Stations
Troy Regional will have first aid stations located at Gate 7 and Gate 2 throughout the season.
Concessions
In addition to the standard concession items, Sodexo will offer specialty items at different locations throughout The Vet.
Alcoholic Beverages
Alcohol is sold within the venue. Only those who are of age and have proper identification are permitted to purchase alcohol. No outside alcohol is permitted. Beer will be available to purchase at four locations throughout Veterans Memorial Stadium.
After providing proper identification at the point of sale, a band will be placed on the customer's wrist to indicate the individual is of legal drinking age. Customers are permitted to purchase no more than two beers at a time. Beer sales will be halted at the end of the third quarter.
Sodexo Food Services, law enforcement personnel and game management officials reserve the right to refuse the sale of alcohol to anyone.
Beer Garden
Sponsored by Horn Beverage, Troy fans who are of age can enjoy a beer garden in Tailgate Terrace from 11:30 a.m. until 2:30 p.m. All drinks purchased in the beer garden must remain in the marked area and all guests must be at least 21 years old to enter.
Shuttles
Transportation Services sponsors game day shuttles from the local motels to Veterans Memorial Stadium for all home football games.
Shuttles are available three (3) hours before kickoff. The shuttle will run this route continuously, approximately every fifteen minutes until kickoff. Return service to the motels will begin immediately following the game and will run continuously for one (1) hour following the game.
Drop-off and pick-up for the shuttle service at the stadium will be directly in front of the Alumni House on Highland Avenue. Pickup downtown is located on the Troy Square.
The shuttle is free when you ride from the motel to the stadium. Drivers will give each rider a coupon for the return trip to the motel.
Printed Game Day Shuttle information is available at all motels in the Troy area and can be obtained by asking the hotel attendant for the brochure.
Lactation Space
The Troy University Title IX office is excited to offer a private lactation space to support nursing parents. This quiet, comfortable and private area provides a place to pump or breastfeed. To access this space on gameday, simply located the closest Swetman security worker for assistance.
Tailgating
Tailgating on Troy's campus is encouraged by all fans. The central hub for tailgating on gameday is Tailgate Terrace behind the outfield wall of Riddle-Pace Field and the South entrance to The Vet. Each game will feature a pregame concert, food trucks and a Kids Fun Zone all within the vicinity of Tailgate Terrace.
Tailgate setup, including tents and similar equipment, may begin at 7 a.m. the day of a home football game or approved athletic event. This is subject to adjustment for early games. All tailgating activities are to conclude an hour after the completion of the game and items must be taken down and removed by 10 a.m. on the following morning to facilitate a smooth post-game campus cleanup.
For complete Troy Football Gameday Tailgating Policies, visit TroyTrojans.com/FBTailgating.
RV parking will be allowed with the appropriate parking pass and will remain at the Trojan Arena parking lot. Fans can contact the Troy Ticket Office, 334-670-3481, to purchase a spot.
