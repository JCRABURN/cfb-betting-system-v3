Acrisure Bounce House
Week 12
UCF Knights
5-5 (3-4 Big 12)

5-5 (3-4 Big 12)
A WIN WOULD...
Be better than a loss
Make UCF bowl eligible for the first time since 2023
Make UCF bowl eligible for the second time in its Big 12 tenure
Make UCF bowl eligible for the ninth time in 11 seasons
Make UCF bowl eligible for 16th time in program history
Set UCF's highest Big 12 Conference wins in a single season (prev. best was three in 2023)
Be
Scott Frost
's 30th win as UCF head coach
1ST AND 10
FALLING SHORT AGAINST SUN DEVILS
The Knights were unable to secure bowl berth in Week 11 against Arizona State, falling 26-13 at home for a second-straight home loss for the first time since 2024. UCF never led in the contest.
UCF's defense bent but didn't break, surrendering four field goals, the first such occurance since 2019. Three of the field goals came in the first half, allowing the Black and Gold to hang around witha 16-7 deficit at the break and getting the ball back after halftime.
The Knights logged just 11 plays for 23 yards in the third quarter while Arizona State posted 23 plays and a touchdown to pull out to a 23-7 advantage early in the fourth quarter.
UCF dialed up a deep ball to
Tyren Hornes
late to slash the Arizona State advantage to 26-13 with 2:12 remaining, a 40-yard strike to the rookie
AB3 RETURNS, LEADS GAME WINNING-DRIVE IN ROAD VICTORY AT KANSAS
After going down with injury during a two-minute drill in UCF's 12-7 loss in a rain-soaked Pittsburgh, QB
Alonza Barnett III
returned for his first start since the injury in the 33-32 road win at Kansas, executing a two-minute drill in exactly one minute to lift the Knights over the Jayhawks
Alonza Barnett III
is the only quarterback in the Big 12 Conference with College Football Playoff experience after leading James Madison to a Sun Belt Conference title and an appearance in the CFP bracket in 2025. Barnett was named Sun Belt Player of the Year and passed for two touchdowns and rushed for another in the Dukes' CFP game at No. 5 Oregon.
Barnett transferred to UCF ahead of the 2026 season and boasts 78 career touchdowns, with 54 through the air and another 24 on the ground. He's thrown five touchdowns and rushed for one as a Knight.
Since 2025, Barnett is 14-4 as a starter, which is a top 10 winning percentage by an active qurterback among Power 4 conferences
FRESH TO IMPRESS
A trio of freshmen on the offensive side of the ball have been impressive so far this season, RB
Taevion Swint
, WR
Tyren Hornes
and TE
Caden Piening
. Together, the trio accounts for eight of UCF's touchdowns. Hornes ranks second on the Knights in receiving yards while Piening ranks fourth.
Heading into Week 12, Piening is tied for the nation's lead in touchdowns by a rookie tight end with three, adding to his total with the game-winning grab at Kansas. He is tied with Dominick Barry of Wake Forest and Frank Abreu of Hawaii; Pieining has the most receiving yards of the trio with 229.
A true freshman from Bradenton, Fla., Hornes has worked his way into a prominent role, experiencing a breakout game in the win over No. 2 BYU, hauling in six catches for 137 yards and a two-point conversion in double overtime. The four-star commit was listed as the No. 74 recruit in the SC Next 2026 ESPN top 300 final release.
Hornes ranks 10th among Power 4 rookie wide receivers in yards with 373
Piening and Swint both arrived at UCF in 2025 but suffered injuries that saw them sidelined for most or all of their true freshmen campaigns. The pair now boast a trio of touchdowns each; Piening has snatched 25 catches while Swint has averaged 4.4 yards a carry across 45 rushes
RUN-STOPPING SUCCESS
UCF is tied for second in the Big 12 when it comes to allowing the fewest chunk rushing plays, surrendering 32 rushes of 10+ yards this season, which is one behind the conference leader of TCU
The Knights have improved their run-stopping compared to the previous campaign and have allowed 135.3 rushing yards per game this season, compared to 150.7 allowed in 2025
UCF ranks sixth in rushing defense when looking exclusively Big 12 Conference games, allowing 136.57 yards per game in league play in 2026
UCF's conference-opening win over TCU saw the Knights limit the Horned Frogs to 33 rushing yards on 27 carries before limiting No. 2 BYU to 116 rushing yards, nearly 100 below its average of 206.7 entering that contest
DEFENSE CREATING CHAOS
UCF has forced 14 turnovers so far this season (seven interceptions and seven forced fumbles), which matches the total from all of last season with three games left in 2026. The seven interceptions ranks sixth in the Big 12.
The Knights had nine interceptions in each of the last two seasons
UCF recovered a pair of fumbles in the road victory at Kansas, one of which resulted in a touchdown two plays later, helping UCF build a 26-7 lead
A turning point in the Knights' win over No. 2 BYU was a tipped ball by DT
Thomas Collins
at the line that was intercepted by LB
Tackett Curtis
. UCF was trailing 14-10 with BYU driving before the two-minute timeout of halftime. The takeaway turned into seven points and a 17-14 halftime lead for the Black and Gold, going on to topple No. 2 BYU 39-31 in double overtime.
STRONG FIRST IMPRESSIONS
UCF's defense has stood strong against opponent's first drive of a game, surrendering just one opening-drive score this season to No. 2 BYU.
Dating back to the beginning of head coach
Scott Frost
's second tenure at UCF in 2025, the Knights have limited opponents to just four scores in their first 22 opening drives thus far. The Knights have forced two turnovers on opponents' opening drives across its seven Big 12 games in 2026.
UCF's offense has scored a touchdown on its first drive in four of 10 games this season, seeing a streak of four-straight come to an end vs. No. 2 BYU
HOSTING ANOTHER FIRST-TIMER
Iowa State is the last of four teams on UCF's 2026 slate that make their first visit to Acrisure Bounce House. The Cyclones have played in Orlando before, earning a 42-41 victory over then-No. 15 Miami in the 2024 Pop-Tarts Bowl at Camping World Stadium.
UCF is 2-1 against first-timers in the Bounce House this season. The Knights beat TCU 21-13 in Week 4, sending the Horned Frogs home from their first trip to Florida since 2003 with a 4-6 record in games in the state. Georgia State left The City Beautiful with a 44-30 loss in Week 3; the Panthers had played in Orlando twice before in the Cure Bowl with a win in 2017 and a loss in 2015. In a scramble to get to Orlando due to FAA grounding all flights, Arizona State made its second-longest road trip in the continental United States (longest was at Miami in 1997), bringing a 26-13 victory back to Tempe.
30-POINT MARK PROVES MAGICAL
UCF is 24-1 across four seasons under
Scott Frost
when scoring 30 points or more in a game, a trend that became apparent in the magical perfect season in 2017 where UCF scored 30+ points in all 13 victories. The Knights have four 30+ point showings in 2026 and have seven since Frost's return in 2025.
The lone blemish in Frost's 30+ points record was the final regular season game of 2016, a 48-31 loss at South Florida, which wound up being the Bull's final win in the rivalry game before UCF won the final six meetings
Between Frost's two tenures, UCF went 46-11 when scoring 30+ points throughout the 2018-24 campaigns with two different coaches at the helm
SEEKING BOWL ELIGIBILITY
UCF is seeking bowl eligibility for the first time since 2023 and for its second time as members of the Big 12 Conference. A bowl berth would be the ninth for UCF in the last 11 seasons after the Knights made it to eight straight bowl games from 2016-23.
Some of UCF's biggest wins in program history came in bowl games. The Chick-fil-A Peach Bowl victory over No. 7 Auburn at the end of the 2017 season earned the Knights a National Championship. A few years prior, the Knights topped No. 6 Baylor in the 2014 Fiesta Bowl, which stood as the highest ranked win in program history until UCF beat No. 2 BYU this season.
WINS SINCE 2017
The Knights have compiled 79 wins since 2017, which is second-most in the state of Florida and third-most in the Big 12. UCF ranks 23rd nationally over that span, which includes a perfect 13-0 in 2017, 12 wins in 2018 and 10 in 2019.
In the Big 12, UCF trails only Utah and BYU with 84 and 83 wins, respectively. The Black and Gold are ahead of Cincinnati, which has 77 wins since 2017.
In Florida, Miami (82) edged in front of UCF (79) in wins since 2017, followed by Florida (68), Florida State (61), Florida Atlantic (59) and South Florida (54)

BERTRAMS BOOTING IT FAR
Punter
Atticus Bertrams
enters Week 12 ranked eighth in the nation and third in the BIg 12 in average distance per punt with a 47.2 yard per punt clip.
Bertrams is in range to set a new UCF record for average punt distance, which is currently held by Matt Prater who averaged 47.9 yards per punt
Bertrams' career-high mark for average yards per punt in a season was 45.3, set during his 2024 campaign at Wisconsin
The Sydney, Australia native was named to Ray Guy Award Ray's 8 for his efforts in UCF's win over No. 2 BYU (more on page 8). He was named to the Ray Guy Midseason Watch List as one of 63 punters listed out of a field of over 100 nominees.
Bertrams averaged 55 yards per punt in the win over Kansas, the highest average in a game of his career including the second-longest punt in his collegiate tenure of 64 yards. He had four punts for 220 yards in the victory.
FROST DURING TENURE NUMBER TWO VS. NEW COACHES
As a coach with a new team himself last season,
Scott Frost
and the Knights finished a perfect 5-0 against teams with a new/interim coach during 2025
The Knights will face a total of three new coaches during 2026; so far the Knights are winless in those games this season, falling to first-year head coach Eric Morris of then-No. 18 Oklahoma State and Baylor interim head coach Joe Klanderman. The Knights will host Iowa State and first-year head coach Jimmy Rogers Nov. 20.
The teams and their new coach the Knights topped in 2025 were: Jacksonville St./Charles Kelly; N.C. A&T (FCS)/Shawn Gibbs; North Carolina/Bill Belichick; West Virginia/Rich Rodriguez; interim Doug Meacham/Oklahoma State. All games were at home and decided by a margin of 181-53.
UCF was one of 30 FBS teams that started 2025 with a new head coach; 28 of which were brand new while two were elevated from interim; Of the crop, Frost and West Virginia's Rich Rodriguez were the only two to coach at a school, leave, and return as head coach again
