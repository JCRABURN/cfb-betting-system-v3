Dean Named American Defensive Player of the Week for Third Time; Robinson Earns Honorable Mention
11/16/2026 10:00:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
BIRMINGHAM, Ala.
– For the third time this season, UAB defensive end
Marlin Dean
has been named the American Conference Defensive Player of the Week, the league announced Monday. Running back
Rod Robinson II
also earned honorable mention recognition following the Blazers' 36-10 victory at Temple on Saturday.
Dean earned the honor for the second consecutive week and third time in the last eight weeks, becoming the first UAB player to receive three American Conference weekly defensive awards in a single season.
The graduate defensive end continued his dominant season against Temple, leading the Blazers with eight tackles, including seven solo stops, two tackles for loss, one sack, a fumble recovery and two quarterback hurries. His biggest play came early in the fourth quarter when he sacked Temple quarterback Ajani Sheppard in the end zone for a safety, extending UAB's lead to 22-10.
Dean helped anchor a defensive effort that limited Temple to just 244 yards of total offense and 85 passing yards, while holding the Owls scoreless in the second half. The victory secured UAB's sixth win of the season and made the Blazers bowl eligible for the first time since 2022.
Dean previously earned Defensive Player of the Week honors following UAB's victories over Navy on Sept. 25 and Charlotte on Nov. 7. Against Navy, he recorded 12 tackles, 4.5 tackles for loss, 2.5 sacks and three forced fumbles. He followed with a career-high three sacks and a forced fumble against Charlotte.
Through 10 games, Dean has totaled 48 tackles, 15.5 tackles for loss, 11.5 sacks, five forced fumbles and two fumble recoveries. His 11.5 sacks rank fifth in UAB single-season history and are tied for the sixth-most in American Conference history. Dean leads the American in sacks and tackles for loss, while his five forced fumbles are tied for the national lead.
With Dean's latest recognition, UAB has now earned four American Conference Defensive Player of the Week awards in 2026, after not receiving the honor during its first three seasons in the league. Linebacker
Ike Esonwune
earned the other defensive honor following the Blazers' victory over ULM on Sept. 12. Combined with kicker
Jonah Delange's
Special Teams Player of the Week recognition on Sept. 14, UAB has collected five conference weekly awards this season.
Rod Robinson II (Ken Shepherd)
Robinson II earned honorable mention following a career performance at Temple, rushing for 202 yards and three touchdowns on 22 carries, averaging 9.2 yards per attempt. The junior running back scored on runs of 9, 33 and 54 yards, with his 54-yard touchdown in the fourth quarter representing UAB's longest rushing play of the season.
Robinson II's performance helped the Blazers establish season highs with 366 rushing yards and 499 yards of total offense. He leads UAB with 753 rushing yards this season and has accounted for six total touchdowns.
UAB (6-4, 4-2 American) returns home Saturday, Nov. 21, to host UTSA in its regular-season home finale at Protective Stadium. Kickoff is set for 7 p.m. CT on ESPNU. The Blazers enter the contest undefeated at home (5-0) and will recognize their graduating student-athletes as part of Senior Day festivities.
