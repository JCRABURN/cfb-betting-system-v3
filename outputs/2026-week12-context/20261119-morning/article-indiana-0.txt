No. 6/5 Indiana (9-1, 6-1 B1G) at Washington (6-4, 3-4 B1G)
Saturday, November 21, 2026
Husky Stadium | Seattle, Wash.
Indiana Game Notes
Washington Game Notes
Setting The Scene
• Indiana travels to Seattle to face Washington for its Week 12 matchup on Saturday (Nov. 21) at 3:30 p.m. in Husky Stadium. The game will stream on Peacock.
• Indiana leads the all-time series, 3-1. Prior to the 2024 matchup, the two programs had met three prior times and had not met since 2003 when Washington won 38-13 in Seattle.
• It will be the first meeting since 2024 when Indiana defeated Washington, 31-17, in Bloomington. It will be just the second meeting as conference opponents..
News & Notes
•
Indiana has won 36 games over the past three seasons (2024-26) which are the most in a three-year span in program history.

• Indiana's defense has limited opponents to under 100 yards rushing on 29 occasions since the start of 2024, which leads the nation. Only Ohio State (25) and Miami (23) have more than 20 such games in that span.

Curt Cignetti
reached 30 wins in his first 32 games as a Power 4 head coach with the Week 3 win over Western Kentucky (9/19). Only Larry Coker (Miami (Fla.), 2001-03) and Gil Dobie (Washington, 1908-12) reached the mark faster - both doing so in 31 games.
