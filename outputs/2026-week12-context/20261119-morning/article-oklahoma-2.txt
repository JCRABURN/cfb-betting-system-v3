Anderson Named Mannelly Award Semifinalist
November  16, 2026
|
Football
Share:
NORMAN —
For the third straight season, University of Oklahoma long snapper
Ben Anderson
has been named a semifinalist for the Patrick Mannelly Award, which is presented to the nation's best long snapper, the award organization announced Monday evening.
Anderson is one of 10 players on the list of Mannelly Award semifinalists, which is in its eighth year.
A fifth-year Sooner from Charlotte, N.C., Anderson has served as the long snapper on all of Oklahoma's punts and placekicks over the last four seasons. He has helped lead a kicking unit that has made 16 field goals and all 22 PAT attempts this season. He has also notched a career-high four tackles on punts through 10 games, including three solo stops. Two of his tackles came in OU's 27-24 win over No. 1 Texas on Oct. 10.
Anderson was an All-SEC Second Team selection last year and a third-team All-SEC pick in 2024. He has played in 49 career games as OU's long snapper and has contributed to a kicking unit that has converted 72 of 94 (77%) field goals and 158 of 159 PATs (99%) over the last four seasons. He has made seven career tackles.
A two-time member of the SEC Fall Academic Honor Roll, Anderson graduated in December 2025 with a degree in multidisciplinary studies. He is pursuing an MBA.
The Patrick Mannelly Award was first presented in 2019 and was established to recognize one of the most important, but often overlooked, players on the football field. The award is named for former NFL long snapper Patrick Mannelly, who played 16 seasons for the Chicago Bears.
Three Mannelly Award finalists will be named Nov. 30, and the winner will be announced Dec. 12 in Chicago.
Oklahoma (6-4, 4-3 SEC) hosts Texas A&M (4-6, 1-6 SEC) on Saturday at Gaylord Family – Oklahoma Memorial Stadium. Kickoff is set for 2:45 p.m. CT on ESPN2.
