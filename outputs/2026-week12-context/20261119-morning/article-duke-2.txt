Duke Returns to Coaches Top-25 Poll
11/15/2026 2:02:00 PM
|
Football
Purchase Friday Game Tickets
All Aboard the Nate Train
Share:
DURHAM, N.C. –
Duke football returned to the US LBM Coaches Poll top-25, slotted at No. 24. It marks the third week in the 2026 campaign the Blue Devils have appeared in the top 25.
The Blue Devils (7-3, 4-3 ACC) cap their home campaign with a Friday visit from Clemson (6-4, 4-3 ACC). Kick is set for 7:30 p.m. on ESPN. As part of its Senior Night ceremony, Duke will honor 26 individuals:

Duke has won its home finale in each of the last four seasons.
The Blue Devils enter Friday aiming for their eighth win and a fifth consecutive season with eight or more victories for the first time in program history. Duke is 7-3 for the second time in the last three seasons and owns seven or more wins through 10 games for just the eighth time since 1963, joining the 1989 (7-3), 1994 (8-2), 2013 (8-2), 2014 (8-2), 2018 (7-3), 2022 (7-3) and 2024 (7-3) crews.
