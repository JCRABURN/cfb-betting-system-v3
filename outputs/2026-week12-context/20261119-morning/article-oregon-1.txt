By:

Series History vs. Michigan State
Overall: Series Oregon leads, 5-3
In East Lansing: Michigan State leads, 3-0
In Eugene: Oregon leads, 4-0
Neutral Site: Oregon leads, 1-0
Last Meeting: Oregon won, 31-10 (Oct. 4, 2024, in Eugene)
Current Streak: Oregon W2
Next Stop: East Lansing
No. 13 Oregon will take a three-game winning streak into its final road trip of the regular season as the Ducks head to East Lansing to face Michigan State on Friday night. UO is averaging 35.3 points per game across its last three games, completing comeback wins against No. 20 Northwestern and No. 12 Ohio State before a dominant 31-7 win over Michigan last week in Eugene. The Ducks are looking for their first win in East Lansing as they make their first trip to Spartan Stadium since 2015. Oregon holds a 5-3 lead in the all-time series with Michigan State, including a 31-10 victory in Eugene in 2024. UO is 5-1 in Friday games under HC
Dan Lanning
.
Did You Know?
OLB
Matayo Uiagalelei
is closing in on Oregon's all-time sack record with 28.0 in his career, just 1.5 away from tying and 2.0 away from breaking the program record of 29.5 (Nick Reed, 2005-08).
A Win Would...
» Be Oregon's third straight over Michigan State and first in East Lansing.
» Improve UO to 23-3 in Big Ten play since joining the league, including 12-1 in Big Ten road games.
» Make UO 21-4 on the road under HC
Dan Lanning
, including 19-3 in conference road games.
Last Time vs. Michigan State
Oregon is 1-0 against Michigan State under HC
Dan Lanning
, defeating the Spartans 31-10 in the Ducks' first-ever Big Ten home game on Friday, Oct. 4, 2024, in Autzen Stadium. Oregon raced out to a 31-0 lead before Michigan State scored 10 points in the fourth quarter. UO racked up 477 total yards, including 264 passing and 213 rushing, while holding Michigan State to just 250 yards with only 59 on the ground. Among current Ducks who played in that game, WR
Evan Stewart
caught two passes for 20 yards and a touchdown, OLB
Teitum Tuioti
had seven tackles and OLB
Matayo Uiagalelei
assisted on a sack.
In the Hunt
Oregon has kept its postseason hopes alive with three straight wins following a 4-3 start to the season, and the Ducks are very much in play for a potential College Football Playoff berth. Oregon is one of six teams with three or more wins over teams ranked at the time of the game, with victories at No. 12 USC, vs. No. 20 Northwestern and at No. 12 Ohio State. UO is also one of six teams with multiple road wins over ranked teams, and one of two (Alabama) to win multiple road games over ranked teams by double digits. UO also has a win over current CFP No. 17 Boise State, which is undefeated since opening the season with a loss in Eugene. Oregon continued its climb up the CFP rankings on Tuesday, moving up three spots to No. 13, and the Ducks own three wins over teams in the current CFP top 18 in No. 14 Ohio State, No. 17 Boise State and No. 18 USC.
Dominant Ducks Maintain Momentum
The Ducks continued their recent resurgence by suffocating Michigan last week in Autzen Stadium, 31-7. Oregon's defense was one broken play away from a shutout, and the offense put together the best performance by any team this season against an elite Michigan defense. UO racked up 467 total yards and 31 points, both the most given up by the Wolverines this season, behind 341 passing yards and four touchdowns by QB
Dylan Raiola
. WR
Jeremiah McClellan
(105) and WR
Dakorien Moore
(101) both went over 100 yards receiving, and RB
Dierre Hill Jr.
racked up 185 all-purpose yards with a 49-yard receiving TD. OLB
Matayo Uiagalelei
continued his pursuit of the UO all-time sack record with two more and ILB
Dylan Williams
led the way with a career-high 11 tackles and a forced fumble as UO held Michigan to just 286 total yards and only 71 yards passing.
Road Warriors
The Ducks have traveled very well under HC
Dan Lanning
, sporting a 20-4 record on the road since 2022. Oregon is 18-3 in conference road games under Lanning, including 11-1 in Big Ten road games since joining the league in 2024. UO won 12 consecutive games on the road beginning with a 35-6 win at Utah on Oct. 28, 2023, a streak that was the longest in the nation before Oklahoma State defeated the Ducks on Sept. 12, 2026. Oregon's .833 winning percentage on the road since 2022 is No. 2 in the nation, trailing only Georgia (17-2, .895), and its 16-3 record (.842) on the road since 2023 is second only to Georgia (13-2, .867). Oregon's loss at Illinois in Week 8 was its first road Big Ten loss since joining the league.
Finding Their Groove
The Ducks have been clicking offensively over the last three games, scoring 34 and 41 points in ranked wins over Northwestern and Ohio State, respectively, before putting up 31 points in last week's win over Michigan. Oregon's 35.3 points per game over the last three weeks is best in the Big Ten and No. 21 nationally in that span, and its 13 touchdowns in that stretch are second in the Big Ten and tied for 18th in the FBS. The Ducks overcame slow starts against both Northwestern and Ohio State before putting together a complete performance against Michigan, including 14 points and 172 total yards in the opening quarter. Oregon is averaging 434.3 total yards per game over the last three contests with at least 395 yards in all three, with only end-of-game kneel downs at Ohio State bringing the Ducks' total down below 400. QB
Dylan Raiola
has been terrific while leading the offensive resurgence, completing 67-of-99 passes for 934 yards with nine touchdowns and zero interceptions over the last three games. His 311.3 passing yards per game rank No. 3 nationally during that three-week span.
Tackling Machines
Oregon has proven to be one of the nation's best tackling teams in 2026, tying for third in the FBS with a 90.1 tackling grade from Pro Football Focus. DB
Ify Obidegwu
leads the team with a 90.8 tackling grade, second-best in the Big Ten and tied for No. 7 nationally, and he delivered huge hits on back-to-back plays in Week 10 at Ohio State. DB
Koi Perich
is second among qualified Ducks with an 87.8 tackling grade while tying for the team lead in tackles at 63 with ILB
Devon Jackson
, who is third on the team with an 87.5 tackling grade.
Owning Fourth Down
Oregon has turned the ball back over to its offense the old-fashioned way recently, holding its opponents to 0-for-7 on fourth down across the last two games. Ohio State was 0-for-4 on fourth down in Week 10 before Michigan went 0-for-3 in Week 11. The seven fourth-down attempts vs. Oregon are most in the Big Ten and tied for ninth-most nationally over the last two weeks, and the Ducks are the only team to have faced six or more fourth-down attempts without allowing a conversion. Opponents are just 10-of-27 (37.0%) on third down in the last two games, creating the fourth-down opportunities. Oregon is fourth in the Big Ten and tied for 28th nationally on the season in fourth-down defense, with opponents going 10-of-19 (42.1%).
Chasing History
OLB
Matayo Uiagalelei
is closing in on becoming Oregon's new all-time sack king, as the senior enters Week 12 at No. 3 in program history with 28.0 career sacks. He is just 1.5 sacks away from tying and 2.0 sacks away from breaking the all-time record of 29.5 held by Nick Reed (2005-08), and his next full sack will tie him with Ernest Jones (29.0, 1990-93) for No. 2. Uiagalelei became the seventh player in program history to reach 20.0 career sacks this season, and he is currently just outside the UO all-time top 10 with 35.0 career tackles for loss.
