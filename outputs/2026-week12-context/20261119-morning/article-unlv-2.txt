4:02 pm PT

: CEFCU Stadium (19,000/Artificial), San Jose, CA

CBS SN (Rich Waltz, Adam Breneman & Jordan Giorgio)

ESPN 1100 AM & 100.9 FM (Matt Neverett, Caleb Herring & Steve Cofield)
SATURDAY'S PREVIEW...
Coming off its first win over a ranked opponent in 18 years, UNLV (6-4/4-2) makes its final road trip of the regular season to San Jose State (5-6/3-4) Saturday on CBS SN ... The Rebels have played in FIVE different time zones this season -- the only FBS program to do so in 2026 -- while the Spartans played in four themselves ... UNLV upset then-No. 23/25 New Mexico last week, 21-17, while SJSU was downed at Air Force, 24-3 ... UNLV is tied for 14th in the nation for the most wins (27) in the FBS since the start of 2024 ... UNLV has more overall road victories than any other team in college football since the start of the 2024 season with 14 ... The Rebels are 17-7 in true road games since the start of 2023 after only winning eight total road games in the previous six seasons (8-25) ... UNLV is the only G6 team this season to have both a win over a P4 opponent (Cal) and a win over a ranked opponent (UNM) ... The Rebels are 32-2 since the start of 2023 when leading after the third quarter, including 13-1 under Dan Mullen ... UNLV star RB Jet Thomas continues to lead the MW in rushing yards with 774. He heads into Saturday standing second in the nation for career touchdowns scored (42 -- 37 rushing, 4 receiving, 1 KOR) among all active players and needs just one more to tie UNLV's all-time record held by Lexington Thomas (2015-18) ... Rebel QB
Jackson Arnold
was named MW Offensive Player of the Week for the second time this fall on Monday. The former Oklahoma and Auburn transfer currently leads the league with 24 total TDs responsible for -- ranking first in passing TDs (17) and fourth in rushing scores (7). He also tops the MW in pass efficiency rating and stands 13th with 587 net rushing yards (sixth-most by a Rebel QB) ... UNLV WR Kayden "Big Play" McGee now leads the nation in yards per reception with 22.46 and also took over the conference lead with his seventh TD catch.
REBELS vs. SPARTANS NOTES...
SJSU leads the all-time series 20-8-1 but the last time these teams faced off UNLV broke a seven-game losing streak in games played in San Jose with a 27-16 win in 2024 ... Because the programs didn't square off last fall, the Rebels are playing two straight at CEFCU Stadium ... Third-year SJSU HC Ken Niumatalolo coached TEs at UNLV under John Robinson from 1999-2001 ... SJSU DC Bojay Filimoeatu was a member of the Rebel staff in 2022.
