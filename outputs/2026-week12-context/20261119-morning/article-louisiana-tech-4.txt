Six Chapters, One Story. Chapter Six: The Next Generation
11/16/2026 8:35:00 AM
|
Football
Buy Tickets
Book Your Hotel
Share:
By:
Kyle Kavanaugn
RUSTON
| Every Bulldog's story started with someone who believed.
A teacher who saw potential. A coach who asked for a little more. A mentor who made time. A parent who never missed a game. A professor who stayed after class to make sure it made sense.
Every Bulldog has people like that, and on Saturday, November 21, Louisiana Tech celebrates them on a day that begins with new graduates and ends with the final home game of the season. The day belongs to the people who invest in the next generation of leaders, students, and Bulldogs.
Senior Day
Before kickoff, Louisiana Tech honors its seniors on the field, joined by their families and coaching staff.
Every senior has a list of people who believed in them long before anyone cheered their name: the coaches who recruited them, the teachers who pushed them, the families who drove the miles to be there. On Saturday, those people stand on the field next to the players they helped shape.
Education Game and Extra Yard for Teachers
The College of Education and Human Sciences is our College of the Game, and the day doubles as our Extra Yard for Teachers game.
Extra Yard for Teachers is the College Football Playoff Foundation's national movement that brings every level of college football together each September to celebrate teachers, because every day is game day for teachers. Since its founding, it has supported more than 756,000 teachers and 14 million students across 96,000 schools nationwide.
Louisiana Tech's part of the story began in September with a surprise classroom visit to Glen View Elementary special education teacher Kaylee Bombard, and it continues on the field in November.
Through a $7,500 grant from the College Football Playoff Foundation, distributed in partnership with the Sun Belt Conference, Louisiana Tech Athletics is funding a dedicated mental health and wellness room for educators at Ruston Junior High, a quiet, restorative space where teachers can step away from the demands of the school day to recharge. It is the latest in a wellness space program that has also created rooms at Cypress Springs, I.A. Lewis Elementary, and Ruston Elementary, with a different Lincoln Parish school chosen each year. The Ruston Junior High room is expected to be completed later this academic year.
During the first quarter, Louisiana Tech recognizes Ruston Junior High's educators, the Lincoln Parish School Board, and the ongoing wellness room project with an on-field presentation, alongside the Sun Belt Conference and the College Football Playoff Foundation.
Faculty and Staff Appreciation
Behind every student's story is a professor, advisor, or staff member who made a difference on campus. Louisiana Tech faculty and staff are recognized for the role they play in student success. Faculty and staff can claim two free tickets to the game, with additional guest tickets available for $15 each.
Presented by FMOL Health | St. Francis
FMOL Health | St. Francis presents the game and joins Louisiana Tech in celebrating the people who invest in others. Learn more at
fmolhs.org
.
Final Home Game
Arkansas State makes the trip to Ruston for a Sun Belt West Division matchup that closes the home schedule and renews a long-running series. The two programs have not met since Louisiana Tech's 47-28 win in the 2015 New Orleans Bowl, and the Bulldogs lead the series 26-12, including 10 wins in the last 11 meetings. Kickoff is set for 6 p.m. on ESPN+. For the seniors, for the fans who filled the stands all season, and for everyone who believed in these Bulldogs along the way, this is the last home Saturday of the year. Come early, stay late, and make it one to remember.
Your Tech Journey
We all started somewhere, and Louisiana Tech Athletics built Your Tech Journey because every stage of life deserves a place to belong.
It starts with Champ's Kids Club, for ages 4 to 12, where young Bulldogs create first memories by coming to games, wearing the colors, and starting traditions of their own.
Varsity Blue picks up from there for ages 13 to 18, with a deeper connection to the program, more chances to take part, and a path toward leading the next generation of Bulldog supporters.
When Bulldogs arrive on campus, Blue Bloods is waiting. It is the official student organization, with gamedays, rewards, traditions, and leadership opportunities for every registered student.
And on Saturday morning, the journey takes its next step. At 9 a.m., the Thomas Assembly Center fills for fall commencement, and the newest alumni of our great university cross the stage. They arrived with someone who believed in them, and they leave with something to build on. As alumni, they carry it forward through LTAC, staying connected to Louisiana Tech, supporting its future, and passing its traditions on to future Bulldogs and Lady Techsters. To encourage graduates and their families to stay and celebrate, graduates can enter Origin Bank Field at Joe Aillet Stadium one last time with their access pass, and their families and guests can get tickets with the grad promo code for $15 in the west and $5 in the east.
Somebody believed in you. To our newest alumni, congratulations, and welcome to the next stage of your Tech journey.
