NFL Broncos: Week 11
11/18/2026 3:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
BOISE, Idaho –
Boise State will be represented by 10 former players in Week 11 of the NFL season, including nine on Sunday. Four former Broncos will play in the early window before five Boise State products compete in the afternoon slot. Ashton Jeanty will lead the Las Vegas Raiders into an AFC West clash with the Denver Broncos, meeting former teammates
Kage Casey
and JL Skinner. John Bates and the Washington Commanders close the weekend on Monday Night Football, hosting the Cincinnati Bengals for a 6:15 p.m. MT game on ESPN. Full Week 11 information can be found below. All times listed are Mountain.
SUNDAY
Buffalo Bills (Khalil Shakir) vs. Miami Dolphins – CBS, 11 a.m.
Shakir had two catches for 26 yards while running the ball three times for a season-best 29 yards in Buffalo's 31-12 win over the New York Jets. The wide receiver is up to 478 all-purpose yards this season and 3,125 in his career. The Bills are tied for the NFL's best record at 8-1 and lead the league in scoring offense (31.9 ppg).
Detroit Lions (Ahmed Hassanein) vs. Tampa Bay Buccaneers – FOX, 11 a.m.
Hassanein was inactive in the Lions' 35-10 win over the New England Patriots, a game played in Munich, Germany. This was Detroit's fifth straight win, putting them in a three-way tie for first in the NFC North at 7-2.
Philadelphia Eagles (John Ojukwu) vs. Pittsburgh Steelers – CBS, 11 a.m.
Ojukwu was elevated to the Eagles' active roster ahead of their in-state showdown with the Steelers. This is the second time he has been added to the 53-man roster. His last game action came in Week Three of the 2025 season, when he played for the Tennessee Titans.
New Orleans Saints (Kellen Moore) at Chicago Bears – FOX, 11 a.m.
Moore and the Saints dropped a 20-17 contest to the Carolina Panthers. Tyler Shough completed 25-of-35 passes, finding rookie Jordyn Tyson 11 times for 80 yards. Kaden Elliss played all 70 defensive snaps and totaled 15 tackles, 1.0 sack and 2.0 TFLs.
Los Angeles Chargers (Scott Matlock) vs. New York Jets – FOX, 2:05 p.m.
Matlock made his second straight start at fullback and played a season-high 33 snaps in the Chargers' 23-20 win over the Baltimore Ravens. He had the team's third-best Pro Football Focus offensive (74.8) and run blocking (71.7) grades, which are both the second-highest of his career.
Denver Broncos (JL Skinner,
Kage Casey
) vs. Las Vegas Raiders (Ashton Jeanty) – CBS, 2:25 p.m.
Jeanty had a team-high four catches for 46 yards while running for 18 yards in the Raiders' 21-13 loss to the Seattle Seahawks. His 38-yard reception in the fourth quarter was Las Vegas' longest of the game, putting his season total at 417 receiving yards, second-most among NFL running backs. Skinner and Casey return to action after being off in Week 10. In Week Nine, the Broncos took a 26-21 victory over the Carolina Panthers. Skinner made his first career start and posted career-bests in total tackles (6) and solo tackles (5). He played a career-high 74 snaps, including 60 on defense. Casey was inactive in the contest, which improved Denver to 7-2, tying it for first in the AFC West.
Jacksonville Jaguars (Ezra Cleveland) at New York Giants – CBS, 2:25 p.m.
Cleveland played all 73 offensive snaps in Jacksonville's 40-23 win over the Tennessee Titans, helping the Jaguars churn out season-highs in total (459) and passing (336) yards. Jacksonville has won three straight to garner a 7-2 record, leading the AFC South by four games. It ranks fourth in the league with 29.9 points per game.
MONDAY
Washington Commanders (John Bates) vs. Cincinnati Bengals – ESPN, 6:15 p.m.
Bates started at tight end and totaled 40 snaps in Washington's 24-21 win over the New York Giants. The Commanders scored the go-ahead touchdown in the game's final minute to defeat the NFC East leaders. He has started all nine games this season and 45 in his career.
--BroncoSports.com--
