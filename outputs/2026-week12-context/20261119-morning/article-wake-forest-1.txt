Wake Forest Head Coach Jake Dickert Named Paul 'Bear' Bryant National Coach of the Week
11/16/2026 12:59:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
Coach Dickert, his staff and his players are off to a 9-1 start this year and have won seven-straight games including four on the road during that span.
By:
Stephen Czarda
Share:
WINSTON-SALEM, N.C. –
Wake Forest football head coach Jake Dickert has been named the Paul "Bear" Bryant National Coach of the Week, the American Heart Association announced on Monday. This is the second time Dickert has earned the recognition.
On Saturday, the No. 13 Demon Deacons earned an emphatic 49-7 victory over SMU, handing the Mustangs their first-ever home ACC loss in the process.
With the victory, Wake Forest is the only program to win five Power 4 road games so far this season.

The Deacs have been on a dominant run over their seven-game winning streak, outscoring opponents 343-65 during that stretch. Wake Forest dominated the first half against the Mustangs, entering the halftime locker room with a 42-0 lead.
In the process, Wake Forest also pushed its unanswered scoring streak to 126 points, tied for the second most in a season since 2013.
Saturday's victory over SMU secured Wake Forest's ninth victory this season. The 2026 Demon Deacons became just the fifth team in 119 years of football at Wake Forest to accomplish that feat as well.
