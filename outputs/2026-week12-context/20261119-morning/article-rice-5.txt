2026 Rice Football Fact Book
Game Nuggets
•Rice is 2-4 all-time vs. teams from Pennsylvania (1-4 in the state), splitting games with Pitt (1950 & 2017 in Pittsburgh) and Duquesne (winning 27-7 in Houston in 1936 and losing 14-0 in Pittsburgh in 1936), and dropping two at Penn State (18-7 in Houston in 1962 and 28-7 in University Park in 1963).
• Lincoln Financial Stadium will be the 11th NFL home stadium to host the Owls in a regular-season game: Acrisure Stadium (Pitt), Astrodome (Houston), Cotton Bowl (SMU), Cowboys Stadium (SMU), Los Angeles Coliseum (USC), Orange Bowl (Florida & Miami), Polo Grounds (Fordham), Raymond James (South Florida), Sugar Bowl (Tulane), Superdome (Tulane). The Owls also played at Aloha Stadium, which hosted the NFL's Pro Bowl for three decades. The Houston Oilers used Rice Stadium as their home field from 1965-67 when they were members of the AFL.
• Two former Owls played for the Eagles after they moved to Lincoln Financial Field. N.D. Kalu, who was drafted by Philadelphia in 1997, returned to the team in 2001 and played for it during its inaugural season at Lincoln Financial. James Casey signed with the Eagles as a free agent in 2013 and played two seasons in Philadelphia. In addition, John MaGee was drafted by the Eagles in the 22nd round of the 1945 draft and started 52 games at guard over eight seasons, including NFL titles in 1948 & 1949.
Notable
Rice will wear a replica of the original Owl wing decals on their helmets for the trip to Temple.  The Owl wings were first introduced by head coach Fred Goldsmith before the 1990 season in conjunction with the "New Set of Wings" campaign.  Goldsmith took over a program in 1989 that had lost 18 consecutive games, including 14 straight in the SWC, and snapped both in his first season. Led by sophomore running back Trevor Cobb, the Owls went 5-6 in their first season under the wings, missing a winning season when Baylor edged them 17-16 in the season finale.  One year later, Cobb would become the first Owl to win a national individual award when he was awarded the Doak Walker Award as the nation's top running back, and two years later, the Owls posted their first winning season since 1963, finishing 6-5 overall (4-3 in the conference).
The Owls threw for 32 yards vs. Tulane, their fewest yards through the air in a game since they had five yards in a 44-10 win over SMU on 10/9/04.
The Owls have scored on six of their last nine opening possessions of either the first or second half since the second half at East Carolina.  The Owls scored a touchdown to start the second half against the Pirates, followed by a TD on their opening series vs. Tulsa, on both of their opening possession at FAU and, after missing a field goal on their opening drive at North Texas, scored a touchdown to open the second half vs. the Mean Green and then kicked a field
Rice has lost three conference games by three points or less (16-14 vs. UTSA, 51-49 @FAU, 20-17 vs. Tulane and the Owls' schedule ranks as the 25th toughest in FBS through 11/15.
Rice was the only FBS team not to face a team with a losing record through its first nine games this season.
Rice leads the American with 35 pass breakups, including six vs. Tulane to match the Owls' season high (ECU), and they have 25 breakups in the last five games.
Next Up:
Rice wraps up the 2026 season by hosting Army next Saturday for Senior Day.  Kickoff is set for 1 p.m. with coverage on ESPN+.
