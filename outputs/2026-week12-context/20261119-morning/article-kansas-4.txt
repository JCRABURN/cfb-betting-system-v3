Leroy Harris III Named Big 12 Defensive Player of the Week, Defensive Line Earns Big 12 Honors
11/16/2026 12:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
LAWRENCE, Kan. –
Kansas football was represented by a pair of Big 12 weekly award honors as
Leroy Harris III
was named Big 12 Conference Defensive Player of the Week and Kansas' defensive line was named the Defensive Line of the Week, the Big 12 Conference announced on Monday.
The pair of awards marks Kansas' first of the 2026 season. Harris is Kansas' first Big 12 Defensive Player of the Week winner since Mello Dotson in 2024, while it is the first time the Jayhawks' defensive line has won the award.
Harris recorded eight tackles, including two tackles-for-loss sack and a pass breakup in Kansas' 37-14 victory over West Virginia on Saturday. Harris helped the Kansas defense to hold West Virginia to season lows in rushing yards (35), scoring (14) and total offense (262), among others.
Harris' 16 tackles-for-loss this season are the fourth most in the NCAA, while leading the Big 12 Conference. Harris also has 7.5 sacks, which are the second most in the Big 12 this season.
Kansas' defensive front was dominant in the win over West Virginia, holding the Mountaineers to just 35 rushing yards while recording nine tackles-for-loss and four sacks. Harris,
Blake Herold
,
Kevin Oatis
and Dak Brinkley all recorded sacks in the win.
Kansas returns home to David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium to host the No. 12 BYU Cougars on Saturday, November 21 at 11 a.m. The game will serve as Senior Day for Kansas' senior class. Fans can purchase tickets for the home finale
here
.
