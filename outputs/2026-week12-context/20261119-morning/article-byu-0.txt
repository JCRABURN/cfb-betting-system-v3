Football stays at No. 12 in AP and AFCA rankings
Coming off its 28-7 win over Baylor, BYU stayed at No. 12 in both The Associated Press Top 25 and US LBM Coaches Poll administered by the American Football Coaches Association.
Date
November 16, 2026
Share
Twitter
Facebook
Email
BYU Photo
PROVO, Utah — Coming off a decisive 28-7 victory over Baylor on Saturday, BYU football stayed at No. 12 in both
The Associated Press Top 25
and
US LBM Coaches Poll
administered by the
American Football Coaches Association (AFCA)
this week. The AFCA poll was released on Sunday while AP delayed its rankings this week until Monday due to rescheduled action over the weekend because of the FAA ground stoppage on Friday.
The Cougars were ranked No. 10 in last week's College Football Playoff rankings. The third assessment of the CFP selection committee will be revealed on Tuesday.
Among the top 12 in the AP and AFCA polls, voters kept the teams in the same rankings as last week with the exception of Wake Forest and Texas Tech switching places with the Demon Deacons moving one palce to No. 10 and the Red Raiders filling their void at No. 11.
Georgia (9-1), Utah (10-0), Notre Dame (9-1) and UCLA (10-0) are the top four teams in both rankings, with Miami (9-1) at No. 5 in AP and Indiana (9-1) at No. 5 in the AFCA. The Hurricanes and Hoosiers flipped to occupy the No. 6 spot in the other poll, followed by agreement on Alabama (8-2), Texas (8-2), Tennessee (8-2), Wake Forest (9-1), Texas Tech (9-1) and BYU (8-2) from No. 7 to No. 12.
Big 12
The Big 12 has five teams in the top 25 of both major polls.
After No. 2 Utah (10-0, 7-0 Big 12), No. 11 Texas Tech (9-1, 6-1) and No. 12 BYU (8-2, 5-2), other Big 12 teams in the rankings this week include No. 19/20 Arizona (7-3, 4-3) and No. 20/21 Houston (7-3, 4-3). Oklahoma State (6-4, 4-3) is receiving votes in the AFCA poll.
BYU in the Polls
Last year, BYU was ranked as high as No. 8 in both the AP and AFCA polls after achieving an 8-0 start to the season. Coming off a 12-2 campaign and 2025 Pop-Tarts Bowl title, BYU entered the 2026 season in the preseason polls ranked 15th by the coaches and tied for No. 14 with USC by AP voters, which marked the second-highest preseason AP ranking for BYU in program history (No. 10 in 1985).
After dropping back to No. 15 in the AP rankings following its
63-7 season-opening win over Utah Tech
, BYU returned to the same slot in the AP poll where it finished last season at No. 11 following its
28-17 conference win over Arizona
while the coaches rated BYU at No. 13. BYU moved up to No. 9 in both polls following its
41-23 road win at Colorado State
of the Pac-12, before dropping to No. 10 in AP during a bye week.
The Cougars moved up to No. 8 in both polls after a
hard-fought
17-10 road win through the rain and injuries at TCU
and stayed at No. 8 following its
24-10 home win over Iowa State
in a Friday night matchup.
BYU jumped to No. 2 with
its
28-24 victory over then No. 2 Notre Dame.
After suffering a
39-31 double-overtime road setback at UCF
and dropping to No. 8/9, the Cougars rebounded with a
27-20 home victory over Arizona State
to earn No. 7/8 rankings before
falling 28-20 at No. 3 Utah
and land at No. 12. BYU stayed at No. 12 after
a dominating 28-7 victory over a surging 6-3 Baylor team
on Saturday.
Historically, BYU has been ranked 316 times overall in the AP poll since the program's first national ranking in 1974. The Cougars have been ranked in each of the last 24 AP polls released since entering last year's AP rankings at No. 25 with a 3-0 record.
BYU boasts a 46-13 record historically when playing as an AP Top 25 team.
