Griffin, Humphreys Look Ahead to South Carolina
November 17, 2026
|
Football
Share:
GEORGIA FOOTBALL VIDEOS
E. Griffin
L. Humphreys
More Videos
ATHENS, Ga. -
Sophomore defensive lineman
Elijah Griffin
and senior wide receiver
London Humphreys
spoke with the media on Tuesday afternoon ahead of Georgia's game against South Carolina at Williams-Brice Stadium on Saturday night.
This weekend's contest marks the first meeting between the two schools in Columbia since the 2022 when the Bulldogs defeated the Gamecocks 48-7. Saturday's match up will air live on ABC at 7:30 p.m. ET.
