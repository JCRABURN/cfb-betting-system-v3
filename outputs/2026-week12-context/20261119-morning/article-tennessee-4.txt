Vols in the NFL – Week 10
November 18, 2026
|
Football
Share:
KNOXVILLE, Tenn. –
Former Tennessee running back Alvin Kamara found the end zone twice, while Byron Young and Alontae Taylor delivered strong defensive performances to headline Week 10 action for former Vols in the NFL.
Kamara scored a pair of touchdowns against Carolina, reaching the end zone once on the ground and once through the air for the New Orleans Saints.
Defensively, Young continued to make an impact for the Los Angeles Rams, recording one sack, three tackles, two tackles for loss and a quarterback hit. Young's disruptive performance helped lead the Rams to a 34-6 victory at Arizona. In Nashville, Taylor paced the Titans with seven tackles against Jacksonville.
Running back Dylan Sampson hauled in four receptions for 31 yards and added six rushing yards in Cleveland's 27-24 victory over Houston, while Theo Jackson recorded four tackles in Minnesota's 30-10 win over Green Bay. For the Commanders, Shy Tuttle made his third consecutive start and totaled three tackles in Washington's 24-21 victory over the New York Giants.
