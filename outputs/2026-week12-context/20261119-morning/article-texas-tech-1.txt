Castellanos named Walter Camp National Offensive Player of the Week
November 17, 2026
|
Football
Walter Camp Foundation News
Share:
LUBBOCK, Texas —
For the first time this season, a Red Raider has been named the national offensive player of the week. Quarterback
Thomas Castellanos
, who has ignited the Texas Tech offense over the past three weeks, was named the Walter Camp Foundation's Offensive Player of the Week on Tuesday as part of the National College Football Awards Association's weekly awards announcement.
Castellanos threw for 263 yards and four touchdowns in his most recent outing, guiding No. 12 Texas Tech to a 52-27 road rout of No. 25 Oklahoma State in a game that extended into early Sunday morning. The victory kept the Red Raiders on track for a return to the Big 12 Championship Game and College Football Playoff. Castellanos added 38 yards on the ground, including a 20-yard run that converted a third-and-16 early in the fourth quarter.
Saturday marked the second time in Castellanos' career that he has recorded at least three touchdown passes in consecutive games. It was also the first time a Red Raider had thrown at least four touchdown passes in consecutive games since Nik Shimonek did so against Oklahoma and Kansas State in 2017. In his previous outing, Castellanos tossed a career-high five touchdown passes in the Red Raiders' home victory over West Virginia.
The Red Raiders have ranked among the nation's top offenses since Castellanos took over at quarterback three weeks ago. Since then, Texas Tech is 3-0 and averaging 46.3 points and 517.3 yards of total offense per game behind a balanced attack that has produced averages of 258.0 passing yards and 259.3 rushing yards per game. The Red Raiders eclipsed 50 points against a conference opponent for the third time this season with the win at Oklahoma State. Texas Tech's three such performances are the most in the FBS.
The last Red Raider to earn Walter Camp National Offensive Player of the Week honors was
Cameron Dickey
, following last season's contest against Kansas. Castellanos is the first Texas Tech quarterback to receive the honor since Alan Bowman on Sept. 16, 2018. Castellanos' honor marks the 11th Walter Camp National Player of the Week selection overall for Texas Tech since 2004.
This week's national defensive player of the week was Jelani McDonald, a defensive back at Texas.
