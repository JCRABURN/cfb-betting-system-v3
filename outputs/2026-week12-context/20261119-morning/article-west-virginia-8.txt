Cook Named Doak Walker Award Semifinalist
November 17, 2026 11:48 AM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Mike Montoro
MORGANTOWN, W.Va. –
The PwC SMU Athletic Forum has announced that West Virginia University senior running back
Cam Cook
is one of 10 semifinalists for the 2026 Doak Walker National Collegiate Running Back Award, presented annually to the nation's top running back.
The field includes the nation's top three players in rushing yards and rushing yards per game, two returning semifinalists and six players who have earned the Doak Walker National Running Back of the Week honor this season.
Cook, who led the nation in rushing yards in 2025, is a returning semifinalist and is currently third in the Big 12 and No. 12 nationally with 108.4 rushing yards per game, totaling 1,084 yards and eight rushing touchdowns.
Has played in 44 career games with 29 starts
Eclipsed 1,000 rushing yards on the season against Texas Tech, marking WVU's first 1,000-yard rusher since Leddie Brown in 2021
Has rushed for at least 120 yards in six games this season
Has scored at least one rushing touchdown in 15 of his last 19 games
Ranks No. 1 in rushing yards among active FBS running backs dating back to the start of the 2025 season (2,743 yards)
Ranks No. 8 in the Big 12 in rushing plays of 10+ yards (23)
Ranks No. 12 nationally and No. 6 in Power Four in rushing yards this season
Recorded 149 yards on 23 carries, averaging 6.5 a carry and added a touchdown at Texas Tech
Totaled 90 yards on 18 carries, averaging 5.0 yards per carry, with a 13-yard touchdown reception at TCU
Carried the ball 15 times for 40 yards and caught two passes for 41 yards with his first career receiving touchdown against Cincinnati
Added a career-high 34 carries for 141 rushing yards and two touchdowns, averaging 4.1 yards per run against Arizona
Recorded 142 yards and two touchdowns on 20 carries at Iowa State
Finished with 193 yards on 29 carries against Oklahoma State
His 75-yard run against OSU was the longest WVU rush without scoring a touchdown since Oct. 25, 2018
In four games, Cook surpassed WVU's 2025 rushing leader (
Diore Hubbard
with 335 yards)
Registered 123 yards on 30 carries against Virginia in the Duke's Mayo Classic
Rushed for 52 yards on 10 carries and added a touchdown against UT Martin
Big 12 Conference Offensive Player of the Week: Coastal Carolina
Finished with 142 yards on 30 carries and added two catches for 11 yards against Coastal Carolina
His 72-yard carry on his first rush was the longest first career carry by a Mountaineer in program history and longest opening play to a season dating back to 1980
115 first-half rushing yards were the most since QB Garrett Greene had 136 against Cincinnati in 2023
Six conferences are represented among the semifinalists. The SEC and Big 12 lead with
three selections apiece, while the ACC, American, Big Ten and Pac-12 each have one.
Three finalists will be announced Tuesday, Nov. 24. The winner will be unveiled live on ESPN during The Home Depot College Football Awards on Friday, Dec. 11.
The formal presentation of the award will take place at the Doak Walker Award Presentation Banquet on Friday, Jan. 29, 2027, in Dallas. FOX Sports' Joel Klatt and Jenny Taft will host the event, which also will honor the recipient of the 2026 PwC Doak Walker Legends Award.
2026 Doak Walker Award Semifinalists
Jadan Baugh, Florida — 6-1, 225, Jr., Atlanta, Ga.
DeSean Bishop, Tennessee — 5-10, 215, Sr., Knoxville, Tenn.
Cam Cook
, West Virginia — 5-9, 203, Sr., Round Rock, Texas
Aiden Flora, Iowa State — 6-0, 195, r-So., Adel, Iowa.
Caleb Hawkins, Oklahoma State — 6-0, 219, So., Shawnee, Okla.
Wayne Knight, UCLA — 5-7, 193, 5th, Smyrna, Del.
Dylan Riley, Boise State — 5-10, 203, Jr., Moreno Valley, Calif.
Jamal Roberts, Missouri — 6-0, 216, Sr., St. Louis, Mo.
Nate Sheppard, Duke — 5-10, 205, So., Mandeville, La.
Jahiem White
, North Texas — 5-7, 190, Sr., Sarasota, Fla.
The Doak Walker Award National Selection Committee, which includes sportswriters, television commentators, analysts, radio sports personalities and former All-America and NFL All-Pro players, will select the three finalists and then cast a separate vote to determine the recipient.
Named after Doak Walker, a legendary figure in college football and the NFL, the Doak Walker Award presented by the PwC SMU Athletic Forum has honored the nation's top running back since 1990. Since 2022, the award has also recognized a national running back of the week. A select 20-person committee from the award's full voting body of media members and past winners chooses each weekly recipient.
The Doak Walker Award is a member of the National College Football Awards Association, which encompasses college football's most prestigious awards. The NCFAA's 25 awards have honored nearly 1,000 recipients since 1935. This season, 13 NCFAA awards will recognize national players of the week each Tuesday.
