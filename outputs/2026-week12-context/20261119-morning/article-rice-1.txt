2026 Rice Football Fact Book
Series Notes
•This is the 38th meeting between the schools overall and the 21st in Houston.
• The series dates back to 1916 when the Owls took the first meeting, 23-13. Rice leads the overall series 20-17-1. The Owls lead the series in Houston 13-7 and at First Community Stadium 9-5.Rice was 6-3 vs. Tulane as members of CUSA, but the Green Wave have won both games in the American.
• The Owls had won three consecutive in Houston until the 22nd-ranked Green Wave held off a furious Rice rally to escape with a 30-28 win in 2023.
• The Owls' 42-34 win in 2005 over the Wave was their first C-USA win.
• Rice's 17-13 win in 2013 was the school's final meeting as members of Conference USA and vaulted the Owls into the C-USA Championship game.
• Tulane took an early 3-0 lead, but Rice took a 17-3 lead into the locker room and rode a stout defensive performance that held the Green Wave to 123 yards on the day to set up a showdown with Marshall, which the Owls won to claim their first outright conference title since 1957.
Of Note
•Rice will wear a replica of the original Owl wing decals on their helmets on Saturday in honor of Homecoming.
•The Owl wings were first introduced by head coach Fred Goldsmith before the 1990 season in conjunction with the "New Set of Wings" campaign.Goldsmith took over a program in 1989 that had lost 18 consecutive games, including 14 straight in the SWC, and snapped both in his first season.
•Led by sophomore running back Trevor Cobb, the Owls would go 5-6 in their first season under the wings, missing a winning season when Baylor edged them 17-16 in the season finale.One year later, Cobb would become the first Owl to win a national individual award when he was awarded the Doak Walker Award as the nation's top running back, and two years later, the Owls posted their first winning season since 1963, finishing 6-5 overall (4-3 in the conference).
•The Owls have scored five touchdowns on their opening possession of either the first or second half since the second half at East Carolina.The Owls scored a touchdown to start the second half against the Pirates, followed by a TD on their opening series vs. Tulsa, on both of their opening possession at FAU and, after missing a field goal on their opening drive at North Texas, scored a touchdown to open the second half vs. the Mean Green.
•Rice is the only FBS team not to face a team with a losing record through its first nine games this season, and the Owls' schedule ranks as the 23rd toughest in FBS through 11/7.
•Cornerback
Jamir Benjamin
has played 155 of the last 158 defensive snaps over the last two games… He played all 69 at FAU and followed that up with 69 of 72 at North Texas… According to PFF, he allowed only two completions in 10 targets against the Green Wave.
Up Next
: A short week for the Owls, who will travel to Philadelphia for a Thursday night matchup with Temple on ESPN at 6:30 (Central). Rice wraps up the 2026 season on November 28 by hosting Army.  Game time will be announced on Monday.
