Cal To Renew Rivalry With Stanford In Berkeley
By:
Cal Athletics
California football (3-7, 2-5 ACC) will renew its rivalry with Bay Area foe Stanford (5-5, 3-4 ACC) in the 129th rendition of the Big Game this Saturday in Berkeley. Kickoff at Databricks Field at California Memorial Stadium is set for 4:30 p.m. PT with the game to be shown live on the ACC Network.
SATURDAY LINEUP
Venue:
Databricks Field at California Memorial Stadium, Berkeley, Calif.
Time:
4:30 p.m. PT
Watch (ACCN):
Justin Kutcher (PxP), Max Browne (Analyst), Madison Fitzpatrick (Sideline Reporter)
Listen (English Radio):
KNBR 104.5 – Justin Allegri (PxP), Mike Pawlawski (Analyst), Ben Ross (Sideline Reporter); Sirius XM Ch. 174 or 234/Sirius XM App, The Varsity App, YouTube Live Stream
Listen (Spanish Radio):
Cal Football YouTube Live Stream – Amaury Pi-González
THE 129TH BIG GAME
- The iconic Bay Area rivalry will be renewed with Cal and Stanford set to meet for the 129th time in history.
- The Golden Bears have won five of the last seven, including the two most recent home tilts, against the Cardinal.
- Stanford, which snapped Cal's four-Big Game win streak last season, holds a 66-51-11 all-time series advantage.
- The last time the Big Game was played in Berkeley, Cal won a 24-21 thriller at California Memorial Stadium when the Bears came back from a 14-point deficit and put together a memorable 98-yard scoring drive in the final minutes to retain The Axe trophy.
- The Big Game dates back to 1892 and is the sixth-longest active running series between two FBS programs and the second-longest in the West.
- The annual contest has been determined by a touchdown or less 56 times and on five occasions has been won on the final play, including arguably the most storied play in college football history - The Play in 1982.
CAL...
- Returns to its home turf for the first time since October 17, 2026, after playing a three-game road swing that included two trips to the East Coast.
- Ranks first in the ACC and fourth nationally in blocked kicks with its three this season. The Bears also rank second in the conference and tie for 13th nationally with one blocked punt this season.
- Ranks second in the ACC and ties for 16th nationally in defensive touchdowns with two. DB
Kingston Lopa
registered a pick-six against Syracuse while ILB
Kamar Mothudi
had a scoop-and-score against Clemson.
- Ranks sixth in the ACC in passes intercepted with seven. Five of those interceptions were registered to Lopa through the first three games while DB
Isaiah Crosby
logged an interception in the opening drive of the game against Clemson and DB
Aiden Manutai
intercepted a pass in the end zone against UNLV in the second quarter.
JKS: JUST KEEP STACKING
- QB
Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele
continues to stack the accolades as he has surpassed 500 completions in his collegiate career. He has 510 completions for 5,346 yards across two seasons.
- Sagapolutele also ties for sixth on Cal's all-time career 250-yard passing games list (11). He surpassed Aaron Rodgers and Fernando Mendoza, who each had 10 at Cal, and currently ties with Davis Webb. Jared Goff holds the Cal record with 30 250-yard games in his career as a Bear.
- Sagapolutele also ranks tied for seventh in career 300-yard passing games (4). Goff holds the Cal record with 22.
- Sagapolutele surpassed 5,000 career passing yards through 20 games played in his collegiate career. He is the second-fastest Cal quarterback in recent history to reach 5,000 passing yards as a Bear behind Goff who reached the milestone in his first 17 games played between the 2013-14 seasons.
- Sagapolutele's 66.0 completion percentage is tied for sixth-best in the conference while his completions per game (21.56) rank fourth in the ACC and tie for 16th nationally.
- He recorded the first punt of his career last weekend at Virginia, sending the ball 32 yards off his boot.
MOHAMMED MAKES IMPACT UPON RETURN
- RB
Adam Mohammed
has brought a spark to Cal's run-game since making his return to the field after being out with injury for several weeks.
- Mohammed leads Cal in rushing yards (404), carries (95), average rushing yards per game (57.70) and he co-leads the Bears in rushing touchdowns (4) this season.
- He finished Cal's game at Virginia as the Bears' leading rusher with 41 yards on 13 attempts (3.2 avg).
- Of his 404 net rushing yards, Mohammed has 357 yards after contact, according to PFF. He is the highest-graded rusher on Cal's offense with a 76.3 rushing grade and a 77.4 offensive grade this season, according to PFF.
TUITELE TAKE DOWN
- ILB
AJ Tuitele
leads the Bears in tackles and ranks in the ACC's top-25 with his 64 total.
- Tuitele posted a team-best and single-game high 11 tackles against Virginia.
- He co-leads the team in sacks with 3.0 and has pushed quarterbacks back a team-leading 25 yards on his sacks.
- Tuitele also leads Cal in quarterback hurries with seven.
DEREK DENIES POINTS
- DL
Derek Wilkins
blocked a Virginia PAT to mark the second blocked kick of his collegiate career. He also blocked a field goal attempt by Oregon State during the 2024 season.
- Wilkins has a knack for getting his paw on the ball from the trenches.
- He batted down two passes in Cal's season-opener against UCLA to register a pair of pass breakups.
- Wilkins recorded eight passes defended in 2025, which were the most by a Cal defensive lineman in recorded history.
- He finished the 2025 season as the highest-ranking defensive lineman in pass breakups in the ACC and was the only defensive lineman to be ranked in the conference's top-25 in passes defended.
LOPA NAMED SEMIFINALIST FOR BEDNARIK, THORPE AWARDS
- DB
Kingston Lopa
was named a Bednarik Award Semifinalist and a Paycom Jim Thorpe Award Semifinalist, making him one of 18 and 15 defensive backs, respectively, on the short lists for the national awards.
- Lopa ranks second in the nation with five interceptions. He led the NCAA for seven straight weeks, despite not playing against Virginia Tech and only playing only one snap at UNLV. He registered at least one takeaway in each of Cal's first three games this season.
- He ranks sixth in the conference and leads the Bears with eight passes defended. Lopa is averaging 1.0 passes defended per game, which is good for fifth in the ACC and ranks in the top-25 nationally.
HENDRICKS HAULS IN THE NUMBERS
- WR
Chase Hendricks
leads the Bears in several receiving categories this season.
- Hendricks paces the Bears with four receiving touchdowns, 65 receptions and 597 receiving yards. He has led the Bears in receptions for eight straight weeks.
- His 65 receptions and 6.5 receptions per game are both tied for fifth-most in the ACC, while his receiving yards rank in the top-15 in the conference.
