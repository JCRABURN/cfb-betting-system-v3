Jackson Action: QB Honored Again By Earl Campbell Award
11/17/2026 11:10:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
LAS VEGAS (UNLVRebels.com) –
For the third time this season, UNLV senior quarterback
Jackson Arnold
has earned Honorable Mention Earl Campbell Tyler Rose Award National Player of the Week, organizers announced Tuesday.
This time around, Arnold (Denton, Texas) threw two touchdowns and ran for the game-winner himself from 44 yards out to help pull off a memorable fourth-quarter comeback win at then-No. 23-ranked New Mexico on Saturday, 21-17.
Arnold was also named Mountain West Offensive Player of the Week on Monday after leading his team in both passing (201 yards) and rushing (98 yards) en route to ending the Lobos' 11-game home win streak. He now leads the league with 24 TDs responsible for with 17 passing and seven rushing.
The Earl Campbell Tyler Rose Award recognizes the top offensive players in the nation who either played at and graduated from a Texas high school or is currently playing at a Division One school in the Lone Star State. Arnold starred at Denton's Guyer High School before signing with Oklahoma to begin his career.
Head coach
Dan Mullen
's squad remains on the road this week at San Jose State on Saturday at 4 p.m. PT. The season finale on Nov. 28 will feature the Battle for the Fremont Cannon vs. UNR at 6 p.m. at Allegiant Stadium. Tickets are available by visiting unlvtickets.com or calling (702) 739-FANS (3267).
