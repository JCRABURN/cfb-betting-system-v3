UNT Drops First American Conference Game at UTSA
11/14/2026 11:49:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Share:
Share:
SAN ANTONIO — UNT's perfect mark in American Conference play came to an end on Saturday night, as the Mean Green fell 35-16 to UTSA at the Alamodome, where UNT has won just one time in program history.
UNT (7-3, 5-1 American) had trouble mustering much offense on a night that saw star quarterback
Tayven Jackson
leave early in the second half with an injury and star running back
Jahiem White
miss parts of the second and third quarters before returning to the game after an injury. On the night, UNT managed just 314 total yards, which is a season-low.
The Mean Green were shut out in the first quarter and trailed 21-3 at halftime before the UNT defense locked down for much of the second half and helped the Mean Green get within 21-16 midway through the fourth quarter before a pair of late UTSA touchdowns put the game out of reach.
UNT will be back in action next Saturday, as the Mean Green will travel to Tulane.
QUICK HITS
The UTSA win gives the Roadrunners a 9-6 advantage in the all-time the series and they have now won five of the last six meetings in the series and are 7-1 against the Mean Green in the Alamodome. UNT hasn't won in San Antonio since 2018.
UNT's captains for the game were
Zakye Barker
,
Tayven Jackson
,
Brec Long
and
Davion Rhodes
.
The Mean Green entered the season with 70 newcomers, which puts UNT at 215 new players over the past three seasons – good for the most in FBS in that time.
UNT is one of two FBS teams in the country to return zero starters from 2025 – the other is Iowa State.
The Mean Green have an average age of 21.01, which is the second-oldest roster in FBS behind Saturday's opponent, UTSA.
The loss snaps a 12-game conference winning streak, which is the longest streak since winning 18 straight from Oct. 13, 2001-Nov. 25, 2003.
When the Mean Green were forced to punt on their opening offensive possession, it snapped a streak of four straight games when UNT has scored on its first possession – the first time since the win at Tulsa on October 1.
The Mean Green are now 0-3 this season when the opponent scores first and 7-0 when scoring first, and
Neal Brown
is 55-16 in his career when his team scores first.
UNT trailed 7-0 after the first quarter, which marked its fourth time trailing after the first quarter this season and second time with no first-quarter points (Indiana).
UNT's 304 total yards of offense marked a season-low.
UNT's 21-3 second-quarter deficit was the largest deficit for the Mean Green this season since the season-opening loss at Indiana.
UNT's three first-half points was the fewest in a first half this season and the fewest since scoring three in the first half against Army on Nov. 9, 2024.
UNT ended a streak of four straight games in which quarterback
Tayven Jackson
had at least 300 passing yards, running back
Jahiem White
had at least 100 rushing yards and wide receiver
Justin Stevenson
had at least 100 receiving yards.
Saturday night's game marked the first time since a season-opening loss at Indiana that UNT hasn't had a 300-yard passer, a 100-yard rusher or a 100-yard receiver.
UNT's first scoring drive of the night, which resulted in a
Dominic De Freitas
field goal, spanned 7:07 and 83 yards on 16 plays, marking season longs in both plays and time for a scoring drive this season.
Fifth-year QB
Tayven Jackson
(Greenwood, Ind.) finished 10 for 20 for 125 yards passing and an interception before leaving the game early in the second half with an injury.
Jackson now has 3,005 passing yards on the season, making him the fifth straight UNT quarterback to throw for 3,000 yards in a season, following Austin Aune (2022), Chandler Rogers (2023), Chandler Morris (2024) and
Drew Mestemaker
(2025).
Jackson's first-quarter pick-six was his sixth interception of the year (first pick-six), and it was North Texas' first pick-six allowed since last season's American Championship Game loss at Tulane.
Sophomore QB
Chris Jimerson Jr.
(Fort Worth, Texas) finished the day 7 of 12 for 70 yards passing and a touchdown and added a team-high 33 yards on the ground and a rushing score after entering the game early in the second half.
Jimerson's 16-yard touchdown pass to
Max Reese
with 8:49 left in the third quarter was his second of the season and third of his career.
Jimerson's 9-yard touchdown run 8:32 remaining was the first rushing touchdown of his career.
Senior RB
Jahiem White
(York, Pa.) finished the day with 13 carries for a season-low 32 yards and added two catches for 3 yards. He missed parts of the second and third quarter after leaving with an injury but returned in the third quarter.
White failed to find the end zone for the first time this season and saw his streak of 11 straight games with a rushing touchdown end, as that streak dated back to Aug. 30, 2025, when he was at West Virginia.
Senior WR
Justin Stevenson
(Philadelphia, Pa.), finished with three catches for 39 yards.
Stevenson saw his streak of four straight 100-yard receiving days come to an end.
Junior WR
Baron Tipton
(Coppell, Texas) finished with two catches for a team-high 63 yards.
Fifth-year TE
Max Reese
(Chicago) finished the day with three catches for 50 yards and a touchdown.
Reese's 16-yard touchdown reception with 8:49 left in the third quarter was his fourth of the season.
Fifth-year LB
Zakye Barker
(Norcross, Ga.) finished the day with 12 tackles, including four solo stops, and 1.5 tackle for-loss.
Barker's 1.5 tackles for-loss gives him 4.0 on the season and 17.5 in his career.
Fifth-year LB
Aaron Alexander
(Belleville, Mich.) finished the day with seven tackles, including four solo stops and 1.5 sacks.
Alexander's 1.5 sacks give him 2.0 on the season.
Sophomore DB
Brandon Jones
(Houston) finished the day with six tackles, including four solo stops, and two tackles for-loss.
Senior DB
CJ Coombes
(Bellafonte, Del.) finished the day with five tackles, including two solo stops.
Sophomore DL
Brayden Knox
(Oklahoma City) finished the day with five tackles, including two solo stops, and 1.5 tackles for-loss.
Knox's 1.5 tackles for-loss give him 7.5 on the year.
Sophomore LB
Brenden Reese
(Longview, Texas) finished the day with four tackles, including three solo stops and an interception.
Reese's interception at the end of the third quarter was the first of his career.
Fifth-year DL
Davion Rhodes
(Camilla, Ga.) finished the day with two tackles, including a solo stop, and 1.5 sacks.
Rhodes' 1.5 sacks give him 6.5 on the season.
Sophomore K
Dominic De Freitas
(Nappanee, Ind.) finished 1-for-1 on extra points and 1-for-1 on field goals, with a 31-yarder to get UNT on the board in the second quarter.
De Freitas is now 13-for-16 on the season (7-for-7 in conference play) on field goals and 25 for his last 31.
