The Rivalry Returns: Philadelphia Awarded Four Future Army-Navy Game Matchups
November 17, 2026
|
Football
Share:
Philadelphia will welcome the Army-Navy Game back to Lincoln Financial Field in 2028, 2030, 2032, and 2034
PHILADELPHIA
– (November 17, 2026) The Philadelphia Convention and Visitors Bureau (PHLCVB), together with the Army West Point Athletic Association and Naval Academy Athletic Association representatives and city and state leaders, announced the steady return of the Army-Navy Game presented by USAA to Philadelphia for four additional years.
Philadelphia, which has hosted 90 of the previous 126 Army-Navy Games, will host the historic rivalry in 2028, 2030, 2032, and 2034. The city is also scheduled to host the 2027 game, which was awarded in a previous bid cycle. The remaining years will be awarded through a national competitive bidding process beginning in January 2027.
"We are thrilled to see the Army-Navy Game return to its rightful home in Philadelphia, a city that has proudly welcomed this iconic rivalry for more than 100 years," said Gregg Caren, president and CEO of the PHLCVB. "Philadelphia's place in our nation's history is deeply intertwined with the founding of both the United States Army and Navy, making our city a fitting and meaningful home for this storied tradition. We are honored to once again welcome the nation's midshipmen, cadets, alumni, fans, and visitors to Philadelphia to celebrate the game, its traditions, and the spirit of service. Thank you to our incredible partners and elected officials for helping support this return, namely Governor Shapiro, Mayor Parker, U.S. Senator McCormick, and Pennsylvania State Senator Hughes."
Known as "America's Game," the rivalry game will be played at Lincoln Financial Field. Additional events will take place across the city over the course of game weekend, including the Patriot Games, Army-Navy Pep Rally, and the Red, White & You Community Outreach Program.
"I am honored to welcome the Army-Navy Game back to Philadelphia right where America's Army, Navy, and Marine Corps began, established to secure the blessings of liberty for our new nation," said Governor Josh Shapiro. "Pennsylvania has a proud and storied history of military service, with generations of veterans, active-duty members, and military families serving here and calling the Commonwealth home. Philadelphia has hosted this matchup a record number of times – there is no better place than Pennsylvania to host America's game four more times in the next decade and that's why my Administration worked hard to ensure this tradition continues in our Commonwealth for years to come."
"The Army-Navy Game belongs in Philadelphia," said Senator Dave McCormick. "As a West Point graduate and Army veteran, I know this historic rivalry represents so much more than football. Bringing America's Game back to Pennsylvania has been a priority of mine since the day I was sworn into the Senate, and I'm proud to have played a role in making it happen."
Historically, Philadelphia has hosted 90 matchups across the game's 126-year legacy—more than any other host city.
"Philly is ready to welcome America's Game back home," said Mayor Cherelle L. Parker. "As our city celebrates the nation's 250th anniversary, securing four more Army-Navy Games is another historic moment for Philadelphia. We're ready for great games and to welcome visitors with the warmth Philly is known for. We're grateful to the PHL Sports team, and to the Philadelphia Eagles for their partnership in bringing America's Game back to Lincoln Financial Field."
"The Army-Navy Game is unlike any other sporting event in America. It represents service, sacrifice, tradition, and something far greater than football. Every great sporting event has a soul, and while America's Game belongs to the entire nation, so much of its soul resides in Philadelphia. We are excited to continue this historic partnership and build upon the tradition that makes this game so special," said Tom Theodorakis, Director of Athletics & CEO of the Army West Point Athletic Association.
"There is no city more closely connected to the history and tradition of the Army-Navy Game than Philadelphia," said Michael Kelly, Director of Athletics at the Naval Academy. "For generations, Philadelphia has provided the stage for some of the most memorable moments in this storied rivalry and there is something truly special about the passion, intensity and pageantry of America's Game when it is played in a city that has embraced this rivalry for so long. While we are excited about the opportunity to bring this extraordinary event to other cities across the country, Philadelphia will always hold a special place in the heart of the Army-Navy Game. This long-term commitment gives us the best of both worlds — a place that feels like home to America's Game while allowing us to share its excitement, tradition and significance with new audiences across the nation. We are incredibly grateful to the city of Philadelphia, the state of Pennsylvania, the Philadelphia Eagles, and all our partners for helping make that possible."
Awarded in a previous bid cycle, the city will also host the 2027 Army-Navy Game presented by USAA at Lincoln Financial Field on December 4, 2027. The game is anticipated to bring 70,000 fans to the city.
"Lincoln Financial Field is thrilled to welcome back the historic Army-Navy Game on Saturday, December 4, 2027," said Don Smolenski, President, Philadelphia Eagles. "This will serve as the 91st time that this legendary matchup will be played in the City of Philadelphia. We are grateful for the support and collaboration of everyone involved, including the Service Academies, city and state officials, our local hospitality community, and the PHLCVB."
In addition to the Academies, the weekend is produced by several local partners, including PHL Sports, a business development division of the PHLCVB, the City of Philadelphia, the Pennsylvania Convention Center, the Philadelphia Eagles, the Greater Philadelphia Hotel Association, among others.
Army West Point Athletic Association, Inc. (AWPAA)
AWPAA is an IRS code 501(c)(3) non-profit corporation established to execute the United States Military Academy's intercollegiate athletics program.  AWPAA is led by a board of directors that provides guidance to the director of athletics, who serves as the primary advisor to the USMA Superintendent and the USMA leadership on all NCAA operations. The AWPAA mission is to recruit, educate, train and inspire leaders of character who are committed to the Army values through an extraordinary Division I athletic experience.
