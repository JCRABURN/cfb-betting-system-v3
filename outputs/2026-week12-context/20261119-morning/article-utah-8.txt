Zereoue Williams Selected as Semifinalist for Outland Trophy
11/18/2026 6:20 PM
|
Football
Share:
Utah earns second straight semifinalist nod and third in program history
OMAHA, Neb.
— Utah offensive tackle
Zereoue Williams
has been named a semifinalist for the Outland Trophy, the Greater Omaha Sports Committee announced on Wednesday.
Williams is one of seven semifinalists from three conferences, with all being centers, offensive tackles/guards or defensive tackles.
The trophy is awarded annually to the nation's best college interior lineman on offense or defense, and semifinalists were selected by The All-America Committee of the Football Writers Association of America (FWAA).
Williams is the third Ute in history and second consecutive Utah player to be named at least an Outland Trophy semifinalist following
Jordan Gross
, who was named a finalist in 2002, and
Spencer Fano
, who won the trophy in 2025.
The Phoenix, Arizona native is an anchor on the Utah front that has earned Joe Moore Award Semifinalist honors. Through Week 10, Williams was Pro Football Network's sixth highest graded left tackle (92.1) in the FBS and has appeared in multiple NFL Mock Drafts as a potential first round selection.
Three finalists for The Outland Trophy will be named on Tuesday, Nov. 25. The recipient will be announced on Dec. 11 at The Home Depot College Football Awards.
