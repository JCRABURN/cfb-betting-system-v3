Buckeyes Speak to the Media Following Wednesday's Practice
11/18/2026 8:42:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
Head Coach Ryan Day
On playing Nebraska
:
“We've got to play good football, clean football, and handle the environment and as every coach would say, play one play at a time with unbelievable effort and focus.”
Ryan Day on Jeremiah Smith:
“There's not much that I'll probably say that you haven't heard already. But to me it's the mindset of somebody who shows up every day and he's never late, he never misses a rep. He goes hard every play, he's locked in and just ultra competitive and the consistency that he shows up with and the seriousness he takes.”
“When you're a coach and you have guys in the program where you're trying to say ‘this is what it's supposed to look like’ you look no further than Jeremiah.”
“I think he has found his voice too within the team. He doesn't say a whole lot, but when he does, you know guys are listening. Everybody's listening because of the respect everybody has for him in the building.”
DE Qua Russaw
On Zion Grady’s performance last week:
“Zion, he's a great pass rusher. He’s very technical and just loves the game. Like I said, I feel like we rush well together, all four guys, and yeah he just had a great game.”
On how he’s improved throughout the season:
“Yeah I've just been trying to get better each and every week. You know, just watching film after practice with the defense and Coach Patricia, just seeing ways I can get better and play faster. I feel like early on in the season, I was playing hesitant, and I didn't trust what I was seeing and that made me play slow. So definitely just trying to get better every week and play faster.”
On the Buckeyes’ playoff hopes:
“Coach Day just tells up to take one week at a time and just control what we can control. I mean, there’s not really anything we can do to control who they decide to let in, so just controlling what we can control each and every week.”
LB Riley Pettijohn
On what felt different about the defense in the win over Northwestern:
“Things were definitely more simple and we just got to focus on the details like effort, technique…and it helped us.”
On how comfortable he is in blitzing scenarios:
“I think I'm pretty comfortable with it. They trust me enough to put me out there in those situations, so, yeah, I'm pretty comfortable with it. The reps help me get better at it.”
On if the recent college football playoff ranking have given the team an extra spark:
“Yeah, for sure. I mean, we can't change anything about the past, but we can always just learn from it. So we just keep stacking days and there’s definitely hope, definitely hope. So, just coming in each and every day working hard.”
OL Ian Moore
On how the offensive line has improved over the last few weeks:
“Just the mentality of I’m rocking off the ball and you’re not gonna stop me. I was really proud of that as an offensive lineman.”
On the challenge of finding the groove with injuries/illness on the offensive line:
“Just rallying around everybody, we haven’t let it diminish our spirits or anything. So I feel like just having that confidence with whoever’s out there is going to play as hard as they can and we’re just going to play our brand of football.”
On Carson Hinzman as a teammate:
“It’s just fun. [He’s] just always uplifting and I mean when you’re around Carson, it’s a game, it’s not a job. It’s the game that we’ve been playing since we were kids and that just brings that life and that energy back into it.”
