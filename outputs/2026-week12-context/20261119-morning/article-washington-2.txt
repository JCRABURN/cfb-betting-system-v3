EAST LANSING, Mich. (AP)
—
Jayden Limar
had a tiebreaking, 1-yard touchdown run with 2:24 left and Washington held on to beat Michigan State 21-14 on Saturday night after being one of the teams whose travel plans were delayed by the Federal Aviation Administration's decision to ground all flights.
The Huskies (6-4 overall, 3-4 Big Ten) became eligible for a bowl with their third straight win on the road.
UW's defense thwarted MSU's two chances to break a 14-all tie in the second half and sealed the win with a forced fumble at the Washington 41 with 35 seconds remaining.
Ramzak Fruean
nearly sacked Cam Fancher and forced an ill-advised pass over his head that
Jacob Manu
intercepted with one hand on the sideline at the Washington 23 late in the third quarter.

November 15, 2026
Washington used a trick play to spark its game-winning drive.
Demond Williams Jr.
tossed the ball to
Dezmen Roebuck
, who threw a lateral back to the quarterback to set up a 32-yard pass to
Decker DeGraaf
and Limar's 1-yard run put the Huskies ahead a few plays later.
Williams finished 22-for-33 for 288 yards and the two TD passes to Lawson.
Lawson caught five passes for 124 yards - his third 100-yard receiving game in four games.
Michigan State quarterback Alessio Milivojevic injured his left arm in the second quarter after completing 7 of 12 passes for 108 yards.
For the second consecutive week, the Huskies held an opponent off the scoreboard in the second half.
Demond Williams Jr.
threw a 58-yard touchdown pass to
Chris Lawson
late in the first quarter and connected with the receiver again for a 17-yard, go-ahead touchdown pass midway through the second quarter.
