SETTING THE SCENE
• SMU hits the road for a major matchup against No. 5 Notre Dame on Saturday on NBC and Peacock.
• The Mustangs are coming off a loss to No. 13 Wake Forest this past weekend at Ford Stadium.
•
Kevin Jennings
finished the game with 209 yards passing and a passing touchdown.
QUICK HITS
• The Mustangs have gone 19-5 in ACC play since joining the conference in 2024, the most wins by an ACC team during that span.
• SMU has recorded 152 sacks since 2023, that is tied for the second most in all FBS during that span.
• Since 2024, the Mustangs have forced 59 turnovers, which ranks tied for 17th in the in country during that span and the second-most in the ACC.
• Jennings has posted a record of 26-10 as the starter for the Mustangs.
• Jennings is the ACC active leader in career passing yards (10,682) and career total yards (11,368).
• Jennings is one of 12 FBS active quarterbacks with at least 10,000 yards of total offense.
• Jennings' 16-2 (.888) home record ranks as the fourth-best among active FBS quarterbacks.
• The Mustangs are 36-11 in their last 46 games, and 34-8 in their last 42 regular-season contests.
• SMU has posted a record of 36-5 during Lashlee's five seasons in charge when they score 30-or-more points in a game.
• The Mustangs have had at least seven receivers catch a pass in 50 of the past 54 games.
• SMU has recorded 32 offensive scoring drives since 2025 that resulted in touchdowns in under two minutes.
•
Rhett Lashlee
boasts a 32-8 conference record in his career, best in program history.
• The Mustangs have recorded 45 wins since 2022, which is second-most by the 13 FBS teams in the state.
• Of those 45 wins, 17 of those have come on the road.
• Lashlee has posted a 15-3 (.833) mark for November since 2022.
• The 15 wins are tied for the seventh most by any team in FBS for the month of November since 2022.
THE SERIES
• This is the 14th meeting in the series.
• Notre Dame leads the series, 10-3.
• The two teams last met in 1989, with the No.1-ranked Irish winning South Bend.
ABOUT THE FIGHTING IRISH
• Notre Dame is coming off a 41-7 victory against Boston College.
• The Fighting Irish have a 6-0 mark at home this year.
CLOSING TIME
• SMU has been able to close in the month of November under head coach
Rhett Lashlee
.
• Lashlee has posted an 15-3 (.833) mark for November in his five seasons as leader of the Mustangs.
• The .833 winning percentage for the month ranks as the highest for a head coach in the program's history.
• The 15 wins are tied for the seventh most by any team in FBS for the month of November since 2022.
