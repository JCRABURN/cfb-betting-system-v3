Bulls’ Regular Season Finale vs. Tulane to Kick Off at 3:30 p.m.
November 16, 2026
|
Football
Share:
South Florida will honor its seniors and history at Raymond James Stadium
TAMPA, Fla. (November 16, 2026)
– South Florida's regular season finale on Nov. 28 against Tulane will kick off at 3:30 p.m. and be streamed on ESPN+, the American Conference announced on Monday.
The Bulls and Green Wave sixth time in series history and first time since 2024. Tulane leads the all-time series 4-1; South Florida's lone win came in 2017, a 34-28 decision at Yulman Stadium.
The game on Nov. 28 will serve as Senior Day and will also include elements to celebrate South Florida's history playing at Raymond James Stadium. The contest is the final scheduled game for the Bulls at Raymond James Stadium before moving into its new on-campus stadium in 2027.
UP NEXT:
South Florida heads a couple hours south to take on FAU in Boca Raton, Fla., this Saturday, Nov. 21 in a 2:30 p.m. kick on ESPN+. USF leads the series 5-2 and has won the last two meetings, including a 44-21 victory in the Bulls last trip to Boca Raton in 2024.

Call 1-800-GO-BULLS (1-800-462-8557) or email BullsTickets@usf.edu to claim your season or single-game tickets. You can also download the South Florida Bulls app on the Android or Apple stores to purchase tickets and parking. Visit USFBullsTix.com for more information.
About South Florida Football
The South Florida football program first took the field in 1997 and will play its 30th season in 2026.
In 2025, the Bulls went 9-4, earned a third straight bowl appearance, made the program's first College Football Playoff ranking appearance, and were ranked four times in the weekly Associated Press Top 25 poll. South Florida completed construction on a new $22-million Indoor Performance Facility in 2023, and construction is in progress on an on-campus stadium and football operations center broke slated for completion by 2027.
The Bulls have posted 18 winning seasons, earned 16 All-America selections (including two consensus selections, the last coming in 2021), and 35 first-team all-conference honorees. South Florida has had 30 players selected in the NFL Draft, while the Bulls have made 13 bowl game appearances (going 8-5), tied for the third-most nationally in the first 25 seasons of a program's bowl eligibility. South Florida posted back-to-back 10-win seasons in 2016 and 2017, logging a program-record 11-2 mark in 2016, while finishing both seasons ranked in the Top 25. The Bulls spent a program-record 20 straight weeks ranked in the Top 25 during the 2016 and 2017 campaigns and reached as high as No. 2 in the AP rankings during the 2007 season.
