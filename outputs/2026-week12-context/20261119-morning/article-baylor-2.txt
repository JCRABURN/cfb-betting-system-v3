WACO, Texas –
Baylor football wraps up its home schedule with a matchup with No. 11 Texas Tech on Saturday at McLane Stadium. Kickoff is slated for 2:30 p.m. on FOX and the Baylor Sports Media Network.
Baylor (6-4, 4-3 Big 12 Conference) faces off with Texas Tech (9-1, 6-1 Big 12) on Saturday to conclude the home schedule. The game will serve as Senior Day and a Salute to Service.
The game will be called on FOX by Jack Kizer (play-by-play) and Mark Helfrich (analyst). On the Baylor Sports Media Network, the game will be broadcast by the Voice of the Bears,
John Morris
(play-by-play), and BU greats Geff Gandy (analyst) and Bryson Jackson (sideline).
SCOUTING THE RED RAIDERS
• Texas Tech is under the direction of former Baylor assistant coach Joey McGuire, who is in his fifth year leading the Red Raiders. TTU opened the year with seven straight wins, including wins over West Virginia, No. 22 Arizona, Arizona State, Colorado, Sam Houston, No. 22 Houston, at Oregon State and Abilene Christian. TTU is coming off a matchup at No. 25 Oklahoma State on Saturday that saw the Raiders open up a large lead early and extend it in the second half to post a 52-27 win and improve to 9-1 and 6-1 in league play.
• Tech averages 39 points, 448.6 yards of offense, including 210.6 rushing and 238 passing in 2026. Opponents are scoring just 24.4 points per game, with 408.6 yards of total offense, including 288 passing and 120.6 rushing.
• QB Will Hammond has a team-high 1,566 passing yards with six TDs and six interceptions. Thomas Castellanos has thrown for 814 yards with 13 TDs and just one interception. Catellanos has rushed for 322 yards and three scores. J'koby Williams leads the ground game with 1,078 rushing yards and eight TDs. Kenny Johnson has a team-high 43 catches for 707 yards and five TDs. Coy Eakin and Terrance Carter Jr. are additional pass-game weapons, with Eakin owning 24 catches, Carter with 27 and five TDs, and Micah Hudson has 17 catches for 306 yards and a TD. Austin Romaine leads the team with 89 tackles, including 5.5 for a loss with 1.5 sacks and two interceptions. Ben Roberts has added 81 tackles, with eight TFLs and 1.5 sacks, picking off two passes.
• Kenny Perry serves as the associate head coach/special teams coordinator/running backs coach for McGuire, with Mack Leftwich serving as the OC/QB coach and Shiel Wood as the DC/ILB coach. Leftwich is in his second season ater coming over from Texas State from 2023-24 and UIW from 2018-22. Wood is also in his second season, after serving as the DC at Houston in 2024, also coordinating Tulane (2023), Troy (2022) and Army (2021) defenses.
HOT START FUELS BYU PAST BEARS IN PROVO
• No. 10 BYU used a hot start to drive a 28-7 win over Baylor football, at LaVell Edwards Stadium.
• Baylor (6-4, 4-3 Big 12 Conference) couldn't overcome a sluggish start as BYU (8-2, 5-2 Big 12) put up a pair of quick, first-quarter touchdowns to jump out to an early lead that the Bears couldn't recover from. The Bears rallied to cut the lead to 21-7 in the fourth quarter but saw the Cougars extend the lead to 28-7 in the closing minutes.
• The loss for BU snapped a two-game winning streak under interim head coach
Joe Klanderman
, who had led the Bears to wins at UCF and vs. Iowa State over the last two weeks. The Bears entered the game needing wins in their last three games - which featured staff tests at top-10 BYU, vs. No. 10 Texas Tech and at No. 14 Houston - to vault into the Big 12 Championship Game contention.
• Meanwhile, BYU entered the game needing to snap a stretch of two losses in the last three outings to remain in the hunt for the College Football Playoff. The Cougars suffered a loss at UCF, won vs. Arizona State, and last week fell 28-20 at now-No. 2 Utah.
• The Baylor offense was thwarted throughout the game, as the Cougars held the Bears to just 238 yards of total offense, including 159 yards passing and 79 yards rushing. The Cougars rolled to 220 yards of offense in the first half and the Baylor defense stiffened in the second stanza, holding BYU to just 92 yards of total offense in the final two periods.
SERIES HISTORY
• Baylor leads the all-time series with Texas Tech, 42-40-1. The Bears have won five of the last seven meetings with Texas Tech and 10 of the last 14 clashes.
• The Red Raiders and the Bears resumed a home-and-home series in 2019 after playing 10 consecutive meetings in Arlington or Dallas, with the Bears owning a 7-3 record in those meetings.
• The Bears own a 20-12 lead in the series in Waco and have won two of the last three meetings. Tech won the last meeting in Waco, 39-14 in 2023.
LAST MEETING IN SERIES
• The Bears put together their most complete performance of 2024, thrashing Texas Tech in Lubbock in front of a sold-out homecoming crowd, 59-32, in a game that saw the lead as large as 52-21 in the closing minutes of the fourth quarter.
• It marked the second consecutive rout for Baylor at Texas Tech, as the Bears dominated the Raiders in Lubbock in 2022, 45-17, giving Baylor 104 combined points over the last two games at TTU. The Bears have now won 10 of its last 15 games against Tech, including five of its last seven. Over those 10 wins, Baylor has wins of 24, 29, 28, 11, 28 and 24 points.
• It marked the first game for Baylor to hang 50 points or more on a Big 12 foe since 2019 and its first 50+ point game since UAlbany in 2022. It was the most points scored vs. Tech since 2015, a 63-35 win in Arlington.
• Baylor's 59 points on Saturday were the most by an FBS program in Week Eight and tied for the most in a conference game in a Power Four league this season.
• The Bears finished with 529 yards of total offense, with 255 rushing and 274 passing, both season highs.
• QB
Sawyer Robertson
returned to his hometown of Lubbock and tossed five TDs, a career high and the most for Baylor QB since Seth Russell in 2015. He was 21-of-32 for 274 yards. Freshman tailback
Bryson Washington
rushed for 116 yards and two TDs on just 10 carries. WR
Josh Cameron
had three receiving TDs with six catches for 75 yards and also returned a punt 74 yards to the one-yard line.
PREVIOUS TWO MEETINGS IN THE SERIES
• Texas Tech posted a strong 39-14 win over the Bears in Waco in 2023, which included a 17-3 lead at halftime and saw the margin balloon to 32-11 with just under 10 minutes left in the game.
• The Red Raiders rolled to 186 yards rushing and threw for 180 yards, totaling 366 yards of total offense, while BU was held to 341 yards offensively, with 324 in the air and 17 yards on the ground. Tech was 10-of-18 in third-down conversions and the Bears were limited to 4-of-15 and 1-of-6 on fourth down. Tech totaled six sacks, including three from current Baylor outside linebacker Steve Linton, who forced a pair of fumbles. Tech QB Behren Morton threw three TDs, Baylor QB Blake Shapen was 22 of 38 for 324 yards and a score, with WR Monaray Baldwin going for five catches for 126  yards.
• The Bears routed the Red Raiders in Lubbock in 2022, 45-17, using a strong defensive effort that forced five Tech interceptions - from three different QBs - to post the win. It was Tech's first home loss of the season and thwarted the hall of fame and ring of honor celebration which featured former Texas Tech QB Patrick Mahomes returning to be honored in the ring of honor.
• Baylor never trailed in the game, opening up with a field goal from John Mayers and after Tech answered with three points of its own, Baylor opened up the game with a 24-3 lead following a second Richard Reese TD rush with 11:09 left in the third quarter. The Bears scored 21 unanswered to end the game, with Reese rushing for a third score, the Bears returning an interception for a TD and BU finishing off the game with a rushing TD.
• The Bears rushed for 231 yards and four TDs and held the high-octane Tech offense to just 159 yards passing on 12 of 38 attempts, with five interceptions. BU outgained Tech, 442-308, sacking the Red Raiders six times.
• Blake Shapen was 19 of 30 for 211 yards and a TD, while Tech used Behren Mortan (11-for-34, 1 TD, 3 INT), Tyler Shough (1-2, 1 INT) and Donovan Smith (0-2, 1 INT) at QB. Reese rushed 36 times for 148 yards and three TDs, while Shapen added 30 yards rushing. Tahj Brooks led the Tech offense with 98 yards on 16 carries.
Gavin Holmes
had six catches for 77 yards and Hal Presley nabbed a TD catch. Defensively, Gabe Hall had three sacks and a QB hurry.
