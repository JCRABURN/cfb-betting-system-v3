Cougar Center Advances to Outland Trophy Semifinals
11/18/2026 7:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
Top offensive lineman named a “walk-on of the year” award semifinalist
HOUSTON –
Houston offensive lineman
Anthony Boswell
became Houston's second-ever Outland Trophy semifinalist this week following a strong start to the 2026 Cougars Football season.
The Outland Trophy is an annual college football award given to the best interior lineman (offensive or defensive tackle, guard or center) and was won by Cougar legend Ed Oliver in 2017.
Boswell started his career at Purdue before transferring to Toledo, where he was a Freshman All-American in 2025, before joining the Cougars this season.
The Indianapolis, Ind., native's 91.4 offense grade leads all Outland Trophy semifinalists and is the second highest graded offensive lineman in the country.
Boswell has been a pillar in Houston's offensive line which has graded as one of the top units in the country and was named to the Joe Moore Award Semifinals earlier this month. The center is the highest graded snapper in the country and is one of two Outland semifinalists yet to allow a QB hit or sack.

ABOUT THE OUTLAND TROPHY
The Outland Trophy winner is chosen from three finalists who are a part of the annual FWAA All-America Team. The FWAA All-America Committee, after voting input from the entire membership, selects a 26-man first team and eventually the three Outland finalists. Committee members, then by individual ballot, select the winner. Only interior linemen on offense or defense are eligible for the award; ends are not eligible.
The Outland Trophy is the third-oldest major college football award. Created in 1946 when Dr. John Outland presented the FWAA with a financial contribution to initiate the award, the Outland Trophy has been given to the best interior lineman in college football ever since. Dr. Outland, an All-American at the University of Pennsylvania in the late 1890s, eventually took up practice in Kansas City, Mo. An avid outdoorsman, Dr. Outland believed linemen did not get the credit they deserved and wanted an award to recognize them.
