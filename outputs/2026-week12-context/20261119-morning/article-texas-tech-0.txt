Simmons recognized as Big 12 Special Teams Player of the Week
November 16, 2026
|
Football
Big 12 Weekly Awards
Share:
Simmons returned the opening kickoff for a touchdown in Texas Tech’s 52-27 rout over Oklahoma State
LUBBOCK, Texas
– Texas Tech junior wide receiver
Malcolm Simmons
was named the Big 12 Special Teams Player of the Week on Monday following his performance in the Red Raiders' 52-27 rout over No. 25 Oklahoma State.
Simmons silenced the capacity Oklahoma State crowd by returning the opening kickoff 97 yards for a touchdown, the first of his career. It marked the second-straight season the Red Raiders had opened their matchup against the Cowboys with a kickoff return for a touchdown. Texas Tech had not returned a kickoff for a touchdown since
J'Koby Williams
went 99 yards in the 2025 win over the Cowboys.
Simmons scored twice in the win as he hauled in a 45-yard touchdown on Texas Tech's first play of the second half, delivering another defining blow to give the Red Raiders a 35-20 lead. It was the first multi-touchdown game of Simmons' career as he helped push Texas Tech to its third win over a ranked opponent this season. Simmons now has four catches on the year, three of which have gone for touchdowns.
The performance was special historically as Simmons became only the second Big 12 player with a 95-yard kick return or longer for a touchdown and at least a 45-yard touchdown reception in the same game since West Virginia's Shelton Gibson in 2015.
The Red Raiders have dominated the Big 12 special teams award this season, claiming the weekly honor for the fifth time already. Simmons joins punter
Will Karoll
– a three-time weekly award winner this year – and kicker
Stone Harrington
on that list of Big 12 special teams weekly award winners.
The win over Oklahoma State kicked off back-to-back weeks on the road for the Red Raiders, who travel to Baylor this Saturday afternoon. Kickoff between the Red Raiders and Bears is slated for 2:30 p.m. with coverage on FOX and the Texas Tech Sports Network.
