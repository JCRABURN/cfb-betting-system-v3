CYCLONES vs.
KNIGHTS
Iowa State and UCF meet for just the second time in the programs' history and for the first time ever in Orlando. The only other meeting came in 2024, a thriller in which the Cyclones came from behind to win 38-35 at Jack Trice Stadium.
In that contest, ISU trailed 35-27 with 11:38 left following RJ Harvey's second rushing touchdown of the game.
Kyle Konrardy
made a 30-yard field goal with 8:53 left and
Rocco Becht
scored a 1-yard TD with 30 seconds remaining to give ISU the win, improving the Cyclones to 7-0 that season.
SCOUTING THE KNIGHTS
UCF is 5-5 in its second season of head coach Scott Frost's second stint at the school. The Knights are coming off a 26-13 home loss to Arizona State last weekend and are 4-2 inside Acrisure Bounce House this season, including a win over then-No.2 BYU. UCF is looking for bowl eligibility for the ninth time in the last 11 seasons and for the second time since it joined the Big 12 Conference.
Quarterback Alonza Barnett III, a transfer from James Madison, returned to his starting job earlier this month at Kansas after being injured in UCF's game against Pitt on Sept. 12. Last season, he was the Sun Belt Player of the Year and led JMU to a College Football Playoff appearance. He has completed 62 percent of his passes for 985 yards and five touchdowns against two interceptions this season. UCF has four receivers with 200-plus yards and multiple TDs, led by Josh Derry's 708 yards and four scores. Leading rusher Duke Watson, who has 596 yards and three touchdowns on the season, missed last week's game due to injury but is probable for Friday's game.
Defensively, UCF ranks outside the top 80 nationally in total defense (380.0) and scoring defense (26.0). The Knights are allowing 135.3 rush yards per game but have largely avoided allowing the chunk plays, surrendering 32 rushes of 10-plus yards this season, the second-fewest in the Big 12. The Knights have forced 14 turnovers this season. Jeffson Lafontant leads the team with six tackles for loss while Tackett Curtis is the team's leading tackler (67). Curtis also has a pair of interceptions and sacks on the season.

NATION'S LEADING RUSHER
Aiden Flora
is putting together one of the best seasons in recent Cyclone football history. He was named to a semifinalist for the Doak Walker Award, given to the nation's top running back, and a finalist for the Paul Hornung Award, given to the most versatile player in college football.
With two weeks remaining in the regular season, Flora leads the nation in rushing (1,335) and is fourth nationally averaging 7.2 yards per carry. His 1,335 rushing yards rank eighth on ISU's all-time single-season record list. He has 12 rushing touchdowns on the season, the 12
th
-most nationally.
A walk-on turned scholarship player, Flora is a threat to score from anywhere on the field with four rushing touchdowns of 60-plus yards, tied for the most nationally. His six rushing touchdowns of 40-plus yards are also tied for the most in FBS this season.
RAYNOR PLAYING HIS BEST BALL
Despite losing many of his top targets, quarterback
Jaylen Raynor
has been playing some of his best ball of the season over the last three games. He is averaging 264.7 passing yards and completing 61.4 percent of his passes over the stretch.
Raynor has five games this season with 200-plus yards passing, including each of the last three since being replaced by
Connor Moberly
in the second half at Arizona.
Among active players, Raynor's 25 career victories as a starting quarterback are tied for the seventh-most in the country and the third-most in the Big 12. His 10-career game-winning drives are the most among active players.
KEY STORYLINES
Last time out, Iowa State's offense continued to roll as it racked up 551 total yards on the way to a 41-13 win against Cincinnati at Jack Trice Stadium.
ISU's defensive unit had one of its better performances of the season, holding the Bearcats to 13 points, its second-lowest scoring output of the season. ISU held UC scoreless in the second half.
The Cyclones are 97 percent in the red zone this season, scoring touchdowns on 18-of-30 possessions (60%). ISU was the last remaining FBS team that was perfect in the red zone this season before missing a field goal last weekend.
ISU is 5-1 at home but 0-4 on the road this season. The Cyclones have played a tough road schedule with three of the four losses coming to current top-20 teams.
Head coach Jimmy Rogers has led the Cyclones to five wins in his first season, the second-most by a first-year head coach in school history, behind only Paul Rhoads (7) in 2009.
A win would make Iowa State bowl eligible for the ninth time in the last 11 seasons. The Cyclone roster underwent a nearly complete overhaul prior to this season. Of the 126 players on the roster, 83 are newcomers to the program.
ISU has played one of the nation's toughest schedules. Its opponents own a .685 winning percentage which is tied for third-best nationally, while ISU's five ranked opponents (at kickoff) are tied for the second-most nationally behind only Ole Miss (6).
Iowa State has been bitten by the injury bug among players they expected to be starters or key contributors. Through 10 games, ISU has lost double-digit players for the season, including five of their expected top seven receivers heading into fall camp.
Cyclone linebacker
Sullivan Schlimgen
is third among Big 12 players with 9.3 tackles per game. He enters this week with 93 tackles as he trends toward being the first Cyclone with 100 tackles in a season since Beau Freyler (2023).
Jamal Polite Jr.
leads all freshman wide receivers nationally averaging 19.5 yards per reception. He has a catch in nine-straight games to begin his career.
Omari Hayes
has a catch in all 10 games and leads the Cyclones with 26 receptions this season.
Makai Sat
has started the previous four games, the first action of his career.
Max Baloun
earned his first sack of the season last week against Cincinnati and later forced a fumble, his first of the season and second of his career. He also recovered the first fumble of his career.
Jace Gilbert
had perhaps his best game as a Cyclone with a career high five tackles, 1.5 sacks, 2.5 TFL, a forced fumble and a QB hurry against Cincinnati.
