Valsin and Rooks to Join Kittley’s Weekly ESPN+ Presser
11/16/2026 3:41:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
BOCA RATON, Fla.
– Florida Atlantic's Hagerty Family Head Football Coach
Zach Kittley
will be joined by senior WR
Kelby Valsin
and graduate DB
Tyson Rooks
for a Tuesday, Nov. 16 press conference, airing live on ESPN+ at 12 p.m.
The trio will look ahead to the Owls' Saturday regular season home finale on Howard Schnellenberger Field at Flagler Credit Union Stadium. Kickoff is at 2:30 p.m. on ESPN+ Fox Sports South Florida 640 AM.
In the team's previous outing on the road at Tulsa, Valsin collected double-digit receptions for the second time this season, with 10 for 94 yards and a touchdown. Ranking No. 5 nationally in receptions per game (7.6), he is also on the verge of a 1,000-yard season, currently at 923 while tallying seven scores. When Valsin crosses the plateau, he will become the second Owl (Messer was the first) to do so this season, the first time FAU has had two receivers with 1,000 yards.
Rooks has settled into a starting safety role over the last three games with 16 tackles, 2.5 for a loss. Most recently, he recorded his first takeaway of the season, a 38-yard interception return at the end of the first half against the Golden Hurricane that contributed to the 3-1 turnover margin.
FOLLOW THE OWLS
Inside the Owls Burrow airs on The Spot, South Florida 39 (WSFL-TV) weekly on Thursdays at 5:30 p.m. in the Miami-Ft. Lauderdale DMA with replays running to be determined. The Spot, South Florida 9 (WHDT-TV), will air the show on Thursday at 6 p.m. in the West Palm Beach-Fort Pierce DMA with replays to be determined.
The Coaches Radio Show presented by Duffy's Sports Grill takes place at the Duffy's on St. Andrew's Boulevard in Boca Raton. Fans can join in person or listen live on FOX Sports South Florida from 6 to 7 p.m. This week's guests include Hagerty Family Head Football Coach
Zach Kittley
and Wide Receiver Coach
Trent Vasey
