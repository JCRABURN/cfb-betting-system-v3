Morgan Scalley Weekly Press Conference: TCU
11/16/2026 4:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
SALT LAKE CITY –
University of Utah head football coach
Morgan Scalley
held his weekly press conference on Monday with members of the media inside the Spence and Cleone Eccles Football Center.
Selected quotes from Coach Scalley can be seen below. Click
here
for the full press conference video.
Opening Statement…
State of the U address. Congrats to Coach Kepler and the women's cross country team. They took fourth in the mountain regionals over the weekend and they got an at-large bid to the NCAA championships. That is awesome. That will be in Indiana this upcoming Saturday. Men's and women's basketball, both big wins last week. Shout out to them. Great start to the season. Women are home tonight against Houston Christian and Coach Jensen and the boys have Eastern Washington tomorrow. Volleyball team, great job. They went 2-0 this week. Swept Texas Tech at home, beat Houston on the road, great job. They're back in the Huntsman this Thursday and Saturday and then good luck to the swim and dive teams as they head back out on the road for competitions this week. Wrapping up the Arizona game, grateful to our fans that traveled. We had a good contingent there and what an awesome environment that was. The back- and-forth. Obviously, we did some things to give them some momentum and we also did some really good things. I think the challenge was to the offensive and defensive lines in this game. We knew that was a big strength for them. We had to play physical, we had to stop the run, which we did, and we had to do a good job running the football, which we did.
Daniel Bray
, Devon, fantastic job by everyone involved there, but again, it all starts with the front, offensive lines, defensive lines, so proud of those guys for the preparation that they put in and executing on game day.
Gage Victor
, I want to point him out, he was our snapper, did the long snaps, did the short snaps, as Logan is continuing to heal, but he did a fantastic job and then
Dillon Curtis
came up big in this game. 100% on PATs and field goals. That was a big factor in the game. Offensively, Devon, Bray, we talked about those guys. Second half, I thought we were much better, contested balls, catching the football. First half, we wish we'd have come down with a couple more of those. It was great to see Byrd get a touchdown for all that he does for the team and the role he plays, super happy for him. On the defense, it was a big stop after the muff punt that we had. A big stop, forcing them to kick a field goal and then four sacks on the day, but we also had a lot of pressure, forcing him to throw off schedule. We have some things to clean up for sure on both sides of the ball. Special teams, mentioned Dillon, but
Orion Phillips
had the 84-yard punt and then the muff punt was costly. There was another instance in that game where I think people were confused as to why Mana would have chosen to go after a ball and it was actually the correct decision for him to do that. It hit an Arizona player at that point. It's going to be dead right where he touches it and there's no, even if Mana were to go up to that ball and say he batted it and they end up jumping on it, it goes back to where it was first touched by them. The only way that we could have lost that ball is for Mana to pick it up, possess it, and then strip it from him. So, we teach him in that instance, if you see that it's hit the opposing team, go see if you can gain anything out of it. He did. It was the right decision by him to do that. The TCU game, this will be our first time, it would have been my sophomore year as a full-time assistant coach, first time since 2009 we'll be going back to Fort Worth to play TCU. We've been there for a bowl game and stuff like that, but first time since 2009 in Amon G. Carter Stadium. Much respect to coach Sonny Dykes and what he's been able to do. His first season, went to the National Championship Game when he took over there at TCU. I know Andy Avalos, their defensive coordinator. They're doing a heck of a job on the defensive side of the ball. They're throwing the heck out of it on the offensive side of the ball, 21st in the country in passing offense. On the defensive side of the ball, they're 12th in scoring defense, first in the Big 12, 22nd in total defense, 23rd in rush defense, second in red zone defense. As good as our red zone defense has been, they're second in the country. They are a tough, tough outfit. They've lost five games by a combined total of 25 points. Every single one of their losses has been a one possession loss, so they are competitive, they are tough. It's going to be at their place, and so this would be a very good test for our guys, going back out on the road again and looking forward to it.
On how much it means to him to represent the program and why…
I think you care about the things that shape you, the things that have made you, so many valuable lessons here. I've lived more of my life in this facility, not this specific facility, than I have outside of it. A lot of memories of me growing up, going to games, obviously playing here, lessons learned from being here. I just want to be in a position to pass that on. In terms of why this is so meaningful to me, I think you're passionate about what you put your time into. I've put a lot of time into this, into the players, into wanting and knowing what this could be, knowing how special this place is and just being in a position to continue to show it to the world. We're in a good spot, but no one cares if you lose the last two. We  have to keep going and so our guys are focused. They understand what's got us here, and we got to continue on, but I'll also say that, again, the minute we start, I start thinking that this is a me thing, there's so many people involved in making this what it is.  I'm never going to be the guy that's sitting there like, OK, I deserve this. It's about us.
On how he would make the case for
Devon Dampier
in the Heisman Trophy race…
He's an integral part of what we do. You look at the position that he plays and not often with that position do you get as much productivity running the ball. He's proven he can catch the ball. He's throwing the ball fairly well. The guy's a competitor. If you're talking about the value that he brings to the team, I think it's invaluable. Add to that the leadership component. Again, I don't have a vote, but I know how valuable he is to us and what we've done and what we're continuing to do. I can't say enough great things about that young man. Whatever honor he gets, he deserves. Very, very deserving and he'll be the first one to tell you, he doesn't get to this point, and he's not recognized as an individual without a team and the guys that block for him and the guys that catch the ball. He's such a team guy and I understand the individual awards show up in football. I'm so grateful for the group awards, the team awards, but in terms of Devon, he deserves everything good that comes his way.
On which players have grown the most this season…
It's hard not to say your offensive line. The amount of guys that we've rotated through there.
Solatoa Moea'i
, I'm proud of him and the reps that he got coming in for Roger when Roger was cramping. Roger, Keith, all these guys have made tremendous progress. Zereoue, I mean, no one was talking about
Zereoue Williams
before the season started and now all of a sudden, he's a projected first-round draft pick. That's pretty awesome and it's a credit also to
Jordan Gross
and his teaching and getting those guys prepared each week and challenging them to get better.
Daniel Bray
, obviously. You've seen the progress there. I'm sure if I sat and watched a ton of film individually, I could say I had a bunch of guys, but I'm very pleased with everyone's commitment to finding ways to add value every week and it seems, again, this week it was another one where it's like, OK, who's going to show up? Wayshawn's down,
Daniel Bray
steps up.
Daniel Bray
, fantastic. Each week it seems like there's someone different that steps up and that is a credit to the depth that we've created.
