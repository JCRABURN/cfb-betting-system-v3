Coastal Carolina at James Madison Kickoff Time Set for Nov. 28
11/16/2026 3:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
CONWAY, S.C. –
Coastal Carolina will close out the regular season at James Madison on Saturday, Nov. 28, with kickoff set for 2 p.m. ET at Bridgeforth Stadium in Harrisonburg, Va. The Sun Belt Conference matchup will air on ESPN+.
James Madison leads the all-time series, 5-1. Coastal earned its lone win in the first meeting, a 31-27 victory in Conway in 2005.
Coastal travels to Louisiana on Saturday, Nov. 21, before heading to James Madison. Kickoff in Lafayette, La., is set for 3 p.m. ET (2 p.m. CT) on ESPN+.
Fans can also listen to the game on the Chanticleer Sports Network.
For complete coverage of Coastal Carolina football, visit GoCCUsports.com and follow @CoastalFootball on social media.
