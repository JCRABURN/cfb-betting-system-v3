2026 Bulldogs in the NFL: Week 10
11/17/2026 1:18:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
By:
N'Gai Washington | Strategic Communications Student Intern
FRESNO, Calif.-
Ten weeks in, former Bulldogs still giving it a perfect 10 in the NFL.
DaRon Bland, Dallas Cowboys
Bland had two tackles in the game versus the San Francisco 49ers, but sustained a high-ankle sprain in Sunday's game.
Week 10 Result:
San Francisco 49ers 46, Dallas Cowboys 40
Week 11 Game:
Sunday (11/22) vs Tennessee Titans (1-8)
Ronnie Rivers, Los Angeles Rams
Rivers had 15 carries for 81 yards in his return game from injury against the Arizona Cardinals. The Rams ended their victory with a 17-play drive that took the final 12:15 of the game and saw the first 15 plays as carries by Rivers. He became the first player with 15+ touches on a single drive over the last 25 seasons.
Week 10 Result:
Los Angeles Rams 34, Arizona Cardinals 6
Week 11 Game:
Wednesday (11/25) vs Green Bay Packers (6-4)
Nikko Remigio, Houston Texans
Remigio was waived by the Chiefs, and the Houston Texans claimed him off waivers on November 12, 2026. Remigio made his regular-season debut for the Texans on Sunday, against the Cleveland Browns, recording four kickoff returns for 114 yards and two punt returns for 29 yards.
Week 10 Result
: Cleveland Browns 27, Houston Texans 24
Week 11 Game
:  Thursday (11/19) vs Indianapolis Colts (3-7)
Jacob Spomer
, Los Angeles Chargers
Spomer was active but did not take any snaps.
Week 10 Result:
Los Angeles Chargers 23, Baltimore Ravens 20
Week 11 Game:
Sunday (11/22) vs New York Jets (2-8)
Evan Williams, Green Bay Packers
Williams had eight tackles and a pass breakup. Williams, according to Pro Football Focus, is rated the top safely in the NFL.
Week 10 Result:
Minnesota Vikings 30, Green Bay Packers 10
Week 11 Game:
Wednesday(11/25) @ Los Angeles Rams (6-4)
