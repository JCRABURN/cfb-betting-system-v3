Kick-Off and TV Info Announced for Wyoming Game
11/16/2026 3:30:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
STORRS, CT
- The kick-off time and TV broadcast information for UConn at Wyoming on Saturday, November 28 was announced on Monday.  The Huskies and Cowboys will kick-off at 12:00 pm EST with the game being broadcast on FS1.
The regular season finale between UConn and Wyoming is the second all-time meeting between the two schools. The Cowboys traveled to Pratt & Whitney Stadium at Rentschler Field in 2021 and posted a 24-21 win.
