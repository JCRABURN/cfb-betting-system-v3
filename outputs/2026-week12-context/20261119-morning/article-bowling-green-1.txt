TOLEDO, Ohio. -
The Bowling Green State University football team will be on the road for the final time this season on Friday (Nov. 20), taking on the rival Toledo Rockets in the Battle of I-75 presented by Fremont Federal Credit Union. Bowling Green and Toledo are tied all-time in the history of the rivalry, dating back to 1919. The game between BGSU and UT is set for a 7:30 p.m. on ESPN2.
Bowling Green vs. Toledo
Bowling Green and Toledo will be meeting for the 91st time in program history with the all-time series tied at 43-43-4.
The BGSU-UT Rivalry dates back to the 1919 season with the two teams now playing for the Battle of I-75 trophy.
In addition to the football trophy, the two teams compete for the Battle of I-75 presented by Fremont Federal Credit Union, a trophy that spans all sports between Bowling Green and Toledo.
The Battle of I-75, Enough Said
Bowling Green State University hits the road for the final time during the regular season, heading north to face the rival Toledo Rockets.
BGSU and Toledo will be playing the rivalry on a Friday night, the first Friday game for BGSU since hosting Miami (OH) on Nov. 29, 2024.
As for the road, it'll mark the Falcons' first road Friday game since playing at Buffalo on Nov. 29, 2019.
Five Top Storylines
The Falcons have won three of the last four meetings against the Rockets, including the last two games played at the Glass Bowl. Among the wins for the Falcons is last year's 28-23 victory when BGSU tallied 28 unanswered points for the win. Over the last four meetings, three have been decided by a touchdown or less.
Myles Bradley
enters the game for the Falcons with 14 sacks this season, the most of any player in the nation and second in the country for sacks per game, averaging 1.40. Bradley is currently second in program history for single-season sacks.
In the last game for the Falcons,
Nakai Amachree
totaled 197 yards of offense for the Falcons, working for 110 on the ground while adding 87 through the air. Amachree's rushing attack marked the first 100-yard rushing game of the season for BGSU. Amachree added a 70-yard receiving touchdown as Bowling Green's first score of the game and a 48-yard rushing touchdown as Bowling Green's final score of the game.
Austin Novosad
threw for three scores against Kent State, becoming the first Falcon since 2019 to throw three touchdowns in his first BGSU start. Novosad and
Jay Kastantin
have both logged three-score games, the first time since 2017 BGSU has had two QBs do so in the same season.
After beating Toledo last season,
Eddie George
is looking to become the fifth BGSU coach to begin a tenure 2-0 or better against the Rockets (Doyt Perry, 10-0, 1955-1964; Gary Blackney, 4-0, 1991-94; Bob Gibson, 2-0, 1965-66; Denny Stolz, 2-0, 1977-78).
Bradley Closing In On Program, MAC Records
Against South Florida,
Myles Bradley
recorded 4.5 sacks for the Falcons, marking what was believed to be a program record and tied for seventh in MAC history at the time.
Bradley bested that number against Kent State with five sacks against the Golden Flashes, another new program record while being tied for the third most in MAC history.
The total against USF marked the most by a Falcon since Chris Jones had 3.5 against Central Michigan on Oct. 30, 2010. Bradley's sacks aided in the Falcons registering six over the course of the game, the most for BGSU in a game since logging six against Central Michigan on Oct. 22, 2022.
This year, Bradley is averaging 1.40 sacks per game, ranking first in the MAC and second in the nation. As for total sacks, Bradley's 14 lead the nation.
For his BGSU career, Bradley now has 20 sacks, tied for sixth in program history with Brandon Hicks (1998-01). The next step for Bradley will be Devon Parks' 22 sacks from 2003-06 that holds fifth. The BGSU all-time career record is held by Kevin O'Brien with 31 from 1989-92.
As for season totals, Bradley has 14 sacks this year, second in program history for single-season sacks and trailing only Kevin O'Brien's 19 from the 1992 campaign, which also stands as the MAC's record.
Making Amachree Of The Defense
With
Austyn Dendy
out against Western Michigan,
Nakai Amachree
led the Bowling Green backfield, rushing for 61 yards on 11 carries, leading the team in both categories. Amachree added three receptions for 17 yards as well.
The outing against Western Michigan was just a taste of what was to come. Against Kent State, Amachree totaled 197 yards of offense with 110 on the ground and 87 through the air, scoring on both a rushing and a receiving touchdown.
Amachree's 110 rushing yards against the Golden Flashes marked the first 100-yard rushing game by a Falcon this season, doing so on 19 carries, the most by a Falcon this year as well.
As for his scores against Kent State, Amachree broke off a 70-yard receiving touchdown on BGSU's first offensive drive and later added a 48-yard rushing score, the longest rush of the season for the Orange and Brown.
For the season, Amachree is leading the Falcons in all-purpose yards, averaging 82.9 per game, while ranking third in total offense, just two yards shy of
Austyn Dendy
.
Leave It To Cleaver
In the game against Kent State,
Blane Cleaver
came down with one of the greatest catches you'll see at any level. In the corner of the end zone, he was able to snag a bullseye throw from
Austin Novosad
while tapping both toes for the score.
Cleaver's score earned the top play on Sportscenter's Top 10 Plays, giving Bowling Green back-to-back days with a top play after men's basketball's Tristan Beckford earned a spot the day prior with a dunk.
A captain for the Falcons, Cleaver's highlight play marked just his fourth reception of the season and his first touchdown of the year. Cleaver has been a key piece in BGSU's blocking scheme, appearing in all 10 games for the Falcons this year with four starts.
Novosad Makes First BGSU Start
Against Kent State,
Austin Novosad
made his first collegiate start, stepping into the quarterback role for the Falcons.
In the game, Novosad completed 60-percent of his passes, going 15-for-25, to total 229 yards while throwing for three touchdowns.
Novosad is the first Falcon to throw for three touchdowns in his first start for the Falcons since Darius Wade threw for three against Morgan State on Aug. 29, 2019.
Both Novosad and
Jay Kastantin
have now logged games this season with three passing touchdowns, the first time BGSU has had two different quarterbacks do so in the same season since Jarret Doege and James Morgan during the 2017 campaign.
