Duff Jr. Named Semifinalist for Biletnikoff Award
Nov 16
|
Football
Share:
PISCATAWAY, N.J.
– Rutgers football WR
KJ Duff
is one of 13 semifinalists for the Biletnikoff Award, the Tallahassee Quarterback Club Foundation, Inc. announced Monday. The Biletnikoff Award annually recognizes the college football season's outstanding FBS receiver, regardless of position.
Duff Jr. recorded 45 receptions for 774 yards and nine touchdowns in eight games for the Scarlet Knights in 2026. That included three games with multiple scores. He currently ranks eighth nationally in receiving touchdowns and 10th with 96.8 receiving yards per game.
Following a two-touchdown performance in the win at Maryland, Duff Jr. earned Big Ten Offensive Player of the Week on Oct. 19. The New York native matched his career high with nine receptions in totaling 113 receiving yards in the road victory, bringing in the No. 1 play of the day by
SportsCenter
with a one-handed grab in the corner of the end zone.
Duff Jr., previously selected to the
Associated Press
Midseason All-America Second Team and a team captain, ranks fourth in program history in career receiving yards (2,283), tied for fifth in receiving touchdowns (17) and tied for 10th in receptions (132).
