Quick Hits
UConn will close out their 2026 home schedule this Saturday afternoon playing host to the Old Dominion Monarchs on Senior Day. Kick-off is slated for 3:32 pm on CBS Sports Network.
This will be the first-ever meeting between UConn and Old Dominion. The Huskies match-up with a team from the Sun Belt Conference for the second-straight week and third time on the season. The Huskies own a road win at Southern Miss and are coming off a 46-22 win at home over James Madison last week. UConn is 3-3 all-time against Sun Belt Conference teams.
A win on Saturday over the Monarchs will make the Huskies bowl eligible for the third-straight season and for the fourth time in the last five years. This would be the second-longest streak in UConn's FBS history of bowl eligibility. The Huskies had a stretch of four-straight bowl appearances from 2007 through 2010.
The Huskies snapped a two-game losing skid with a lopsided 46-22 win over James Madison last Saturday.
Kalieb Osborne
had 362 yards of total offense, completing 16-of-20 for 274 yards and rushing for 88 yards in the win. Osborne tossed three touchdowns on the day. Running back
Cyncir Bowers
rumbled to 123 yards and two scores while catching a third touchdown and receiver
Shamar Porter
hauled in four passes for 135 yards and a score, all career-highs.
On the defense, linebacker
Hayden Pegg
continued to be a play maker for the Huskies with 11 tackles, a sack and an interception against JMU. Pegg had double digit tackles for the third time this season and lead the team with 79 stops..
Scouting the Monarchs...
The Old Dominion Monarchs come to town off their bye week and at 3-7 overall and 2-5 in the Sun Belt. ODU is coming off a road win at Coastal Carolina last time they took the field, 21-8. The Monarchs are one of the lowest scoring teams in the NCAA (16.8 ppg) but sport one of the top-defenses, ranking fourth in the SBC and 43rd in the nation in total defense (339.0 ypg). ODU has been tough against the run, allowing 131.4 yards per game, ranking 47th. They are also averaging 2.5 sacks per game (37th) and forced 19 turnovers this year, ninth best in the nation. Defensive back Brandon Crutchfield has four interceptions on the season, leading the Sun Belt and eighth in the country. Defensive end Javen Gibson has 11.0 tackles for loss including 5.0 sacks to lead the team. On offense, quarterback Quinn Henicle has been a dual threat this season, throwing for 701 yards and four scores while running 352 yards and a team-best five touchdowns.
UConn vs. the Sun Belt...
UConn is 3-3 when matched-up against the Sun Belt Conference. The Huskies are 2-0 this season with wins over James Madison and on the road at Southern Miss. UConn snapped an 0-3 streak against the league in 2024 with a 34-27 win at home over Georgia State and has now won three-straight. UConn was 0-2 in 2023, losing at Georgia State, 35-17, and at James Madison, 44-6.  The first meeting came in the 2022 Myrtle Beach Bowl, a 28-14 loss to Marshall.  The Huskies are 0-2 all-time against Marshall with the previous loss coming when the Thundering Herd was a member of Conference-USA. UConn is 2-5 against JMU all-time and 0-1 against Georgia Southern, dating back to the FCS years.
