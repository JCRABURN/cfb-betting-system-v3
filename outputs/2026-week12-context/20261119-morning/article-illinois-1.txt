Collin Dixon Named One of 13 Biletnikoff Semifinalists
November 16, 2026
|
Football
Share:
News
CHAMPAIGN, Ill. —
Illinois wide receiver
Collin Dixon
was named one of 13 semifinalists for the prestigious Biletnikoff Award, the Tallahassee Quarterback Club Foundation announced Monday. The Biletnikoff Award annually recognizes college football's best receiver.
Dixon has been one of the top-producing receivers in the nation through 10 games with 72 receptions for 946 yards and 13 receiving touchdowns. He ranks third in the nation in receiving touchdowns, eighth in receptions, and 10th in receiving yards, and is one of only five players in the nation to rank in the top 10 all three categories, along with fellow Biletnikoff semifinalists Jesse Legree (Oregon State), Easton Messer (FAU), Jeremiah Smith (Ohio State), and Malachi Toney (Miami).
Dixon ranks second in the Big Ten in receiving touchdowns and receptions, behind only Ohio State's Smith, and ranks third in the conference in receiving yards, as one of the top candidates for the Big Ten's Richter–Howard Receiver of the Year Award. He is one of our four Big Ten receivers on the Biletnikoff semifinalists list, along with Smith, Charlie Becker (Indiana), and KJ Duff (Rutgers).
Dixon broke both Illinois' single season receiving touchdowns record (13) and Illinois' Gies Memorial Stadium record for single-season receiving touchdowns (11), and is on the verge of Illinois' 10th 1,000-yard receiving season in program history. With two regular seasons games remaining and a postseason appearance, Dixon is 29 receptions and 341 yards away from reaching Illinois' single-season records in all three major receiving categories.
The Biletnikoff Award will name three finalists on Nov. 24. A final vote by the highly distinguished Biletnikoff Award National Selection Committee — a group of 650 prominent college football journalists, commentators, announcers, Biletnikoff Award winners, and other former receivers — will determine this year's Biletnikoff Award winner, which will be announced on Dec. 11 on ESPN at the Home Depot College Football Awards Show.
Any player, regardless of position (wide receiver, tight end, slot back, and running back) who catches a pass is eligible for the award. As such, the Biletnikoff Award recognizes college football's outstanding receiver, not merely college football's outstanding wide receiver.
Biletnikoff Semifinalists
Charlie Becker, Indiana
Justin  Bowick, Oklahoma State
Collin  Dixon, Illinois
KJ Duff, Rutgers
Reed Harris, Arizona State
Jackson Harris, LSU
Umari Hatcher, Syracuse
Carlos Hernandez, Wake Forest
Cayden Lee, Missouri
Jesse Legree, Oregon State
Easton Messer, FAU
Jeremiah Smith, Ohio State
Malachi Toney, Miami
