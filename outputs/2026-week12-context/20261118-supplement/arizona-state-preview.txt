November 17, 2026
TEMPE —
Sun Devil Football hosts Oklahoma State for Homecoming and
Senior Night on Saturday, Nov. 21 at Mountain America Stadium. Kickoff is set
for 8:15 p.m. Arizona time on ESPN.
Arizona State and Oklahoma State are both 6-4 overall and 4-3 in the Big
12. ASU won 26-13 at UCF. Oklahoma State fell 52-27 to No. 12 Texas Tech in
Stillwater.
The Sun Devils secured bowl eligibility for the third straight season, their
longest such run since 2017-19. They have won two straight games for the
first time this season and three straight in Tempe.
The Cowboys
The series is tied 3-3. ASU won 42-21 in Stillwater in 2024, the teams’ most
recent meeting. Oklahoma State won the last meeting in Tempe, 27-15, in
2023.
Oklahoma State is in its first season under Eric Morris. The Cowboys won
four of their first five games and scored 66 points in their most recent road game, a win at Kansas State.
Drew Mestemaker has completed 233 of 351 passes for 3,239 yards and
30 touchdowns. The Cowboys average 334.9 passing yards and 191.8 rushing yards per game.
Oklahoma State averages 42.2 points and 526.7 yards per game. The
Cowboys allow 29.7 points and 389.8 yards per game, including 153.6 rushing and 236.2 passing.
The Cowboys have converted 21-of-22 field goals and all 47 extra-point
attempts. They have 11 interceptions and four fumble recoveries.
Caleb Hawkins leads Oklahoma State with 1,172 rushing yards. LaDainian Fields has seven interceptions and 13 passes defended.

Reed Harris
and Justin Bowick)
that were named Biletnikoff Award semifinalists and two kickers (
Carson Smith
and Sam Keltner) that were named as Groza Award semifinalists this week.

Schedule odds and ends (mostly odds)
Arizona State’s road schedule features two teams that participated in the College Football Playoff this past season (Texas A&M and Texas Tech) and one that was among the first two teams left out of the CFP (BYU).
With two road games and a bye following the Morgan State tilt, 2026 marks the first time since the 1991 season that ASU will play just a single home game before the month of October (ignoring the unique circumstances of the COVID-altered 2020 season in which ASU didn’t have a home opener until December). The 1982 season was the last time it occurred before it became more commonplace, pre-1975, when the season typically did not start until the second or third week of September.
ASU played the inaugural Union Jack Classic at Wembley Stadium on Sept. 19, in the first college football game played on British soil and defeated Kansas 24-17.
All told, Sun Devil Football will post over 17,000 round-trip miles over the course of the coming season. For perspective, ASU logged about 9,000 air miles during the 2025 campaign.
In fact, ASU will be making two of its longest road trips of all time this season alone. The approximate 5,200 miles between Phoenix and London represents the second-longest trip in history, behind only the 1990 Coca-Cola Classic in Tokyo. The Sun Devils have played seven games all told in Honolulu, Hawai’i (four regular-season games at Hawai’i and three appearances in the Aloha/Hawai’i Bowl, ~2,900 miles). Arizona State took on Miami (FL) in a road tilt in 1997 (~1970 miles), and then ASU’s contest at UCF this season (~1,850 miles) is next on the list.
ASU will be playing games in Orlando and College Station for the first time in program history. ASU previously played Texas A&M in a neutral-site game in Houston in 2015, and both contests in the program’s history against UCF have been played in Tempe.
The Sun Devils will play games on nine consecutive weekends following their Bye Weekend on Sept. 26. That will be the most since ASU closed out 11 straight weekends in the 2009 season. The 2003 season started with 11 consecutive weekends of action prior to a bye week ahead of the Territorial Cup. There were also nine-game stretches in the middle of the 1999 and 2000 seasons.

Arizona State enters Oklahoma State week at 6-4 overall and 4-3 in the Big

consecutive season and won back-to-back games for the first time this year.
ASU hosts the Cowboys for Homecoming and Senior Night.

Reed all about him
Reed Harris
has 987 receiving yards and nine touchdowns on 59 catches
through 10 games. He has matched his touchdown total from 29 games at
Boston College. His 80 yards at UCF pushed him to 2,148 career receiving
yards, tied for 21st among active Division I players after Saturday.
Harris was named a semifinalist for the Biletnikoff Award
, which honors college football’s top receiver. He is the fourth Sun Devil to earn the distinction and the first since
N’Keal Harry
in 2018.
His 987 yards are the fourth-most through 10 games in ASU history. He is 13
yards from 1,000 and 117 from tying Shaun McDonald’s 1,104 in 2001 for 10th on ASU’s single-season list.
Harris leads the Big 12 with 987 receiving yards and 98.7 yards per game and is second with nine touchdowns. He ranks eighth nationally in total receiving yards and ninth in yards per game.
Harris’ six touchdown receptions vs. press coverage are tied for 10th-most in the nation, and his four touchdown receptions against man coverage are tied for ninth (as of Nov. 13).
Harris has 17 catches of at least 20 yards and 25 catches of at least 15 yards,
ranking sixth and seventh nationally, respectively.
Harris’ 86.0 receiving grade is 16th in the nation and second in the Big 12 (min. 60 targets), while his 84.5 overall offensive grade ranks third among Big 12 wide receivers and 23rd among all FBS receivers.
His 43 catches for a first down or touchdown are tied for eighth nationally. He has 357 yards after the catch, which is 23rd nationally.
His 185 yards after contact rank 14th nationally, and his 11 catches on contested targets rank 12th.
ASU quarterbacks have a 118.7 NFL passer rating when targeting Harris this
season, the 26th-best mark nationally.
Cardae Mack
was named a Shaun Alexander National Freshman of the Year semifinalist on Nov. 4, the second Sun Devil to reach that stage since the award began in 2018.
Return of the Mack
Cardae Mack
is the 17th true freshman running back in a power conference since 2016 to rush for more than 150 yards in multiple regular-season games.
The list includes current NFL backs Bijan Robinson, Jonathan Taylor and Braelon Allen, Quinshon Judkins, TreVeyon Henderson, J.K. Dobbins, Justice Hill and AJ Dillon.
Mack was named a
Shaun Alexander National Freshman of the Year semifinalist
on Nov. 4, the second Sun Devil to reach that stage since the award began in 2018.
Mack leads FBS freshmen with 714 rushing yards, ahead of Pitt’s Damon
Ferguson Jr. (694). He averages 5.62 yards per carry.
Mack has 127 carries and is 156 yards shy of tying Keegan Herring’s ASU
freshman rushing record of 870. He has 141 receiving yards, giving him 855 scrimmage yards on 142 touches. He has rushed for 500 yards over his last five games.
Mack has 98 carries for 473 yards and five rushing touchdowns over the last
four games. His 98 carries rank third among FBS true freshmen in any four-
game span since 2021, behind Quinshon Judkins (109, Ole Miss, 2022) and
Caleb Hawkins (105, Iowa, 2025).
Mack’s 85.0 rushing grade this season is 24th in the nation (min. 100
attempts). His 82.9 overall offensive grade is 21st among FBS halfbacks.
Mack is forcing 0.31 missed tackles per rush attempt this season, fourth- most in the country (min. 125 attempts). He has forced 39 missed tackles on
running plays this season (19th nationally) and 46 missed tackles overall (22nd
nationally).
He is averaging 3.3 yards after contact per attempt, good for 25th among FBS running backs.
Mack is earning a first down or touchdown on 33.1% of his carries,
ranking third in the nation among those with at least 125 attempts.
Mack has 21 explosive runs of at least 10 yards on 127 attempts, ranking 22nd in the country. He has the second-fewest carries among the nation’s top 50 players in explosive runs.
Mack earned his third Big 12 Freshman of the Week honor after the UCF win. His 78.7 PFF grade was ninth among FBS running backs for the week and led true freshmen. He forced seven missed tackles and posted an 81.1 pass-blocking grade.
Cardae Diem

touchdown pass against Colorado. He is one of five FBS true freshmen since
2021 to rush for, catch and throw a touchdown in a season.
Mack’s 111-yard game at UCF made him the first ASU true freshman to rush for 100 yards in four games in a season. He is the 14th FBS true freshman since
2021 with at least four 100-yard rushing games in a season.
In two games on the road against Top-10 teams (#10 Texas Tech, #8 BYU),
Cardae Mack

Kalen Ballage

Mack’s 159 rushing yards in the K-State game were the most by a Sun Devil true freshman back since Keegan Herring had 197 yards against Northwestern on Sept. 17, 2005.
Mack became the first Sun Devil freshman to record four rushing touchdowns since Leon Burton had five on Oct. 1, 1955, against San Diego Navy. It was the first time an ASU true freshman accounted for four total touchdowns since
Demario Richard
had four (two rushing, two receiving) in the 2014 Sun Bowl.

2024), Evan Hull (Northwestern, 2019), Jermar Jefferson (Oregon State, twice
in 2018), AJ Dillon (Boston College, 2017), Damarea Crockett (Missouri, 2016)

home games. He also threw his first career touchdown against Colorado, finding
Jake Fette
from one yard out.

Fette Wap

Fette has completed 61 of 106 passes for 623 yards and three touchdowns
this season. In his first three starts, he went 57-of-98 for 565 yards with no
interceptions, becoming the first Sun Devil since
Jayden Daniels
in 2019 to
go interception-free in his first three career starts.
Fette can become the first ASU true freshman quarterback since Jayden
Daniels in 2019 to win three consecutive starts.

Smith has made at least one field goal in eight of 10 games and multiple field
goals in five. He has made four field goals twice this season, two of the 15 such performances in program history.

Hold on to your butts
ASU is 11-3 (.786) in games decided by seven points or fewer since the start
of 2024. The 11 wins are tied for fourth-most nationally. The most recent was a 27-20 loss at BYU.
The loss at BYU snapped ASU’s nine-game winning streak in Big 12 games
decided by seven points or fewer.
ASU’s .786 winning percentage in games decided by seven points or fewer ranks seventh among FBS teams with at least five such games.
ASU’s 10 such victories in 2024-25 were tied for the most over a two-season
span in Big 12 history and tied for eighth-most for an FBS team since 1996 (as of Nov. 13).
Since
Kenny Dillingham
took over in 2023, 16 of ASU’s 28 victories have
come by 10 points or fewer, including two this season.
Last season, 30 Big 12 games were decided by eight points or fewer. ASU went 5-1 in those games. Thirteen were decided by three points or fewer, and the Sun Devils went 3-0, beating Baylor and TCU 27-24 and also defeating West Virginia.
The Sun Devils have won eight games in the past two-plus seasons in which they were trailing or tied at some point in the fourth quarter.
Since the start of 2024, ASU has scored on 19 of 40 non-garbage-time drives when trailing in the fourth quarter. Its 47.5% scoring rate ranks
14th nationally.
Last season, ASU scored 51 non-garbage-time points when trailing in the
fourth quarter, 15th-most in the FBS. The Sun Devils scored on nine of 19 drives in those situations. The nine scoring drives tied for eighth in the FBS, and the 47.4% rate ranked 35th.
Opponents are averaging just 4.3 yards per play when ASU is trailing in the
fourth quarter in non-garbage time over the last two-plus seasons - the 14th lowest tally in the FBS.
Since the start of 2024, opponents have gained a first down or scored a
touchdown on 35 of 145 non-garbage-time plays when ASU trailed in the
fourth quarter. The 24.1% rate is the 26th-lowest in the FBS.
Four opponents have added to their leads over ASU by eight points in the fourth quarter since the start of 2024. They were Texas Tech in 2024,
Arizona in 2025, and Texas A&M and BYU in 2026.
With a fourth-quarter lead of 10 points or fewer since the start of 2024,
ASU has allowed 3.8 yards per play, 23rd-lowest nationally. Opponents have gained a first down or scored a touchdown on 24 of 105 plays, a 22.9% rate that ranks 33rd-lowest.
Setting the tone

Mountain America Stadium, Home of the ASU Sun Devils
Arizona State University and Sun Devil Athletics announced a multi-year naming rights partnership in 2023 with Mountain America Credit Union to form one of the most dynamic naming rights deals in college athletics.
The 15-year partnership with Sun Devil Athletics, the most important deal in athletics department history, includes ASU’s football stadium, which will now be called Mountain America Stadium, Home of the ASU Sun Devils.
Both Mountain America and ASU are committed to giving back to the community, and this partnership will provide support to the community and student-athletes in a variety of ways:
Football Field Goals:
Mountain America will donate $250 to the Sun Devil Club for every field goal made to enhance ASU’s student-athlete experience.
Shoe Donation Program:
ASU and Mountain America will develop a donation program to provide brand-new shoes to community members in need.
Scholarships:
Each year, Mountain America will provide $20,000 to ASU student-athletes, with $10,000 for women’s sports and $10,000 for men’s sports.
Financial Education Programs:
Mountain America will offer specialized financial education programs for student-athletes, including personal coaching sessions.
Internship Program:
Mountain America will also offer an internship program to help student-athletes develop the skills and experience needed for future endeavors.
