Memphis-Navy to Air on ESPN2
Nov 15, 2026
|
Football
Share:
IRVING, Texas –
The University of Memphis football team's matchup on Saturday, Nov. 21 at Navy will air on ESPN2, the American confirmed Sunday morning.
The matchup, already set for an 11 a.m. CT start, features a Memphis (8-2, 5-1 American) program now in a three-way tie for first place in the American Conference standings, and a Navy (3-6, 2-4 American) program that has gotten the best of Memphis in each of the past two seasons, with the most recent game in Annapolis ending 56-44 in favor of the Midshipmen.
Memphis goes into the game boasting one of the nation's top offenses, currently ranking third in total offense (520.1), with the 12
th
-ranked passing attack (328.9) eighth-best rushing offense (227.3) and scoring offense (39.0). On the other side of the ball, the Tigers also rank 20
th
nationally in sacks per game (2.80) and have the 10
th
-best red zone defense (.722).
Despite its record, Navy has kept its identity with the third-best rushing attack (298.1) in the nation, while having the seventh-highest average time of possession (33:07). That offense is led by Jackson Gutierrez, who has the eighth-most rushing yards in the nation and third-most rushing and total touchdowns on the season.
