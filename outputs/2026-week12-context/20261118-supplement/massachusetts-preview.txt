AMHERST, Mass. --
Massachusetts football hosts Akron on Wednesday, November 11, for Senior Night and Fan Appreciation at 7 p.m. at McGuirk Alumni Stadium in Amherst, Massachusetts. The game will be televised on CBS Sports Network.
SERIES HISTORY
UMass will be playing Akron for the seventh time in series history, with the Zips leading 4-2.
UMass and Akron also played in 2013 (MAC), 2014 (Mac), and 2015 (MAC).
During that inaugural game of the series, the Minutemen defense had four interceptions, and the win was also UMass' first win as an FBS program.
The first matchup of the two teams occurred on Nov. 10, 2012, when UMass posted a 22-14 win.
The UMass defense also came away with two interceptions, one by Cole McCubrey and a pick six by current Minnesota Viking Isaiah Rodgers.
The Minutemen offense was led by the running back trio of Bilal Ally (97 yards), Michael Curtis (69 yards) and Kevin Brown Jr (50 yards) who totaled 216 yards on the ground.
The most recent UMass victory came on Sept. 28, 2019, when the Minutemen bested Akron 37-29.
Kicker
Derek Morris
also drilled a then-UMass record 55-yard field goal in that game.
The lone Minutemen touchdown was caught by offensive lineman
Magnus Von Saldern
, who was playing tight end at the time.
Last season, UMass fell to the Zips by a score of 44-10.
The Minutemen are 1-2 against the Zips when playing at home.
QUICK HITTERS
The Minutemen rank third in the MAC and 42nd nationally in total defense (337.9 yards against per game); UMass is also giving up 22.30 points per game to rank 50th in FBS.
Starting cornerback
Isaiah Reed
ranks second in the MAC and 12th nationally in passes defended per game (1.11).
The Minutemen are also averaging 17.30 yards per punt return to rank first in the MAC and ninth nationally.
Edward Phillipson
is averaging 45.60 yards per punt which ranks first in the MAC and 14th nationally.
Derek Morris
enters week 11 as one of seven FBS kickers with at least four field goals of 50 yards or more this season: Blake Craig of Missouri (5), David Olano of Texas A&M (4), Manaki Watanabe of Fresno State (4),
Derek Morris
(4),
Brock Taylor
of Vanderbilt (4), Nolan Hauser of Clemson (4) and Nico Cavanillas Alti of Coastal Carolina (4).
Pop Watson III, who was forced to miss the Miami (OH) and Buffalo games and most of the Toledo game with lower body injuries, has a .689 completion percentage on the season to rank first in the MAC and 14th nationally.
Last season, nine FBS players accomplished the feat over the entire 2025 season, including Paul Hornung Award winner KC Concepcion (Texas A&M).
Entering week 12,
T.Y. Harding
is one of only two FBS players with a receiving touchdown, a rushing touchdown and a punt return touchdown this season (Florida Atlantic wide receiver Easton Messer is the other).
The Minutemen have thrown four interceptions on the season, the second-lowest total in the MAC (ranks 21st nationally).
UMass is giving up 1.50 sacks per game to rank second in the MAC and tied for 37th nationally.
THE BALL
The Minutemen have 13 gained turnovers this season on seven interceptions and six fumble recoveries.
Massachusetts leads the MAC with a +.40 average turnover margin.
