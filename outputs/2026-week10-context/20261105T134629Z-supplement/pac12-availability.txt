2026 Pac-12 Football: Player Availability Reports
9/12/2026 12:00:00 AM
Story Links
To promote the integrity of competition, protect the well-being and privacy of student-athletes and institutional staff, and provide consistent public transparency, the Pac-12 will provide player availability reports in football for the 2026 season. For all conference matchups, the league will provide reports in coordination with its member institutions for public release on Pac-12.com.
Timing for report submissions by Pac-12 institutions will include the following, to follow shortly on Pac-12.com:
Three days prior to the scheduled game by 7 p.m. PT.
Two days prior to the scheduled game by 7 p.m. PT.
One day prior to the scheduled game by 7 p.m. PT.
No later than two hours prior to publicized game time on game day.
In addition to conference matchups, the Pac-12 will also participate in player availability reporting for non-conference matchups only when the opponent and opponent’s conference participate for non-conference matchups. Timing for report submissions will adjust in accordance with participating non-conference opponents as applicable.
