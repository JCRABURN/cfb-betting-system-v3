BOISE, Idaho (AP) --
Max Cutforth
threw three touchdown passes in the final seven minutes in relief of injured starter
Maddux Madsen
, and No. 20 Boise State rallied to beat Texas State 46-45 on Saturday night after blowing a 25-point halftime lead.
Boise State (7-1, 4-0 Pac-12) needed a furious comeback after the Bobcats scored the first 36 points of the second half, taking a 39-28 lead with 8:58 left when
Jayden Bradley
returned Madsen's first interception of the season for a touchdown.
Texas State (4-4, 2-2) was poised to pull the upset behind
Brad Jackson
, the quarterback who threw for 327 yards and four TDs.
But in a game that saw three lead changes in the final five minutes, Cutforth found
Akeem Wright
for an 18-yard scoring strike with 2:40 remaining to put Boise State ahead for good.
Madsen, who threw for 236 yards and a touchdown and ran for two more scores, left int he fourth quarter after taking a hard shot near his left knee. He watched the rest of the game on crutches from the sideline.
Chris Dawn, Jr.
, led the wide receiver room with five catches for 71 yards and a touchdown, while Jackson's TDs went to three additional receivers in
Beau Sparks
,
Ty Stamey
and
Zechariah Sample
. On the ground, Jackson ran for 54 yards and a score. Defensively,
Michael Patterson III
had a game-high 10 tackles and a forced fumble, while Bradley added nine tackles and a half TFL to his 20 yard interception return TD.
