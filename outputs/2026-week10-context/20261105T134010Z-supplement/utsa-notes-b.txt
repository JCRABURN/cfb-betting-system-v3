OFFENSIVE PLAYER OF THE WEEK
Marcus Stokes • Jr. • QB • Memphis
Marcus Stokes was fantastic in Memphis’ 49-22 win over Army Saturday, accounting for a total of five touchdowns.
Stokes completed 29 of 35 passes for 371 yards and four touchdowns while also rushing seven times for 30 yards and
adding another score on the ground.
DEFENSIVE PLAYER OF THE WEEK
CJ Hicks • Gr. • DE • South Florida
CJ Hicks tied a school record and became the first American Conference player since 2024 to register at least four sacks
in a single game in South Florida’s 30-10 win over UAB. Hicks had five total tackles, 4.0 sacks and a pass deflection in
the win over UAB.
SPECIAL TEAMS PLAYER OF THE WEEK
TJ Smith • Sr. • DB • Tulane
TJ Smith returned a pair of kickoffs for an average of 63 yards including a 95-yard return for a touchdown to help lead
Tulane to a comeback 38-31 overtime win over Charlotte. It was the longest kickoff return by a Tulane player since 2024.

AMERICAN NOTEBOOK
Competitive Balance
The American has had 18 of 30 American conference
games (60%) decided by one score and four have went to
overtime, the most OT games of any league in the country.
Eyes on West Point
For the first time in history, FOX Big Noon Kickoff will travel
to an American Conference school as they travel to West
Point for the Nov. 7 contest between the Black Knights
and Air Force. In addition, FOX NFL Sunday will also be in
West Point prior to the NFL slate on Nov. 8.
Going Bowling
Four American teams have reached bowl eligibility in UTSA,
Memphis, UNT and South Florida while Army and Florida
Atlantic are both one win away. The American has the
most bowl eligible teams of any non-A4 conference. The
league set a record in 2025 with nine bowl eligible teams.
Roadrunning Among the Elite
UTSA is one of just 14 FBS teams with a 7-1 record or
better. Among those teams, the Roadrunners are one
of seven programs who’s lone loss is to a top-10 team in
the AP Poll along with Miami, UCLA, Utah, Kentucky, Notre
Dame and Wake Forest.
The Nation’s Leading Rusher
UNT RB Jahiem White leads the nation in rushing with
1,054 yards on the ground. White has scored 13 rushing
touchdowns and is also third in FBS with 7.42 yards per
carry and is also first in the country in all-purpose yards.
Offensive Juggernauts
The American has two of the top five teams in FBS in total
offense as UNT leads the country averaging 540.5 total
YPG while Memphis is fifth averaging 513.3 YPG.
Run the Rock
Four teams from the American rank in the top-10 in FBS in
rushing offense.  Army leads the country with 342.5 yards
per game, while Navy is third at 295.8 YPG.  Rice sits just
behind in fourth averaging 271.5 YPG while Memphis is in
the 10th spot with 222.8 YPG.

Elite Rushers
The American has five of the top-20 rushers in the country
in yards per game average. UNT’s Jahiem White (1st,
131.8), Navy’s Jackson Gutierrez (4th, 122.4),  Army’s Cale
Hellums (12th, 109.3), Memphis’ Jaylin Carter (15th, 103.2),
and Rice’s Jacurri Brown (19th, 95.6) are five of just 19
FBS players averaging over 95 rushing yards per game.
The Kick is Good
UAB’s senior kicker Jonah Delange is currently third in
the nation in made field goals.  He is 18-for-22 on the
season. His 48 made career field goals ranks ninth in
conference history.
SportsCenter on Campus
The American has had a number of programs featured
on SportsCenter prior to Thursday night contests in the
last couple of years and the trend continues and grows
in 2026. The following games have had SportsCenter
featured on member campuses with more still to come:
 Oct. 1 UNT at Tulsa
 Oct. 8 South Florida at UTSA
 Oct. 15 East Carolina at UAB
 Oct. 22 East Carolina at Memphis
 Oct. 29 Florida Atlantic at UNT
 Nov. 5 UTSA at Florida Atlantic
Thursday Night Lights
American Conference Thursday night games on ESPN have
delivered high-level excitement week-after-week. Of the
five games on Thursday night in the league, four have
been decided by one socre, three have gone to overtime
and the average margin of victory in those contests is
just seven points.
There’s No Place Like Home
Week nine of American Conference action saw home
teams finish 4-1 in conference games. It was in stark
contrast to week five in which home teams went 0-4. In
total, home teams are 18-12 in league games this season.
Solo Tackle Machines
The American has four of the top six players in FBS in
solo tackles, including each of the top two. Tulane’s Kevin
Adams III leads the nation with 53 solo stops, UAB’s
Muazz Byard is second with 47, Temple’s Curly Ordonez
sits fourth with 46 and UAB’s Jo Pierce is sixth with 44.
Hicks Breaking Records
South Florida defensive end CJ Hicks tied a school record
and became the first American Conference player since
2024 to register at least four sacks in a single game in
South Florida’s 30-10 win over UAB. Hicks had five total
tackles, 4.0 sacks and a PBU in the win over UAB.
No Fly Zone
UAB has the top passing defense in the nation this season
allowing just 116.0 total passing YPG.
Long Distance
Prior to the season, American Conference kickers had
made just three field goals all-time of 57+ yards. This
season, the league has already produced three field
goals of 57+ yards.

Home Cooking
Navy has won 79-consecutive games at home when
leading after three quarters, the nation’s longest streak.
Putting Up Points
Memphis has scored at least 15 points in 90-consecutive
home games. It is the second-longest active streak in
the FBS and the fourth-longest streak overall since 1978.
61 Initiative Partnership
The American Conference has partnered with the Shawn
Clark Legacy Foundation and its 61 Initiative to prioritize
coach physical and mental health.

Foundation rep Jonelle Clark addressed American head
coaches at the 2026 American Kickoff in St. Petersburg,
Florida, encouraging coaches to dedicate one extra
minute daily to personal well-being. The conference
has formally adopted the initiative across all football
programs.
The Streak Lives
Memphis became bowl eligible for the 13th straight
season with a 41-34 OT win over East Carolina. The 13
straight bowl games is tied for the sixth-longest active
streak in FBS and the longest among non-A4 teams.
American Tailgate Tour
The American Tailgate Tour is a gameday activation from
the American that will bring a conference-branded tailgate
destination to select football matchups beginning in
2026. The Tour gives the conference a traveling platform
to reach fans directly, create original social content, and
support sponsors inside the college football gameday
environment. The stops for the Tailgate Tour:
 Sept. 12 Temple vs Penn State
 Nov. 12 South Florida vs Memphis
 Nov. 21 Florida Atlantic vs South Florida
 Dec. 5 American Championship Game
We’re Available
The American will issue player availability reports in
advance of all conference games in the 2026 season.
Reports will be published to the American website and
on social media no later than two hours prior to kickoff
for conference games.
Mean Green Rebuild
After reaching the 2025 American Championship game,
UNT returns zero starters from its runner-up squad. The
Mean Green added key offensive transfers in RB Jaheim
White (West Virginia) and QB Tayven Jackson (UCF).
UNT is one of two FBS teams in the country to return zero
starters from 2025 – the other is Iowa State.

in their first full seasons with their respective programs
in 2026.

Special Teams
Florida Atlantic went back-to-back weeks with a special
teams touchdown.  Easton Messer returned a punt for
a touchdown in week 4, while Leonard Farrow tied an
American record with two kickoff returns for touchdowns .
Streak Extender
Tulsa defeated Oklahoma State 24-10 in Week 1, marking
the first time since 1952 that the Golden Hurricane have
defeated the Cowboys in consecutive games.
Shula Bowl
Florida Atlantic regained the Don Shula Award, defeating
FIU 16-10 in the 24th edition of the Shula Bowl. The Owls
lead the series 18-6 and have won eight of the past nine
games in the rivalry.
Top 25
American Conference teams have played a combined
seven games against AP Top-25 teams, five of which came
against teams ranked inside the top-10.
Ground Attack
Army and Navy both eclipsed 500 rushing yards in the
same day for the first time since 2017 in Week 1 as Army
ran for 569 yards, while Navy totaled 506 rushing yards.
Coach of the Week
Memphis head coach Charles Huff was named the Bear
Bryant National Coach of the Week after leading the
Tigers to a 27-21 comeback win over UNLV in Week 0.
Commander in Chief’s Trophy
Navy will look to claim the Commander in Chief’s Trophy for
the 19th time and the third consecutive season in 2026.
No team has won the trophy more than two years in a row
since the Midshipmen earned that distinction from 2003-
09. Army will look to reclaim the Commander in Chief’s
Trophy for the 11th time and the first season since 2023.
Us Vs. Them
The American was the only conference in the nation to
earn non-conference wins over teams from the Big 12,
Big 10, SEC and ACC during 2025.
Select Company
The American produced three teams with 11 or more wins
in 2025 (Navy, UNT, Tulane). The only other leagues with at
least three 11-win teams were the Big Ten, Big 12, and SEC.
Championship Week
The 2026 American Conference Football Championship
presented by LHH Healthcare takes place Saturday, Dec.
5, in primetime on ABC.

The Bracket
The format of the College Football Playoff is built on a 12-team bracket.
Four automatic bids will be given to the champions of the ACC, Big Ten, Big
12 and SEC, with one automatic bid coming from the highest ranked team
from the remaining conferences. The remaining seven at-large bids will be
given to the best remaining teams from any conference.
• Notre Dame receives an automatic bid if it is ranked in the CFP Top
12 on Selection Day. If so, six (6) at-large bids would be awarded.
• Seeds 1-4 receive first-round byes
• Seeds 5-12 play first-round games at the higher seed’s home sta-
dium
• Any automatic qualifiers ranked outside the top 12 will be placed in
order at the bottom of the seeding.
• The No. 1 seed will select its quarterfinal bowl site and the semifinal
site into which it will advance should it win its quarterfinal game.
• The No. 2 seed, followed by the No. 3 seed, will then select from the
remaining quarterfinal bowl sites. Those two teams will be assigned
to the remaining semifinal.
• The No. 4 seed will be assigned to the remaining quarterfinal bowl
site.
• With the four highest-ranked schools’ bowls selected, their four CFP
Quarterfinal opponents will be dictated by the bracket (i.e., No. 1 vs.
No. 8/9 winner, No. 4 vs. No. 5/12 winner, No. 2 vs. 7/10 winner; No. 3
vs. 6/11 winner.)
• The CFP bracket will follow the selection committee’s rankings, with
no modifications made to avoid rematches between teams that
may have played during the regular season or are from the same
conference.
• The bracket will remain in effect throughout the CFP (i.e., no re-
seeding).
• All 11 games will be under the CFP umbrella with the administrative
policies determined by the CFP.
Selection Committee
A dedicated group of high-integrity football experts, with experience as
coaches, student-athletes, college administrators and journalists, along
with sitting athletics directors, comprise the CFP Selection Committee.
Each fall, the committee meets in person to rank the top 25 teams,
assign the schools to the 12-team playoff bracket and assign the CFP
Quarterfinal and CFP Semifinal bowl sites. Members of the committee are
Troy Dannen, Mark Dantonio, Doug Gillin, Mark Harlan, Ivan Maisel, Gus
Malzahn, Chris Massaro, Randall McDaniel, Mike Riley, Jeff Tedford, Wesley
Walls, Carla Williams, and Hunter Yurachek.

The American Bowl Lineup
American Conference teams are guaranteed at least eight bowl
appearances in 2026 as part of the conference’s postseason lineup,
which began in the 2020 season.
The American champion would play in the College Football Playoff if it
is ranked among the five highest-ranked conference champions by the
CFP selection committee.

Freedom Mortgage Military Bowl in Annapolis, Maryland, and the

Birmingham Bowl, the Cure Bowl, the Gasparilla Bowl, the Boca Raton
Bowl, the Frisco Football Classic, the SERVPRO First Responder Bowl,
the Isleta New Mexico Bowl, the Myrtle Beach Bowl, the Salute to
Veterans Bowl.

2026 season, the American has a secondary agreement with the
