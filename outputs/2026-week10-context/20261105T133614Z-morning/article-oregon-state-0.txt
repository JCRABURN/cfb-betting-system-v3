Temple, Ojeda Lead OSU In Loss At Fresno State
November 01, 2026
|
Football
Box Score
Oregon State Post-Game Notes
Photo Gallery
Share:
By:
Athletic Communications
FRESNO, Calif. —
Decorion Temple caught six passes for 97 yards and
Caleb Ojeda
made all three of his field goals, but Oregon State's five-game win streak ended with a 38-22 loss at Fresno State on Saturday at Valley Children's Stadium.
The Beavers (5-3, 3-1 Pac-12) led 3-0 on Ojeda's 54-yard field goal in the first quarter but trailed from late in the period on.
Temple finished three yards short of 100, but his 97 receiving yards are the most by an Oregon State tight end since Connor Hamlett had 119 against Arizona State on Nov. 16, 2013. It was a career high for Temple, who has 29 catches for 283 yards this season.
Ojeda's 54-yarder is a career long and the longest by a Beaver since Everett Hayes made a 55-yarder on Nov. 23, 2024. He also connected from 29 and 28 yards, giving him 10 field goals this season.
Braden Atkinson
completed 33 of 59 passes for 373 yards and a touchdown without an interception. It was his eighth straight 300-yard game, breaking Sean Mannion's school record of seven in a row from 2013. He has 2,977 yards and 25 touchdown passes this season, tying Derek Anderson (2002 and 2003) for fourth on the single-season list.
Eric Olsen
caught seven passes for 71 yards, including a 9-yard touchdown with 1:45 left, and has caught a pass in 23 straight games.
Jesse Legree
had seven catches for 70 yards.
Fresno State led 21-9 at halftime and 38-16 after three quarters.
Cornell Hatcher Jr.
added a 5-yard touchdown run in the third quarter before Olsen's late score.
Oregon State intercepted Fresno State's Jayden Mandal on the Bulldogs' second play, with
Sailasa Vadrawale III
making the pick, and drove to the Fresno State 4-yard line before turning the ball over on downs.
Fresno State ran for 190 yards, led by Bryson Donelson's 108 on 16 carries, including a 63-yard touchdown. Mandal threw two touchdown passes to Josiah Freeman and ran for two more scores.
Oregon State returns to Reser Stadium to host Texas State on Saturday, Nov. 7. Kickoff is set for 7:30 p.m. PT on The CW.
