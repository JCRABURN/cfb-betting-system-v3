Football: Game Notes vs. Florida State
Head Coach Bill O'Brien Media Availability
Bo MacCormack Media Availability
Carter Davis Media Availability
Share:
Official game notes, depth chart, weekly news conference and more ahead of the Eagles' week-10 match-up against the Seminoles
By:
Athletic Communications
Match-Up:
Boston College (4-4, 2-3 ACC) vs. Florida State (3-5, 1-4 ACC)
Date and Time:
Saturday, Nov. 7 • 12:00 p.m. ET
Location (Stadium):
Chestnut Hill, Mass. (Alumni Stadium)
Watch:
ACC Network
Radio:
WEEI 93.7 FM
GAME NOTES
FIRST-AND-10
» Boston College Football returns to Alumni Stadium to take on Florida State on Saturday, November 7 at 12:00 p.m.
» The game will be televised on ACC Network.
Jorge Sedano
(play-by-play) and
Dave Clawson
(analyst) are on the call with sideline reporter
Annabelle Hasselbeck
.
» Coverage on the Boston College Sports Network begins 30 minutes prior to kickoff on WEEI 93.7 FM.
Jon Meterparel
(play-by-play) is joined on the call by former BC linebacker
Pete Cronan
in the booth and
Scott Mutryn
on the sideline.
EAGLES BY THE NUMBERS
» The Eagles defense has 14 takeaways (11 interceptions, three fumble recoveries) through eight games, which is the most in the ACC.
» Boston College’s 11 interceptions is also the most in the ACC.
»
Charlie Comella’s
four interceptions leads the Eagles in 2026, and is tied for sixth-most nationally.
» Through eight games, Boston College has allowed 188.5 passing yards per game to opposing teams, which ranks third in the ACC.
»
KP Price
leads the ACC in solo tackles (37).
»
Price
reached the 250 career tackle mark at Duke (10/31) after finishing with eight tackles.
»
Anthony Palano
ranks fourth in the ACC in total tackles (69) and solo tackles (34), tying
Marcus Upton
in solo tackles.
»
Palano
led the Eagles in tackles in back-to-back weeks with nine total tackles.
»
Carter Davis
picked off his second pass of 2026 at Duke (10/31).
»
Davis
also added a 100-yard return after a missed PAT, becoming the first Eagle to return a missed PAT for points since 2024.
»
Mason McKenzie
ranks in the top-10 in two rushing categories in the ACC: Total rushing yards (638, 7th overall, best among ACC quarterbacks) and rush yards per game (79.8, 7th, best among ACC quarterbacks).
»
McKenzie
has recorded seven designed runs of 20-plus yards this season, which is the most among ACC quarterbacks and tied for the sixth-most nationally among quarterbacks.
» Averaging 168.1 rushing yards per game, The Eagles own a top-five rushing attack in the ACC (5th).
» After missing the first seven games due to injury, tight end
Kaelan Chudzinski
returned to the starting lineup at Duke (10/31), his third career start and his first start since November 1, 2025 vs. Notre Dame.
»
Chudzinski
finished his true freshman season in 2025 with 24 catches for 313 yards and four touchdowns, which was the best among all freshman tight ends in the country.
