Steven Robinson Named Sun Belt Conference Offensive Player of the Week
11/2/2026 1:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
HATTIESBURG, Miss. –
Southern Miss redshirt junior running back
Steven Robinson
(Baltimore, Md.) earned the Sun Belt Conference Offensive Player of the Week honors Monday after helping lead his Golden Eagles to a 35-21 victory at ULM last Saturday.
Robinson recorded his second 100-yard rushing game in a row and third this season as he raced for a game- and season-high 151 yards on 21 carries.
His 33-yard rushing score in the second quarter opened the scoring for the Golden Eagles and marked his longest TD run of the year, giving him nine rushing scores in 2026. Robinson also added his longest rush in 2026 of 42 yards early in the third quarter to set up the Golden Eagles first score of the second half. He averaged 7.2 yards per rush, and along with
Jamarice Wilder
, helped provide double 100-yard rushing performances for the second-straight game, which is the first time that has happened since 2016.
Because of his exploits, it enabled Southern Miss to rush for over 200 yards for the first time in consecutive games since they did it three-straight times in 2023.
The Golden Eagles now return to action this Thursday at 7 p.m., when they entertain league foe James Madison in a nationally televised game on ESPN2. The game can also be heard on the Southern Miss Sports Network.
Tickets for the game can be purchased as well by going to
SouthernMiss.com/tickets
.
