West Virginia Game Notes
Texas Tech Media Center
LUBBOCK, Texas
– Texas Tech (7-1, 4-1 Big 12) will look to build on its second AP top-25 victory of the season Saturday when it hosts West Virginia (5-3, 2-3 Big 12) for an 8:30 p.m. kickoff on ESPN at Galaxy Stadium. The Red Raiders are coming off a 34-26 victory over then-No. 22 Arizona this past Saturday and will look to earn their eighth win of the season while extending their home winning streak to a program-record 14 consecutive games.
ESPN will televise Saturday's game, with Jay Alter handling play-by-play, Tom Luginbill serving as the analyst and Sherree Burruss reporting from the sideline. The broadcast will also be available through the ESPN App on computers, mobile devices and supported television streaming services.
Texas Tech Sports Network will broadcast the game across more than 20 affiliates throughout Texas and New Mexico, with Brian Jensen providing the call alongside analyst John Harris and sideline reporter Chris Level. The radio broadcast can also be heard on SiriusXM channel 83 and the Varsity app.
SATURDAY NIGHT WEAR BLACK GAME VS. WEST VIRGINIA IS SOLD OUT
Texas Tech announced Saturday night's game versus West Virginia is a sell out, marking the sixth advanced sell out of the season and 13th-consecutive home game sold out dating back to the start of the 2025 season.
Fans are encouraged to utilize
SeatGeek
, the official secondary ticket provider of Texas Tech Athletics for tickets to Saturday's game.
Fans with tickets that are unable to attend Saturday should utilize the
Every Seat Counts
program, which is designed to ensure Galaxy Stadium is at full capacity each gameday.
Limited ticket inventory remains for the Red Raiders' home finale on Nov. 26 versus TCU.
Single-game tickets can be purchased online at
TexasTech.com
or by contacting a ticket sales representative at 806-742-TECH.
On May 11, Texas Tech Athletics announced it had sold out its season ticket allotment for the 2026 football season, marking the fourth consecutive year Red Raider football season tickets have sold out. Due to significant demand following the program's continued rise, Texas Tech increased its season ticket allotment for 2026. Red Raider fans purchased more than 33,400 season tickets en route to another sellout, while the season-ticket renewal rate reached 95 percent, creating only limited availability for the wait list.
Fans are encouraged to download their digital tickets to their smartphone's mobile wallet before arriving at the stadium. For assistance, visit
texastech.com/feature/digital-ticketing
.
THIS WEEK IN THE POLLS
College Football Playoff:
Texas Tech was revealed as the
No. 15
team in the first CFP rankings released live on ESPN Tuesday evening.
Associated Press:
Texas Tech remained at
No. 13
in the Associated Press poll Sunday despite the Red Raiders' second top-25 victory of the season Saturday in a 34-26 win over then-No. 22 Arizona.
AFCA Coaches Poll:
The win versus the Wildcats did move the Red Raiders up one spot to
No. 13
in the U.S. LBM coaches poll.
This is the 27th-consecutive week the Red Raiders have appeared in the Associated Press poll, the longest run in program history. The Red Raiders previously appeared in the Associated Press poll over 20-straight weeks during the course of the 2004-06 seasons. Texas Tech rose to as high as No. 10 during that stretch.
West Virginia enters the weekend unranked in all three polls.
RED RAIDERS SEEKING HOME WIN STREAK RECORD ON SATURDAY
The Red Raiders matched the school record this past Saturday for the longest home winning streak with their win over No. 22 Arizona, earning their 13th consecutive home win. The streak ties the mark set during the World War II era, spanning the 1939-42 seasons.
Texas Tech, which boasts the nation's third-longest active home winning streak, is an impressive 27-5 at home under
Joey McGuire
, including a 17-4 mark against Big 12 opponents.
Texas Tech seeks to establish a new record of 14 with a win this Saturday against the Mountaineers.
WELCOME TO JOEY-VEMBER...
Texas Tech heads into the month of November deep in the Big 12 title race after a 7-1 start. The good news for the Red Raiders is that Texas Tech has been the Big 12's winningest program in the month of November under
Joey McGuire
. The Red Raiders are a combined 13-3 in November games under McGuire, which is the best in the Big 12, with Arizona in second with a 12-5 record (.706) during that span.
Joey McGuire
's 13-3 record during the month of November is the highest winning percentage for a Texas Tech head coach in regular-season games in November or December through his first four seasons. McGuire is also tied with DeWitt Weaver (13-4-1) for the most November wins through the first four seasons in program history.
The Red Raiders were a perfect 4-0 during the final month a year ago, which marked the first undefeated November since 1993 when Texas Tech closed out a five-game winning streak to end the regular season with victories over TCU, SMU and Houston. Texas Tech enters this weekend having won six-consecutive games in November dating back to wins over Oklahoma State and West Virginia in 2024.
RUNNING GAME COULD BE KEY THIS WEEKEND
All eyes will be on the running game for both teams this weekend as West Virginia enters averaging 261.6 rushing yards per game and will face a Texas Tech defense that is giving up only 94.9 yards of its own. The Red Raiders surrendered only 12 rushing yards to Arizona, marking their fewest to a Big 12 opponent since facing Kansas in 1997. Texas Tech will look to repeat its own rushing performance, meanwhile, as the Red Raiders compiled a season-high 328 yards behind two 100-yard rushers in
J'Koby Williams
and
Thomas Castellanos
.
INSIDE THE WEST VIRGINIA SERIES
Texas Tech and West Virginia will meet for the 16th time in history and the 15th time as Big 12 members in a back-and-forth series that the Red Raiders now lead 8-7 overall. The Red Raiders have won eight of 14 games since the Mountaineers joined the Big 12 Conference prior to the 2012 season, including six of the past seven meetings after a 49-0 rout a year ago in Morgantown.
Texas Tech's lone setback in its last seven games versus West Virginia came in its 2023 visit to Morgantown. The Mountaineers frustrated Texas Tech's offense that afternoon on the way to a 20-13 victory. It is the lone loss against West Virginia under
Joey McGuire
, who is 3-1 versus the Mountaineers during his tenure.
The two schools have closed the regular season against each other in each of the past two seasons as the Red Raiders delivered a 52-15 rout at home in 2024 before a 49-0 shutout victory a year ago in Morgantown. Prior to those two wins, the series had traditionally featured several close games with seven of the first 12 meetings as Big 12 members being decided by one possession. The Red Raiders are 3-4 in one-possession games all-time against the Mountaineers.
The only other meeting between the two schools outside the Big 12 era came in the 1938 Sun Bowl where West Virginia edged the Red Raiders, 7-6, in front of 12,000 fans in El Paso. It was the first of now 42 bowl appearances for the Red Raiders, who rank among the top 25 teams all-time in postseason trips.
The 2027 season will snap a streak of 14-consecutive seasons the two schools have faced each other since becoming Big 12 members. The Mountaineers will not be on Texas Tech's 2027 slate, which is the final season in the Big 12 Conference's four-year scheduling matrix.
A CLOSER LOOK AT THE LAST MATCHUP IN 2025
Then-No. 5 Texas Tech dominated West Virginia, 49-0, on Nov. 29, 2025, in Morgantown, improving to 11-1 overall and 8-1 in Big 12 play in its regular-season finale. The Red Raiders scored touchdowns on four of their first five drives.
Behren Morton
threw for 310 yards and three touchdowns, completing 25 of 32 passes before exiting early in the third quarter. He connected with
Caleb Douglas
for two touchdowns and
Cameron Dickey
for another.
Caleb Douglas
led the receiving corps with five catches for 127 yards and two touchdowns, while
Micah Hudson
added two touchdown receptions from
Lloyd Jones III
.
Linebacker
Jacob Rodriguez
scored his second offensive touchdown in as many games, taking a direct snap for a 1-yard touchdown on Texas Tech's opening offensive series.
Amier Boyd
returned an interception 67 yards, setting up one of Hudson's touchdown receptions.
The Red Raiders' defense shut out West Virginia; it was the 2025 defense's second shutout of the season, having also shut out Oklahoma State earlier in the season.
