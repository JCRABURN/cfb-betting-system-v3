UTSA/North Texas game selected for ESPNU broadcast
Share
Twitter
Facebook
Email
IRVING, Texas —
Officials from the American Conference and ESPN announced Monday that UTSA’s Homecoming game against North Texas on Saturday, Nov. 14, has been selected for an ESPNU broadcast. Kickoff is scheduled for 8 p.m. at the Alamodome.
This will mark the 15th meeting between the Roadrunners and Mean Green, making it the most-played series in program history. UTSA has won four of the past five matchups against UNT and holds an 8-6 advantage in the series, including a 5-1 mark at home.
Tickets for all home games are on sale by visiting
goUTSA.com/tickets
or by calling the UTSA Ticket Office at 210-458-UTSA (8872). Current UTSA students receive free admission to all home games by downloading tickets via their Account Manager. The first 10,000 fans in attendance will receive exclusive homecoming jerseys, courtesy of Firstmark Credit Union.
Off to a 7-1 start, the Roadrunners are riding a five-game winning streak and are tied with UNT atop the league standings at 4-0 after a 42-19 road triumph over Tulane on Oct. 24 in New Orleans. UTSA, which is receiving votes in both major polls and bowl eligible for the seventh straight year, will return to action this Thursday, Nov. 5, against Florida Atlantic (5-3, 2-2). Kickoff is set for 7:30 p.m. ET/6:30 p.m. CT in Boca Raton, and the game will be televised nationally on ESPN.
