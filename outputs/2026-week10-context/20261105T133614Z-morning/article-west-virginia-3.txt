Mountaineers in the NFL – Week 8
November 04, 2026 02:37 PM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Calvin Kleppner
In Week 8 of the 2026 NFL season, six former Mountaineers saw action. Three former Mountaineers Geno Smith, Zach Frazier, and Dante Stills started for their respective teams.
Arizona Cardinals (Cardinals 33, Cowboys 30)
Dante Stills – DE – 4th year – played in 55 career games with 28 starts… recorded six tackles, with one pass deflected… saw action in 46 defensive snaps.
Cleveland Browns (Browns 6, Steelers 27)
Rex Sunahara – LS – 3
rd
year – Played in 37 games…recorded one tackle… saw action in 6 plays on special teams.
Jacksonville Jaguars (Jaguars 42, Colts 7)
Wyatt Milum – OT – 2
nd
year – Played in 16 games… saw action in 38 plays on offense and six on special teams.
New York Jets (Jets 33, Patriots 38)
Geno Smith – QB – 14
th
year – Played in 117 games while making 106 career starts… completed 20 of 32 passes for 290 yards and finished the game with a 99.7 QBR… threw two touchdowns… played in all 64 offensive snaps.
