Phelps Nominated for Burlsworth Trophy
11/4/2026 9:30:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
BOISE, Idaho –
Boise State linebacker
Boen Phelps
was nominated for the Burlsworth Trophy, the organization announced. The senior is one of 65 nominees for the award, which is presented annually to the top college football player who began his career as a walk-on. This is the second consecutive year Phelps has been nominated.
Through eight games, Phelps leads the Broncos in solo tackles (24) and total tackles (49). He has 3.5 tackles for loss, 1.0 sack, an interception, three pass breakups and a blocked field goal. A native of Spokane, Washington, Phelps has started every game for a unit that ranks 12th-nationally in 3rd down conversion defense (30.9%) and 13th-nationally in 4th down conversion defense (31.6%). Boise State holds opponents to 99.5 rushing yards per game, 19th-fewest in the country.
Following an eight-tackle performance at No. 2/2 Oregon on Sept. 5, Phelps was named the Burlsworth Trophy Walk-On of the Week. He also added eight tackles, 2.0 TFLs and a blocked field goal against Memphis on Sept. 12. He notched his first interception of the year at Washington State on Oct. 24 and matched his season-high with eight more tackles against Texas State on Oct. 31.
A product of Freeman HS, Phelps came to Boise State as a walk-on safety, making his debut against Fresno State as a true freshman before playing all 14 games in his second year, primarily on special teams. He transitioned to linebacker before last season, making nine starts and finishing the year with 66 tackles and 4.0 TFLs. He was named Mountain West Defensive Player of the Week on Sept. 29 following his performance against App State, when he totaled six tackles and had a 33-yard interception return for a touchdown.
The Burlsworth Trophy Selection Committee will announce semifinalists on Nov. 10 and three finalists on Nov. 17. The 2026 Burlsworth Trophy winner will be announced at the annual Burlsworth Trophy Award Show on Dec. 7 at Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art in Bentonville, Arkansas.
Phelps and the Broncos return to action on Nov. 7 at Colorado State. Boise State will meet the Rams in Fort Collins at 4 p.m. The game will be televised on USA Sports. The Broncos are No. 25 in the College Football Playoff rankings, No. 20 in the AP Poll and No. 21 in the Coaches Poll.
--BroncoSports.com--
