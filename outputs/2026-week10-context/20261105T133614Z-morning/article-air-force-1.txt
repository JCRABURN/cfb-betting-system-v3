Kemper Hodges earns Davey O’Brien Award Great 8 honors
11/3/2026 10:03:00 AM
|
Football
Share:
Honor is third this week for senior QB
Senior quarterback
Kemper Hodges
earned Davey O'Brien Great 8 honors for his performance last week vs. UConn.
Hodges recorded career highs in every rushing category with 35 carries for 200 yards, four touchdowns and a long run of 62 yards for a touchdown. The 100-yard game is the first of his career and his 62-yard run is the longest play by a Falcon this season. Hodges is the first Falcon to rush for 200 yards since Emmanuel Michel had 203 on 35 carries vs. James Madison in 2023. He is also the first Falcon with four rushing touchdowns since Michel had four vs. James Madison in 2023. The four rushing touchdowns are tied as the third most in program history while his 35 carries are the eighth most and most since Michel's 35 vs. James Madison in 2023. Hodges' first touchdown put the Falcons on the board and gave them the lead for good while his final touchdown came with 3:09 left and make it a two-score game. Besides his four touchdowns, Hodges had 11 carries for first downs, including three on fourth down. Hodges is the first Air Force quarterback with a 200-yard rushing game since Arion Worthman had 215 vs. San Jose State in 2016. His 200-yard game is the 20th program history and 8th by a quarterback. He is just the fourth quarterback in program history with a 200-yard game (Dee Dowis, Beau Morgan, Arion Worthman).
Hodges was also named Mountain West Offensive Player of the Week, as well as the National Football Foundation, Colorado Chapter, Player of the week.
Full release from Davey O'Brien Award follows below.
FORT WORTH, Texas (Nov. 2, 2026) —
The Davey O'Brien Foundation has selected the eight quarterbacks named to the Davey O'Brien Great 8 for week nine. The group features Air Force's
Kemper Hodges
, Tayven Jackson of North Texas, Baylor's DJ Lagway, Nick Minicucci of Delaware, Oregon's Dylan Raiola, Jaylen Raynor of Iowa State, South Carolina's LaNorris Sellers and Marcus Stokes of Memphis.
All quarterbacks who were named to the Great 8 during the first nine weeks of the season officially joined the Davey O'Brien Midseason Watch List. Through nine weeks, 53 different quarterbacks were honored.
The Davey O'Brien National Quarterback of the Week, selected from the Great 8 list, will be revealed Tuesday at 11 a.m. (CT).
The Davey O'Brien Foundation was created in 1977 and is celebrating its 50th anniversary season this year. The National Quarterback Award, the oldest and most prestigious college quarterback award, was first issued in 1981.
Over its time, the Davey O'Brien Foundation has given away more than $1.4 million in scholarships and university grants to help high school and college athletes transform leadership on the field into leadership in life.
In 1938, O'Brien, who wore No. 8 as a star quarterback for TCU, became the first player ever to win the Heisman Trophy, Maxwell Award and Walter Camp Award in the same year. He was inducted into the College Football Hall of Fame in 1955.

Davey O'Brien Great 8: Week 9
Kemper Hodges
, Air Force, Sr., 6-0, 245, Santa Rosa Beach, Fla.
·      Rushed 35 times for 200 yards and four touchdowns in a 35-26 victory over UConn, becoming the first Falcon to achieve either of those totals since 2023.
Tayven Jackson, North Texas, Sr., 6-4, 209, Greenwood, Ind.
·      Accumulated 445 total yards (404 passing, 41 rushing) while going 33-for-46 (.717) through the air with four touchdown passes in a 48-41 win over Florida Atlantic.
DJ Lagway, Baylor, Jr., 6-3, 237, Willis, Texas
·      Connected on 16 of 25 passes for 319 yards (19.9 yards per completion) and four touchdowns, while also adding 23 rushing yards, in a 41-9 rout at UCF.
Nick Minicucci, Delaware, Sr., 6-2, 215, Midland Park, N.J.
·      Was 32-for-38 (.842) passing for 414 yards and four touchdowns as Delaware defeated Western Kentucky, 35-21.
Dylan Raiola, Oregon, Jr., 6-3, 232, Buford, Ga.
·      Helped lead Oregon to a 34-26 upset over No. 20 Northwestern by completing 22 of 31 passes for 336 yards and three touchdowns.
Jaylen Raynor, Iowa State, Sr., 6-1, 208, Winston-Salem, N.C.
·      Completed 18 of 28 passes for 309 yards and three touchdowns and rushed 12 times for 48 yards and a score in a 51-49 upset of No. 24 Oklahoma State.
LaNorris Sellers, South Carolina, Sr., 6-3, 238, Florence, S.C.
·      Totaled 397 yards of offense (301 passing, 96 rushing) and five touchdowns (three passing, two rushing) as South Carolina downed Oklahoma, 42-25.
Marcus Stokes, Memphis, Jr., 6-2, 209, St. Augustine, Fla.
·      Was 29-for-35 (.829) for 371 yards and four touchdowns and added 30 rushing yards and a score in a 49-22 win over Army.
About the Davey O'Brien National Quarterback Award
The Davey O'Brien National Quarterback Award is presented annually to the nation's best college quarterback and is the oldest and most prestigious national quarterback award. The Davey O'Brien Award honors candidates who exemplify Davey O'Brien's enduring character while exhibiting teamwork, sportsmanship and leadership in both academics and athletics. Celebrating its 50th anniversary this year, the Davey O'Brien Foundation, which is headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas, has awarded more than $1.4 million in scholarships and university grants to help high school and college athletes transform leadership on the field into leadership in life. For more information, visit
www.DaveyOBrienAward.com
