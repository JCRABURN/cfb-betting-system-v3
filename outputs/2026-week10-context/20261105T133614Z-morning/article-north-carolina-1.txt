October 29, 2026

Share:
Chapel Thrill Game Day Timeline
4:30 PM: Modelo Kick Off Club, Coca-Cola Fan Zone and tailgating open
5:00 PM: Old Well Walk begins
5:30 PM: Chapel Thrill Concert Series presented by The Rams Club: Neon Trees
6:00 PM: Kenan Stadium gates open
6 PM: Marching Tar Heels perform in The Pit
7:00 PM: Last call for Coca-Cola Fan Zone and Chapel Thrill
7:30 PM: Kickoff
Trick or Treating
Kids coming to the game will have several ways to snag some Halloween candy throughout the night.
First, the Carolina Football team will be handing out candy during the Old Well Walk, which will start around 5 pm. Cheer on the team as they walk from the Old Well through Polk Place and around the Bell Tower as they prepare for the game.
Once Kenan Stadium gates open at 6 PM, kids aged 13 and under can trick or treat on the concourse pregame. Candy will be available at several locations around the main concourse, including at Gates 2, 3, and 6, as well as Blue Zone North Entrance, the Blue Zone South Entrance, and on the Blue Zone concourse.
Candy will be distributed from 6-7 p.m., or while supplies last. Clear bags will be provided.
Costume Policy
Fans are encouraged to wear their Halloween costumes to the game.  Costumes must comply with our Fan Code of Conduct, which prohibits obscene, indecent, or offensive clothing and signs. Costumes cannot impede public safety or block the view of other fans. Costumes or props that could be considered weapons are prohibited.  Guest who do not comply with the Fan Code of Conduct or Prohibited Item policies will be denied entry.
Light Displays
Fans attending the game should be aware that flashing lights, lasers, and pyrotechnics may be used throughout the night.
50/50 Raffle
Carolina Athletics is partnering with Elite Charitable Solutions to run a 50/50 raffle for each home football game this season.  Of the total pot at each game, half will go to the winning fan, and the other half will benefit The Rams Club.
50/50 sales will be available both inside and outside of Kenan Stadium on game days, and will close at the end of the third quarter.  The winner will be announced during a fourth quarter timeout.   The winner does not need to be present, but must retain the original ticket or receipt and claim the prize within 30 days.
For more information, and to enter the raffle yourself, visit
HERE
.
First Game Fan Certificates
Are you or someone from your party attending their first game in Kenan Stadium? Stop by a fan services location at Gates 2 and 6 to pick up a first game certificate and button.
