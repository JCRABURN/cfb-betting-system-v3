Blake Anderson Weekly News Conference – The JMU Game
11/2/2026 1:44:00 PM
|
Football
Media Availability
Share:
HATTIESBURG, Miss. –
Southern Miss football coach
Blake Anderson
addressed the media on Monday, Nov. 2, in the Duff Athletic Center ahead of the Golden Eagles' Thursday night nationally televised game at home against James Madison. Game time is set for 7 p.m. at Renasant Stadium.
Southern Miss (3-5 overall, 2-2 Sun Belt) looks to win its third-straight game when they meet the Dukes (7-2, 4-2).
On the call for the ESPN2 crew will be Mike Corey (Play-by-Play) and Henry Burris (Analyst), while the Southern Miss Sports Network's radio pregame show begins at 1 p.m. with the Voice of the Golden Eagles,
John Cox
, analyst Lee Roberts and sideline reporter Jason Baker on the call. Tickets for the game can be purchased as well by going to
SouthernMiss.com/tickets
.
Here is what Anderson had to say to the media Monday morning:
Opening Statement:
"Great win it's that simple. On the road, conference play, to get a win. Don't want to take that for granted. I said ugly win Saturday, and it was in a lot of ways, but still a win, and where we're at in the stage of this team and this program, and what we're trying to get through, and how we've started to where we're at. Just, I mean, I don't want to take for granted an opportunity to get a win. So I'm pleased. We celebrated it. Didn't celebrate it quite like I thought. To be truthful, I think our guys really understood just how many mistakes there were in the game. Understood that in the third quarter we, you know, we just couldn't put it away the way we needed to, and felt relieved, probably more than excited. And I think that's a good thing in the sense that I didn't come into a locker room that was you know high fiving and jumping all over the place, you know, like kind of we did the week before. To be truthful, we celebrated that one really big. I came into a locker room that understood, like we almost let this thing get away. We didn't play the way we needed to, especially in the third quarter. But we found a way to finish, and we survived and learned a lot. And that was really the approach and the mindset coming into this building yesterday. Which I appreciate, and I think is good for us, because I don't want to make more of it than it is, and I want to be very transparent, realistic. That that is not good enough to win every week. It was good enough to win Saturday. That's it. But in that, if we take that same approach to some of the things that we did, we won't be able to win at the level that we want to. So, I was pleased with that response. I mean, you don't ever want to take a win for granted, but you want to be very open and honest, and that's the approach we took. And I think that they felt the same way. So, short week, we've got to get ready quickly. The game we played Saturday doesn't win Thursday night, and that's really the reality of it. We got to play better. We're playing a very, very talented opponent that you can't have ups and downs, and you can't have critical mistakes and expect to beat. So, we've got to do what we can to get better this week and play our best game. "
Q: For this week's game, JMU has a very solid group with a big front seven
"They've got a great roster, and they've been through transition, but there's a tradition there, and there's a lot of seniors on the roster as well. They did keep a bunch too. They're built well. You know they're built well. That program's been really good for a long time, and this is no different. I know they're coming off a two-game slide, but you look at the roster, they got people. We're going to have to play well. We're going to have to match physicality. We're going to have to tackle well in space. It carries over into special teams. Like every phase of the game, you've got to be your best."
Q: You guys get to the point now, we've spoken about before, you can kind of hang your hat on that run game now. People are starting to realize you're running the ball, but yeah. I guess if I'm the DC over there, I'm going to make
Landry Lyddy
throw.
"I think at this point in the season, there's not a lot of secrets anymore. It always gets tougher in late tendencies and weaknesses and all the above. So, yeah, I think we've got to find some consistency offensively where we're more balanced than we've been. We've struggled in that area the last couple weeks, and it's not all just Landry. I mean, it's across the board protection, running routes, making plays, and some of it's been him too. But I would fully expect that yeah they're going to challenge us and make us beat them in in those situations. And I don't know exactly what that'll look like. I mean, you're always kind of guessing, but yeah, we've run the ball pretty well the last couple weeks, and you've got to got to keep trying to do that. But also, we've got to be more balanced."
Q: Coach, can you kind of talk to
Grant Page
coming back and then having an immediate impact?
"Yeah, it was good. Good to get him back. I don't know that any of us expected he'd be back really, this early, he's playing with the brace. I think he's playing through some discomfort. It was a huge catch when we needed it pretty desperately. Good to have him back. There's a maturity and a presence about him. He's been with us for a while. You know, he came with us from Utah State and been in the system, and so he kind of knows what we're wanting to do. And it was good to have him. Made a big play when we need it, and hopefully he'll factor in even more as we're as we're moving forward."
Q: What would do think about the quarterback play overall this weekend and where can it go?
"Yeah, I mean, I think it was a similar conversation we've had. I thought he did some really good things at times. I thought he forced the ball several times. We talked about it pretty, pretty critically the last couple days about you know pushing the ball into harm's way. We were fortunate that a couple of those ended up hitting the ground, but something we can't do. We sure can't do it Thursday night. These guys will not; they will not miss opportunities. So we've got to get better play there. He's got to be more consistent with it and understand the circumstances. So it's no different than any other position, in the sense that we are brutally honest with where they are and what we're getting from them. And he knows, and he was critical of himself. Got to stay within the framework of the offense, and be willing to check the ball down, throw the ball away, drop the ball down under coverage. Way too many force throws downfield. We can't panic. We got to take what comes to us, and so hopefully that's something we'll do a better job of this week."
Q: Special teams took a pretty significant step forward, you know, with the kicking, obviously, but then the punting as well. I mean, how big is that to see that group put everything that has happened behind them?
"I think really, to some degree, the field goal kicking has been glaring. Our coverage teams have been good all year. To you know, we had the one kick against Lafayette that we really missed a tackle that should have been tackled on the 15-yard line, and that one got scary. So grateful that we got that on the ground. It was great to get some field goals to go through the uprights, no doubt. Huge to get a punt return. We've really struggled, but we went into that game thinking we would have an opportunity. We prepared, and you know that's one of the few places I actually run that group and coming out of the penalties he had against Lafayette, I want to do something positive in that area. But it was good. We had thought we had a good matchup, and it was good to see it get started and get going. In a game like Thursday night, it is critical. They are really really good in that area, in all phases of that area, and it's got to be a place that's a strength for us. It cannot be a place where we give up big plays. They got one of the best returners in the country. I think they've got a couple of punt returns for a touchdown. They got a couple of huge kickoff returns. It's got to be a place where we play well. So, it's good to have some positive things happen on Saturday, but still, I still think we got better ahead of us. We got to keep trying to be more consistent through all phases on each given week, and that's an area that can impact wins in a big, big way, and it's not talked about probably enough."
Q: Talk about
Mario Sanders
a little bit too, because I mean, just is that evolving over the season to being this kind of dominant shooter for you guys? I mean, I know coming out of fall, we all thought he was going to be a big piece, but I don't know if we ever extended it to be this big of a dominant factor.
"You don't have crystal ball you don't really know exactly what to expect I think he's done a great job of continually to get more and more comfortable in what we're doing. That position in this system has typically been a guy that has plenty of opportunity and some guys take it and run with it more than others and I think he's really comfortable in his role at this point. I would like to get him even more involved. You know, last couple weeks it's not been quite as good as we'd like it to be, but he's kind of been a stabilizing force because he's been consistent, and we see that during the week at practice. We were excited when we recruited him; thought there was potential there, but until you see it on Saturday, you're always you know kind of holding your breath. He's been a pleasant surprise to be as consistent as he's been, play as many snaps as he's played, doesn't come off the field very much, and to make plays in big moments, you know that we've needed him to. So, obviously he's been a really good surprise for us."
Q: The defense seems like, I mean there's still stuff to work on, but it seems like since the Troy game had kind of turned a corner, at least from an aggressive standpoint.
"Well, we've played better. There's no doubt. I mean, you. Hard to play much worse. That was about as bad of a defensive performance as you could have, and it's crazy to have watched how they've progressed. You got to give them and the coaching staff credit that we very critically self-assessed where we were, weren't happy with the product, knew we needed to play better, had to stop missing tackles. Needed to be more all on the same page. Way too many assignment errors, and those have reduced every week against teams that look very similar to Troy. You know, I didn't feel like we got out athlete'd. We just didn't play well. Lafayette and Monroe physically, you know, looked very similar in terms of what we're trying to tackle and just the sheer size and kind of matchup. So yeah, I've been really really pleased with their approach to critically attacking problems and getting better. We've simplified some. We have been more aggressive at times. It tends to play in our nature. Maybe a little bit. Maybe it simplifies things even a little bit more for the guys. Instead of reading things out, like I attack a gap and I go make a play, sometimes that just makes things a little bit easier for guys that haven't played a whole lot. And I think they're gaining some confidence, but they're about to be tested in a big, big way. So, we're going to find out where we really are at this point, but I'm proud of what they've done, and we don't win the last two games without them playing at a really high level because we've just not played great football offensively. We've run the ball well, but we just haven't played collectively real balance, explosive offense. You just hadn't seen that as much as we needed to, and maybe as much as we even saw early in the year at times. So, the defense has done a phenomenal job. So, the test is much bigger for everybody this week. To be truthful."
Q: This is the second week in a row where somebody on the offensive line steps up in a backup role and has a good game for you at right tackle. Talk about his play.
"Christian [Young] played a good bit, and Larry [Edwards] played a good bit too. So, we've gone through a bunch of dudes, you know, and they've done well. Still, some mistakes that we got to try to avoid, but to be able to run the ball like we did with plugging and playing several guys. I mean, Zerian [Hudson] played the whole game at left guard. Larry played about half the game at left tackle, and Christian played about half the game at right tackle. That's not how we expected that thing to go. And ugly as it was, to still be able to score 35 points, that's pretty good."
Q: How's the health report for this week?
"Oh, we're beat up. I think we'll get Luke [Rogers] back. I think there's a really good chance we'll get [Greg] Nunnery back too. I don't know, maybe not a full game, but both should be back and involved. So, we could get back to looking a little bit more normal. But all those guys stepped in and played good snaps, and not sure that we'll just shut that down. Those guys may still play."
Q: Didn't have a huge freshman class this first time out but did like
Talik Parkman
part to come on that D-line, seeing him play more and more and develop.
"Yeah, I love the freshman class. I mean, literally, think we, I think we hit on every one of them, and they're all going to have great careers here. Talik's a guy that we started kind of throwing in in practice a couple weeks ago, little by little, starting to gain more confidence in him. We've already seen [Caleb Young-Triplet], we've seen [Broderick] Peters, feel really good about what we did, and we're going to take that same approach again. We're going to sign, you know, I would think 10 to 15 high school guys a year with what we're trying to do. Now, that means some patience is going to be needed in the process because they're not all going to be ready to play as freshmen. And but there's just too good of football in in our footprint to not try to develop players. And there's some guys I haven't seen yet that I think have just as much potential that we'll see. You know, this if not later in this year, definitely in next season with a good off-season under their belt."
Q: Coach, you mentioned at the end of that Louisiana game how the crowd had an impact in that fourth quarter. How big is it that you get that same kind of energy this Thursday?
"Well, that'd be huge if we had a packed house, and I know that's asking a lot, hadn't been a lot to cheer about this season. I think the people that are connected to what we're doing understand what we're doing here, and we've got nothing but positive. I mean, so much positive. There's always a negative group, and I get it, man. They've got to have something to be frustrated about, and it's plenty to be frustrated about this year. So are we. But I don't know. We felt it in that game. It got loud. It can get louder. There's plenty of seats open for folks to come fill. This would be a huge, huge moment for our football team and where we are, if we could, if we could pack this thing and get a win on Thursday night, just, hopefully people see where we're trying to go and how we're trying to go about doing it, and the approach we're trying to take, and understand, you know, that we need your support now. You know, we don't want to wait till we get there. We'd like to have it along the way, and so I just encourage people to come out. It would be huge for us. Our guys do feed off the energy in the building, like it's real, and we just saw what it looks like when nobody shows up, and that was really hard. That's really hard, and I'm not, you know, I wasn't trying to be disrespectful to Bryant [Vincent] or Monroe at all. Like just the reality of it's different when the place is packed with energy from Eagle Walk to the end and we feed off of it and our guys appreciate it as do I so yeah anybody that's done— I mean what else you going to do on Thursday night in Hattiesburg, like why not come out here. Our guys are going to play hard and we're getting better. We are going to get there. Hopefully sooner rather than later. But man, we do need the support of the community and the fan base. If they can get there, we'd appreciate if they would."
Q: You don't have a lot of recovery days this week, speak to how you will man your staff.
"Well, you can't have a lot of physical this week. We're beat up pretty good, but everybody is at this point. So it's not like we're different than anybody else. So, it will be a lot more mental and recovery type approach than it will be banging. We'll go out today. We typically don't go out on Mondays. We go out today. We'll bang a little bit. Be the only padded day of the week tomorrow and Wednesday will be a lot of mental trying our best to you know in a short week feel the best we can on a Thursday night. I love the fact they got to come to us, but they also played Thursday, so they got a couple extra days on us on that. So we'll try to catch up as best we can. I did find though early in my career that if you try to do too much on a short week, it shows in heavy legs and no juice in the fourth quarter. So, where we've been successful and have been in some Thursday night games and some short weeks, you know, in this league, went on the road and beat a ranked App State team, went on the road and beat a ranked Troy team. Both of them on short weeks. We found the formula was more recovery and mental than it was a ton of physical. You can't squeeze a full week into a short week. You can't do it. If you do, I promise you, you'll not like the result. So we're going to stick with that formula. That's worked for us in the past, and we'll try to be fresh and pissed off on Thursday night."
