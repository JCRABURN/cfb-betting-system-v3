Caleb Ojeda Named Pac-12 Special Teams Player Of The Week
November 02, 2026
|
Football
Share:
By:
Athletic Communications
CORVALLIS, Ore. –
Oregon State kicker
Caleb Ojeda
has been named the Pac-12 Conference's Special Teams Player Of The Week, it was announced on Monday.
Ojeda connected on three field goals Saturday at Fresno State, including a career-long 54-yarder for the Beavers' first points of the game. He also made a 28-yard field goal early in the second quarter and a 24-yarder midway through the frame.
The effort marked the first of his career with three field-goal makes.
Ojeda is 10-for-13 on field-goal attempts this season, with four coming from 40 or more yards.
The honor is the first of his career.
Ojeda and the Beavers return home for a 7:30 p.m. PT matchup with Texas State Saturday at Reser Stadium.
