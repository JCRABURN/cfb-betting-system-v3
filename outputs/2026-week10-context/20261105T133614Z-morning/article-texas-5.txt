No. 10/11 Football’s Biles named Bednarik Award Player of the Week
11.03.2026
|
Football
Share:
Biles also earned his third SEC Co-Defensive Player of the Week honor on Monday.
By:
Grace Tafolla
AUSTIN, Texas –
No. 10/11 Texas Football senior linebacker
Rasheem Biles
was named the Bednarik Award's National Player of the Week, the Maxwell Football Club announced Tuesday.
Biles is the second Longhorn to collect weekly honors from the Bednarik Award, joining junior EDGE
Colin Simmons
, who claimed the same honor on Sept. 29.
It marks the first time Biles has received the Bednarik Award's Player of the Week. On Monday, Biles earned his third SEC Co-Defensive Player of the Week honor of the season. Prior to the season, Biles was tabbed to the Bednarik Award's preseason watch list. Biles was selected as a semifinalist for the Butkus Award on Oct. 28.
Biles led Texas to a 31-3 victory over Mississippi State on Oct. 31 at DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium. The Longhorns limited the Bulldogs to just 187 yards of total offense for just 2.8 yards per play and just three points. Mississippi State was held to 68 yards on the ground and 119 yards in the air. Coming into the game, the Bulldogs had averaged 439.1 yards per game and 6.3 yards per play. As a whole, the defense recorded seven sacks and nine tackles for loss.
Biles compiled eight tackles, three sacks for 26 yards lost, four tackles for loss for a total of 27 yards, and two forced fumbles, one of which was returned for a 61-yard touchdown by Watson, as Texas held Mississippi State to only three points in a 31-3 victory. The three points the defense allowed even came following a turnover by the Longhorns offense in their own territory. The Columbus, Ohio product's three sacks and two forced fumbles were both career highs. The three sacks are also tied for the most in a game this year by a true linebacker in the FBS. Both forced fumbles came on the same plays as sacks, the first of which changed the complexion of the game. Just before the half with Texas holding a 10-0 lead, and Mississippi State at the Longhorns' 34, Biles sacked the quarterback and knocked the ball loose. Teammate
Maraad Watson
scooped it up and ran it 61 yards for a touchdown to extend the lead to 17-0 at halftime. Later in the third quarter, after the offense had turned the ball over at the Texas 28 and the Bulldogs had moved it to the 14, Biles made another key play, disrupting what would have been a pass by a receiver to register a sack where the player attempted to get rid of the ball, but the tackle forced him to throw it backwards for a lateral that bounced out of bounds and ended up as a fumble for a loss of 15 yards. Two plays later, Mississippi State kicked a field goal for their only points of the game. Biles had opened the game with a tackle for loss on first down of the series that ended Mississippi State's first possession. Early in the fourth quarter, he produced his third sack, which created a three-and-out and gave the ball to the offense, which scored for the 31-3 final margin. Biles now leads the SEC with three forced fumbles (6th FBS), leads the team with three interceptions (3rd SEC/16th FBS) and seven pressures, and is second in tackles (61), sacks (4), and tackles for loss (9.5), and tied for second in pass breakups (4). The 9.5 tackles for loss are fourth in the SEC and 23rd in the FBS.
Biles is a part of a Texas defense that ranks seventh nationally in team sacks (3.25), eighth in team passing efficiency defense (106.91), 10th in total defense (273.1), 10th in defensive touchdowns (2), 12th in passing yards allowed (170.8), 16th in red zone defense (0.750), 19th in scoring defense (17.1) and 24th in rushing defense (102.4).
Up next, Texas hits the road to Columbia, Mo., to take on the No. 19/19 Missouri Tigers at 11 a.m. CT on Saturday, Nov. 7, at Faurot Field. The game will broadcast on ESPN with Joe Tessitore (play-by-play), Jesse Palmer (analyst) and Kris Budden (reporter) on the call.
