NFL Broncos: Week Nine
11/4/2026 3:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
BOISE, Idaho –
Boise State will be represented by 10 players and one head coach in Week Nine of the NFL season. Two players will compete in primetime, giving the Broncos three days of action. Ezra Cleveland and the Jacksonville Jaguars kick things off at 6:15 p.m. MT on Thursday, meeting the Baltimore Ravens on Prime Video. Sunday features eight former Broncos, including New Orleans head coach Kellen Moore, whose statue was recently unveiled at Albertsons Stadium. Khalil Shakir and the Buffalo Bills close the weekend on Monday Night Football, meeting the Minnesota Vikings at 6:15 p.m. MT on ABC/ESPN. Full Week Nine information can be found below. All times listed are Mountain.
THURSDAY
Jacksonville Jaguars (Ezra Cleveland) at Baltimore Ravens – Prime Video, 6:15 p.m.
Cleveland played 20 total snaps before leaving due to injury in Jacksonville's 42-7 trouncing of the Indianapolis Colts. The Jaguars churned out 366 yards of offense, running for a season-high 183 yards. With the win, they improved to 5-2 and took a three-game lead in the AFC South race.
SUNDAY
Denver Broncos (
Kage Casey
, JL Skinner) at Carolina Panthers – CBS, 11 a.m.
Skinner played a season-high 29 snaps and had a pair of tackles in Denver's 28-24 win over the Kansas City Chiefs. He made one of the game's biggest plays with nine seconds remaining in regulation, making a solo tackle on Kenneth Walker at the 1-yard line, stopping a potential go-ahead touchdown. Skinner has 10 tackles this season and 31 in his career. Casey was inactive in the victory, which moved the Broncos to 6-2 and put them atop the AFC West standings.
Washington Commanders (John Bates) vs. Los Angeles Rams – FOX, 11 a.m.
Bates started his ninth consecutive game at tight end and made a solo tackle on punt coverage in Washington's 27-10 win over the Philadelphia Eagles.
Detroit Lions (Ahmed Hassanein) at Miami Dolphins – FOX, 11 a.m.
Hassanein was inactive in Detroit's 19-16 win over the previously undefeated Minnesota Vikings. The victory moved the Lions to 5-2, including a 4-0 mark at home.
New Orleans Saints (Kellen Moore) vs. Cleveland Browns – CBS, 11 a.m.
Moore and the Saints return from a week off after their 16-10 loss to the Pittsburgh Steelers, a game played in Paris. Quarterback Tyler Shough completed 30 passes for 278 yards and a touchdown. Wide receiver Chris Olave, who ranks fifth in the league with 90.9 receiving yards per game, had six receptions for 56 yards.
Los Angeles Chargers (Scott Matlock) vs. Houston Texans – FOX, 2:05 p.m.
Matlock played a season-high 30 snaps in the Chargers' 27-9 loss to the Los Angeles Rams, including a season-high 22 on offense. The Chargers totaled 295 yards of offense, including a season-best 231 passing yards.
Las Vegas Raiders (Ashton Jeanty) at San Francisco 49ers – FOX, 2:25 p.m.
Jeanty caught three passes for 64 yards and added nine rushing yards in the Raiders' 38-33 win over the New York Jets. The second-year running back has a team-high 713 scrimmage yards this season and ranks second in the league with 312 yards after catch. He has 376 receiving yards, 82 more than any running back in the NFL.
Seattle Seahawks (George Holani, DeMarcus Lawrence) vs. Arizona Cardinals – FOX, 2:25 p.m.
Holani had a career-high 153 all-purpose yards in Seattle's 34-17 loss to the Chicago Bears, returning six kickoffs for 144 yards and adding a 9-yard rush. The third-year running back has 833 career all-purpose yards. Lawrence totaled three tackles while forcing and recovering a fumble, earning a team-high 76.3 Pro Football Focus defensive grade. He has forced 25 fumbles in his career, recovering 12.
MONDAY
Buffalo Bills (Khalil Shakir) at Minnesota Vikings – ABC/ESPN, 6:15 p.m.
Shakir totaled 64 yards on a team-high five receptions in Buffalo's dominant 36-10 win over the Baltimore Ravens. The fifth-year player is tied for the team lead with 27 receptions this season. He averages 10.1 yards after catch per reception, most in the league among NFL wide receivers with at least 20 targets. The Bills, who boast an AFC-best 6-1 record, lead the league in scoring offense (33.3 ppg) and rank second in total offense (393.1 ypg).
--BroncoSports.com--
