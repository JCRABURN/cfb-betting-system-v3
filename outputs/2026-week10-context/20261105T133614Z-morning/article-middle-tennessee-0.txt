Countdown to Kickoff Luncheon set for Friday ahead of Homecoming
11/4/2026 4:00:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
By:
@MTAthletics
MURFREESBORO, Tenn.
— Middle Tennessee football fans will have an opportunity to get an inside look at Saturday's conference matchup with WKU when the Blue Raiders host the Countdown to Kickoff Luncheon Friday, Nov. 6, at 11:45 a.m. in the SRM Concrete Club at Floyd Stadium.
The luncheon, held the day before every home game (due to the Thanksgiving holiday there will not be one before the final home game on Nov. 28), will feature an in-depth look at the upcoming matchup from head coach
Derek Mason
and running backs coach Al Poree.
Mason will break down video and discuss the Blue Raiders' preparation for WKU while also taking questions from those in attendance. Poree will provide additional insight into the Blue Raiders' offensive game plan and his strong stable of running backs as the Blue Raiders prepare for Homecoming.
Friday's guest alumni speaker will be Marvin Collier, a former Blue Raider player and Hall of Fame member, who works with State Farm Insurance. Collier played for MTSU from 1985 to 1988 under legendary coach Boots Donnelly and was part of the 1985 team that enjoyed an undefeated regular season and achieved a No. 1 national ranking.
The Countdown to Kickoff Luncheon provides Blue Raider fans with a unique opportunity to hear directly from members of the MTSU football staff while enjoying lunch and getting ready for Saturday's game at Floyd Stadium.
Admission is $10, which includes lunch. The program begins at 11:45 a.m., with the luncheon and football discussion continuing through approximately 12:45 p.m.
