Red Raiders in the NFL: Week 8
November 03, 2026
|
Football
Share:
Washington Jr. records first career interception as Brooks and Rodriguez combine for 19 tackles
By:
Jay Strader
Red Raiders in the NFL | 2026 Regular Season Week 8, Oct. 29-Nov. 2
LUBBOCK, Texas –
Broderick Washington recorded his first career interception against the Bills on Sunday for the Ravens while Jordyn Brooks and
Jacob Rodriguez
combined for 19 tackles against the Patriots.
A complete list of statistics by former Red Raiders in the NFL is below.
ARIZONA CARDINALS
at Dallas Cowboys (W, 33-30 OT)
S Dadrion Taylor-Demerson (2019-23):
Taylor-Demerson played 29 snaps on special teams and recorded a tackle in the Cardinals win.
Taylor-Demerson this week: 1 solo tackle
Taylor-Demerson this season: 5 GP / 2 GS | 13 tackles (10 solo)
WR
Reggie Virgil
(2025):
Virgil saw just two targets on 38 snaps this week as he made his first career start.
Virgil this week: 2 targets
Virgil this season: 4 GP / 1 GS | 2 receptions for 29 yards, LNG 16
BALTIMORE RAVENS
at Buffalo Bills (L, 36-10)
DL Broderick Washington Jr. (2016-19):
Washington recorded his first career interception against Josh Allen and the Bills and also recorded a tackle in 37 snaps.
Washington Jr. this week: 1 tackle, 1 INT
Washington Jr. this season: 6 GP / 3 GS | 7 tackles (2 solo), 2 PD, 1 INT
CAROLINA PANTHERS
at Green Bay Packers (L, 27-19)
DL
Lee Hunter
(2025):
Hunter played 22 of the 69 snaps on defense recording two tackles
Hunter this week: 2 tackles
Hunter this season: 7 GP / 5 GS | 16 tackles (6 solo), 1 QBH
CINCINNATI BENGALS
vs. Tennessee Titans (W, 43-36 OT)
RB Tahj Brooks (2020-24):
Brooks registered 17 snaps on special teams for the Bengals
Brooks this week: No stats
Brooks this season: 7 GP / 0 GS | 3 returns (76 yards), 3 carries for 6 yards | 2 special teams tackles
DALLAS COWBOYS
vs. Arizona Cardinals (L, 33-30 OT)
OL Terence Steele (2016-19):
Played all 74 snaps for the Cowboys making his eighth start of the season at the right tackle position.
Steele this week: No stats
Steele this season: 8 GP / 8 GS
DETROIT LIONS
vs. Minnesota Vikings (W, 19-15)
DL
Skyler Gill-Howard
(2026):
Gill-Howard played 27 of the 53 snaps on defense and recorded his first career solo sack as the Lions took down the undefeated Vikings on Sunday.
Gill-Howard this week: 3 tackles, 1 sack
Gill-Howard this season: 7 GP / 0 GS | 11 tackles (2 solo), 4 QBHs, 2.0 sack
KANSAS CITY CHIEFS
at Denver Broncos (L, 28-24)
QB Patrick Mahomes II (2014-16):
Mahomes II bounced back from a tough performance last week with a 321-yard passing day along with three touchdowns. The Chiefs, however, fell to the Broncos on the road.
Mahomes this week: 30-for-41, 321 yards, 3 TD | 2 rushes for 6 yards (long of 4)
Mahomes this season: 7 GP / 7 GS | 154-for-232, 1,795 yards, 12 TDs, 4 INTs, 1 rushing TD
LAS VEGAS RAIDERS
at New York Jets (W, 38-33)
OL Caleb Rogers (2020-24):
Rogers recorded two snaps on offense and six on special teams against the Jets.
Rogers this week: No stats
Rogers this season: 8 GP / 3 GS
MIAMI DOLPHINS
vs. New England Patriots (L, 24-19)
WR
Caleb Douglas
(2024-25):
Douglas caught four passes on six targets for 43 yards including a 19-yard catch and run.
Douglas this week: 4 catches for 43 yards on 6 targets.
Douglas this season: 4 GP / 4 GS | 12 receptions for 226 yards, 2 TDs
LB Jordyn Brooks (2016-19):
Brooks recorded a team-high 11 tackles, six of which were solo, while playing every snap for the defense.
Brooks this week: 11 tackles (6 solo)
Brooks this season: 7 GP / 7 GS | 63 tackles (28 solo), 2 TFLs, 1 PD, 1 QBH
LB
Jacob Rodriguez
(2023-25):
Rodriguez played every snap for the Dolphins recording eight tackles and forcing a fumble with the punch out move that Texas Tech fans became so familiar with last season.
Rodriguez this week: 8 tackles (2 solo), 1 FF
Rodriguez this season: 7 GP / 7 GS | 69 tackles (28 solo), 3 TFLs, 1 INT, 1 QBH, 1 FF
NEW ENGLAND PATRIOTS
at Miami Dolphins (W, 24-19)
QB
Behren Morton
(2021-25):
Morton was listed on the inactive list prior to the game as the emergency No. 3 quarterback.
Morton this week: No stats
Morton this season: 0 GP / 0 GS
NEW ORLEANS SAINTS
(Bye week)
DE Tyree Wilson (2020-22):
Wilson and the Saints return to action this week when they host the Cleveland Browns at noon on Sunday.
Wilson this week: No stats
Wilson this season: 5 GP / 0 GS | 15 tackles (7 solo), 1 sack
NEW YORK JETS
vs. Las Vegas Raiders (W, 38-33)
DE
David Bailey
(2025):
Bailey was ruled inactive against the Raiders with a groin injury.
Bailey this week: No stat.
Bailey this season: 7 GP / 7 GS | 34 tackles (21 solo), 9 QBHs, 7.0 TFLs, 5.5 sacks, 3 FF
P Austin McNamara (2020-23):
McNamara punted two times for 110 yards (55.0 avg) as his longest boot of the day went for 62 yards (season high). McNamara also had kickoff duties for the first time this season.
McNamara: 2 punts for 88 yards, 1 i20
McNamara this season: 8 GP / 0 GS | 31 punts for 1,466 (47.3) yards, long 62, 11 i20, 2 TB
WASHINGTON COMMANDERS
vs. Philadelphia Eagles (W, 27-10)
DB Tyler Owens (2022-23):
Owens played 22 snaps on special teams for the Commanders in their Sunday night win against the Eagles.
Owens this week: No stats
Owens this season: 6 GP/ 0 GS | 5 special teams tackles (4 solo)
SAN FRANCISCO 49ERS
(Bye week)
DL
Romello Height
(2025):
Height and the 49ers take on the Raiders this week at 3:25 p.m. in San Francisco.
Height this week: 2 tackles
Height this season: 6 GP / 5 GS | 8 tackles (3 solo), 5 QBHs, 1.5 sacks
Red Raiders on Practice Squads
Erik Ezukanma, WR (2018-21), Philadelphia Eagles
Josh Kelly, WR (2024), Houston Texans
Bralyn Lux, DB (2023-24), Cincinnati Bengals
Cole Wisniewski
, S (2025), Philadelphia Eagles
