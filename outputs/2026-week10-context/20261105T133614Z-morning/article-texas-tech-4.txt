Texas Tech announces sellout for West Virginia game
November 03, 2026
|
Football

Gameday Guide
Share:
Texas Tech will have its 13th-consecutive advanced sellout Saturday for its Celebrate America Game
LUBBOCK, Texas
– No. 13 Texas Tech will have another capacity crowd Saturday when the Red Raiders host West Virginia inside Galaxy Stadium. The athletics department announced Tuesday it has sold out of its primary ticket inventory for the 8:30 p.m. CT kick.
The advanced sellout is the sixth already for the 2026 season and continues Texas Tech's trend of capacity crowds at Galaxy Stadium. Texas Tech has now sold out of its primary ticket inventory for 13-consecutive home games dating back to the start of the 2025 season.
Once a game reaches sellout status, fans are encouraged to utilize
SeatGeek
, the official secondary ticket provider of Texas Tech Athletics. Texas Tech still has tickets available for the Nov. 26 regular-season finale versus TCU, which can be purchased online at
TexasTech.com
or by calling the Athletics Ticket Office at 806-742-TECH.
With another capacity crowd expected, fans are urged to download their tickets prior to arriving to Galaxy Stadium to ensure quick entry. Fans with tickets that are unable to attend Saturday should utilize the
Every Seat Counts
program, which is designed to ensure Galaxy Stadium is at full capacity each gameday. For a full listing of gameday protocols and timelines, please visit the
Gameday Guide
each week on TexasTech.com.
Kickoff between Texas Tech and West Virginia is slated for 8:30 p.m. with television coverage provided on ESPN.
