Owls’ Football at Tulsa Announced for 3:30 p.m. on ESPN+
11/2/2026 12:52:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
By:
Ben Paul
BOCA RATON, Fla.
– Florida Atlantic football will face American Conference foe Tulsa on the road Saturday, Nov. 14, kicking off at 3:30 p.m. ET on ESPN+ as announced by the league on Monday.
This will mark the sixth all-time meeting between the Owls (5-3, 2-2) and the Golden Hurricane (4-4, 1-3), with Florida Atlantic looking to grow the series lead to 4-1
FAU has defeated Tulsa in three consecutive matchups, most recently on Nov. 8, 2025 in a 40-21 victory. The Owls last won in Tulsa on Nov. 30, 2024, 63-19, the third-highest point total in a game in program history.
Before the following Saturday, Florida Atlantic will seek a home win on Thursday, Nov. 5, against RV UTSA. Kickoff is at 7:30 p.m. on ESPN. To purchase tickets, click
