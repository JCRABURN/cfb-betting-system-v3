Kemper Hodges earns National Football Foundation, Colorado Chapter Player of the Week honors for performance vs. UConn
11/3/2026 7:31:00 AM
|
Football
2026 NFFCC Week10 110326
Share:
Air Force senior quarterback
Kemper Hodges
earned National Football Foundation, Colorado Chapter, Player of the Week honors for his performance in Air Force's 35-26 win over UConn last week.
Hodges recorded career highs in every rushing category with 35 carries for 200 yards, four touchdowns and a long run of 62 yards for a touchdown. The 100-yard game is the first of his career and his 62-yard run is the longest play by a Falcon this season. Hodges is the first Falcon to rush for 200 yards since Emmanuel Michel had 203 on 35 carries vs. James Madison in 2023. He is also the first Falcon with four rushing touchdowns since Michel had four vs. James Madison in 2023. The four rushing touchdowns are tied as the third most in program history while his 35 carries are the eighth most and most since Michel's 35 vs. James Madison in 2023. Hodges' first touchdown put the Falcons on the board and gave them the lead for good while his final touchdown came with 3:09 left and make it a two-score game. Besides his four touchdowns, Hodges had 11 carries for first downs, including three on fourth down. Hodges is the first Air Force quarterback with a 200-yard rushing game since Arion Worthman had 215 vs. San Jose State in 2016. His 200-yard game is the 20th program history and 8th by a quarterback. He is just the fourth quarterback in program history with a 200-yard game (Dee Dowis, Beau Morgan, Arion Worthman).
Hodges also earned Mountain West offensive player of the week honors. Both awards are the first of his career. He is the third Falcon to earn the award this season, joining
Liam Szarka
and
Evan Medders
.
