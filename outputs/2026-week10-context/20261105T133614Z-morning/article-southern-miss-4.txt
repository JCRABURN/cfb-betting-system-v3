Second Annual Turkey Toss in Conjunction with South Alabama Football Game
11/2/2026 8:53:00 AM
|
Football
Registration Link
Share:
HATTIESBURG, Miss. --
Southern Miss Athletics will host its 2nd Annual Turkey Toss cornhole tournament ahead of the Golden Eagles' football game against South Alabama on Saturday, Nov. 21.
Throughout the tournament, there will be the chance to win prizes through a raffle ticket that each participant will receive. The winning team will receive a trophy, custom-made Southern Miss cornhole boards, and on-field recognition during the game. Additionally, the winning team will receive a cash prize to be split between members and the opportunity to watch the team run out to the field prior to kickoff.
Registration is now open to fill the 64-team field. Each team will consist of two competitors, with matches taking place in Spirit Park. Exact start times will be relayed to teams that sign up on Monday, Nov. 16, after kickoff times have been announced.
Registration costs $150 for members of the public, while students and Southern Miss football season ticket holders can register for just $100. Each team will receive two t-shirts for their competitors to wear during their games and four tickets to Saturday's game, with the opportunity to purchase additional tickets. Light refreshments will be provided.
Registration will close on Nov. 13, or when all 64 slots are filled.
