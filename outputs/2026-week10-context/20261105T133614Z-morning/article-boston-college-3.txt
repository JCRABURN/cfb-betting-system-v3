Eagles' Clough Nominated For Burlsworth Trophy
November 03, 2026
|
Football
Share:
Tight end is one of nine ACC nominees for the award
By:
Athletic Communications
CHESTNUT HILL, Mass.
–
Boston College tight end
Brady Clough
was named a 2026 Burlsworth Trophy nominee, the organization announced Tuesday.
In three seasons on The Heights, Clough has appeared in 10 games, contributing as a reserve tight end and on special teams. In 2026, the Scarborough, Maine, native has earned three starts at tight end and recorded career highs in receptions (4) and receiving yards (31) at Cincinnati on Sept. 5.
In its 17th year, the Burlsworth Trophy honors the most outstanding player in college football who began his career as a walk-on. The award is named in honor of Brandon Burlsworth, the former University of Arkansas walk-on who defied the odds to become a first-team All-American offensive lineman and the 63rd overall selection in the 1999 NFL Draft.
The Burlsworth Trophy Selection Committee will announce semifinalists on Nov. 10 and three finalists on Nov. 17. The winner will be announced at the annual Burlsworth Trophy Award Show on December 7 at Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art in Bentonville, Arkansas.
