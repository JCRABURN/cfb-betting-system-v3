SAN JOSE, Calif. -
This Week in San José State Athletics returns to catch you up on last week's action and see what's ahead for the Spartans!

Football
This Week:
The Spartans return home to face New Mexico on Saturday, Oct. 31 at 1:00 p.m. The game will be featured on NBC Sports Bay Area and SJSU Spartans Sports Media Network.
Last Week:
The Spartans dropped their game to Nevada, 28-13.

Women's Basketball
This Week:
The Spartans will host an exhibition game against Cal St. East Bay at the Provident Credit Union Event Center on Wednesday, Oct. 28 at 7:00 p.m. Admission will be free to all!
Upcoming Schedule

Cross Country
This Week:
The Spartans will participate in the 2026 Credit Union 1 Mountain West Championships presented by Palms Casino Resort on Friday, Oct. 30.
Upcoming Schedule
Oct. 30 - MW Championships, Washoe County Golf Course, Reno, Nev.

This Week:
The Spartans will face Santa Clara on the road on Saturday at noon.

The Spartans hosted the Julian Fraser Memorial and won all three of their matches.

The Spartans dropped their match to Air Force, 3-0.
