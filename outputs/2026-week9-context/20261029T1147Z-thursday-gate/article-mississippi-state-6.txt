#STATEtoSundays: Week 7 Update
October 27, 2026
|
Football
Share:
By:
Logan Lowery, Assistant Director/Communications
Dak Prescott guided Dallas to a 38-21 victory at Philadelphia on Monday Night Football this week. In doing so, Prescott surpassed Cowboys legend Roger Staubach for the second-most wins in franchise history with 86.
The Mississippi State great needs eight more victories to match Troy Aikman's record for all-time wins in Dallas.
Prescott threw for 212 yards, three touchdowns and no interceptions against the Eagles marking his 80
th
career game with multiple TD throws. That milestone passed Tony Romo for the most in Cowboys history.
Prescott tossed two touchdowns to CeeDee Lamb of 9 and 16 yards and hit George Pickens on a 21-yard scoring strike. He also added to his huge night by running in a two-point conversion late in the third quarter.
Speaking of running, Woody Marks started in the backfield and led Houston in rushing for the second straight week. Marks carried 19 times for 66 yards and now has 271 yards and two touchdowns on the ground this season.
Elgton Jenkins started at center for Cleveland and once again logged all 70 offensive snaps.
Martin Emerson (Saints) led the way for former Bulldogs in the NFL defensively with five tackles. Jeffery Simmons (Titans) followed up with four tackles while Emmanuel Forbes (Rams) and Jaden Crumedy (Texans) each recorded a pair of stops.
