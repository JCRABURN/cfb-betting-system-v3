Boise State Invites Bronco Nation to Celebrate Halloween on The Blue
10/26/2026 1:50:00 PM
|
Football, General, Bronco Athletic Association
Share:
Costumes, trunk-or-treat and statue unveiling to highlight inaugural The Blue After Dark football game on Saturday
BOISE, Idaho
– Boise State Athletics invites fans of all ages to embrace the Halloween spirit with costumes, candy and plenty of Bronco fun when Boise State hosts Texas State on Saturday, Oct. 31, at Albertsons Stadium. Kickoff is scheduled for 8 p.m.
The Halloween celebration will mark the inaugural The Blue After Dark game, a conference nickname for late‑night games. Fans are encouraged to wear black and the first 3,000 Boise State students will receive an exclusive, custom-designed game day t-shirt. During halftime, the Keith Stein Blue Thunder Marching Band and Spirit Squad will perform, incorporating new lighting elements. Immediately following the band's performance, the ceremony to unveil the Kellen Moore
bronze statue
will occur on The Blue.
"Bronco Nation, we're inviting all tailgaters to participate in our Trunk-or-Treat event and help make Halloween special for the kids this Saturday," said Boise State Director of Athletics
Jeramiah Dickey
. "It's about 6 games, 6 sellouts. Everything counts and everything matters. We believe in EPIC and encourage Bronco Nation to embrace the opportunity and help us make this a Halloween to remember. Whether you're handing out candy at your tailgate, bringing your little Broncos trunk-or-treating, or cheering us on inside Albertsons Stadium, we want everyone to be part of the fun and what should be a very special night."
With Halloween falling on game day, Boise State is encouraging fans to go all out, turning the tailgate lots around Albertsons Stadium into a festive trunk-or-treat experience. Fans are invited to dress in their favorite costumes, decorate their tailgate spaces and bring individually wrapped candy to share with fellow Broncos, creating a fun and memorable Halloween celebration for the entire family. The festivities begin well before kickoff, with parking lots opening at noon and stadium gates opening at 6 p.m., giving fans plenty of time to enjoy Halloween activities and the game day atmosphere. Fans interested in participating in the trunk-or-treat are encouraged to register in advance.
Register here by Wednesday, Oct. 28 at 4 p.m. (MT).
The Halloween fun continues at the Ford Fan Zone, located at the Hall of Fame Plaza and DeChevrieux Field. Free and open to all fans, the Ford Fan Zone offers a family-friendly gathering place before the Broncos take the field. The space features the Kids Zone presented by Regence BlueShield of Idaho. The Ford Fan Zone opens four hours before kickoff along Bronco Lane, between DeChevrieux Field and the Larry and Marianne Williams Plaza. It offers a variety of activities for young Broncos fans, including yard games like cornhole and spikeball, hassle-free tailgating, and Bronco-themed photo backdrops. Additionally, fans can pick up their exclusive "Sticker of the Game" while supplies last;
please note that only one sticker is allowed per person.
Fans are encouraged to keep the Halloween spirit alive inside Albertsons Stadium as well. Costumes are welcome both outside and inside the stadium,
but any mask or face covering must be removed when passing through the security checkpoints at all gates.
Fans may also bring unopened, individually wrapped candy into the stadium to share in the celebration. Boise State's clear bag policy remains in effect, and only clear bags or containers are permitted inside Albertsons Stadium.
Fans bringing candy in non-clear containers will need to check those containers at bag check before entering the stadium. Clear containers will be available for fans who check their candy containers.
