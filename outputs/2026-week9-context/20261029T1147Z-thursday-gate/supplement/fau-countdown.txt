By:
Ben Paul
DENTON, Texas
— Florida Atlantic football is on the ESPN flagship station for a Thursday night matchup, facing North Texas on the road, Oct. 29. Kickoff at DATCU Stadium is at 7:30 p.m. ET.
This marks the Owls' fourth appearance on the main ESPN network and first in a regular season game since 2018. Fans can also listen to the call on the Florida Atlantic Sports Network (FOX Sports 640 locally).
FAU last played on Thursday night in 2024 and has played 13 Thursday games across its 26 seasons. The first win against an FBS opponent occurred on a Thursday, with Florida Atlantic defeating Middle Tennessee, 20-19, on Aug. 28, 2003.
Both Florida Atlantic (5-2, 2-1) and the Mean Green (5-2, 3-0) boast nationally ranked offenses with each in the top 20 in points scored, completion percentage, passing yards per game, and total offense per game.
In their most recent outing, the Owls came out on top in a 51-49 shootout over Rice behind a historic performance by fifth-year WR
Easton Messer
, who totaled a program-record tying five touchdowns to go with 262 all-purpose yards. His stat line of two rushing touchdowns, two receiving touchdowns, and a punt return score is the only instance by an NCAA player in at least 20 seasons.
For his efforts, Messer won American Offensive Player of the Week, the Paul Hornung Award National Player of the Week, and the Burlsworth Trophy Walk-On of the Week. He sits as the American Conference leader in receptions per game (8.29) as well as receiving yards (709), and tied for second touchdowns (six). His teammate, senior
Kelby Valsin
is ranked No. 2 in the league for each of those categories (7.71 receptions per game for 679 yards and six touchdowns).
Graduate QB
Caden Veltkamp
is in the national rankings as well, leading the FBS in completions per game (27.1) and ranking fourth in percentage (73.1%).
Florida Atlantic's defense continues to feature standout performances from fifth-year LB
Joseph Sipp Jr.
(49 tackles, two sacks) and graduate DL
Blake Burris
(31 tackles, three for a loss) while the Owls' special teams remain one of the nation's best, currently standing as the FBS leader with four combined touchdowns between kick and punt returns.
The Owls and the Mean Green have faced off 15 previous times, with FAU holding an 8-7 series lead.
