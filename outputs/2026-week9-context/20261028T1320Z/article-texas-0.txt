No. 19/18 Football’s Kanu named Jason Witten Collegiate Man of the Year semifinalist
10.21.2026
|
Football
Jason Witten Collegiate Man of the Year semifinalists
Share:
It marks the third-straight season Texas has had a semifinalist for the award.
By:
Grace Tafolla
AUSTIN, Texas –
No. 19/18 Texas Football DL
Hero Kanu
was named one of 20 semifinalists for the Jason Witten Collegiate Man of the Year Award, the SCORE Foundation announced Wednesday.
Compiled by a subset of the Jason Witten Collegiate Man of the Year Selection Committee, the semifinalists have all demonstrated a record of leadership by exhibiting exceptional courage, integrity and sportsmanship both on and off the field.
It marks the third-straight season Texas has had a semifinalist for the Jason Witten Collegiate Man of the Year Award. Last season, former UT DB
Michael Taaffe
was selected as a semifinalist for the award, while former UT center Jake Majors was tabbed a semifinalist in 2024. Former Texas quarterback Sam Ehlinger was named the Jason Witten Collegiate Man of the Year in 2021.
Over the past two years, Kanu has hosted eight free football camps in Germany and France to inspire young people ranging from six-year-olds to young adults, teaching them the game of American football as well as the discipline, dedication and work ethic necessary to succeed as student-athletes, with the hope they can one day follow in his footsteps and earn a college scholarship. Inspired by the Kicks for Kids program, he donated more than 100 pairs of shoes to local children in need and participated in promotional efforts to raise awareness of the charitable initiative. Kanu has volunteered at the Boys & Girls Club of Austin through Longhorn great and board member Jamaal Charles and served as a celebrity waiter at an event supporting College Football Hall of Famer Derrick Johnson's Defend the Dream Foundation, which provides low-income and inner-city youth with the opportunities and resources to reach their full potential in school and in life. Kanu has sponsored two aspiring student-athletes - one from Germany and one from Nigeria - on trips to America to attend college camps and pursue their athletic and educational dreams, just like he did. Kanu helps support club teams in Germany by providing access to minivans for travel to 9U and 11U flag football games across the country. In support of a UT student organization, Kanu provided free ice cream to students for two hours on campus to help raise awareness for the group and its community activities. Kanu is passionate about growing the game of American football and expanding flag football for opportunities for boys and girls around the world.
Kanu grew up in Geltendorf, Germany, a small community of just over 1,000 residents less than one hour west of Munich. His favorite sport growing up was soccer, where he played goalkeeper and center back at a high-level from age six to 16. As he continued to grow, Kanu eventually became too big for soccer and began transitioning to football. Kanu was first introduced to American football as a 15-year-old in Germany in 2019 by Brandon Collier, a former college football player who founded the international scouting and training organization Premier Prospects International (PPI). Kanu trained with Collier and PPI before taking a huge leap toward pursuing his football dreams and moving to California in October 2020 as a high school junior. Kanu spoke broken English, had never played organized football and left his family and friends behind in Germany for the opportunity to eventually play college football in America. Kanu played organized football for the first time in the spring of 2021. His parents and siblings have been extremely supportive of his decision to leave home and chase his dream of playing football in America. When he first arrived in the U.S., he lived with a host family in Ladera Ranch, Calif., -Seana and Michael Pierce and their son, Evan - and remains very close with them today.
Kanu is a second-year player who has played in 18 games with 17 starts across two seasons at Texas after transferring from Ohio State in spring 2025. In six starts this season, Kanu has recorded 16 tackles (seven solo), 3.5 tackles for loss, a 0.5 sack, one pass breakup and four quarterback hurries. His four quarterback hurries tie for the team lead while his 3.5 tackles for loss rank third on the team.
Prior to the 2026 season, Kanu garnered preseason Fourth-Team All-America and Second-Team All-SEC honors from Phil Steele. Kanu was also named a finalist for the 2026 William V. Campbell Trophy on Sept. 23.
Kanu will graduate in December 2026 with a Communication & Leadership degree and holds a 3.42 cumulative GPA. Kanu was selected to the 2025-26 SEC Academic Honor Roll and was a 2025 member of the Texas Student-Athlete Advisory Committee.
Three finalists will be named for the award on Tuesday, Dec. 15. The winner will be announced at the award ceremony at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, on March 11, 2027.
Kanu and the Longhorns return to action against No. 7/7 Ole Miss at 6:49 p.m. CT Saturday, Oct. 24, at DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium. The game will broadcast on ABC with Chris Fowler, Kirk Herbstreit and Holly Rowe on the call.
