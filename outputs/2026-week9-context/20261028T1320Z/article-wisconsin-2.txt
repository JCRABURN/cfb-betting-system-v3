October 26, 2026
|

Wisconsin edge rusher turned in a dominant performance against USC
By:
Lydia Picotte
MADISON, Wis. –
Wisconsin OLB
Sebastian Cheeks
has been named the Big Ten Defensive Player of the Week, announced Monday by the conference. The Badger edge rusher turned in an impressive performance in Wisconsin's 24-7 home win over No. 16 USC on Saturday night.
The fifth-year senior tallied five total tackles, including 2.5 sacks and 3.5 tackles for loss. He contributed to a defensive that held the Trojans to -18 rushing yards and 239 total yards, their lowest output since 2018. Cheeks has posted 34 pressures this season, per PFF, the most in the Big Ten.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Wisconsin Football (@badgerfootball)
This week, No. 25 Wisconsin (5-2, 3-1 Big Ten) will travel to Iowa City to take on Iowa (5-2, 2-2) in a Halloween matchup. Kickoff is set for Saturday, October 31 at 11 a.m. CT inside Kinnick Stadium. The game will air live on FOX.
