Former Head Coach June Jones Passes Away
Oct 29
|
Football
Share:
SMU Athletics mourns the passing of former head football coach June Jones.
Coach Jones helped restore a winning tradition on the Hilltop, leading the Mustangs to their first bowl appearance in 25 years and four consecutive bowl games.
Our thoughts are with his family, friends and the countless players and coaches whose lives he touched.
