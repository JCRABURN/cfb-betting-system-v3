NFL #ProDucks: Week 7
10/27/26
|
Football
Share:
By:
Drew Tinson
Bucky Irving and Tez Johnson each found the end zone on Sunday, powering the Buccaneers' offense to a win with its highest point total of the season. Bo Nix and the Broncos secured their third consecutive victory, highlighted by Nix's standout touchdown scramble.
Tampa Bay Buccaneers 43, Carolina Panthers 13
Irving and Johnson both had huge weeks in a rout of the Panthers. Irving rushed for 106 yards and two touchdowns on 24 carries, adding one reception for four yards. Johnson found the end zone for the first time this season on a 14-yard catch late in the second quarter. He finished with two receptions for 33 yards.

October 25, 2026
Detroit Lions 20, Green Bay Packers 17
Penei Sewell delivered another impressive performance on the Lions' offensive line, allowing no pressures, quarterback hits or sacks. He earned a pass-blocking grade of 86.4 according to Pro Football Focus (PFF).
Evan Williams continued to make his presence felt for the Packers' defense, recording nine tackles and a pass defended. Jordon Riley also played for Green Bay, finishing with one tackle.
Denver Broncos 30, Arizona Cardinals 16
Nix and the Broncos improved to 5-2 on the season. Nix completed 15-of-29 passes for 222 yards and a touchdown. His biggest play of the game came on a 62-yard scramble, the longest touchdown run by a quarterback since 2017. He finished with seven carries for 73 yards and a touchdown.
Troy Franklin and Alex Forsyth both played for the Broncos.
Jordan Burch made one tackle for the Cardinals.

October 25, 2026
Chicago Bears 30, New England Patriots 24
Dillon Thieneman
and Noah Sewell both played for the Chicago defense. Thieneman finished with eight tackles and half a sack, while Sewell added two tackles.
Christian Gonzalez made four tackles for the Patriots.
Atlanta Falcons 29, San Francisco 49ers 20
Brandon Dorlus made two tackles in the win over San Francisco, helping hand the 49ers their first loss of the season.
Deommodore Lenoir finished with six tackles for the 49ers. Jordan James contributed on special teams, returning four kicks for 119 yards. James added one carry for six yards and 11 receiving yards on one catch.
New York Giants 27, Houston Texans 20
Kayvon Thibodeaux and Niko Reed both played for the Giants' defense. Thibodeaux finished with four tackles, while Reed made one.
Minnesota Vikings 16, Indianapolis Colts 10
DeForest Buckner made four tackles for the Colts.
Pittsburgh Steelers 16, New Orleans Saints 10
Juwan Johnson caught one pass for seven yards for the Saints.
Miami Dolphins 28, New York Jets 25
Kenyon Sadiq
caught one pass for six yards in the AFC East showdown against Miami.
Los Angeles Rams 35, Las Vegas Raiders 21
Jackson Powers-Johnson and
Malik Benson
both saw action for the Raiders.
Dallas Cowboys 38, Philadelphia Eagles 21
T.J. Bass and Johnny Mundt both saw action in the primetime NFC East showdown. Bass played for the Cowboys, while Mundt suited up for the Eagles.
