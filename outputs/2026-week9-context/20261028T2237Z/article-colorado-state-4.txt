GAME NOTES
Saturday will be the 81st meeting with Utah State. Making it one of the longest played series in CSU history, behind the Border War with Wyoming.
The series began in 1902 and has spanned six conferences -- Rocky Mountain Athletic Conference, Mountain States/Skyline Conference, Western Athletic Conference, Mountain West and now in the rebuilt Pac-12.
Colorado State leads the overall series 40-38-2 with the Rams winning the last meeting in 2024 in Fort Collins, 42-37, after a 29-point fourth-quarter comeback.
Saturday's game will fall on Halloween, which CSU has played 15 times prior, including facing Utah State twice with victories.
CSU is 5-15 all time on Halloween, with just one of those wins coming since the 1970s.
