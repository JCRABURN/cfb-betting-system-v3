Oregon Relieves Offensive Coordinator Mehringer of Duties
10/25/26
|
Football
Share:
EUGENE, Ore. —
Oregon offensive coordinator
Drew Mehringer
has been relieved of his duties, head coach
Dan Lanning
announced Sunday morning.
"Drew has been a valued member of this staff since 2022, and I am appreciative of everything he's done for Oregon," said Lanning. "He played a tremendous role in what this program has accomplished over the last several seasons. This was a decision I felt was in the best interest of our football program, as I believe a new direction is necessary to move our offense forward. I thank Drew for his efforts and wish him all the best."
Mehringer joined the Oregon staff in 2022 as the tight ends coach, and he added co-offensive coordinator duties ahead of the 2025 season.
