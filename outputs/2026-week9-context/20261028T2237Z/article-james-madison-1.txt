Scott Collects Midseason All-America Honors
10/21/2026 11:19:00 AM
|
Football
Scott Bio
The Sporting News Midseason All-America Team
Share:
HARRISONBURG, Va. –
James Madison wide receiver
Michael Scott
has been named to The Sporting News Midseason All-America Team, the organization announced on Wednesday.
Scott earned a spot on the team as a return specialist. The York, Pennsylvania, native is the nation's leader in combined return yards, with 685. He also ranks No. 1 nationally in kick returns (38.8) and No. 2 in punt returns (20.0).
Additionally, Scott is one of four players in the country with two punt return touchdowns this season, and he has logged kick returns of 92, 76 and 72 yards.
Scott ranks No. 12 nationally in all-purpose yards (138.0), which included a 303-yard effort against Wagner on Sept. 12, and 196 against Georgia State on Oct. 17.
Offensively, Scott has added 19 catches for 241 yards and two touchdowns on the year.
Along with The Sporting News, Scott also has received midseason All-America recognition from various outlets such as Sports Illustrated, on3, CBS Sports and Athlon Sports.
The Sporting News is one of five organizations used to determine consensus All-America status.
