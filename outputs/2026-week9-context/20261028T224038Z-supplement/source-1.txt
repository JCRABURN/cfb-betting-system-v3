Live Stats
ALBUQUERQUE, N.M. –
The Lobos, 6-1 overall and 3-0 in the Mountain West, head west to try to exorcise a pretty big demon. The Lobos travel to CEFCU Stadium to take on the San Jose State Spartans at 1 p.m. Pacific (2 p.m. Mountain Time).
The game is UNM's final MW+ broadcast, and fans can sign up for a subscription at golobos.com/watch. By signing up there, UNM reaps the benefit of your subscription as the money goes directly back to the athletic department.
San Jose State is 4-4 overall and 2-2 in the Mountain West. The Spartans are three wins away from bowl eligibility, having played 13 games. The Lobos reached bowl eligibility last game with a 21-16 win over North Dakota State, but they'll be attempting something that hasn't happened in 57 years: a win in San Jose. UNM's last road win against the Spartans was in 1969.
New Mexico is the second team listed in the receiving votes category in the AP Poll, and the fourth team listed in the Coaches Poll. UNM has never been ranked in the AP Poll, and spent one week at No. 25 in the Coaches Poll in 1997. UNM has the nation's No. 1 scoring defense and the No. 5 overall defense, but San Jose State ranks third with 29 sacks.

FAST FIVE
• New Mexico is 3-0 in the Mountain West for the first time in its history.
• The Lobos have won nine straight conference games, tying for the longest current conference winning streak in the FBS, and it is the longest in school history.
• UNM has won a weekly conference honor five straight games.
• UNM is the only team in the nation with multiple takeaways in every game they have played this season.
• The Lobos lead the nation with 86 points off turnovers.
