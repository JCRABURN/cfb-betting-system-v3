Florida Atlantic Hosts UTSA on ESPN
10/26/2026 1:01:00 PM
|
Football
Purchase Tickets Nov. 5 vs. UTSA
Share:
By:
Ben Paul
BOCA RATON, Fla. –
Florida Atlantic football will air on the ESPN flagship station for the second time in as many weeks, hosting UTSA on Howard Schnellenberger Field at Flagler Credit Union Stadium, Thursday, Nov. 5. Kickoff is now scheduled for 7:30 p.m., moved up 30 minutes from its previously announced time.
The Owls (5-2, 2-1) will face a Roadrunners team (7-1, 4-0) that is on top of the American Conference standings. FAU currently holds a 3-2 series advantage against UTSA.
This marks the first Florida Atlantic Thursday home game since 2006 and the first time in Boca Raton at Flagler Credit Union Stadium. Thursday's game will be the sixth nationally televised game of the 2026 season, the most in program history during the regular season, with a designation for the final three regular season games to be announced at a later date.
Florida Atlantic has played on ESPN four previous times, with Thursday, Oct. 29 at North Texas being the first since the 2020 Montgomery Bowl.
The UTSA at FAU game will be sponsored by Publix and will be Paradise Appreciation/Vice Night. To purchase tickets, click

FOLLOW THE OWLS
Inside the Owls' Burrow airs on The Spot, South Florida 39 (WSFL-TV), will air the show weekly on Thursdays at 5:30 p.m. in the Miami-Ft. Lauderdale DMA with replays running to be determined. The Spot, South Florida 9 (WHDT-TV), will air the show on Thursday at 6 p.m. in the West Palm Beach-Fort Pierce DMA with replays to be determined.
Florida Atlantic's Coaches Show, presented by Duffy's Sports Grill, will be on Tuesday, Oct. 27 from 6-7 p.m. at the Duffy's Sports Grill on St. Andrew's Boulevard in Boca Raton.
Fans can join in person or listen live on FOX Sports South Florida 640 AM. Hagerty Family Head Football Coach
Zach Kittley
will be joined by Tight Ends Coach
Jajuan Dulaney
as well as Head Men's Basketball Coach John Jakus.
