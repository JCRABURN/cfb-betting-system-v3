'Cuse and Clemson Tabbed for Noon Kickoff
10/26/2026 2:00:00 PM
|
Football
Get Tickets
Share:
Syracuse will have back-to-back games on ESPN2, as ESPN and the Atlantic Coast Conference announced the Orange's Nov. 7 matchup with Clemson will be on ESPN2 as at 12 p.m. kickoff.
The Orange host No. 19 SMU on Saturday in the same timeslot before the Tigers come to town a week later. Tickets for both matchups are on sale at
Cuse.com/FootballTickets
.
You won't want to miss Saturday's game against SMU as in addition to an ACC showdown with a ranked opponent, plenty of Halloween festivities await Orange Nation. In addition to the enhancements made to 'Cuse on the Quad and in-game entertainment already this season, Saturday will also feature
Trick-or-Treating during 'Cuse on the Quad
and a kids costume parade after the NYE Auto Quad Walk along the same route. Inside the building there will be
Suites 4 Treats
, a Halloween photobooth in the flex space and a $30 Dome concession special that includes four hot dogs, four drinks and two popcorns.
Secure your seats today at
Cuse.com/FootballTickets
!
