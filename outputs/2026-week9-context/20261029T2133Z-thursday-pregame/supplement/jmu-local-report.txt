JMU seeks bounce-back against Troy in rematch of last season’s Sun Belt title game

(WHSV) - A week after a crushing road loss at App State, James Madison is eager to wipe the slate clean.
The Dukes return home to Bridgeforth Stadium on Thursday night, looking to bounce back against a familiar Sun Belt Conference rival.
“I think it’s a really good opportunity for us to come back and play the ball that we know and love,” offensive lineman Zach Greenberg said. “It’s an exciting opportunity for everyone to respond.”
Standing in JMU’s way is Troy, an opponent the Dukes faced less than a year ago. The Trojans travel to Harrisonburg having lost three of their last four games, though they appear to be getting healthier at the right time. Quarterback Goose Crowder and wide receiver TK Keyes have both battled injuries recently, but each is available to play Thursday.
Head coach Billy Napier noted Troy’s identity remains tied to its physical ground attack, regardless of who is under center.
“The issues are relative to the issues they’ve had at quarterback, in my opinion. Napier said. “I think that’s had a lot to do with their struggles. They’re committed to the run game. It’s a vertical, physical run game. They’re going to challenge their gap integrity. That’s the center of what they do. I do think that’ll be part of the challenge, matching their physicality.”
To counter Troy’s downhill attack, the Dukes need to generate more push up front. JMU failed to record a single sack in last week’s loss at App State and has struggled to consistently generate pressure on opposing passers this season.
Defensive coordinator Robert Bala emphasized the need for steady improvement up front.
“We are who we are,” Bala said. “We’ve got to continue to get better and keep sharpening the sword on what we do good, and keep working on the things we’ve got to do better at.”
On the other side of the ball, JMU’s offense enters Thursday looking to establish a run game that sputtered a week ago. With leading rusher George Pettaway sidelined, the Dukes will rely on their backfield depth to carry the load against a Troy defense that has shown flashes against high-level competition.
“They have been pretty good defensively,” offensive coordinator Cam Aiken said. “Flipped on the Missouri tape earlier in the year and had a real good game against those guys. Just a unit that’s going to come ready to play.”
Kickoff is scheduled for 7:30 p.m. Thursday at Bridgeforth Stadium.
Availability Report
In addition to Pettaway’s absence, JMU will be without wide receiver Smoke Collins, defensive back Jayon Harvey, and linebacker Jayden Studio Thursday night. All three players were
ruled out on the Sun Belt Conference availability report.
