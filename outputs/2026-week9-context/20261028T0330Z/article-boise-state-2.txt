Broncos Move up to No. 20 in AP Poll, Remain No. 22 in Coaches Poll
10/25/2026 12:05:00 PM
|
Football
Share:
BOISE, Idaho
– Following its 31-10 victory over Washington State on Saturday, Boise State moved up one position to No. 20 in the Associated Top 25 Poll. The Broncos (6-1, 3-0 Pac-12) remained at No. 22 in the US LBM Coaches Poll.
The Broncos received 422 points in the AP Poll, tying Northwestern for the 20th ranking. It was an increase from the 295 points they earned a week ago. Boise State garnered 396 points in the Coaches Poll, up from the 266 points tallied a week prior.
Boise State is the only Group of Six team represented in either poll. Oregon State is the only Pac-12 team that is receiving votes in either poll. The Beavers tallied 10 points in the AP Poll and 15 in the Coaches Poll.
The Broncos scored 21 unanswered points in the fourth quarter to pull away against the Cougars on Saturday.
Dylan Riley
finished with 128 yards and two touchdowns on 13 carries to help seal the victory. Boise State's defense notched four sacks and seven TFLs in the win.
The win was the team's sixth straight victory, clinching bowl eligibility for the 29th consecutive season, the second-longest active streak in the nation. The Broncos have been ranked in 22 of the last 25 seasons.
Boise State will return to Albertson Stadium to host Texas State on Saturday, Oct. 31. Kickoff is scheduled for 8 p.m. MT and will be broadcast nationally on The CW.
