GAME NOTES
Saturday will be the 39th meeting with San Diego State. The two played last season in San Diego with the Aztecs winning 45-24.
SDSU leads the all-time series 23-15.
Colorado State won the last meeting in Canvas Stadium in 2023, 22-19.
Both team's defense are in the bottom 10 percent in the country - allowing a combined 979.3 yards/game.
Colorado State's offense is in the top quarter of the country - racking up 445.3 yards/game, while the Aztecs are at the bottom with 292.9 yards/game.
The game will be the first Pac-12 game between the two schools who joined the league after playing in the Mountain West.
