Halloween Fun Planned for Syracuse vs. SMU
10/27/2026 12:10:00 PM
|
Football
Get Tickets
Game Day Central
Quad Information
Share:
Syracuse and SMU will kickoff at Noon on Halloween and Orange fans of all ages will have plenty of holiday-themed entertainment, in addition to watching an ACC battle against a ranked Mustangs squad.
'Cuse on the Quad:
Young Orange fans can participate in their own costumed version of the NYE Auto Quad Walk. Shortly after the team walks their traditional pregame route into the JMA Wireless Dome (9:40 a.m.), there will be a costume parade along the same route. Kids will get the opportunity to walk the same route the team does and be cheered on by Orange Nation as they showcase their Halloween costumes.
'Cuse on the Quad will also feature trick-or-treating. Stop by the marketing table and
collect your map
and bag (which adheres to the Dome's clear bag policy and can be brought into the venue) for trick-or-treat locations with candy.
The Orange Uniform Showcase on the Quad will also be Halloween themed this week, so fans can take pictures and create family memories at the photo station.
These activities come in addition to the usual fare of fan-fun and enhancements to 'Cuse on the Quad this season, including: a large video board showing ESPN's College Gameday to get set for the action, live music, additional seating and gathering areas, food trucks, the Toyota Fun Zone with family-friendly activities, lawn games and inflatables and the Beak & Skiff Adventure Park with a rock climbing wall and mechanical bull.
Orange teams – including the women's hockey program which is off to its best start in program history – will be signing autographs on the Quad.
Inside the Dome:
The trick-or-treating fun doesn't stop outside the building. From 10:30 – 11:30 a.m., Syracuse Athletics will host "Suite-4-Treats." Participating suites inside the JMA Wireless Dome will be handing out candy to trick-or-treaters once gates open.
Check out the Suite-4-Treats map for more information.
The Flex Space behind the student section in the 100-level concourse will be Halloween themed this week, with a photo booth so fans in costumes or Orange gear can create custom photos to celebrate the day.
Concession Specials:
Miron Victory Court will have a unique Halloween themed 'spooky' cocktail special and food offerings.
Families can also take advantage of a special concession offer, featuring four hot dogs, four soft drinks and two popcorns for just $30.
Otto Helmets:
Even the football team will take part in the dress up. For the first time, the beloved mascot Otto will be on the team's helmets.
