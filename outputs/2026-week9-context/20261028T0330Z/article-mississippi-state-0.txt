State Drops 23-15 Decision To Sooners
October 24, 2026
|
Football
Box Score
Photo Gallery
Share:
STARKVILLE –
In the first-ever meeting between the two schools, Mississippi State fell 23-15 to Oklahoma on Saturday at Davis Wade Stadium. The Sooners scored the game’s first 17 points to take control on the way to claiming victory.
1st Quarter
11:10 - Oklahoma got on the board first with a 42-yard field goal. Sooners took a 3-0 lead.
2nd Quarter
0:39 - Oklahoma added to its lead just before halftime with a 36-yard touchdown pass. The Sooners increased their advantage to 10-0.
3rd Quarter
13:44 - Oklahoma wasted no time coming out of the locker room. The Sooners drove 75 yards on just three plays for a touchdown that made the score 17-0.
6:55 - Mississippi State punched back and regained some momentum with a little trickeration. A double pass resulted in tight end Sam West throwing to Camden Capehart for an 18-yard touchdown toss. Oklahoma's lead was cut to 17-7.
3:33 - Oklahoma used a 30-yard field goal to go up 20-7.
4th Quarter
4:24 - Oklahoma made it 23-7 with another field goal, this one a 38-yarder.
2:10 - Billy Puckett connected with Sanfrisco Magee for a 42-yard touchdown strike. After a successful two-point conversion, the Dawgs got within eight.
FINAL - OKLAHOMA 23, STATE 15
Next Up…
Mississippi State faces a road test at Texas on Saturday, Oct. 31. The Bulldogs and Longhorns are set for a 6:30 p.m. kickoff in Austin.
