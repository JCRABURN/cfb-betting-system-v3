Trojan Talk Returns to Baumhower’s Tonight; 81 Cent Wings
10/26/2026 1:29:00 PM
|

By:
Troy Athletics Communications
TROY, Ala. –
Troy's weekly coaches show, Trojan Talk, returns tonight (Monday), live from Baumhower's Victory Grille in Troy, with football head coach
Gerad Parker
and Voice of the Trojans
Barry McKnight
previews Thursday's game at James Madison. Women's basketball forward Detavia Salter, men's basketball forward Caden Diggs and men's golf head coach Clay Bounds will also be on the show tonight.
Baumhower's features wall-to-wall TVs throughout the restaurant and will offer 81-cent wings and $2.99 pints of Trojan Ale, along with its other daily specials each week during Trojan Talk. Fans in attendance will also have the opportunity to enter a drawing for prizes given out throughout the show.
