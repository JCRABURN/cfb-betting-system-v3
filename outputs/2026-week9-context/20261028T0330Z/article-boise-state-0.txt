Dominant Fourth Quarter Propels No. 21/22 Broncos Past Cougars
10/24/2026 8:10:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Gamebook
Game Notes
Photos
Highlights
Photo Gallery
Share:
PULLMAN, Wash.
- Boise State scored three touchdowns in the final quarter to pull away from Washington State, winning 31-10 on Saturday.
With the game tied at 10,
Maddux Madsen
found
Cam Bates
for a 6-yard touchdown pass early in the fourth quarter to give the Broncos (6-1, 3-0 Pac-12) a lead.
Boen Phelps
picked off the Cougars (1-7, 0-4 Pac-12) on the next drive, setting up a 33-yard
Dylan Riley
rushing score to make it 24-10.
Riley put the exclamation point on the contest with a 72-yard touchdown run - his fourth 70+ yard rush of the season - with a little over six minutes to play.
With the victory, Boise State is bowl-eligible for the 29th consecutive season, the second-longest active streak in the nation. Georgia achieved its 30th straight season of bowl eligibility last week.
The Blue and Orange put points on the scoreboard first. Facing a third and seven on its second offensive possession, Madsen found
Rasean Jones
for back-to-back receptions, putting the ball inside the 10-yard line.
Sire Gaines
took over from there, punching it in from nine yards out to give Boise State an early 7-0 advantage.
Washington State evened the score with a touchdown with 46 seconds left in the first half, putting together a 16-play, 73-yard drive. Boise State responded, driving 54 yards to connect on a 28-yard field goal to end the half with a 10-7 edge.
The Cougars tied the game with a 48-yard field goal midway through the third quarter. On the ensuing drive, Madsen rushed for two separate first downs with his legs. It paid off, with the fifth-year quarterback finding
Cam Bates
for a 6-yard touchdown pass at the start of the fourth quarter.
On the next possession,
Boen Phelps
snatched his second career interception, returning it 13 yards to the Cougar 33-yard line. Two plays later,
Dylan Riley
rushed 33 yards to the end zone, giving Boise State a 24-10 lead with 11:40 remaining.
Riley put the exclamation point on the victory, scoring a rushing touchdown from 72 yards out to give the Broncos a 21-point lead.
Top Broncos
-
Dylan Riley
rushed for 128 yards and two touchdowns on 13 carries. Riley recorded 106 rushing yards in the final quarter.
-
Maddux Madsen
threw for 149 yards and a touchdown in the win. He added four rushes for 34 yards.
-
Rasean Jones
hauled in five receptions for 69 yards.
-
Lopez Sanusi
notched a pair of sacks in the victory.
-
Boen Phelps
and
Jayden Virgin-Morgan
led the team with six tackles each. Phelps also recorded an interception.
Notables
- Boise State is bowl-eligible for the 29th consecutive season. That is the second-longest active streak, trailing Georgia, who became bowl-eligible for the 30th consecutive year earlier this season.
- The Broncos have won six consecutive contests. Boise State has won 6+ straight games in two of its three full seasons under
Spencer Danielson
.
- Danielson notched his 30th career victory in the win, becoming the eighth Bronco head coach to record 30+ victories. Of those eight coaches, Danielson is one of six who have done so with single-digit losses in their time with the Broncos.
- Boise State has started 3-0 in conference play in each of
Spencer Danielson
's three full seasons as head coach. The Broncos have accomplished the feat in four of the last five seasons.
- The Broncos scored 31 points in the contest, marking the sixth straight game the program has scored 30+ points. Boise State scored 30+ points in six straight games for the first time since 2023. It is the first time the Broncos have scored 30+ points and won six straight games since the 2014 season.
-
Rasean Jones
is the fourth freshman in Boise State's FBS era to notch at least five catches in three or more games on the season.
-
Maddux Madsen
ends the game with 14 touchdowns and zero interceptions on the season. He is the only quarterback in the nation with 10+ touchdowns and no interceptions.
- Madsen is the first Boise State quarterback to start seven straight games without throwing a pick since the program joined the FBS ranks in 1996.
-
Dylan Riley
rushed for 110 yards in the second half, marking his fifth game with 100+ rushing yards in a single half this season. He has done so seven times this season.
- Riley's 72-yard rushing touchdown in the fourth quarter marking his fourth 70+ touchdown rush of the season.
- Boise State held Washington State to 53 rushing yards. It is the third time the Broncos have held their opponent under 100 rushing yards this season, and the 10th time overall under head coach
Spencer Danielson
.
What's Next
The Broncos will return to Albertson Stadium to face off against Texas State on Saturday, Oct. 31. The game will kick off at 8 p.m. MT and will be televised nationally on The CW.
