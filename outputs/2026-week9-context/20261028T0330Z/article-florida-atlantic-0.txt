Messer’s Five Touchdowns Lead Owls to Nail-Biting Homecoming Win
10/24/2026 5:27:00 PM
|
Football
Box Score
Photo Gallery
Share:
By:
Ben Paul
BOCA RATON, Fla.
– Five touchdowns from fifth-year WR
Easton Messer
, tying the single-game program record, led Florida Atlantic football to a 51-49 barnburner victory over Rice on Howard Schnellenberger Field at Flagler Credit Union Stadium.
The Saturday afternoon that marked the conclusion to Homecoming weekend saw 100 combined points and 14 touchdowns between the Owls of Florida Atlantic (5-2, 2-1) and the Owls of Rice (1-7, 0-4). A 55-yard field goal attempt in the final minute by Rice came up short to secure FAU's fourth straight home victory.
Messer produced one of the greatest games by a Florida Atlantic player in program history, with 262 all-purpose yards, and accomplished a feat rarely seen in college football: a rushing (twice), receiving (twice) and punt return touchdown all occurring in the same game. Per @ESPNInsights, Messer is the only FBS player in the last 20 seasons with two rush touchdowns, two receiving touchdowns, and a punt return touchdown in the same game.

THE BEGINNING
Two early Rice rushing touchdowns put Florida Atlantic in an early hole through most of the first quarter. Late in the first, a fumbled backwards pass from Rice was recovered by senior DB
Ahlston Ware
, setting up FAU's opening score. Graduate QB
Caden Veltkamp
escaped from danger to find sophomore WR
Branden Hoch
30 yards into the end zone for Hoch's first collegiate touchdown.
Florida Atlantic's first safety since Sept. 9, 2015 resulted from a blocked punt by senior DB
Derrick Rogers
, trimming the deficit to 14-9 early in the second quarter. The lead was then captured on the next offensive possession with Messer taking a toss from the backfield into the end zone for a 2-yard rushing score.
Messer's second touchdown of the quarter came via a reception to grow the lead to 23-14. Rice responded with a long run for a score, but the 23-21 Florida Atlantic advantage carried into halftime.
THE TURNING POINT
Out of the locker room, the Owls of Florida Atlantic moved down the field with a similar toss play to Messer for his second touchdown on the ground. Veltkamp later found Messer deep down the field for a 68-yard score, his fourth of the day.
Rice continued to respond, shrinking a nine-point FAU lead down to two on five different occasions. The two teams traded touchdowns in the final two minutes of the third, with Florida Atlantic holding a 44-42 advantage.
THE FINISH
With just under four minutes remaining, the Florida Atlantic Owls forced a punt that landed in the hands of Messer, who took it all the way back from 82 yards out for his record-tying fifth touchdown, putting 50 on the board.
After another touchdown from Rice and a punt by FAU, the Florida Atlantic Owls' defense prevailed on a 3rd-and-5 pass attempt to force a 55-yard field goal that fell short, allowing for the game-ending kneeldown.
OFFENSE
Messer was responsible for 30 points from scrimmage, tied with Devin Singletary for the most in a single game in program history. He is the first Florida Atlantic wideout to rush for two touchdowns in a game and the first to tally multiple scores on the ground and as a receiver. He concluded the day with 168 receiving yards on 12 receptions.
Veltkamp completed 15 of his first 17 passing attempts and finished with a line of 29-for-37 for 339 yards and three passing touchdowns, with an additional score on the ground. He surpasses Chris Robison for sole possession of second place in the most 300+ yard passing games in program history, now at nine across two seasons.
Fifth-year RB
Leonard Farrow
carried the ball 17 times for a season-high 86 yards.
DEFENSE
Graduate DL
Blake Burris
led the team in tackles for the second consecutive game, finishing with nine.
Sophomore DB
Damon Allen
had seven tackles, a collegiate career high.
Graduate LB
Leon Hart Jr.
and senior DL
Wilky Denaud
joined forces for a sack.
SPECIAL TEAMS
Messer's 82-yard punt return touchdown is the longest in program history. He joins Brittney Tellis as the only Owl to take multiple punts to the house and is the first to do it twice in one season.
This marked Rogers' second blocked punt as an Owl, making him one of 10 players in team history to block multiple kicks.
