October 24, 2026
Hawkeyes Fall at Minnesota, 13-10
The University of Iowa football team tied the game in the fourth quarter, but Minnesota converted a 46-yard field goal as time expired to hand the Hawkeyes a 13-10 loss Saturday night at Huntington Bank Stadium.

Box Score
MINNEAPOLIS
– The University of Iowa football team fell to Minnesota, 13-10, on Saturday afternoon at Huntington Bank Stadium.
The Hawkeyes outgained the Golden Gophers, 288-209, and finished with advantages in first downs (15-11), passing yards (198-137) and yards per play (4.3-3.4). Iowa's defense limited Minnesota to 72 rushing yards, recorded 10 tackles for loss and three sacks, and held the Golden Gophers to 2-of-15 on third down.
Hank Brown
completed 11-of-21 pass attempts for 136 yards and a touchdown.
Jeremy Hecklinski
added 62 passing yards, completing 9-of-16 attempts.
Reece Vander Zee
led Iowa's receiving corps with four receptions for 69 yards and a touchdown.
DJ Vonnahme
added six catches for 31 yards, while
Tony Diaz
hauled in a team-long 57-yard reception.
L.J. Phillips Jr.
paced Iowa's rushing attack with 25 yards on seven carries.
Kamari Moulton
added 22 yards on 10 attempts.
Defensively,
Zach Lutmer
led the Hawkeyes with 11 tackles.
Cam Buffington
added 10 tackles and 1.5 tackles for loss, while
Bryce Hawthorne
recorded eight tackles and three tackles for loss.

PHOTOS
HOW IT HAPPENED
Minnesota capitalized on a blocked punt on Iowa's opening possession, setting up a 31-yard field goal by Luke Drzewiecki with 12:14 remaining in the first quarter.
Deshaun Lee
intercepted a Minnesota pass in the second quarter, leading to a 32-yard field goal by Cade Buhr that tied the game at 3-3 with 6:49 remaining before halftime.
Minnesota regained the lead midway through the third quarter when Darius Taylor scored on a one-yard touchdown run on fourth-and-one, giving the Golden Gophers a 10-3 advantage.
Iowa answered with 3:46 remaining in the fourth quarter, driving 98 yards in four plays and tying the game at 10-10 on a 37-yard touchdown pass from
Hank Brown
to
Reece Vander Zee
.
Minnesota moved 44 yards on its final possession before Drzewiecki connected on a 46-yard field goal as time expired for the 13-10 final.
POSTGAME NOTES
The Gophers’ victory snaps Iowa’s five-game winning streak in Minneapolis. It is Minnesota’s first home win over the Hawkeyes since 2014.
The loss was Iowa’s first against an unranked opponent since 2024 at UCLA.
INDIVIDUAL NOTES
QB
Hank Brown
and QB
Jeremy Hecklinski
both saw action in the contest.
Brown was 11-of-21 for 136 yards with one touchdown and two interceptions.
Hecklinski was 9-of-16 for 62 yards with one interception.
Brown's 37-yard touchdown pass to WR
Reece Vander Zee
was Iowa's longest touchdown pass of the season.
RB L.J. Phillips finished with seven carries for 25 yards to lead the team in rushing.
DB
Zach Lutmer
finished with a team-leading 11 tackles – his third double-digit tackle game of the season.
LB
Cam Buffington
had 10 tackles – his third double digit tackle game of the season – and DL
Bryce Hawthorne
had a career-high eight tackles.
DB
Deshaun Lee
had an interception and 28-yard return in the second quarter. It was his team-leading third interception of the season (sixth of his career).
P
Boston Everitt
had a career-long 60-yard punt in the first half.
K
Caden Buhr
connected on a 32-yard field goal in the second quarter.
He has made nine straight field goals from under 50 yards and is 10-of-12 on the year.
MISCELLANEOUS
Minnesota held Iowa to a season-low 10 points.
The Hawkeyes lost the turnover battle, 4-1, and the Gophers blocked an Iowa punt in the first quarter.
Iowa committed four turnovers for the first time since its 2023 game at Penn State.
Iowa had a successful fake punt via a 4-yard
Cam Buffington
rush on fourth-and-2 from the Iowa 38 in the second quarter.
It was the Hawkeyes’ first successful fake punt since 2017 vs. Illinois (
Amani Hooker
).
Iowa's four-play, 98-yard touchdown drive in the fourth quarter was its longest of the season.
Minnesota won the toss and deferred to the second half. The Hawkeyes have played 348 games under head coach
Kirk Ferentz
. Iowa has opened the game on offense 257 times (166-91). The Hawkeyes have opened the game on defense 91 times (52-39).
UP NEXT
Iowa returns to action on Halloween, hosting Wisconsin at 11 a.m. (CT) inside Kinnick Stadium.
