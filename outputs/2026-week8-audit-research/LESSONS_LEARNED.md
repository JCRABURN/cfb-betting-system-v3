## Results and prioritized causes

1. **Totals error tails and overconfidence, not a grading failure.** Week 8 totals were 23–29, MAE 15.010, RMSE 18.955 and mean actual-minus-forecast +4.681 points. Six of 52 errors exceeded 30 points in magnitude; 16 exceeded 20. The frozen residual standard deviation was 16.712. The strongest totals five went 3–2, but ranks 6–10 went 0–5. Across Weeks 5–8, totals scores implied 131.646 wins but delivered 112 of 225; descriptive Brier score was .2660, worse than a constant .50 score's .2500. The score AUC was .4475 (within-week permutation p=.182), and Week 8 alone was .4273 (p=.378). These samples do not justify reversing the ranking either.

2. **The totals inputs omit possession volume and player availability.** Exact component replay confirms four team EPA inputs, separate linear home/away score components and a shared normal residual model. There is no explicit neutral-situation tempo, projected possessions, QB availability, offensive-line continuity, or return-touchdown process. This identifies structural omissions; it does not identify a causal point adjustment. In San Diego State–Colorado State, projected away/home points were 23.938/30.767 versus 49/56 actual: +25.062/+25.233 errors. Both offenses contributed, so blaming one QB alone is unsupported. The game had 1,114 yards, third-down conversions of 8/13 and 9/12, and 59 points by halftime. The locked 64.5 Under was already lost during the third quarter; it was not merely a late meaningless score. Two turnovers by each team also rule out a simple one-sided turnover-count explanation.

3. **Context can invalidate confidence without supporting an invented adjustment.** Colorado State's official October 19 preview discussed Hauss Hejny's anticipated return; its October 24 recap confirms 333 passing yards, 85 rushing yards and five combined touchdowns. Oklahoma State's pregame October 20 preview documented five consecutive 300-yard passing games and 30-point team performances. The Week 8 model projected only 26.528 Oklahoma State points; actual points were 57, contributing 30.472 of the game's 34.309-point total error. These are plausible omitted-context hypotheses, not fitted causal effects. The pregame publication dates establish availability; a newly retrieved page is not claimed as an original frozen model input. No postgame replacement pick is created.

4. **Texas State demonstrates why a fixed narrative adjustment is unsafe.** Week 7 Colorado State–Texas State finished 27–66: 93 points against 59.345 forecast. Texas State's official recap documents 716 yards, 503 rushing yards and ten consecutive scoring drives; all 66 points were scored by the end of the third quarter. Week 8 Utah State–Texas State finished 17–20: 37 against 51.076 forecast. Texas State managed 250 yards (167 passing, 83 rushing); Utah State gained 285. Neither side committed a turnover. Texas State's 20 included a kickoff-return TD, while its modeled offensive component was 32.736. Its error therefore changed sign sharply despite the prior explosion. Separate gross pace from offensive efficiency and special teams; neither season EPA nor one extreme game determines next week's tempo. Do not add a flat 'Texas State Over' adjustment.

5. **Confidence and mixed-market ranking remain unvalidated.** ATS confidence is 1 for all 52 games; governed uncertainty is null. The governed Top 5 went 2–3 and ranks tied on reliability inputs. Research ATS Top 5 went 3–2 and Top 10 went 6–4, but the full card was 26–26. Across Weeks 5–8 the research ATS Top 5 was 13–7, versus totals 9–11; only Weeks 7–8 have explicitly sealed comparable research ranks (ATS 6–4; totals 5–5). W5–6 score ordering is retrospective. Overall ATS score AUC .5475 (n=225, p=.229) is weak evidence. Comparing min(.60,.50+.005*ATS edge) with a normal-CDF totals score has no common calibrated probability basis.

## Dependence and human decisions

Across 225 paired games, signed ATS/total forecast-error correlation is .0043; absolute-error correlation is −.0152; outcome-indicator correlation is .0756. Week 8 alone has −.0258 signed-error correlation and −.0387 outcome correlation. There is only one same-game overlap between the two markets' weekly Top 5 lists across four weeks. These estimates do not establish independence: teams recur and same-game picks share conditions. Use week/team clusters in any future uncertainty calculation.

Week 7's recorded owner five went 2–3 versus 3–2 for its ATS research five: a descriptive difference of one win, with selection confounding and n=5. Week 8 has no approval artifact; its frozen 10-candidate review pool went 6–4, but that is not a human-selected contest card. No value claim is supported.
